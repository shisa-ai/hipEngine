Attention-area verification (2026-09-10, XTX, K_M 512-row prefill)

Objective item: "attention" as a prefill lever. Verification of each
attention-area stage from the DEDUPLICATED device-side stage census
(2026-09-10 revision: the first census summed alias keys, double
counting 85.273ms; this revision drops aliases at mark time and its
total, 552.5ms, matches the 557ms prefill wall):

1. full_attn core_gate (10.942ms across 32 layers): AOTriton
   compact-varlen (vendor-tuned), ~0.34ms/layer at 512 rows with GQA
   24q/4kv x 256 head_dim. No in-tree lever.
2. qkv_head_norm_rope stage (28.598ms): dominated by the QKV linear
   projections (already on the optimized wide-N owners). The non-linear
   remainder measured standalone:
   - head RMSNorm + partial RoPE (512 tokens, 24+4 heads x 256,
     rope 64): 103.4us per layer call = 3.31ms across 32 layers -
     one 256-thread block per (head, token) at ~600 GB/s effective on
     its 25MB of traffic. No lever.
   - split_qgate + bf16->f32 K cast: a few ms total (same class).
3. full_attn_output (12.412ms): output projection linear + gate
   postprocessing; the linear is on the optimized owners.
4. The remaining non-linear mass is the GDN linear-attention recurrence
   (35.330ms across 96 layers) - a dedicated chunked-scan kernel
   subsystem (compact peer-wave32 and segmented modes), a separate
   campaign from the attention lever, recorded as the open item.

Deprioritization rationale (not proof of no remaining optimization):
the attention core is vendor-tuned, the norm/RoPE chain is at memory
bandwidth, and the stages around them are the already-optimized linear
owners. The next prefill levers by measured size are the GDN recurrence
(35.3ms) and the norm/RoPE/residual fusion chains.
