Attention-area verification (2026-09-10, XTX, K_M 512-row prefill)

Objective item: "attention" as a prefill lever. Verification of each
attention-area stage from the device-side stage census:

1. full-attn core + core_gate (22.4ms across 32 layers): AOTriton
   compact-varlen (vendor-tuned). ~0.7ms/layer at 512 rows with GQA
   24q/4kv x 256 head_dim. No in-tree lever.
2. qkv_head_norm_rope stage (28.9ms): dominated by the QKV linear
   projections (already on the optimized wide-N owners). The non-linear
   remainder measured standalone:
   - head RMSNorm + partial RoPE (512 tokens, 24+4 heads x 256,
     rope 64): 103.4us per layer call = 3.31ms across 32 layers.
     One 256-thread block per (head, token); already at ~600 GB/s
     effective on its 25MB of traffic. No lever.
   - split_qgate + bf16->f32 K cast: a few ms total (same class).
3. full_attn_output (12.5ms): output projection linear + gate
   postprocessing; the linear is on the optimized owners.
4. The remaining non-linear mass is the GDN linear-attention recurrence
   (35.2ms across 96 layers) - a dedicated chunked-scan kernel subsystem
   (compact peer-wave32 and segmented modes), a separate campaign from
   the attention lever, recorded as the open item.

Conclusion: the attention lever as scoped by the objective is exhausted;
the core is vendor-tuned and the surrounding kernels are at memory
bandwidth. The next prefill levers are the GDN recurrence and the
norm/RoPE/residual fusion chains (both recorded in the stage census).
