# W7900 Qwen3.8: 45-row prefill runs 6-15x off both W7900 ceilings, at 512 rows we do not

## Summary

The residual C1-C3 loss is quantified against hardware ceilings rather than against another
engine. Three independent methods put the fixed cost of one prefill at **250-271 ms** on the
shipping route. Kernel attribution says why: a 45-row prefill runs at **6.6% of peak memory
bandwidth and 9.6% of the measured BF16 WMMA ceiling**, while a 512-row prefill runs at
**56.3% of that same WMMA ceiling**. The small-row path is not slow because of host overhead
and not because a fast path is missing - the WMMA path is selected and simply does not
saturate anything at 45 rows.

## Changes

- `scripts/gguf_first_token_probe.py`: `--length-scan` no longer dies after nine good
  measurements. A `context_length_exceeded` 400 is now recorded as a skip and ends the sweep;
  previously the 512 target raised `RuntimeError` and exited 1 with no output file, because the
  synthetic builder overshoots its target (target 256 -> 665 actual tokens, target 512 -> 1433
  against `--max-sequence-length 1024`). The scan also prints the fit it exists to produce.
- `fixed_marginal_fit()` factored out with regime selection, and
  `tests/test_scripts_gguf_first_token_probe_fit.py` pins it: recovers a known
  fixed+slope exactly, excludes long prompts that bend the slope, refuses fewer than three rows
  and a single distinct x rather than inventing a slope, drops rows with missing fields instead
  of zeroing them, and holds the measured W7900 magnitude in range. 6 passed.

## Validation

Fixed term, three routes over 45-token-scale prompts that agree to 8%:

| source | fixed | marginal | R2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| parity entry rates at 45-512 tokens | 265.1 ms | 0.64 ms/token | 0.9831 |
| shipping stage runs (45, 512) | 252.2 ms | 1.13 ms/token | 1.0000 |
| today's scan, 9 lengths, small-row regime | 271.2 ms | 0.64 ms/token | 0.8351 |

The third fit is noisier because single-request walls repeat at +-15% at the shortest lengths
(9 tokens: 0.3041 s and 0.2644 s back to back), which is itself worth knowing: a C1 measurement
needs several repeats, and this is the same width noise the AR paired band showed.

Ceiling utilization, from `docs/ROOFLINE.md` (864 GB/s theoretical VRAM, 84.8 TFLOP/s measured
BF16 WMMA) and the model's own `total_tensor_nbytes` 17,095,778,304:

| shape | wall | effective BW | % of 864 GB/s | TFLOP/s (2 x 27B) | % of 84.8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 45 rows | 0.2976 s | 57.4 GB/s | 6.6% | 8.2 | 9.6% |
| 512 rows | 0.5793 s | 29.5 GB/s | 3.4% | 47.7 | 56.3% |

The ratio depends on one assumption - 27B active parameters per token, so `2NL` FLOPs - and the
compute column should be read as a range of 6x-15x off the roofline, where the bandwidth column
is assumption-free because one pass over the weights is the floor at any shape: 19.8 ms at
theoretical bandwidth, 26.4 ms at 75%, against 297.6 ms measured.

Kernel accounting for one 45-row prefill (`/tmp/he-trace-ship-45/epyc/101786_kernel_trace.csv`,
6358 records over 1 warmup + 2 measured runs, so every figure below is the capture total divided
by three; the parts sum to 287.0 ms against a measured 297.6 ms wall):

| class | ms / prefill | share | launches / prefill |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WMMA prefill GEMM (5 families) | 243.6 | 84.9% | 336 |
| GEMV (vector) GEMM | 29.4 | 10.2% | 530 |
| norm / rope / add | 4.6 | 1.6% | 514 |
| linear-attention + GDN + paged-attention prefill | 6.6 | 2.3% | 129 |
| copy / fill | 2.0 | 0.7% | 297 |

Top individual families: `gguf_q4_t16_dense_dual_wmma_prefill_silu_bf16` 77.1 ms over 64
launches, `gguf_q4_t16_dense_wmma_prefill_shared_b_bf16` 67.0 ms over 112,
`q6_k_t16_qmicro_planar_wmma_prefill_bf16` 60.2 ms over 64, `gguf_q5_t16_dense_wmma_prefill_bf16`
23.3 ms over 48, then `q4_k_t16_dense_dual_local32_silu_gemv` 10.0 ms over 64,
`gguf_q4_t16_dense_unequal_dual_wmma_prefill_bf16` 9.8 ms over 24,
`q6_k_t16_qmicro_planar_gemv_bf16` 8.5 ms over 66, `q4_k_t16_dense_single_local32_gemv` 6.3 ms
over 160.

So 95.1% of a 45-row prefill is dense-projection GEMM split 85/10 between the WMMA and GEMV
routes, and every piece of glue - norm, rope, adds, KV writes, copies, fills - totals 6.6 ms.
With 2119 launches in 287 ms the mean kernel is 135 us, so this is not launch-bound either: at
~7 us of launch overhead the whole dispatch cost is roughly 15 ms of the 287. The time is inside
GEMM kernels that are sized for large M and cannot use either ceiling at M=45.

Instrument note, unchanged: `gpu_stage_timings_ms` reports `enabled: False` with an empty
`prefill` map and `rocprof_selected_region: none` on exactly this run, so per-stage attribution
still has to come from the raw kernel CSV, not from the harness field.
