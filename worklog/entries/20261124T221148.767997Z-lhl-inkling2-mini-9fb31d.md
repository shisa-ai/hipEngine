# Add the Inkling 2 GGUF profile gate and record its first rows

## Summary

Campaign task 8. `scripts/inkling2_profile_gate.py` gates one Inkling 2 GGUF decode arm against
the strict plan on the full protocol: 672 full-vocabulary teacher-forced rows on 25 prompts in
nine categories (41 to 2,289 prompt tokens), the router-routing-scoped envelope of Execution
Profiles section 2.11 as the binding quality verdict, controls derived from the schedule, three
bit-stable repeats, a reversed-order replay in a fresh process, public-route parity and the paired
known-answer check. It writes its own packet kind, as the GGUF c1 route gate does: the standard
packet admits `strict`, `production` and `batch_invariant`, and these arms are `fast`.

First rows, measured on `epyc` (Radeon Pro W7900, gfx1100, HIP 7.2.53211), mainline `UD-Q4_K_M`,
with the Q8-dot expert chain that the opt-in path had at the time, on a tree that held the
uncommitted gate:

| Arm | Median KL | p90 KL | Rows over 5e-2 | Top-1 | Section 2.11 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `fast` | 5.74e-5 | 3.03e-3 | 1.49% | 99.55% | fails `code`: p90 2.98e-2 |
| `fast`, device routes, resident decode | 5.34e-5 | 3.68e-3 | 2.08% | 98.81% | fails `code`: median 1.36e-3, p90 4.19e-2, 10.4% over the line |
| the same with the decode graph | 5.34e-5 | 3.68e-3 | 2.08% | 98.81% | fails `code` on the same three bars |

Every other gate passed in all three rows: the 744 scheduled session calls of each run matched
the controls with no mismatch, three repeats were bit-stable in logits, calls and state, 25 of 25
requests gave bit-identical logits in the reversed-order replay, the public greedy chain equaled
the captured route on 25 of 25 requests, and the known-answer check scored 9 of 12 for strict and
for each arm, with the same generated IDs on all 12 cases.

- **The damage sits in one category and, for `fast`, in one prompt.** `natural_code_lru` has a
  median KL of 6.3e-3 over its 24 rows with strict's top-1 on every row; the other code prompt
  stays at p90 2.6e-3. The prefill row of every short prompt is at 1e-12 or below, so the
  divergence is made in the decode visit.
- **The dense-envelope summary fails all three rows** (`fast`: mean 3.1e-3, p99 9.7e-2, max 0.19),
  as a row set whose tail is routing flips must. It is reported and is not binding.

## Changes

- `scripts/inkling2_profile_gate.py`: the arms `fast`, `fast_resident` and `fast_graph`; the
  section 2.11 verdict over the canonical row metric; schedule-derived controls compared with what
  the session was asked at each call (token, position, width, transaction phase, graph bucket,
  attention ring); a hash of the live attention banks and convolution histories after each
  trajectory; the reversed-order worker; the public-route and task checks.
- `benchmarks/prompts/inkling2-mini-profile-gate.jsonl`: the 12 known-answer prompts, 10 short
  natural prompts in six languages or code, and three long prompts of 1,162, 2,289 and 1,004
  tokens. Two start with the 1,024-token attention window full; the third fills it at row 21.
- `scripts/inkling2_decode_pairs.py`: a run keeps `calls_per_token`. The first paired run of the
  day lost its artifact to that key: the summary raised `KeyError` after the measurements and the
  caller had filtered stderr. The per-run outputs of its 2,057-token cell were copied out of the
  scratch directory before it was removed and are quoted in the F32-expert entry.
- `docs/campaigns/INKLING2-MINI-GGUF-EXECUTION.md`: the gate command and the adapter note.
- Tests: `tests/test_unit_inkling2_profile_gate.py`, `tests/test_gpu_inkling2_profile_gate.py`
  (a two-prompt screen per candidate; outside default discovery) and
  `tests/test_unit_inkling2_decode_pairs.py`.
