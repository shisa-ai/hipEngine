# Concurrency2 C2-5 token-budget c1-c32 planning

## Summary

Implemented C2-5's token-budget scheduling round and exact execution-compatibility/physical-width planner through logical c32. One round now rotates multiple bounded prefill quanta by stable slot, then advances every due decode row exactly once; registered dense runners lower arbitrary logical membership into certified physical widths with honest masked, compacted, or serial-c1 labels.

## Changes

- Added `ExecutionCompatibilityKey`, `ExecutionPlan`, and `plan_execution_groups()`: exact backend/layout/kernel/work/context/workspace grouping followed by stable-slot physical lowering over backend-declared widths.
- Added explicit execution labels `registered_masked_or_exact`, `registered_dense_compaction`, and `serial_c1_fallback`; no fallback group is reported as native batched execution.
- Added `TokenBudgetSLO.from_targets()` and `EngineLoopConfig` round prefill/decode budgets with CLI/env resolution.
- Added the opt-in `token_budget` engine-loop policy. It can execute multiple round-robin prefill chunks per tick and one decode step over every due resident row; decode budget must cover resident capacity.
- Extended `ResidentBatchScheduler` with stable-slot round-robin prefill and validated decode subsets while retaining legacy FCFS methods for compatibility policies.
- Extended `DenseKVResidentRunnerAdapter` to execute registered physical prefill/decode group entry points, enforce exact compatibility keys and backend physical widths, validate one decode result per logical row, and expose planner/path/width telemetry.
- Kept existing GGUF policy defaults unchanged and tracked removal of generic `protect_decode`/`protect_ttft`/`fair` production choices after C2-6 SLO/hardware qualification.
