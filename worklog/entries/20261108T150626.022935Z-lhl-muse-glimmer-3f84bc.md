# Muse Glimmer verify Q6 rowtile hoists metadata loads; #2 GEMV ceiling mostly inherent

## Summary

Task #2 of the §11.1 long-context track ("verify GEMV efficiency", projected
21-32 ms/cycle ceiling). Phase-tagged rocprofv3 of the verify block on gfx1151
splits the verify-vs-AR GEMV gap at 32K into Q4 rowtile +11.5 ms (68.7 vs 57.3),
Q6 rowtile +3.4 ms (17.1 vs 13.8), Q5 head +2.7 ms (7.2 vs 4.5). Two levers were
tested on the dominant Q4 piece and both are neutral: (a) vectorizing the nibble
and activation loads to match the AR grouped owner's wide loads, and (b) the
alternative col4 / two-wave geometries. The Q4 gap is the exposed cost of five
bit-exact row dot-products (5x the FMA of the rows-1 AR owner) over a weight read
that is already amortized; it is not a load or geometry defect. The §11 "reach
AR-grouped (rows-1) efficiency" ceiling is therefore largely unreachable for an
exact multi-row verify. The recoverable piece was the Q6 rowtile, which re-read
per-column d and per-g16 scales inside the K loop and read each 12-byte qmicro
record as three byte loads. Hoisting d once per superblock and reading the scale
row / record with wide loads (the AR hoisted owner's optimizations, ported to the
weight-amortized rowtile) cuts Q6 verify GEMV 17.13 -> 15.6 ms/cycle (~1.5 ms,
-8.5%), context-independent, bit-identical. The remaining Q6 gap (~1.9 ms) and the
Q4/Q5 gaps are the same inherent multi-row FMA cost.

## Changes

- hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q6_k_t16_gemv.hip:
  `q6_k_t16_qmicro_planar_gemv_rowtile_col8_kernel` hoists the per-column d[8]
  once per superblock (uint4 load), reads each g16 scale row as a uint4 and each
  qmicro record as three dwords instead of per-weight byte loads. Adds
  `fp16_bits_to_float`. Values, the integer coefficient product `scale_i*q_i`,
  the FP32 FMA stream `acc += xr*w`, the K traversal (tid, tid+128), the wave
  tree, and the ordered four-wave sum are unchanged -> bit-identical to the
  planar decode parent. The grouped/residual col8 siblings are untouched.
- A Q4 load-vectorization prototype and a col4/w2 geometry sweep were measured
  and reverted (neutral); no Q4 source change lands.
