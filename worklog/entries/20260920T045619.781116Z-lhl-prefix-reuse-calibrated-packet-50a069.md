# The 1024-row calibrated packet fails the production envelope: 11 flipped rows across 6 of 8 scopes, mean KL 7.7x the bar

## Summary

The 4-row probe was too small to see the real rate. Running the prefix-reuse gate
at the row count `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` requires -- 128 teacher-forced
steps per scope over both the category suite and the 18-prompt heldout suite,
1,024 shared-chain rows in eight scopes -- the production prefix-reuse route
fails the §6.1 calibrated production envelope against its own registered strict
serial fallback (`HIPENGINE_GGUF_PREFIX_BATCHED_SUFFIX=0`). Eleven rows flip and
six of the eight scopes carry at least one flip, including heldouts. This
supersedes the "one flip on one constructed prompt" reading in
`20260920T040925.692107Z-lhl-dense27b-ja-flip-localized-per-step-d87c26` and
`20260920T043417.618640Z-lhl-dense27b-ja-margin-diagnosis-correction-393bc4`,
which were measured with four rows per scope and could not resolve a 1% rate.

above the `5e-2` absolute ceiling.

Against the §6.1 binding limits (mean <= `1e-3`, p95 <= `5e-3`, p99 <= `2e-2`,
max <= `5e-2`, top-1 >= `99%` overall and >= `97%` per scope): **mean fails**
(7.7x the bar), **max fails** (60x the ceiling), **top-1 overall fails**
(`0.9893` versus `0.99`), and **`heldout-general_en` fails the per-scope top-1
bar** (`0.9688`). p95 and p99 pass, p99 only narrowly at `0.019703` against
`2e-2`.

Two readings this packet rules out. It is not a Japanese-prompt artifact: the
largest single row is `heldout-code` at `3.002259`, and `heldout-general_en`
fails three bars while `heldout-general_ja` and `heldout-mixed_ja_en` are the
two cleanest scopes in the packet. It is not a tail-only effect: four scopes
exceed the mean bar, so the difference is broad, not confined to 11 rows.

The 11 rows above `2e-2` require the top-k overlap and strict logit-margin
diagnosis §6.1 demands, and the 4 rows above the `5e-2` ceiling are not
admissible at all.
