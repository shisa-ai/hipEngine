# W7900 Qwen3.8: correction - MTP packs the wave fine; the loss is 3.21x per-row verify cost

## Summary

My earlier framing "MTP cycles never pack across the wave" over-read the data. Fitting
the same retained K3 packet properly, wave packing is fine - MTP's per-cycle fixed cost
is **21.4 ms, lower than AR's 27.6 ms**. The loss is the **marginal cost per verified
row: 21.51 ms for MTP vs 6.71 ms for AR, 3.21x**. Restoring acceptance alone cannot
make MTP beat AR at width, so the objective's MTP row needs the verify path to get
cheaper per row, not a scheduler change.

Supersedes the interpretation in
`20260830T025717.533327Z-lhl-w7900-mtp-accept-width-invariance-break-f7c44a.md`. Its
measurements stand (per-lane cycle cost flat, acceptance 0.79 -> 0.47 at width >= 2,
C4 identical to C8); its "never pack / zero amortisation" conclusion does not, because
cost is near-linear in *verified rows*, which grow with lanes, and the fixed component
does amortize.

## Changes

Analysis only. Adds this entry and corrects task #9's target: the fix is the per-row
verify cost, with the width-dependent acceptance loss as a secondary +27% lever.

## Validation

Least-squares fits over C1-C8 of the same packets, both near-perfect:

| route | fixed | marginal | R2 | source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AR decode | 27.6 ms/step | 6.71 ms/row | 0.9972 | `decode_step_ms` in `benchmarks/results/2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-c1c8-submodule-decomposition.json` |
| MTP verify | 21.4 ms/cycle | 21.51 ms/row | 0.9951 | `mtp.draft_cycles` / `mtp.draft_tokens` per row in the K3 packet |

MTP rows per cycle per lane is 3.80-3.85 across every width (candidates plus the base
row), so row counts are known and the fit is not fitting two free knobs to eight
points. Per-width wave-cycle cost grows 6.35x for 8x lanes, and 8x lanes is 8x rows -
consistent with row-linear cost plus a small amortized fixed part, which is exactly
what the two fits say.

Consequence, computed rather than asserted: at unchanged row cost, restoring C8
acceptance to its C1 rate (2.67 -> 3.38 tokens/cycle/lane) is **+27%**, i.e. MTP
31.45 -> ~39.8 tok/s, still below our own AR **45.66** at C8. So acceptance is worth
fixing but is not the binding constraint at width; 3.21x per-row verify cost is.
