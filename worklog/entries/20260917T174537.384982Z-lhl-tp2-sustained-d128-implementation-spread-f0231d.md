# TP2 sustained D128 failure is an implementation-spread artifact, not a TP2 defect

## Summary

The sustained 128-step gate failure on `mixed_ja_en_translate` (decode index 82,
absolute position 146) is not evidence of a TP2 arithmetic defect. On one
revision, one prompt, 128 teacher-forced positions, **hipEngine TP1's own
token-serial prefill route** — which contains no TP2 code — breaches the same
production max-KL ceiling at **the same decode index** and reaches **0.6092**
(12x the ceiling) by index 93, while TP2 token-serial reaches 0.1084 and TP2
bulk prefill 0.1671. TP2 is closer to the bulk teacher than TP1's own alternative
prefill schedule on mean, p99, max and top-1, and at the failing position TP2
sits 10.5x closer to a **same-schedule** TP1 control (0.028379) than that control
sits to the teacher (0.299279).

Mechanism, measured rather than inferred: the breaching positions are exactly the
flat ones. At index 82 the teacher's top-1 holds **0.4866** of the mass with a
**0.6233** top-2 logit gap and entropy **1.2390**, against neighbours at top-1
probability >= 0.99 and gaps of 5-21. Every arm keeps the **same top-1 token**
(248046) there. 2-3 of 128 positions exceed 0.01 KL; p95 stays inside
5.7e-04..9.9e-04 for every arm. The absolute max-KL ceiling over a long forced
horizon is therefore a bit-exactness test in disguise on this prompt: a ~0.1-1.0
max-abs logit difference between two legitimate implementations moves a lot of
mass where the model is undecided, and no route reaches zero.

Consequence for the campaign: the discriminating sustained measurements are
mean/p95 KL, top-1 agreement, and a **same-schedule** implementation-spread
comparison, not the raw max against a single bulk-prefill reference. Which gate
replaces the absolute max ceiling is a normative decision
(`docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md`) and is left open here with the evidence attached.

## Changes

- `scripts/tp2_prefill_schedule_failure_probe.py` (new): runs four arms over the
  same teacher-forced prefix on one revision — `tp1-bulk` (the teacher arm,
  re-run to confirm it reproduces its own rows), `tp1-serial` (schedule
  sensitivity reference with no TP2 arithmetic), `tp2-serial` (the committed TP2
  route) and `tp2-bulk` (the rank-local bulk prefill candidate) — and reports the
  full per-position KL curve, the production envelope summary (mean/p95/p99/max,
  top-1, count over 0.01 and over the ceiling), the distribution shape and
  max/mean absolute logit difference at each comparison's worst position, and the
  pairwise arm-to-arm curves. It refuses to compare across an identity mismatch
  in any arithmetic or source field and only proceeds past a **host-process**
  mismatch (`--allow-host-process-drift`) with the diff recorded in the artifact.
- `scripts/tp2_resident_control.py`: the resident TP1 adapter now exposes
  `prefill_use_bulk` (`None` = the session's own default, `False` = token-serial)
  instead of hardcoding `use_bulk=None`, so the schedule reference needs no
  monkeypatch. No default behaviour change.
- `scripts/tp2_teacher_coverage_broad.py`: the sustained capture takes
  `--tp2-bulk-prefill`, so the gate itself can score the TP2 bulk candidate
  instead of the committed token-serial route; the artifact records the schedule
  it measured. It refuses the flag on a non-TP2 arm. Previously uncommitted work
  from the same investigation, now covered by tests.
- `tests/test_unit_scripts_tp2_prefill_schedule_failure_probe.py` (new): CPU-only
  coverage for the distribution-shape classifier (flat vs sharp top, offset
  invariance), the envelope summary's breach counting, and the identity-diff
  split between host-process and arithmetic fields.
- `tests/test_unit_tp2_sustained_quality.py`: covers the TP2 schedule label, the
  factory's refusal to hand the TP2 bulk candidate to a TP1 arm, and the resident
  `prefill_use_bulk` pass-through.
- `benchmarks/README.md` and `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`: the TP2 section's "genuine
  end-to-end quality failure" reading is replaced by the four-arm measurement,
  and the diagnostic is rolled up with its evidence links.
- `docs/QWEN38-27B-GFX1100-TP2.md`: the sustained-failure interpretation now
  carries this measurement instead of the "no single arithmetic term decides the
  comparison" placeholder.

## Validation

Same revision `9cbfdc5d2658e85b6377377ac45e7e5cfdb62fb6`, one physical host
(W7900 + RX 7900 XTX, gfx1100), model `Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M.gguf`, production
profile, capacity 200, 64-token prompt, 128 teacher-forced decode transitions
(positions 64..191), `forced_inputs` identical across arms.

```
HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 python3 scripts/tp2_teacher_coverage_broad.py \
    --sustained-arm tp1-d0 --max-sequence-length 200 --repeat-tp2 3 \
    --json /tmp/tp2-d128-cur/teacher-cur.json
python3 scripts/tp2_prefill_schedule_failure_probe.py \
    --teacher /tmp/tp2-d128-cur/teacher-cur.json \
    --prompt-id mixed_ja_en_translate --steps 128 --allow-host-process-drift \
    --json benchmarks/results/2026-09-17-tp2-prefill-schedule-failure-probe.json
```

Identity: the teacher capture and the probe ran at the same revision, and the
probe recorded an **empty** identity diff — the four arms and the reference share
`model_sha256`, `source_revision`, `source_sha256`, `profile_sha256`,
`staged_diff_sha256`, capacity, kv/recurrent/sampling and host. The
`--allow-host-process-drift` flag was passed but was not needed (this environment
re-nices long-running shells, which is why the probe splits the identity check;
the earlier attempt without a fresh capture was correctly refused on
`source_sha256`).

At the failure index 82 (position 146): teacher top-1 prob 0.48658 / gap 0.62328 /
entropy 1.2390 / token 248046; `tp2-serial` 0.47968 / 0.15644 / 1.0978 / 248046;
`tp2-bulk` 0.57517 / 0.49132 / 0.9759 / 248046. `tp1-serial`'s own worst position

max|dlogit| 2.28454 / mean 0.18290, an order of magnitude more state difference
than TP2's 0.99300 / 0.10830 at index 82.

(0.2992785432102255, `localization.comparisons.bulk_vs_serial.kl` in
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-16-tp2-native-product-d128-blocked.json`, whose
`localization_source` is the script that ran it), so the schedule sensitivity is
stable across revisions and across two independently written measurement scripts.
The `tp1-bulk` self-check arm also reproduced the teacher's own rows exactly
(max KL 0.0 over all 128 positions), so the reference is not in question.

Artifacts: `benchmarks/results/2026-09-17-tp2-prefill-schedule-failure-probe.json`

## Next

- The gate question is the blocking decision: whether the sustained profile keeps
  an absolute max-KL ceiling that a legitimate TP1 route also fails, or moves to
  (a) the stable statistics (mean/p95, top-1), (b) a same-schedule
  implementation-spread bound, and (c) an explicit authorized horizon. No
  promotion claim is made here either way; `decode_partial_dtype="bf16"` and
  token-serial prefill both stay shipped.
- The bulk-prefill candidate's own arithmetic effect is bounded by
  `tp2-serial_vs_tp2-bulk` = 0.0258852 max KL, top-1 1.000, mean 4.673e-04 —
  inside the ceiling on the schedule-difference axis — but its distance to the
  single bulk teacher grows 1.4-2.3x on mean/p95/p99/max relative to the serial
  arm. Retaining it needs the gate decision above plus the full mtp-bench
  category suite; it is not retained on this evidence.
