# MT-1 body pass found: already exists, bit-exact, and sub-linear in rows

## Summary

MT-1's stated blocker was "a body pass whose cost grows **sub-linearly** with rows
while keeping c1 arithmetic". **Both halves are already satisfied** for the q8_0
linear family (DE-3) by an existing registered kernel. No new kernel is needed for
that half; what remains is wiring, not arithmetic.

## The arithmetic half (RED, done first)

MT-1 requires RED before any timing, so that came first. Two links were already
tested independently but **never composed**:

| link | test | asserts |
|---|---|---|
| c1 decode == scalar GEMV | `test_q8_0_pack8_f32_matches_scalar_gemv_bits` | bit-exact, rows=1 |
| rowbatch == scalar GEMV | `test_q8_0_f32_rowbatch32_matches_scalar_prefill_bits` | bit-exact, rows=37 |

Link 2 is checked at a **single** width, and neither asserts **batch-composition
invariance** - that row `i` of a batched launch equals what serial c1 produced for
that row, at every width. That was the real gap.

New test, against the serial c1 kernel directly with no scalar-GEMV intermediary:
`test_q8_0_coltile8_rowbatch4_is_width_invariant_to_serial_c1[2..8]`. It runs
`coltile8_rowbatch4` at width `rows` and compares to `rows` separate single-row
`pack8` launches. **7/7 widths pass.**

## The cost half

| rows | batched | serial c1 | speedup | per-row batched | per-row serial |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 81.4 us | 65.9 us | **0.80x** | 81.4 | 65.9 |
| 2 | 94.7 us | 108.4 us | 1.14x | 47.3 | 54.2 |
| 4 | 115.6 us | 192.2 us | 1.66x | 28.9 | 48.0 |
| 8 | 198.1 us | 366.0 us | **1.85x** | **24.8** | 45.7 |

Per-row cost falls **82.1 -> 24.8 us (3.3x)**; 8x the rows costs **2.42x**.

**rows=1 is slower (0.80x)**, which I am flagging rather than burying: batching
only wins from rows>=2. That is the right regime for a verifier consuming 2-8
draft rows, but the pass must not be used at width 1.

## What I am not claiming

- **Not an end-to-end verifier speedup.** This is the q8_0 GEMV kernel pair. MT-1
  needs the *whole* target pass sub-linear; this is one component - the one DE-3
  owns.
- **Does not cover DE-1** (MoE expert GEMV pair, 11.3 ms/token). Separate link in
  the same chain, untested here.
- **Part of the 1.85x is launch-count reduction, not throughput.** The serial arm
  issues `rows` launches, the batched arm one. That is a real cost a serial
  verifier pays, but the ratio is not a pure throughput measurement and I have
  labelled it as such in the artifact.
- No MTP or speculative-decode end-to-end run.

## Why this changes the row

MT-1 was scoped as "pick the two largest decode owners and **build** rows<=8
variants that reproduce the c1 kernels' per-output reduction bit for bit". For the
q8_0 linear family that build is **already done and already in the prefill path**
(the census shows this kernel at rows=1024 in the p4096 prefill). The remaining
work is composing it into a verifier body pass and measuring the complete pass - a
different and smaller unit than the row assumed.

I did not change the row's status to closed, because MT-1 is about the verifier and
this packet measures a component of it.
