# Record the rows at 256-row Inkling fast blocks

## Summary

Clean-worktree rows at `cbe01e818`, where `fast` reads 256-row blocks and the profile gate runs both
arms on the candidate's blocks. Measured on `epyc` (Radeon Pro W7900, gfx1100, HIP 7.2.53211),
mainline `UD-Q4_K_M`, one greedy stream, prefix cache off, drafting off. Every artifact records the
commit with `dirty` false.

Decode bench through the in-tree harness, 128 outputs, five repetitions after a warmup:

| Profile and width | 47 tokens: first token, decode | 575 tokens | 2,057 tokens |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `fast`, 64 rows ([earlier row](../benchmarks/results/2026-11-25-w7900-inkling2-mini-gguf-block-prefill-bench.json)) | 0.208 s, 18.05 tok/s | 2.334 s, 17.92 | 9.732 s, 17.46 |
| `fast`, 256 rows | 0.208 s, 17.92 tok/s | 1.856 s, 17.88 | 7.705 s, 17.43 |
| `strict`, 64 rows, no profile requested | 0.796 s, 14.82 tok/s | 9.804 s, 12.85 | 37.447 s, 11.26 |

- **`fast` at 256 rows:** the first token comes 1.26 times sooner after 575 and 2,057 prompt tokens.
  The 128 output tokens are the earlier row's. Repetition ranges are 0.206 to 0.210 s, 1.852 to
  1.869 s and 7.701 to 7.708 s.
- **`strict` is the public route today.** Its row is the baseline for the default surface; its
  outputs equal `fast`'s after 575 and 2,057 prompt tokens and differ somewhere in the 128 after 47.

Profile gate, 687 rows against strict: 672 decode rows and 15 prefill block ends at 256 tokens:

| Arm | Median KL | p90 KL | Top-1 | Damaged rows | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `fast`, device routes, resident decode | 1.0e-13 | 3.5e-12 | 100% | 0 | passed |
| the same with the decode graph | 1.0e-13 | 3.5e-12 | 100% | 0 | passed |
| `fast` | 4.8e-5 | 4.2e-3 | 98.54% | 1.0% | fails `code`: median 1.1e-3, p90 2.7e-2 |

- **The two opt-in arms pass every gate on the candidate's own schedule**, isolation included: 25 of
  25 requests bit-identical in the reversed-order replay. Their dense envelope is mean 7.9e-5, p95
  8.0e-7, p99 1.0e-3, maximum 1.7e-2. Their candidate logits are the same bytes in both rows.
- **`fast` without the flags fails only the `code` category**, as at 64 rows.
- Each gate row took about 7.5 minutes, the two bench rows 3 and 8 minutes. They ran from a clean
  worktree of the commit; a chain started in the main tree had picked up uncommitted kernel code.
