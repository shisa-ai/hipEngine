# Bind dense GGUF launch decisions to profile owners

## Summary

Dense gfx1100/gfx1151 profile binders create independent launch-decision
owners and carry them through GGUF resident session construction. Prefill
scopes bind the owner without retaining live resources in decisions.

## Changes

- Allocate one bounded decision owner for each bound dense generator.
- Transport it with resident-session policy arguments, even when broad Q8T16
  rowtile policy is unspecified.
- Bind ordinary/packed and layer-outer prefill scopes to the session's owner.
- Preserve direct/unbound behavior when no owner is supplied.
