# Optimize gfx1151 standard-Q6 WMMA prefill

## Summary

Rejected a fundamentally different standard-Q6 prefill dataflow for the 24
K5,120/N10,240 recurrent-QKV owners. Reusing weights across four row tiles and
activations across three output tiles remains BF16-bit exact, but loses to the
retained highly parallel 16x16 workgroup at every 512/1K/4K cell. Keep the
standard-Q6 producer byte-for-byte.

## Changes

- Temporarily converted the standard-Q6 WMMA kernel from one 16-column x 16-row
  output tile per wave to a 48-column x 64-row tile. Every output retained the
  same standard-T16 decode, K16 WMMA sequence, and BF16 publication boundary.
- Restored
  `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q6_k_t16_gemv.hip` byte-for-byte
  after the leaf failed. No kernel, symbol, gfx1151 override, registry route, or
  runtime change ships from this unit.
- Added compact rejection evidence at
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-15-gfx1151-qwen38-27b-q6-standard-tiled-rejected.json`.

## Validation

- Two immutable Qwen3.8 recurrent-QKV weights cover 86,016,000 source bytes.
  Three warmups plus 15 alternating HIP event pairs per tensor and row are
  BF16-bit exact, finite, and teardown-clean.
- `blk.0.attn_qkv`: retained/candidate speedup is **0.9606x/0.9028x/0.9506x**
  at M512/M1024/M4096; candidate wall is 4.10%/10.76%/5.19% higher.
- `blk.1.attn_qkv`: **0.9355x/0.9190x/0.9423x**; candidate wall is
  6.90%/8.81%/6.13% higher. The candidate wins only **2/90** pairs overall.
- Source restoration passes `git diff --exit-code`; the failed leaf does not
  justify an expensive full-model shape or natural gate.
