# Gemma 4 expert GEMV production siblings

## Summary

Item 1 of the verify-block work. The selected expert GEMVs scale with lanes,
not distinct experts, and with every lane on one cache-resident expert they are
barely faster than with distinct experts: ALU-bound. The strict Q4T16 kernel
gives each thread one k of a sub-block per step, so every weight re-derives
d*scale and dmin*min; the Q5_1 pack8 kernel re-reads d, m and the high bits per
weight inside a 256-thread tree. Production siblings with one wave per output
tile and eight-input runs per thread decode scales once per eight weights:
gate/up 1.43x/1.59x/1.71x and down 1.18x/1.64x/1.78x at 8/40/72 lanes. They are
offered only on the unpinned MoE route (production); strict pins `grouped`.
Five-row verify 25.9 -> 22.6 ms; MTP 1.44x -> 1.58x at budget 4. Decode is
launch-bound and unchanged.
