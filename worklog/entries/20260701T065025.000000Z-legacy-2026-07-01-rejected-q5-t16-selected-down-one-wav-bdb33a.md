q8_1/dp4a direct kernel:

- `HIPENGINE_GGUF_T16_SELECTED_Q5_DP4A_THREADS={32,64,128}` affects only
  `launch_qk_q8_1_dp4a_direct` (Q5/Q6 selected-down). Unset inherits the

  the retained Q4 scheduler.
