# Surya transcription fixtures: clipping defect, ground truth, and scorer

## Summary

Scoring the intended full-page HTML protocol against the text *drawn* on the
bench pages exposed a latent bug in `scripts/surya_bench_pages.py`: four of
seven pages draw text past the canvas, so their intended text is not what is in
the image. The model was reading correctly and being scored as wrong.

Concretely, on the 512 px bench pages the Japanese body lines are 576-663 px
wide against 480 px of usable width, the mixed-script Latin lines are 503/506 px
against 480, and the degraded-scan lines reach ~531 px against 476. `page_long`
draws six 224 px blocks from y=168 on a 1024 px canvas, so blocks 5 and 6 land
off the bottom edge and are simply not in the image.

Re-rendering the same content and fonts on a canvas where every line fits shows
the model transcribes the Japanese and mixed-script pages **exactly**: recall
1.0, exact 1.0, CER 0.0, zero reading-order violations, `eos`. The 512 px pages
appeared to show CER 0.220 (`ja`) and 0.039 (`mixed`), and the apparent
"hallucinated word" on `mixed` (`line.` -> `literature`) was the model
completing a clipped word.

The bench pages are left byte-identical so their captured oracles and the
committed benchmark artifacts stay valid. Acceptance fixtures are added for the
four broken pages; re-cutting the bench suite is a separate unit.

## Changes

- `scripts/surya_bench_pages.py` — public `GROUND_TRUTH`, `expected_lines`,
  `acceptance_page`; a `--fit` fixture family (`page_<name>_fit.png`) for
  `ja`/`mixed`/`scan`/`long`; and `_draw_text_fit`, which refuses to draw a line
  off the canvas so the fit property cannot silently regress. The 1024x1600
  long page keeps all six blocks on-canvas.
- `scripts/surya_transcription_score.py` — pure scorer: reading order, line
  recall and omissions (presence bar), line exact rate and character error rate
  (correctness), table shape/header/cell accuracy, and truncation, with named
  failure reporting.
- `tests/test_surya_transcription_score.py` (31 tests),
  `tests/test_surya_transcription_fixtures.py` (12 tests).
- `tests/fixtures/surya/page_{ja,mixed,scan,long}_fit.png`.

## Validation

- `pytest tests/test_surya_transcription_score.py
  tests/test_surya_transcription_fixtures.py tests/test_surya_protocol.py` — 62
  green. Scorer RED demonstrated by removing the module (collection error);
  Levenshtein verified against an independent DP.
- Fit pages regenerate byte-identically; the fit assertion fires on the bench
  geometry (`ja` at 512, `long` at 1024), so the defect cannot return silently.
- Bench pages verified byte-identical after the refactor (regenerated to a temp
  dir and compared).
- Measured fp32 on gfx1151, full-page protocol, non-clipping fixtures:
  `ja` and `mixed` recall 1.000 / exact 1.000 / CER 0.000 / 0 order
  violations / `eos`.
