# C8 iter38: direct-load dp4a Q5 built + contracted, leaf screen rejected 0.64–0.72x

## Summary

Implemented the iter37 inspection's named candidate — a direct-load
(LDS-free, barrier-free) dp4a Q5_K MMQ32 kernel — and leaf-screened it
against the retained raw mmq32 owner on actual weights. **The axis is
measured-rejected.**

Built (candidate-only, not wired to dispatch):

- `gguf_k_mmq32_direct_dp4a_q5_kernel<output_t, ROW_TILE=8>` in
  `gguf_k_mmq_prefill.hip`: thread per 4-k group across an 8-column tile,
  u32 ql+qh record loads straight from global, dp4a partials, the Q5_K
  minimum correction taken from the producer's `s4` (the f32 sum of the
  ORIGINAL pre-quantization group values — the integer `Σq8` form is NOT
  equivalent and carries the quantization residual; caught by the parity
  contract at 0.0065 abs on one element), wave-shuffle + cross-wave
  reduction mirroring the proven Q6 grouped-dp4a structure.
- Launchers `gguf_q5_k_mmq32_direct_dp4a_q8_1_d4s4_f32_bf16_{bf16,f32}_out`
  (same ABI as the raw owner) + py wrappers.
- Contract tests `tests/test_gguf_k_mmq_direct_dp4a_q5.py`: 5/5 pass at
  rows 8/24/32 — q8-oracle agreement (rtol 2e-2), raw-owner parity
  (rtol 1e-4), exact-outer KL/top-1 floor, bf16==bf16(f32),
  deterministic repeats, bad-shape rejection.
- Screen script `scripts/qwen38_c8_p2_q5_direct_dp4a_leaf.py`.

Screen (actual ssm_out Q5_K weights, alternating order, 5 samples,
operation-complete incl. quantize): **direct loses on every weight** —
geomean raw/direct 0.64x at R32 and 0.72x at R24 (6 weights; full 48
unnecessary after unanimous early losses). Two variants measured: the
per-thread scale recompute (best) and a pack-lane-0 + `__shfl_up` scale
broadcast (slower: 0.537x R32 — divergent-shuffle form was also
incorrect until made uniform; both facts recorded).

Root cause (consistent with the iter37 inspection): the raw GGUF Q5_K
layout has no record structure permitting coalesced direct loads — each
thread reads 8 columns at weight-row stride (4224 B), so direct loads are
uncoalesced-sector-bound while the retained owners' LDS staging coalesces
through shared memory. This is precisely why the source-layout owner
exists and why the proven 101–134 GB/s schedule family (T16 qmicro
planar) is built on a repacked tile layout.
