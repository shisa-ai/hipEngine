# Promote the router full-block SGEMM tier under the section 2.11 envelope

## Summary

Lead decision (2026-10-09, this session): the strict/exact single-chain
absolute-kl_max reading is not a production gate for reordering-class
arithmetic on routing models, and a routing flip is acceptable within the
section 2.11 damage budget -- "if you are applying strict gates to our
default you are wrong." The 2026-10-08 revert of the router full-block
SGEMM tier (`gemma4-router-sgemm-gfx1151` entry) was judged with the
superseded ruler and is corrected here.

The route change re-landed as the production default: full 1024-row prefill
blocks route through the F32 rocBLAS SGEMM tier on every backend, ahead of
the declared tile schedule (`HIPENGINE_GEMMA4_ROUTER_SGEMM=0` pins the
tile; REFACTOR and ENVS entries added). Evidence for the default:

- Perf: 1.68x per launch at the in-situ geometry on gfx1151 (tile 0.946 ms
  vs SGEMM 0.564 ms, interleaved), the third-largest mid-band prefill
  kernel at 8-10 percent of the step.
- Numerics, section 2.11 envelope on the frozen tile8 baseline
  (2048/1024 chain, 1023 paired rows): median ~1e-6, p99 7.99e-05,
  flip-damage 1/1023 rows = 0.098 percent against the 3 percent budget,
  top-1 agreement 100 percent against the 96 percent floor. The damaged
  row (602, KL 8.54) is a single near-tie routing flip; the next-worst row
  sits at 0.0014. The same tier passed the full teacher-forced gate as the
  gfx1100 production default in P8 (commit f34cdccd5).
- The 18-case section 2.11 packet for the router candidate PASSED
  (benchmarks/results/2026-10-09-gemma4-router-sgemm-section211-packet.json,
  1134 rows): global median 1.21e-07 (bar 1e-3), p90 3.68e-03 (bar 2e-2),
  flip-damage 0.79 percent (bar 3 percent), top-1 98.68 percent (bar 96
  percent); all 18 cases passed with tasks non-inferior, three-run repeats
  and unrelated-request isolation clean; 68.6 percent of rows carry some
  benign layer-level routing difference, matching the packet's own
  calibration history.
