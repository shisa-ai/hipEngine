# T16 natural-input owner gate: 2.17-2.18x on real weights, drift in class

## Summary

The natural-input owner gate for the T16 expert-major route passes decisively
on real weights and real activations. The screen hooked the production
iu8-risk gate/up kernel at its registry key during a canonical code-p4096
prefill, captured the natural call (40,960 compact rows, 512 experts, real
gate/up weights at layers 12/24/46), repacked the same real weights through
the production T16 layout, and ran both arms on identical inputs across six
balanced repetitions per layer:

| Layer | iu8-risk + repair (ms) | T16 dual (ms) | Speedup | rms_abs drift | max_abs |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 12 | 59.38 | 27.22 | **2.181x** | 2.188e-4 | 0.0078 |
| 24 | 59.76 | 27.48 | **2.175x** | 2.152e-4 | 0.0078 |
| 46 | 58.35 | 26.90 | **2.169x** | 5.238e-4 | 0.0625 |

The speedup exceeds the synthetic 4-arm estimate (1.33x on the GEMM alone)
because the natural arm times the complete operation including the repair pass
the T16 route eliminates by construction. Input and raw-weight bytes are
immutable in every capture. The drift sits in the same magnitude class the
MX-1 operand-error budget measured for F16-class routes (which were 8-9.5x
below the BF16 floor already in the path) - the compensated accumulate is
doing its job.
