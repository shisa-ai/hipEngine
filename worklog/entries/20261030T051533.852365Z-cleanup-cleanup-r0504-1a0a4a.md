# Preserve prompt activation claims until all sinks finish

## Summary

R0504 exposed a concrete claim-lifetime defect: partial success or abort released
an activation claim while another sink remained. Keep the claim until the last
sink closes. Co-activation scheduling remains separate deferred work.

## Changes

- Guard claim release in `finish_prompt_streaming` and `_abort_prompt_streaming`
  with the remaining sink map. Preserve conservative resource ownership until
  the entire activation completes.
- Cover split completion with ordinary and released-bulk targets, plus aborts
  of either row and the complete group. Existing joint completion stays covered.
- Move the remaining overlap evaluation to the ledger bottom; preserve
  token-equality, acceptance, cancellation and ownership requirements.
