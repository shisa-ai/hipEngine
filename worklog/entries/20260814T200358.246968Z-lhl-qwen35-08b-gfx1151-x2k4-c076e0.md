# Scope single-kernel attention core

## Summary

X2-K4 rescoped the full-attention decode owner before any kernel rewrite:
the D4-era "5.9x / 153 us per layer" was marker-inflated. The retained
split-K producer + gated reducer pair measures 57 us/layer standalone at
the exact production shape (rows1, 8Q/2KV/D256, ctx 514/641, spans ABI),
i.e. a 2.2x kernel-level distance to Vulkan's 26 us worth roughly
0.15-0.2 ms/token - not the 0.96 ms the ladder estimated. No rewrite was
started at that ROI.

## Changes

- None landed (scoping unit). Bench: /tmp/d08-x/attn_bench.py using the
  production wrapper qwen35_paged_full_attn_decode_split_k_gate_bf16_spans
  with a hand-built KVLiveSpans.

## Validation

- Standalone pair timing (host-synced, 30 calls x 7 medians): ctx 514 ->
  56.5 us, ctx 641 -> 57.1 us per layer; K+V traffic 1.57 MB (28 GB/s,
  latency-bound); Vulkan reference 26 us.
- Census (rocprof on the production bench process): decode attention is the
  generic split_k_ctx_tensor producer (24 blocks) + split_k_reduce_gate
  pair plus write_paged_kv_mixed_value_position and split_qgate, i.e. the
  3-4 launch package D4 retained.
- Cross-check of isolated owner times vs the 8.3 ms/token graph wall leaves
  roughly 2.3 ms/token unattributed (isolated sums ~6 ms incl. dense 2.0,
  linear 1.35, attention 0.5, norms 0.6, LM head 0.9). Isolated microbenches
  are also MALL/L2-flattered by repetition; both effects mean marker-based
  role shares overstate individual owners.
- The remaining decode discrepancy therefore points at per-node graph-replay
  overhead or marker inflation, not at any single 5.9x kernel. Direct
  replay-gap profiling is blocked on this stack by the rocprofv3
  zero-duration issue.
