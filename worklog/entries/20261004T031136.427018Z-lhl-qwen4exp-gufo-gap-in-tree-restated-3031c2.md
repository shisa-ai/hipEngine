# Restate the gufo prefill gap at 5.78x from this tree's own measurements

## Summary

`20261004T024023.708970Z-lhl-gufo-prefill-gap-verified-705a17` states the
Qwen4Exp prefill gap to gufo as **5.17x** and reports a **-8.9% decode
regression** from a larger prefill chunk. Both numbers came from harnesses run
as `python3 scripts/...`, which import the separate `hipEngine-main` clone
rather than this tree
(`20261004T030644.952503Z-lhl-python-env-resolves-stale-clone-573911`).

Re-measured under `uv run`:

- the gap is **5.78x**, not 5.17x;
- the chunk 1024 -> 4096 prefill gain is **+8.41%**, not +13.6%;
- there is **no decode regression**. Decode is 17.40 tok/s at chunk 1024 and
  17.72 at chunk 4096, i.e. **+1.83%**.

The gufo side of the comparison is unaffected: gufo is its own prebuilt binary,
so gufo's 1705.94 tok/s and the source/wall-clock verification of it stand
unchanged. Only the hipEngine rows and the ratio derived from them move.

This entry supersedes the rates and the decode claim in
`20261004T024023.708970Z-lhl-gufo-prefill-gap-verified-705a17`. That entry is
committed and therefore byte-frozen; it was not edited. Everything else in it -
that gufo's `pp4096` is a genuine full-prompt device-synchronized prefill, that
the campaign objective is a kernel-throughput gap and not a measurement
artifact, and that the gap is uniform rather than MoE-specific - is unchanged.

## Limits

- Only `code-p4096` was re-measured in-tree. The other three categories in the
  earlier pass sweep have no in-tree number yet, so the in-tree chunk effect is
  established on one category.
- The chunk-1024 arm has six samples across two arms and the chunk-4096 arm has
  three; the arms were not interleaved within a single process.
- The earlier session's other committed artifacts were not audited for which
  interpreter produced them. The mechanism predates this session and the
  exposure is unknown.
