# PF-5 GDN w32 prefill lever closed as measured loss

## Summary

Lever 1 of PF-5 (GDN M6 port: 32 warps/block = 1024-thread prefill blocks) is
closed as a **measured loss**: w32 is slower than the production columnwarps
<4,4> owner at every shape >= 64 rows (+1.6% to +6.8%), with only a
noise-level -1.6% at rows=16. Parity held bit-exact at all shapes. The M6
mechanism does not transfer to this host; no promotion. Lever 2 (tile-16
state-in-register) is deferred behind PF-0 per the binding T1 escalation rule
in declaration entry 55a7d9.
