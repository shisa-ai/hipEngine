Removed the default-off rolling-slot MTP serving experiment. The measured route
kept at most four slots live but exposed replacement open/prefill wall and
reached only 61.23 tok/s at c8 versus the retained 79.61 tok/s chunked policy.

target verification stays capped/chunked at four and can overlap independent
