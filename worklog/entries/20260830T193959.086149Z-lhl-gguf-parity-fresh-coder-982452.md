# Fresh-coder handoff after full GGUF parity campaign

## Summary

The assignment was to microbenchmark/profile every important Qwen3.8-27B `Q4_K_M` serving
submodule on the W7900 (`epyc`, gfx1100) and make hipEngine match or beat llama.cpp current/Laurent
at C1-C8. The initial rows supplied by the user were:

- AR: `21.871, 30.455, 35.625, 39.231, 41.124, 42.490, 43.570, 44.338` tok/s.
- Explicit diagnostic K3: `31.146, 29.639, 30.547, 31.033, 30.755, 30.248, 30.596,
  30.845` tok/s.

The retained current rows in `benchmarks/README.md` are:

- AR: `21.999, 31.916, 45.309, 54.151, 61.881, 71.226, 74.903, 78.667`.
- Explicit diagnostic K3: `31.455, 39.820, 54.590, 55.780, 47.960, 49.020, 55.225,
  55.860`.
- Prefill: `149.072, 175.905, 212.235, 318.412, 312.682, 347.625, 426.692,
  397.655`.

The objective is **not complete**. AR now beats both peers at 7/8 widths, but C2 is 31.916 vs
llama.cpp current 35.440 (-9.9%). Prefill still loses at C1-C3, C5, C6 and C8 (the material gaps are
C1-C3 plus C6/C8). Diagnostic K3 wins C3/C4, but loses C1/C2 and C5-C8; C8 is 55.860 vs Laurent
101.072. These are measured gaps, not a completion claim.

The largest retained win is grouped prefill, commit `72a29e9ad`:
`GGUF_C2_PACKED_PREFILL_MAX_ROWS = 8` on gfx1100. It moved AR by +3.5% to +72.2% at C2-C8,
made AR lead 7/8 widths, and flattened diagnostic-K3 acceptance to 0.7889 at every width with all
content gates passing. It did not solve the short-prompt fixed cost.

The current product/diagnostic distinction is essential:

- The README K3 row is an **explicit Generation-2 diagnostic** produced with
  `--candidate-budget 3 --mtp-request-mode explicit --generation2-diagnostic`.
- Shipped gfx1100 evidence for this model is only `c2-k2-d24` with
  `max_realized_group_rows=2`.
- Automatic MTP remains unconditionally hard-disabled in `hipengine/server/api.py`; that is a
  product decision, not an evidence-lookup result.
- A K3 invocation without `--generation2-diagnostic` is expected to decline
  `physical_group_not_qualified`; it is not a K3 reproduction.

## Changes

### Retained campaign work

- Tasks #1-#4 created matched profiler/decomposition tooling and the first cross-engine submodule
  table. The durable output is
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-c1c8-submodule-decomposition.json`.
  Its original hipEngine admission/MTP packet predates grouped prefill; use
  `2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-c1c8-admission-decomposition-post-grouping.json` for current AR admission.
- Grouped-prefill promotion and current matrix are in
  `2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-c1c8-hipengine-grouped-prefill-promotion.json`,
  `2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-c1c8-hipengine-prefill-row-grouped.json`, and
  `2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-c1c8-parity-refresh-repeat-pair.json`.
- The genuine repeat pair measures AR run-to-run spread of 0.66%-5.29% and K3 spread of
  0.01%-1.06%; it confirms the winner map without changing the published rows.
- A 512-token comparator found device prefill compute at parity with llama.cpp (~884 vs 881 tok/s).
  The C1-C3 loss is therefore the short-prompt fixed cost, not bulk token throughput.
- C2 decode is not the problem. A kernel trace found C2 decode changes to rowtile owners and
  amortizes well; the retained complete-wall loss is downstream of C2 admission/prefill.
- The K3 accept-cycle accounting is usable after its correction chain: at C5-C8 the per-batch accept
  window is 88.0-104.3 ms, consisting of ~0.2 ms enqueue, 20.3-24.5 ms commit, and 67.5-79.6 ms
  blocking target-accept readback. Device busy is <=55% vs AR ~95%. Cycles/output token are
  width-invariant at 0.296. Remaining designs are software pipelining across cycles or graph capture.
- All-GEMV short-row prefill was measured and rejected (15%-36% slower). The apparent 256-row
  scratch lead was disproved: compute extent is live rows. Mixed-length grouping already ships.
