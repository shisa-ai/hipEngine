# Retain sole-T16 Q4 FFN-down F16 rocBLAS prefill

## Summary

Extended the bounded source-F16 prefill owner from planar Q6T16 to the existing
sole-resident Q4T16 layout, but admitted only dense FFN down. The actual-weight
component screen, complete category quality gate, XTX full-engine matrix, and
W7900 safeguards all pass. Gate/up, attention, decode, MTP, other models, and
peer backends retain exact owners.

## Changes

- Added a Q4T16-to-F16 bounded output-tile producer to the existing Q6 F16 HIP
  family and registered its unfused dequant and composed rocBLAS linear keys.
- Generalized the owner context to carry independent Q4/Q6 shape policies while
  keeping compatibility aliases for the original Q6 API.
- Added the gfx1100 model policy only for K=17408/N=5120 FFN-down at row anchors
  512/768/1024/4096. The candidate casts the dead dense FFN intermediate in
  place and reuses the three liveness-aliased Q6 scratch views, so no resident
  weight or scratch allocation is added.
- Added CPU-reference, registry, bounded-workspace, shape-fallback, and dispatch
  tests. Updated the kernel catalog, rollback debt ledger, and benchmark rollups.

## Validation

- Actual Qwen3.6 Q4T16 FFN-down component speedups are **1.403x/1.341x/1.223x**
  at M512/M1024/M4096 with output tiles 1024/512/512. Gate/up lose or are flat;
  attention roles were not admitted because they are smaller/variable wins and
  need a different scratch lifetime.
- Complete `mtpbench-code-general-ja` teacher-forced gate across 10 prompts,
  four categories, and 330 transitions passes: minimum per-prompt top-1
  **96.97%**, max KL **0.0317583**, deterministic repeats, 640 asserted Q4 and
  1,280 asserted Q6 candidate dispatches.
- RX 7900 XTX full-engine prefill improves the retained Q6-only matrix:
  **780.849 -> 801.326 (+2.62%)**, **839.336 -> 859.484 (+2.40%)**, and
  **827.209 -> 847.283 tok/s (+2.43%)** at 512/1K/4K. Decode remains within
  noise at **33.523/34.514/31.387 tok/s** (-0.21%/-0.03%/-0.02%), final token
  9707 is deterministic, and tracked peaks remain exactly
  **15.605/15.720/16.368 GiB**.
- W7900 safeguards improve prefill **+2.35%/+2.42%/+1.92%** to
  **725.849/777.508/751.982 tok/s** at unchanged tracked peaks; decode remains
  neutral.
- Focused CPU/HIP bundle passes **99 passed, 9 skipped**. `rocprofv3` names 10
  Q4 tile producers (1.451 ms total), 10 rocBLAS consumers (4.251 ms), and 12
  cast calls (0.224 ms); no compiler ran in the profiled process.
- Lineage check reports only the already-known unrelated nano-vllm-amd drift.
