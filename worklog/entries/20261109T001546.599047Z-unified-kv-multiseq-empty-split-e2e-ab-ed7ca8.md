# Empty-split end-to-end A/B: ~1% mixed ITL at 128K, noise at 64K

## Summary

Final end-to-end measurement of the merged empty-split elimination
(`73c8947ce`), closing the follow-up left open by the kernel-level work.
Same-host A/B on the gfx1151 Desktop (stable clocks), Qwen3.8-27B-UD-Q4_K_M,
before = `c62d367a3` vs after = `6a17cbbd1` (runtime diff verified = exactly
the kernel commit; after server ran from the `unified-kv-multiseq` worktree,
cwd checked via /proc). Protocol: speculation off, retained decode profile,
`--prefix-cache off`, seeded counting-tail prompts with `ignore_eos`,
window-average phase timing; arms alternated to cancel drift.

**Option B (64K long + 3 short, 2 reps/arm): effect not resolvable.**
Mixed-phase ITL medians 293.51 -> 294.03 ms (**+0.18%**); controls alone
+1.64%, post -0.14%. Per-rep mixed: before 289.3/297.7, after 291.8/296.3 —
fully overlapping. The expected ~1-2% effect at 64K is below run-to-run noise.

**Option A (128K long + 3 short, 3 reps/arm): ~1% realized.**
Mixed-phase ITL medians 347.46 -> 343.87 ms (**-1.04%, -3.6 ms/token**);
controls alone **0.00%**, post **-0.04%** (flat). Per-rep mixed: before
347.9/347.5/337.7, after 334.0/343.9/343.9. Directionally consistent, small,
and at the edge of resolvability — reported as ~1%, not a precise constant.

Interpretation: the mixed step (~347 ms) is dominated by non-attention work
(dense FFN at batch width 4, short-prefill interleaving); the removed
empty-split attention waste is ~3.6 ms of it. This matches the kernel-level
ceiling estimate and answers the original question: **real but modest in-vivo
uplift (~1% of long-request ITL during co-residency at 128K), not noticeable
at <=64K**. The optimization stays: it removes demonstrated waste with zero
control regression, and its share grows with attention's share of the step.
The kernel probe remains the primary rigorous quantification (-79.8%
worst-case empty-split attention time).

Protocol lessons (each cost a confounded run, now in benchmarks/HARNESSES.md):
SSE streams only non-empty text deltas, so summarize-style prompts finish
early into invisible special tokens and collapse phase counts — use a seeded
counting continuation; median inter-chunk interval is invalid under bursty
SSE — use window averages; prefix cache must be off for identical cold
prefill; speculation must be off.
