# Co-locate packed Inkling per-visit KV and span storage

## Summary

Packed Inkling attention allocates one device buffer per visit instead of five. Key/value rows, offsets, position entries, and the eviction mask retain their separate views and lifetimes under the owning request.

## Changes

- Align the value subrange to 16 bytes, including odd cache geometries, and initialize the same offsets and eviction mask as before. Preserve lazy allocation, stream use, scores, and undo behavior.
