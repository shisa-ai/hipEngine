# Gemma 4 speculative adapter verifies under the verify contract

## Summary

`tests/test_gpu_gemma4_assistant_forward.py::test_the_adapter_cycle_agrees_with_a_token_by_token_decode`
started failing at `eba4d1e55` (bisected: passes at `e8cc517a9`, fails at
`eba4d1e55`): round 1 accepted none of a draft the token-by-token walk accepts
two of. `Gemma4SpeculativeAdapter.verify_and_accept` forwarded the verify block
without `verification=True`, so it ran plain multi-row prefill arithmetic, whose
rows differ from one-token decode by up to ~1.8 logits on Gemma 4 with or
without that change; the dense-kernel change moved a 0.12-logit near-tie. The
verify contract (`verification=True`, exact dense by default) makes the rows
bit-identical to decode. The adapter is used only by this test; the production
MTP provider already verifies with `verification=True`.

## Changes

- `hipengine/runtime/gemma4_speculative.py`: `verification=True` on the verify forward.
