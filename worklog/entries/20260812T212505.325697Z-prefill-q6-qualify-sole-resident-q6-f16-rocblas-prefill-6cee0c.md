# Qualify sole-resident Q6 F16 rocBLAS prefill

## Summary

Qualified and integrated a changed-arithmetic Q6 dense-prefill route for the
sole-resident Qwen3.6-27B planar T16 owner. Actual-weight component screens,
full-category teacher-forced quality, and full-engine W7900/XTX safeguards are
positive. The route is package-default only for the qualified model, shape,
and row intervals; decode and MTP stay exact.

## Changes

- Added a planar-qmicro T16-to-F16 tile producer and registered bounded
  F16/rocBLAS BF16-output linear owner; exact T16 remains every key miss and all
  decode/MTP rows.
- Added model/shape/row/tile backend policy and owner-scoped dispatch. The route
  keeps one persistent T16 weight layout and consumes liveness-aliased F16
  activation/weight/output planes; dense FFN down casts its dead BF16 activation
  buffer in place to preserve the existing 1K/4K arena size.
- Added independent source-byte, actual-shape, registry/fallback, overlap,
  liveness, and lifecycle tests. Excluded the gfx1100-only owner from gfx1151
  aliasing pending an independent gate.

## Validation

- Component screen, RX 7900 XTX actual Qwen3.6 weights, seven paired samples:
  FFN-down 1.55x/1.58x, fused QKV 1.31x/1.35x, and narrow V 3.06x/2.55x at
  M512/M1024; M768/M1280/M4096 also positive for all three roles.
- Production-policy ten-prompt code/general-English/general-Japanese/mixed
  suite: 330/330 teacher-forced top-1 matches, max transition KL 0.0411284,
  byte-exact candidate repeats, and 1,280 asserted candidate dispatches. The
  first auto-policy rerun exposed an exact-row admission miss (KL 0 fallback);
  interval admission was fixed and the explicit dispatch-hit gate rerun passed.
- Focused CPU/HIP bundle over rocBLAS, linear dispatch, Q6 numerical kernels,
  dense scratch liveness, and runner tests passes (97 passed, 9 skipped for
  model-fixture scope); independent planar dequant bytes match source F16.
- Final no-reserve XTX full-engine medians versus the prior exact matrix improve
  prefill 727.961 -> 780.849 (+7.27%) at 512, 785.347 -> 839.336 (+6.87%)
  at 1K, and 779.243 -> 827.209 (+6.16%) at 4K. Decode deltas are within
  noise (+0.25%, -0.04%, and +0.00%); outputs are deterministic and tracked
  residency remains 15.605/15.720/16.368 GiB. W7900 safeguards are positive
  at 512/1K/4K; final 512/1K/4K rows are 709.171/28.428, 759.104/28.986, and
  737.828/26.266 tok/s at unchanged tracked residency; the 4K decode -0.46%
  delta is within the existing safeguard margin.
- `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` names the planar dequant producer and rocBLAS GEMM
  consumers. `rocblas_set_workspace(nullptr, 0)` removes the handle
  default device reserve while preserving the qualified no-workspace GEMMs.
