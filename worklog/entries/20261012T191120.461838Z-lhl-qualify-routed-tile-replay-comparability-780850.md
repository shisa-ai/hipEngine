# Qualify routed tile replay comparability

## Summary

The gufo policy replay is useful but not directly promotable. Gufo's 16..80
objective chooses a column-tile width in its MMQ routed GEMM, whereas hipEngine's
Q4 grouped prefill maps expert rows with `qwen35_moe_mmq32_tile_map` and related
owners. A width-16 gufo-policy winner cannot be translated to a hipEngine tile
choice without reporting both dimensions, padding, and launch counts on the
same bucket histogram.
