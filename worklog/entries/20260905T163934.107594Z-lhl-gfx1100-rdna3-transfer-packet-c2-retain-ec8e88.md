# Retain packet C2: Q8T16 pair rowtile at physical c8 on W7900

## Summary

Retained the exact 128-thread Q8T16 qkv+gate dual-split rowtile col8 pair
owner on `hip_gfx1100` at rows≥8
(`GGUF_Q8_T16_DECODE_PAIR_ROWTILE_MIN_ROWS = 8`, retention commit
`735a4a47d`). The same-host W7900 A/B on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B improved native_c8
275.244→282.160 tok/s (+2.51%) with the candidate sample range fully above
the control range, and widths 2/4/8 stayed exact and repeatable. Rows below
the floor keep the per-row dual owner; the broad all-projection rowtile
route stays rejected per packet C1. Full evidence in
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-05-gfx1100-gguf-q8t16-pair-rowtile-c8-retained.json`.

## Decisions

- The binding gate is the candidate-vs-rollback differential (gfx1151
  precedent). The state/lifecycle differentials are arm-identical on 5/5
  configurations (steady c2/c4/c8, c8 shrink-sparse, arbitrary-c8
  cancel/compact lifecycle): every semantic axis SAME.
- The c8 census at the retention commit shows
  `q8_0_t16_dual_split_rowtile_col8_gemv_kernel` ×27 with uniform launch
  shape (128 threads, VGPR72, LDS512, scratch0), route check passed, exact
  c1/c8 tokens, and zero scalar fallbacks.
- A tracked-clean 18-prompt category+heldout batch-route capture (new
  `q8t16_pair_candidate` profile, `measurement_valid: true`) produces
  byte-identical `candidate_logits_sha256` to the rollback arm across the
  complete matrix (dynamic 8→4→2→1 retirement, sparse c8, 3 deterministic
  repeats), so the capture's nonzero KL surface vs the strict c1 teacher is
  the documented pre-existing packed-vs-teacher divergence, unaffected by
  the flip.
- Gate-script extension lesson: the pair profile must *unset* the
  diagnostic envs, not set them to `0` — an explicit `0` overrides the
  package floor instead of deferring to it. The first capture run with
  env `0` was therefore a rollback-arm capture and is kept as the
  differential reference.
