# TP2 bulk prefill shares the resident GGUF dispatch context per rank

## Summary

Repaired the missing rank-local GGUF linear dispatch context in the TP2 bulk
prefill. The measured first divergence (Q6_K `attn_qkv` projection, rel 3.73e-03
with bit-identical input) was caused by a control defect, not arithmetic drift:
`MlpTP2GenerationSession.bulk_prefill` called the shared resident layer helpers
directly, while `Qwen35GGUFResidentSession` wraps its whole bulk prefill in
eleven session-scoped dispatch owners. Six of those are plain process-global
toggles that participate in `launch_gguf_linear`'s dispatch resolution **and its
dispatch cache key**, so the same Q6_K weight at `rows=64` resolved
`t16_wmma_prefill_bf16_bf16_out` on the teacher and
`t16_gemv_decode_bf16_bf16_out` on the candidate. A CPU dispatch probe over every
layer-0 and shard launch shape identified **`wmma_prefill`** as the only owner
that changes the Q6_K `attn_qkv` leaf; the Q4_K `attn_gate` shape already
resolved its WMMA variant without the context, which is exactly why it had
measured bit-identical. After the fix all **15** layer-0 producer fields are
bit-identical to the teacher. End-to-end the saved failing prompt improves
sharply but the production envelope still fails on both prompts, so the bulk
route stays opt-in and default-off.

## Changes

- `hipengine/runtime/qwen35_gguf_runner.py`
  - New module-level `resident_prefill_dispatch_session(runner, *, prompt_tokens,
    use_wmma_prefill, use_gemv_decode)` — the single source of the six
    **device-free** session-scoped owners (`q8_t16_two_wave_prefill_session`,
    `wmma_prefill_session`, `gemv_decode_session`,
    `q8_t16_dual_wmma_prefill_session`,
    `q4_pack8_dual_wmma_silu_prefill_session`,
    `q4_t16_unequal_pair_prefill_session`). Exported in `__all__`.
  - `Qwen35GGUFResidentSession._run_bulk_prefill_and_sample` now nests that
    helper in its `with (...)` instead of repeating the six owners inline. The
    five device-pointer owners (`_prefill_f16_staging_context`,
    `_q6_integer_mmq_context`, `_iq_dense_mmq_context`,
    `_q8_mmq_prefill_context`, `_q6_f16_rocblas_prefill_context`) stay with the
    session that owns their scratch.
- `hipengine/runtime/gguf_linear.py`
  - Moved the shipped resident prefill policy down next to the toggles it feeds:
    `resident_session_wmma_prefill_default()` (was
    `_resident_session_wmma_prefill_default` in

    `HIPENGINE_GGUF_DIAGNOSTIC_WMMA_PREFILL` escape hatch and same fail-closed
    typo behaviour. `generation/qwen35_gguf.py` now delegates to it.

    `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q8_0_t16_prefill.py`, imported

- `hipengine/distributed/tp2_generate.py`
  - New `use_wmma_prefill` / `use_gemv_decode` constructor parameters on
    `MlpTP2GenerationSession`; `None` resolves to the shipped resident policy
    (`resident_session_wmma_prefill_default()` / `True`). They are read only
    inside `bulk_prefill`; the single-row decode route keeps its explicit
    per-launch `use_gemv_decode=True` arguments and is untouched.
  - `_bulk_attention_layer` and `_bulk_norm_residual_layer` enter
    `resident_prefill_dispatch_session` **inside the per-rank device loop** (the
    owners are process-global, so entering once around the whole multi-rank loop

    chain launches its own GGUF linears through
    `MlpShardRank.forward_partial`, the six owners are device-free and resolve

                {'gemv_decode': True, 'q8_t16_two_wave_prefill': True,
