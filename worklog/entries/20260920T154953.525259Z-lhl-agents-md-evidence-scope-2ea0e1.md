# Split optimization rules out of AGENTS.md and scope the evidence policy

## Summary

Lead-directed restructure of the always-loaded ground rules. Roughly 40% of
`AGENTS.md` was kernel/benchmark-specific, and it was the densest and most
quotable 40%. Agents working on the product surface (server, API, defaults,
feature enablement) were reading a file that is mostly about kernel promotion
gates and generalizing those gates to product decisions. Four observed
consequences, all named by the lead:

1. Product features gated as "unqualified", creating a catch-22 — the gate
   prevents the exercise that would clear the gate.
2. Production-correct optimizations rejected for failing an exactness contract
   they were never required to meet.
3. Candidates that benchmark well through a harness but are never selected by
   the shipping path.
4. Flag proliferation instead of sensible production defaults.

The governing rules for (1) and (2) already existed — `EXECUTION-PROFILES.md`
§1.1 and `AGENTS.md` line 36 respectively — but were buried: §1.1 sits inside a
938-line numerics document that product agents never open, and the "no exactness
contract" rule was bullet 15 of 16 in a summary list, immediately after a bullet
leading with `KL ≤ 0.05 AND top-1 ≥ 90%`. Proximity won. The rules for (3) and
(4) did not exist; `AGENTS.md` previously *legitimized* flag-adding by making
"add a REFACTOR.md entry" the only obligation.

Observed instance of the pattern, not repaired in this unit: the native GPU
sampler is default-on in the PARO generator
(`hipengine/generation/qwen35_paro.py:3938`, `_env_flag(..., default=True)`) and
default-off in the GGUF generator
(`hipengine/generation/qwen35_gguf.py:12214-12216`, which treats an unset value
as off) behind the same `HIPENGINE_QWEN35_NATIVE_SAMPLER` name. The GGUF route
also carries a real defect — packed native sampler capacity 0 — recorded in
`docs/SERVER-DIRECT-PARITY-ROADMAP.md:722` and `docs/REFACTOR.md:4252`. The
blocker was correctly recorded, but the route stayed unreachable long enough for
its own defect to go undetected, which is the catch-22 in its mature form.

Flag surface at the time of this entry: 577 distinct `HIPENGINE_*` names in
`hipengine/`, about 130 mentioned in `docs/ENVS.md`.

## Changes

- Added `docs/OPTIMIZATION.md` (239 lines): the scoped rules for kernel work,
  performance claims, and benchmark rows. Opens by declaring what it does *not*
  govern, and by distinguishing itself from the `OPTIMIZE*.md` candidate boards.
  §1 is a trigger table with an explicit not-a-trigger list. Carries the moved
  evidence policy, anti-gaming rules, correctness gates, promotion rules,
  lineage workflow, profiling recipes, rollup requirements, kernel verification
  tiers, and kernel blockers.
- `AGENTS.md`: added **What Evidence Is For**, a two-column statement of what
  evidence governs (claims, arithmetic promotion, unattended agentic gating,
  one's own tuning choices) and what it does not (feature enablement, whether an
  implemented path may run, product/API/server behavior, the lead's decisions).
- `AGENTS.md`: added **Product Defaults** with four named rules — *Ship it on*,
  *A gate nothing can clear is a bug in the gate*, *Validate the space, not the
  gate point*, *Works in the harness is not works*.
- `AGENTS.md`: promoted "Untested is not failed" from a clause inside a six-line
  bullet to its own top-level ground rule pointing at `EXECUTION-PROFILES.md`
  §1.1.
- `AGENTS.md`: rewrote the flag rule as **Flags are a cost, not a feature** —
  prefer none; a warranted flag defaults to the production behavior; the
  `REFACTOR.md` entry is the price, not the licence.
- `AGENTS.md`: removed the kernel/perf/benchmark bullets, workflow steps,
  verification-tier rows, and blocker rows now owned by `docs/OPTIMIZATION.md`,
  leaving pointers. Added a server/API/product-surface verification tier and a
  "exercise it the way a user reaches it" step to *After Changes*.
- `docs/README.md`: indexed `OPTIMIZATION.md` in Start here, disambiguated it
  from `OPTIMIZE.md`, added a reading path, refreshed `Last updated`.
- `docs/BENCHMARK.md`: repointed its header line at `OPTIMIZATION.md`.

No content was deleted. Every rule in the previous `AGENTS.md` is present in
either the new `AGENTS.md` or `docs/OPTIMIZATION.md`.
