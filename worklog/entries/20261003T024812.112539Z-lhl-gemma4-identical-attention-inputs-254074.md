# Capture identical Gemma attention inputs through public generate

## Summary

A new diagnostic reaches attention through `hipengine.LLM.generate()` on the
saved 8,191-token `general_en_plan` prompt, generates three greedy tokens, and
captures seven distinct prefill/decode geometries. It runs strict attention on
the same Q/K/V/mask pointers into a separate output buffer, verifies that the
selected output bytes are unchanged, and records sampled float64 CPU comparisons.
Raw tensor snapshots stay under `/tmp/`; compact reports are tracked.

The first captured full-attention block has 853,584 differing BF16 elements out
of 4,194,304 versus strict. At its twelve sampled row/head pairs, strict has zero
BF16 disagreements with the rounded float64 reference; WMMA has 886. The
external probability-residual prototype reduces full-block disagreements to
4,799 and sampled reference disagreements to five on identical verified tensors.
It also reduces the captured 8,192-key decode disagreements from 1,598 to eleven.
These are local arithmetic diagnostics, not throughput or full-model quality wins.
The full-model prototype gate still fails as recorded in `ce6adf9b4`.

Examining the largest residual-prototype BF16 differences finds results close to
rounding midpoints. For example, one full-prefill element's float64 reference is
-16.4375146; strict rounds it to -16.5, while the residual prototype writes
-16.375. Another decode example has strict on the opposite side from the nearest
float64 BF16 value. Strict bitwise equality is therefore not the mathematical
oracle. Remaining model-level amplification must be diagnosed rather than
assuming that another precision term will fix it.
