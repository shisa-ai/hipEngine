# Retain exact rocBLAS prefill solution indices

## Summary

Retained zero-workspace rocBLAS FP16 solution indices for five exact effective
GEMM shapes in the bounded Qwen3.6-27B Q4/Q6 source-F16 prefill route. The
policy is owned by the gfx1100 backend package, guarded by the exact rocBLAS
build prefix, and falls back to standard dispatch for every other version,
shape, backend, decode row, and MTP row. It improves full 4K prefill by 1.33% on
XTX and 1.32% on W7900 without changing outputs, decode, or tracked memory.

## Changes

- Extended the torch-free rocBLAS wrapper with explicit solution-index GEMM and
  runtime version reporting while preserving `None` as standard dispatch.
- Added validated effective `(M,K,N)` solution policy transport through the
  caller-owned T16 F16/rocBLAS session and bounded tile launcher.
- Published five gfx1100 shape policies only for rocBLAS
  `5.2.0.dabb6df2b98*`; rejected M4096/K5120/N256 after the long repeat.
- Added focused wrapper/session/backend policy tests and refreshed the benchmark
  artifact, scoreboards, changelog, root export, and campaign status.

  leaves improve 1.30-1.47x; rejected N256 repeats at 0.934x XTX / 1.000x
  W7900.
- Full-prefill A/B, seven pairs: XTX +0.43%/+1.33% and W7900
