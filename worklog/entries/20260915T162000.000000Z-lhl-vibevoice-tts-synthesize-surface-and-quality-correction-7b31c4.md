# The `synthesize` contract lands, and the semantic GEMV's quality objection is disproven

## Summary

Two units. The model contract's user-facing `synthesize(script, speaker_references)`
surface is implemented and reachable as `LLM.synthesize`, with `LLM.reset`. And the
ten-seed quality re-measurement that the previous entry's decision rested on shows
that decision was wrong: the semantic encoder's `rows=1` GEMV dispatch is
quality-neutral, bit-identically so.

## What was built

`hipengine/generation/vibevoice_tts.py` implements the contract table in
`docs/MODEL-VIBEVOICE-TTS.md` over `hipengine.runtime.vibevoice_tts_session`;
`hipengine/models/vibevoice_tts.py` registers `VibeVoiceForConditionalGeneration`
as the `vibevoice` plugin, and `LLM.synthesize` / `LLM.reset` forward to it.

Three things the checkpoint does not supply and the adapter therefore pins:

- **The tokenizer.** The TTS snapshot holds only the three safetensors shards,
  `config.json` and `preprocessor_config.json` — no tokenizer bundle. The text
  tokenizer and the speech control ids come from `microsoft/VibeVoice-ASR-HF`,
  the checkpoint the model was trained against. `TOKENIZER_MODEL_ID` names it.
- **`cfg_scale`.** `config.json` carries no `cfg_scale` key, so this is a request
  parameter rather than a checkpoint value. The frozen oracle request uses 1.3,
  which is the default.
- **The diffusion step count.** This is *not* a request parameter: the spec reads
  `ddpm_num_inference_steps` (20) from the checkpoint, and the adapter refuses to
  construct if the solver's spec disagrees with the config.

`VibevoiceTtsSession.reset()` was added: both KV caches, the codec's streaming
state, the semantic cache, the negative branch and the random stream. It stores
the construction seed so a reset restores the stream a fresh session would have.

## Evidence

`tests/test_live_vibevoice_tts_synthesize.py`, 8 tests on the pinned
single-speaker request:

- The adapter's prompt is **121 tokens**, matching the oracle manifest's recorded
  value for that request, so tokenizer and speaker formatting agree with the fork.
- `eos` status, 24 chunks of exactly 3200 samples, `chunk_samples` summing to the
  output length, `output_seconds` consistent with it, RMS 0.056 and peak 0.35.
- **A request is reproducible at a fixed seed and differs at a different one.**
  This is the per-request RNG ownership the contract requires; it fails if the
  voice-prompt VAE draw and the diffusion frames do not share one request-owned
  stream.
- `reset()` returns the session to its initial state: two requests separated by a
  reset are bit-identical.
- Cancellation returns `cancelled`, the audio produced so far, and fewer chunks;
  an immediate cancel returns zero chunks and still reports `cancelled`.
- Argument validation rejects empty scripts, 0 or 5 references, a bare 1-D array,
  and a non-24 kHz reference rate.

`uv run pytest tests/test_live_vibevoice_tts_synthesize.py
tests/test_unit_vibevoice_join_checks.py` -> **14 passed**.

`tests/test_unit_vibevoice_join_checks.py` (7 tests, no GPU) drives the new
boundary check with synthetic waveforms: a smooth signal passes, a count that does
not add up and a partial frame are reported, a click at a boundary fails while the
same click inside a chunk does not, and a duplicated sample is reported as the
opposite signature.

## The correction

The previous entry on this branch recorded that routing the semantic encoder's
`rows=1` GEMMs to the per-output GEMV "degrades quality — 15/18 with `two-2turn`
failing on two seeds including a 0.647-WER runaway" and reverted it on that basis.

Re-running the suite over **ten seeds** with the dispatch on and with it off gives
**bit-identical outcomes**:

| State | Passed | Failing request-runs |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| dispatch on | 58/60 | `two-2turn` seed 20260919 (WER 0.1176), `two-long` seed 20260918 (attribution) |
| dispatch off | 58/60 | `two-2turn` seed 20260919 (WER 0.1176), `two-long` seed 20260918 (attribution) |

Same seeds, same WER, same attribution failure. The dispatch is quality-neutral.
The 15/18-versus-17/18 difference it was blamed for came from `--phase score`
re-scoring PCM left over from pre-change code, which the previous entry had itself
identified as the measurement bug but then attributed to the wrong change.

The dispatch stays reverted, but for an honest reason rather than that one: its
speedup has never been measured and retained on this host. The only numbers were in
a working-tree comment. It is recorded in `docs/REFACTOR.md` as a candidate to
restore with a same-host A/B and a numerical-envelope check, **not** on the
disproven quality objection.

## The ten-seed quality result

58 of 60 request-runs pass. Both failures are at the edge of their gates:

- `two-2turn` measures 0.118 WER against the 0.10 gate on one seed; its other nine
  measure 0.0 to 0.059, and the excess is a single word.
- `two-long` misassigns one 1 s window inside the first turn on one seed, so the
  encoder attribution sees a spurious flip. Its transcript is unaffected and its
  other nine seeds reproduce `[0,1]` cleanly.

Every request reaches EOS, nothing clips, the longest internal gap is under 1 s, no
repeated 4-gram appears, and all 60 runs are sample-count continuous with whole
codec frames and no boundary click (largest boundary step 0.64x the interior p99.9
step).

The earlier 0.353-WER failure that the previous entry established as a genuine
synthesis defect does not reproduce here: the same request measures 0.118 at its
worst seed.
