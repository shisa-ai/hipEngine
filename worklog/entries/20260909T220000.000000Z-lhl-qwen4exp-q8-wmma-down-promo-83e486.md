  rejected Q5_K bundle sat at 1.2e-3; promoted gr-iu8 at 7.4e-8), top-1

  routes bit-deterministic.

  1024, balanced six-slot arms): prefill geo-mean **+8.44%** with every
  case winning (+7.65..+9.59); decode neutral (-0.31%). Cross-mode output
  digests diverge on 8/12 cases as expected for a T1 route (free-running
  decode divergence; gr-iu8 precedent 10/12); the declared production
  numerical gates above are the acceptance evidence per the harness
  contract.

  Q8_0-down layer) under the production profile with the env unset.
