## The two negative results that matter

1. **TP2 is not deployable on this host as a full model.** Host-staged
   collectives at 6-8 GB/s make the per-token weight pass the floor, and
   1.03 ms of pure weight traffic per token plus ~1.3-1.5 ms of chain time
   cannot be recovered by halving weight bytes. The break-even projection was
   recorded before any engine integration, so the packet stopped at the
   decision point instead of building a slower configuration.

2. **Link width is not the limiter.** Both cards train at PCIe 4.0 x16 under
   load. The `x8` reading in `pp_dpm_pcie` is a DPM capability table. Peer DMA
   is blocked by the 256 MB BAR and by ACS redirection, not by width.

## Follow-on scope (Packet 3 and later)

- The manifest hash is content-bound now, so serving evidence can identify a
  shard layout from the artifact alone.
- A TP2 MTP speedup claim needs a matched no-MTP AR baseline and the full
  mtp-bench category suite on this host; the retained W7900 MTP row is not that
  baseline.
- Direct peer DMA or a smaller collective payload (per-layer overlap, quantized
  gradients) is the only route that changes the break-even conclusion; the
  break-even model in `scripts/tp_break_even.py` takes bandwidth as an input, so
  a re-measurement after either change is a one-command rerun.
