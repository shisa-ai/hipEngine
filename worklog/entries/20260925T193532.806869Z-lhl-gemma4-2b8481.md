# Iteration 19: decode attention is 53% of the token, not the MoE; two cost hypotheses closed by measurement

## Summary

The campaign has been optimizing the wrong kernel. A decode-phase kernel trace
(rocprofv3, two runs diffed so the 1024-token prefill cancels) puts
**`gemma4_attention_decode_kernel` at 24.0 ms of the 45.7 ms token (53%)**;
the MoE decode linears the campaign's G3 line pursued are **6.2 ms combined
(13.6%)**, dense projections 3.9 ms, norms 1.4 ms, and a further **8.0 ms is
host dispatch across 1116 launches per token (7.2 µs each)**.

Two candidate explanations for the attention cost were closed by measurement,
and the change that tested one of them was reverted:

- **Not memory-bandwidth-bound.** An all-zero keep-mask removes every K/V load
  while leaving the tile loop and reductions intact; it is only 11% faster
  (sliding/1024: 497.9 vs 558.8 µs) and 36% faster (full/1024: 756.8 vs
  1184.7 µs). Issued traffic runs at 46-81 GB/s, ~5-8% of peak.
- **Not barrier-bound.** The launcher's tile selection started its candidate
  loop at 8, so its `case 16`/`case 32` entry points were unreachable. Widening
  the ceiling to 32 and sweeping the whole curve at sliding/1024 gave:
  tile 1 = 817.3, 2 = 701.1, 4 = 633.6, **8 = 598.5**, 16 = 1076.8,
  32 = 722.5 µs. Four times fewer barrier rounds is *slower*; tile 8 was
  already the optimum. Reverted; decode parity re-run green.

The mechanism is **latency-bound execution at 16-block occupancy**: one
256-thread block per (token, head), a 16-block grid on a 96-CU GPU, and a
2-byte dependent global load per thread per key. The lever is memory-level
parallelism, not byte count and not barrier count.

Traced total 37.7 ms/step against 45.7 ms/step wall: the 8.0 ms difference is
host dispatch for **1116 launches per step (7.2 µs each)**, a second independent
target. The launch count is the trace delta over 24 forwards; the gap is
`(wall - traced) / launches`.

The 29 MoE-layer counts and the 205 dense launches match the earlier probes,
which cross-checks the method. Iteration 15's "9.15 + 5.15 ms/step" MoE
figures are the same two kernels seen from a different trace; the campaign
doc's G3 framing of the MoE decode path as the dominant cost does not survive
this profile.

## Why the change was reverted rather than kept

The tile-ceiling patch is a measured regression (598.5 → 722.5 µs sliding,
1195 → 1530 µs full at keys=1024) and the knob was already at its optimum. The
worktree is back to the base commit's kernel; `tests/test_gpu_gemma4_attention_decode_parity.py`
(37 tests, bit-exact decode-vs-block parity) passes on the reverted tree.
