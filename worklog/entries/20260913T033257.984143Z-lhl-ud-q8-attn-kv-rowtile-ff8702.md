# The Q8_0 attention K/V projections leave the prefill WMMA owner at verifier rows

## Summary

UD MTP B3 rises **45.973 -> 46.192 tok/s (K_M, +0.5%)** and **46.990 -> 47.023
(K_S, +0.1%)**, at flat AR (31.934 -> 31.909 and 31.358 -> 31.313), with
MTP/AR **1.4396 -> 1.4476x** (K_M) and **1.4985 -> 1.5017x** (K_S). The
plain-control arms moved -0.31% and +0.18% over the same interval, which bounds
the run-to-run spread at about a third of a percent, so the K_M move is at the
edge of separation and the K_S move is inside it.

The attributable change is not at that granularity. On the verifier itself the
Q8_0 family drops **0.900 -> 0.404 ms/step (-0.495, -55%)** at an unchanged
84 in-window calls per step, and the per-call cost of the work in question falls
**122.6 -> 51.8 us (2.37x)**. That is the measurement this unit retains.

## How the census found it

The 2026-09-13 UD-Q4_K_M verifier census attributed kernels to the twelve
`gguf_mtp_verify_block_N` marker ranges rather than dividing the whole trace by
the step count. That distinction matters: `gguf_q8_0_t16_prefill_wmma` has two
instantiations in the trace, and only one of them is verifier work.

| kernel | gridX | wgX | gY | in-window calls | ms/step | us/call | out-window calls |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `q8_0_t16_dual_split_gemv` | 768 | 128 | 3 | 576 | 0.8754 | 18.2 | 144 |
| `gguf_q8_0_t16_prefill_wmma` | 1024 | **32** | 1 | 84 | **0.8581** | **122.6** | 29 |
| `q8_0_t16_gemv` | 8192 | 128 | 1 | 36 | 0.0419 | 14.0 | 9 |
| `gguf_q8_0_t16_prefill_wmma` | 96 | **32** | 1 | **0** | 0.0000 | - | 96 |

The `gridX=96` instantiation runs **only outside** the verifier windows, so it
is prefill cost. The `gridX=1024` instantiation runs 84 calls per step inside
them. Decoded against the model's Q8_0 tensor histogram - `attn_v` (1024, 5120)
x 8, `attn_k` (1024, 5120) x 2, `ssm_alpha` (48, 5120) x 48, `ssm_beta`
(48, 5120) x 48 - `gridX=1024` is 32 blocks of 32 threads at `tile_m=32`, i.e.
the ten `attn_k`/`attn_v` projections.

So the verifier was running those ten projections through a **prefill** owner
sized for prefill: **one wave32 per block, 32 blocks total, at most 1024
threads on a 96-CU part**, at 122.6 us per call. On 5.571 MB tensors that is
**45 GB/s**. This is the same defect the Q5_K gate/up pair row gate removed one
day earlier, found the same way, on a different owner family.

## The change

`_use_q8_t16_all_rowtile` previously admitted only
`in_features == _Q8_T16_QWEN35_ATTN_IN` (2048), which is why setting
`HIPENGINE_GGUF_Q8_T16_ROWTILE_ALL=1` had been a measured no-op on these shapes.
It now also admits an explicit `(in_features, out_features)` shape set,
`_Q8_T16_ALL_ROWTILE_SHAPES = {(5120, 1024)}`, which routes to
`gguf_q8_0_t16_gemv_decode_rowtile4_bf16_bf16_out` - a 128-thread owner that
reads each tile once for four rows, which is the right geometry for three
verifier rows. The set has its own env override
(`HIPENGINE_GGUF_Q8_T16_ROWTILE_SHAPES`) for bisection and is deliberately
**not** the broad boolean.

The broad boolean is rejected for two independent reasons and stays off. First,
audit packet C1 measured it as a regression on gfx1100 for the decode widths
(Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, c2 -2.94%, c4 -1.04%, fully separated sample ranges), and the
rejection is encoded in the package. Second, this unit measured it: with the
broad boolean the generated token sequence **diverges** from the baseline at
step 4, because the broad guard also reaches the `ssm_alpha`/`ssm_beta`
single-projection launches, whose arithmetic then changes. The shape set does
not reach them.

## Measurement

Census, physical GPU1 (RX 7900 XTX, gfx1100), both arms, 985 calls/step each:

```

  python scripts/gguf_mtp_verifier_rocprof.py \
    --model /models/gguf/Qwen3.8-27B-UD-Q4_K_M.gguf --quant gguf_ud_q4_k_m \
    --mode block-verify --block-rows 3 --block-verify-mode native \
    --native-spec-target-cycle --steps 12 --warmup 3
```

| kernel | baseline ms/step | after ms/step | delta |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `gguf_q8_0_t16_prefill_wmma` (gridX=1024) | 0.8581 | **0.0000** | -0.8581 |
| `q8_0_t16_rowtile_gemv` (gridX=8192) | 0.0000 | **0.3627** | +0.3627 |
| `q8_0_t16_dual_split_gemv` | 0.8754 | 0.8856 | +0.0102 |
| `q8_0_t16_gemv` | 0.0419 | 0.0421 | +0.0002 |

The Q8_0 family therefore drops **-0.495 ms/step** at an unchanged 84 in-window
calls, from 2.0% to 0.9% of verifier kernel time.

The aggregate is 42.872 -> 42.537 ms/step (-0.335) and 48.082 -> 47.873 host
(-0.209). The gap between -0.495 and -0.335 is drift: the seven unrelated
kernels in the same comparison moved +0.002 to +0.045 ms/step, summing +0.155,
and an identical-configuration repeat of the baseline (`census-ud-base2`)
measured -0.106 ms/step of aggregate drift with individual kernels moving
+/-0.03. The family-level number is the one outside the noise band, which is why
it is the retained claim.

Paired protocol, same host, c1 / natural25 / B3, ten prompts, two repeats,
recorded production graph replay for the true-AR arm:

| Tier | AR | MTP B3 | MTP/AR | UD/plain AR | UD/plain MTP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `UD-Q4_K_M` | 31.909 | **46.192** | **1.4476x** | 0.858x | 0.731x |
| `UD-Q4_K_S` | 31.313 | **47.023** | **1.5017x** | 0.789x | 0.708x |

All four arms are `complete_exact`, generated-ID exact across two deterministic
repeats, GPU/CPU acceptance agreement, `timing_evidence_valid`, binding gates
passed.

The K_M/K_S asymmetry is explained by the shape count, not by measurement
quality: `UD-Q4_K_M` carries ten `(1024, 5120)` Q8_0 projections (8 `attn_v` +
2 `attn_k`) and `UD-Q4_K_S` carries three (2 `attn_v` + 1 `attn_k`).

## Correctness

Three independent checks, all passing:

1. **Generated tokens are bit-identical.** The smoke emits twelve token IDs. The
   changed route reproduces the baseline sequence exactly
   (`24, 13, 271, 9764, 579, 1436, 1204, 567, 628, 615, 1017, 13`), and a
   baseline repeat produced the same sequence, so the comparison is against a
   bit-deterministic baseline rather than a lucky one. The broad-boolean variant
   of the same change diverged at step 4, which is how the scope of the guard
   was settled.
2. **The section-6.1 teacher-forced gate improves on every metric.**
   `scripts/ud_mtp_ar_verify_numerics_gate.py --prompt-tokens 64 --budgets 1,2,3
   --repeat-runs 2 --require-native-graph`, 18 prompts, 162 rows:

   | Artifact | mean KL | p95 | p99 | max | top-1 | deterministic |
   | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
   | before | 3.92e-05 | 1.88e-04 | 4.06e-04 | 4.06e-04 | 1.0000 | yes |
   | after | **3.02e-05** | **1.23e-04** | **2.81e-04** | **3.66e-04** | 1.0000 | yes |

   Limits are 1e-03 mean and 5e-02 max, so the worst margin improves from 24.6x
   to 32.0x on the mean. The reduction is consistent with the replaced owner
   being the less accurate of the two: the WMMA prefill path and the rowtile
   GEMV path are bit-identical in output here, but the prefill owner's
   accumulation was contributing more drift to the AR-versus-verifier residual.
3. **Narrow test bundles pass.** `tests/test_qwen35_gguf_q8t16_rowtile_policy.py`,
   `tests/test_gguf_q8_0_t16_gemv_decode.py`,
   `tests/test_qwen35_gguf_dense_q8_dp4a_routing.py`,
   `tests/test_mtp_q8_0_gemv_adapter.py`,
   `tests/test_gguf_q8_0_t16_wmma_prefill.py`,
   `tests/test_gguf_q8_0_wmma_prefill.py`,
   `tests/test_gguf_q8_0_wmma_prefill_dual.py`,
   `tests/test_qwen36_gguf_gfx1100_profiles.py`,
   `tests/test_gguf_q8_0_raw_to_t16.py` -> 178 passed.
