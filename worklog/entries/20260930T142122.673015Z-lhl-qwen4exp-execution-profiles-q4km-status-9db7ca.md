# The normative profile contract gains the decision section it claimed to have, and q4_k_m's numerical failure is recorded there

## Summary

`docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` §2.1 lists seven `(model, backend, quant)`
combinations that resolve to `production` when the profile is omitted, and
states that **"Each one is covered by a decision section below."** For the two
`qwen4_exp_gguf`/`hip_gfx1151` entries that was false: `qwen4_exp` appeared in
the file only in those two list lines, and no section §2.2-§2.8 mentioned it.

The gap mattered more than a missing cross-reference, because today's gate on
`gguf_q4_k_m` showed its production composition **fails the calibrated
envelope** while §2.1 lists it as default-eligible with no qualification. A
reader of the normative contract would have taken the shipped default for a
certified plan.

## The new §2.9, and what it records

Two quants, two different statuses, both measured:

**`gguf_ud_q4_k_xl`** — the arithmetic-recovery composition (fifteen unqualified
selectors disabled before resources are configured). mean/p95/p99/max KL
`3.065e-4/1.785e-3/4.271e-3/7.771e-3`, top-1 `0.99556` (448/450), **no scope
failures and no row over the `2e-2` review boundary**, `prefill_last` /
`prefill_to_c1` bit-identical at mean KL `0.0`. Gate status is
`requires_task_review` on the task axis — 15 of 18 strict-exact task outputs
with three deterministic cross-route divergences — so the section says
"numerical gates pass; task gate requires review" rather than claiming a clean
pass. Manifest `a55324f9…`, unchanged by later GDN work.

**`gguf_q4_k_m`** — the unrecovered composition, and it **fails**: max KL
`0.05958` against `0.05` on one `heldout_general_ja_speculative` row (top-1
unchanged, strict margin `0.9091`, top-5 overlap `1.0`), `prefill_last` mean KL
`1.333e-3` against `1e-3` (18 rows, max `8.572e-3`), five scope failures, gate
status `failed`, with all 1350 dispatches of its own kernel. The section records
the attribution control that shows this is the composition's result rather than
a route's — pinning the GDN prefill owner to the superseded
`qwen4exp_gdn_tiled16_prefill` fails the same five scopes with a worse tail (max
KL `0.06645`, `prefill_last` mean `1.475e-3`) under the byte-identical strict
teacher — and the 2026-09-14 artifact that measured the same composition at max
KL `0.054642` and stated the decision to leave it unchanged.

**The caller-facing consequence is stated plainly:** the `qwen4_exp_gguf` plugin
declares `default_quant = "gguf_q4_k_m"` (`hipengine/models/qwen4_exp.py:16`),
so loading a UD-Q4_K_XL GGUF with the quant omitted resolves to the composition
that fails the envelope, and the in-envelope arithmetic requires naming
`quant="gguf_ud_q4_k_xl"`.

## No default was changed, and the section says why

The two profiles differ by a measured **40-55% of prefill** (`-40.94/-41.89/
-54.53%`) and `-12.60/-12.43/-36.88%` decode, so the choice between
"in-envelope at recovery cost" and "out-of-envelope at full speed" is a product
policy call, not a documentation correction. What this unit fixes is the
contract's claim about the combination, not the combination.
