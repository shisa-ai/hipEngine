# Concurrency2 C2-6 implementation closure and product blocker

## Summary

C2-6 implementation is complete enough to unblock C2-7 host/backend work, but full product-load promotion remains blocked. Global/native short concurrency, actual long context, mixed membership, isolated pressure, changing-page graph replay, and focused static SLO gates pass. The canonical tuning/load/overload/soak packet repeatedly faults the W7900 GPU during high-count oracle ownership before tuning and cannot be retained.

## Changes

- Stable `GlobalDeviceKVPool`/`KVStorageView`, batch-shaped slot views, exact physical-c2 lowering, direct-top1 c1 scope, owner tuple flushing, and same-round decode fairness.
- Sole-driver idle pool generation rebuilds and per-child retryable admission rejection.
- Graphs fail closed for c1 edges and multi-physical-group rounds sharing owner workspaces; sole c1 and single-group c2 graph routes remain.
- Direct benchmark invocation is worktree-pinned. Exact oracle ownership supports bounded per-process partitioning and digest merge, although the hardware campaign remains unstable.

## Blocker

Five canonical production attempts fail before candidate reporting with `Memory access fault by GPU node-1: Page not present or supervisor privilege`. All 13 oracle IDs complete, but teardown/handoff faults; in-process owner separation, process isolation, and 4/4/4/1 partitioning did not yield a stable measured owner after repeated faults. A later four-key worker also stalled after the accumulated GPU faults. Per focused-repair policy, no more expensive broad retries are justified in this GPU generation.

No gfx1151 device, vLLM, or SGLang is available. llama.cpp HIP exists but its matched rerun was stopped once the local generation became hardware-unstable.
