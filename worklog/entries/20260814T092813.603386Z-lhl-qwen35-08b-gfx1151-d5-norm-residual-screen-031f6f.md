# Select 0.8B wave-shuffle norm route

## Summary

Selected combined fixed-hidden-1024 wave-shuffle RMSNorm plus add+RMSNorm C for
one D5 full-model gate. It improves the complete 48-node package 1.229x,
projects 0.12570 ms / 1.511% graph saving, is actual-weight bit-exact, and adds
no bytes or nodes.

## Changes

- Added separately registered fixed c1/hidden-1024 standalone and unrounded-add
  norm candidates. Local256 caches four values/thread, uses two wave32 shuffle
  reductions, eight shared wave sums, and two block barriers.
- Added RED/GREEN for registry and pre-load validation, current-t256 GPU
  comparison on random/finite BF16-edge inputs, and independent CPU oracles.
- Screened exactly A) 24 standalone candidates, B) 24 add+norm candidates, and
  C) combined over every actual norm weight; generic t256 remains fallback.
- Published the screen artifact and updated campaign/benchmark/kernel docs.

## Validation

- RED failed on absent wrappers/keys. GREEN passes 2 focused tests; the 17-test
  affected gguf-ops and prior staged-rejection bundle passes.
- All 24 standalone norms, 24 add norms, and 24 residuals are bit-exact and
  finite. Independent CPU gate: 48 rows, max KL 2.84e-5, top-1 100%.
- A: 1.118x, 0.07115-ms / 0.855% projection, 4/5 blocks; rejected alone.
- B: 1.133x, 0.07923-ms / 0.952%, 5/5; rejected alone below 1%.
- C: 1.229x, 0.67405 -> 0.54834 ms, 0.12570-ms / 1.511% projection,
  17/20 samples and 5/5 blocks; selected.
- `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` records both expected template names at grid/local
  256/256, LDS512, scratch0, VGPR16/24, SGPR128. Timestamps are zero under the
  known gfx1151 profiler blocker; device-clock screen timings are plausible.
