# Reject busy-card external benchmark admission

## Summary

The nasone32 survey's adaptive and shape attempts started with substantial
pre-existing GPU1 VRAM. Reject their timing evidence and add a startup
admission guard to prevent accepting the same condition silently.

## Changes

- Default external raw-suite admission requires <=128 MiB whole-card VRAM.
  Explicit --idle-limit-mib supports other declared idle baselines.
- /tmp/nasone-adaptive.json started at 1.6357 GiB and ended at 4.4847 GiB;
  /tmp/nasone-shapes.json started at 7.6771 GiB. Their timing is contaminated,
  not evidence of adaptive regression or engine speed.
- No foreign processes were terminated. Later capacity attempts started at
  the normal 0.0261 GiB baseline. The guard checks startup only and does not
  prove exclusivity throughout execution.
