# Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_S evidence link pointed at a superseded artifact

## Summary

Follow-up to the Qwen3.8-27B rollup audit
(`worklog/entries/20260911T212611.068301Z-lhl-qwen38-27b-benchmark-rollup-audit-78625e.md`),
found while resolving whether the gfx1151 `Q4_K_S` lane had a missing
speculative-decode row. It does not: `Q4_K_S` is published in its own dedicated
section rather than in the cross-platform MTP scoreboard, and the topline cell
(23.9) matches the closure. But that section's evidence list pointed its B3
citation at the wrong artifact.

## Changes

- `benchmarks/README.md`, "Radeon 8060S: Qwen3.8-27B Dense GGUF" evidence list:
  the published mini-table rows are **true AR 13.36641** and **B3 23.85263
  (1.7845x)**, and both live in `2026-08-17-gfx1151-qwen38-27b-q4ks-g6-closure.json`
  (`exact_b3_result.unsampled`: `true_ar_tok_s` 13.366409, `b3_tok_s`
  23.852634, `b3_vs_true_ar` 1.784521). The list instead annotated
  `[exact B3]` as if it carried those rows, but that artifact contains a
  *different* pair — true AR 13.27259 and B3 24.19347 (1.82281x) — and none of
  the published values. The `exact B3` artifact is the earlier task-23
  retention measurement on a source that predates the commit
  `4aa4bb65 fix: make Qwen3.8 Q4_K_S native B3 exact`; the G6 closure is
  task-24 at that commit and at `20e5106d` (`source_commit`), and its `decision`
  names 23.85263/1.7845x as the closure B3. The list now names the G6 closure as
  the source of the true-AR and B3 rows and marks `exact B3` as the earlier
  superseded measurement. No published number changed.
