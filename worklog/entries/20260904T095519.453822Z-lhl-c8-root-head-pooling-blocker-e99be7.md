# c8: root-head rows-pooling blocked by cycle serialization and rowtile-8 geometry

## Summary

Resolves the iter44 recorded blocker question ("whether the root hidden is
available at the verify batch point, what orders the root-head call in the
cycle, and what it depends on") for the root full-vocab head rows=1 call
(6.242 ms/cycle at 167 GB/s, 1,042.9 MB/call). The rows-pooling lever is
closed by cycle serialization plus the measured rowtile-8 geometry ceiling.

## Code-grounded findings

- Call sites: `_sample_device_from_hidden`
  (hipengine/runtime/qwen35_gguf_runner.py:23252) launches the rows=1
  `launch_gguf_linear` + `argmax_f32` pair; it is invoked from
  `step_async_top1` (:20709, AR serving slots) and the verify-block per-row
  fallback loop (:19727, rows outside 2-8). The verify batch path itself
  routes rows 2-8 through `_verify_lm_head_rowtile_chunked` (:26247, iter44
  retained route).
- Root hidden availability: `_last_target_hidden_ptr` (:16820) holds the
  final trunk hidden row, is set from the provider catch-up root hidden
  (qwen35_gguf_mtp2.py:4153/4298), and is documented valid "until the next
  target forward overwrites its scratch" — i.e. the root hidden IS resident
  at the verify batch point.
- Ordering dependency: the root head's sample output is the committed root
  token; proposal generation conditions on that token, so the root-head call
  is a serial cycle dependency and cannot be deferred to (or merged with) the
  verify batch, which runs after proposals exist.

## Why the lever cannot close further

1. Pooling the root row with the proposal-batch head call (rows 2-8 → 3-9)
   requires a rows=9 single launch: measured-blocked (iter45/46 — the Q6
   rowtile primitive max_rows is 8, the 16-col single-read shape is
   occupancy-starved at 0.81-0.82x, and the chunk-interleaved single launch
   measured 0.36-0.73x).
2. `_small_b_rowtile_chunks(9, max_chunk=8)` = [8, 1]: the 1-row tail falls
   back to the rows=1 decode kernel, giving 2.5 + 6.242 ms — arithmetic-
   neutral vs separate calls (no win by construction).
3. The rows=1 kernel at 167 GB/s is the measured floor for a single-row
   weight traversal (no cross-row reuse exists at rows=1); the registered
   dp4a top1 fused path (rows==1 only, env-gated off) removes ≤0.76% of call
   traffic — not material by the iter30 criterion.
