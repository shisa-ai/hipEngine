# R6: MoE decode owner split and next decode target selection

## Summary

Split the decode MoE owner into subowners and quantified the exclusive
host/launch overhead bucket, from the post-GR family capture's decode
windows (code-p4096, fixed-live4097, average of three repetitions, six
promotions active at 3e6be5848).

Per-transition wall 54.6 ms (TG 18.31 tok/s), device 49.3 ms, so the
**measured host/launch overhead bucket is 5.3 ms/token (9.7% of wall)**.
Launch structure: ~1700 hipLaunchKernel calls carry 31.2 ms of device
work; the MoE block (18.1 ms) is already hipGraphLaunch'd.

MoE subowners (18.09 ms total): expert gate/up Q4_K dp4a dual
**5.68 ms** (42 calls, ~135 us each), expert down/combine Q5_1 weighted
sum **5.60 ms** (43 calls), shared-expert block 6.19 ms (pack8 gate/up
1.85 + dense down 1.43 + selected prefill 1.39 + Q4 dual-silu variant
1.52), router 0.33 ms, quantize/cast 0.29 ms.

Linear family (24.73 ms incl. the GR projection legs): attn_qkv 5.05,
lm_head 3.25, attn_gate 3.12, ssm_out 3.10, attn_q 1.98, GR down legs
3.63, GR up legs 2.08. GDN core 2.44, QSA decode 2.69, GR epilogue 0.86,
boundary 0.49 ms.

Vulkan comparator gaps (device): MoE +5.6 ms, linear+GR +3.2 ms, QSA and
GDN faster than Vulkan.

## Selected next decode target

**Decode graph expansion beyond the MoE block.** The exclusive measured
overhead bucket exists (5.3 ms/token wall-minus-device), the launch
structure is quantified (~1700 individually-launched kernels), the
change carries no numerical risk (no arithmetic reordering), and the
estimated recoverable cost is 3-5 ms/token (+6-9% TG). Second target:
the MoE expert GEMV pair (11.3 ms today, ~5.6 ms at Vulkan parity).
