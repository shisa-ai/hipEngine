# Build and reject grouped Qwen4Exp prefill candidate

## Summary

Built a default-off grouped/WMMA/peer Qwen4Exp prefill candidate that raises
warm 512-row structural throughput from 8.67 to 211.76 tok/s and the complete
18-prompt natural suite from 5.36 to 35.75 tok/s. The candidate is rejected for
promotion because its full-suite production numerical gate fails.

## Changes

- Added grouped MoE scratch ownership and reused the in-tree Qwen3.5 count,
  prefix, scatter/gather, WMMA tile-map, Q4 compact dual WMMA, and grouped
  weighted-lane reduction owners.
- Added exact scalar grouped Q5_1 down and a raw-Q5_1 grouped WMMA down kernel;
  retained direct selected GEMV as strict fallback.
- Routed the one Q5_K gate/up layer and five Q8_0 down layers through existing
  grouped WMMA owners.
- Added Qwen4Exp-exact parallel GDN prepare and sigmoid RMSNorm gate around the
  in-tree compact peer-wave recurrence; strict serial recurrence remains
  fallback.
- Added a diagnostic Q8 WMMA tile override and model-local Q4 tile selection.
- Kept all performance routes behind explicit environment controls. Artifact:
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-27-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-prefill-grouped-candidate.json`.

  checker is blocked by missing external `/home/lhl/amd-gpu-tuning/reference/atlas`.

  grouped output is bit-exact to direct selected GEMV; Q5_1 WMMA and peer-GDN
