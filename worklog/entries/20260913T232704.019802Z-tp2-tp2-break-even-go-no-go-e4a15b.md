# TP2 Packet 0: break-even artifact and Packet 3 go decision

## Summary

**Go.** Packet 0's last item is the break-even artifact and the go/no-go decision
before Packet 3. Both sides of the comparison are measured on the same host: the
TP1 rows (W7900 27.9 tok/s, XTX 29.82 tok/s at 8192 context) and the collective
ladder (a 20 KB fp32 all-reduce at 398 us depth 1, 827 us depth 16, 1388 us
depth 32, so 36 collectives per token is 1.3-1.5 ms).

The model is one line, `token_time = fixed + weights / bandwidth`, with two
deliberate constraints: `bandwidth` is *implied* by the TP1 row rather than
assumed, and `fixed` (attention over the KV cache, the GDN recurrence, launch
overhead) is not guessed at all - only its share of the TP1 token time, reported
across 0-30%.

Break-even collective budget, `(1 - fixed_share) * T1 * (1 - rank_weight_fraction)`:

| Device | TP2 rank weights | Fixed share | Break-even collective | Headroom at 1.3-1.5 ms | Projected speedup |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| W7900 (27.9 tok/s) | 8.646 GiB of 15.652 | 0% | 16.04 ms | 10.7-12.3x | 1.68-1.70x |
| W7900 | | 30% | 11.23 ms | 7.5-8.6x | 1.37-1.38x |
| XTX (29.82 tok/s) | 7.009 GiB of 15.652 | 0% | 18.52 ms | 12.3-14.2x | 2.03-2.06x |
| XTX | | 30% | 12.96 ms | 8.6-10.0x | 1.52-1.53x |

Every row clears the plan's 1.3x target, and the collectives would have to be
7.5x worse than measured to reach break-even. The decision is therefore to
proceed to Packet 3, with the graph-capture limit from the Packet 1 entry as a
design constraint rather than a blocker.
