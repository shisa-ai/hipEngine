# Review target 5 audit: server packed workspace reservation

## Summary

Audited the service owner's eager packed-execution workspace lease
(review target 5). The lease is sized `serving capacity x max(full
configured context, 1024)` pages of uniform INT8 backing (8 MiB pages on
this geometry) and is taken at global-pool creation under the
`qwen35_gguf_packed_execution` key.

## What the reservation guarantees (verified in code)

- Admission accounting always sees the pinned pages: no request can be
  admitted against workspace pages, preserving the no-admission-without-
  rollback contract.
- `_GGUFPackedTargetState.allocate` can always draw its pages from the
  lease without mid-request failure; the workspace never grows beyond the
  lease by construction, union geometry is genuine-growth-only, and an
  over-need allocation fails closed.
- Growth invalidates live packed decode graphs and flushes deferred state
  before reallocating (no graph binds freed buffers).

## Audit conclusion

The full-context lease is honest for the dense serving route. The packed
AR prefill path — the serving prefill slab, including the c1 direct-int8
slot-local route — requests `slot_capacity = max(1024, live context)`
unconditionally and rebinds its layout to workspace pages. The C1/256K
figure (8 GiB workspace on top of 8 GiB request KV, 16 GiB total) is the
real cost of the packed slab backing, not accounting waste. The MTP
verify and NextN priming paths are bounded at the 1024-token floor
(4 pages = 32 MiB per slot).

Reservation by context (deterministic arithmetic, page = 8 MiB):
16K -> 0.5 GiB; 64K -> 2 GiB; 128K -> 4 GiB; 256K -> 8 GiB; C8 multiplies
the workspace by 8. Full table in the artifact.

## Bound: proposed, not implemented

A naive bound to the 1024 floor would break long-context serving prefill.
The safe bound requires either (a) an instrumented GPU experiment proving
the c1 slot-local route's workspace planes are scratch-only (KV written
through the scheduler page table) — not run; GPU1 was occupied by an
external benchmark during this audit — or (b) routing long-context
slot-local prefill to per-session paths under capacity pressure. Recorded
in the artifact's bound_proposal block.

The DMS capacity route is unaffected: it uses private-C1 session
construction, not this server pool.
