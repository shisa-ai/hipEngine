# Gemma 4 expert tensors plan the pack8 layout, keeping the raw copy as the fallback

## Summary

Part one of the pack8 expert port: the resident plan and the device materializer
now carry the pack8 layout for Gemma 4's rank-3 Q4_K expert tensors.

**Plan.** A rank-3 Q4_K expert tensor gets `LAYOUT_Q4_K_PACK8` and four
allocations: `raw`, `qweight`, `scales`, `mins`. Only Q4_K, because the pack8
expert GEMV family registers q4_k, q5_k and q6_k and this artifact's expert
tensors are Q4_K; a rank-3 tensor in another type keeps the raw layout rather than
being planned into a route that cannot serve it.

**Materialize.** The repack handles one expert and rejects a rank-3 input
(`raw_qweight must have GGUF Q4_K dense byte shape [out_features, bytes_per_row]
(rank-2)`), so the loader repacks per expert and stacks. The stacked shapes are
exactly what the kernel indexes: `qweight [experts, out_features/8, in_features]`
int32 plus one fp32 scale and min per 32-value group. On the fixture that is
`(4, 16, 256)` int32 + two `(4, 8, 128)` fp32.

**The raw copy stays.** Three of the four routes in `gemma4_project_experts_rows`
-- `grouped_prefill`, `grouped_row4` and `per_expert_offset` -- read
`allocation("raw")`. Dropping it would turn each of them into a missing-key error
rather than a working fallback, so the packed arrays sit alongside the raw blocks.
That is the 36.2 GB option measured earlier, against a 124 GiB ceiling, and the
duplication is recorded in `docs/REFACTOR.md` with its removal condition.

**Residency accounting.** `resident_bytes` previously summed the artifact bytes.
It now adds the packed arrays, sized from the source shape alone so planning still
allocates nothing. Without that the total would under-report by 19.2 GB on the
real artifact -- an out-of-memory discovered after the correctness gate passed.

## Changes

- `hipengine/loading/gemma4_gguf_device.py`: `LAYOUT_Q4_K_PACK8`,
  `_PACK8_ALLOCATION_NAMES`, `_pack8_shapes`, `_pack8_nbytes`, the `_plan_one`
  branch, `resident_bytes` accounting, `pack8_arrays`, and `_materialize_pack8`
  with cleanup on a partial upload failure.
- `tests/test_unit_gemma4_gguf_device.py`: four new tests and three updated to the
  new contract.
- `docs/REFACTOR.md`: the raw/packed duplication with its removal condition.

## Validation

- `tests/test_unit_gemma4_gguf_device.py`: 12 passed, none skipped (HIP is
  available on this box).
- Device materialization verified directly: the four allocations upload with the
  planned shapes and dtypes, `nbytes` equals `source.nbytes + _pack8_nbytes`, and
  `free()` releases cleanly.
- `python3 audit/audit.py check` passes.
- **One failure in the broader gemma4 unit suite,
  `test_unit_gemma4_runner.py::test_incremental_decode_matches_a_dense_prefill`,
  is not from this change.** It fails with the identical max abs diff of 4.417
  with these two files stashed, so it is pre-existing at HEAD. It is worth its own
  investigation and is not fixed here.
- **Not verified:** that the pack8 route produces the same numbers as the raw
  route. The route is not wired yet, so nothing reads the packed arrays. That is
  part two and its correctness gate.
