# The wmma false pass: cause found, fixed, 16-47 re-certified bit-identically

## Summary

Resolved. Supersedes both earlier entries on this defect
(`20260929T145430.832594Z-lhl-qwen4exp-wmma-route-false-pass-ad392f.md`, which
published a wrong cause, and its correction).

## The defect

`--route wmma --layers 16-47` - the certified configuration of a **promoted**
route - returned identical candidate/teacher logits hashes, KL exactly `0.0` on
every statistic, and `status: complete` with `measurement_valid: true`. A green
status covering a route that never ran. The 2026-09-16 certification of the same
route had a genuine exact-chain teacher and mean KL 3.796e-4, so the
certification was not reproducible at HEAD.

## Cause

The route cleared **only** its own selector. It never cleared
`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_DENSE_WIDE`, which the certified plan binds to 16-47 -
the same window this route is gated at - and `_q8_dense_wide_claims_parent`
accepts the WMMA-rewritten parent (`wmma_<parent>`, ABI `wmma_raw`) as well as
the exact parent. The wide route also runs later in the launch chain. So it
claimed the dispatch whichever way the WMMA selector was set, both arms resolved
to `dense_wide256_f32_f32_out`, and the gate compared one configuration against
itself.

The reciprocal clearing was already understood: the `dense_wide` route clears
the WMMA selector in both arms, and the `Route` docstring explains exactly why
("the two routes would contend for the window under test"). It was simply never
added in the other direction.

## Fixed, and re-certified bit-identically

The `wmma` route now clears the wide route in both arms, matching the composed
and staged routes. Re-run:

| | teacher | candidate | mean KL | outliers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-16 | `550bb9b8` | `65370710` | 3.796e-4 | 0 |
| HEAD now | `550bb9b8` | `65370710` | 3.79552e-4 | 0 |

Same teacher hash, same candidate hash, same KL to four digits, every gate
passing, zero outliers. **The route's arithmetic has not drifted since
2026-09-16; only its gate was broken.**

I checked the teacher hash directly rather than inferring it from the KL. An
identical candidate/teacher pair is the exact failure this entry documents, and
it has now occurred twice in this packet.

## Two corrections of my own work, kept in the record

1. **A wrong cause was published first.** The first entry named the dispatch
   memoization key as the *verified* cause. The false pass reproduced
   **unchanged** with that fix in place. The key genuinely did lack a WMMA
   layer-scope term, and that fix and its RED test stand as a latent-defect fix
   - but establishing that a suspected cause is *present* is not establishing
   that it is *the* cause. Corrected in `50c894f0a`; the wrong claim is named in
   the ledger rather than edited away.
2. **An outlier attribution was withdrawn and is now restored.** I first
   attributed the composed run's 2 outliers to the staged tile on the strength of
   the control; the control was a false pass, so I withdrew it. The control is
   valid now, and both runs share the same teacher (`550bb9b8`), so they are
   directly comparable: WMMA 16-47 alone has **0** outliers, adding staged 13-15
   gives **2**. The attribution is sound because engagement is verified, not
   assumed. The outliers are a real property of the staged tile and remain an
   open item against promotion.

## What this does not affect

The staged-f32 results are unaffected, and the composed certificate is
**exonerated**: it cleared the wide route in both arms, so its WMMA@16-47
component really was WMMA and its teacher really was the exact chain. My previous
turn's concern that it was "inherited, not re-verified" was wrong.
