# UD gfx1151 optimize2 E4b: post-E4a re-census and Q4_K single composition adjudication (negative)

## Summary

Iteration 9 executed campaign E4's mandated re-rank after E4a's retained route, then adjudicated
census item 2 (the Q4_K single local32 owner gap) as **composition, not an owner defect** — a
negative result the campaign explicitly wants recorded.

Re-census (same E0/E2d/E4 protocol, 32-step graph window per arm) confirms E4a's effect:
`qk_t16_selected_direct_gemv<ushort,5>` — E4's #1 at 20.47 ms/tok — is gone from the top 12;
`q5_k_t16_dense_tile8` now carries all Q5 work at 24.02 ms/tok and 116.2 launches/tok. The Q5
family fell 25.22 → 24.02 ms/tok with 10.7 fewer launches/tok; UD's pure window moved
85.53 → 84.83 ms/tok against plain's unchanged 79.81, and the plain control arm is identical in
totals (571.59 launches/tok) — the arm-untouched property E4a predicted.

Item 2's cross-arm gap (UD 158.0 vs plain 119.5 µs/launch on the same
`q4_k_t16_dense_single_local32` symbol) is explained without any owner inefficiency:

- Same kernel symbol on both arms; same-kernel + same-shape speed is arm-identical (the Q5
  tile8 precedent: 111.2 plain vs 111.5 µs/launch), so per-shape cost cannot differ by arm.
- Per-block ffn quant map: plain is 65/65 layers `(Q4_K, Q4_K)` — every layer pairable, hence
  62 dual-SiLU launches/tok. UD has only 5 `(Q4_K, Q4_K)`, 20 `(IQ4_XS, IQ4_XS)` (pairable via
  the IQ dual, which fires 19.4/tok in-window), 9 `(Q5_K, Q5_K)`, and **13 mixed-quant
  gate/up layers that no same-quant pair rule can ever fire** — 26 singleton launches.
- Role/shape skew: UD's remaining Q4 singles are big shapes (attn_qkv (10240,5120)×29,
  attn_gate (6144,5120)×24, ffn 17408-class ×24, down ×10) while plain's singles skew small.

Closing the mixed-layer scatter would mean re-planning the artifact's per-tensor quants — a
bytes/arithmetic change under the execution-profile gate, not a code-route lever. Per campaign
rules, composition-driven bytes are not an owner bug. Re-rank: item 3 = the IQ4_XS local32
family (24.25 ms/tok), UD-only quants with no plain counterpart — a within-UD variant/shape
question, E9-adjacent.
