# Reject small-M against production Q4-T16 rowtile owners

## Summary

Task #12 is closed with a production-owned measurement, and small-M is rejected.
Across all **35** actual-weight standard-Q4 cells (five roles x rows 2-8),
small-M is bit-exact to strict shared-B and finite, but is **2.52-14.84x slower
than the incumbent by HIP events** and **2.51-13.53x slower operation-complete**.
It wins zero cells. The existing dense rowtile/col4 owners remain default; no
runtime or profile change and no new MTP promotion packet are justified.

This also resolves stale scope assumptions in the task. On this exact
Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_M model, FFN gate is standard Q4-T16 and was measured, while
FFN down, recurrent QKV, and full-attention V materialize as Q6 qmicro-planar
and are ABI-ineligible for the Q4 small-M candidate. The measurable standard-Q4
roles are FFN gate, recurrent attention gate, and full-attention Q/K/output.
