# Gemma 4 gate scopes the full-layer WMMA check to plans that reach it

## Summary

`scripts/gemma4_teacher_forced_gate.py gate` failed every unpinned candidate
that did not plan the full-layer WMMA walk (`no_wmma_full_launches_observed`),
including the gfx1100 production plan, whose decode scope means decode steps
never reach that walk; the long-row flash gate had to pass
`--wmma-full-slices 1` as an exemption. The gate now asks the candidate runner
whether a decode step can reach the walk (no decode scope and
`gemma4_wmma_flash_full` in the prefill request, the gfx1151 plan). If not, the
verdict records `wmma_full_split_scope: not_reachable_under_candidate_plan`,
and any walk observed anyway fails as `wmma_full_launches_outside_candidate_plan`.
A reachable walk is still required as before.

## Changes

- `scripts/gemma4_teacher_forced_gate.py`: `wmma_full_reachable`, `require_wmma_full_split(reachable=)`.
- `tests/test_unit_gemma4_teacher_forced_gate.py`: reachability and verdict tests.

## Validation

- Gate unit/route tests 35 passed.
- Real run: the 32K long-row flash gate without the exemption flag passes with
  the same numbers (mean KL 1.18e-6, max 2.9e-4, zero flips) and records the scope.
