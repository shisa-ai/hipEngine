# Promote Qwen4Exp prefill chunk 512

## Summary

Promoted the Qwen4Exp strict prefill default chunk from 256 to 512 and fixed
the hidden configuration gap: the PLE staging ring capacity was hard-defaulted
to 256 rows, silently capping any larger `prefill_chunk_size`. Same-session
counterbalanced sweeps show chunk 512 wins -2.22% (p508) and -1.82% (p1012)
with non-overlapping distributions and identical logits SHAs; chunk 640 is
neutral versus 512. The natural 16K gate passes faster at 341.177 s versus the
retained 364.306 s (-6.35%, 47.989 tok/s).
