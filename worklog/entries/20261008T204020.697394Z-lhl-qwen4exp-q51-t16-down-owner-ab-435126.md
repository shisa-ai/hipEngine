# Q5_1 T16 down owner A/B: -57.7% median vs production pair2

## Summary

The down-leg T16 build's owner A/B (step 3 of the committed spec,
4d94cbbb5 / 5e90c9b9e / bfd5ec470): the new Q5_1 T16 expert-major
compensated WMMA kernel measured against every registered down owner on
the real geometry (512 experts, K=640, N=2560), 15 repetitions, all 8
row x distribution cases.

**Result (main-kernel timing, no correctness claim):**

- T16 Q5_1 expert-major: **-49.4% to -69.5% vs the production pair2 down
  across all 8 cases, median -57.7%** (3.58ms vs 9.80ms at
  balanced-4096; 5.50ms vs 17.12ms at balanced-10240).
- It matches or beats the F16 compact down at every sparse point and
  balanced-4096 and below (e.g. sparse-512: 0.999ms vs 1.016ms), and
  the F16 compact stays ahead at the two largest balanced points
  (balanced-10240: 4.456ms vs 5.502ms).
- The F16 compact remains the fastest registered owner on median
  (-59.9%), with the T16 Q5_1 second - the same class, as designed.

**Why this matters for the admission:** the F16 compact down was
rejected at the prefill_last scope (X-7) at top-1 98.667%; the T16
Q5_1 kernel is the compensated sibling targeting exactly that gap - the
same -57.7% median wall class with the Kahan compensated accumulate
the T16 gate/up demonstrated at 8-9.5x below the BF16 floor. The
numerical admission (engine A/B + the layer2 candidate with
prefill_last as the known risk axis) is the remaining step.
