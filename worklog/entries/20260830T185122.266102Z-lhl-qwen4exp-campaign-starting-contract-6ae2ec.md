# Freeze Qwen4Exp campaign starting contract

## Summary

Froze the canonical Qwen4Exp screening as the campaign's explicit starting
contract. The campaign now separates repeatability correctness from unmeasured
accuracy, records the exact non-stock patch bundle used to start current
upstream HIP on this host/model, and names the p4096 decode-depth cliff as a
measured blocker rather than unexplained aggregate variance.

## Changes

- Classified hipEngine, pristine upstream Vulkan, and patched-upstream HIP as
  12/12 repeat-exact; EngramHalo p4096 and every Nathan row remain diagnostic.
  The document now states that this screen did not measure KL/task/human
  accuracy and points to the existing BF16-teacher and production gates.
  Full-continuation pairwise equality is retained as diagnostic evidence:
  hipEngine matches upstream Vulkan 1/12, patched HIP 0/12, and EngramHalo 1/12.
- Recorded the full SHA-256 identities of the #25992 host-buffer and per-buffer
  mmap patches. Scoped the tested pair as required for this exact upstream HIP
  comparator on `zbook`, not a universal upstream requirement or a stock
  result; no single-patch necessity claim is made without an ablation.
- Added the measured decode-depth table. hipEngine falls 13.823→10.395 tok/s
  from p512→p4096 and adds 23.67 ms/token from p1024→p4096, versus 5.61–8.47
  ms/token on the repeatability-valid upstream lanes.
- Confirmed the mechanical boundary: QSA token budget 2,048, compression ratio
  4, and dense-equivalent limit 2,051. p4096 decode activates index-query
  projection, score/top-k, and selected sparse attention in all 12 QSA layers.
- Added a P6 action to profile QSA live counts 2,051/2,052 and p4096 before any
  short-AR parity claim. The 15–20 ms/token excess remains unattributed until
  that role-resolved profile exists.
