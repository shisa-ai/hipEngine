# Qwen3.8 Y0 prefill sweep multiplicity instrumentation

## Summary

Closed extension Y0 with a cache-only nine-row-point `rocprofv3` trace on the
physical gfx1151 host. The measurement rejects Y's original blanket
`ceil(rows/16)` Q4 premise: current shared-B owners already reuse one decoded
weight slab across multiple M tiles, producing byte-weighted Q4 sweep
multiplicity **1.00/1.30/1.34/2.02/2.09/1.17/2.87/3.00/4.00** at rows
16/35/48/72/96/256/288/536/1024. Q4's perfect-single-sweep full-wall upper
bound is only **13.1-14.3%** at C2-like rows35/48, but **36.6%** at C8-like
rows288. All Q4/Q5/Q6 multiplicity removal bounds C2 at **26.1-27.5%**, below
the 34.0% wall cut required for 139.8->211.888 tok/s; C2 therefore needs the
post-dataflow issue/fusion track even if Y1/Y2 are perfect. This is
instrumentation and sizing, not a performance claim.
