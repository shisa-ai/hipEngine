# D4 preliminary: multi-prompt native-cycle acceptance + recall

## Summary

Ran the native DFlash2 cycle (forward + selector + sequential greedy verify)
on one prompt per mtpbench category in a single resident session
(`scripts/dflash2_gguf_suite_smoke.py`, Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M, gfx1151, 40 new
tokens each). All four categories reproduce pure-AR greedy exactly (1.00).
Acceptance is **prompt-dependent** — 0.158-0.429 accepted/draft — which
revises the earlier single-prompt "DFlash2 has low acceptance / selector
hurts" reading: the smoke prompt was the worst case, and the good prompts
imply a ~1.8-2.0x-AR ceiling with a batched chain verifier, competitive with
exact MTP B3 (1.78x AR).

## Changes

- `scripts/dflash2_gguf_suite_smoke.py` (new): loads the target + drafter once
  and runs the native cycle on one prompt per mtpbench category (single
  resident session, per-prompt AR reference), reporting acceptance, recall@1/
  @16/unary-argmax, and AR agreement.

## Validation

| category/id | mean_acc | acc/draft | r@1 | r@16 | unary | tok/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| code_merge_intervals | 3.64 | 0.377 | 0.763 | 0.947 | 0.711 | 6.63 |
| general_en_plan | 3.33 | 0.333 | 0.718 | 0.949 | 0.718 | 6.55 |
| general_ja_plan | 2.11 | 0.158 | 0.538 | 0.769 | 0.513 | 5.22 |
| mixed_ja_en_translate | 4.00 | 0.429 | 0.811 | 0.973 | 0.838 | 6.69 |

All greedy == AR 1.00.  Sequential-verify throughput is 5-6 tok/s vs AR ~10
(the verify dominates the ~0.36 s/cycle; the native drafter is already fast).

## Key findings

1. DFlash2 acceptance on this model is strongly prompt-dependent; the earlier
   smoke-prompt numbers (acc/draft 0.117) are the floor, not the norm.
2. With ~2 target steps/cycle (bonus + first reject), the acceptance on
   code/mixed_ja_en (~0.38-0.43) implies a ~1.8-2.0x-AR ceiling once
   verification is batched — competitive with exact MTP B3 (23.85 tok/s,
   1.7845x AR). D3.1 (chain verify at B=7) is the decisive next step.
3. Bilinear-selector vs unary-argmax recall is also prompt-dependent (bilinear
   better on general_ja, worse on smoke/mixed_ja_en): the D4 suite must
   measure the selector value per category, not from one prompt.
