# Withdraw the gfx1151 C8/K3 cell from the production policy

## Summary

The census and width refresh committed as `96c456a3a` recorded the gfx1151
production `(8,3)` cell as withdrawn on evidence: 0.936x of its own true-AR
baseline and 0 of 10 cells reproducing the AR output, because the backend's
prompt-streaming policy admits widths `(1,2,3,4)` and refuses an eight-row
pending set (`physical_streaming_category_rejected`) while the route ran its
cycles anyway. This unit makes the withdrawal real instead of documentary, so no
production request can reach the broken route: the cell is removed from
`GGUF_SPECDEC2_MTP2_PHYSICAL_WIDTH_DEPTHS["production"]` and the serving-evidence
row that admitted it is deleted, which leaves an explicit capacity-8 MTP request
on the registered strict fallback (`gguf_target_ar`). gfx1100 is untouched: its
prompt-streaming policy admits width 8, and its own C8/K3 row keeps its evidence.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1151/__init__.py`: production owns `(1..4, 1..3)`
  only; the comment records the withdrawal, the measurement, and why gfx1100's
  cell is unaffected.
- `hipengine/models/qwen35.py`: remove the
  `qwen38-q4km-gfx1151-production-bf16-c8-k3-d24` serving-evidence row.
- `tests/test_unit_qwen35_gguf_mtp2_seam.py`: the package-scope test pins the
  gfx1151 table without a wide cell; the policy test now asserts C5-C8 are all
  refused and that capacity 8 plus per-row eligibility cannot widen a group past
  C4 (it previously asserted `(8,3)` admission, `max_physical_requests == 8`,
  and a 32-row frontier); the M5 partition test pins the last listed cell at
  `(4, 3)`.
- `tests/test_unit_speculative_mtp_serving_capability.py`: the C8/K3 row test
  becomes `..._is_withdrawn` and asserts the key resolves to the strict fallback
  with no wide speculative cell.
