# Rank c8 projection family mechanisms

## Summary

Closed #37's family-ranking measurement phase with exact encoded-weight bytes,
current production-owner c1/c4/c8 marked traces, one hardware-counter pilot,
and static code-object evidence.

Planar Q6 row8 is first: 64 actual base-model tensors carry 3.406 GB encoded
weights per transition and take 10.570 ms (322.2 GB/s encoded-weight-only).
Duration rises 6.551→10.570 ms from c4→c8 (+61.4%). The row8 body is local128,
VGPR136, LDS1 KiB, scratch0; static ISA has 320 bpermutes and 233 waitcnt sites.
Its diagnostic gap to the Q4-pair family's attainable same-step encoded rate is
3.865 ms, which ranks work but is not a promised wall saving.

Q4 singleton is second: 4.082 GB / 10.537 ms / 387.4 GB/s, c4→c8 +41.0%,
VGPR216. Q5 is third by a 2.185 ms diagnostic rate gap but only 4.228 ms total.
GDN is fourth pending a recurrence/state ledger. Q4-pair is fifth despite its
12.634 ms sum because its 6.417 GB payload reaches the highest family rate,
508.0 GB/s.
