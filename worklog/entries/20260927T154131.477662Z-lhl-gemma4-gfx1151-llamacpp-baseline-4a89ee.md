# gfx1151 llama.cpp baseline: the prefill gap is 3.5-5.1x, not 30x

## Summary

The gfx1151 llama.cpp baseline exists for the first time, and it says the
objective's premise is out of date. The prefill gap is 3.54x at 512/128 and
5.14x at 1024/128, not the ~30x the work was scoped against.

| row | hipEngine prefill | llama.cpp prefill | gap | hipEngine decode | llama.cpp decode | gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 512/128 | 257.2 | 909.3 | 3.54x | 21.06 | 36.03 | 1.71x |
| 1024/128 | 230.0 | 1183.1 | 5.14x | 19.95 | 33.66 | 1.69x |

Two of the objective's six items close with this. The model is the UD-Q4_K_XL
artifact the gfx1151 work used, and the branch is not behind `origin/main` -
`git fetch origin main` then `rev-list --count origin/main ^HEAD` is 0, so there
is nothing to rebase. MTP resolves as a model capability gap: Gemma 4 declares
`supports_speculative_mtp = False` in `hipengine/generation/gemma4_gguf.py`, the
MTP adapters are registered for Qwen only, and the model card states the guide
does not cover speculative decode. That is a correct capability declaration
rather than a missing engine feature, and the engine respects it before routing.

## Validation

- Both engines measured GPU-exclusive, never concurrently, on the same prompt
  ids from `exact_prompt_ids`. llama.cpp's own tokenizer round-trips them:
  `text_probe.llama_tokens` equals `prompt_tokens` at both lengths, so the two
  are scored on identical input rather than on two tokenizations.
- llama.cpp runs needed `--idle-limit-mib 512`. The default 128 MiB is calibrated
  for a dGPU; this iGPU carries 168 MiB of driver baseline and the guard refused
  to measure. The guard is doing its job - the failure was loud and named the
  device and the numbers - it just needed the right limit for this box.
- hipEngine numbers come from `scripts/gemma4_campaign_bench.py` at commit
  `940255881`, clean tree, with the artifact's `head_sha256_64MiB` recorded.

## Next

- The gap widens with length and from opposite directions: hipEngine falls
  257.2 -> 230.0 going from 512 to 1024 prompt tokens while llama.cpp rises
  909.3 -> 1183.1. A fixed per-prefill cost amortising over more tokens explains
  llama.cpp rising; hipEngine falling points at work that grows with length.
  That is the campaign's first open item - the 5 global layers at head_dim 512
  have no tiled kernel, so past the window later blocks fall back to the slow
  one - and this is the first measurement on this box that separates it from
  fixed overhead.
- Decode is flat at about 21 against llama.cpp's 34-36, so the objective's 2x
  target of about 42 tok/s is above llama.cpp rather than at parity with it.
- The parallel workstream's changelog entries have already closed the decode
  speculative path as a capability gap and the decode attention kernel's barrier
  batch as spent, and they localize the MMQ route's defect to the group scatter's
  `expert_start`/`packed_hidden` handoff rather than the leaf. Their stated next
  unit instruments that handoff, so I am not duplicating it; the baseline above is
  what that work should be measured against.
