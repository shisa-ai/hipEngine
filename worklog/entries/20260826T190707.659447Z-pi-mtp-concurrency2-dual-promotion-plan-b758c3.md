# Freeze dual-model CONCURRENCY2 MTP promotion campaign

## Summary

Froze the binding campaign contract for independently promoting real
Generation-2 MTP scopes on W7900 for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MoE GGUF `UD-Q4_K_M` and
Qwen3.6-27B Dense GGUF `Q4_K_M`. Production generated-ID equality is explicitly
diagnostic; exact control/ownership plus strict-teacher distribution tails,
determinism, isolation, task quality, BF16-relative evidence, lifecycle, and
same-suite true-AR economics bind.

The first promotable keys are narrow, mechanical C1 scopes: 35B/K2 and 27B/K3,
BF16 KV, production profile, raw greedy, context 4-95, natural D24. Every other
shape/profile/model/sampler key remains K0 until independently qualified. The
campaign does not close until both keys promote and a cross-model fail-closed
serving audit passes.
