# Long-context MTP ratio after the batched full-attention chain

## Summary

The batched full-attention verifier chain (parent entry
`20260917T195925.422701Z-lhl-gguf-long-context-eager-staged-attention-2c4ca3.md`) is
necessary but not sufficient for long-context MTP economics. With split-K decode
left at its default threshold, MTP now engages at a 3,530-token prompt where the
row-wise route made it useless, and the ratio improves from the previously
measured 0.57x to **0.68x** AR — still below break-even. The remaining gap is the
**linear-attention** chain: 48 of the model's 64 layers keep the row-wise strict
route whenever `strict_long_rows` is true, so only the 16 full-attention layers
gain from the batched leaf.

## Validation

Server: `python -m hipengine.server --model /home/lhl/models/gguf/Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M.gguf
--backend hip_gfx1151 --max-context-tokens 8192 --kv-storage bf16
--speculative-mtp-serving enabled --speculative-candidate-budget 3` (vllm env ROCm SDK,
`HIPENGINE_MTP2_MAX_CONTEXT_TOKENS=8192`, split-K decode at its default 1024
threshold). Host: gfx1151 (Strix Halo), same physical machine as the screen.
Harness: `/tmp/bench_1to1.py --shapes 1024,4096 --decode 128 --repeats 3`, AR arm
via `BENCH_EXTRA_JSON='{"speculative_mtp": false}'` against the same process.

| Prompt (945/3,530 tok) | MTP staged full-attn | AR (same session) | ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,024 | 10.48 tok/s (10.48/10.48/10.56) | 11.64 (11.64/11.64/11.65) | 0.90x |
| 4,096 | 7.35 (7.35/7.35/7.36) | 10.88 (10.87/10.88/10.88) | 0.68x |

Repeat spread is <0.1%. A non-streaming probe at both shapes reports
`effective_route=speculative_mtp`,
`decision_reason=qualified_automatic_realized_singleton_c1_b3`, `used=true`, and
`mtp_coverage=0.992`, so MTP is engaged rather than silently declined. The screen's
`batched_rows` arm (split-K threshold raised so *both* chains batch) measured
1.38x at 4,096, which is the ceiling the linear chain still gates.
