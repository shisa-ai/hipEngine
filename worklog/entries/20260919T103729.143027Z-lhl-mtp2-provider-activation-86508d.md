# MTP2 provider-activation audit: a provider-less group-mate, not the draft pass, is the measured waste

## Summary

Task #13's first step was an audit: a speculative row that ends up autoregressive
pays its draft provider activation during prefill, so the intended fix was to stop
activating a provider for a row whose due group can never be served. The audit
measured the pre-fix tree on the W7900 production profile at c=2 and c=4 with the
`sharegpt_mtp_routing_pass` pair and coupling lanes (8 rows each, 2.5k-10k-token
contexts, seed 373, `--cases long,mid` and `short,long`).

Two results changed the plan.

First, the 2026-09-18 premise does not reproduce as a share loss. Every realized
speculative group is now one row (`groups_by_realized_rows: {'1': ...}`) and the
speculative arm keeps 96.9-99.4% of its output tokens from MTP at both
concurrencies, with TTFT at parity with the AR arm (pair c=2: 3114 ms median vs
3308 ms; pair c=4: 8579 ms vs 8851 ms). The provider activation is used, so a
width cap that refuses the second concurrent speculative row would forfeit a real
share win to save a wait the deferral design intends. That cap was implemented,
measured against these baselines, and dropped (`/tmp` patch only, not landed);
the c=2 lane's one 8248 ms TTFT against a longer prompt's 4381 ms is the deferral
wait itself, ~3.9 s, not a wasted draft pass.

Second, the coupling c=4 lane found the actual waste. All four speculative rows
are `qualified_automatic_realized_singleton_c1_b3` and report the planner's
`no_provider`; two of them hold a provider and one never gets one:
prompt 17 / 208 generated tokens / 0 MTP tokens / `first_fallback_position: 0` /
`selected_depth_histogram: {}` / 207 `no_provider` events, while its
near-identical twin (prompt 20) speculates on 159 cycles at depth 3. The
provider-less row is planned into the group its twin is also in, so the group is
refused and the *providing* row loses those cycles (208 events against 159 served
cycles). The waste is therefore the group-mate, not the draft pass: one row that
never receives its prompt hidden rows costs its whole group's speculation for the
row's lifetime, and it reports `no_provider` rather than a reason of its own
(`specdec2_mtp2_prompt_fallback_reason` is `None` on all four rows, and that field
has no consumer in-tree).

The two short prompts (20 and 17 tokens) are one chunk each and arrive in the same
tick, which makes a packed-prefill handoff that gives the hidden rows to only one
of the batch's rows the leading hypothesis. It is not yet confirmed.

## Changes

- None landed. The two candidate fixes were measured against the baselines and
  reverted: the concurrent-width cap (would forfeit a 97-99% share) and a
  refusal-report helper that zeroes `mtp2_candidate_budget` on the four
  non-zeroing refusal paths (`test_mtp2_physical_prompt_streaming_is_rejected_before_provider_open`
  documents the opposite contract: a refused row keeps its requested depth so the
  response can report intent separately from realized execution).
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-19-gfx1151-q4km-mtp2-provider-activation-audit.json`
  records the baseline lanes, the per-row reasons, and both rejected fixes.

## Validation

- Pre-fix baseline on the production profile (Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M.gguf, gfx1100
  W7900, `--backend hip_gfx1151 --execution-profile production
  --speculative-mtp-serving auto --speculative-candidate-budget 3
  --max-context-tokens 8192 --kv-storage bf16 --port 8081`,
  `HIPENGINE_MTP2_OUTPUT_SPANS=1`): harness
  `scripts/sharegpt_mtp_routing_pass.py --num-prompts 8 --seed 373 --record-ids
  --route-mix off,auto --max-concurrency <c>` for lanes `pair` (long,mid,
  `--min-prompt-len 300 --max-prompt-len 1023 --max-total-len 2048
  --output-len 0`) and `couple` (short,long, `--max-total-len 1024`) at c=2 and
  c=4. Full command lines and the exact server environment are in the artifact.
- `tests/test_unit_qwen35_gguf_mtp2_seam.py` was run against the refused-helper
  candidate: 1 failure, the test above, which is the evidence that the intent
  retention is a contract rather than an oversight.
- The per-arm `decode_tokens_per_second` columns in these artifacts are *not*
  comparable: `--route-mix` assigns arms to different prompts, so the arms served
  different answer lengths (auto 224 vs AR 1025 completion tokens in the pair c=4
  lane). A matched protocol needs `--prompt-repeats 2` with an odd
  `--num-prompts`; that protocol is not yet run.

## Next

- Confirm or refute the packed-prefill handoff hypothesis: run the coupling lane
  at c=4 with diagnostic spans and check `specdec2_mtp2_prompt_prime_rows` and the
  hidden-row handoff for each row of a same-tick pair.
- Fix the group poisoning: a row that cannot be admitted by its provider must not
  keep a speculative intent that refuses its group-mates. Either give every packed
  row its own hidden rows, or demote the intent at the point the provider refuses
  admission, with the row's own reason.
- Then, and only then, revisit the deferral wait (the ~3.9 s TTFT on a deferred
  row) by priming the provider at the first admitted cycle from retained prompt
  hidden rows instead of deferring the row's prompt start.
