# W7900 Qwen3.8: the ceiling experiment never ran and the verify fit method is unreproducible

## Summary

Chasing down two numbers I have been quoting since this morning. One turns out never to have
been measured at all, and the other is corroborated in magnitude but its published method
cannot be reproduced. Neither reaches the public rollup, but both live in committed entries,
so they get corrected here rather than left as hearsay.

## Changes

Analysis and record only. No code, no benchmark row changed.

## Validation

**The "ceiling" experiment never ran.** Its supervisor log is 0 bytes, so no arm ever
executed: it waited on `/tmp/he-packed-grouping/probe.json`, which the crashed grouping probe
never produced, and was then stopped. `find /tmp -maxdepth 2 -name "*ceiling*"` returns
nothing and `/tmp/he-ceiling/` does not exist. So the "~160 ms/row verify cost is inherent"
figure and the "C8 ceiling 42.6 vs native K3 45.7" contrast are **non-measurements**, not
retracted measurements - stronger than what `20260830T032618...-3a02c8` recorded, which said
the packet "is gone from disk" and implied it once existed. Nothing in `docs/`,
`benchmarks/` or `README.md` cites them (grep over those trees finds only unrelated
occupancy/bandwidth "ceiling" uses), so no published text needs editing; the promotion
entry's hedged note stands as the closest committed reference and this entry supersedes it.

**The AR half of the per-row story reproduces exactly.** Fitting the committed
`decode_step_ms` series in
`benchmarks/results/2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-c1c8-submodule-decomposition.json` against width
re-derives `20260830T030216...-37f4a1`'s numbers to the digit: **27.6 ms fixed + 6.71
ms/lane, R2 = 0.9972**. Note `decode_step_ms` is a *wave* step cost, so 6.71 is a per-lane
slope, not a per-step value - reading it as 34-82 ms/row is a misreading I made once today.

**The MTP half does not reproduce as published.** That entry states 21.4 ms/cycle fixed +
21.51 ms/row with R2 = 0.9951 from `mtp.draft_cycles` / `mtp.draft_tokens` in the K3 packet.
Three constructions of mine give: per-row wall vs drafted+cycles **slope 21.26 ms, R2 0.02**;
width-mean per-lane wall vs rows **slope 334 ms, R2 0.56**; per-row wall vs cycles **slope
80.21 ms/cycle**, which at the entry's own 3.80-3.85 rows/cycle implies **~21 ms/row**. So the
*magnitude* ~21 ms/row is corroborated by two independent parameterizations, and the
consequent ~3.2x against the AR 6.71 ms/lane slope is consistent, but the exact 21.51 and the
R2 0.9951 cannot be recovered, because the fitting script was never committed and the source
column names a quantity rather than the aggregation. Until a proper measurement lands, treat
3.21x as corroborated magnitude with unreproducible method.

Method note, so nobody repeats my dead end: raw per-row `wall_seconds` in a resident wave is
dominated by wave admission and lane queueing, which scale with width, so R-squared fitted on
it is meaningless (my 0.02). A verify-cost fit must subtract admission using the d1-protocol
decomposition or use trace timings - which is exactly why the block-row sweep in task #10,
not more regression of packet walls, is the way to settle this.
