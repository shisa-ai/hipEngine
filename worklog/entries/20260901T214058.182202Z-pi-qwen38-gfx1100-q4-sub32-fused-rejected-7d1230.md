# Reject sub-32 and grouped-R6 fused Q4 paths

## Summary

Rejected and fully removed both bounded ways to extend fused Q4 gate/up+SiLU
below R32. Reusing the retained two-active-wave WMMA sibling is fast but changes
production BF16 bits against the current exact grouped-rowtile chain. A novel
`grid.y`-grouped fused-R6 sibling is bit exact and positive in isolation, but
regresses target/device time under the real C8 schedule.

## Changes

- Temporarily generalized the exact fused R6 kernel over `blockIdx.y`, added a
  grouped-R6 launcher/wrapper/registry policy, and proved repeated-chunk parity.
- Removed the kernel, wrapper, registry entry, environment policy, and tests
  after marker rejection. No runtime or rollback debt remains.
- Added a compact rejection artifact; no benchmark scoreboard row changed.

## Validation

- WMMA row32 at R28/R24, actual Qwen3.8 gate/up layers 0/32/64, 40 alternating
  HIP-event pairs and three launches/sample: **1.276–1.490x** faster but
  **73,201–86,555 BF16 mismatches/cell** versus the production composition.
  Rejected at the strict leaf gate.
- Grouped fused R6 at R12/R18/R24/R30/R36 on layers 0/32/64: all 15 cells are
  bit exact and improve **1.026–1.054x**, with at least 29/40 pair wins. The
  temporary grouped primitive passes 3/3 repeated-R6 exactness nodes.
- Dirty current-source cached-only C8/K3/D12 marker versus the immediately
  prior exact-C8 trace: launches fall **4,714→4,590**, but combined Q4 wall
  rises **1.30%**, target wall **519.014→521.836 ms (+0.54%)**, and device
  union **441.460→444.319 ms (+0.65%)**. The grouped dual owner executes 64
  times at grid `(139264,4)` for 47.803 ms. Rejected before full-suite spend.
- Final source tree matches `be6e4000c`; only this worklog and
  `benchmarks/results/2026-09-02-w7900-q4km-k3-q4-sub32-fused-rejected.json`
  remain.
