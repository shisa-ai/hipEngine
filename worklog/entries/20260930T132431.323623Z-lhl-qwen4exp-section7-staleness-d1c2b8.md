# Six section-7 rows call live production routes 'not retained', and nothing tracks the reopen conditions

## Summary

Section 7 is the ledger's "tried and not retained" table, and its only purpose is to tell a
reader what not to retry. **Six of its 78 rows name flags that are currently bound to
`"1" if production`.** Verified by reading the binder, not inferred from names or comments.
**Three of the six were withdrawn by association** - "part of the failed recovery
composition" given as the whole reason, with no measurement of the route itself - which is
exactly the defect that hid the multi-column GDN win.

**The systemic finding is more general than the arithmetic: every section-7 row carries a
"Reopen only with" condition, and nothing in the repository evaluates whether one has been
satisfied.** `GR_IU8`/`GR_IU8_DOWN` is the settled proof: the row records mean KL
1.40e-3, while the binder records what actually shipped at **KL <= 7.4e-8** - five orders
of magnitude apart - because the row's own reopen condition ("a structurally different
mechanism") was met and nothing tracked it.

## Why this is a candidate source and not bookkeeping

The obvious reading is that these six rows are stale records and the fix is to annotate
them. That reading misses the point.

**The multi-column GDN win - 1.34x-1.68x, retained this session - was found by asking
whether a recorded rejection still held.** It was not found by optimizing anything. The
same question applied systematically produced six more rows contradicted by current state,
three of them condemned without ever being measured on their own.

**A rejection table with unevaluated reopen conditions is not a record of what failed. It
is a record of what was believed at the time, presented as if it were a measurement.** The
`GR_IU8` case makes the size of the error concrete: 1.40e-3 against 7.4e-8 is not a
rounding difference or a protocol difference, it is a different mechanism entirely.

## What I did not do

I did not test the three association-withdrawn routes. Each needs its own A/B against the
current default, and the honest statement is that the census now *names* them rather than
answering them. **The pattern has produced one win from one row; whether it produces more
is an open question, and the objective's bar is still measured in retained wins, not in
rows corrected.**
