# Make Q5 planar workspace selection caller-owned

## Summary

Make Q5 planar DP4A selection caller-owned inside its workspace session. Remove
the duplicate dispatch environment check, preserving the optional sidecar cost,
runner opt-in, resource/shape fallbacks and kernel arithmetic.

## Changes

- `q5_raw_mmq_target_session(planar_dp4a=True, ...)` carries an explicit choice
  with its library/workspace. Dispatch no longer silently overrides it from an
  unset or zero environment, or raises on a new ambient typo after selection.
- Loader and runner still resolve their optional sidecar/physical-C8 policy.
  A disabled session cannot be enabled by environment mutation. Missing sidecar,
  unsupported geometry and R32 source-layout ownership retain their prior paths.
- Update dispatch tests to distinguish default-disabled sessions from explicit
  enabled requests, and cover nested disable/exception restoration.
- Correct stale L4-pending docstring and documentation describing the extra
  environment read. No registered kernel/body/default or profile promotion.
