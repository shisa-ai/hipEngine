# the 128-wide K pass works, is bitwise correct, and is a wash at the production K depth

## Summary

The three entries before this one scoped a wider K staging on the argument that **176 barriers
per block against 5632 dp4a per thread** made the synchronisation a first-order term.
`20260928T214500` and `20260928T220500` landed the mechanism. **This entry instantiates it and
measures it, and the measurement does not support the argument.**

## What was built

`gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_q8_1_ds4_mmq32x128_prefill_compact32_kernel` stages four Q4_K
subblocks per pass instead of one, taking the gate/up projection from **88 staging iterations
and 176 `__syncthreads()` to 22 and 44** at identical arithmetic. It is reachable through a real
launcher and a real Python wrapper, not only from a bench.

**A staging bug was found by the test, and it is the exact failure mode predicted.** `MMQ_K`
had been serving double duty: it set the K depth *and* the weight-staging column stride
(`col_offset += THREADS * 8 / MMQ_K`). Raising it to 128 silently reduced the column stride to
8, so the staging loop ran four times over four *column* sets instead of once over four
subblocks, and the trip count `in_features / MMQ_K` staged a quarter of the data. The result
was a **wrong answer, not a compile error.** Both were decoupled: the staging loop walks one
subblock per iteration (`k32_iters = in_features / 32`) and the column stride is fixed at the
subblock width.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q4_k_q8_1_selected_prefill.hip`: the raw MMQ32
  kernel templated on `KSUB` with `weight_tile[32 * KSUB]`; `MMQ_K` added to the host
  launcher template; a new `extern "C"` launcher for the 128-wide route; `k32_iters` and the
  column stride decoupled from `MMQ_K`.
- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q4_k_q8_1_selected_prefill.py`: the wide symbol
  and a thin wrapper that refuses `in_features` not divisible by 128.
- `tests/test_gpu_gguf_q4_k_q8_1_selected_prefill.py`: `raw_k128` layout and the bitwise
  parity test.
- `scripts/gemma4_mmq_k_width_leaf_bench.py`: the leaf bench.
- `docs/REFACTOR.md`: the unwired-route entry.

## Validation

`tests/test_gpu_gguf_q4_k_q8_1_selected_prefill.py`, three geometries (256, 512 and 768
`in_features`):

```
uv run pytest tests/test_gpu_gguf_q4_k_q8_1_selected_prefill.py -q
# 72 passed, exit=0   (69 before, 3 new)
```

The new test asserts `np.testing.assert_array_equal(wide, narrow)` -- **bitwise**, not
within tolerance. The accumulation order into each output element is unchanged, so any
difference is a staging bug rather than rounding. It then checks the wide route against the
CPU reference, so the two are confirmed correct rather than merely equal.

## Measurement

`scripts/gemma4_mmq_k_width_leaf_bench.py`, buffers and packing hoisted out of the timing
loop, `counts=64,64,64`, `out_features` 128 + 128:

| `in_features` | narrow (K=32) | wide (K=128) | wide/narrow |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 512 | 40.5 us | 39.9 us | 0.986x |
| 1024 | 67.1 us | 60.2 us | **0.897x** |
| 2816 | 159.5 us | 145.8 us | 0.914x |

**The 2816 row does not hold up.** A first run of the same geometry gave narrow 138.2 us and
wide 161.6 us -- the opposite verdict -- and across both runs the minima were 133.6 against
138.4 us. **At the production K depth the two routes are the same speed within noise.**

**So the barrier count is worth about 10% at best, not the factor of 1.36 the estimate
implied.** The hypothesis is refuted at the depth that matters. The remaining gap to
llama.cpp's staging depth is not synchronisation.

## The measurement has one real limitation

The bench runs at **128 output features, not the production 1408**: the synthetic weight
generator overflows `uint8` past 128, so every point above has a quarter of the intended
arithmetic intensity. That makes the absolute rates low (1.00 TMAC/s against the 3.00 TMAC/s
the gate/up projection reaches in situ) and it means the result is **not a decisive refutation
at production geometry** -- only a decisive refusal to promote on this evidence.

## Why nothing routes to it

**A leaf-level win that is 10% at one geometry, nothing at 2816, and needs an
`in_features % 128 == 0` dispatch condition the narrow route does not need is not promotable.**
Wiring it would be promoting a non-result. The kernel, launcher, symbol and test stay, because
they are verified and they are the instrument for a re-measurement; `docs/REFACTOR.md` carries
the entry naming both clearing conditions and says to delete it if neither happens.

## Next

- **The K-staging line is closed at this geometry.** Do not re-derive it without either a

- **The remaining prefill gap is not barrier count.** The in-situ 3.00 TMAC/s against the
