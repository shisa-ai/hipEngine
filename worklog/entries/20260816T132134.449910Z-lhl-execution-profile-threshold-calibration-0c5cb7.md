# Calibrate production execution-profile thresholds

## Summary

Calibrated the production execution-profile numerical envelope from clean,
full-vocabulary captures on Radeon 8060S / gfx1151 without accepting historical
labels at face value. The native Qwen3.5-0.8B `chain_peer_cluster8` positive
passes every category/shape/transition scope; fresh Qwen3.6-35B `chain_k2` and
`chain_wave32_tree` negatives fail distribution gates. The historically
accepted gfx1100 `chain_peer_wave32` route fails when transplanted to current
gfx1151, so it is a portability rejection rather than a positive for this
scope. Every control is bit-stable across three repeats.

Freeze the proposed envelope unchanged: mean/p95/p99/max KL
`1e-3/5e-3/2e-2/5e-2`, overall/per-scope top-1 `99%/97%`, and KL `2e-2` as a
manual-review boundary. All global and scope gates bind. Every applicable task
check must pass its predeclared paired non-inferiority margin; there is no
universal task-score threshold. No runtime route is certified and no
performance claim is made by this unit.
