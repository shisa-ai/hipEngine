## Validation

Numbers come from the packets analysed in `20260830T032618...-3a02c8`; the rollup re-derives
them from the packet files rather than copying prose, and both artifacts carry
`source_packet` paths. Content-exactness is stated at row level: **720** comparisons
(360 AR + 360 MTP) with **0** mismatches for the one-token packet pair. That supersedes the
"**160/160** comparisons" phrasing in `20260830T030710...-be46f9`, which counted the 80 AR
+ 80 MTP *cells*; both are true, the row-level count is the meaningful one.

Two things deliberately not done:
- No public README row. The "Multiple requests" table lives inside the generated
  `TOPLINE:README_HIGHLIGHTS` block; a hand-added line was reverted by
  `sync_benchmark_readme.py --write` on the first run, which is the correct behaviour. A
  new public row needs the export registry, which is a separate unit.
- No best-of-arms row. `max(AR, K3)` against `max(peer AR, peer K3)` is 3 of 8, and
  presenting it beside the same-arm rows would invite comparing a selected arm against a
  peer's fixed arm; the MTP-vs-AR landscape is stated in prose instead.
