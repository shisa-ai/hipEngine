# Screen gfx1151 planar-Q6 WMMA row80

## Summary

Rejected a gfx1151 planar-Q6 48-column x 80-row WMMA geometry. The
extra row tile is BF16-bit exact and helps the wide FFN-down at M1024/M4096,
but it regresses that dominant owner at M512 and the narrow-V owner at M1024.
Even a new wide-only M>=1024 dispatch policy projects below the campaign's >=1%
complete-request gate. Keep the retained 48-column x 64-row producer.

## Changes

- Temporarily increased the planar-qmicro Q6 prefill owner from four to five
  row tiles per wave and changed the matching grid from 64 to 80 rows. The
  candidate preserves the resident layout, K16 WMMA association, and BF16
  output boundary.
- Restored
  `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q6_k_t16_gemv.hip` byte-for-byte
  after the leaf failed. No kernel, dispatch policy, flag, or runtime change
  ships from this unit.
- Added compact rejection evidence at
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-15-gfx1151-qwen38-27b-q6-planar-row80-rejected.json`.

## Validation

- Actual immutable K17,408/N5,120 FFN-down and K5,120/N1,024 attention-V
  weights cover 77,414,400 source bytes. Three warmups plus 15 alternating HIP
  event pairs per role/row are BF16-bit exact, finite, and teardown-clean.
- Wide FFN-down retained/candidate speedup is **0.9039x/1.0399x/1.0501x** at
  M512/M1024/M4096 with 0/15, 15/15, and 15/15 wins. Narrow-V is
  **0.9968x/0.9347x/1.1636x** with 6/15, 0/15, and 15/15 wins.
- Applying row80 globally projects -24.57/+15.86/+90.20 ms at 512/1K/4K,
  or **-1.65%/+0.52%/+0.73%** of the retained complete-prefill wall. A
  wide-only M>=1024 route projects only about 0.54%/0.68%, below the >=1% gate.
- Source restoration passes `git diff --exit-code`; the failed leaf does not
  justify an expensive full-model shape or natural gate.
