# Rank disabled Qwen4Exp families by recoverable prefill time

## Summary

Measured all nine disabled prefill families on one real production prefill
(`code-p4096`) and ranked them by seconds recoverable, as the task asked, rather
than by historical headline gain. The largest is `Q8_IU8_WMM` at **1.388x
(+6.614 s)**. The four largest measured **together** give **1.493x (+7.808 s)**,
well below the +13.094 s their individual rows sum to, so the sum is not a
reachable number.

The risk-criterion trigger rate is the most useful single result. It is
**0.711-0.716% across all nine candidates**, including the two that are
bit-identical to the fallback and the one that is slower. That is expected once
the mechanism is visible: the risk queue comes from the always-on exact
`Q4_IU8_EXACT` route, which the fallback already runs. Enabling another family
does not add risk to the iu8 path. So an exact risk-plus-repair variant is not
what makes these families expensive, and a corrected arithmetic would not have to
pay a materially larger repair cost than the default already pays.

## Changes

- `scripts/qwen4exp_disabled_family_sweep.py` (new). Runs the production prefill
  once as the current default and once per family with a post-binder
  `--override`, then ranks by seconds recoverable. `--reuse-scratch` rebuilds the
  ranking from runs already on disk instead of re-measuring, which matters
  because one sweep is about 40 minutes.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-16-disabled-family-recoverable-time/` (new).
  `sweep.json`, `combined-top4.json`, the eleven raw harness outputs, and a
  README.

## Validation

Diagnostic measurement, not a rate row. No performance claim is made and no
candidate is promoted.

- Harness: `scripts/qwen4exp_profile_gap.py --mode prefill --risk-diagnostics`,
  two repetitions per arm, median reported, post-binder overrides recorded in
  each run's `route_env`.
- `named_profile_intact` is **false** for every candidate, which is expected:
  these are diagnostic overrides and none of these configurations is a supported
  profile state.
- Repair rate and `over_capacity_calls` are read from the opt-in
  `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_RISK_DIAGNOSTICS` instrument added in `768c17f23`.
- The combined run is a measurement, not arithmetic on the individual rows.

## What this does not establish

- One case at one shape. The family shares differ at 512 and 1024 and this sweep
  says nothing about them.
- Two repetitions per arm. Ranks 5-9 span 0.066-0.228 s, which is inside the
  run-to-run spread this project has already measured; those rows mean "no
  material effect", not a ranking.
- No numerical evaluation. Six of the nine already differ from the fallback at
  the final prefill token's logits hash. That is not a quality gate and it is
  also not nothing: a full evaluation is a precondition for any of them.
- `GR_IU8` and `GR_IU8_DOWN` already have recorded numerical rejections in
  `docs/REFACTOR.md` (780-row gate, mean KL 0.0014024 and 0.0013128). Their
  speedups are what a *corrected* arithmetic would be worth, not an argument to
  re-enable the unchanged route.

## Next
