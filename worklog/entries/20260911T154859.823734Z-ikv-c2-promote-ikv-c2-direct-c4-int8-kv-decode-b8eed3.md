# Promote IKV-C2 direct C4 INT8 KV decode

## Summary

Audited IKV-C2 and found it already implemented but inert. The row-batched
24Q/4KV/D256 INT8 split-K producer and its strided BF16 reducer, the runtime
dispatch (`kv_live_spans_int8_batch`), and the packed-AR multi-session wiring
all existed; the only thing keeping serving on physical C1 was the gfx1100
capability entry declaring `max_direct_rows = 1`. Promoting it to 4 makes the
packed direct INT8 decode the default C>1 serving route on the declared
hardware. Measured on the W7900, aggregate complete-request throughput over the
same-server serial control moves from 1.0016x to 1.2468x at C2 and from 1.0121x
to 1.4240x at C4, with `serial_decode_fallback_steps` going from 62/64 to 0.

## Changes

- `hipengine/models/qwen35.py`: gfx1100 Qwen3.8-27B INT8 KV capability
  `max_direct_rows` 1 -> 4 and scope
  `explicit_no_mirror_c1_direct_c4_serial` -> `explicit_no_mirror_direct_c4`,
  with the reason rewritten to state why the retained quality artifact still
  applies. The `decode_batch_variant` field already named the batch variant.
- `scripts/qwen38_int8_packed_transition_gate.py` (new): the serving-lifecycle
  gate. Prefills six sessions through the exact scalar path, then replays a
  4 -> 2 -> 4 lane schedule (lanes 1 and 2 retire after step 2 and are refilled
  by two newcomers at step 4) as packed steps, diffing every active row's token,
  logits, captured hidden state, and Conv/GDN + INT8 KV payload/scale state
  against its own c1 trajectory, and asserting excluded retired lanes stay
  byte-frozen.
- `scripts/qwen38_int8_batch_decode_gate.py`: added
  `_live_kv_payload_nbytes` and an optional `kv_live_nbytes` argument to
  `_capture_state`, and replaced the hardcoded `expected_device` XTX string with
  a rocm-smi product-name lookup for the selected `HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES` slot
  plus an `--expected-device` override.
- `scripts/gguf_server_cwidth_probe.py`: added `--server-log-dir` (server
  stdout/stderr was previously discarded, which made a failed width
  undiagnosable) and `--min-vram-free-gib` with a `vram_free_gib` helper that
  skips a width instead of measuring it when another process still holds VRAM.
- `tests/test_qwen38_int8_packed_transition_gate.py` (new, 12 CPU tests):
  schedule invariants — unique in-range lanes per step, exactly one narrowed
  step immediately after the retirement step, retired roles never outliving it,
  newcomers only after a lane frees, and a steady lane surviving the transition.
- `tests/test_qwen38_int8_kv_capability.py`: capability id, scope, and
  `max_direct_rows` updated; the `..._keeps_c1_limit` test renamed to
  `..._at_direct_c4` and its `_qualified_kv_decode_batch_route` expectation
  moved from 1 to 4.
- `tests/test_gguf_server_cwidth_probe.py`: three tests for the VRAM helper and
  a guard-ordering test asserting the skip check runs before the server launch.
- Docs: `docs/QWEN38-INT8-KV-CONTINUOUS.md` status/ledger/promotion sections
  reconciled, `docs/SERVER-DIRECT-PARITY-ROADMAP.md` P5 status updated,
  `docs/KERNELS.md` catalog line notes the qualified c4 width,
  `benchmarks/README.md` gains a C>1 INT8 KV route subsection and a refreshed
  `Last updated`, and `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md` gains the dated one-liner.

Measurement hazard found and fixed rather than worked around: two promoted
attempts failed with `HIP error 2: out of memory` while a previous probe's
server still held roughly 16 GiB (the failing C2 server started with 0.66 GiB
free against 21.86 GiB in the healthy run). That is external device pressure,
not a route change — the packed route's extra workspace is megabytes, not
gigabytes — and it is now caught by `--min-vram-free-gib` so a poisoned phase is
skipped instead of published.

## Next

- Exact cancellation during an in-flight packed prefill/decode mix is still
  unqualified; the transition gate covers retire/admit and row isolation, not a
  cancellation racing a packed step.
- Packed decode graphs and the MTP native spec target graph still require BF16
  KV, so the promoted route applies to eager packed decode only.
- Batched prefill remains IKV-C3-blocked; the promoted width does not claim
  shared prefill ownership.
- Per-request latency at C>N is measured but not published as a topline figure.
