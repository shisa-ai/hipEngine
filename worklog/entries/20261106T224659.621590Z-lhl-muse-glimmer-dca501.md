# Muse Glimmer AR projection wall: int8 WMMA slower than bf16

## Summary

The Q4 prefill projection is compute-bound near the practical bf16 WMMA peak
on gfx1151 (shared_b/w64, 22.9 TFLOPS at ffn_gate 6656->19968 rows=512). Every
int8 WMMA variant tried is slower: the per-subblock scale/min/activation-sum
application keeps integer (AGPR) and float (VGPR) accumulators live and spills
to LDS, adding traffic the bf16 dequant-to-LDS path avoids. The restructured
OUT_TILES=1 / launch_bounds=2 variant was correct (cosine 0.99983) but 0.21x
bf16 (28 vs 5.9 ms). int8 dp4a has ~1/4 the WMMA compute rate. Reverted.
