# Qwen4Exp exact fused Q5 down weighted decode

## Summary

Promoted an exact c1 fusion of selected Q5_1 down projection with routed
weighted sum. Counterbalanced graph decode improves 13.379 to 13.523 tok/s
(+1.06%) on zbook/gfx1151 with fused/unfused BF16 bits, full logits, and IDs
exact.

## Changes

- Added and registered `selected_weighted_sum_logical256_t64_bf16_bf16_out`.
- One CTA per H=2560 output processes route slots in order, retains every strict
  Q5 partial/reduction and route BF16 rounding, then replays the original F32
  routing-weight `fmaf` chain.
- Made it the c1 Q5-down default; separate exact down and weighted kernels remain
  the cN and strict fallback chain.

## Validation

- `HIPENGINE_HIP_ARCH=gfx1151 uv run pytest -q tests/test_qwen4_exp_q5_1_selected.py -x`
  — 3 passed; fused combine BF16 bits equal the separate kernels.
- Counterbalanced one-residency `S/C/C/S/S/C`, p1 + 8 warm + 32 measured:
  median 2.391767 to 2.366419 s, 13.379231 to 13.522543 tok/s; every full-logit
  SHA and generated ID exact.
- `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` recorded the expected
  `q5_1_selected_weighted_sum_logical256_t64_bf16_bf16_kernel`; target launches
  fell 3,822 to 2,016 and cycle-wall 369.240914 to 313.534702 ms (-15.09%).
- Evidence:
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-29-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-exact-q5-fused-weighted-decode.json`.
