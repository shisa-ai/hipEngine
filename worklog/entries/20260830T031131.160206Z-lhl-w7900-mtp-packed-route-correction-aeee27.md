# W7900 Qwen3.8: correct the packed-route claim and the counter attribution method

## Summary

Two of my own claims are wrong and are corrected here, with the method that settles them.

1. **"MTP stays on the c1 route at every width / packed verify cycles never dispatch" is
   false.** It came from a C1-only diagnostic, where packing is impossible by definition.
   Per-arm deltas from the retained K3 packet show MTP reaching the packed route at every
   width >= 2: 24, 12, 53, 27, 57, 39, 86 packed decode steps at C2..C8 (0 at C1). The
   width-dependent acceptance drop (0.75 at C1 down to 0.57-0.66 at C2+) therefore happens
   inside a packed verify path that is engaged, not because cycles fail to pack.
2. **"Packet route counters are unattributable" (my REFACTOR item an hour ago) is also
   wrong.** They are cumulative across the process, but the packet's cell list order is
   execution order, and inside a cell the `order` field says which arm's snapshot came
   first, so differencing gives exact per-arm-per-prompt attribution. My earlier failed
   attempt used the `order` field as if it were a cell sequence, which is why every delta
   collapsed onto one arm.

## Changes

No code. Corrects `20260830T030945...-50b888` (which cited "0 graph replays for MTP" - a
bad reading) and supersedes the REFACTOR paragraph claiming the counters cannot be
attributed.

## Validation

Method: walk cells in list order; inside a cell take the first-listed arm's snapshot, then
the second's; each difference is that arm's work. Applied to the retained K3 packet every
delta is non-negative, which is the check that the ordering assumption holds - a wrong
order produces negative deltas immediately.

| C | AR packed | AR c1 | MTP packed | MTP c1 |
| - | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | 253 | 0 | 2 |
| 2 | 220 | 20 | 24 | 13 |
| 4 | 240 | 20 | 53 | 15 |
| 8 | 280 | 20 | 86 | 28 |

What the table does not say: MTP's own draft/verify loop barely touches these counters (at
C1 only 2 steps attribute to MTP), so the packed-vs-unpacked ratio of MTP *work* is not
recoverable this way - it settles only that the route is entered. Whether verify time is
host-launch bound stays the verify profile's question.

Consequence for the running experiment: the `serial_exact` A/B is better motivated, not
moot - it tests whether the engaged packed verify path is what costs acceptance.

Also fixing names I got wrong in prose: the knob is `HIPENGINE_GGUF_MTP_VERIFY_MODE`
(values `native`, `serial_exact`), not `GGUF_MTP_VERIFY_IMPL`; the bench has
`--mtp-request-mode` and no `--mtp-mode`, so an arm written with `--mtp-mode` dies at
argparse. The arm actually running uses the env var and is fine.
