# RT-1 re-measured: chunk 4096 is 4.94% faster, bit-identical, not promoted

## Summary

`RT-1` was the ledger's largest remaining lever and its named next action was to
re-measure 2048/4096 against today's default. Measured: **chunk 4096 is 4.94%
faster** than the 1024 default on `code-p4096`, with **bit-identical output**.

## Result

Three repetitions per arm, one process per arm:

| chunk | samples (s) | median | within-arm spread |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1024 (default) | 15.642, 15.625, 15.613 | **15.625** | 29.3 ms |
| 4096 | 14.977, 14.854, 14.841 | **14.854** | 135.4 ms |

**-771.9 ms, -4.94%.** Separation between medians is **5.70x** the larger
within-arm spread and the arms do not overlap, so the effect is established at
this sample size rather than read against zero.

A 1-rep screen covered 2048 as well: 15.665 / 14.957 / 14.761 s for
1024/2048/4096 (-4.52%, -5.77%). **The median supersedes the -5.77%** - the
single sample overstated it, which is exactly why I did not record the screen.

## Output is identical

`token_id = 248068` and `logits_sha256 = 7270a972da43ff4642e557b1` on every arm
at every repetition, `fell_back_to_strict = False`, `named_profile_intact = True`.
Chunk size changes scheduling, not arithmetic.

## Cost

Peak allocation 84.44 -> 86.09 -> 89.40 GB for 1024/2048/4096, so 4096 costs
**+4.96 GB (+5.9%)**. Recorded as an admission trade, not a free win.

## Protocol

Recovered from the recorded command in the `2026-09-28-qwen4exp-current-attribution`
artifact, so all arms share case, fixture, execution profile and compiler-version
file, with `--require-cached-build` keeping `hipcc` out of the measured process.

## Not promoted, and why

Two concrete blockers, both recorded rather than waved at:

1. The numerical gate must be re-run at the new chunk size (`X-6`). A chunk
   change moves every row count the gate exercises.
2. `RT-3` records that short-request costs historically kept 1024 as the default,
   and this is one long case. The right form is a **shape-based chunk policy** -
   4096 for long prompts, 1024 for short - not a blanket default flip.

Also not claimed: one case, one host, prefill only; not category/heldout
qualification, not a decode or MTP result. The September 15 engine A/B's +3.94%
is neither confirmed nor contradicted (different date, changed composition) and
is not presented as confirmed.
