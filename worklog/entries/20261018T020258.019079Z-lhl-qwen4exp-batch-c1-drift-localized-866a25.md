# Batch-vs-c1 decode drift localized to two kernel pairs

## Summary

The C1-versus-batch logit gap recorded as a blocker in
`20261018T015123.029605Z-lhl-qwen4exp-batch-real-smoke-divergence-1f8595.md` is
localized. On a short prompt with one row, every policy and argument the two
paths share is bit-identical; the only two differing computations are kernel
pairs that implement the same math with a different reduction schedule, and the
resulting drift measures below this repo's own single-ulp round-off control.

No production file changed: the taps are host-side wrappers in `/tmp` around
existing runtime entry points.

This is evidence for **one short-context, single-row case**. It is not a
statement that the batch runner is free of semantic bugs: the sparse
index-selection path above `dense_equivalent_limit` (2051 positions for
UD-Q4_K_XL) was never exercised, and neither was multi-row composition at
depth.

## Changes

Host: AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ PRO 395, gfx1151. Checkpoint
`/models/gguf/unsloth-Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-UD-Q4_K_XL/UD-Q4_K_XL`, 48 layers
(36 GDN, 12 QSA), PLE at layer 1, 24 query / 2 KV heads, head_dim 256, index
ratio 4, block budget 512. One shared weight residency, one row, prompt
`[9707, 11, 1879, 264, 4337]`, teacher-forced step `13`,
`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_MOE_GRAPH=0 HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_LAYER_GRAPHS=0`.

Per-layer residual taps, installed after c1's prefill and after the batch
import so each side records exactly one decode step (49 stages each):

- Layers 0-7 (including the PLE stage and the layer-3 QSA layer) are
  **bit-identical**.
- First differing computation is at **layer 3 (QSA)**: `q_projected`,
  `key_projected`, `value_projected`, post-RoPE `query`/`key` and `gate` are
  bit-identical; only the attention `context` differs, `max|d| 7.15e-7`
  (rel 2.2e-7). The layer's BF16 residual output is still identical.
- First differing residual is at **layer 8 (GDN)**: `max|d| 7.63e-6` on
  `|a|max 0.26`, in 1 of 10240 elements (one BF16 ulp). Within that layer,
  `gr_attn`, `mix_qkv`, `mix_gate`, `mix_alpha`, `mix_beta`, `mix_conv`,
  `after_attn` and `moe` are all bit-identical; only `mix_core` differs,
  `8.94e-8` (rel 4.7e-7).
- Deterministic amplification: 1 element at layer 8, 9 at layer 9, 886 at
  layer 10 (rel 1.9e-3), rel 1.6e-2 by layer 33, `max|d| 3.13e-2` (rel 7.1e-3)
  at layer 47, final logits `max|d| 9.01e-2` mean 1.26e-2.

The two kernel pairs:

1. QSA dense decode attention: c1 `qwen35_paged_full_attn_decode_context_bf16_spans`
   versus batch `qwen4_exp_qsa_sparse_attention_paged_bf16_rows_f32`, over the
   same positions.
2. GDN recurrence: c1 `qwen4_exp_gdn_decode_f32` versus batch
   `qwen4_exp_gdn_decode_rowsplit_indexed_f32`. The batch kernel's docstring
   states it: same operands and outputs, "differs from the column-major decode
   owner by FP32 summation order".

Corroborating A/B: with `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_GDN_DECODE_ROWSPLIT=1` c1 moves to
its rowsplit variant, the GDN difference leaves the first-divergence list (the
first nonzero moves to the layer-3 QSA) and total drift grows to rel 1.5e-1 at
layer 47 - two summations of one function, neither privileged.

## Round-off comparison, and what it is not

Three short prompts, one teacher-forced step each, drift measured both ways:

```
batch-vs-c1     : mean KL 3.067e-03  max KL 8.954e-03  max|dlogit| 0.796  top-1 3/3
one-ulp control : mean KL 1.550e-02  max KL 2.712e-02  max|dlogit| 1.092  top-1 3/3
```

The control flips one BF16 ulp in one element of the **residual stream** after
the embedding repeat. That is deliberately **not** the normative calibration of
`docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` section 6.1, which flips one ulp in one element of
one **prompt embedding row** and reruns `strict` once per seed on the long
numerical prompts. The residual-stream variant is a cheaper local control of the
same class, not the gate's control arm, and it is **not production admission**.
The drift being 5x below this local control supports "round-off class" for these
three rows; it does not admit the batch path against the calibrated envelope,
which binds against the strict teacher on the full row set.
