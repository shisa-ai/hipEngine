# Reject grouped packed FFN-down residual stores

## Summary

Rejected and removed exact grouped Q4/Q6 packed-target FFN-down plus
rounded-BF16 residual-store candidates. The current C5/C7/C8 Q4 leaves lose at
the required actual-weight operation-complete gate, while planar Q6 is only
noise-flat to +0.33%; no marker or category+heldout product suite was spent.
The retained grouped projections plus standalone `gguf_bf16_add` remain the
optimal registered chain.

This follows `docs/BRIDGESPEC-AUDIT.md` B1: first audit/measure the existing
rowtile owner on actual operation shapes and stop before product-gate spending
when the operation-complete family does not improve. No BridgeSpec source or
launch constants were copied.

## Changes

- RED candidate tests named the missing grouped row6/row8 Q4 and planar-Q6
  `linear+residual` owners and required BF16-bit equality to each retained
  projection followed by the standalone BF16 add.
- The temporary candidate parameterized the retained Q4 WG64 and Q6 WG128
  grouped kernels with a compile-time residual-store owner. It preserved each
  projection's K/FMA/reduction sequence and first BF16 boundary, then consumed
  BF16 residual bits, added in FP32, and rounded the final BF16 output.
- A temporary backend-declared, registry-only segment plan proved the intended
  packed route without backend/quant branches in runtime: C5/C6 R24 used one
  grouped-R8 composite, C7 R28 used grouped-R8 R24 plus strict R4, and C8 R32
  used one grouped-R8 composite. An explicit environment zero restored the
  primitive chain during the screen.
- Removed all candidate kernels, wrappers, registrations, dispatch policy,
  environment seam, and candidate-only tests after the leaf stop gate failed.
- Recorded the compact rejection artifact at
  `benchmarks/results/2026-09-02-w7900-q4km-grouped-down-residual-rejected.json`
  and updated `docs/KERNELS.md` plus `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`; no topline metric
