# Correction: DP-2/DP-5 are priced and open, not stale

## Summary

**Correction.** In `worklog/entries/20260929T155624.495299Z-lhl-qwen4exp-dp3-split-operand-rejected-5409a9.md`
I wrote that `DP-2`/`DP-5` are "stale, not pending" because F16 activation staging was
promoted on 2026-09-26. **That claim is wrong**, and this entry supersedes it.

## What I got wrong

I read the ledger's *description* column and treated it as the status, and I
conflated two different things:

- The 2026-09-26 promotion bound the **cast + `f16in` composite** - a separate
  cast launch feeding the F16-input matmul. That is what runs today.
- `DP-5` is about **eliminating that cast** by having the producing kernel write
  the F16 copy directly into its epilogue. Different change, not done.

The ledger was already current and I was not. Its `DP-5` final column reads:

> **PRICED (`20260929T`)**: the X-3 capture at `2789c19d6` totals F16 staging
> casts at **131.001 ms** of a 15,741.963 ms kernel union (0.83%) and a
> 16,319.888 ms request.

and it explicitly flags that this is **17.5x** the 7.478 ms figure the row
previously cited, so the old "only 7.478 ms" framing understated it by more than
an order of magnitude. It also records the state correctly as "**Not yet built**".
`DP-2`'s final column is `OPEN, low value`.

**I did not modify either row**, so no ledger damage was done - the wrong claim
exists only in the superseded worklog entry.

## Why this matters more than the correction itself

The ledger's final column is the status and it is accurate, including on rows
where the description column reads like an open estimate. The index I needed was
there the whole time; I used the wrong column and then reported the ledger as
stale on the strength of my own misreading.

This is the same defect class I spent this session attributing elsewhere - a
label read as narrower or broader than what it covers - and it was mine. It is
also the more consequential direction: claiming finished work is stale invites
redoing it.
