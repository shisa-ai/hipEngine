# Add bounded split-K compact DMS attention

## Summary

Replaced the long-context LDS blocker with a bounded 256-row split-K compact
attention producer/reducer. The existing one-workgroup body remains the strict
small-live fallback; device-backed DMS automatically selects split-K above 256
rows and can address the ideal CR2 live counts for 128K and 256K.

## Changes

- Added deterministic compact split producers carrying per-split `(m,l,P@V)`
  and a global reducer; LDS is fixed at 2,048/1,024 bytes instead of
  `8 * max_live_count` bytes.
- Added raw wrapper and `grouped_gqa_splitk` registry variant with gfx1151 peer
  aliasing.
- Added lazy bounded workspace ownership and direct-pointer attention over
  persistent compact metadata.
- Extended numerical, deterministic, registry, and cached-only profiler gates.

## Validation

- Split-K/affected backend bundle: 19 passed on gfx1151; earlier direct storage
  bundle remains 39/39.
- Multi-tile numerical fixture (up to 513 live rows) passes KL/top-1 and is
  bit-deterministic.
- Explicit ideal CR2 live-count probes pass:
  - 128K: 65,664 rows/head, 257 splits, 268,959,744 K/V bytes,
    6,365,376 workspace bytes, max abs `2.03e-08`, KL `1.23e-15`, top-1 and
    deterministic pass; launch+readback 57.3 ms.
  - 256K: 131,200 rows/head, 513 splits, 537,395,200 K/V bytes,
    12,705,984 workspace bytes, max abs `1.69e-08`, KL effectively zero,
    top-1 and deterministic pass; launch+readback 74.1 ms.
  These are single-layer primitive probes, not full-model performance claims.
- Cached-only profiler records producer/reducer identities at fixed LDS
  2,048/1,024, scratch0, VGPR16. Trace SHA-256
  `bb504658a751ba3bf808ff7574b6ba8b87ac6ec89941397a1617773d8627979a`.
