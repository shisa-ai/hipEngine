# Review round 3: tokenized category gate, complete predicate, capability-declared owner contract

## Summary

All five round-3 findings are resolved. The repaired category gate
tokenizes its prompts and, run for real, splits the campaign's verdict:
UD-Q4_K_S passes every binding statistic while UD-Q4_K_M fails the absolute
max-row ceiling on a single position - the Q3_K W4A16 prefill route's 1-ULP

## F1 [P1] Category prompts were not tokenized - FIXED, and it changed the verdict

The gate prepended 8 random IDs to the first 64 UTF-8 bytes of each prompt.
The repaired gate (`scripts/gguf_ud_combined_stack_gate.py
--category-heldout`) tokenizes each fixture with
`Qwen35GGUFTokenizer` + the chat template, then extends to 512 tokens with
the incumbent arm's own continuation, so the instruction content is the
real fixture and the prefill window crosses the 129-row owner boundary
(tokenized prompts alone are 39-71 tokens). The candidate is teacher-forced
on the incumbent's tokens.

**K_S passes** every statistic (pooled mean 8.9e-5 / p95 4.2e-4 /
p99 8.5e-4 / max 5.1e-3 / top-1 99.83%).

**K_M FAILS the absolute max-row ceiling**: one position
(code_lru_cache, decode step 1) reaches KL 9.4e-2 (ceiling 5e-2); mean
2.5e-4, p95 4.6e-4, p99 3.3e-3 all pass; top-1 is 100% on the worst prompt.
Attribution (each arm measured on the same forced tokens):

- Q3_K W4A16 prefill route: 5.33e-2 max alone; dropping it from the policy
  makes the entire rest of the shipped stack (IQ4_XS dual, IQ4_NL, IQ3_S,
  IQ2 additions, decode strict) **bit-exact** vs the incumbent.
- IQ4_XS local32 decode: 2.67e-2 max alone (mean 1.5e-3 on that prompt).
- coop32-vs-one-wave for Q3_K is bit-exact; the divergence is
  W4A16-vs-strict itself. Layer level (blk.0.ffn_up, rows=512): 1.17% of
  bf16 outputs differ by exactly 1 ULP, max 1 ULP - the f32
  accumulation-order class, no arithmetic bug. The model-level KL is that
  class amplified through 60 layers of MoE routing.

**Calibration**: the incumbent 4-quant tree itself measures mean 2.28e-4 /
max 4.24e-2 vs all-strict on the same 18 prompts - the admitted baseline
breaches the 2e-2 diagnosis threshold against exact arithmetic. The
absolute 5e-2 ceiling sits below the reference's own noise tail on natural
prompts; every earlier qualification under-sampled it (9 forced positions,
random-token floors, the 162-row gate).

**Decision open** (not unilaterally taken): revert the Q3_K prefill route
(+26% UD-only prefill, 550.9 -> 695.5 ms per 512-token prefill on the A/B;
plain K_M has no Q3_K tensors so the 0.950x claim inverts) or amend the
max-row ceiling with the calibration evidence. The perf numbers stand as
measurements either way; artifacts and README say so.
