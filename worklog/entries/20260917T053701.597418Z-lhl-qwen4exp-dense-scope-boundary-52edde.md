# The dense scope boundary is measured: 16-47 passes, 12-47 and 8-47 fail

## Summary

Priority 2 in `docs/QWEN4EXP-STATUS.md` carried a ~2.5 s estimate for extending
the wide-row dense route below layer 16, on the premise that the wide route's
accumulation order differed from the WMMA arm that failed at `0-47`. That premise
is wrong, and the item is now measured and closed.

**The wide route is a retiling of the f16 WMMA arithmetic, not a reassociation.**
Both routes emit `candidate_logits_sha256 65370710c0b9c40f` and the same top-1
agreement at layers 16-47, in two separate gate artifacts, so the route inherits
the WMMA arm's `0-47` failure (mean KL 1.099e-3) rather than escaping it.

Two full gates settle the boundary: **12-47 fails** on category `general_en`
(mean 1.089e-3, p95 6.06e-3, both over the same limits the global envelope
passes), and **8-47 fails** on max KL (7.30e-2 against 5e-2) and global top-1
(1532/1548 = 0.98966). Drift grows monotonically with the number of f16 layers,
`general_en` binds at every scope, and the promoted 16-47 scope already spends
about 40% of that category's headroom.

At most a few extension layers remain, worth at most ~0.4 s by the measured
per-layer costs, at the numerical limit and with a real chance of failing their
own gate. Reopening the item needs an arithmetic change, not a scope change.

## Evidence

Protocol for both runs: `scripts/execution_profile_q8_wmma_prefill_layers_gate.py
--route dense_wide --layers <scope> --decode-steps 128 --repeat-runs 3
--prefill-chunk-size 1024`, Qwen4Exp UD-Q4_K_XL canonical exact-token fixture,
12 cases (4 categories at 512/1024/4096), production profile, teacher the exact
coltile chain with both selectors cleared in its arm.

| Scope | Rows | Mean KL | p95 KL | Max KL | Top-1 | Verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 16-47 (promoted) | 1548 | 3.80e-4 | 1.76e-3 | 1.53e-2 | 0.99354 | pass |
| 12-47 | 1548 | 6.47e-4 | 3.61e-3 | 2.25e-2 | 0.99031 | **fail** (`general_en`) |
| 8-47 | 1548 | 8.44e-4 | 4.07e-3 | **7.30e-2** | **0.98966** | **fail** (max KL, top-1) |

Per-category means, same three runs:

| Scope | ALL | `code` | `general_en` | `general_ja` | `mixed_ja_en` |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 16-47 | 3.80e-4 | 4.10e-4 | **5.24e-4** | 3.40e-4 | 2.45e-4 |
| 12-47 | 6.47e-4 | 4.81e-4 | **1.089e-3** | 5.76e-4 | 4.44e-4 |
| 8-47 | 8.44e-4 | 8.04e-4 | 9.79e-4 | 8.69e-4 | 7.25e-4 |

The aggregate is monotone in scope depth. `general_en` is not (1.089e-3 at 12-47
against 9.79e-4 at 8-47) while remaining the maximum at every scope, so
per-category values carry roughly 15% spread and the trend is the finding.

The bit-identity claim is read from the artifacts, not re-derived: the 16-47
wide gate and the 16-47 WMMA gate both carry
`strict_logits_sha256 550bb9b832bc7ba2…` and `candidate_logits_sha256
65370710c0b9c40f…`, with identical top-1 agreement (0.99354) and identical
per-category values.

## Validation

Both runs are the full production gate protocol at 1548 rows with three candidate
repeats, so the RED/GREEN state is the harness's own verdict rather than an
inspection: `hard_gates_passed: false` for 12-47 and 8-47, with scope failures
recorded (`general_en`; `code`/`c1`/`steady`), and `requires_outlier_review: true`
for both. No code changed in this unit, so no other test is applicable.

## What this does not establish

- **Not a rejection of the promoted route.** Layers 16-47 keep their
  certification; only deeper scopes fail.
- **Not a statement about 13-47, 14-47 or 15-47.** They were not run. The
  measured facts are 8-47 fail, 12-47 fail, 16-47 pass with 2.6x headroom.
- **Not a certificate for anything else.** Both artifacts are
  `timing_protocol: none_full_logits_only_v1` with `performance_claim: false`;
  they say nothing about task quality or long-form factuality, and nothing about
  quants other than `gguf_ud_q4_k_xl`.
- **Not a proof that no arithmetic can reach 0-47.** The suggested route is a
  split-operand (f16x2) wide variant: it attacks the operand rounding that binds
  here at roughly 3x the f16 matmul cost (hi·hi + hi·lo + lo·hi), which is still
  about 2x faster than the exact chain's 17.3 ms per launch on this role set. That
  is an estimate from the kernel's structure, not a measurement.
