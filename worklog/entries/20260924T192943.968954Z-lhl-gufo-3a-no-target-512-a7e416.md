# GUFO §3A epilogue fusions have no 512-row target on the selected-WMMA route

## Summary

The GUFO campaign's primary mechanism (§7 item 3 / §3A norm/activation-to-Q8_1
epilogue fusions) has no measurable target on the route Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_M
actually selects at 512 rows, measured 2026-09-24 on gfx1151:

- A full prefill kernel trace dispatchs **zero activation-quantize kernels**.
  The three standalone passes §3A names (`gguf_q4_k_quantize_bf16_q8_1`,
  `gguf_q8_1_d4s4_f32_quantize_bf16`, `gguf_q8_0_mmq128_quantize_bf16_d4x3`)
  are registered imports that never run on this route. Activations stay BF16
  from the fused gate/up SiLU GEMM epilogue into the next GEMM — gufo's c192
  pass elimination is already structural here (one pass fewer than their Q8_1
  chain).
- The only standalone passes that do execute are weight-side and in a
  different accumulation-order class: `gguf_q5_k_t16_dequantize_f16_tile_octet`
  (8.2 ms/pass), `bf16_to_f32` (4.2 ms/pass), feeding the hipBLAS `Cijk`
  GEMMs (36.7 ms/pass). Folding or flipping them is a production-profile
  change, not strict-class.
- The remaining strict-class fold (c173 norm into the GEMM prologue) caps at
  12.58 MB saved per norm site; even over-counting all 129 norm dispatches per
  pass gives ~1.6 GB ≈ 1.9 ms at 864 GB/s = **0.14%**, against the +0.31%
  needed to clear the 382.12041 baseline (same-day stock 380.9576). It cannot
  pass keep-revert by construction.
