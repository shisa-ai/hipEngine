# Attribute Q4 layer scopes through child owner reports

## Summary

Selector-ranking and family-sweep reports use the child's Q4 layer owner scope
rather than treating ambient process values as selected routes.

## Changes

- Carry the child scope through ranking, family summaries and per-family rows.
- Exclude the owner key from effective/baseline process maps in ranking reports.
- Compare family requests against effective owner scope, recognizing empty/all
  and range/list equivalence. Current owner records with a missing layer value
  are unknown rather than confirmed by ambient values; legacy environment-only
  reports keep their historical interpretation.
