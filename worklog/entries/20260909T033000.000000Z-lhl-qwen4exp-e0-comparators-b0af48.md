   protocol measures TG 24.0-25.2 (our protocol excludes MTP; their
   headline includes MTP and an unpublished deployment preset). Their
   PP is real.

   requests on our host - consistent with the source review's stale
   union-count performance-path selection.

   digest comparison cannot validate cross-backend correctness. A
   greedy-mode logits cross-check is the required next step before

   remains the pinned baseline per the standing rule.

at PP 709 / TG 18.4 on p4096 - the real gap to the comparator frontier
is larger than the b212548e0-pinned view (HE PP ~257 estimated after
