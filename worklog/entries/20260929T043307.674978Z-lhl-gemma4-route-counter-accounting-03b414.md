# gemma4 expert route counters are exclusive and the tile residency comments are truthful

## Summary

Two reporting defects from the prior review are fixed. Routing and arithmetic are
unchanged.

**The gate/up route counters double counted and misattributed.** The Q4T16 tile
leaf recorded `gate_up_t16` and the caller then recorded `gate_up_mmq32` for the
same projection, so a tile-route launch moved two counters. The Q5_K iu8 leaf
recorded nothing at all, so every iu8 launch was counted as `gate_up_mmq32`, a
route it never ran. The names now come from the one place that knows which leaf
ran -- `gemma4_project_experts_gate_up_mmq` -- and each served projection moves
exactly one counter: `gate_up_mmq32`, `gate_up_t16`, or the new `gate_up_iu8`.
The caller records the ladder route and nothing else. The down projection was
already exclusive (one record per served projection) and is untouched.

**The raw-plus-tiles residency comments understated the cost.** Two of them said
the Q4T16 tiles are "2.78 percent larger" in a way that read as if they stood in
for the raw blocks. The raw blocks are retained, so a Q4_K expert stack holds
2.02778 times its raw bytes, with the tiles alone at 1.02778 times. A third site
the review did not name -- the T16 leaf's own docstring in `gemma4_experts.py`,
which claimed "no second copy of the tensor" -- is corrected with them. The
estimator itself was already correct and was not touched.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/gemma4/gemma4_experts.py`
  - `gemma4_project_experts_gate_up_mmq` records the leaf that served the
    projection and documents the accounting. The iu8 leaf's return value and the
    dispatch order are unchanged.
  - `_gemma4_project_experts_gate_up_wmma_t16` no longer records its own route;
    the record moved to the wrapper's dispatch point.
  - `gemma4_experts_forward_bf16` no longer records `gate_up_mmq32` after the
    wrapper reports success. The ladder record is unchanged.
  - The T16 leaf docstring: "no second copy of the tensor" becomes the second
    resident layout and the 2.02778x total.
- `hipengine/loading/gemma4_gguf_device.py` -- the planner comment on the Q4_K
  expert stack states that both layouts are held and gives the 1.02778x / 2.02778x
  ratios.
- `hipengine/quant/gguf_q4_k.py` -- `repack_gguf_q4_k_tile16_tile_bytes`'s
  docstring states the same ratios and that the raw blocks are kept.
- `tests/test_unit_gemma4_expert_route.py` -- three CPU tests: the wrapper counts
  the leaf that served it (t16 and iu8, parametrized); the expert block counts one
  gate/up route per projection; a declining MMQ leaf leaves the accounting to the
  ladder route.
- `tests/test_unit_gemma4_gguf_device.py` -- the tile price is pinned to the exact
  `(2304 + 2368) / 2304` identity against the block constants, so the ratios the
  comments state are machine-checked rather than restated.
