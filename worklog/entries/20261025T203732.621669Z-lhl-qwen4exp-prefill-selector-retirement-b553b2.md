# Retire the prepared prefill workspace selector

## Summary

R0020's remaining action was to remove the private prepared-prefill selector, not
enable it. The shape policy it existed to provide measured a 1.06% loss on the
small owner at 0.017x the within-arm spread, with identical `logits_sha256`, and
the extra owner cost 1.555 GiB. `docs/REFACTOR.md` revised the disposition to
"remove the selector, do not enable it" and named the chunk-1024 default's tests
as the thing a removal must keep intact.

The selector had no production caller: only
`scripts/qwen4exp_rt1_same_owner_selection.py` and focused tests invoked it.
`_allocate_extra_prefill_workspace` and the ownership/capacity/cleanup contracts
remain.

## Changes

- `hipengine/runtime/qwen4_exp_runner.py`: removed
  `_configure_prefill_workspace_selection`, the `prefill_chunked` selection
  branch, and the `_prefill_workspace_selection` reset in `close()`.
  `prefill_chunked` now always calls `_prefill_chunked_impl`. Kept
  `_allocate_extra_prefill_workspace`, the extra-owner allocation/close paths,
  and the wide-F16 staging capacity rule, which still sizes from the original
  `_prefill_workspaces[0]` owner. The selector's own admission checks (foreign
  or duplicate owners, owners above the original staging capacity, and the
  reserve check) retired with it; no production caller borrows an owner.
- `scripts/qwen4exp_rt1_same_owner_selection.py`: preserved the diagnostic
  evidence's reproducibility by moving the smallest-fitting-owner rule into
  script-local instrumentation. `SELECTION` and `selected_workspace` borrow a
  prepared owner with the benchmark-only `scripts.qwen4exp_chunk_workspace`
  `use_workspace` helper. Report fields `selection_enabled`,
  `selection_choices`, `selector_disabled`, `owners_that_served` and
  `selection_correct` keep their meaning; the docstring and report
  `purpose`/`limits` now state that the production selector is retired and cite
  `audit/refactor-sweep/2026-10-25.json` R0020 plus the preserved
  `benchmarks/results/2026-09-29-qwen4exp-rt1-same-owner-selection/` evidence.
- `tests/test_unit_qwen4exp_same_owner_selection.py`: new deterministic coverage
  for the script-local rule using `simulated_runner` and `capture_workspace`:
  smallest fitting owner, largest owner on overflow, restore of all six prefill
  fields after a failure, and the disabled path.
- `tests/test_unit_qwen4exp_prefill_owner.py`: removed the three
  selector-specific tests. Kept the frozen allocation census, extra-owner
  ownership, foreign-borrow close, and both allocation-failure cleanup tests.
- `tests/test_unit_qwen4exp_runner_f16_workspace.py`: rewrote
  `test_selected_smaller_workspace_keeps_original_staging_capacity` as
  `test_borrowed_smaller_workspace_keeps_original_staging_capacity`, borrowing
  the smaller owner directly instead of configuring the retired selector. The
  contract under test is unchanged: F16 staging is still sized by the original
  32-row owner while a 16-row owner is borrowed.
