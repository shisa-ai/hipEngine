# EAGLE-4 graph device gap closed by rocprof: no replay penalty, verify is GEMV-bound

## Summary

Decision: close the "graph-replay device gap" investigation opened in
20261109T145920...6536ad.md as a MEASUREMENT ARTIFACT. rocprofv3 per-kernel
attribution shows the graph replay and the eager staged-chain verify have
identical device kernel time; there is no graph-replay penalty to optimize.
Supersedes the "~50 ms unattributed graph-replay gap" framing in 6536ad.

rocprofv3 --kernel-trace --marker-trace, ROCTx-tagged graph_verify vs eager_staged
ranges, 6 cycles each, short prompt, post-draft device_synchronize
(`/tmp/eagle4_graph_rocprof.py`, breakdown via scripts/rocprof_marker_breakdown.py):

| range | wall/call | kernels/call | launches/call | gap/call |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| eager_staged | 130.88 ms | 119.09 ms | 1006 | 11.80 ms |
| graph_verify | 130.47 ms | 120.97 ms | 947 | 9.50 ms |

Device kernel time is the same (~120 ms); the graph issues fewer launches (947 vs
1006) and less host/launch gap. The kernel families and per-kernel durations match
almost exactly. The verify is GEMV-bound: the top seven kernels are quantized
matmuls summing to ~108 ms of the ~120 ms —
q4_k_t16_dense_dual_rowtile_silu (39 ms), q6_k_t16_qmicro_planar_rowtile_col8
(24 ms), q5_k_t16_dense_rowtile (11 ms), q4_k_t16_dense_rowtile_residual (10 ms),
q6_k_t16_rowtile_col8 (9 ms), q4_k_t16_dense_rowtile16_w2 (9 ms),
q4_k_t16_dense_rowtile (7 ms). Attention is small (paged full-attn decode 0.7 ms,
GDN recurrent 4.6 ms).

Why the earlier numbers misled: the `sync_stage_timings` "layer_total" stage
(66 ms forced-views / 95 ms shared-cache in /tmp/full_attn_route_ab.py) undercounts
actual device kernel time relative to rocprof's full-kernel sum (119 ms). It is a
host stage-boundary measure, not a device-kernel total, and must not be used to
claim a graph-vs-eager device gap. rocprof kernel-trace is the authoritative device
measurement here.

Reconciled picture of the committed cycle win (9f4049f90, verify 140.8->126.2 ms):
the graph captures the faster staged full-attention route (eager's DEFAULT
short-context path is the slower shared-cache owner), and removes per-cycle host
dispatch. The graph's device floor equals the eager staged device floor (~120 ms).
The graph is doing its job; no graph-route fix is warranted.
