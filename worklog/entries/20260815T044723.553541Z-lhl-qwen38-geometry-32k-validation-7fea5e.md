# Validate Qwen3.8 geometry policies at 32K

## Summary

Validated the pulled geometry-keyed dense policy tree for Qwen3.8-27B on the RX 7900 XTX. A real single-request BF16 server now completes the full 32,768-token admission boundary at 21.869 GiB peak with 2.115 GiB headroom.
