ranked material owner. Fresh markers put dense projection at 34.007 ms and its
normalized historical Vulkan gap at 8.948 ms. The current rows512 down path

  rows512/K3584/N1024 dense-BF16 FFN-down owners. The store rounds the FP32 WMMA
  result to BF16, adds the BF16 residual, then rounds again to preserve the
  primitive chain boundary exactly.
- Keep the registered WMMA projection plus `gguf_bf16_add` chain as fallback;
  add no resident or scratch bytes and do not change routing before the leaf

  FFN-down weights. Continue only for byte-exact output, scratch0, expected
  trace symbol, and a positive stable combined leaf saving. No tile or schedule
  ladder.

  masks it, provided Q4 complete-model blocks are non-regressive and decode,
  Q8, resident bytes, and cumulative semantics pass their guards.
