# The same-route reference resolves it: 0 flips, 0 state mismatches, and every calibrated bar passes

## Summary

**Resolved.** Building the gate's reference prefix through the candidate's own
route family (`prefill_batch_native`, the packed wave the served engine uses)
removes the divergence entirely: 0 flips out of 128 rows, **0 mismatched state
components**, and every §6.1 calibrated bar passes with wide margin. The
126-127 component mismatch that drove this whole investigation was the
cross-route comparison, exactly as the in-tree record predicts ("the routes
persist different final-state arithmetic").

| scope | flips / 128 | mean KL | p99 | max | semantic mismatches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `category-general_ja` | **0** | 0.000194 | 0.003427 | 0.005034 | **0** |
| `heldout-code` | **0** | 7.937e-05 | 0.001962 | 0.002783 | **0** |

Against §6.1 (mean <= `1e-3`, p95 <= `5e-3`, p99 <= `2e-2`, max <= `5e-2`,
top-1 >= `99%` overall and >= `97%` per scope): mean, p99, max and top-1 pass in
both scopes, and p95 is bounded by p99 in each. Compare the same two scopes
against the single-session reference: `category-general_ja` 2 flips, mean
0.022005, max 1.758347; `heldout-code` 2 flips, mean 0.024089, max 3.002259.

## Changes

- `scripts/gguf_prefix_reuse_gate.py`: `--reference-prefill-mode` gains `packed`,
  which builds the reference prefix with `prefill_batch_native([prefix],
  sessions=[oracle_session], full_prompt_lengths=[len(continued_prompt)])`, and
  `packed` is now the **default**. `bulk`, `native`, and `None` remain available
  as diagnostics. 7 unit tests pass.

## Validation

Two scopes at 128 teacher-forced steps, the calibrated packet's commands with
`--reference-prefill-mode packed` (`/tmp/t30-packed-ref`), and the full 1,024-row
packet over both suites re-running with the new default (`/tmp/t30-calibrated-packed`).

`initial_state_mismatches` stays at 128: that comparison walks the suffix one
token at a time against the candidate's batched suffix, so it is cross-shape by
construction and remains a diagnostic, not a binding term.
