# W7900 Qwen3.8: automatic MTP route declines at every width and the AR paired band is measured

## Summary

Two things about the published rows were assumed rather than measured. The **automatic** MTP
route does not engage at all on this host and model, so the K3 row is engine-ranking only;
and AR has a paired cross-session band of about **1.54%** per cell, with **C5 an outlier at
+11.9%**.

## Validation

Automatic arm, protocol matched to the published packet field-by-field (`mtp_request_mode: automatic`, `server_mtp_mode: auto`, same prompts, widths, capacity 8,
max tokens 24, budget 3, window 20 ms, contract `ar_exact`): 80 cells, **0 cells with MTP
engaged**, **0 accepted draft tokens**, packet `status: failed` because
`--expected-mtp-widths` asserted engagement. Decline reasons per width: C1
`artifact_not_qualified`, C2 `resident_capacity_not_qualified`, C3-C8
`physical_group_not_qualified`. The reference packet's explicit arm accepted 5652 of 7164
drafts (0.7889), so the contrast is engagement versus none, not a measurement difference.
The automatic arm's per-width AR-arm rates (C1 22.3, C2 31.3, C3 43.4, C4 54.3, C5 68.2, C6 69.8, C7 73.1, C8 77.4) are AR work under a different
request path, which is why an apparent "automatic MTP beats AR at C8" reading of that packet
is wrong - there was no speculation in it.

Paired AR band, same protocol, prompt-to-prompt, 80 cells: median per-width
delta C1 +1.0%, C2 +1.5%, C3 +1.3%, C4 +1.0%, C5 +11.9%, C6 +0.5%, C7 +0.3%, C8 +0.1%; median absolute per-cell delta **%.2f%%**. That is the number to judge future
single-packet AR claims against, and it says the C5 row of any packet needs a second packet
before it means anything. The published promotion deltas (+3.5%%..+72.2%%) clear this band at
every width including C5, so the promotion is not at risk from it.
