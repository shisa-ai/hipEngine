# Gemma4 D12 resume and BF16 baseline

## Summary

Recovered the completed D11 unit at `b7de13285` in the `gemma4` worktree.
D12's D2/D4 prerequisite is met. The remaining work is implementation:
Gemma allocates BF16 K/V, appends BF16 by memcpy, has no scale planes, and
its attention consumers accept BF16 rather than INT8 payloads. The public
INT8 request therefore still fails with a named implementation miss.

The resumed loop is a quick goal. Use progress logs rather than comparing
its legacy `currentMetric`: previous iterations mixed throughput, wall time,
device-busy time, and a test count. No new performance delta is claimed here.
