# Retain compact per-K-head peer GDN prefill

## Summary

- Promoted a compact gfx1100 prompt-prefill GDN producer/consumer ABI that
  materializes unit-normalized Q/K as `[token, k_head, 128]` instead of
  duplicating them as `[token, v_head, 128]`. Value, beta, and decay remain
  per value head and the admitted wave32/XOR recurrence arithmetic is unchanged.
- The complete prepare + recurrent + RMSNorm/gate leaf is BF16-output-bit and
  FP32-state-bit exact to `chain_peer_wave32`. It saves 14 MiB / 28 MiB of
  transient Q/K at M512/M1024 and adds no persistent bytes.
- Made `chain_compact_peer_wave32` the gfx1100 backend-package default and kept
  `chain_peer_wave32` as fail-closed rollback. gfx1151 remains on its exact
  `chain_lds32_direct_nonvolatile` route.

## Changes

- Added compact prepare/recurrent HIP kernels, raw-pointer wrappers, and
  four-axis registry keys in `linear_attn/gdn.{hip,py}`.
- Added explicit runtime plan members/mode selection and architecture package
  policy without changing decode, packed-segment, native verifier, or MTP
  ownership.
- Added focused RED/source/registry/GPU bit-exact coverage and the reusable
  `scripts/gguf_gdn_compact_peer_bench.py` leaf harness.
- Updated `docs/ENVS.md`, `docs/REFACTOR.md`, the Qwen3.6-27B campaign,
  benchmark scoreboard/changelog, and compact artifact
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-14-qwen36-27b-gdn-compact-peer-retained.json`.
