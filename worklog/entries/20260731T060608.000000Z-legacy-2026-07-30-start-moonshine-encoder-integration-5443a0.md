### Fixed device encoder handoff

- RED fails only on absent `set_encoder_state_from_device`. GREEN zero-pads the fixed resident FP16 hidden/int32 mask bucket on its stream, copies the caller-owned contiguous source prefix with exactly two `DEVICE_TO_DEVICE` operations, synchronizes ownership, invalidates stale cross K/V, and preserves every allocation/address. Pointer, source-frame, active-generation, byte-count/kind, and lifecycle contracts pass in the fake runtime.
- A real compiled-PyTorch-encoder/custom-graph smoke validates same-device pointers across runtimes without host tensor materialization. It pads waveforms to 1/5-second certified encoder buckets, converts the frame mask to device int32, performs D2D handoff plus resident cross-K/V, and reproduces all six optimized-PyTorch token sequences exactly with zero timed hipEngine allocation and clean tracked teardown.
- The one-iteration smoke is diagnostic because the first 207-frame case still overlaps compilation/autotuning. Excluding that compile-contaminated row, observed handoff is roughly 0.04-0.09 ms and complete hybrid rows are roughly 5.4-10.7 ms. Commit, rerun warm repeated measurements from a clean hipEngine tree, and retain only exact full-model evidence.

### Encoder integration keep

- Clean commit `b62c73629` passes all runtime/Moonshine tests and the real D2D hybrid.
