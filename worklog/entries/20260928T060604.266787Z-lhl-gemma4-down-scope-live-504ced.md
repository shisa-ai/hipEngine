# the down divergence runs on the production family so its scope check is the live one

## Summary

`20260928T060316` pinned the down projection as the second row-count divergence
and `20260928T060531` answered the same question for the gate/up: the MMQ route
is invariant at the true geometry, so its defect is a toy-shape artifact and
production is unaffected. The down does **not** get the same answer, and reading
its route guard is enough to see why the question is live rather than academic.

## The down's route guard declines the fixture's quant

`gemma4_project_experts_down_mmq`'s docstring:

> Run the DS4 DP4A MMQ route over a **Q5_1 or Q8_0** expert down projection.

The fixture's `ffn_down_exps` is Q4_K, so this route declines it and the call
falls through to `gemma4_project_experts_rows` -- the fp32 grouped family. That
is the family the direct projection probes cleared, at the fixture's degenerate
shape.

## Why that makes this the live scope question

The gate/up MMQ route is an *optional accelerator*: turning it off restores a
correct path, and its defect at a toy shape affects nothing that runs. The
grouped family is not optional in the same way. It is what **every** expert down
projection takes that is not Q5_1 or Q8_0, which includes the Q4_K artifact this
work is built around. So if the grouped family is row-count dependent at the
true `704 -> 2816` shape, **production prefill and decode disagree** and the
defect is not narrow.

The two defects therefore have opposite dispositions:

| defect | route | disposition if it holds at the true geometry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gate/up | fused MMQ (optional accelerator) | already answered: toy-shape only, production unaffected |
| down | fp32 grouped (the general path) | **production affected -- must be fixed** |

## What the fixture's shape makes degenerate here

The fixture's down runs `in_features = intermediate = 64` against
`out_features = hidden = 256`. Sixty-four input features is **below one Q4_K
block (256 elements) and below one Q8_1 activation group (128 elements)**, so the
K dimension is sub-block in both quantizations at once. The real down is
`704 -> 2816`: 704 is between two and three Q4_K blocks, and the gate/up's
`in_features=256` was *exactly* one. So this is the same class of degenerate
shape that produced the gate/up result, in the opposite direction -- a K below
one block rather than a K of exactly one.

## What is not yet known

Whether the grouped family is row-count invariant at `704 -> 2816`. It is not
established either way, and the previous unit's evidence does not transfer: the
gate/up's answer came from the *MMQ* route at `2816 -> 1408`, which is a
different kernel family from the grouped one.
