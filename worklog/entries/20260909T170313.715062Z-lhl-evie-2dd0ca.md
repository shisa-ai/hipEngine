# EVIE vision f16 stream: rejected on measurement

## Summary

Task #16 (vision full-f16 activation stream to eliminate GEMM cast
traffic) was evaluated and rejected: the task's ~20-30 ms/page estimate
predates the launch-bound profile. Direct instrumentation of the cast
kernels inside a full document-page encode measures **4.97 ms total cast

vision share bounds the campaign (6-7 new f16 kernel variants + runner
rewiring) at ~3-4 ms/page, ~1.5% end to end.
