# Mint the U6 MTP pin at the measured c1 / 1023 envelope

## Summary

U6 item 6 is closed and both UD certification records are complete, so
`_UD_MTP_PRESET_FINGERPRINTS` now carries both fingerprints and the two UD
artifacts derive `GGUF_PRESET_SCOPE_MTP` alongside `ar`.

The declared envelope is **width c1, context 1023, backend hip_gfx1100,
execution profile production**. It is narrower than the U6 design note's
"c1..c8" because the widths were measured, and the measurement says c1 is the
only width that earns admission:

| Cell | AR tok/s | MTP tok/s | MTP / AR | exact | engaged |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| c1 K3 | 13.891 | **34.205** | **2.4624x** | 10/10 | 10/10 |
| c2 K2 | 30.102 | 31.359 | 1.0418x | 10/10 | 10/10 |

- **c2 is correct but not useful.** Both cells hold `ar_exact`; at c2 the
  server path's own AR rate nearly doubles with two requests in flight while MTP
  barely moves, so the speculative advantage falls from 2.46x to 1.04x. An
  evidence row for c2 would widen the admission surface for noise.
- **c4 has no production physical cell.**
  `GGUF_SPECDEC2_MTP2_PHYSICAL_WIDTH_DEPTHS["production"]` is
  `((1, 2), (1, 3), (2, 2), (8, 3))`, and the bench records the refusal.
- **c8 does not fit.** `HIP error 2: out of memory` at capacity 8 on the
  25,753,026,560-byte default GPU against a 16,464,440,224-byte artifact, at
  max-sequence-length 1024, 512 and 256 alike.

Context 1023 is the shared sentinel: the MTP2 adapter refuses at
`prompt + 1 >= min(1023, max_sequence_length)`, and the packed verifier's
`strict_long_rows` term drops to a per-row route at
`start_position + rows >= 1024`, so a longer gate result measures the fallback
rather than the verifier.

## Why this is the plan's intent and not a widening

The U6 item-6 contract is a declaration contract: the record declares the
backend, quant identity, execution profile, maximum context, and serving widths
it authorizes, and scope outside the declaration is not admitted. It does not
require a specific width tuple. `widths` is a per-record field, and the
declaration here is the measured one.

Phase 5's completion definition requires "a populated artifact-scoped U6
certification and derived admission pin", and item 4 requires complete
width/context evidence. Both are now satisfied for the declared scope, with the
out-of-scope cells recorded as measured or structurally absent rather than
silent.

## What changes for a user

A pinned UD artifact gains the MTP scope, so `speculative_mtp` is available on
it without an in-process grant. The measured benefit at the admitted scope is the
retained paired result: `UD-Q4_K_M` MTP 43.830 tok/s against 31.927 AR
(1.3728x) and `UD-Q4_K_S` MTP against AR at 1.81x over its own AR arm, both
with the calibrated numerical and control gates passing. The envelope is c1 and
context below 1023; outside that the strict fallback applies and no speedup is
claimed.
