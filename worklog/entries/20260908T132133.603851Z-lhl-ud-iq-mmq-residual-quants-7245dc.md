# Residual GEMV quants: which are MMQ-expressible, and IQ4_NL held out

## Summary

Resolves the second open item. Determines rigorously which of the residual
GEMV quants can use the dense integer-MMQ route, implements the one that needs
no new table (IQ4_NL), and **holds it out of the default** on a concentrated
numerics cost with the blocker recorded.

## Feasibility determination

The MMQ expansion contract is one float scale plus 32 signed int8 per K32
group. Tested per quant by recovering the group scale as a **real GCD** of the
dequantized magnitudes.

A first probe inferred the scale as `min|nonzero|` and reported IQ4_XS as
*not* expressible - a false negative, since IQ4_XS's codebook contains no
+/-1. That probe was wrong, not the quant; the GCD version reproduces the two
shipped quants correctly and is the one to trust.

| quant | scale granularity | integer range | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IQ4_XS | per 32 | [-127, 127] | shipped |
| IQ3_XXS | per 32 | [-31, 31] | shipped |
| IQ3_S | per 32 | [-15, 15] | expressible; needs its 2 KB grid table ported into the MMQ TU - **not wired** |
| IQ4_NL | per 32 | [-127, 127] | expressible with **no new table**; implemented, held out |
| Q3_K | per **16** | [-128, 128] | blocked - 128 does not fit int8 under a per-32 scale |
| IQ2_S | per **16** | [-1333, 1333] | blocked |
| IQ2_XS | per **16** | [-1333, 1333] | blocked |

IQ4_NL is free because 256 elements are eight consecutive 18-byte blocks, each
K32 group is exactly one of them with its own FP16 scale, and the nibble order
matches IQ4_XS so the existing packer applies unchanged.

## Why IQ4_NL is held out

Kernel-level it is correct and fast: max relative error 0.0052-0.0072 at
correlation 0.999983 against the strict GEMV, crossover **1 row**, up to 16.8x
at 512 rows. Enabling it measured prefill **127.2 -> 155.2 tok/s (+22%)**.

But the context512 teacher gate moves:

| arm | mean | p95 | max | top-1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dense MMQ (default) | 0.0010796 | 0.0051188 | 0.0354152 | 162/162 |
| + IQ4_NL | 0.0013457 | 0.0071143 | 0.0412277 | 161/162 |

The outer gate still passes, but that mean is the **worst of any default
measured**, and the cost is **concentrated rather than diffuse**: the median
per-position delta is exactly 0.000000 and p90 is +0.000925, while a few
positions diverge 100-300x - prompt 4 step 2 goes 0.000225 -> 0.022335, step 3
0.000061 -> 0.019991, prompt 10 step 1 0.000028 -> 0.008484. 89 of 162
positions worsen at all.

That is a small number of sensitive tensors, not general precision loss, so
taking the +22% now would trade a localized quality cliff for throughput
without understanding it. Only 7 IQ4_NL tensors exist in K_M (0.33 GB) across
`attn_qkv`/`ffn_down`/`ffn_gate`/`ffn_up`, so a role- or layer-level exclusion
is likely tractable.
