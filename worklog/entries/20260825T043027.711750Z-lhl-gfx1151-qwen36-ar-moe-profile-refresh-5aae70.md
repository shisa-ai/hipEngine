# Refresh gfx1151 Qwen3.6 AR MoE ownership

## Summary

Captured a clean, current-main AR-only ownership profile for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B
`UD-Q4_K_M` on physical host `zbook` (Radeon 8060S/gfx1151, HIP 7.15) without
touching the active SPECDEC2/MTP2 lane. The five-repeat p512/d128 baseline is
exact at **30.00296 tok/s**. The 24-step marked rocprof capture has positive
kernel durations and ranks GDN input Q8 **4.200 ms/token**, selected-Q4 gate/up
**2.435**, Q6 LM head plus argmax **2.268**, selected-down **1.774**, and GDN
output Q8 **1.487**.

The fresh result does **not** admit another implementation. It reproduces the
same role order already closed by the independent high-power SH campaign. The
obvious exact GDN, selected-Q4, selected-down, shared/combine, LM-head, graph,
and branch-overlap mechanisms are already retained or rejected under frozen
leaf/complete-wall gates. Repeating the 1.1466x selected-Q4 tile8 near-miss or
relabelling trace gaps as recoverable would violate the no-repeat/evidence
policy. Resume only after SPECDEC2/MTP2 changes actual route identity or a
materially new exact representation/dataflow has a >=1.15x leaf or >=0.5
ms/token projected premise.
