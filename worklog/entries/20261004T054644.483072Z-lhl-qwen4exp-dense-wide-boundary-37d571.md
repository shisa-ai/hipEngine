# The dense-wide scope boundary is non-monotone; 14-47 is the deepest passing scope and is not promoted

## Summary

The full ladder above the certified 16-47 binding of
`PRODUCTION_Q8_DENSE_WIDE_PREFILL_LAYERS`, at the canonical gate protocol (12
cases, 128 decode steps, 3 repeats, chunk 1024, HEAD `04d0f4161`, one process
per run):

| scope | engaged | kl_mean | kl_p95 | kl_max | top-1 | hard gates | why |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| 16-47 | 0.337 | 4.527e-4 | 2.320e-3 | 2.341e-2 | 1538/1548 | **PASS** | certified default |
| **14-47** | 0.359 | 5.190e-4 | 2.636e-3 | 3.979e-2 | 1536/1548 | **PASS** | deepest passing scope |
| 12-47 | 0.379 | 6.406e-4 | 3.229e-3 | 3.249e-2 | 1532/1548 | FAIL | aggregate top-1, one row short |
| 8-47 | 0.421 | 8.839e-4 | 4.647e-3 | 4.146e-2 | 1534/1548 | FAIL | `general_en` mean KL 1.181e-3 |
| 0-47 | 0.508 | 1.468e-3 | 7.517e-3 | 5.140e-2 | 1530/1548 | FAIL | KL and top-1, 6 scopes |

The **strict teacher hash is identical in all five runs**
(`4377006f4f0827dd419e9d92da9b61b0dbf65074195ffec5852fb98cc7e65ed7`), so the
candidate hashes are the only difference between them and the five runs are
directly comparable. Engagement scales monotonically with scope, confirming each
run dispatched the layer set it claimed.

**Two findings.**

1. **The boundary is not monotone.** `kl_mean` and `kl_p95` grow monotonically
   with scope, but `kl_max` and the top-1 count do not: 12-47's `kl_max`
   (3.249e-2) is better than 14-47's (3.979e-2) while its mean is worse, and
   8-47's top-1 (1534) is better than 12-47's (1532). This reproduces on a
   second, independent route the same non-monotonicity the staged-f32 ladder
   found (15-47 failing while 14-47 and 16-47 pass). So the boundary cannot be
   located by monotone search, and **a passing scope is not evidence about a
   shallower one** - 14-47's pass says nothing about 13-47 or 12-47.
2. **14-47 is admissible and is not promoted**, because its speed win is below
   this session's drift floor. See below.

## Changes

### 14-47's speed win is not resolvable

Three-arm A/B/C, one process per arm, order A-B-C with the baseline on both
sides of the candidate, `code-p4096`, chunk 1024, production profile, 1 warmup +
3 repetitions, `--require-cached-build`, all under `uv run`:

| arm | scope | pp tok/s | tg tok/s |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| A | 16-47 | 268.877 | 17.545 |
| B | **14-47** | **275.305** | 17.556 |
| C | 16-47 | 272.589 | 17.378 |

Same-code drift A against C is **+1.38%**. The pooled candidate effect against
`mean(A, C) = 270.733` is **+1.69%** prefill and **+0.54%** decode. A
signal-to-drift ratio of 1.2 is not a measurement.

The per-layer cost model predicts about **2.2%** (2 layers at ~0.152 s per layer
off a ~14 s prefill), so the measurement is *consistent with* the prediction -
but consistency is not evidence, and the alternative reading (B's 275.305 is
part of the same upward drift that produced C's 272.589) fits the data equally
well. **Changing the KV rounding of two layers for a benefit that cannot be
shown is the wrong trade**, so 14-47 is recorded as admissible and left
unpromoted.

This A/B is also much noisier than the 0-47 A/B earlier in the same session,
whose same-code drift was **0.37%**. That is a session-state difference, not a
protocol difference - both used the identical design - so the two A/Bs are not
comparable in precision and the 0-47 result's 51x separation should not be read
as the normal noise floor.

### Why 12-47's failure is recorded as a failure

12-47 passes every KL gate with 1.5x headroom, has **zero scope failures**, and
misses the aggregate top-1 by **one row**: 1532 of 1548 against the 1533 the
0.99 limit requires. The 16-47 control reproduced its recorded certificate with
a **one-row** top-1 difference (1538 here against 1539 recorded), so this metric
has at least one row of run-to-run variation and 12-47 sits *at* the boundary
rather than clearly beyond it.

It still cannot be certified. A gate whose verdict flips on one row out of 1548
is not a gate whose passes can be banked, and the honest reading is that 12-47
is unresolved rather than passing. Recording it as a pass because it is close
would be the same error as recording 0-47 as a pass because its failures are
near-ties.

## Limits

- One host (`gfx1151`, machine-id `55ea6c509d0b49eea8de7094a1023668`, not the
  gfx1100/W7900 default hardware), one model, one quant, one execution profile,
  one chunk size (1024), one route.
- The gate reports `status: invalid_or_screen_only` and
  `measurement_valid: false` for **every** run including the two that pass,
  because `qualification_blockers` carries the standard uncollected-certificate
  list (category-heldout, strict-teacher/BF16-relative, control/isolation,
  task-quality, engagement) plus the review row. `hard_gates_passed` is the
  separating field.
- The 14-47 speed A/B is one case (`code-p4096`) and the gate is the twelve-case
  protocol; the two were not measured on the same case set.
- 13-47 was not run, so the deepest passing scope may be 13-47 rather than
  14-47 - but the non-monotonicity means 14-47's pass does not imply it.
- No kernel-level trace of any extended scope, so the per-layer cost model
  remains inferred from the 16-47 trace.
- The profile constant is outside the recorded `manifest_sha256`, so a gate
  artifact alone would not show which scope ran; engagement is the evidence.

## Next

  within one process rather than three separate processes; the current design's
  drift floor is the binding constraint, not the effect size.
- Do not search further up the ladder by monotone assumption. A scope must be
  gated on its own evidence.

  16-47 and which the KV-rounding finding above reframes as an arithmetic
