# W7900: a 2-row AR wave doubles prefill, not decode; decode amortizes at 1.24x
## Summary

Traced pure-AR at rows=1 and rows=2 on the W7900 answers the rows-scaling question and corrects
today's earlier reading of it. Doubling the rows of a wave costs 1.40x the wall per decode step, and
92% of that growth is GPU kernel time (44.2 -> 59.9 ms/step, 1.355x). The growth is almost entirely
prefill: kernels with `prefill` in their name go 5,675 -> 10,427 ms (+9.9 ms/step) because a 2-row
wave admits two prompts and prefill scales near-linearly with rows. Decode amortizes well: the route
serving 1 row costs 28.2 ms/step and the route serving 2 rows costs 34.7 ms/step, i.e. 1.24x for
double the rows. So C2's deficit is an admission/prefill-cost problem, and the "batch the decode
GEMVs" hypothesis is retired with a mechanism rather than a shrug.

This supersedes the decode conclusion of
`w7900-gguf-q4ks-ar-amortization-prefill-split-2026-08-30`, which reported "1.52x per-step cost,
decode does not amortize weights". That figure divided the wave wall by 24 steps while subtracting a
single prefill at both widths, but a 2-row wave legitimately carries two prompts' prefill. The
right; the arithmetic underneath it was not.

## Validation

- Pure AR is verified, not assumed: all 30 cells across the two runs report `mtp_engaged=False` and
  `backend_telemetry.mtp_verified=None`. Step counts match exactly (24 steps/cell, 10 cells, 2 arms,
  480 wave-steps per width), so rows-per-step is the only thing that changed.
- Launch classification: 21 flat, 1 partial, 18 `per_row_launches`, and 18 kernels present only at
  width 2. Per-launch means of the width-1 decode kernels are unchanged (157.11 -> 157.41 us,
  40.28 -> 40.22, 89.62 -> 87.98, 59.50 -> 57.38) while their launches divide ~14x - a route being
  replaced, not slowed.
- Mechanism: at rows>=2 decode moves to a `*_rowtile_*` family that never runs at rows=1
  (`q4_k_t16_dense_dual_rowtile_single_lane` 4,774 ms / 31,232 launches at grid 139264,
  `q4_k_t16_dense_rowtile_gemv_kernel` 1,914 ms / 50,752 launches, plus q5/q6 rowtile variants).
- Two earlier attempts deadlocked with the GPU idle (`hipcc --version` probe stuck, parent in
  `wchan=anon_pipe_read`). `HIPENGINE_REQUIRE_CACHED_BUILD=1` alone does not prevent it: the builder
  still probes to compute the cache key. `hipengine/core/build.py` prescribes the pair - fail closed
  AND a precomputed `HIPENGINE_COMPILER_VERSION_FILE`. With both, no probe spawns and both traces
  complete (124 MB of kernel trace at width 1).
- Traced medians (21.467 / 30.642 tok/s) are used only for per-step ratios, never as throughput
  claims; published untraced C1 AR is 21.999-22.10.
