# Gemma 4 decode: MTP is a model capability gap, and the attention barrier batch is at its budget

## Summary

**MTP is a model capability gap, not an engine gate.** The artifact carries 658
tensors and none of them match `mtp`, `nextn`, `next_n`, `draft`, `spec` or
`eagle`; its top-level prefixes are `blk`, `output_norm`, `rope_freqs` and
`token_embd`, and no metadata field mentions MTP. The in-tree speculative path is
a NextN head that reads `nextn.enorm` and `nextn.hnorm`, so it keys on tensor
presence and nothing refuses a Gemma 4 MTP head by model name. The generator
already declares `supports_speculative_mtp = False` and surfaces it through
`engine_service`, so this confirms the declaration against the artifact rather
than changing it. Decode on this model cannot be accelerated by speculative
decoding.

**The obvious next decode lever is already at its hardware budget.** The
2026-09-24 decode specialization batches barrier rounds across 8 keys and halved
the reduction barrier cost, so a larger batch looks like the next step. It is not
a tunable: `gemma4_attention.hip` derives the batch per launch as the largest of
8, 4, 2, 1 whose partial rows fit the 64 KB LDS budget, and the switch already
carries a `case 16` the search never selects. Raising it needs a different
reduction layout, not a larger constant.

No decode throughput was added by this unit. The measured improvement over the
gfx1151 baseline stands at **16.09 -> 21.06 tok/s (1.31x)** from the recorded
campaign units, with public-path token-id parity exact.

## Changes

- No production code changed. The MTP declaration already matched the artifact.
- Evidence row `benchmarks/results/2026-09-27-gemma4-decode-mtp-capability.json`
  and a campaign-document note, so the gap is recorded where a reader looking for
  a speculative route will find it.

## Validation

- **The artifact was read, not assumed**: `GGUFReader` over the real
  `UD-Q4_K_XL` file, 658 tensor names, matched against seven speculative-decoding
  naming conventions and the metadata field list.
- **The engine's route was read, not assumed**: the NextN path's tensor names
  (`nextn.enorm`, `nextn.hnorm`) are in `qwen35_gguf_nextn.py` and
  `qwen4_exp_mtp.py`, which is what makes the gap the model's rather than a gate.
- **The barrier batch was read against the hardware limit**, including the
  `case 16` in the dispatch switch that the selection loop cannot reach.
