# Close W2 on multi-family co-scheduling bound

## Summary

At current-head R32, the complete target pass is 51.223 ms host and 49.016 ms
summed kernels. A co-scheduled owner cannot share bytes between distinct
Q4/Q5/Q6 tensors; the runtime already attempts mixed pair/triple and gate/up
owners. After W1's within-tensor one-decode work, the optimistic remaining W2
share is the entire 2.207 ms host-kernel gap: 4.31% of target and less of full
wall, versus 19.2%/20.1% required at C6/C8.

The historical giant-owner guard is negative: the gfx1100 B4 FFN megakernel was
2.66x slower on GPU. Building another megakernel without a larger bound would
repeat that rejected approach.

## Changes

Added a compact W2 bound artifact and marked W2 complete as measured blocked.
No code, route, or default changed.

## Validation

Bound uses current-head paired rocprof target windows, source-level pair/triple
topology, and deliberately grants W2 every non-kernel target microsecond. The
upper bound still cannot cover the wall gap.

## Next

Proceed to W4, which can run after W1/W2 sizing. W3 remains conditional on W1
flatness and cannot open from a blocked W1 result.
