# MTP IU4 review corrections and R3-R8 punchlist

## Summary

Fresh review of `MTP-IU4.md` and the R0-R2 evidence. The document is
structurally complete — all 13 referenced artifacts, kernels, scripts, and tests
exist and `docs/KERNELS.md` carries the catalog rows — but its central R2
attribution is wrong, and that error propagates in both directions.

**R2 compared a naive candidate against a fully tuned control and attributed
the gap to the U4/S4 representation.** The artifact's own fields refute this:

- Candidate core sustains **150-155 GB/s** at M2-M16; its own control sustains
  **218.3 GB/s** on the same host, same layer-0 pair, same launch. The candidate
  runs at **~70% of the 221 GB/s memory roof the control demonstrably reaches**.
- Candidate executes **9.6-12.5 TOPS** against the R0-measured **109.715 TOPS**
  IU4 roof — **9-11% of peak**. It is neither compute-bound nor at the memory
  roof, so it never entered the regime the document's roofline reasons about.
- Candidate core time is **flat at ~0.59 ms from M2 through M16** while row
  utilization rises 8x. Insensitivity to the tiny-M axis is the tell.

Cause is register blocking, confirmed in source and in the retained rocprof
block. `iu4_s4_dual_silu_bf16_kernel` (`iu4_s4_sidecar.hip:143`) has **one**
`i32x8_t` accumulator — a 320-deep single dependent WMMA chain — 64 threads,
**24 VGPR**, 1 KiB LDS, one 16-column N-tile, and no operand staging, so all
1,088 x-blocks re-read the full activation tile (~44.6 MB redundant against
89.4 MB of weights). The in-tree production owner
`gguf_q4_k_pack8_dual_wmma_prefill_silu_bf16_kernel`
(`gguf_q4_k_prefill.hip:515`) has **16** accumulators per wave (`acc_a[2][4]` +
`acc_b[2][4]`), 128 threads, and a 32-KiB LDS weight-staging union. The
candidate has 1/16th the register blocking of the lineage it sits beside.
R0's own screen established 8 independent chains as the requirement for the
109.715-TOPS roof. The M32-M128 bandwidth climb (161->196 GB/s) confirms it:
the only thing changing is `grid.y` growing 2->8, supplying between blocks the
parallelism the kernel fails to express within one.

**The tiny-M rejection survives, on a corrected and sharper basis.** Projected
onto the control's own 218.3 GB/s the candidate reaches 0.419 ms inclusive =
**1.10x/1.13x/1.32x at M2/M3/M4**, reversing the recorded losses. But that
projected win is *exactly* the 1.12x byte ratio (89,407,488 S4 vs 100,270,080
Q4_K_S) and matches the document's own 1.14x bandwidth-only ceiling. So: **at
tiny M the only lever is bytes, not ops** — the 2x arithmetic lane contributes
nothing because both sides are pinned at the weight-bandwidth roof. Through
R1's measured 23.93% B3 gate/up wall share that is **1.06x on the target
window** for +5.329 GiB, 15.9% weight NRMSE, U4 activations, and a T3 campaign.
Reject, as before, for a different and more durable reason.

**The wide-M rows cut the other way and are withdrawn.** R2's M64-M128 results
(3.190x-4.463x) are *understated*, measured with the same 1-accumulator core at
11% of the IU4 roof, against the wrong control. They are not a bound on IU4.
The document's priority-1 dense-prefill ranking is therefore *under*-argued, not
over-argued — and it previously quoted no prefill number at all.
