# Correct DMS ring-head expiry premise

## Summary

R0003's proposed unconditional ring-head replacement is not valid for DMS
retention semantics. Correct the premise and defer the layout choice at the
ledger bottom rather than treating a storage redesign as a mechanical cleanup.

- Inspected the BF16 HIP append predicate and host live-mask/append paths:
  non-evictable history survives beyond the retention window, so an expired
  row can be inside the extent rather than at its head.
- Named prefix-only acceleration versus separated-history/window layout as
  design choices, with reader, allocator, snapshot and rollback obligations.
- Preserved the race-repair worklog and specified clearing tests. No runtime,
  kernel or default changed; no performance claim added.
