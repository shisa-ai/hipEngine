## Summary

serving ledger entry was stale. Keep the explicit host-reference constructor.

  `device_payloads` means host reference; public serving continues passing
  `True`. Explicit device requests now propagate missing-kernel/runtime errors
  rather than silently constructing a host-backed backend.

  the retired variables in `docs/ENVS.md`.

  cases, including the later direct-device guard cases.
