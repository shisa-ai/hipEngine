# UD decode item C: strict-GEMV census, fused-LUT bit-exact speedup, and the unrouted local32 candidate

## Summary

Route-plan item C on the W7900 (GPU0), decode side. The strict IQ4_XS GEMV
owns 45% of decode (117 launches/token at ~230 us). A mutation bisection
found its cost is two dependent loads per element (payload byte ->
`__constant__` codebook byte), not memory: the 47.3 MB payload is
L2-resident on this part and a pure-read roofline for the access pattern
measures 755 GB/s against the kernel's 207 GB/s useful.

Two changes landed:

1. **Bit-exact strict speedup (shipped)**: the codebook load is fused with
   the nibble extraction into a 512-entry byte-indexed LDS table. The
   nibble half is thread-invariant (j = tid & 31 for both h phases), so one
   ds_read replaces the extract-and-load pair with identical values and an
   unchanged multiply order. Leaf: 0.229 -> 0.188 ms (+18%); end-to-end
   decode 16.5 -> 17.45 tok/s.

2. **The local32 decode owner (registered, wired, deliberately unrouted)**:
   a new `gguf_iq4_xs_local32_gemv_kernel<WAVES>` modeled on the retained
   pack8/T16 local32 family - one wave per 8 output columns, each lane owning
   8 contiguous K (one 32-group, one nibble half, one 8-byte payload window
   per lane), two u32 payload loads per column-block, split-K across the
   block's waves for the narrow-N ffn_down shapes. Measured 2.5-2.9x the
   strict GEMV per launch on every real shape (max_rel <= 2.3e-4), and
   22.76 vs 17.45 tok/s end-to-end with the route enabled.

Also: decode-graph capture and the decode step functions now open the
dense-IQ execution-owner session (a graph captured without it bakes in the
strict owner for every replay), and `_iq_dense_mmq_context` admits a
decode-only policy. The W4A16-vs-GEMV crossover was measured
(scripts/gguf_iq_w4a16_crossover.py): W4A16 wins only from 12 rows and is
4x slower at rows=1, so decode cannot route through W4A16, and the shared
min_rows=8 is wrong for W4A16 on gfx1100 (0.68x at 8 rows) - that is task-6
material.

## Why the local32 candidate is unrouted

The teacher-forced probe (scripts/gguf_iq_local32_decode_gate.py, incumbent =
route off, candidate teacher-forced on the same tokens) over 8 seeds of
512-token contexts: 6/8 pass the section-6.1 mean (1e-3), but seed 59
breaches both the mean (2.19e-3) and the absolute max-row ceiling
(5.85e-2 > 5e-2). Top-1 agreement 100% everywhere; the prefill-position
sanity KL is exactly 0. The error is pure summation-order noise - the
per-element products are identical to the strict owner - so unlike the
integer-MMQ route there is no arithmetic defect to fix, but a route that
cannot provably pass stays unrouted (the same discipline that held Q3_K).
The policy ships empty with the evidence in the declaration comment;
enabling is a one-line edit pending the full 162-row
production-referenced gate or an explicit envelope decision.

End to end, published protocol, quiet host: prefill 409-413 throughout;
decode 16.5 (before) -> 17.45 (shipped, fused LUT) -> 22.76 (candidate,
route on). Decode census with the route on: IQ4_XS decode GPU time

local32, attention) are shared with plain and at parity.
