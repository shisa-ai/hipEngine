# Flash-Next MTP head discovery and provider inference

## Summary

`hipengine serve --model <Flash-Next shards>` loaded no MTP head: the target
GGUF carries no NextN block, the head ships as a separate GGUF, and only Gemma 4
declared a discovery hook. Serving MTP needed `--speculative-provider
qwen4_exp_mtp --draft-model <head>`, and `--draft-model` alone was refused for
every model with `draft_model requires speculative_provider`, even when the
model plugin declares exactly one MTP provider.

## Changes

- `hipengine/loading/qwen4_exp_mtp_gguf.py`: `discover_qwen4_exp_mtp_sidecar`
  searches nearest first (the shard directory and its `MTP/`, the parent and
  its `MTP/`, then sibling directories of both whose names mention `mtp`) and
  admits a candidate only when its header passes `validate_qwen4_exp_mtp_gguf`.
- `hipengine/models/qwen4_exp.py`: the plugin declares
  `speculative_mtp_provider = "qwen4_exp_mtp"` and the discovery hook.
- `hipengine/llm.py`: `_resolve_speculative_owner` resolves the pair at load.
  A draft model alone takes the plugin's declared provider and fails by name
  when the plugin declares none; discovery is skipped when MTP serving is
  `off`. `LLM` and `ServerConfig` no longer refuse a draft model without a
  provider at construction.
- `--draft-model` help and the `docs/ENVS.md` row describe both forms.

## Validation

- `tests/test_unit_qwen4exp_mtp_sidecar_discovery.py` (search order, invalid
  candidates, absence, provider inference, `off` skips discovery), the updated
  server-config test, and the Gemma 4 MTP loader tests: 46 passed.
- Live, gfx1151, `hipengine serve --model .../UD-Q4_K_XL --max-context-tokens
  32768` with no MTP flags: the head resolved to
  `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-MTP-Q8_0/mtp-Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Q8_0.gguf`, the MTP
  smoke ran, and a `speculative_mtp: true` completion reported
  `used: true`, acceptance 0.76.
- `--draft-model .../mtp-Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Q8_0.gguf` alone: MTP request
  `used: True`. The second local head
  (`Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-MTP-apepojken-Q8_0`) has a different tensor layout and is
  refused at load naming the missing tensors; discovery never selects it.
