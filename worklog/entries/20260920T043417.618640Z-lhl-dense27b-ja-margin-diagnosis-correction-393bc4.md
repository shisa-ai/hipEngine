# The flipped position is not a near-tie: the margin diagnosis supersedes the knife-edge bound and the probe is below the row-count standard

## Summary

The margin diagnosis that `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` requires for a row above
`2e-2` KL **contradicts the knife-edge characterization** recorded in
`20260920T040925.692107Z-lhl-dense27b-ja-flip-localized-per-step-d87c26`. At the
position where `general_ja` flips, the reference prefers its token by ~0.99 nat
and the candidate ranks that token **16th** -- a substantive disagreement, not a
rounding-level tie. That entry's "knife-edge first-token decision" bound is
superseded; the localization (shape-dependent, production-shaped comparison
bit-exact, KL concentrated in the two steps the flip touches) stands.

## Changes

- `scripts/gguf_prefix_reuse_gate.py`: records `margin_diagnosis`
  (`reference_margin`, `candidate_margin`, `top_k_overlap`,
  `reference_top1_rank_in_candidate`, `top_k`) per teacher-forced step through a
  new `_per_step_margins` helper. Diagnostic only; no gating change.
- The helper exposed a latent shape bug: the captured logits rows are
  `(batch=1, vocab)`, so the stack is `(steps, 1, vocab)` and indexing
  `[:, -2:-1]` hit the singleton batch axis. The stacks are now flattened to
  `(steps, vocab)` before any per-step diagnostic runs, which also makes
  `evaluate_logits` receive exactly the intended shape. The first margin run
  produced no artifacts at all (`IndexError`) and was re-run after the fix. The
  per-step KL values committed earlier are unaffected -- the KL sum is over the
  vocabulary axis, so the singleton batch axis contributed nothing.
- `tests/test_unit_gguf_prefix_reuse_gate.py`: new
  `test_margin_diagnosis_reports_rank_and_near_tie_margin`, including a 3-D
  stacked-input case that pins the flattening contract. 7 unit tests pass.

`general_en` is the contrast that makes the reading clear: its step-0 KL of
0.028723 is above the `2e-2` diagnosis threshold and its margins differ (1.81
versus 2.45), but its argmax agrees (`rank_in_cand = 0`) and its top-8 overlap is
8/8, so it is a benign distributional difference at a confident position.
`general_ja` is different in kind: the routes disagree about which token to pick,
with the reference's choice sitting 16th in the candidate's ordering.

**The probe cannot resolve the bars it is being read against.**
`docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md`'s September 10 row-count standard states that a
1-flip-in-60 probe resolves the 99% bar as "zero flips allowed" and cannot
distinguish a 0.5% flip rate from 2%, that ~60-row screening probes are valid
quick passes only when they show zero top-1 flips, and that **any flip requires a
500-1000-row probe with shared-chain teacher forcing** before rate conclusions.
This gate runs four teacher-forced rows per category (16 total), so its
`top1_agreement` of 0.75 for `general_ja` is "3 of 4 steps agreed" at 25%
granularity, and its `kl_mean` is a four-step mean. The gate does use
shared-chain forcing (both arms consume the reference's chain), so the row-count
requirement is the only part of the standard it does not meet.
