# The MMQ route's perturbation is out-of-distribution; realistic input shows no divergence

## Summary

The route's disagreement with its fp32 owner is a property of out-of-distribution
input, not of the route. On realistic prose and on a truncated Python snippet -
the exact class the flag cites as its cause - the route matches fp32 token for
token, and where the arms do part they part from llama.cpp together rather than
from each other.

Measured across four corpora with the same three-arm harness:

| corpus | class | result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| frozen, 1022 rows | near-one-hot repeated prose | zero flips, exact agreement |
| probe, 96 ids | random ids, out of distribution | arms split, route tracks llama, max logit delta 9.76 |
| PLAN.md prose, 2004 ids | realistic, in distribution | 10 steps identical, fp32 and route then agree with each other, max delta 3.46 |
| code, complete 181 ids | realistic | all three agree, first-token margin 16.77 |
| code, truncated 511 chars | the flag's named class | 23 steps identical, no degeneration |

The route's raw-logit perturbation is 3x smaller in distribution than out (3.46
against 9.76 max absolute difference), which is the mechanism behind the table:
on input the model was trained for, the quantisation step is small enough not to
move a decision, and on random ids the logits are small and flat enough that it
does.

The flag's recorded cause is a Python snippet that agrees for two tokens and then
collapses into a fourteen-token repetition. That did not reproduce on a
comparable prompt here, and the record's own framing is that the cause is a
measurement for the lead rather than a defect. This entry supplies a better
measurement of the same question; it does not change the default, because the
record reserves that call.

## Validation

- Prose, 2004 ids, 24-token continuation, all three arms:
  - Steps 0-9 identical across all three, including step 8 at a margin of +1.98
    and step 9 at +5.63. The first token is a near-tie (fp32 +0.179, route
    +0.167) and all three agree on it.
  - Step 10 is the first divergence and it is not the route leaving the others:
    llama.cpp emits 4926 while fp32 and the route both emit 99161.
  - Raw logits: median difference +0.241, max absolute 3.46, top-5 span 5.58
    against 5.35 - the route's distribution is essentially the same shape.
  - No degeneration over 24 tokens.
- Truncated code, 511 chars, 24-token continuation: steps 0-22 identical across
  all three arms. Step 23 parts with llama.cpp on 236761 against fp32 and the
  route agreeing on 17825, at a route margin of +0.16. No repeated-token run.
- Complete code, 181 ids: all three agree on the first token at margin 16.77.
- Determinism holds on every row: bit-identical logits across two prefills in one
  process, and the softcap preserves the argmax in both arms.
- The `n_probs` request still does not return `completion_probabilities`, so the
  anchor's own per-step confidence is `n/a`. The token comparison does not depend
  on it; establishing whether llama.cpp was itself near a tie at a divergence
  point does.
