# Disabled-selector ranking for gguf_ud_q4_k_xl: only the GR family wins

## Summary

The disabled-selector ranking sweep was run for `gguf_ud_q4_k_xl` on gfx1151
(`code-p4096`, chunk 1024, 2 measured repetitions per arm, 12 arms, source
`aa49c0850`, `tracked_clean: True`, `accelerator-performance` for every arm).
Baseline median prefill **15.544 s** (263.5 tok/s).

**Only the GR family wins.** `GR_WIDE` **+1.841 s (1.1344x)**, the
`GR_IU8`+`GR_IU8_DOWN` pair **+1.717 s (1.1241x)**, `GR_IU8` **+1.590 s
(1.1140x)**, `GR_IU8_DOWN` +0.146 s. Everything else is a loss or noise:
`Q8_MMQ_PREFILL` **-0.539 s**, `PRODUCTION_MOE_PREFILL` **-0.550 s**,
`Q4_IU8_PREFILL` **-2.822 s**, and `GDN_COLWARPS_PREFILL` / `QSA_FLASH_PREFILL`
/ `GDN_PEER_PREFILL` all inside +/-0.4%.

**Enabling every disabled prefill selector at once is worth only +1.7%**
(`all_prefill`, 13 overrides, +0.264 s). The GR pair alone is 6.5x what the
whole set delivers.

**Minimum re-admission set: none of the nine.** The three material wins are all
blocked on numerics for this profile, which needs `prefill_last` bit-identical:
the two iu8 routes sit 3x and 8.6x above a ~5e-9 prefill-boundary cliff and the
wide tile fails the 780-row gate (max KL 7.349e-2, top-1 768/780). The other six
are losses or noise, so there is no value to requalify at any risk.

**The reachable value is a kernel task, not a re-admission.** `docs/REFACTOR.md`
names the GR-up wide tile's bit-exact levers as "the only actionable ones":
`ROW_BATCH` 4 -> 8 at `HIDDEN_TILE=2` (halves the 4x2x4 block tile's weight
re-read, no change to k-order, shuffle tree or the 4-wave LDS sum, **estimated
~900 ms**) plus dropping the dead `gate` store (~107 ms). Those are ~1.0 s of the
1.841 s `GR_WIDE` measures, and bit-exact is the one property this profile
requires. The measured `RB8/HT1` retile (0.80x) is not evidence against lever 1:
it moved `ROW_BATCH` and `HIDDEN_TILE` together, so it is confounded.

## Validation

- **Engagement verified, not assumed.** Every arm's `route_env` in its
  `child.json` records the selector actually bound, `effective_route_missing_keys`
  is empty for all twelve arms, and `named_profile_intact` is `false` for every
  overridden arm and `true` for the baseline. This matters here because
  `docs/REFACTOR.md` records a GR/dense route screened against the *default*
  prompt suite never executing at all (39-71 tokens against a `rows > 256`
  admission) and "scor[ing] a perfect zero, which reads as a pass".
- Unit guards: `tests/test_unit_qwen4exp_disabled_selector_ranking.py` 6 passed
  before the sweep.
- All 12 arms produced the same generated `token_id` (248068) and 12 distinct
  logits hashes, as expected for different arithmetic.
- **The 2026-09-16 sweep is stale in ORDER, not just in level.** Its fallback
  was 23.648 s against today's 15.544 s (recovery baseline 1.52x faster), and
  its top two entries are both now unavailable: `Q8_IU8_WMM` (1.388x) is closed
  (14 of 17 claiming roles fail the canonical envelope alone) and
  `Q8_MMQ_PREFILL` inverted from 1.203x to **0.9665x**. The largest current
  winner, `GR_WIDE` (1.1344x), was not in that sweep. `Q4_IU8_PREFILL` inverted
  from 0.997x to **0.8463x**.
- **A near-duplicate harness exists and is unflagged.**
  `scripts/qwen4exp_disabled_family_sweep.py` produced the 2026-09-16 artifacts;
  `scripts/qwen4exp_disabled_selector_ranking.py` was written 2026-09-15 and had
  never been run until now. Both answer the same question with different arm
  sets. Noted for `docs/REFACTOR.md`; not resolved here.
- **The task's own premise needed two corrections:** there are **9** disabled
  selectors for this quant, not 8 (the list omitted `QSA_FLASH_PREFILL`), and a
  ranked recoverable-seconds sweep *does* already exist at
  `benchmarks/results/2026-09-16-disabled-family-recoverable-time/`.
