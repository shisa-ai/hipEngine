# The WMMA prefill failure is the partial block, not the row count

## Summary

Checkpoint on
`worklog/entries/20260927T212328.556974Z-lhl-the-wmma-prefill-difference-compounds-with-depth-ac22fc.md`,
which established that the WMMA route's divergence is amplification of a small
per-layer arithmetic difference rather than a defect, and predicted the shape
condition would be block-boundary-shaped. **Confirmed**, and the failure is
sharper than expected.

The campaign evaluator at four prefill shapes, each against a frozen WMMA-off
baseline captured at the same shape:

| prefill rows | blocks | kl_mean | kl_max | top-1 flips | passed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 256 | one partial | 2.49e-02 | 1.60 | 6 | ✗ |
| 512 | one full | **2.71e-04** | 7.36e-02 | **0** | ✗ (marginal) |
| 768 | full + partial | 2.59e-02 | **13.23** | 1 | ✗ |
| 1024 | two full | **4.91e-05** | **3.65e-02** | **0** | ✓ |

## What the table says

**The partial block is the failure, by two to three orders of magnitude.**

- A full block gives `kl_mean` 2.71e-04 against a 1e-3 limit — twenty times
  inside it — with **zero top-1 flips**. It misses only on `kl_max`, at 0.0736
  against 0.05.
- Two full blocks pass on **every** axis.
- A full block followed by a partial one is as bad as a partial block alone
  (`kl_max` 13.23). So this is **not** a function of total length and **not** a
  function of row count: it is the presence of a partial block.

This is consistent with everything already measured. The 512-row direct
comparison landed at 3.9e-06, and the campaign bench at `--prompt 512` is exactly
one full block — which is the 19 percent win.

## Why this is a capability condition, not a hiding place

The repo's rule is that a restriction needs a named concrete cause and a named
command that would lift it. This has both:

- **Cause:** partial blocks fail the gate at `--prefill 256` and `--prefill 768`.
- **Clearing command:** fix the partial-block path, then run
  `scripts/gemma4_teacher_forced_gate.py` at `--prefill 256` and confirm it
  passes.

And the feature still runs on the shape that matters. Prefill cost is dominated
by long prompts, and a long prompt is full blocks plus at most one partial tail.
Selecting the tiled route for a partial block is being conservative about *which
variant* a shape uses among working paths, which is permitted — not deciding the
feature does not run.

I am **not** landing it in this iteration. The single-full-block case is marginal
on `kl_max` (0.0736 against 0.05) even with zero flips and a `kl_mean` twenty
times inside its limit, and I have not established whether that is sampling noise
or a real tail.
