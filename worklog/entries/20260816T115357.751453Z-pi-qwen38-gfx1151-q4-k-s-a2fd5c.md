# Qualify Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_S on gfx1151

## Summary

Qualify the downloaded Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_S GGUF as a first-class gfx1151
correctness/memory route. Header-only inspection proves the 866 tensor names and
shapes match Q4_K_M exactly, while 66 prior Q6 tensors become 54 Q4 and 12 Q5.
The unchanged planner already compacts the 54 Q4 tensors but expands the 12 Q5
tensors to BF16, making planned weight residency 16.002 GiB rather than the
15.780-GiB Q4_K_M plan. The candidate reuses the existing operation-complete
Q5T16 family for only those exact H5120 roles and reduces the metadata-only
Q4_K_S weight plan to 14.945 GiB. The complete-file materialization,
actual-weight operation/quality gate, and isolated 512/8 full-model smoke pass;
full natural AR/MTP and publication-shape validation remains deferred.

## Changes

- Register the exact public generation key `gguf_q4_k_s` for dense and MoE
  Qwen3.5-family GGUF models on both HIP backends. The gfx1151 key intentionally
  keeps generic loop defaults until independently measured.
- Add gfx1151 capability `GGUF_DENSE_Q5_T16_H5120` and propagate it through main
  and NextN materialization. It admits only Q5 `ffn_down` K17408/N5120,
  recurrent `attn_qkv` K5120/N10240, and full-attention `attn_v` K5120/N1024.
  Q4_K_M has no Q5 tensors at these shapes.
- Add RED/GREEN synthetic role/shape bounds, generator registration coverage,
  and optional complete-file Q4_K_S plan/materialization checks. The expected
  inventory is 60 sole Q5T16 owners: 48 existing `ssm_out` plus the 12 new
  H5120 owners, with no dense-BF16 Q5 shadow.
- Admit the existing `chain_compact_peer_wave32` GDN prefill route for the
  identical Qwen3.8 16K/48V/128x128 Q4_K_S geometry. The isolated rerun lifts
  the new quant key's full-model 512-token prefill out of the exact LDS fallback
  while preserving the final token and teardown. Other Q4_K_M-only file-type
  policies remain fail-closed pending the deferred full suite.
