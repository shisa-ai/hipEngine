# Establish the prefill profile run-to-run measurement floor

## Summary

Three captures of the same 4096-token `code-p4096` prefill — two at the
production default, one with `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_PLE_WARM=1` — contain an
**identical 2007-row (role, kernel) set** and attribute 100% of kernels. The
configuration difference is invisible in the kernel set, so it can only appear in
the times, and there it does not.

What the exercise did produce is the noise floor the gap attribution needs:

- Per-(role, kernel) times at or above 20 ms move by **median −0.3%, stdev 5.1%,
  range −32% to +36%** between two runs of the **same** configuration.
- Family aggregates move **0.5–2.1%**; the prefill total spans 22053–22561 ms
  (1.4%).
- The scatter **grows** with row size (stdev 1.3% at 20–50 ms, 7.5% at
  50–100 ms), the opposite of averaging noise.

Consequence: gap attribution is interpretable at the family level and not at the
individual-component level. A per-component "recoverable ms" figure needs
repetitions, not a single pair of captures.

## Warm PLE is decode-only and does not change the prefill

`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_PLE_WARM=1` populates the PLE table into page cache so
**decode-step** row gathers hit warm pages. Its own source comment records the
price: one mmap-touch sweep of ~28.8 GB, **+15.4 s and ~6,015 major faults per
runner construction**, amortized over 577–15,400 generated tokens (median
~2,050). A prefill-only profile generates no tokens, so it pays the construction
cost and gets none of the benefit.

It also does not perturb the prefill. cold-A vs cold-B (both default) and
cold-B vs warm-PLE have the same scatter (stdev 5.09% vs 5.15%) and the net delta
changes sign between comparisons of the same configuration (−314.6 ms vs
+507.6 ms).

An earlier reading of the 100–300 ms band suggested warm PLE made the largest
rows ~15% faster. That was n=5 with one outlier: `layers.15.attn_q` reads
139.1 / 94.6 / 95.3 ms across the three runs, so cold-A alone drives the median,
and `layers.23.attn_q` swings +12.6% then −25.5% in opposite directions. No
warm-PLE effect is established.
