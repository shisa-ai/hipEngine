# Batch and pin Inkling routed expert activation transfers

## Summary

Run grouped expert projections and pointwise work across the rows of one packed target block. Submit the activation upload and download through page-locked staging, with one stream wait per exchange.

## Changes

- Preserve row-local routing, packed weights, FP32 arithmetic order, and provisional target ownership. Ordinary decode still selects the single-row kernel. Dense projections and experts share the staging implementation, with an ordinary-copy fallback when page locking is unavailable.
