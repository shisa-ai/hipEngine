# Attribute Gemma no-thinking MTP differences at identical prefixes

## Summary

The observed no-thinking request has a production AR/MTP token difference at
zero-based position 17. It does not reproduce under the strict profile. Exact
cursor, published-prefix, retained-KV-byte, and write-offset checks passed;
three same-schedule MTP runs and a replay after unrelated work also matched.
The result is numerical attribution, not a full production quality verdict.

The initial investigation compared the measurements to the generic maximum-KL
cutoff. That is not Gemma's binding envelope: `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` §2.11
applies to sparse top-k MoE. No arithmetic rollback or admission restriction is
warranted merely because a row exceeds the generic cutoff.
