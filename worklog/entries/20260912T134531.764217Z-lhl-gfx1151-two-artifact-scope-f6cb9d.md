(`2026-09-02-w7900-q4km-k3-c7-fused-r28-periodic-strict-retained.json`) is a

compositions at all.

## Capability-ledger derivation

Over `hipengine/**/*.py` at this commit, by AST-walking every
`backend_package_capability(...)` call site and intersecting its string-literal
name arguments with the gfx1100-only set:

- **47** `GGUF_*` names are defined on gfx1100 and absent on gfx1151.
- **19** are live-read through `backend_package_capability`.
  `GGUF_SPECDEC2_MTP2_PHYSICAL_C1` (`generation/qwen35_gguf_mtp2.py`) is the one
  missing from section J's list of 18.
- The other 28 sit in the gfx1100 package's `__all__` without being read that
  way; most are internal composition blocks folded into a parent policy in the
  same file, for example `GGUF_Q5_F32_ORDERED_PREFILL_H7G_POLICY` (defined at
  `hip_gfx1100/__init__.py:934`, merged into a combined policy at `:957`).

The 19 are the ledger to close. The 47 is the size of the gfx1100-only
capability surface, not the number of outstanding verdicts.
