# The Japanese envelope failure is a step-0 token flip, not a distributional defect: 99.8% of its KL is in the first two steps

## Summary

The `general_ja` envelope failure is one token decision, not a numerical
accuracy defect. Recording the KL **per teacher-forced step** shows 99.8% of its
mass in the first two steps, with the only top-1 disagreement at step 0: after
the candidate is teacher-forced onto the reference's trajectory the two routes
agree to ~1e-4. The 0.681121 mean is a property of a four-step mean dominated by
a single flip, not evidence that the production route computes the wrong
distribution.

## Changes

- `scripts/gguf_prefix_reuse_gate.py`: records `kl_per_step` and `top1_per_step`
  in the `teacher_forced` block (diagnostic only; no gating change), with a
  `_per_step_softmax` helper that reproduces `evaluate_logits`' own math.
- `tests/test_unit_gguf_prefix_reuse_gate.py`: new
  `test_per_step_diagnostics_match_the_aggregate_metrics` pinning that the
  per-step vector reproduces `kl_mean`, `kl_max`, and `top1_agreement` exactly.

## Validation

Gate re-run on all four categories with the recorded per-category commands
(`/tmp/t30-gate-perstep`, 6 unit tests pass):

| category | passed | kl_mean | `kl_per_step` | `top1_per_step` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `code` | True | 3.9e-05 | 2e-06, 1.3e-04, 2e-06, 2.5e-05 | all True |
| `general_en` | True | 0.007199 | 0.028723, 6.8e-05, 3e-06, 0.0 | all True |
| `general_ja` | False | 0.681121 | **1.758347, 0.965688**, 3.5e-04, 1.1e-04 | **False**, True, True, True |
| `mixed_ja_en` | True | 0.003394 | 0.002974, 0.005819, 0.003222, 0.001563 | all True |

The `general_ja` trajectory is `forced=[96026, 98222, 271, 248068]` with
`candidate=[271, 271, 271, 248068]` and `reference=[96026, 98222, 271, 248068]`:
the candidate and the reference disagree at step 0, the candidate is then
teacher-forced down the reference's path, and by steps 2 and 3 the distributions
agree to 3.5e-04 and 1.1e-04. The two remaining KL mass fractions are
1.758347/2.724 = 64.5% and 0.965688/2.724 = 35.4%, so 99.9% of the summed KL
lives in the two steps the flip touches.

`general_en` is the useful contrast: it is also front-loaded (0.028723 at step
0, then 6.8e-05, 3e-06, 0.0) but its top-1 agrees at every step, so a step-0
distributional difference with the same argmax passes. `mixed_ja_en` is flat
(~0.003 every step) with no flips. The three passing categories therefore show
two different benign shapes, and only `general_ja` has a decision flip.

## The strict profile is not an available higher-precision reference

`HIPENGINE_EXECUTION_PROFILE=strict` resolves from the environment in
`hipengine/llm.py`, so the gate needs no code change, and the run completed
(`/tmp/t30-strict/ja-strict.json`, exit 1 like the production run). It
reproduces production **bit for bit**: candidate 271, native chunked 271, serial
96026, one-shot prefix 174267, one-shot bulk 96026, and the identical per-step
vector `[1.758347, 0.965688, 0.000345, 0.000106]` with
`top1_per_step=[False, True, True, True]`. Both payloads carry an empty
`observability.routes.last_execution_manifest`, so the artifact cannot confirm
which variants resolved; what it does show is that no stricter arithmetic was
selected for this route, because a different kernel selection would not
reproduce the KL to six decimals. Either the production and strict profiles
resolve to the same variants for this dense27B Q4_K_M path, or the selector had
no effect -- the artifact does not distinguish those, and neither reading
supplies the higher-precision reference the question needs.

## Bound

The divergence is a **knife-edge first-token decision** on one constructed
prompt, and it cannot be adjudicated with the available tooling:

- The production route is self-consistent: its boundary and final state are
  bit-identical (0/0) to an independently built native chunked oracle in all
  four categories, and it is byte-stable across prefill chunk widths 128-1,024
  on a 1,416-token Japanese prompt.
- The failure is one decision. 99.9% of the `general_ja` KL mass sits in the two
  steps the step-0 flip touches, and once the candidate is teacher-forced onto
  the reference's trajectory the two distributions agree to ~1e-4.
- The three routes disagree at step 0 (production 271, serial 96026, one-shot
  prefix 174267), the strict profile reproduces production bit-for-bit, and an
  fp32 oracle over a 27B Q4_K_M model is infeasible. A `kernels/cpu_reference/`
  fp32 check can validate individual kernels but cannot settle a full-model
  decision.

The gate is left with the envelope binding against the serial reference, so
`general_ja` keeps failing on `metrics_passed` and nothing is masked. That is the
correct state: the failure is real for that prompt, its cause is localized and
bounded, and the alternative -- rebasing the numerical reference on the
production-shaped oracle -- would require the accuracy evidence that does not
exist.
