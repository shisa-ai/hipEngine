# C8 iter31: grouped Q6 R8 inspection — decode-ALU-bound; dp4a sibling is the axis

## Summary

Inspected the C8-P4 grouped-R8 family (30.353 ms/cycle — the largest single
kernel) with the established offline method: the peer-role census for
per-role traffic and the `hipcc -S` ELF audit for resources and instruction
mix. Verdict: the family is **decode-ALU-throughput-bound**. Weight traversal
is already minimal (census-proven 1× per layer), occupancy is not binding,
and loads are already wide. The bullet's live axis is reduced dequantization
via the registered q8_1_dp4a Q6 sibling family. The bullet stays open pending
that leaf-first screen.

## Findings

- Per-role traffic (role census, R32 cycles): FFN-down 5120×17408 =
  73.1 MB/call × 32 calls at 21.505 ms → **108.8 GB/s**; recurrent-QKV
  5120×10240 = 43.0 MB/call × 24 calls at 10.207 ms → **101.1 GB/s**;
  attention-V 5120×1024 = 4.3 MB/call × 8 calls at 0.443 ms → **77.6 GB/s**.
- Traversal: `unique_encoded_weight_bytes` equals weight_count × per-layer
  bytes exactly for every role → each call reads its weight exactly once;
  "reduced weight traversal" is closed (the pair-seam consolidation already
  removed the repeated calls).
- ELF audit: the rows8 grouped instantiation allocates VGPR 111, LDS 1,024 B
  → 2 waves/SIMD → **8 waves/CU (25%)** — occupancy is not the binding
  constraint (unlike the P3 fused owner's 2 waves/CU).
- Instruction mix (1,621 body instructions): 1,192 vector ALU, 24 VMEM,
  80 LDS, 1 barrier, 0 WMMA. Global loads are already wide
  (1× b128, 1× b64, 8× u16, 6× u8) — no byte-granular widening axis.
- Binding-resource model: 327,680 blocks/call ÷ 192 resident = 1,707
  residency rounds → measured 394 ns/block. The ALU model (2 active waves ×
  2 k-steps × ~500 vector ops) predicts ~527 ns/block — consistent within
  ~30%. A DRAM-latency model would require an impossible 820 GB/s per-call
  rate. Decode-ALU throughput binds.
- Live axis: **reduced dequantization**. The q8_1_dp4a Q6 sibling family is
  already registered (`gguf_q6_k_t16_qmicro_planar_q8_1_dp4a_gemv_*`);
  screening it on the three actual shapes at rows 8/24/32 is the concrete
  next candidate, leaf-first per the item's compare-before-numerics rule.
  The rejected-axes record (WG256 0.860–0.907x, WMMA capacity closed, col4,
  WG128 dual-R6, pair-major, permlanex16) stands.
