# Measure the wide-row Q8_0 dense prefill route end-to-end at its certified scope

## Summary

`dense_wide256` is worth **5.4% of prefill wall** at 1K and 4K prompt tokens
against the route it takes precedence over, and **22-24%** against the exact
coltile chain that preceded both. At 512 tokens the wide route and the certified
WMMA route are indistinguishable inside the protocol's repeat spread.

| Route at layers 16-47 | 512 | 1K | 4K |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| exact coltile chain | 2.822 s | 5.487 s | 22.648 s |
| WMMA (production default) | 2.225 s | 4.389 s | 18.208 s |
| `dense_wide256` (opt-in) | **2.201 s** | **4.151 s** | **17.217 s** |

Median prefill wall, `code` category, one host (`gfx1151`, Radeon 8060S,
machine id `55ea6c50…`), production profile
`35e57360e648bcc8`. Artifact:
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-17-q8-dense-route-ab/`.

## Protocol, and why the earlier numbers were not one

Every wide-row timing before this was unusable as a comparison: the packet
measurement is one operation (2.573 ms against 4.401 ms for the WMMA route on
`layers.8.attn_qkv` rows=1024), and the two end-to-end passes in the previous
entry were single repetitions with a model load between them.

`scripts/qwen4exp_dense_route_ab.py` (new) runs three arms in **one process with
one model load**, flipping the selectors **after** the production binder (a
pre-binder value is inert, because the binder writes them), with one warmup per
arm per case after its flip and three measured repetitions whose arm order is
rotated. Arms:

| Arm | Layers 16-47 | Layers 0-15 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `exact` | exact coltile chain | exact coltile chain |
| `wmma-16-47` | `wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out` (the named default) | exact coltile chain |
| `wide-16-47` | `dense_wide256_f32_f32_out` | exact coltile chain |

Each arm records its own launch census and logits digest, so the run shows which
kernel served each arm rather than assuming it.

## Evidence

- **Route identity, not just timing.** All three arms launch 7191 kernels over
  the same roles and shapes. The exact chain's count falls 7164 -> 2412 and the
  4752 launches that disappear are exactly the 4752 the wide family takes over;
  the remaining 2412 exact-chain launches and 27 `pack8_gemv` launches are
  identical in every arm.
- **Arithmetic.** The wide arm is **bit-identical to the certified WMMA arm** on
  all three cases in both runs (`logits_sha256` per case, not per class), and all
  arms sample token 248068. Both dense families differ from the exact chain, as
  their f16 operands imply. Digests are identical across two processes.
- **Repeatability.** A second process reproduced every ratio within 0.1
  percentage points (e.g. 4K wide-vs-default -5.44% / -5.42%, wide-vs-exact
  -23.98% / -23.95%). Per-arm repetition spread is at or below 0.06% at 4K and
  2.0% at 512, so every delta is outside its own arm's spread except the
  512-token wide-vs-default gap, which is reported as a tie.
- **Prediction check.** The 16-47 row in `docs/QWEN4EXP-STATUS.md` §4 was a
  `7.0409 s x byte share` interpolation (+4.42 s). The measured 16-47 saving is
  +4.439 s, within 0.4% - but the sweep's exact arm is 23.840 s against 22.648 s
  here (its `--risk-diagnostics` instrument synchronizes every repair call), so
  this confirms the interpolation's shape rather than reproducing its number.
  §4 now carries the measured value with that caveat.

## Where the win is, and where it is not

- 512 tokens: no measurable difference (1.09% apart). The route swap is
  per-shape work and the 512-row chunk does not give the wide tile enough rows to
  pull ahead.
- 1K/4K: 5.4% of the whole prefill, which is 0.99 s of 17.217 s at 4K.
- The single-operation packet result (1.71x against the WMMA route) does not
  carry through end to end, because the layer-16-47 dense roles are only part of
  a prefill. The share is inferred from these two measurements, not profiled.

## What this does not do

The wide arm's 12-case production numerical gate is still owed
(`scripts/execution_profile_q8_wmma_prefill_layers_gate.py --route dense_wide
--layers 16-47`). Bit-identity with the certified WMMA arm on three cases and two
processes is evidence that it inherits that arm's arithmetic, not a substitute
for the envelope verdict. The route stays opt-in
(`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_DENSE_WIDE`), one category is measured, and nothing here
is a topline rate row.

## Validation

- Route identity and arithmetic as tabulated above: same 7191 launches per arm,
  the 4752-launch family substitution, bit-identical wide/WMMA digests per case
  in two processes, and one sampled token across all arms.
- Two full protocol runs (`--repetitions 3`) on `gfx1151`, agreeing within 0.1
  percentage points on every ratio; per-arm repetition spread at or below 2.0%.
- `python3 scripts/sync_benchmark_readme.py --check` passes after the README
  edits.
