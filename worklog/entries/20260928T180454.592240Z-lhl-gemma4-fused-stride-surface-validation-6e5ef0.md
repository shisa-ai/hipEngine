# Close the fused-stride entry-point surface and re-validate the Gemma4 fused gate/up route

## Summary

The fused-stride work reached the loader, the leaf and the RAW entry point, but
not the X8 and T16 Python wrappers. Both are thin forwards onto the RAW wrapper
and neither accepted nor passed `expert_stride_rows`, so both always fell back
to the split-read default. `gemma4_project_experts_wmma` resolves its launcher
from the registry and passes the keyword unconditionally, so a
`gguf_q4_k_x8_v1` or `gguf_q4_k_t16_v1` quant would have raised `TypeError`
instead of reading the fused stack. Nothing had failed yet because the artifact's
expert gate_up is plain `gguf_q4_k`; the route was a latent trap rather than a
live defect.

Closed the gap, then re-validated the whole fused gate/up route end to end
against a baseline captured before the fused-stride fix, so the numbers below
are an independent check and not a self-gate.

Two pre-existing defects surfaced while validating and were corrected in their
own commits: `test_auto_keeps_the_exact_routes` asserted the pre-`13532ae2b`
behavior and failed at HEAD in isolation, and the campaign scoreboard still
carried pre-P14 prefill numbers.
