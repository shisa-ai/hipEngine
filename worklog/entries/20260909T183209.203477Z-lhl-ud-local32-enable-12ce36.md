# local32 IQ4_XS decode route enabled via the natural-prompt gate: +30% decode

## Summary

UD impact-list task 7 asked for the structural IQ4_XS T16-analog decode repack
"only if tasks 1-4 fall short of parity - decode GEMV at ~60us would make
this moot for decode." The moot condition is now met, not by new structural
work but by resolving the gate on the existing local32 decode candidate: its
holdout was measured with uniform-random-token prompts, which the Q3_K
unblock (20260909T160819Z) showed measure a ~1e-3 prompt-sensitivity floor
that masks route differences. Re-gated on natural self-generated prompts, the
route passes cleanly on all three held-out seeds - including seed 23, the one
that failed at 98.44% top-1 under random tokens. Enabled the route; decode
improves +30% on both cards. The structural T16-analog repack is therefore
not pursued: the largest-effort item is moot for decode by its own
criterion, and the prefill side already took its wins (W4A16 tile re-sweep,
Q3_K split, Q5 dual).
