# R12 window: tile sweep negative, d4x2 plane candidate +1.4x identified and wired default-off

## Summary

R12 plane/tile screen for the dense Q8 MMQ legs, plus a default-off two-plane
candidate. The retained 128x128 tile is optimal in its family (128x64 only
1.00-1.05x, 64x64 0.93-0.97x, 64x128 0.77-0.82x), so the LDS/occupancy
hypothesis is **weak**; every tile variant is bit-identical to 128x128. In the
plane sweep, d4x2 is +1.39-1.44x on every leg while d4x1 is 2.3-2.6x but too
coarse as a standalone replacement.

`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_MMQ_PLANES=2` wires the two-plane d4x2 guarded chain
(default-off). The whole-model smoke at code-p512 is ~3.2% faster prefill
(1658-1670ms versus 1715-1720ms), repeat-deterministic within planes, with
max|dlogit| 1.14 accumulated over 48 layers. If qualified, the projected
code-p4096 effect is ~0.6s saved of the 13.49s device total (~4.5% prefill),
which would close the dense gap to the newer halo-box HIP engine. d4x2 is **not**
bit-exact, so it is a T1 bounded-drift route that must clear the full envelope and
task gates.

## Measurements (rows=1024, 8 pairs, random-activation stress screen)

**Tile sweep (d4x3, raw-vec4):** the retained 128x128 tile is optimal in
its family. 128x64 gains only 1.00-1.05x on the wide-N legs; 64x64
0.93-0.97x; 64x128 0.77-0.82x. The LDS/occupancy hypothesis is
WEAK - halving the shared footprint does not pay for the extra
activation re-reads. All tiles bit-identical to 128x128 (template
guarantee verified per geometry).

**Plane sweep (128x128, raw-vec4):**

| Plane count | attn_qkv 2560->10240 | attn_q / attn_gate 2560->6144 | ssm_out 6144->2560 | drift vs d4x3 (stress screen) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| d4x3 (retained) | 8.0 ms | 4.5 ms | 4.4 ms | reference |
| d4x2 | **1.39-1.41x** | **1.40-1.42x** | **1.44x** | rel_p50 2.0e-5, rel_p999 1.3e-2 |
| d4x1 | 2.31-2.34x | 2.37-2.38x | 2.58-2.63x | rel_p50 5.3e-3 - too coarse alone |

The three-plane d4x3 exact chain costs ~1.4x compute versus d4x2; the
second residual plane is the expensive one (x1->x2 costs more than
x2->x3). d4x1 is fast but too coarse as a standalone replacement.

## Candidate wired (default-off)

`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_MMQ_PLANES=2` selects the two-plane raw-vec4
d4x2 guarded chain (policy replace, dispatch re-pointed from the
non-vec4 PF-1d wrapper to the vec4 variant the screen measured). The
env must be set before generator construction (the policy resolves
once). Whole-model smoke at code-p512: warm prefill 1715-1720 ms (d4x3)
versus 1658-1670 ms (d4x2), **~3.2% faster**, repeat-deterministic
within planes; logits drift max|dlogit| 1.14 accumulated over 48 layers
(needs the envelope gates, not the stress-screen relative metric).

Projected code-p4096 effect if qualified: MMQ-owned legs (attn_qkv
1.079s + attn_q 0.424s + ssm_out 0.626s = 2.13s) at 1.4x -> **~0.6s
saved** of the 13.49s device total (~4.5% p4096 prefill). This exceeds
the newer engine's whole dense-linear advantage (0.5-0.8s) - d4x2
would close the dense gap to the newer halo-box HIP engine and beat
the old-pin gap (~2.45s of the old-pin linear excess).

## Validation

- Tile sweep (d4x3, raw-vec4): 128x128 retained; 128x64 1.00-1.05x on the wide-N
  legs, 64x64 0.93-0.97x, 64x128 0.77-0.82x. All tiles bit-identical to 128x128,
  with the template guarantee verified per geometry.
- Plane sweep (128x128, raw-vec4): d4x2 +1.39-1.41x (attn_qkv 2560->10240),
  +1.40-1.42x (attn_q / attn_gate 2560->6144), +1.44x (ssm_out 6144->2560);
  d4x1 2.31-2.63x. Stress-screen drift versus d4x3: d4x2 rel_p50 2.0e-5 /
  rel_p999 1.3e-2; d4x1 rel_p50 5.3e-3.
- Whole-model smoke at code-p512: warm prefill 1658-1670ms (d4x2) versus
  1715-1720ms (d4x3), ~3.2% faster, repeat-deterministic within planes;
  max|dlogit| 1.14 over 48 layers.
- Stated limits: the stress-screen relative metric is not an envelope gate, and
  the ~0.6s code-p4096 saving is a projection (MMQ-owned legs 2.13s at 1.4x), not
  a measurement.
