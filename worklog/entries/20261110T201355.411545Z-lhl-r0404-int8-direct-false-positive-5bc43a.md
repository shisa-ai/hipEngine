## Analysis / open root cause

The gate builds the session with `kv_policy=resolve_kv_policy("int8_per_token_head",
scale_dtype="fp32", scale_granularity="per_token_head").create_policy()`
(gate line ~393-406) but the resident KV storage dtype comes out BF16. The
`PURE_INT8_ENV` (`HIPENGINE_GGUF_INT8_KV_ALLOW_UNVERIFIED_LONG=1`,
`HIPENGINE_GGUF_INT8_KV_BF16_FULL_LAYERS=none`) is applied via
`route_environment` only INSIDE the per-arm blocks (gate lines 426/450), AFTER
`Qwen35GGUFResidentSession(...)` is constructed (line 397). If the INT8 KV
admission/storage-dtype decision is made at session construction, those env
vars are set too late and the session falls back to BF16. Runner admission reads
`_GGUF_INT8_ALLOW_UNVERIFIED_LONG_ENV` at qwen35_gguf_runner.py:10464/10615.
Not yet confirmed whether the dtype decision is at construction or re-planned
per arm (`_rearm_bulk_workspace` releases only the bulk workspace).
