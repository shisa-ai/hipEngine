# Read production's dense Q8_0 projections through the residual F16 tile

## Summary

The single-product F16 tile failed the production gate on long prompts
([decision](20261126T173747.968486Z-lhl-inkling2-mini-81272f.md)), so `production` read the exact
coltile kernel again and with it 35% of the prefill. `dense_wide64x128_residual_f32_f32_out` is a
fourth form of the same tile: every activation and every weight is staged as a high and an unscaled
residual F16 value, and a step takes three products. The high by high product accumulates on its own
and the two products with a residual on another, added once at the end. Against the dequantized
reference in FP64 its error is 7.1e-6 of the RMS over K2304 and 9.5e-6 over K3072, where the single
tile's is 2.9e-4 and the exact kernel's 1.1e-7.

Chained into the one accumulator, as the expert projection does, the three products cost 2.1e-5 and
2.8e-5 at the same time (W7900, K2304 by N3072, 1,024 rows: 631 to 640 us under the profiler for
both). So the residual products stay apart.

`production` takes the residual tile from 192 rows for 1,024 or more outputs and from 512 for fewer;
`fast` keeps the single tile from 64 and 384 rows. The two `linear` scopes of the plan are
`prompt_block_q8_0_dense_n1024_n2304_n3072` and `prompt_block_q8_0_dense_n384`.
