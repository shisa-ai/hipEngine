# GD-4 is not a port: five components, one with no precedent in the family and one that changes a test contract

## Summary

GD-4 reads as "chunked GDN on fp16 WMMA", i.e. a port of a known technique. Checking what
the tree already has shows it is **not a port: five components, one with no precedent in
the `linear_attn` family and one of which changes a test contract rather than adding to
it.** WMMA appears **only in the attention family**, used inline inside
`process_attention_tile` in `attention/laguna_flash_attention_prefill.hip` — attention-shaped,
not a callable generic GEMM helper — and the GDN family is **entirely scalar-FMA**. And
component 5 is not "add a test": the existing prefill contract holds every route **exact to
serial decode**, which a chunked scan **cannot satisfy by construction** because reassociating
the recurrence's floating-point sums is the entire point.

## What changed about the estimate

Nothing about the arithmetic, numerics or operating point changed in this iteration -
those were settled. What changed is **what "build it" means**: the row's phrasing
("chunked GDN on fp16 WMMA", naming an upstream PR) implies porting a known artifact
into a family that already has the primitives. It does not. The family is scalar-FMA,
and the one WMMA implementation in the tree is shaped for attention rather than exposed
as a GEMM.

**The estimate consequence is the point of recording it.** A build started under
"port it" reaches component 1 and discovers it is writing a new GEMM primitive; a build
started under this record knows that component 1 is the risk and can validate it in
isolation before the scan exists.

## Component 5 is the one most likely to be missed

It is easy to read "add a test" as local work. It is not: the tree's prefill contract is
*exact to serial decode*, and a chunked scan cannot meet it at all. Adding an
envelope-based contract alongside the exact one is an execution-profile change governed
by `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md`. **A builder who writes the kernel and then looks for the
test to make it pass will find that the test they need does not exist and cannot be
made to.**

## Still no retained win

Three iterations (115-117) have now turned GD-4 from a row reading "screen state drift"
into a fully specified build with a known cost, and none of them produced a performance
improvement. The objective's bar is still unmet.
