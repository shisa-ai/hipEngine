# Bind GR DPP reduction to execution ownership

## Summary

R0443 moves GR DPP selection to execution ownership without changing the
registered DPP, wave-scale or fused-parent arithmetic and fallback order.
Unchanged-code captures and CPU RED tests preceded implementation.

## Changes

- Runtime dispatch now consumes the plan-published `gr_dpp_reduction` field
  through scalar/prefill/head/layer, late-clone, batch and mixed-target owners.
  The environment reader and writer are retired; kernel bodies are unchanged.
- Campaign and state-gate scopes bind the measured runner, close wide/IU8
  preemptors, hold wave-scale on and restore all controls. Wave comparisons
  explicitly close the DPP owner. K320 screens bind their required DPP parent;
  journey snapshots and split phases reconstruct their former decisions.
- Updated environment and kernel references to the owner-bound interface.
- Created `/tmp/r0443-capture.py` before editing production. It selects both
  DPP states with wide/IU8 preemptors closed and wave-scale held on, then
  observes actual resolved leaves rather than inferring them from controls.
