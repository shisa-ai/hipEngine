# Hardware counters confirm HC-1's LDS premise and close DP-8's LDS route

## Summary

**A full `rocprofv3` hardware-counter census of the Qwen4Exp kernel families
confirms `HC-1`'s LDS premise and closes `DP-8`'s LDS direction.** The GR up
kernel `q8_0_gr_up_sigmoid_mean_coltile2_branch4_rowbatch4_f32` waits on LDS for
**32.432% of its wave cycles** - the only kernel in the top nine with a material
LDS stall - which is why its staging screen could reach 2.388x; the route stays
blocked on mean KL. The exact coltile leaf
`gguf_k_prefill_out_coltile_rowbatch_pipelined_kernel` waits on LDS for
**0.015%**, the lowest figure in the census, so there is no LDS-scheduling prize
in the exact chain - and that chain is **3.8% of device time** regardless. Every
top kernel issues VALU on 0.4-9.0% of wave cycles with near-zero LDS wait: the
decode device path is uniformly VMEM-latency bound.

`DP-8` named "the hardware-counter capture" as its last unstarted route, and
`HC-1`'s 2.388x LDS-staging screen had never had its premise checked with
counters. Both are now answered.

## Changes

- **No production code, kernel, flag or dispatch changed.** This is a
  measurement unit; the only edits are this entry and the `DP-8` / `HC-1` rows
  in `docs/QWEN4EXP-OPTIMIZATION-LEDGER.md`, which now carry the counter
  evidence.

### Method, and the step that has to be right

The capture is `rocprofv3` over
`scripts/qwen4exp_canonical_ar_bench.py hipengine --case-id code-p512
--prefill-chunk-size 1024`, PMC set
`SQ_WAIT_INST_ANY_sum,SQ_WAIT_INST_LDS_sum,SQ_WAVE_CYCLES_sum,SQ_INSTS_VALU_sum,SQ_INSTS_LDS_sum,SQ_INSTS_SALU_sum`,
plus `--kernel-trace --runtime-trace -f csv`.

**The recipe in `benchmarks/HARNESSES.md` "Profiling these harnesses under
rocprofv3 (read before launching)" must be followed in full, including step 2.**
Two attempts that exported only `HIPENGINE_REQUIRE_CACHED_BUILD=1` without
`HIPENGINE_COMPILER_VERSION_FILE` produced **374 traced kernels, all
`__amd_rocclr_copyBuffer` / `__amd_rocclr_fillBufferUnAligned`, and
`samples: []`** - the run exits cleanly, writes a JSON with an empty sample
array, and looks like a successful capture of an idle device. Adding
`hipcc --version > <file>` captured **outside** the profiler and exporting
`HIPENGINE_COMPILER_VERSION_FILE` to it yields **902,778 kernel rows and
4,513,890 counter rows** with 3 valid samples. The failure mode is silent, so
the kernel count is the check: a capture with no named model kernel is a failed
capture, not a fast device.

### Findings

**1. `HC-1`'s premise is confirmed, and it is unique in the capture.** The GR up
kernel is the **only** kernel in the top nine with a material LDS stall:
**32.4% of its wave cycles wait on LDS**, while every other top kernel is at or
below 1.66%. That is exactly the condition its LDS-staging screen exploited for
2.388x, and it explains why that screen was able to reach 2.388x at all. **This
does not reopen the route**: the screen is blocked by broad-based mean KL at
every scope, and the counters say the premise was right while the precision
route remains unavailable. `HC-1`'s disposition is unchanged; its diagnosis now
has direct evidence.

**2. `DP-8`'s LDS direction is closed by measurement.** The exact coltile chain
leaf `gguf_k_prefill_out_coltile_rowbatch_pipelined_kernel` waits on LDS for
**0.015% of its wave cycles** - the lowest stall figure of any kernel measured -
while carrying the **highest VALU issue rate of the memory-bound group (4.674%)**.
It is the least-stalled kernel in the census and it is **3.8% of device time**.
So: there is no LDS-staging prize in the exact chain (the stall is not there),
and the chain is not the lever regardless (3.8%). `DP-8`'s "16.1% of its own
instruction-mix ceiling" is consistent with this - the ceiling is a VALU-issue
ceiling, and a kernel waiting 0.015% on LDS is not going to reach it by
scheduling LDS better.

**3. The decode device path is uniformly VMEM-latency bound.** Every top kernel
issues VALU on **0.4-9.0% of wave cycles** with LDS wait near zero. For decode
GEMVs that is the signature of global-memory latency on the weight stream, which
is the expected regime and matches `DE-1`'s finding that the MoE family is
**not** bandwidth-bound at 37% of roof. The one exception is the wmma risk
prefill kernel at 9.006%, the most ALU-dense kernel in the capture.

**4. `DE-3`'s owner rank is reproduced from an independent capture.** The
linear family's owner `gguf_k_pack8_prefill_out_kernel` is the single largest
kernel at **37.7% of device time over 254,620 dispatches**. `DE-2` recorded it
as "predominantly the LINEAR family's owner at 22.184 ms/step of 23.166". This
capture agrees from a different run, and adds that its dispatch count is
~1,989 per decode step (41.4 per layer).

### Method note: do not profile decode for wall numbers

Under the same harness, the profiled arm measures **prefill 258.5 tok/s against
272.3 unprofiled (-5%)** but **decode 9.72 tok/s against 20.95 (-54%, a 2.16x
slowdown)**. Decode issues ~2,000 launches per step, so per-launch interception
costs it 2.16x while prefill - few launches, long kernels - is nearly untouched.
The profiled arm's prefill number is usable at the 5% level; **its decode number
is not a decode measurement at all** and must not be compared to an unprofiled
decode baseline.

## Validation

- **The capture is self-validating on two independent axes.** The profiled arm
  returns 3 valid samples (prefill 258.4/258.7/258.5 tok/s, a 0.12% spread)
  rather than the empty `samples: []` of the failed attempts, and the kernel
  trace contains **902,778 named model kernels** rather than 374 copy/fill
  entries. Both were checked before any counter was read.
- **The counter ratios reproduce the ledger's independent family attribution.**
  `DE-2`'s join recorded `gguf_k_pack8_prefill_out_kernel` as the linear family's
  dominant owner at 22.184 ms/step of 23.166; this capture ranks the same kernel
  first at 37.7% of device time from a different run, and `DE-1`'s per-kernel
  join names `q5_1_selected_weighted_sum_logical256_t64` and
  `gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_silu_logical128_t64` among the top owners, which this
  capture also ranks in the top nine. Two independently produced rankings agree.
- **No performance claim is made from this capture**, so the profiler's own
  inflation of launch-dense phases does not propagate into the result; the
  deliverable is the stall ratios and the device-time shares.
- **No correctness gate applies**: no kernel, arithmetic, flag or dispatch was
  modified.
