# Gemma 4: the largest prefill route's tile shape was never swept

## Summary

The Q4_K expert gate projection is the largest single route in Gemma 4's
512-token prefill (1037 ms, 38%) and its staged grouped prefill had never had its
tile shape swept at the geometry the route is actually asked for. `row_batch` 4
against the carried 8 is **32.52 against 35.75 ms (1.10x)**, and the 512-token
prefill is **3.095 -> 3.002 s (1.031x)**. It is bit-exact, so it ships on the
default path with no flag and no gate.

The direction is the opposite of the Q5_1 route's closed tile space, which is the
part worth remembering: a wider output tile is worth 1.4-1.6x to *this* kernel
and is the wrong choice for Q5_1. The two stage different things - this one
stages activations, Q5_1 stages weight metadata - so a tile result from one does
not transfer to the other. Both are also not weight-traffic results: `row_batch`
4 doubles the passes over the block's weight bytes and is still faster.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q4_k_gemv.hip`: the staged grouped
  prefill launcher's `row_batch` is 4 instead of 8, with the sweep recorded in
  the comment. No other change; `out_tile` stays 8, and `threads`/`k_tile` are
  not free because the block size is also the k-stride.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-27-gemma4-q4k-moe-prefill-rowbatch4-accepted.json`
  and the campaign document carry the sweep and the public row.

## Validation

- **Geometry from the route, not assumed.** A live 512-token forward with the
  registered kernel wrapped recorded in 2816, out 1408, 128 experts, 4096
  compact rows, row_bytes 1584, 285.5 MB of weights, grid 176 x 128 and 567
  passes at `row_batch` 8. The previous iteration's lesson - an assumed geometry
  cost 2x - is why this is recorded rather than derived.
- **Bit-exactness.** 0 of 11,534,336 bf16 outputs differ between the shapes at a
  skewed distribution with 11 empty experts and a 219-row maximum, so partial
  last batches are exercised. `tests/test_unit_gemma4_expert_route.py` passes 46
  tests, including the grouped-prefill case that compares against the per-row
  gather rather than against another tile shape.
- **Kernel isolation.** Six timed launches per shape, minimum reported, all
  shapes in one process at the route's geometry.
- **Public A/B.** The same timing script in two git worktrees, one warm-up and
  five timed 512-token forwards each: 3.095 -> 3.002 s medians (minima 3.090 ->
  2.990). The -93 ms public delta matches the predicted -93.5 ms route delta, so
  the route change accounts for the whole effect rather than the measurement
  being confounded by anything else in the tree.

## Next

- **One accounting gap this opened.** The route is called once per layer with
  `out_features` 1408, so a single launch covers the whole expert projection per
  layer. If the model's gated MLP projects a separate up half, that half is not
  in the registry route attribution at all - the same way attention is not - and
  has never been accounted for in the prefill budget. Worth resolving before the
  next profile, because a second 2816 x 1408 expert projection per layer would be
  comparable in size to the routes already at the top of the table.
- Every other large route is now a gate candidate awaiting the `kl_max`
  decision: Q4_K gate/up 943 ms (MMQ, measured 1.27x), Q8_0 dense 739 ms (MMQ
  family), Q5_1 down 652 ms (`K_TILE=512`, 1.17x).
