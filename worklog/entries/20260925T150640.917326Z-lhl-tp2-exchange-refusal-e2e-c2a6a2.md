# the exchange refusal is inert on both real TP2 routes

## Summary

`79a66af91` made the device exchange refuse a timeout-flag clear while a
submitted step is unobserved. A refusal that fires on a legitimate sequence
would be worse than the hole it closes, so the two routes that submit through
the exchange were run end-to-end after that change:

| route | exchange shape | prefill | decode | session VRAM/rank |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| bulk prefill | `begin_device_group` reset, per-layer bump, one wait | 1065.99 tok/s | 42.33 tok/s | 8.961 GiB |
| token-serial prefill | `step_begin` per step, one wait per step | 43.99 tok/s | 41.72 tok/s | 8.961 GiB |

Neither run raised the refusal, so the two legal shapes are exactly the ones the
rule admits: a group that resets once and bumps per layer, and a step that begins
and waits before the next begins. The rates also match the promoted route
(bulk 1065.99 against the interleaved 1046-1056 tok/s, decode 42.33 against
41.55/41.66), so the refusal did not perturb the schedule it guards. The 8.961
GiB is the after-load reading, which is the one `tp2_c1_bench` prints; the
after-warmup reading that the route comparison quotes is 11.952 GiB.
