# C2 AR has no independent cause; it is the C2 prefill deficit

## Summary

Task #18 asked to close the C2 AR gap (32.14 vs 35.44 tok/s, -9.32% against a 3.77%
spread). There is no
independent C2 AR problem to close: the gap is the **C2 prefill deficit arriving inside
complete-wall
timing**. Replacing only the admission component of the C2 wall with a peer-equal
prefill moves the rate
to **35.88 and 35.05 tok/s** on the two repeat runs, against the peer's 35.44 - inside
the noise band on
one run and 1.1% short on the other. #18 is therefore downstream of #12, not a separate defect.

## Changes

- No code change. Collapses two open deficits into one and retargets #18.

## Evidence

- **The decode path is not the cause.** From
  `scripts/gguf_engine_submodule_decomposition.py` over both
  repeat pairs, AR decode-step cost is 34.12 / 34.28 ms at C1 and 42.29 / 42.81 ms at C2.
  Aggregate decode
  throughput scales C1 -> C2 by **1.61x**, where the peer long-rate scales **1.63x**. A
  1.2% difference in
  two-lane decode scaling cannot produce a 9.32% complete-wall deficit. From C3 our decode
  scaling leaves
  the peers behind (2.33x vs 1.42x), which is why C3+ are wins despite prefill still being
  0.892x there.
- **Admission is the difference.** C2 admission is 268.2 / 282.6 ms per lane on the two runs, and C2
  prefill measures 0.681x of peer. Applied to the admission component only:

| run | wall/lane | admission/lane | measured rate | at peer-equal prefill |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| r1 | 754.5 ms | 268.2 ms | 31.81 | **35.88** |
| r2 | 774.9 ms | 282.6 ms | 30.97 | **35.05** |

- Peer `current` at C2 is 35.440, carried forward from `benchmarks/README.md`.
- Reading: C2 is the crossover width where our prefill deficit is no longer offset by a
  decode advantage
  that only starts at C3. Fixing small-row prefill closes C2 as a side effect.

## Stated assumption

The correction treats C2 admission as pure prefill cost scaled by the measured 0.681x,
with no decode
interaction. That is an assumption, not a measurement. What makes the direction safe is
that both runs
agree and the residual after correction is within the measured 3.77% band; the precise
post-fix value must
be measured after a prefill fix, not asserted from this arithmetic.
