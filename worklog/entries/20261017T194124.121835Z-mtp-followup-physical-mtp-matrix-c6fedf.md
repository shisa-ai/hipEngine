# Verify Qwen MTP physical execution across C1 to C4

## Summary

The corrected real-server replay distinguishes HTTP submission shape from
resident physical work. Qwen3.8 Q4_K_M and Q4_K_S both completed AR and
explicit-MTP C1, C2, C3, and C4 replays with HTTP 200 responses. Explicit MTP
reported `used=true`; AR reported `used=false`. `/ready` reported
`max_packed_rows=8` for all three loaded BF16 sessions after the packed-width
repair.

Qwen3.6-35B-A3B Q4_K_M also completed AR C1-C4 and greedy explicit-MTP C1-C4.
Its MoE adapter uses singleton native target passes at C1-C3 and physically
batches at C4 with proposal width 2 and target token-row width 6. That is a
capability-shaped route, not a failed C4 replay.

## Evidence

The compact diagnostic artifact is
`benchmarks/results/2026-10-17-mtp-physical-execution-matrix.json`. It records
SHA-256 hashes of the 24 replay outputs under the local recovery directory and
contains no throughput or numerical acceptance claim.

- Q4_K_M: AR C2-C4 increased `native_packed_decode_steps`; MTP C2/C3/C4
  recorded proposal widths reaching 2/3/4 and target widths reaching 8/12/16.
- Q4_K_S: AR C2-C4 increased `native_packed_decode_steps`; MTP C2/C3/C4
  recorded proposal widths reaching 2/3/4 and target widths reaching 8/12/16.
- Qwen3.6 MoE: AR C1-C4 completed; MTP C1-C3 recorded target passes without
  packed proposal counters, while C4 recorded proposal widths 2 and target
  width 6. All replay rows passed the HTTP/MTP-used checks and generated 32
  tokens in the deterministic greedy rerun.

The initial sampled MoE attempt was not treated as a failure: one row selected
EOS after five tokens at `temperature=0.7`. The rerun used `temperature=0.0`
and produced the fixed 32-token rows above.

## Validation command

Each model was launched with the gfx1151 backend, BF16 KV, prefix cache off,
`--max-active-requests 4`, `--generation-batch-window-ms 0`, and Prometheus
metrics enabled. Requests were sent through `scripts/mtp_http_replay.py` at
C1/C2/C3/C4. The Q4 dense run also asserted that each C2-C4 AR replay advanced
`native_packed_decode_steps`; MTP physical counters came from
`diagnostics.specdec2_mtp2.physical_execution`.

This is physical-route evidence, not a production-profile numerical gate or a
performance claim. The four-model public acceptance matrix, including Gemma
controls and strict-teacher numerical evaluation, remains open.
