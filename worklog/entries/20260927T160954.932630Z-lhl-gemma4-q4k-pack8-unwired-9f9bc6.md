# The unwired q4_k pack8 GEMV is never-finished, not retired

## Summary

Established why the q4_k selected pack8 GEMV is unwired, which the previous entry
left as an open hypothesis. It is **never finished, not retired**, and the
mechanism is a missing registry entry.

Evidence:

- The symbol came in with `7371f7c0b`, *"perf: improve qwen35moe GGUF decode
  partially"* - a Qwen35 decode commit.
- The device kernel is real: `launch_gguf_q4_k_selected_pack8_gemv_out` at
  `gguf_q4_k_gemv.hip:1432`, with its `extern "C"` entry at :2894.
- `tests/test_gpu_gguf_expert_pack8_gemv.py` covers the family, so this is not
  untested scaffolding.
- A repository-wide grep finds **no caller** for the q4_k wrapper - not in
  gemma4, and not in `qwen35_gguf_runner.py` either, which *does* call the q5_k
  and q6_k pack8 wrappers at :38900.
- The registry variant map in `gguf_k_gemv.py` registers `selected_pack8_gemv`
  for q8_0 (:1599), q5_k (:1641) and q6_k (:1679), and **not for q4_k**.

That last point is the mechanism. `gemma4_project_experts_selected` resolves its
kernel through the registry by `(backend, linear, quant_key, variant)`, so an
unregistered variant is unreachable **by construction** rather than by a decision
to avoid it. Nothing was retired; the entry was never added.

This is the concrete instance of the objective's premise that Qwen35 work
transfers. The same family is wired for three quant types and missing for the one
Gemma 4 uses for its expert gate/up - which is also the largest single line in
decode at 28.0%, running at about 15% of bandwidth.
