# UD-U1 review repair 2: rank-3 IQ3_XXS selected-expert MoE coverage

## Summary

The U1 admission whitelist for `moe_experts` omitted `IQ3_XXS`, even though the
materializer keeps rank-3 IQ3_XXS experts raw and `gguf_iq_gemv` registers
selected `gguf_iq3_xxs` `moe_linear` consumers on hip_gfx1100 and hip_gfx1151
(selected GEMV, dual-SiLU, tile4, k1024-wave4, weighted-down variants). A MoE
artifact with IQ3_XXS experts was therefore refused by the U1 preflight — a
regression against pre-U1 load behavior. This restores the actual expert
support as an explicit raw rank-3 contract (NOT a dense rank-2 T16/X8 claim).

## Changes

- `hipengine/loading/qwen35_gguf_admission.py`: dedicated `moe_experts`
  coverage records for `IQ3_XXS` on `LAYOUT_RAW_GGUF` only, naming the
  registered `gguf_iq3_xxs` selected consumer family as the strict fallback;
  bf16 in/out; rows_1_8_row_local + prefill_rows scopes. No IQ3_XXS record on
  repacked layouts (no such repack exists), and rank-2 dense IQ3_XXS remains
  planner-refused until UD-U2..U5.

## Tests (tests/test_gguf_ud_admission.py, RED first)

- `test_rank3_iq3_xxs_selected_experts_keep_their_registered_moe_consumers` —
  synthetic qwen35moe map with rank-3 IQ3_XXS experts: RED (refused with
  "no certified consumer ... source=IQ3_XXS layout='raw_gguf'"), then GREEN;
  pins raw-only layout, `moe_selected` family, bf16 dtypes, both row scopes,
  and support on both HIP backends.
- `test_rank2_dense_iq3_xxs_is_refused_not_silently_supported` — a rank-2
  IQ3_XXS `ffn_gate` is aggregated as `planner_refused` ("rank-3"), no
  certificate.
