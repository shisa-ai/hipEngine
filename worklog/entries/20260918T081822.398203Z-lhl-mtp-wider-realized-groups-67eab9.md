# Wider realized MTP groups: the c=4 lane is AR-favorable, and the width-2 path does not execute

## Summary

The review's top lever was "wider realized MTP groups plus mixed K/K0
partitioning" on the strength of a c=2 measurement (2 of 3 rows never entering
speculation, 31.9% speculative share). Re-measured on the fixed verifier
(`4edc9c273`), that premise does not reproduce, and the wider-group promotion is
not supported:

- **c=2 is already healthy**: 8 of 8 automatic rows ran speculation, each at
  99%+ coverage, with exactly one boundary-token refusal each; the in-run true-AR
  control measured **24.01 against 20.14 tok/s (+19.2%)**. The reviewed 31.9%
  figure came from the head tree, whose verifier did not reproduce true AR.
- **c=4 lands at parity**: 12.06 against 12.14 (-0.7%), 8 of 8 rows at realized
  width 1, `no_provider` events 8 -> 448. The control tree measured the same
  (-2%), so the fix restored the concurrent lane as well as the sequential one.
- **The width-2 automatic cell fails its retained gate and stays closed.**
  `2026-08-29` measured exactly that cell after the faster AR rebase at
  **1.0517x overall with mixed Japanese/English at 0.9056x**, which is why its
  automatic promotion was withdrawn then (`529f502a3`). Nothing on this tree
  changes that: a speculative cycle at c=4 costs about 2.2 autoregressive steps
  (median ITL 208.4 against 141.1 ms) while emitting about 2.1 tokens
  (2.10 accepted drafts per cycle), so the lane can only reach parity.
- **Enabling the width-2 bound makes c=4 worse and does not create a width-2
  group.** A temporary local edit (`max_realized_group_rows=2` on the promoted
  automatic width-1 row) left `groups_by_realized_rows` at the width-1 bucket in
  both lanes, and at c=4 took the lane from -0.7% to **-2.0%** (13.48 against
  13.75) with `no_provider` events 448 -> 1050. One 820-token row lost its route
  entirely: 0 cycles and 819 autoregressive steps (coverage 0.728 -> 0.000) after
  a single `precommit_failure_ar_fallback`.

An in-process probe shows the claims contract is not the blocker: with
`max_realized_group_rows=2` the adapter reports `partition_max_requests((a,b)) = 2`,
`capability()` is not `None` at width 2, and the two-row plan is all-positive-K
with `claims_fit` True. The blocker is the engine/state lifecycle, and two
defects are recorded below.

Protocol: `scripts/sharegpt_mtp_routing_pass.py --cases short,long --route-mix
off,auto --record-ids`, 8 ShareGPT prompts, natural output lengths,
`--max-total-len 1024`, stagger 400 ms (c=2) / 250 ms (c=4), 2 prefix repeats, 2
cancels, one warmup, `HIPENGINE_MTP2_OUTPUT_SPANS=1`, one server process per run
on `--max-context-tokens 8192 --kv-storage bf16 --execution-profile production
--speculative-mtp-serving auto --speculative-candidate-budget 3`. The same load
carries the true-AR control in-run because `--route-mix` assigns the arm per
request.

Per-row economics at c=4 (same prompt served by both arms): the 824-token
speculative row finished in 148.2 s against its AR peer's 187.5 s (**+26%**),
while the 820-token row that lost its route finished in 188.3 s against its AR
peer's 83.1 s (**-56%**). The lane's parity is the mix of those two outcomes, not
a uniform wash: rows that keep speculation win, rows that lose it lose badly, and
the aggregate is a coin flip on which happens.

## Defects recorded

1. **Group-wide K0 fallback.** When a due group's plan has a positive-K subset
   smaller than the group, `claims_fit` refuses it and
   `engine_loop._maybe_run_speculative_cycle` replans with `claims_fit=False`,
   which zeroes *every* row of the group (`RESOURCE_CLAIM_MISS`/`no_provider`).
   That is the group-wide split the review named. At c=4 it costs the blocked
   rows 223 of their 401 steps. Fixing it requires either a qualified width >= 2
   or an explicit decision about serial singleton cycles, which
   `docs/QWEN38-27B-GFX1100-CONCURRENCY2-BETTER-MTP.md` currently forbids
   ("do not allow a multi-request due batch to become serial singleton MTP
   calls").
2. **Sticky route loss after a recoverable failure.** After one
   `precommit_failure_ar_fallback` a row can end up without provider state
   (`capability()` returns `None` because the request is in neither `_states` nor
   `_prompt_hidden_rows`), after which every step reports `no_provider` for the
   rest of the request. Latent on the shipped width-1 bound, reachable as soon as
   a wider group is admitted.

## Decision

- Do **not** promote the width-2 automatic cell. The retained measurement of that
  exact cell is 1.0517x with a category below 1.0, and this tree adds no
  contrary evidence.
- Keep the c>=4 lane AR-favorable (fail closed). A speculative cycle's cost, not
  the grouping width, is what decides this lane: pairing two rows would need the
  two-row verify to cost what the one-row verify costs.
- The lever worth pursuing next is the cycle's own cost (verify plus
  commit/rollback plus draft forward), not wider groups.
