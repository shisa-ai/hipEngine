# Revalidate adaptive MTP economics on Qwen3.6 35B

## Summary

Revalidated ROCmFPX transferable opportunity 5 on the admitted Qwen3.6
35B-A3B UD-Q4_K_M + NextN path. The candidate is hipEngine's existing prompt-
agnostic policy: admit cycles at `p_min=0.5`, begin with a B1 probe, promote
depth only after a full accept, demote after partial acceptance, and cap the
active depth at B1/B2/B3. Matched controls keep every admitted cycle at a fixed
B1/B2/B3 depth with the same vocab cap, p-min, strict top-1 target, verifier,
and direct commit.

The adaptive structure is economically positive: adaptive B3 is **27.054
tok/s / 37.140 ms-output**, versus current true AR **23.852 tok/s** (1.134x),
best fixed B1 **24.250 tok/s / 41.328 ms** (1.116x rate, -10.13% wall), and
fixed B3 **22.115 tok/s / 45.297 ms** (1.223x, -18.01% wall). It reduces B3
target rows/output **1.596 -> 1.288 (-19.32%)** while giving up four accepted
tokens. All 30 candidate and all 30 fixed prompt/budget rows exactly match the
same-run AR token stream.

This is a diagnostic revalidation, not a new topline or budget promotion. The
raw child provenance observed unrelated dirty execution-profile docs/tests, the
strict-profile absolute denominator is not comparable with the retained public
MTP row, and adaptive B3 beats adaptive B2 by only 0.97% in one run. Keep the
already-existing adaptive mechanism; do not add a prompt map or token-specific
selector and do not change the production cap from this packet.
