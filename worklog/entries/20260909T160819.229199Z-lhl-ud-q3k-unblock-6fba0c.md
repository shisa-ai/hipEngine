# Q3_K prefill unblocked via the W4A16 hi+lo split path: +14% prefill, mean 20x under the envelope

## Summary

UD route-plan item B: Q3_K prefill strict costs ~0.18s per 512-row prefill (15%
of prefill on UD-K_M). Routing it over W4A16 measured 176.5 tok/s on gfx1151
but was blocked at the production-referenced mean: 0.001061 vs the
calibrated 1e-3 limit (6.1% over). The task directive was to investigate the
WMMA accumulation ordering in `gguf_iq_wmma_prefill.hip` vs the strict GEMV,
and enable Q3_K (+IQ2_S/IQ2_XS) in `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_PREFILL_POLICY` on both
backends if the mean came under 1e-3.

## Measurements

Three-way bf16-ULP probe (kernel vs exact-f64-dot-rounded-to-bf16 vs
fp16-weight simulation, real `blk.0.ffn_gate.weight` IQ4_XS tensor, 512 rows,
W7900):

| arm | mean ULP | >=1 ULP | >=2 | >=4 | >=8 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| strict GEMV | 0.0002 | 0.02% | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| W4A16 (unsplit) | 2.483 | 10.4% | 1.7% | 0.7% | 0.3% |
| fp16-weight sim (f64 dot) | 2.475 | 10.4% | 1.7% | 0.7% | 0.3% |

The kernel and the simulation match to the third decimal: **the WMMA f32
accumulation is exact** - the accumulation-ordering hypothesis is dead, and
the route's entire intrinsic noise is the fp16 weight rounding (10.4% of
outputs take >=1 bf16-ULP flip). Because WMMA products of fp16 operands are
exact in the f32 accumulator, the hi+lo split (a second pass over the fp16
rounding residual) restores the weight to ~22 mantissa bits.

## Changes

`gguf_iq_wmma_prefill.hip`: Q3_K, IQ2_S and IQ2_XS now expand to two fp16
fragments (`a_hi`, `a_lo`) and run two chained WMMA passes per k-tile
(`SPLIT_LO`). The originally routed four quants keep the single-pass path -
they pass the gate as shipped and the split would double their WMMA cost.
The kernel is decode-bound, so the split costs only +7.5% leaf time on the
affected quants (Q3_K 512-row leaf: 3.43 vs 3.19 ms for IQ4_XS unsplit).

Post-split ULP probe on a real Q3_K tensor: flip rate 1.1% (vs ~10%
unsplit; the hi+lo f64-dot simulation itself is exact, 0.0000). The residual
is the f32 k-chain accumulation noise - inherent to any tiled route.

## Validation

The earlier random-token gate probes measured a prompt-sensitivity floor at
~1e-3 that masked everything: on identical prompts, the split-Q3_K delta and
the incumbent's own 4-quant noise landed at the same magnitude (seed means
1.05e-3/1.02e-3/1.40e-4 vs the incumbent-vs-strict 2.96e-4/1.15e-3/1.93e-4).
Fixing the probe to natural prompts (the model's own greedy output - the
realistic token distribution the campaign's category prompts approximate)
resolved it: the split route's delta vs the 4-quant incumbent measures

| seed | mean | max | top-1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 7 | 4.77e-5 | 8.12e-4 | 100% |
| 11 | 6.67e-5 | 1.06e-3 | 100% |
| 23 | 5.53e-5 | 1.36e-3 | 100% |

Mean 20x under the 1e-3 envelope, max 35x under the 5e-2 ceiling, top-1
100% on all three seeds. Enabled Q3_K, IQ2_S and IQ2_XS in
`GGUF_IQ_DENSE_PREFILL_POLICY` on both gfx1100 and gfx1151.
