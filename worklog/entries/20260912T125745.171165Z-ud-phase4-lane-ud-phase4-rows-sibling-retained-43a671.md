The plain controls moved by -0.6% (K_M MTP 63.465 -> 63.088) and +1.5% (K_S
64.094 -> 65.067) between the two runs, which bounds the run-to-run MTP
variance at a few percent. Both UD AR arms are flat to within 0.2%, so the
gain is confined to the verifier, which is where the change is.

All four arms report `timing_evidence_valid`, `speed_claim_eligible`, complete
20-row-per-group evidence, deterministic repeats, GPU/CPU acceptance agreement,
a positive MTP/AR ratio, and generated-ID exactness (`complete_exact`, zero
divergent rows). The UD-Q4_K_S `general_ja_plan` near-tie divergences that the
2026-09-11 baseline recorded are gone: the AR route and the verifier now share
the local32 accumulation order on the IQ tensors, so the K_S near ties were a
consequence of the owner mismatch rather than independent sampling drift.

The UD/plain MTP gap is now 30.5% (K_M) and 31.7% (K_S), down from 44.0% and
48.4%. It is still concentrated in MTP compute rather than AR (14.1% / 21.3%),
and the largest remaining measured contributor is the t16
decode-versus-rowtile accumulation order (Q4_K 27.4% + Q5_K 25.7% + Q6_K 8.1%
of K_M rank-2 MACs).
