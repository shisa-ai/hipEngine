# Add a current-state tracker and a document map for the Qwen4Exp campaign

## Summary

The Qwen4Exp work had three documentation layers — immutable evidence
(`worklog/entries/`, `benchmarks/results/`), normative contracts
(`EXECUTION-PROFILES.md`, `KERNELS.md`, `REFACTOR.md`, `PLAN.md`) — and dated
campaign plans, but no current-state layer. `docs/README.md` did not mention the
QWEN4EXP family at all, so all six of its documents were invisible from the
index, and the index still described the September 8 Qwen3.8 performance
campaign as "Active".

Each session therefore either re-derived state from benchmark directories or
wrote another campaign document. Four overlapping Flash-Next campaign documents
exist for that reason.

**Decision: one living status document per active model family, rewritten in
place, carrying no raw evidence of its own.** This follows the precedent already
in the tree — `LAGUNA-PARITY-STATUS.md` is described as the canonical pause and
resumption handoff, separate from its campaign documents.

- `docs/QWEN4EXP-STATUS.md` is new. It carries our start-versus-current rates,
  the competition on both the end-to-end and kernel-time axes, the accuracy
  basis per row, the mechanism ledger of what makes the comparators fast,
  certified-but-not-promoted routes, and what is left.
- Every number in it points at the `benchmarks/results/` directory or worklog
  entry that owns it, and each is labelled measured, derived, or predicted.
- The six dated `QWEN4EXP-*` documents each gained a one-line pointer to it.
- `docs/README.md` lists the family with the status document marked as current
  state and the rest marked as dated records.

### The two axes it tracks

The end-to-end axis and the kernel-time axis disagree about who is ahead, so
both are reported with their own protocol rather than reconciled:

- End to end, hipEngine's **decode is ahead of halo-box HIP** (20.396 against
  15.441 tok/s at p512) while prefill trails it (297.1 against 427.1).
- At kernel level on one 4096-token prefill, hipEngine attributes **22368 ms**
  against halo-box PR #63's 3760 ms, and 46% of ours is a single kernel family
  (`gguf_k_prefill_out_coltile_rowbatch_kernel`, 10070 ms of matmul) running at
  9.3% of the machine's measured FP32 FMA rate.

### Accuracy is a column, not a footnote

Competitor numbers are not accuracy-comparable to ours and the document says so
per row. The comparator's dense path is the BF16 MMB line — about **33x coarser**
than the F16 path this campaign gated, and shipped ungated. Our bar is stated
explicitly as **production correctness under the calibrated envelope in
`EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` 6.1, not bit-exactness**: a candidate that is not
bit-identical to the strict parent is admissible if it passes.

## Changes

- `docs/QWEN4EXP-STATUS.md`: new current-state tracker.
- `docs/README.md`: family rows added, status document marked current, stale
  "Active" label corrected on the Qwen3.8 performance campaign, date refreshed.

  pointer line marking each as a dated record.

## Validation

- Documentation only; no dispatch, kernel, or arithmetic path is touched. The
  layers 16-47 gate arm running concurrently recorded
  `execution_affecting_dirty: false` with these paths dirty, confirming the
  classification treats them as inert.
- Every figure in the status document was read from a committed artifact in this
  session; the two that are not measurements (the BF16 arithmetic row and the
  per-scope savings below 32-47) are labelled derived and predicted
  respectively.
