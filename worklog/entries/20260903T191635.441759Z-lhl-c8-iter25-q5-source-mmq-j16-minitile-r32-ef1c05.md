# C8 iter25: Q5 source-MMQ J16 minitile at R32

## Summary

Attacked the remaining 7.280 ms/cycle Q5 gap (C8-P2) by fixing the source-MMQ
launch geometry instead of the kernel body. At R32 the retained J32 policy
launches 80 workgroups of 128 threads (4 waves) on 96 CUs — under-occupied
with one serial K chain per wave. The J16 minitile instantiation already in
the binary halves the per-block serial K chain and doubles the workgroup
count (160). An offline ISA audit also killed the "LDS byte-assembly" theory:
clang already merges the WMMA fragment packs into ds_load_b128.

## Findings

- Leaf A/B on all 48 actual ssm_out weights at rows=32 (counterbalanced arm
  order, HIP events, consumer-only bursts, shared kmajor producer excluded):
  J16 sum **9.581 ms vs J32 sum 11.506 ms = 1.2007x**, **48/48 weight wins**,
  all outputs **bf16 bit-identical** (same per-(row, output) accumulation
  order; only the launch geometry changes).
- ISA audit of the current J32 kernel (hipcc -O3 --offload-arch=gfx1100):
  inner loop has 24x ds_load_b128, 16x v_wmma_i32_16x16x16_iu8, 26x
  global_load_b32, and zero ds_read_u8 — the pack_u8x4/pack_i8x4 byte
  assembly is fully merged by the compiler.
- Default policy now resolves rows>=32 to the J16 minitile behind the
  zero-valued `HIPENGINE_GGUF_Q5_SOURCE_C8_J16` rollback (literal J32 policy
  restored when set to 0). Forced-J16 explicit launchers added for leaf use.
- Projected e2e effect is modest: the R32 source consumer is only part of the
  10.940 ms/cycle Q5 pool (raw owner still owns R24 rounds, plus producers),
  so the 1.20x consumer win translates to an estimated ~1-2 ms/cycle. The L2
  marker and L3 full C8 gate (next iteration) will measure the real number.
