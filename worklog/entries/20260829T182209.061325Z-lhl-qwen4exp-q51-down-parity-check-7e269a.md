# Q5_1 down already at parity; iu8 variant rejected

## Summary

Measured the Q5_1 down-projection family against llama before porting the
iu8 approach there: the existing f16-WMMA down kernel reaches
3.93 ms / 3.39 TF/s at the real shape (5080 rows, 512 experts, 640->2048) —
already FASTER than llama's down-projection rate (16.65 GFLOP at ~7.6 ms =
2.2 TF/s). A prototype iu8 down kernel (exact q + 3 residual planes,
single-matrix, short-tail K chunks) measured 5.54 ms / 2.40 TF/s: the LDS
staging and plane overhead dominate at K=640. The iu8 down variant is
rejected as a regression; the uncommitted prototype was discarded.

## Changes

- None retained (prototype reverted); this entry records the measured
  boundary so the down family is not re-ported without new evidence.

## Validation

- Microbench at the real shape with a NumPy dequant reference: f16 vs ref
  max_abs 0.032, iu8 vs ref max_abs 0.031 (both correct); timings as above.
  Script /tmp/qwen4exp_q51_iu8_check.py.

## Next

- The down family is at parity; remaining gaps are GDN (8.7x), QSA
  (9.8x), and decode (1.1-1.55x vs llama HIP/Vulkan tg32).
