## It is not automatically admissible, and the reason is not a threshold

`status` is `invalid_or_screen_only` and `measurement_valid` is `false`. **No
calibrated threshold was exceeded.** `quality_passed` folds in a third term -
`not requires_outlier_review` - and that is what fails: two rows sit above the
0.02 review boundary.

| request | step | KL | top-1 equal | top-k overlap | strict margin |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `code-p4096` | 259 | 2.766e-2 | yes | 1.0 | 0.414 |
| `general_ja-p4096` | 1047 | 2.819e-2 | yes | 1.0 | 0.792 |

Both have identical top-1 and full top-k overlap, and the chosen token keeps a
large margin. The KL comes from mass moving within the tail, not from a ranking
change. That reads as benign tail redistribution rather than a ranking hazard -
but the gate requires the review explicitly, `eligible_for_automatic_admission`
stays false, and I am not going to relabel a required review as a pass.

**The blocker label is misleading and worth knowing:** `qualification_blockers`
reports "calibrated quality thresholds" because `quality_passed` folds in the
outlier-review requirement. Read the threshold table, not the blocker name. I
initially read that label as a threshold failure and had to go to the source to
find it was not.

## Why the marginal run was not enough

`staged_f32_over_wmma` already passed (4.818e-4 mean against a production
teacher). That answers "what does adding this cost" but not the question the
envelope asks, which is defined against the exact chain. The tempting shortcut -
adding the marginal cost onto WMMA's own 16-47 drift - is a summed-KL budget,
which this packet has now falsified four separate times (0-7's mean exceeds
0-47's; 8-47's max exceeds 0-47's; 15-47's max exceeds 14-47's and 16-47's;
staged 13-14 is worse than 13-15). So I measured the composition directly.

## Value is still estimated, not measured

**~0.66% of prefill** (3 layers x 8.985 ms per layer-step x 4 chunks of 1024
rows, from the kernel packet), against a ~16,300 ms request. That is an estimate
from a microbenchmark, not an end-to-end measurement on the promoted path, and
it is small enough that it must be measured rather than argued before anything is
promoted.

applicable BF16-relative evidence, exact control/isolation/graph/lifecycle
evidence, task-quality evidence, and candidate route engagement/repair-oracle
coverage. A green numerical gate is one certificate, not the whole envelope.
