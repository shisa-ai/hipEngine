# Gemma 4 / shisa-de-1 format decision and kernel gap audit

## Summary

Reviewed `shisa-ai/shisa-de-1` (full fine-tune of `google/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it`,
`Gemma4ForConditionalGeneration`, 25.2B total / 3.8B active) to decide how to run
Gemma 4 in hipEngine. Decision: **GGUF, quantized, on the Laguna path.** Q8_0
(~26.9 GB) as the primary artifact on the W7900; Q4_K_M (~17 GB) for the 24 GB
7900 XTX and for the quantization-drift study. Safetensors is not a deployment
path — 51.6 GB against 44.98 GiB of VRAM with single-GPU support.

The port is tractable because Laguna S 2.1 already implements the two hardest
pieces: mixed sliding/full attention with two RoPE contracts, and a 128-expert
MoE with an always-on parallel dense branch.
