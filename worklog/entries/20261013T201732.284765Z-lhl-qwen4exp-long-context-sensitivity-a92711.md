# Qwen4Exp long-context gate: sensitivity floor, not route drift

## Summary

Follow-up to
`worklog/entries/20261013T175218.475957Z-lhl-qwen4exp-production-f16-composition-4c11ba.md`.
Clean-tree evidence for the promoted `production` plan, a strict bug found on
the way, and the first teacher-forced gate at long context.

- Clean tree `6d8306d1e`, canonical 12-case protocol, Radeon 8060S / gfx1151,
  `UD-Q4_K_XL`, chunk 4096: prefill 769.2 / 932.9 / 1108.4 tok/s at
  p512/p1024/p4096 (previous plan 305.6 / 331.6 / 335.5), decode 20.91 /
  20.15 / 20.57.
- Default-suite gate on unmodified `production` at `6d8306d1e`: mean/p95/p99/

- `strict` could not prefill more than 256 tokens in one chunk since
  `8377372cf` (2026-10-06) removed a kernel entry point its hyper-connection
  read still resolves. Fixed in `517dcc661`. Consequence: no teacher-forced
  gate before today covered a prefill chunk above 256 rows.
- Long-context gate (four prompts of 1,981-2,572 tokens, 100 rows) at
  `517dcc661`: `production` mean KL 2.10e-3, p95 1.28e-2, max 2.06e-2, top-1
  99/100. The previous plan on the same prompts: 8.30e-4 / 5.17e-3 /
  9.81e-3, 99/100.
- Isolation: each F16 route alone on the previous plan gives mean 1.6e-3 to
  2.3e-3, and a control that only reorders the F32 summation of one
  hyper-connection projection (`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_GR_STAGED=1`) gives
  2.09e-3 with max 4.81e-2.

## Decisions

- **The promotion stands.** At 2,000+ tokens a round-off-class perturbation
  moves the distribution as far from `strict` as the whole F16 composition
  does, and the routes do not add. The long-context result therefore does not
  attribute drift to a route, and reverting the composition would not make
  `production` pass: the previous plan fails p95 on the same prompts and any
  later reassociation would put it at the same 2e-3.
- **Open for the owner:** the envelope in `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md`
  section 6.1 was calibrated on short prompts and is not attainable by any
  non-bit-identical plan at 2,000+ tokens. My recommendation is a
  context-relative criterion for long prompts: a candidate must not exceed a
  declared multiple of a round-off control measured on the same prompts, with
  top-1 and task floors unchanged. Recorded in section 6.1 as an open item,
  not adopted.
- **Sample size caveat.** Four repetitive prompts and 100 correlated rows
  support the paired comparison between arms; they are too few to set a
  numeric limit. A calibrated long-context suite needs more and more varied
  prompts.
