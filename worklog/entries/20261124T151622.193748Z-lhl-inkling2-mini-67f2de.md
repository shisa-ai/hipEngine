# NVFP4 eight-route decode ceiling on gfx1100

## Summary

Added an eight-route NVFP4 selected-projection probe to
`scripts/inkling2_dispatch_probe.py` (the third and last step-0 ceiling item
after launch cost and Q8_0 width, following the pm4/aql transport probe in
20261124T151510.725571Z-lhl-inkling2-mini-3c32ab.md). Shapes are the Inkling 2
Mini expert projections at decode (rows = 8 routes, 128 experts, NVFP4
36-bytes-per-64-values layout, expert-major rows, int32 route ids, fp32 outer
scales, random weights, `hipengine_gguf_nvfp4_selected_f32_f32_out`):

- gate/up (in 2304, out 2304, fused): 23.9 MB of selected-expert bytes in
  ~104 us -> **~229 GB/s**
- down (in 1152, out 2304): 11.9 MB in ~46 us -> **~257 GB/s**
- combined per visit: 35.8 MB in ~151 us -> **~237 GB/s** selected-expert
  read bandwidth for the routed projections

Context: the same host measures ~307 GB/s on the 491 MB Q8_0 vocab-head shape
(L2-exceeding) and ~221-307 GB/s on the Q8_0 dense decode shapes (L2-resident,
not DRAM figures). Q8_0 head at ~307 GB/s is ~53% of the 576 GB/s peak. The
NVFP4 selected-path numbers are per-call including launch overhead; the
weights are 24/12 MB, so some L2 residency is possible across reps — treat as
an upper bound on the cold-cache case and a lower bound on per-call time.

At 48 visits per token, routed-expert weight traffic is ~1.7 GB per decode
token; at ~237 GB/s that is ~7.2 ms of pure expert weight traffic per token
before any attention, dense, or host cost. This is the number that makes the
step-2/4 (device residency, fusion, graph capture) work non-optional on this
part. Random-id routing (experts 0-7 selected, uniform) is least favorable for
L2 reuse; real router outputs may do better or worse.
