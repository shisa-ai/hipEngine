# Correcting the INT8 sampled-MTP clearing condition: route scope, not the declaration

## Summary

Supersedes the clearing condition in
`worklog/entries/20260921T104827.540128Z-lhl-int8-mtp-sampled-route-e75874.md`
(commit `11cd8848e`). That entry measured the refusal correctly but named the
wrong fix: it said to clear it by adding `"sampled"` to the three INT8
declarations' `sampling_modes`. **Do not do that.** The greedy-only declaration
is not an INT8 omission; it is the scope of every declaration in the plugin, and
widening it would open a route whose two named preconditions are unmet.

The measured part of the earlier entry stands and is re-verified below: the
sampled accept is storage-agnostic and never reads KV storage. That is what makes
this a route-scope question rather than a kernel question — and it is also why no
INT8 kernel change can clear it.

## What was wrong

The earlier entry inferred "declaration only" from the fact that the INT8
declarations omit `sampling_modes`. It never checked the BF16 declarations, which
also omit it. Measured across the plugin:

| declaration | storage | `sampling_modes` | automatic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gguf_dense_bf16_gfx1100_c1_native_chain` | bf16 | `('greedy_fast',)` | yes |
| `gguf_dense_bf16_gfx1100_group_native_chain` | bf16 | `('greedy_fast',)` | yes |
| `gguf_dense_bf16_gfx1151_c1_native_chain` | bf16 | `('greedy_fast',)` | yes |
| `gguf_dense_bf16_gfx1151_group_native_chain` | bf16 | `('greedy_fast',)` | yes |
| `gguf_dense_int8_native_chain` | int8_per_token_head | `('greedy_fast',)` | yes |
| `gguf_dense_int8_gfx1151_group_native_chain` | int8_per_token_head | `('greedy_fast',)` | yes |
| `gguf_dense_int8_gfx1100_group_native_chain` | int8_per_token_head | `('greedy_fast',)` | yes |

All seven are greedy-only. The sampled route is opened for gfx1151 BF16 by its
four **evidence** rows (`automatic_native_sampled_c1_c4`) and by nothing else. So
the axis that differs between INT8 and BF16 is evidence coverage on a route with
open preconditions — not storage, and not the declaration's honesty about the
kernels.

## The actual cause, in the plugin's own words

`hipengine/models/qwen35.py` states it where the declarations are built:

> The declared sampling mode is greedy-fast only: the sampled accept route stays
> closed until it can honour the autoregressive finish rule (stop tokens and EOS
> mid-cycle) and a device-side accept removes the eager host-logit restriction;
> the gfx1151 cells that advertise `sampled` are admitted by their own evidence
> rows instead.

Two named preconditions, both concrete:

1. **The autoregressive finish rule.** `docs/REFACTOR.md` "Sampled-route
   finish-rule blockers (open)": the cycle commit ends a row only when its last
   visible token is the row's EOS, and a stochastic accept has no
   `greedy_chain_eos_limit` bound, so a stop token or EOS can land mid-cycle. The
   request-level blocker set (`min_tokens`, `eos_token_id`, `stop_token_ids`,
   `stop_token_sequences`) keeps those shapes off the route, which is containment,
   not a fix.
2. **The eager host-logit restriction.** A sampled row that is not on a device
   accept plan asks the verifier for `return_logits=True`
   (`hipengine/generation/qwen35_gguf_mtp2.py`, `execute_target_frontier`), which
   reads the whole row-major logits matrix back to host so
   `sampled_accept_summary` can process it.

Neither is a KV-storage or kernel-execution gap. That is the useful correction:
the earlier entry's conclusion "a declaration gap, not a capability miss" is true
about the *kernels* and misleading about the *fix*.

## What was verified again

- The sampled accept is storage-agnostic.
  `sampled_accept_summary` (`hipengine/generation/mtp_sampled_accept.py:96`) takes
  the verify batch, the verifier's row-major logits, the request's sampler state,
  its params, and its draws; `sampled_accept_from_distributions`
  (`hipengine/speculative/sampling.py:202`) takes `TargetVerifyBatch` plus two
  `SparseDistribution` sequences per row. Neither reads KV storage.
- The logits the accept needs are already requested on INT8. The sampled route
  sets `return_logits=True` whenever it is not on a device accept plan, and the
  non-native verifier branch fills `prepared.target_logits` from
  `self.target.step(..., return_logits=True)`. Storage does not enter that path.
- The refusal reason is identical on INT8 and on the BF16 *declaration*
  (`mtp_sampling_unsupported`); BF16 differs only by escaping through evidence.

## Next

The sampled route's own preconditions are the work, and they are not INT8 work:
implement the autoregressive finish rule for a stochastic accept, and add a
device-side accept that removes the eager host-logit readback. Only then does the
sampled scope belong in a declaration — for BF16 and INT8 together, so the two
storages never disagree about what the engine implements.
