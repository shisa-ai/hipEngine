# Screen gfx1151 Qwen3.8 Q4T16 prefill column subdivision

## Summary

Rejected a materially distinct 16-column output subdivision of the retained
operation-complete dual-Q4T16 WMMA gate/up+SiLU prefill owner. The candidate is
BF16-bit exact, halves LDS and lowers VGPR 248 -> 224, but raises actual-weight
wall 50.35-63.30% at every required row and wins none of 45 pairs. The added
workgroups and activation transport dominate the modest register reduction.
