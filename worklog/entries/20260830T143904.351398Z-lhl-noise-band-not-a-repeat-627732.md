# The +-2% noise band was never a repeat; cell classifications rest on it

## Summary

The session has repeatedly used a "repeat-noise scale" of about +/-2% to decide whether a
parity cell is
a real loss. That band is not a repeat. The figures behind it - +1.95% at C1 and +1.68% at C2 in
`20260830T141918…` - are **old-vs-recomputed** deltas of admission ms (302.35 -> 308.24 and
584.47 -> 594.30), i.e. the published number against a recomputation of the same packets.
Calling that
a "straight re-measure" is wrong: a re-measure requires running the same protocol twice,
and nothing was
run twice. I compounded the error by citing that band in `20260830T142952…` to excuse a
**2.1% AR rate**
difference, which is a different metric on a different arm.

## Changes

- No code change. Corrects the evidential basis for several parity classifications.

## Finding

- `20260830T141918…` Measured table: C1 302.35 -> 308.24 (+1.95%), C2 584.47 -> 594.30
  (+1.68%). Its own
  prose then says "C1/C2 moved +1.95%/+1.68% on a straight re-measure. That is the repeat-
  noise scale, so
  the +4.9% (C6) and +3.7% (C8) losses are not separable from noise". The two numbers are agreement
  between a superseded artifact and a recomputation, not run-to-run variation. They say the
  recomputation
  left C1/C2 roughly untouched; they say nothing about variance.
- Consequence: the dismissal of the C6 (+4.9%) and C8 (+3.7%) admission losses is
  unsupported. Those may
  still be noise, but the evidence does not show it.
- My own use in `20260830T142952…` is worse: it applied an admission-metric agreement figure
  to an AR tok/s
  spread (`AR C8 78.27 vs 79.96 = 2.1%`). Cross-metric borrowing, on a band that was never a repeat.
- Attempt to repair from disk failed, and the failure is itself informative. `he-d1` and
  `he-d24` are the
  only parity packets left, and that entry describes them correctly as "he-d24 at 24 tokens,
  he-d1 at 1
  token". AR rates in `he-d1` are 3.24 / 3.37 / 4.99 / 6.57 / 7.69 / 7.78 / 8.50 / 9.11
  tok/s against
  `he-d24`'s 22.20 / 32.71 / 44.33 / 58.42 / 62.85 / 71.72 / 77.25 / 78.27: a one-token arm measures
  prefill and admission, not decode. So the pair cannot serve as an AR repeat in either
  direction, and my
  first pass at this analysis wrongly compared them and produced +584% "deltas" before the
  raw numbers
  exposed the assumption.
- No retained artifact carries a repeat: `20260830-w7900-q4km-c1c8-submodule-
  decomposition.json` holds one
  measured AR/MTP series and `…-admission-decomposition-post-grouping.json` holds measured
  hipEngine AR
  plus carried-forward peer AR. Neither contains two same-protocol runs, so no "within noise"
  classification made today can be re-derived from the repository.
