# Attribute Laurent prefill operation differential

## Summary

Compared current hipEngine and pinned Laurent Vulkan on the canonical C2/C8
one-output prefill shapes. Laurent is 1.01x faster in complete wall but
1.18x/1.25x faster in summed GPU-node time. Quantized Q4/Q5/Q6 work explains
the GPU difference: hipEngine/Laurent quant time is 1.28x at C2 and 1.31x at
C8.

Laurent does not win by reducing dispatch count. Its timestamp-query logger
records 3,588 node dispatches at each width versus 1,955 classified hipEngine
dispatches. It chunks rows72 as 64+8 and rows288 as 256+32. Its final Q6 head
runs once over n=2/n=8 at 4.57/5.63 ms.

Pinned source `ggml/src/ggml-vulkan/ggml-vulkan.cpp:4002-4019,9647-9686` at
`c28d538df` identifies the strongest mechanism: quantized dense `MUL_MAT`
defaults to an F16 activation-B operand, halving B bytes and `buf_b` shared
memory while preserving the existing F16 staging boundary. This is fused
activation/dequant dataflow, not quantized-weight reuse.

`GGML_VK_PIPELINE_STATS` measures the large/small Q4 F16-B pipelines at
VGPR120/108, LDS20/5 KiB, scratch0, and no spills. Exact resident-wave
occupancy and video-memory weight-fetch bytes remain unavailable: two
`rocprofv3` attempts emitted no RADV kernel/copy CSV. The artifact records
that instrumentation bound rather than inferring values.
