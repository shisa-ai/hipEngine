# Parity P2.3: low-VGPR Q6-qmicro-planar prefill bands retained; Q5 A/B recorded

## Summary

Fifth retained kernel unit of the external-parity campaign, extending the
proven low-VGPR treatment to the Q6-qmicro-planar family (103.1 ms/pass at
184 VGPR) and recording the Q5 dense A/B for the next unit.

Changes: both kernels gained ROW_TILES/OUT_TILES template parameters
(default template arguments preserve the existing geometry and arithmetic)
plus `<2,1>`/`<3,1>`
low-VGPR instantiations (one 16-column out tile per 32-thread block,
16/24-float accumulators). The gfx1151 Q6-qmicro-planar selector now routes
rows 17-32 to lowvgpr on the six physical shapes, rows 33-48 to lowvgpr48 on
(5120,17408) and lowvgpr elsewhere in the six shapes, with plain/`>=512
shared4` behavior unchanged.

Measured A/B (synthetic Q5T16/Q6T16-qmicro-planar weights, warmup 4 + 20
iters, all bit-exact vs the plain owners, max_abs 0.0):

- Q6-qmicro rows 45: lowvgpr/lowvgpr48 win on all six shapes by -23% to -50%
  (e.g. 17408->5120 2.373->1.182 ms, 6144->5120 0.714->0.357 ms;
  5120->17408 takes lowvgpr48 1.199->0.867 ms).
- Q5 rows 45: lowvgpr wins on five of six shapes (-4% to -34%); plain keeps
  (5120,17408) (0.950 vs 0.995/1.068 ms). Q5 dispatch wiring deferred: its
  dense path resolves through the compact-alias registration that also
  serves qualified Q5 verifier rowtile cells, so the override needs its own
  gated unit.

Real-model (physical gfx1151, standard Q4_K_M parity model):

- Isolated forced-bulk 45-token prefill: **0.331 -> 0.290 s (-12.4%)**,
  n=3 (0.290/0.290/0.289); -43.7% vs the campaign-start 0.515 s.
- Cached real-model `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace`: measured-pass Q6 planar
  family 103.1 -> 57.7 ms. Across the warmup + measured trace, `<2,1>` ran
  64 times at 88 VGPR and the plain `<4,3>` owner ran 16 times at 184 VGPR.
  A cached synthetic trace separately exercised both new instantiations:
  `<2,1>` and `<3,1>` each launched 8 times at 88 VGPR, with plausible
  9.3/13.5 us median duration. A corresponding pinned-cache Q5 trace ran
  `<2,1>` and `<3,1>` 8 times each at 96/112 VGPR with 10.5/13.6 us medians.
- Server C1/C2 frozen-protocol re-measure follows commit.

Routing test note: RED-first was impractical for the Q6 band test (the
wrappers did not exist before this unit); the measurement-first A/B with
bit-exact cross-validation is the selection evidence, and the post-hoc
routing test guards the bands.
