# GR up wide-tile route: 4.38x projection screen, cliff screen passes

## Summary

The fused GR up+sigmoid+gated-mean kernel is the largest single kernel in the
code-p4096 prefill (2589.5 ms of 17114 ms, 15.1%) and runs at 1.03 TFLOP/s =
3.9% of the gfx1151 FP32 peak. Measured against the promoted dense wide tile on
real layer weights at the production chunk shape, the limiter is load issue on
the K=320 weight stream: the same weights through a 256-column tile reach 4.50
TFLOP/s, a 4.38x projection speedup worth 1937 ms of the prefill (plus 579 ms
from the GR-down leg, 14.7% total). Fusion is already earning its keep but only
1.21x over the strict chain (6.538 against 7.899 ms).

The wide arms are not bit-identical (relative L2 4.7e-05 up, 2.9e-04 down)
against the 1.1e-07 - 3.7e-07 that the rejected GR-up iu8 route measured, and
the GR gate is a cliff at a ~5e-9 prefill-boundary logits KL. A default-off
`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_GR_WIDE` route was added and screened: 5.17e-11
(code-p512) and 3.40e-10 (general_en-p512), 8.5-85x below the passing bracket,
deterministic in both arms. The screen is not a qualification - the publication
row has a ~22-logit argmax gap, so KL is insensitive to the logits that did
move (median absolute drift 0.095, max 1.20). The 780-row production gate is
the binding test.

## Changes

  `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_GR_WIDE` branch in the GR-up read. Dispatches the
  promoted dense tile `gguf_q8_0_dense_wide256_f32_f32_out` plus the retained
  exact `qwen4_exp_sigmoid_f32` / `qwen4_exp_gated_mean_f32` epilogue. Guarded
  by `rows > 256` and `low_rank % 64 == 0` (the kernel's `K_TILE`), so the
  strict and disabled paths are untouched.

  replacing the positional flag ternary with a `ROUTES` map.

  `coltile2_branch4_rowbatch4_f32_exact`), and `GR_WIDE` added to
  `CONSERVATIVE_ARITHMETIC_FLAGS`.
