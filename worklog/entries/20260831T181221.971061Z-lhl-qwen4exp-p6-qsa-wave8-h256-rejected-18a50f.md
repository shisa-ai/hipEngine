# Reject Qwen4Exp H256 wave8 sparse attention

## Summary

Rejected and removed the default-off T1 H256 wave8 sparse-attention candidate.
Its speedup is real, deterministic, and large, but the complete p4096 numerical
and generated-task gate fails. Strict H256 sparse attention remains default.

## Changes

- Removed the HIP/Python wrapper, registry key, runtime selector, focused
  candidate tests, dedicated gate, and refactor-ledger flag.
- Added compact rejection artifact
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-31-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-p6-qsa-wave8-h256-rejected.json`.
- Updated the campaign and changelog; no manifest/default changed.

## Validation

Performance evidence:

- actual 12Q/2KV/H256/2,048-selected primitive: **2.179→0.481 ms (4.534x)**;
- live 2,052 exact-context wall: **95.880→67.837 ms (1.413x)**;
- live 4,097: **96.016→67.611 ms (1.420x)**;
- candidate repeats and request-state hashes are exact; lifecycle closes at zero.

Binding correctness rejection over four canonical p4096 categories, 24 forced
steps plus prefill row, three candidate repeats, and 32-token free generation:

- mean/p95/p99/max KL: **0.000809/0.003600/0.008535/0.010108**;
- top-1: **98/100**, below the 99% gate;
- category failures: general English, general Japanese, mixed Japanese/English;
- free-generation tasks: **2/4 exact** (English and mixed diverge);
- repeat determinism and lifecycle pass.

The candidate splits selected tokens into eight interleaved wave subsequences
and merges partial online-softmax states. That reassociation is too large at
model depth despite a primitive max absolute delta of only 1.63e-8.

Post-removal focused QSA sparse/registry/runner bundle passes 19/19.

## Next

Do not retry token-subsequence softmax splitting under another tile constant.
A new H256 mechanism must preserve global selected-token online-softmax order,
or independently improve the full p4096 category/task gate. Continue P6 with
operation-complete dense/MoE owners while investigating an order-preserving
attention pipeline.
