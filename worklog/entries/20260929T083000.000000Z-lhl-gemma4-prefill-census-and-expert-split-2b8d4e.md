# the dense GEMM is the biggest single prefill term, not the experts; and attention is the only superlinear one

## Summary

Three measurements, same session, pp512, against a 0.657 s baseline.

**The census** (`gemma4_prefill_kernel_census.py`) resolves the step to 97.9 percent and its
expert decomposition is exact -- `153.82 + 114.85 + 30.32 = 298.99`:

| kernel | n | ms | share |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `gemma4_experts_forward_bf16` | 30 | 298.99 | 44.7% |
| -- `gemma4_project_experts_gate_up_mmq` | 30 | 153.82 | 23.0% |
| -- `gemma4_project_experts_down_mmq` | 30 | 114.85 | 17.2% |
| -- expert-block glue (block minus leaves) | 30 | 30.32 | 4.5% |
| **`launch_gguf_linear`** | **206** | **189.31** | **28.3%** |
| `gemma4_attention_prefill_bf16` | 30 | 130.82 | 19.6% |
| `gemma4_router_topk_bf16` | 30 | 17.45 | 2.6% |
| norms, rotary, adds | 330 | 16.5 | 2.5% |
| host and unmeasured | -- | 14.1 | 2.1% |

**The dense GEMM is the largest single term at 189.31 ms**, not the experts. The expert block is
larger only as a sum of three things, and its two projections are individually smaller than the
dense term.

**The route ablation** (`gemma4_prefill_route_ablation.py`, repaired in `20260929T080000`) agrees
and adds the arms the census cannot express:

| arm | pp512 | pp1024 | ratio |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| baseline | 0.657 s | 1.578 s | 2.40 |
| `no_experts` | 0.302 | 0.603 | **2.00** |
| -- `no_experts_gate_up` | 0.169 | 0.332 | 1.96 |
| -- `no_experts_down` | 0.098 | 0.191 | 1.95 |
| `no_dense_q8` | 0.176 | 0.363 | 2.06 |
| `no_attention` | 0.118 | **0.499** | **4.23** |
| `no_router` | 0.012 | 0.020 | 1.67 |
| `no_moe_elementwise` | 0.014 | 0.032 | 2.29 |
| `no_norm_rope` | 0.005 | 0.010 | 2.00 |

**Attention is the only superlinear term: 4.23x for 2x the tokens.** Everything else scales at or
below 2.06, so the prefill's falling tok/s with length is entirely attention's growing share.
This confirms `20260928T025924`'s conclusion from the other direction -- it measured the
attention *kernel* rate as flat and attributed the falling prefill to attention's share, and this
is the in-situ share.

## What the expert scaling does not settle

The expert block scales at exactly 2.00, which is consistent with **two** different structures:
no reuse at all, or a 32-row tile whose rows-per-expert doubles when the token count does. The
14.4 GB figure the split probe quotes is `pairs x bytes-per-expert`, an arithmetic identity
rather than a measurement, and it is not evidence for the first reading.

It matters, because the two imply opposite levers. It is not settled here and should not be
assumed either way.

## Retractions and supersessions

- **`20260929T040000`'s "the gap to llama.cpp is 122.8 ms"** was measured against a 0.686 s
  prefill and priced from the broken ablation. The current gap is 0.657 against 0.563, **94 ms**.
- **`20260929T010000`'s "the expert MMQ kernels are DRAM-bound"** and its 25 percent line
  efficiency: retracted by `20260928T130000`'s successor entries and not revived here. The
  census says the two expert projections are 268.7 ms of kernel time; what limits them is still
  open, and `20260928T114215`'s stall-bound finding is the most recent evidence.
- **`20260928T114215`'s 8 percent session-to-session drift** applies to every number above.
  All three measurements here are single-session, so the ratios are sound and the absolute
  milliseconds are not comparable across sessions.
