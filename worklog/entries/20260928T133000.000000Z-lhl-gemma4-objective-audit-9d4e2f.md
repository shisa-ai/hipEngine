## A claim retracted before it was committed

Reading the census refresh (`20260928T115240`) against the isolated attention bench,
the census's attention term looked 2.2x the kernel's own time at 512 tokens --
173.97 against 80.3 ms -- which would have put 93.7 ms, 13.7 percent of the step,
inside the attention route but outside the kernel. **The 80.3 ms was stale and the
comparison was invalid.** The bench at 512 tokens, run for this audit:

```
swa  256d 16q/8kv window=1024    3.56 ms/launch    89.1 ms all layers
full 512d 16q/2kv window=0       6.93 ms/launch    34.6 ms all layers
the model's 30 attention layers: 123.7 ms
```

**123.7 ms, not 80.3.** At 2048 the same bench gives 1807.9 ms against the census's
1993.9 -- within 10 percent -- so the census's attention term is 1.1x to 1.4x the
isolated kernel at both lengths. That is ordinary in-situ overhead: the KV write, the
mask, and the launch are in the wrapper's interval and not in the kernel's. There is
no missing 93.7 ms and the "the attention route is 2.2x its kernel" finding does not
exist. It is recorded here because it was one command away from being written up as a
result, and the command that refuted it is the one in this entry.

The general shape is the fifth measurement error this branch has produced, and the
first caught before a commit rather than after. The four that got through were three
phase-mixing errors and one extraction-boundary error, all found by tooling built to
find them rather than by reading the numbers.

## Where the prefill gap actually is

At 512 tokens the census's three terms are close and none dominates:

| term | ms | share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| routed experts | 320.25 | 44.0% |
| dense projections and lm head | 184.85 | 25.4% |
| prefill attention | 173.97 | 23.9% |

Against llama.cpp's 563 ms at that length the gap is 122.8 ms, so no single term
accounts for it. At 2048 tokens attention becomes the largest single term (1993.9 of
5186.8 ms, 38.0%) and the deficit is 2.17x, but even removing attention entirely
would leave 2.358 s against llama.cpp's 1.919 -- still 1.23x short. **The gap is
distributed, and the "attention is the whole gap" reading in `20260928T093930` is
stale: it was written when the step was 584 tok/s and attention's share was larger.**
