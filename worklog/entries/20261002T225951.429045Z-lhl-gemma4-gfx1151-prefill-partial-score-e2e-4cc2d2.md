## Why this is the check that matters

The kernel probe says 1.20x at 262,144 keys; the public surface says 1.133x. The
gap is the non-attention part of a prefill, which this change does not touch, and
it is the expected direction -- the attention walk is ~46% of a long prefill, so a
1.20x on that share lands near 1.09-1.13x end to end. The two numbers are
consistent rather than one confirming the other.

It also confirms the intended path ran: the campaign harness drives the same
`Gemma4Runner` methods in the same order as `Gemma4GGUFGenerator.generate_detailed`
and reports `path parity=True`, so the WMMA full-layer kernel is what served the
262,144-token prefill and not a fallback.
