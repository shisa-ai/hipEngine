# Gemma 4: the Q5_1 expert down route's tile space is closed by measurement

## Summary

The Q5_1 expert down projection is 20% of Gemma 4's 512-token prefill (610-652 ms
of 3119 ms) and it has **no remaining bit-exact lever that measurement
supports**. Six tile shapes and one shared-weight-staging rewrite were built and
measured at the route's real geometry. Every one is bit-identical to the
incumbent and every one is slower, so the space is closed rather than untested.

This is a negative result that cost one iteration and saves the next one from
re-deriving it. The route's own accepted row already chose the staged variant
over the compact one (1.91x); this entry establishes that the staged variant's
tile shape is also optimal, and that the two traffic hypotheses which explain
the route's 5%-of-peak FLOP rate are both wrong.

## Changes

- No production change. The experimental kernel instantiations and the
  shared-weight rewrite were measured and then reverted; they are slower and
  nothing selects them, so landing them would be dead dispatch routes.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-27-gemma4-q5_1-staged-tile-space-rejected.json`
  records the geometry, every variant, and the refuted hypotheses.
- The campaign document's Q5_1 section now carries the closed space and the one
  surviving faster shape.

## Validation

- **Geometry taken from the route, not assumed.** A live 512-token forward with
  the registered kernel wrapped recorded: in 704, out 2816, 128 experts, 4096
  compact rows, row_bytes 528, 190.3 MB of weights, grid 704 x 128, and 1069
  passes (`sum ceil(rows / 4)`). An earlier probe used in 2816 / out 1408 - this
  projection transposed - and was 2x off production; the live number is what the
  route is asked for.
- **Bit-exactness for every variant.** 0 of 11,534,336 bf16 outputs differ from
  the incumbent in each of the seven new variants, at a skewed expert
  distribution with 7 empty experts and a 183-row maximum so partial last
  batches are exercised. `compact_rowbatch8_out8` is the one exception
  (1,306,624 differ) and is 1.83x slower, which confirms the recorded claim that
  the staged variant's contraction is the incumbent's.
- **The incumbent reproduces production.** 21.03 ms per layer against the route
  attribution's 22.5 ms per layer (652 ms over 29 layers).
- **Tests.** No production code changed, so the narrowest relevant tier is the
  gemma4 bundle plus the route file: 508 + 46 pass.
