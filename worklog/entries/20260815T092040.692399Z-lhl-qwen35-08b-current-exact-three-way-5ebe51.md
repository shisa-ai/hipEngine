# Publish synchronized 0.8B HIP Vulkan comparison

## Summary

The synchronized current-HEAD comparison answers the open question with a mixed
result: llama.cpp Vulkan still leads **core** pp512/tg128, but hipEngine now
beats llama.cpp HIP decode for both quants, nearly matches Vulkan Q4 public
decode, and beats Vulkan Q8 public decode.

Six serial counter-rotated blocks measure hipEngine / llama HIP / Vulkan core at
Q4 **4354.16/144.18 / 4876.15/119.47 / 5326.06/193.88 tok/s** and Q8
**5002.83/140.59 / 4667.43/109.48 / 5684.88/159.54**. Public decode is
hipEngine **123.30/117.04**, llama HIP **89.22/83.96**, and Vulkan
**126.31/111.81** Q4/Q8. Thus hipEngine is **0.976x/1.047x Vulkan public
decode** and wins every Q8 public-decode block.
