# W7900: AR C2 decomposed - half prefill fixed cost, half un-admitted c-aware projection

## Summary

AR C2 was the last scoreboard deficit with no explanation, because prefill cannot account for it (C3
is 1.47x on the same prefill cost). It decomposes, using only committed numbers plus one measured
prefill wall: roughly **4.8% prefill fixed cost and 5.1% decode amortization**, which predicts
1.504 s vs 1.355 s = 0.90x - the published deficit. Artifact
`2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-decode-amortization-c2.json`.

## The measurement

Per-token decode cost from the published rate rows, `wall = width*24/rate` and
`decode = (wall - 295 ms)/(width*24)` where 295 ms is the median-of-3 measured wall of one ~42-token
prefill:

| width | decode ms/token | weight-bound ideal | ratio | hipEngine/llama |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 33.2 | 33.2 | 1.00x | 1.01x |
| 2 | 25.2 | 16.6 | **1.52x** | 0.90x |
| 3 | 18.0 | 11.1 | 1.62x | 1.47x |
| 8 | 11.2 | 4.2 | **2.69x** | 1.34x |

C1 landing on the weight-read ideal (16 GB at ~460 GB/s) is a coincidence check on the 295 ms, not a
fit - it was measured independently. The finding is that decode cost per token falls **sub-linearly**
with width: weights are not amortised across rows. We still win from C3 up because llama.cpp is worse
there; C2 is the width where that trade stops paying.

## Mechanism, observed rather than inferred

`throughput_claim_eligible` is **false in 80/80 AR cells** of `/tmp/he-c1c8-cap8`, the packet behind
the AR row promoted today, while `native_caware_decode` is true in all 70 width>1 cells (false in the
ten C1 cells, the expected shape). In `hipengine/dispatch/projection.py` that flag is true only on
path `benchmark_accepted_caware_projection`; rows==1 takes `row_gemv_c1`, and rows>1 falls back to
`row_gemv_until_caware_benchmark` when no benchmark-accepted c-aware projection candidate applies.
The only accepted-evidence artifacts in the tree are the July PARO safetensors runs - nothing admits a
c-aware projection for the GGUF Q4_K_M path on gfx1100. Same shape as the automatic-MTP finding: a
route that exists, runs a slower default, and is waiting on benchmark evidence rather than code.

## Limits on the claim

The flag does not invalidate the promoted AR row - those walls reproduce to 0.13% across independent
runs - it says the row understates achievable decode rather than overstating it. That admitting a
c-aware candidate would close the ratio column is **not measured**: candidates are supplied by the
caller and were not exercised. The 1.52x-2.69x column is the bound on the prize for decode, not a
forecast.
