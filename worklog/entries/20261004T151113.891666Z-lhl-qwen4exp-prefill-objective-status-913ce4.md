# The prefill objective is blocked on a campaign-level scope-and-threshold decision, not on missing kernel work

## Summary

This session set out to close the prefill gap against the comparator (measured 302.81 tok/s
at `code-p4096` against 421.87). It did not close it, and this entry states the verified
reason rather than another hypothesis: **every remaining prefill-speed lever in the
campaign's own 71-row census is either closed, gated on the production numerical envelope,
or — for the one row that names the blocking mechanism — explicitly a scope-and-threshold
decision that the ledger itself says is "not another kernel or gate experiment".**

The session produced one retained engine change (+8.43% prefill, the chunk capacity guard,
commit `ee19f3321`), one new measurement (the prefill stall census), and **four refuted
hypotheses**, each refuted by evidence already in the tree. That ratio is the finding: the
prefill's remaining headroom is not undiscovered, it is blocked.

## Evidence chain

**1. Where the prefill's time actually is** (census, `20261004T150839`, prefill-only
`rocprofv3`, 5,840 kernels, 27.102 s device time):

| family | share | stall character |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| HC-1 `gr_up_sigmoid_mean` | 18.26% | **30.82% LDS wait** — the only materially LDS-stalled kernel |
| exact chain `coltile_rowbatch_pipelined` | 16.57% | 0.03% LDS wait, 3.76 inst/CU-cycle — at the issue ceiling |
| routed MoE gate/up `wmma_iu8` | 16.38% | 0.15% LDS wait, 2.67 inst/CU-cycle |
| routed MoE down `wmma_iu8<16>` | 8.92% | 0.10% LDS wait, 2.75 inst/CU-cycle |
| GDN prefill (register route) | 5.93% | register-bound at 256 VGPR; already priced slower |
| router logits | 4.55% | 60.1 waves resident, still 1.30% VALU issue — latency-bound, unattributed |
| qsa sparse attention | 3.31% | 4.03 inst/CU-cycle — at the issue ceiling |

The two largest kernels have **opposite** stall profiles, which refutes the §1.2.1 reading
that the gap is one uniform per-kernel throughput deficit.

**2. Each candidate lever, and why it is not available:**

| lever | share at stake | status |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| HC-1 LDS staging | 18.26% | **blocked on mean KL.** Its staging screen reached 2.388x, but the route fails the production envelope. Width direction closed by measurement (72 VGPR / 28 waves per CU; 130 VGPR crosses the 128 granule) |
| dense-wide scope extension | large | **blocked on the envelope.** 13-47 is the widest scope that passes; 11-47 and 12-47 fail on different grounds. The passing scope is already promoted |
| GDN register->LDS state | 5.93% | **closed.** Already recorded in the kernel comment (`qwen4_exp_gdn.hip:1110-1117`, "confirmed by hardware counter"), already built (GD-4), already priced slower: 0.8727x at 1024 rows, 0.7922x at 2048. The state is 65,536 B = the entire per-block LDS budget |
| MoE operand-class / compact owners (MX-1) | 16.38% | **closed today.** All three alternatives are 8/8 cases slower — median +138.5% and +157.8% (`benchmarks/results/2026-10-04-qwen4exp-moe-f16-compact-owner-ab/gate-up-3arm.json`) |
| repair-pass fixed cost | 3.88% | **refuted.** A two-point fit put F = 2794.1 ms at 4.30% of prefill; the committed artifact measures the true fixed cost at ~1% of the pass, which runs at 96.6% of the DRAM roof. The kernel comment already said "invariant in grid size... bandwidth-bound rather than latency-bound" |
| router latency | 4.55% | **unattributed, not unblocked.** It needs a VMEM wait counter this `rocprofv3` build does not expose |
| exact chain, qsa attention | 19.88% | **not levers.** 3.76 and 4.03 inst/CU-cycle against a ~4/cycle ceiling |

**3. The campaign's own audit agrees.** Ledger §11 classifies all 71 rows: 18 actionable,
15 gated, 37 closed, 1 not-a-candidate. Of the 18 actionable, the prefill-relevant ones are
`DP-2` ("OPEN, low value") and `QA-4` ("demoted — long-context lane, 3.02% ceiling at 64K");
the rest are qualification (X-1, X-2, X-9, X-10, X-11, ED-3), serving lanes (RT-5, RT-6,
DE-9, DE-10), or MTP (MT-8, MT-1).

**4. And the row that names the blocking mechanism says so itself.** `X-7`'s state cell
reads: *"decide whether a route whose per-logit perturbation is ~0.4 can meet a 1e-3
mean-KL limit on a 4-row `prefill_last` scope at every prompt, and set the scope-and-
threshold policy that answers it — **a scope-and-threshold question for the qualification
packet, not another kernel or gate experiment.**"*

That is the same question blocking HC-1, the dense-wide scope, and the multi-column GDN
route. It is one decision, not three.

## Changes

The retained engine change from this session, for the record: the prefill chunk capacity
guard (commit `ee19f3321`) — production takes the largest chunk whose admission still
yields the requested context, falling back 4096 -> 2048 -> 1024 on the residency plan
before any weight is materialized. At `code-p4096`, 6 samples per arm interleaved:
**279.28 -> 302.81 tok/s prefill (+8.43%)** and **18.127 -> 18.431 decode (+1.68%)**, with
token ids bit-identical across strict and production at both chunk sizes, the layer2 gate
reproducing the chunk-1024 numbers exactly, and max context unchanged at 262144.

Four hypotheses were refuted and recorded rather than quietly dropped: the repair-pass
fixed cost, the GDN register->LDS lever, the uniform-stall reading, and (from the session's
earlier work) the dense-wide scope's novelty claim.

## Validation

The census is a real capture under the HARNESSES.md recipe with a stated limit
(`SQ_WAIT_INST_ANY` is silently dropped by this build, so the non-LDS stall is
unattributable). The lever statuses are read from committed artifacts and source, not
recomputed. The retained engine change carries its full gate. Nothing in this entry claims
a speedup that was not measured.

## Next

**The prefill objective cannot be advanced further by kernel work until one decision is
made**, and it is the user's to make: whether a route whose per-logit perturbation is ~0.4
can meet the 1e-3 mean-KL limit on the `prefill_last` scope. If yes, HC-1's staging screen
(2.388x on an 18.26% family) and the dense-wide scope extension both re-open, and together
they are the largest named headroom in the campaign. If no, the prefill is at its
production-envelope floor and the objective should be restated.

The only unblocked, unattributed item is the router's memory latency (4.55%), and
attributing it needs a VMEM wait counter this `rocprofv3` build does not expose — so even
that requires a tooling change before it can be a unit.

One model, one quant, one host (gfx1151, not the gfx1100 default hardware).
