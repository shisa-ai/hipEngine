# TP2 f32 down partials: implemented, verified, rejected as an envelope fix

## Summary

Answered the arithmetic question the layer-0 boundary work left open
(`5a0b1d571`) by building the missing half of it: the rank-parallel down
projection can now write its **unrounded f32 accumulator** for both down quant
families, selected per session. Measured end to end against the optimized
resident TP1 teacher, the f32 partial is **worse**, not better, so the shipped
bf16 partial boundary stays.

The blocker was never the Q6_K side. 32 of the model's 64 `ffn_down` tensors are
**Q4_K** and only 32 are Q6_K, and the group carries a single staging dtype, so
the Q4_K f32 consumer is what the route needed. It now exists
(`q4_k_t16` `dense_single_local32_bf16_f32_out`, the same local32 owner with a
wider store; the Q6_K `t16_gemv_decode_bf16_f32_out` leaf was already
registered). `MlpTP2GenerationSession(decode_partial_dtype="f32")` drives it,
`reduce_mode="host"` is enforced (the compiled device-exchange driver stages
bf16 rows and would read an f32 row at half width), and the two T16 dispatch
rewrites that could silently hand an f32 request a bf16 store are now
dtype-guarded (a rows>1 f32 request has no `hip_gfx1100` leaf, so it cannot
reach a bf16 GPU store; note the resolver's last-resort candidate is the generic
CPU reference, so this rests on that absence plus the guard, not on a
`MissingKernelError`).

**Result.** The f32 partial is arithmetically exact — on layer 0 each rank's
partial matches an independent f64 oracle to max_abs **5.0e-08** / **7.7e-08**
and the two-rank f32 sum matches the resident TP1 f32 down output to
**1.9e-09** — and the transport control is exact (bf16 partials through the
device spin-sum and through the host staged sum produce **byte-identical**
logits, in separate processes; the f32 arm is also byte-identical across
processes). End to end, on the same 2 prompts × 128 forced-decode rows the
shipped route uses, agreement with the resident TP1 teacher gets **worse**: max
KL **0.108406 → 0.732890** (`mixed_ja_en_translate`) and **0.046893 → 0.242473**
(`heldout_mixed_summary`), mean KL **0.0014286 → 0.0084696** / **0.0010041 →
0.0028254**, top-1 **1.000 → 0.9766** / **0.9844 → 0.9766**, 3 flipped rows on
each.

Widening the measurement to a 2×2 (prefill route × partial dtype) shows the
partial dtype is not the only route perturbation that moves this metric, and
that the four arms do **not** order by distance from the teacher: the shipped
serial+bf16 schedule is best on every reported metric (and sits at the envelope
edge rather than far outside it — heldout mean KL 0.0010041 vs the 0.001 bar,
p99 0.0288 vs 0.02, max 0.0469 inside the 0.05 ceiling), bulk prefill makes it
worse (mean KL 0.002407 / 0.002558), and serial+f32 is worst on `mixed` (max
0.7329) while bulk+f32 is best of the two f32 arms there. p95 barely moves
across all four arms (5.7e-04 … 1.02e-03) while mean/p99/max move by 2-6x. Read
with the exact layer-0 boundary and the exact within-route repeatability, that
is bf16-ULP-scale chaos amplified over 64 layers: each route perturbation moves
the tail by more than the envelope width, so no single arithmetic term decides
this comparison.

The comparison basis is the remaining problem, and it is not arithmetic. The
reference is **hipEngine TP1's own logits**
(`quality-tp1-d0.json`, arm `tp1-d0`), while hipEngine's production quality basis
is the independent llama.cpp BF16 teacher protocol
(`scripts/qwen38_llama_teacher.py`). A route-parity metric between two
legitimately different arithmetic routes cannot separate a TP2 defect from
legitimate route difference, which is what the layer-0 1.9e-09 measurement
demonstrates.

`decode_partial_dtype="bf16"` therefore stays the shipped schedule. The f32
option stays implemented, registered, documented and fail-closed as a qualified
alternative and bisection control, with the device-side f32 driver recorded as
the follow-up in `docs/REFACTOR.md`. The next decision is the qualification
basis, not another kernel.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_t16_selected_gemv.hip`: the Q4_K
  local32 dense owner is templated on the output scalar (`out_t`, default
  `scalar_t`) and stores through `float_to_scalar<out_t>`; new exported entry
  point `hipengine_gguf_q4_k_t16_dense_single_local32_gemv_bf16_f32_out`
  instantiating `launch_q4_dense_single_local32<uint16_t, float>`. Geometry, FMA
  sequence and the wave32 shuffle tree are untouched — only the store widens.
- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_t16_selected_gemv.py`: the f32
  wrapper (same guards as the bf16 sibling), registration under
  `("hip_gfx1100", "linear", "gguf_q4_k_t16_v1",
  "dense_single_local32_bf16_f32_out")`, and the `__all__` entry.
- `hipengine/loading/qwen35_gguf_consumer_surface.py`: the Q4_K T16 rows>1
  rewrite is now dtype-preserving (`t16_wmma_prefill_{activation}_{output}_out`
  from the row's own pair instead of a hardcoded `..._bf16_bf16_out`), plus the
  new `(gguf_q4_k_t16_v1, bf16, f32)` surface row. The rows>1 f32 leaf is
  deliberately **unregistered**, so a multirow f32 request fails at resolution.
- `hipengine/distributed/tp2_generate.py`: `decode_partial_dtype` ("bf16"
  default) plumbed to the decode/serial-prefill group only (the bulk group keeps
  bf16), validated, and rejected when combined with `reduce_mode="device"`.
- `hipengine/runtime/gguf_linear.py`: `_q4_t16_dense_native_dispatch` (rows 2-8)
  and `_t16_c1_variant_dispatch` (rows 1) take `output_dtype` and return the
  dispatch unchanged for a non-bf16 output. Both return bf16 leaves from
  shape/row policy alone; the f32 row was safe only by accident (the Q4_K c1
  policy map is empty and the native-batch-decode session is off).
- `scripts/tp2_bulk_prefill_diagnostic.py`: `--decode-partial-dtype` /
  `--reduce-mode`, the route record for both, and a `gguf_linear.resolve`
  wrapper that records every selected leaf (`resolve_log` in the JSON) — the
  surface row names the rows=1 owner, so the log is what proves which kernel the
  route actually launched.
- `scripts/tp2_mlp_slice_e2e.py`: `--down-output-dtype {f32,bf16}` so the layer-0
  f64-oracle check can be pointed at either partial dtype. Both exchange paths
  (`staged_exchange_reduce`, `measure_tp2_segment`) and the fused-candidate probe
  now size their staging and their read-back by that dtype instead of assuming
  f32 rows, so the two arms run the same script on the same input.
- Tests: `test_q4_t16_dense_single_f32_out_is_the_same_kernel_with_a_wider_store`
  (GPU, `bf16_round(f32_out) == bf16_out` bit-for-bit plus "the f32 store is at
  least as close to the f32 reference"), registry resolution for both new leaves
  in `test_p9_h3d_registry_keys_resolve`, the dtype-preserving rewrite test in
  `test_unit_gguf_linear_dispatch.py`,
  `test_q4_t16_f32_output_never_acquires_a_bf16_rowtile_leaf`, the session
  knob/guard tests in `test_unit_distributed_tp2_generate.py`, and the updated
  surface-parity / row-count expectations.
- Docs/artifacts: `benchmarks/results/2026-09-17-w7900-tp2-f32-down-partial-ab.json`,
  `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`, `docs/QWEN38-27B-GFX1100-TP2.md` (P2 status),
  `docs/REFACTOR.md` (the knob, the f32 device driver follow-up, and the

## Next

- **Decide the TP2 qualification basis before any more arithmetic work.** Three
  options: (a) score TP2 against the independent llama.cpp BF16 teacher capture
  on the mtp-bench protocol the TP1 production profile already uses
  (`scripts/qwen38_llama_teacher.py`, `TEACHER_STEPS=9`) and gate TP2's own
  oracle-KL against the same envelope; (b) declare a TP2-specific envelope from
  TP2's own repeat/isolation distribution; (c) close the route difference by
  moving the sharded leaves into the resident leaf family. (a) is the principled
  option and reuses existing infrastructure; it is also the only one that can
  distinguish "TP2 is wrong" from "TP2 is a different route".
- Do not promote the f32 partial as a numerical fix, and do not relax the
  envelope to accommodate the current comparison.
- If the f32 partial is ever wanted for the graphed device reduction, add the
  staged-dtype template to `device_exchange_host.cpp` (`row_bytes` and the
  spin-sum) — recorded in `docs/REFACTOR.md`, together with the rows>1 f32 leaf
  and the still-unaudited dtype-blind rewrite steps in `gguf_linear`'s dispatch
  chain.
- Keep the pre-existing unrelated worktree edit in
  `scripts/tp2_d1_boundary_bisect.py` (`LENGTHS = (18,)`) and the untracked
  `epyc/` directory out of this unit.
