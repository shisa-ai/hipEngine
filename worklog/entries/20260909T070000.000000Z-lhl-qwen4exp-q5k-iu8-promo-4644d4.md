# #15 Q5_K bundle KL-shaving variant: promoted as the exact iu8-WMMA route

## Summary

The reopened #15 target is closed with a stronger resolution than a KL
shave: instead of rescuing the rejected T2 f16-WMMA bundle, the
production-enveloped iu8-WMMA risk+repair machinery was adapted to Q5_K
weights for layer 2 (the model's sole Q5_K MoE gate/up layer). The route
is **bit-exact versus the strict row4 parent**, so the T2 drift class
(prefill-last mean KL 0.001179 > 0.001) cannot recur by construction.

Layer 2's MoE prefill was the largest single prefill lever left: 1029 ms
at code-p4096 (7.7x the median layer's 133 ms, 13.2% of the MoE family).
