# Separated WMMA PV residual accumulators still fail numerical bars

## Summary

Keeping high and residual P*V contributions in separate WMMA accumulators does
not clear the frozen numerical bars under strict scores/global statistics/no
split. An additional probability residual term also fails. Ordinary expert
routing is unchanged.

| Probability terms | Chain | Mean KL | Max KL | Top-1 |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Two, separated | Code | 0.001524 | 0.065007 | 1.0 |
| Two, separated | Mixed summary | 0.008217 | 0.250581 | 1.0 |
| Three, separated | Code | 0.001099 | 0.051341 | 1.0 |
| Three, separated | Mixed summary | 0.015417 | 0.493947 | 1.0 |

The third term reduces local BF16 mismatches on the first full-prefill capture
from 3,989 to 908 out of 4,194,304 elements, but worsens the mixed-summary chain's
maximum KL. Local closeness and top-1 alone do not satisfy the mean/tail/max
contract. No shipping arithmetic or default changed, and no performance claim.
