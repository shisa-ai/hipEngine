# Retire W1 wide-Q6 shared4 verifier candidate selection (R0183)

## Summary

The W1 wide-Q6 shared4 verifier candidate (C8/R32 packed verification routing
R20/R24/R32 Q4 and Q6 shapes to the B-stationary `shared_b2w2`/`shared4` WMMA
leaves) was default-off with recorded no-promotion results
(`benchmarks/results/2026-09-01-gfx1151-qwen38-w1-q6-shared4-candidate.json`,
`...-w1-q4-existing-candidate.json`). Its only enablers were the verifier numerics
script and tests. Under the lead's 2026-11-07 retire-failed-candidates decision, the
unconsumed candidate selection is removed. The kernels stay registered for their
other callers, as R0183 specifies for a rejection.
