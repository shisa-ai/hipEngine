# gfx1151 selected-expert prefill pair32_nwave clears the R12 target

## Summary

R12 ladder step 1, implementing the one change the design basis
(`worklog/entries/20261003T112247.855338Z-lhl-gfx1151-prefill-r12-design-basis-c1eb0a.md`)
declared and pre-declared a target for.

**Result: 4096-token prefill 1450.8 -> 1603.7 tok/s (+10.54%)**, against the
pre-declared primary target of **>= 1600 tok/s (+10.0%)**. A second A/B pair on
the committed tree measured 1454.0 -> 1639.3 tok/s (+12.75%). All three pair32
samples in the conservative pair are >= 1600. The combined expert legs move from
the design basis's 8.51 TFLOP/s to **11.49 TFLOP/s** by its own FLOP accounting,
clearing the 11.3 TFLOP/s bar.

The change is the design the design basis named: a `NUM_WAVES`-wave block that
stages a 32-row activation tile in 1 KiB of LDS and pairs two 16-row tiles per
wave, so one dequantized Q4_K/Q5_K weight fragment feeds two WMMA accumulators.
The shipping bodies were one-wave (`block=[32,1,1]`), **LDS = 0**, and
dequantized once per 16-row tile.

Both new bodies are **bit-identical** to the kernels they pair.

The per-quant q4/q5/q6 dequant bodies are transcribed from
`gguf_k_t16_selected_wmma_prefill_compact_kernel`, including its per-block
`d`/`dmin` hoist and its `__gfx1100__` `#pragma unroll 1` on the subblock loop.
When the two row tiles of a wave resolve to **different** experts — which the
compact padding makes common, since every expert's row count is padded to a
multiple of 16 — each tile is dequantized against its own expert's column tile
with its own `d`/`dmin`/scale/min, exactly as the baseline would load them.

## Next

- **`pair32` is a selector, not the automatic default.** The promotion blocker
  is recorded in `docs/REFACTOR.md`: the gfx1151 selected-prefill LDS-staging
  family already has one repeated-128K silent lifecycle-stall precedent
  (`compact32_shared_x`, GPF-3A), and neither new body has been through that
  gate. Promote to `auto` for gfx1151 only after three independent 128K
  warmup+3 processes pass; delete the paired bodies and both mode maps if the
  gate fails.
- The **stretch target (>= 1870 tok/s, expert legs at the dense legs' 22.22
  TFLOP/s)** was not attempted. At 11.49 TFLOP/s the legs are still 1.93x behind
  the dense rate, and the isolated legs show the paired kernels are now
  register-limited (VGPR 88 and 104 against 56 and 48) rather than
  instruction-limited, so the next ladder rung is occupancy, not more pairing.
- **Open:** the pair32 arm drifted 2.2% between the two A/B pairs while the
  baseline arm moved 0.2%. Not explained here; the conservative pair is the one
  carried forward. Worth a same-session interleaved repeat before any further
  ladder step, so the next rung's delta is not read against a drifting arm.
- gfx1100/W7900 is unverified. The change is bit-identical with an unchanged K
  traversal and operand order, so it is not gfx1151-specific by mechanism, but
  the magnitude is not measured on the default hardware.
