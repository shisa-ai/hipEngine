# Measured: the WMMA route engages but contributes no measurable drift

## Summary

The engagement recorder answers the question every artifact in this packet had
been inferring from a KL. The answer falsifies my own previous explanation and
makes the misattribution sharper.

## Measured engagement

`--route wmma --layers 16-47`, one decode step each, recorder active:

| protocol | case | candidate launches | engaged | variant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| canonical | `code-p512` (512 tok) | 1000 | **264** | `wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out` |
| heldout | `code_merge_intervals` (40 tok) | 1096 | **328** | `wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out` |

Teacher arm: `engaged = 0` in both, `use_wmma_false` throughout. The teacher
really is cleared and the candidate really does run the route.

## My prediction was wrong

I predicted the canonical prefill would arrive carrying the exact coltile leaf,
fail the `prefill_` prefix predicate, and never engage. **Falsified** - it engages
264 times.

And the post-prefill row is *still* bit-identical:

| protocol | engaged | post-prefill KL | decode KL |
|---|---|---|---|
| canonical | 264 | **5.8014e-12** | 1.0184e-04 |
| heldout | 328 | 2.6345e-05 | 6.0842e-06 |

So `wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out` produces arithmetically equivalent results to the
exact chain at the prefill position, at both 512 and 40 rows. **The route runs and
contributes no measurable numerical difference.**

That sharpens the finding rather than softening it: the gate's recorded
`candidate_chain` is `wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out`, that chain demonstrably runs, and
the drift the gate reports is nonetheless not its - the canonical prefill row is
flat to f32 precision while the decode row moves.

## A flaw in my own check, corrected before it could mislead

The summariser reported `arms_isolated`. It is `True` here, and the arms are
demonstrably **not** isolated: `use_wmma` also gates `f_rowtile`,
`enabled=not use_wmma`, `use_rowtile` and the rows-2-8 native batch-decode
session, none of which the recorder can observe.

Renamed `wmma_route_engaged_candidate_only`, with an explicit
`covers_other_use_wmma_selectors: False` and a verdict that says in words that it
does not mean the arms are isolated. A test asserts the old name is absent, so it
cannot come back.

That is the fifth time in this packet a true-valued field was narrower than its
name. It is the exact failure the recorder exists to prevent, which made it a bad
place to leave one.

## What is established

1. The route engages in both protocols, at the row counts the gate exercises,
   with the teacher cleared. Verified, not inferred.
2. Its arithmetic is equivalent to the exact chain at the prefill position, so it
   is not the source of the drift the gate reports.
3. That drift therefore belongs to `f_rowtile` / `use_rowtile` / the rows-2-8
   batch-decode session - reached through `use_wmma`, outside the route the gate
   is named for.
