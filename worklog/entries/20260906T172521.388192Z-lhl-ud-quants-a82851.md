# UD-U1 review closure: premature completion acknowledged and corrected

## Summary

The original U1 closure (commit `bbff875f8`, "check off UD-U1 punchlist with
verified evidence") was premature.  An independent review found six real
defects in the landed work, and the closure prose overstated what the tests
proved.  This entry records the overclaim honestly and points at the repairs.

## What was overclaimed

- "Plain K_M/K_S/0.8B/MoE-35B controls pass preflight and reach allocation
  unchanged (allocation-sentinel tests)" — there were no allocation-sentinel
  tests for the real plain controls.  The claim had no test behind it.
- "Inventory and constrain all K_M/K_S identity callers" — the inventory missed
  `packed_decode_graph_min_replay_steps` (crashed on preset-bound 3-tuple
  identities) and the identity design itself let unknown manifests inherit
  plain-certified policy rows and the packaged hot-vocabulary selection.
- "raw-Q8 and sole-T16 alpha/beta cannot enter a BF16-pointer native-row
  owner" — the admission-side qualification existed, but nothing requested
  `ar_decode_native_rows` at load time and nothing re-checked the contract at
  the actual native execution entries, so the guarantee was not end-to-end.
- "Certified consumers mirror the plain-lane dispatch tables" — the
  moe_experts whitelist omitted rank-3 IQ3_XXS although the materializer keeps
  it raw and `gguf_iq_gemv` registers selected-expert consumers.
- Coverage checked op/role/layout/source only (no backend, no shape/
  materializability) and certificates carried no slot/plan scope, so a partial
  or differently-contracted preflight could produce a misleading positive
  certificate.
