# Remove the Gemma 4 expert down WMMA rollback lever

## Summary

R0046 is resolved: `HIPENGINE_GEMMA4_MOE_DOWN_WMMA` and its reader are gone, and
the unpinned Gemma 4 MoE prefill takes the grouped WMMA expert down owner
unconditionally. The arithmetic owners, the pinned modes, the low-population
fallback, and the declining-owner fallback are unchanged.

Both halves of the removal condition were re-verified at HEAD before editing,
independently of the sweep plan:

- Numbers and llama.cpp pairing. The route's section 2.11 packet is
  `benchmarks/results/2026-10-10-gemma4-gfx1100-expert-down-wmma-promotion.json`
  (18 cases / 1,134 rows, `passed: true`, `promotion_eligible: true`, public
  prefill 1253.2 -> 3164.7 tok/s at 512 with ids equal at every length). A
  llama.cpp-paired W7900 production-profile ladder that includes the route is
  `benchmarks/results/2026-10-15-gemma4-gfx1100-llamacpp-latest-comparison.json`
  (back-to-back per length, `prompt_sha_equal` and `ids_equal` true). That
  artifact names commit `f4259f19d`, which is not an object in this clone, so
  its tree could not be diffed here; the route's presence in the measured
  profile is instead established by the lever landing at `259245cd0`
  (2026-10-12, an ancestor) and by its presence in every tag from `v0.8.0`.
- One release cycle without a rollback. The lever is present in `v0.8.0`
  (2026-10-20), `v0.8.1`, `v0.8.2`, and `v0.8.3` (2026-10-24) -- four tagged
  releases -- and no worklog, artifact, script, or test sets the lever to `0`
  outside the promotion packet's own A/B.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/gemma4/gemma4_experts.py`: deleted
  `_GEMMA4_MOE_DOWN_WMMA_ENV` and `gemma4_moe_down_wmma_enabled()`; the down
  selector is now `down_wmma = gate_up_wmma or use_mmq or not pinned`, which is
  the previous expression with a default-on lever folded in. The comment above
  it states which owners remain reachable instead of naming the lever.
- `hipengine/generation/gemma4_profiles.py`: the `_GROUPED_DOWN_PRODUCTION`
  comment no longer advertises the rollback; it names the pinned `grouped`
  mode as the owner of the exact staged grouped kernel.
- `scripts/gemma4_wmma_promotion_packet.py`: removed the `down_wmma` entry from
  `_LEVER_KINDS` and its `--candidate-kind` help text. This is the load-bearing
  part of the deletion: `set_lever_arm` writes `os.environ[lever]` only for a
  kind in the map, so leaving the kind behind would have run both arms through
  one route and reported `passed: true` for a route compared with itself.
- `docs/ENVS.md`: deleted the lever's row.
- `docs/REFACTOR.md`: rewrote the section as an R0046 removal record, naming
  the artifact, the paired ladder, the four releases, the owners that remain,
  and the cost of losing the only isolated strict arm.
- `tests/test_unit_gemma4_expert_route.py`: dropped the lever column from the
  down-route parametrization and kept the default, pinned-`grouped`, and
  low-lane-count cases.
- `tests/test_unit_gemma4_fused_wmma_route.py`: replaced the lever
  parametrization with a WMMA-owner decline case, so the "declining down owner
  falls through to the exact ladder owner" contract keeps its coverage without
  a deleted control.

Not changed: the exact grouped owner, `gemma4_project_experts_rows`, the
Q5_1T16 tile leaf, the int8 MMQ down lever, `HIPENGINE_GEMMA4_MOE_PREFILL`, and
every other rollback flag. `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md` and the closed campaign
documents keep their historical mentions, following the R0042 precedent.
