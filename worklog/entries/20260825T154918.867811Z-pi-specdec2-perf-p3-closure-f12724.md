# Close SPECDEC2-PERF P3 stable slabs

## Summary

Close SPECDEC2-PERF P3 with a retained mechanical/stable-pointer verdict and no
headline speed claim. Source/profile audit found exactly two steady-cycle device
allocation owners: proposal root-hidden packing and physical provider repair
packing. Before P3 they allocate/free one width×H BF16 buffer each per cycle
(**40,960 bytes C2; 81,920 bytes C4**), while first target cycles separately
grow persistent target/graph workspaces.

P3 moves both hidden batches into one adapter-owned `RuntimeWorkspace` with
distinct max-width proposal/repair tensors. Full-suite current-source evidence
shows every **510 C2 + 510 C4** cycle sample allocates/frees zero. C1 records
**219/252** zero-allocation cycles; its remaining 33 are request-local graph
bucket first use, including three late tail-shape captures, and become zero after
each shape is warm. P4 owns those graph/device-chain boundaries.

Complete full-suite C1/C2/C4 throughput changes
**-0.264%/-0.300%/-0.128%**, far inside **4.98%/10.14%/9.95%** cross-prompt
sample CV. Small category signs are mixed. P3 is therefore retained for exact
stable pointers, removal of `hipMalloc`/`hipFree` synchronization, and P4 graph
eligibility—not as a complete-wall speed or policy claim.
