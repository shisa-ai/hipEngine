# MTP dispatch keeps the hard thinking budget

## Summary

The MTP batch dispatch relaxed a request's host-sampler thinking enforcement
unconditionally, so once the sampled route began serving a thinking budget, a
live `--speculative-mtp-thinking hard` request was served on that route with the
budget silently dropped. The dispatch now reads the request's thinking policy:
`hint` relaxes (unchanged), `hard` keeps the request's own contract.

Found by exercising the product surface after the sampled-route work rather than
by a test: one server, `max_tokens=10` with `hard_think_cap=8`, one MTP arm and
one autoregressive arm. The AR arm reached the answer phase; the MTP arm stayed
in reasoning at 10 tokens with a cap of 8.

## Changes

- `hipengine/server/api.py`: `_mtp_dispatch_sampling(sampling, *,
  thinking_policy)` replaces the inline unconditional
  `relax_thinking_budget_for_mtp(sampling)` call at the MTP batch dispatch. The
  policy travels on the queue item (`_QueuedGeneration.thinking_policy`, set by
  `submit`/`stream` from `_request_speculative_mtp_thinking`) into
  `_generate_prompts`. An unset policy keeps the previous relaxation, which is a
  no-op for a request that carries no enforcement.
- The same file's `_request_speculative_mtp_thinking` docstring no longer claims
  `hard` treats the budget as a hard MTP blocker; the sampled route serves one.
- `tests/test_unit_server_mtp_dispatch_sampling.py`: pins the policy split over
  six request shapes, the hint relaxation, the unset-policy default, and the
  no-enforcement identity. The first version of this file used a "sampled
  serving mode" condition instead of the policy; the hint-policy integration test
  failed on it, which is what showed that a hint request is served by the sampled
  route too and that only the policy separates the two contracts.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-23-gfx1151-qwen38-bf16-mtp-forced-tokens.json`:
  the thinking-case coverage text, including the corrected claim that a greedy
  thinking request is not served by the route at all.

  was not captured in the log).
- Live product surface, one `hipengine serve` with `--speculative-mtp-serving
  enabled --speculative-mtp-thinking hard`, one chat request per arm at
  `max_tokens=24`, `hard_think_cap` in {4, 8, 16, 32}, temperature in {0.0, 0.7},
  `speculative_mtp` in {true/thinking=hard, true/thinking=hint, false}. The
  MTP and AR arms agreed at every cap and temperature under `hard` (cap 4, 8, 16
  -> `phase: answer`; cap 32 -> `phase: reasoning`, since the cap is not reached
  within 24 tokens), both routes reporting `speculative_mtp` and `default`
  respectively. Under `hint` the MTP arm reported `phase: reasoning` with the
  budget relaxed, which is that policy's documented behavior. Before the fix the
  MTP arm reported `phase: reasoning` under `hard` at a cap of 8 with 10 tokens.
