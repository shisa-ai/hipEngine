# gfx1100 Qwen3.8 MTP CK sweep selects K0 at every width

## Summary

The C x K speculative-depth sweep is complete for Qwen3.8-27B `Q4_K_M` on the
W7900. **Speculative decoding does not pay at any measured concurrency**: all
20 cells across C2-C8 x K1-K3 run below their own AR arm. Automatic MTP is
withdrawn for this model. K4 was enabled, measured, and found **non-functional**.

## Changes

- `hipengine/models/qwen35.py`: both Qwen3.8 gfx1100 rows (C2/K2, C8/K3) become
  `automatic_eligible=False` with the reason suffixed
  `_measured_slower_than_ar_2026_09_06`. They stay admissible so explicit
  opt-in and future re-measurement still work; only the automatic route changes.
- `MTP2_MAX_CANDIDATE_DEPTH` stays 3, now documenting the measured K4 hang. The
  single-source-of-truth unification from `c6e55a147` is kept: the bound was
  previously duplicated as a validator constant, a hardcoded `min(3, ...)`
  adapter clamp, and a `getattr` default, while the server env accepted 1-4.
- `GGUF_SPECDEC2_MTP2_PHYSICAL_WIDTH_DEPTHS` is unchanged. It is backend-wide
  and also serves Qwen3.6 C1/C2 cells this sweep did not cover; per-model depth
  policy belongs in that model's serving evidence, which is where the decision
  was applied.
- Retained from `4d1189274`: serving evidence qualifies an inclusive **maximum**
  depth rather than one exact depth, and `_admit` selects
  `min(requested, qualified)`. This is what made the matrix measurable at all.

## Validation

Canonical 10-prompt mtpbench suite, D24, 20 ms window, greedy, `ar_exact`.
Each cell pairs MTP against an AR arm in the same process; medians over 2-4
repeats. C2 ran at its own capacity; C3-C7 ran inside a capacity-8 owner, which
is both what the evidence gate admits and the realistic serving shape.

| Cell | MTP | AR | ratio | accept | tokens/cycle | cycle cost (AR steps) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| C8/K3 | 90.881 | 91.783 | **0.9902** | 0.785 | 3.355 | 3.388 (+1.0%) |
| C2/K2 | 37.851 | 41.380 | 0.9147 | 0.901 | 2.801 | 3.062 (+9.3%) |
| C3/K3 | 50.358 | 56.548 | 0.8905 | 0.789 | 3.367 | 3.781 (+12.3%) |
| C7/K3 | 77.592 | 88.224 | 0.8795 | 0.780 | 3.340 | 3.798 (+13.7%) |
| C6/K3 | 71.729 | 83.856 | 0.8554 | 0.789 | 3.367 | 3.936 (+16.9%) |
| C4/K2 | 57.911 | 67.678 | 0.8557 | 0.907 | 2.814 | 3.289 (+16.9%) |
| C5/K3 | 61.686 | 76.812 | 0.8031 | 0.789 | 3.367 | 4.192 (+24.5%) |
| C7/K1 | 61.722 | 87.844 | 0.7026 | 0.957 | 1.957 | 2.785 (+42.3%) |

All 20 cells are 10/10 token-exact with tight spreads. Draft acceptance is
depth-driven and essentially width-independent: K1 0.957, K2 0.901-0.907,
K3 0.780-0.789.

**Economics.** `tokens_per_cycle = 1 + K * acceptance`, and cycle cost in AR
decode steps is `tokens_per_cycle / ratio`. MTP wins only when the verify cycle
costs fewer AR steps than the tokens it yields. C8/K3 misses by 1.0%. Reaching
1.10x there needs the cycle **10% cheaper**; 1.20x needs 17.5%. Because cost is
denominated in AR steps, every AR improvement raises this bar -- which is what
happened: this campaign's GDN, rowtile, and pair-reuse work landed on the AR
path, and at C2 the AR arm moved 30.720 -> 41.380 (+34.7%) while MTP moved
36.726 -> 37.851 (+3.1%).

**K4 is non-functional.** With the bound raised to 4 and a K4 row proposed on
the C8 evidence, the bench hung: two runs emitted no prompt output at all and
were killed at 1200 s and 3000 s, against ~4-5 minutes at K3. The stall precedes
the first completed prompt. The bound is back at 3 and the proposal reverted.
Note the server env has always accepted budgets 1-4, so this bound is the only
thing keeping a K4 request off that path; before the unification a hardcoded
adapter clamp did it by accident.

**C1 is not measurable for this model.** The physical partition path forces
width-1 groups to K0 (`resolved = bound if bound > 1 else 0`) and Qwen3.8 has no
C1 serving-evidence row. Qwen3.6 reaches C1 MTP through the separate singleton
route.

## Next

- An MTP campaign has a well-posed target: **10% cheaper verify cycle at C8**
  buys 1.10x. Below C8 the gap is much larger (24.5% at C5), so the payoff is
  width-dependent.
- Raising acceptance is the other lever: 0.785 -> 0.85 at K3 gives 3.55
  tokens/cycle and flips C8/K3 to roughly 1.05x with no cycle work, but that is
  draft-model quality, not kernels.
- K4 needs the hang diagnosed before it can be qualified. The frontier at C8/K4
  is 8x5=40 rows against a padded accept bound of 36 at K3, so a bucket or graph
  fallback is the first place to look.
- Qwen3.6 C1/C2 MTP cells remain automatic and unswept; they deserve the same
  treatment.
