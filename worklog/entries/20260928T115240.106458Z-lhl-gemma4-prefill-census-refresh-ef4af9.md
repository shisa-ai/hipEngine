# layer 29 is closed on both expert routes and the campaign's largest term is now prefill attention

## Summary

Two things happened, and the second is the useful one.

**The layer-29 finding is closed.** The census artifact
`2026-09-28-gemma4-gfx1151-prefill-kernel-census.json` recorded one layer of
thirty costing 11.7 percent of a 2048-token prefill because it is quantized
differently from its 29 siblings and fell off both fast expert routes, onto fp32
grouped fallbacks 14.1x slower per layer-block. Both named fixes have since
landed. Re-running that census on the current tree confirms it:

| | artifact | now |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `gemma4_project_experts_gate_up_mmq` calls | 116 of 120 | **120 of 120** |
| `gemma4_project_experts_down_mmq` calls | 116 of 120 | **120 of 120** |
| `q5_k` grouped_row4 + `q8_0` grouped_prefill rows | 606.6 ms | **absent** |
| 2048-token prefill | 5.242 s / 390.7 tok/s | **4.166 s / 491.5 tok/s** |

The gate/up guard is now `intermediate % 16` rather than `% 128`, because the
leaf resolves the gate/up half per column and so does not need a half to be a
multiple of 128; the down route now accepts `gguf_q8_0` as well as `gguf_q5_1`,
the two sharing the DS4 activation pack. The +25.8 percent is the sum of three
changes -- the MMQ routes becoming default, the Q5_1 down projection's
inner-loop memory traffic, and layer 29 -- and is not attributable to layer 29
alone; the previous artifact's own 11.7 percent is that share.

**The useful part is that the bottleneck moved.** At 512 tokens the three terms
are close, and at 2048 attention is the largest single one and still growing:

```
512 tokens     experts 320.25 ms 44.0%   dense+lm_head 184.85 ms 25.4%   attention 173.97 ms 23.9%
2048 tokens    attention 2010.70 ms 48.3%   experts 1246.91 ms 29.9%   dense+lm_head 717.62 ms 17.2%
```

Attention costs 11.6x at 2048 what it costs at 512, for 4x the tokens. The
expert routes have been the campaign's focus and have been improved repeatedly;
attention has not, and its absolute cost now sets the 2048-token number. Against
llama.cpp on this host the remaining gap is 1.29x at 512 (909.3 against 712.8)
and 2.17x at 2048 (1067.14 against 491.5), so the deficit is concentrated where
attention lives.

## Next

**Give prefill attention tensor-core QK and PV.** This is the campaign's largest
remaining term and it is not a tuning problem. The kernel is issue- or
latency-bound, and that is already measured three ways rather than argued: a
`--dtype f32` probe that doubles every byte moved at identical arithmetic costs
7.8 percent on the sliding layers and 14.2 percent on the full ones, so it is not
paying for bytes; the tiled variant is a wash against the untiled one at this
shape (150.65 against 150.5 ms over six geometries); and it runs at 464 GFLOP/s
on the 256d layers against an fp32 FMA ceiling near 25 TFLOP/s, about 2 percent.
Both QK and PV run as fp32 FMA today, so the work is a rewrite of both plus the
causal mask and the sliding window, with the fp32 softmax max and sum kept
outside the tensor-core path. No time estimate is offered; attention is
2010.70 ms of a 4166 ms step at 2048, so the ceiling is large but unmeasured.
