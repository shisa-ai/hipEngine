# VibeVoice Q4 runtime end-to-end: 16/16 greedy chain vs torch

## Summary

The Q4_K_M backbone runs end-to-end through the VibeVoice runner and
reproduces the torch greedy chain 16/16 despite quantized weights.

## Changes

- hipengine/runtime/vibevoice_qwen2_q4.py: VibevoiceQwen2Q4Runtime
  subclass (block-buffer layers, q4 kernels namespace, dense bf16
  lm_head logits override, incremental q4 prefill route).
- hipengine/kernels/vibevoice.py: quant-aware resolution
  (quant='q4_k_m' selects the q4 linear primitives; bf16 default
  unchanged) + resolve_vibevoice_prefill_route.
- hipengine/kernels/{hip_gfx1100,hip_gfx1151}/__init__.py: register
  the q4 vibevoice kernels per backend.
- hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/vibevoice/q4.py: per-pointer GGML-type
  routing across Q4_K/Q5_K/Q6_K raw-block GEMV families (llama-quantize
  Q4_K_M adaptively upgrades attn_v/ffn_down to Q5_K or Q6_K on half
  the layers); dual gate+up emulation with slot-keyed scratch.
- hipengine/loading/vibevoice_asr_gguf.py: publishes
  GGUF_WEIGHT_TYPES (ptr -> ggml type) and widens biases to f32.
- tests/test_gpu_vibevoice_qwen2_q4_runner.py: three gates.

## Validation

- Debug trail (all committed in this entry's message context): NaN was
  traced to three causes - (1) Q6_K attn_v read as Q4_K blocks, (2)
  Q5_K ffn_down (byte shape 74x210) read as Q4_K, (3) the dual
  emulation reusing one scratch buffer for gate and up (identical
  outputs). Each fixed and re-verified.
- First-position logits: rel 4.7e-2 vs torch, top-1 match. Greedy chain
  16/16 EXACT vs the torch fixture (incremental prefill + q4 decode).
- Standalone kernel refs on real blocks: q4 gemv rel 4e-7, q6 gemv rel
  4e-7 (cos 1.0 each).
- Bundles: q4 runner 3/3; dense + q4 + registry/protocol units 53/53.
- Prefill (incremental route) ~11 s per 146-row request; batched q4
  prefill is the speed follow-up.
