# Review mlx-serve Qwen3.8 Flash-Next support; add E9 long-context lane

## Summary

Source-level review of [ddalcu/mlx-serve](https://github.com/ddalcu/mlx-serve)
v26.9.2 (`1ec580a`) after its reported Qwen 3.8 Flash-Next long-context
behavior: prefill ~1700-1800 tok/s staying ~1000 to 1M context, decode
100+ tok/s to 16K, 80+ to 256K, 60/40 at 500K/1M. Read-only blob-less clone;
no external runtime, weights or kernels built or executed. Zig/MLX on Apple
Silicon (M4/M5 Max, 4-bit MLX pack, llmprobe protocol) - not a comparator
row for our gfx1151/UD-Q4_K_XL campaign; mechanisms only. mlx-serve was
already a named GDN-prework donor in `docs/OLMX-IDEAS.md` for the 3.5/3.6
lane; this is the first review of its Flash-Next path.

Findings (full section in the forks review doc):

1. **Most of the flatness is the shared hybrid architecture**, not a secret:
   O(1) selected-attention window (top-512 of kv/4 blocks = our fixed
   2048-token budget), O(ctx/4) pooled-block scoring, GDN recurrent state,
   few full-attention layers, constant PLE I/O. Our p512->p4096 prefill is
   already flat (239.7 -> 237.9).
2. **One genuinely adoptable mechanism - the QSA raw-indexer-key ring
   (`e0a4264`, #381).** They proved raw indexer keys are read only at block
   close, authority check and rollback (18% of per-token cache cost,
   3.2 GB at 1M in their bf16 layout) and replaced full retention with a
   32-row ring, the pooled block bank as history, and checkpoint leftovers.
   Our engine retains the same payload in FP32 -
   `fp32_raw_index_bytes_per_token` = 6,144 B/token (12 QSA layers x 128
   dims x 4 B), double their pre-fix cost: ~1.6 GB at 256K, ~6.4 GB at 1M,
   plus full-history `Qwen4ExpHostQSAIndexSnapshot.raw_keys[:count].copy()`
   host snapshots. Our device consumers match theirs
   (`prepare_complete_blocks` pooling, restore/rollback), so the ring is
   adoptable in principle behind state/snapshot redesign and our exactness
   gates.
3. **Their launch economy validates R6** from the opposite side: their
   decode is a compiled fused graph with few launches; our R6 split
   measured 5.3 ms/token host/launch overhead over ~1700 launches.
4. **Not applicable:** Ngram/PLE mmap pread pool and page-cache warm (our
   PLE is resident, constant 3.1 MB H2D), MTP adaptive depth (W7900 lane),
   Metal/NAX specifics.

Gap identified in our own evidence: we have no current-path throughput
curve beyond p4096. The P10 4K/16K/64K rows (53.08/51.48/48.02 tok/s) are
pre-campaign, chunk512, retrieval-instrumented - not a baseline for
adoption decisions.
