# Expose the device-resident Inkling expert chain in fast

## Summary

The explicit Inkling GGUF `fast` profile now performs split gate/up activation on the device. The strict and default paths still evaluate the original FP32 formula on the host.

## Changes

- Register the device and host activation variants, and identify them in the execution-profile manifest and request diagnostics. Keep the gate/up projection, activation, down projection, ordered mix and optional convolution consumer on stream zero under the projection owner lock. Only the input and mixed output cross the host boundary.
- Reuse the intermediate projection buffer after its gate/up output is consumed. Leave routing and mix order unchanged. The existing integer-dot projection remains limited to its supported single-token geometry.
