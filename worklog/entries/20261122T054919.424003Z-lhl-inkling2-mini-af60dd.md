# Screen grouped integer dot for packed Inkling prefill

## Summary

The four-route integer-dot prototype was faster than the FP32 control in every tested packed projection cell on both GPUs. It does not preserve FP32 arithmetic. The results justify a public prefill trial, not a default change.

## Changes

- Scratch only. Keep the packed 36-byte weight blocks and four-route metadata. Pack activation codes and scales once, decode each FP4 fragment into integer dot operands once per same-expert tile, and apply the per-route activation and weight scales before the ordered output.

## Validation

- `hipcc -O3 -shared -fPIC --offload-arch="$arch" -I hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant /tmp/hipengine_inkling_nvfp4_q8_tile4_trial.hip -o "$so"`; `python -u ./hipengine_inkling_nvfp4_wmma_trial.py "$backend" "$model" "$so" q8_tile4`. Reused the [matrix screen's real bank shapes and synthetic routing](20261122T054650.102910Z-lhl-inkling2-mini-0d2355.md) on W7900/mainline Q4_K_M and Strix Halo/pinned Loom Q4_K_XL. All outputs were finite and three independently decoded CPU elements per shape agreed under the respective FP32 and block-Q8 arithmetic. Relative RMS difference from the FP32 parent was about `0.00535`.
- HIP device events include activation packing. Four samples of eight dispatches per arm, in both orders. At 64 uniform rows the FP32/grouped Q8 medians on Strix were 7.185/4.660 ms for gate/up and 3.716/2.672 ms for down; on W7900 they were 2.803/1.839 ms and 1.463/1.100 ms. The 13-row and skewed cells also improved in this isolated test. Raw data: `/tmp/hipengine_inkling_nvfp4_q8_tile4_trial-w7900.jsonl`, `/tmp/hipengine_inkling_nvfp4_q8_tile4_trial-strix.jsonl`.
