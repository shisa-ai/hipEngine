# Correction: the LDS staging entry's baseline reproduction bound

## Summary

`20260929T005933.308479Z-lhl-gemma4-t16-gate-up-leaf-lds-staging-478b49.md` says the HEAD arm was
"measured first and re-measured last and reproduced to within 0.7 percent". That bound is right for
one pair and wrong for the other, and the entry is immutable, so the numbers are restated here and
in the artifact.

**What was actually measured.** The HEAD arm was run three times at 4096 and twice at 512, across
two sessions:

| arm | prompt | instrument | leaf ms/call | prefill |
| :--- | ---: | :--- | ---: | ---: |
| HEAD | 4096 | census, this session (run a) | 4.205 | 4.024 s, 1017.9 tok/s |
| HEAD | 4096 | census, this session (run b) | 4.170 | 3.979 s, 1029.4 tok/s |
| HEAD | 4096 | census, the prior unit's session | 4.136 | 3.961 s, 1034.1 tok/s |
| HEAD | 512 | census, this session | 4.087 | 0.440 s, 1162.7 tok/s |
| HEAD | 512 | census, the prior unit's session | 4.114 | 0.442 s, 1157.3 tok/s |
| K-major LDS | 4096 | census, this session | **3.0723** | **3.7132 s, 1103.1 tok/s** |
| K-major LDS | 512 | census, this session | **2.987** | **0.405 s, 1265.0 tok/s** |

So the reproduction bound is **0.84 percent at 4096** (4.170 against 4.205) and **0.66 percent at
512** (4.087 against 4.114), not 0.7 percent for both. The entry's 0.7 percent describes the 512
pair only.

**Nothing else moves.** The candidate is below every baseline measurement on every instrument, so
the honest form of the result is a range rather than one ratio: the leaf improves **1.346x to 1.369x
at 4096** and **1.346x to 1.368x at 512**, and prefill improves **1.067x to 1.084x at 4096**. The
same-session pairs the entry reports as primary are unchanged: 4.170 -> 3.0723 at 4096 and
4.087 -> 2.987 at 512.
