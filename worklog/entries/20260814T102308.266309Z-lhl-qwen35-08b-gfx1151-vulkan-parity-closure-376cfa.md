# Block 0.8B closure on Vulkan parity

## Summary

Completed C1/C2 and evaluated G1-G3. Exact shared-token correctness passes and
Q8 public decode beats Vulkan, but Q4 misses Vulkan in every required scope and
all five blocks. G2 fails; G3 and D08-T1 remain blocked. The campaign is not
closed.

## Changes

- Added a compact committed `.Q`/token-9707 p512/tg128 RLE+hash fixture that
  tokenizes identically in both engines and both quants.
- Added a hipEngine no-sampler production-graph core harness and a pinned
  llama.cpp direct-`llama_decode` helper. Both report core and public greedy
  scopes without including model load or graph capture.
- Ran five counter-rotated serial engine blocks per quant on an idle GPU and
  published all raw hashes, hardware/software state, exact commands, memory,
  and explicit invalid-row exclusion in the closure artifact.
- Updated the campaign and benchmark rollups without claiming parity/closure.

## Validation

- C1: both tokenizers emit exactly 9707 x512; teacher continuation is 9707
  x128. Committed fixture hashes and two focused fixture tests pass.
- Cross-engine exact-token correctness: forced and public-equivalent top-1 are
  each 645/645 per quant over five blocks; all rows are finite and deterministic.
- Q4 exact core: hipEngine/Vulkan 0.424x pp and 0.734x tg; exact public: 0.463x
  pp and 0.942x tg. hipEngine wins 0/5 blocks in every Q4 scope.
- Q8 exact core: 0.664x pp and 0.872x tg; exact public: 0.729x pp and 1.028x tg.
  Q8 public decode wins 5/5, but prefill/core fail.
- Fresh canonical shape-only supplement: Q4 0.377x/0.595x and Q8 0.690x/0.697x
  pp/tg. A transient nonexistent-ICD command produced a CPU-labeled row and was
  discarded/replaced by correct `radeon_icd.json` Vulkan rows.
- Memory: Q4 hipEngine owned 1017.6 MiB vs llama declared device 1015.4 MiB;
  Q8 981.8 vs 1281.8 MiB (23.4% lower). Scopes are close, not identical.
- D5 correctness remains 900/900 at max KL 0.001745; CPU reference and M12
  recording/state gates pass. D5 affected 72, closure fixture 2, build 8, and
  generation scheduler 320 focused tests pass.
- Required `uv run pytest -v` was stopped at 16% after concurrent timing made
  it invalid. Before stop it exposed existing benchmark-scoreboard and local
  27B-fixture failures. Per focused-repair policy it was not automatically
  repeated, and no all-green milestone suite is claimed.
