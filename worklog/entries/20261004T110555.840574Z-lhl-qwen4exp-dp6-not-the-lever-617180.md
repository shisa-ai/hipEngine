# DP-6 is worth 1.09% of the prefill union, so it is not the prefill lever

## Summary

**I named `DP-6` as the next prefill lever in worklog `20261004T111511` without
reading its pricing entry, and the pricing had already ruled it out.** The
committed `20260930T142624` attributes `DP-6`'s whole scope against the X-3
capture's own 15,741.963 ms kernel union:

| pass | ms | % of union |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| SSM causal conv (`conv_prefill_exact` + `conv_prefill_state`) | 116.121 | **0.738%** |
| q/k norm + RoPE (`qsa_split_norm_rope` + `qsa_norm_rope` + `qsa_pool_norm_rope`) | 52.199 | 0.332% |
| KV store | 3.263 | 0.021% |
| **`DP-6` whole scope** | **171.583** | **1.090%** |

**Fusing everything `DP-6` covers buys at most ~1.1% of prefill, against a
1.45-1.55x per-token deficit that needs ~31-35%.** It is not the lever, and the
ledger's own action column marking it "actionable now" does not mean "large".

**This is the same failure class as the `DE-11` retraction two iterations
earlier:** naming a lever from the ledger's action column and commit dates
without reading the measurement that prices it. The pricing entry was committed
on 2026-09-30 and was three lines into the same row's evidence.

## Next

**The prefill time is in the main GEMMs, not in epilogue passes.** With `DP-6` at
1.09% of the kernel union, a uniform 1.45-1.55x per-token deficit has to be
GEMM throughput, which is exactly what the matched-granularity decomposition
already says: **dense projections 6.93x, routed MoE 5.59x, attention/GDN/router
4.61x** of the gufo gap.

**The one lever with a measured large effect is the wide GEMM route**, which
delivers **+18.88% prefill and +5.79% decode** at layers 0-47 and is blocked only
by the production numerical gate (1.47x mean KL at 0-47; the deepest passing scope
14-47 is worth ~+1.7%). **So the highest-value next action is to find where in
0-47 the KL drift enters and whether a narrower but still large scope, or a
different wide-route arithmetic, clears the envelope at a useful speedup** - not
to build epilogue fusions.

**Before naming any further lever from a ledger row: read the row's own pricing
evidence first.** Two of the last three iterations were wasted by skipping that.
