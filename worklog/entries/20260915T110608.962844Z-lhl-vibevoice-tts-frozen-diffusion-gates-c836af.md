# VibeVoice-TTS diffusion gates are frozen constants, not a function of the run under test

## Summary

The diffusion parity gates took the form `max(nominal_limit, measured_band)`,
where the band was measured by replaying the trajectory under test from a
±1 bf16 ULP perturbation. That is circular: an implementation that becomes less
stable measures a wider band and therefore widens its own tolerance. The gates
are now frozen constants, and the band measurement is a diagnostic that feeds no
threshold.

The rewrite also closes a second, independent hole. `test_gpu_diffusion_replay_parity[two]`
was already failing before any of this session's head work: it gated GPU-vs-CPU
eps at 0.10 while the frozen two-speaker trajectory measures 0.48 there. It was
a pre-existing failure, not a regression, and it is now handled by the same
split as the CPU lane.

## The boundary

Amplification has a measurable onset at step 6, and that onset is what the gates
are split on. Measured on the committed fixtures (CPU reference vs oracle, then
device head vs CPU reference):

| comparison | request | steps 0-5 (eps) | late (eps) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CPU vs oracle | single | 0.0127 | 0.0502 |
| CPU vs oracle | two | 0.0074 | 0.3655 |
| device vs CPU | single | 0.0085 | 0.0393 |
| device vs CPU | two | 0.0091 | 0.3246 |

Both requests are gated over steps 0-5, where the implementation is what the
comparison measures. The single-speaker request is additionally gated over all
20 steps and on its final and scaled latents. The two-speaker request carries no
late-step threshold, because no value there separates a defect from the
fixture's conditioning.

## Negative control

A gate that cannot fail is not a gate, so the pre-onset limit was checked
against injected head defects. Perturbing every 2-D weight tensor by a relative
factor and re-measuring the steps 0-5 eps spread:

| weight perturbation | single | two | caught by the 0.025 gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| none | 0.0127 | 0.0074 | no (correct) |
| 1e-4 | 0.0087 | 0.0086 | no (correct: at the noise floor) |
| 1e-3 | 0.0130 | 0.0188 | no |
| 1e-2 | 0.0606 | 0.1562 | yes, both |

A 1% head error is caught with a 2.4x margin; a clean implementation passes with
2-3.4x margin. A 0.1% perturbation is below the gate, which is appropriate -- it
is inside the accumulation-order noise floor the gate exists to absorb.

## Changes

- `tests/test_unit_vibevoice_tts_diffusion.py`: frozen `_FROZEN_*` constants with
  provenance; steps 0-5 gated for both requests; whole-trajectory and
  latent/scaled gates for `single` only; magnitude smoke bounds for `two`;
  `test_fixture_chaos_band_is_measured_not_assumed` renamed to
  `test_trajectory_conditioning_is_diagnostic` and reduced to a diagnostic that
  asserts the *reason* the late gate is absent still holds.
- `tests/test_live_vibevoice_tts_diffusion_gpu.py`: same split, with the
  GPU-vs-CPU thresholds measured separately (eps 0.039 whole-trajectory on
  `single`) rather than reusing the CPU-lane numbers.
- `docs/MODEL-VIBEVOICE-TTS.md`: the "Trajectory sensitivity" section now states
  the onset, the frozen split, the negative control, and why the thresholds are
  not derived from the run.

## Validation

- `pytest tests/test_unit_vibevoice_tts_diffusion.py tests/test_live_vibevoice_tts_diffusion_gpu.py`
  -> 13 passed, on both the pre-change and post-change head configuration, so the
  gate change is order-independent of the head work.
- `pytest tests/ -k vibevoice` -> 106 passed, 3 skipped, 2 failed. Both failures
  are the pre-existing ASR WER driver tests, reproduced with these changes
  stashed.
- Negative control as tabulated above.

## Next

The two-speaker request's *output* is not qualified by anything here. Its late
trajectory is unreproducible against the oracle, so numerical agreement cannot
be its acceptance evidence; that request now has none in the diffusion lane. Its
replacement is a generated-audio quality suite over several held-out requests
(intelligibility, speaker and turn attribution, missing or repeated speech,
duration), which is the next unit.

The review that prompted this change also flagged that the session benchmark
sets its generation budget from the oracle's expected token count and that its
reported "cold" run follows a warmed traced synthesis. Both are unfixed and
belong with the benchmark-comparability work.
