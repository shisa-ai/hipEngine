# W7900 Qwen3.8: fused gate/up floor lands at rows33 after MTP verify regression at rows12 (AR +1.3%/+3.6%, MTP +1.7%/+2.9%)

## Summary

Checkpoint on the `w7900-qwen38-dual-silu-prefill-rows12` decision after the
post-commit topline re-check. The prefill win was real but the rows12 floor was
**not shippable**: `_run_post_attention_ffn_rows` is the shared FFN stage for
prefill, packed decode *and* target verification, so lowering the floor to 12
also moved MTP verification into the fused owner. Verification runs in captured
physical groups of 16 rows (partitioned) / 32 rows, and the fused owner is
slower there even though the identical-shape prefill A/B is faster.

| floor | AR C1 | AR C8 | MTP C1 | MTP C8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 512 (published baseline) | 21.87 | 44.34 | 31.15 | 30.84 |
| 12 (commit `f11835b23`) | 22.16 | 46.01 | 31.70 | **28.30 (-8.2%)** |
| **33 (commit `3d11d88ff`, shipped)** | **22.15 (+1.3%)** | **45.95 (+3.6%)** | **31.69 (+1.7%)** | **31.72 (+2.9%)** |

MTP acceptance shifts between runs (mean C8 draft cycles 9.3 / 8.6 / 7.8), so the
aggregate MTP walls are not a clean comparison. The binding evidence is the
same-cycle paired comparison against the baseline packet - prompts whose cycle
count did not change, which isolates per-cycle cost:

- floor 12: 7/7 prompts slower, mean **+11.9%** wall (worst `code_markdown_table`
  +24.5% at 6 cycles, `mixed_ja_en_review` +10.9% at 12 cycles).
- floor 33: 7/7 prompts faster, mean **-2.6%** wall (spread -2.3%..-2.8%), i.e.
  verification keeps the unfused chain and the remaining MTP gain is admission
  prefill.
- floor 12 MTP C8 walls replicated at 6.7841 / 6.7640 / 6.8260 s against baseline
  6.2248 s, so the regression is not a single-run artifact.

  33/45/48/128/256/511/512; unfused fallback renamed to

  the verify group widths are a pinned contract rather than an accident of the
  constant.

  reason instead of rows12.
- `docs/REFACTOR.md`: new Cleanup Ledger row - the row-count floor is an explicit
  proxy for prefill-vs-verify context; the real fix is a session-scoped floor
  whose value participates in `_DISPATCH_RESOLVE_CACHE` /
  `_PAIR_DISPATCH_RESOLVE_CACHE` keys. A scope that is not in those keys would
  leak dispatch decisions between prefill and verify, which is why the stateless
  proxy was chosen over the scope today.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-t16-dual-silu-prefill-rows33-promotion.json`
  (promoted) and the rows12 artifact flipped to `status: superseded` with
