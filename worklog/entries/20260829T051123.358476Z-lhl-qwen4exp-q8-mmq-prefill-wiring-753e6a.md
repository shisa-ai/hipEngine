# Wire guarded Q8 MMQ prefill into Qwen4Exp

## Summary

Wired the guarded raw-Q8 MMQ128 prefill path into Qwen4Exp as an env-gated
(default off) session over chunked prefill. New F32-activation/F32-output
variants of the D4x3 quantize producer, guarded MMQ body, and sparse exact
repair join the existing BF16 chain. A first dispatch-order bug (the variant
is `prefill_f32_f32_out` at rows>1, not `gemv_f32_f32_out`) and a degenerate
repair pass (the BF16-rounding-boundary risk criterion is meaningless for F32
outputs; 1e-5 threshold queued most elements and cost 0.634 s) were found and
fixed by measurement. p508 strict wall falls 8.270 to 7.393 s (-10.6%, 61.43
-> 68.71 tok/s) with deterministic logits and unchanged top-1.

  sparse with the 1e-5 threshold.
- p508 candidate (6 runs, stable SHA `1887fc28...`, top-1 248068): median
