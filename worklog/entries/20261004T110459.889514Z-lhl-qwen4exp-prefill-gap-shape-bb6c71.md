# The prefill gap is a uniform 1.45-1.55x per-token throughput deficit, not setup cost

## Summary

**The prefill gap to llama.cpp is flat in shape, which rules out per-request setup
as its cause.** Converting the retained rates to per-token cost:

| prompt tokens | hipEngine ms/token | llama.cpp ms/token | gap | ratio |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 512 | 3.769 | 2.591 | +1.178 | **1.455** |
| 1024 | 3.489 | 2.372 | +1.117 | **1.471** |
| 4096 | 3.671 | 2.370 | +1.301 | **1.549** |

Rates are this session's canonical-bench readings at chunk 1024 (265.33 / 286.64 /
272.38 prefill tok/s, `code-p512` / `code-p1024` / `code-p4096`) against the
retained llama.cpp rows (385.97 / 421.60 / 421.87).

**The per-token cost is essentially constant across a 8x shape range on both
engines** (hipEngine 3.49-3.77, llama.cpp 2.37-2.59), and the ratio varies only
1.455 -> 1.549. **A fixed per-request setup cost would show as a large per-token
penalty at p512 that decays with shape; it does not.** hipEngine's p512 per-token
cost (3.769) is *higher* than its p4096 cost (3.671) by only 2.7%, and the p1024
point is the cheapest of the three.

**What this implies for the fix:** the ~1.1-1.3 ms/token deficit is a steady-state
GEMM/attention throughput deficit and must be attacked with kernel-level work
across the whole prefill, not with amortization, batching, or setup elimination.
It is consistent with the matched-granularity decomposition already recorded -
dense projections 6.93x, routed MoE 5.59x, attention/GDN/router 4.61x - which
also shows a broad rather than a localized deficit.

## Validation

**Arithmetic from retained, already-gated rates**; no new GPU run. The hipEngine
rates are this session's canonical-bench fresh-process readings at chunk 1024 and
the llama.cpp rates are the retained scoreboard rows. **One host, one model, one
quant, one profile, three shapes; the llama.cpp rows are a different process and
were not measured in the same session**, so the ratio is the claim and the
absolute per-token figures are indicative.
