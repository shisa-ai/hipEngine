# Q8 dense prefill WMMA layer-scope gate

## Summary

The f16 WMMA dense Q8_0 prefill route (`wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out`, the family the
new `dense_wide256` kernel is bit-identical to) passes the calibrated numerical
envelope at layers 32-47 with wide headroom and fails it marginally at layers
0-47. The failure is scope, not arithmetic: the drift is 53x larger with layers
0-31 added, and it lands where the in-tree suffix screen already put it.

| Scope | Cases | Rows | Mean KL | p95 KL | Max KL | Top-1 | Verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| layers 32-47 | 12, 4 categories | 1548 | 5.81e-5 | 1.75e-4 | 1.36e-2 | 0.99677 | pass |
| layers 0-47 | 3, `code` only | 387 | 1.099e-3 | 5.017e-3 | 3.913e-2 | 0.9922 | fail |
| limit | | | 1e-3 | 5e-3 | 5e-2 | 0.99 | |

Both scopes are deterministic across 3 repeats; the 32-47 arm also reports
`hard_gates_passed` and `eligible_for_automatic_admission` true with no scope
failures and no outlier-review rows. The `code`-category mean at 32-47 is
2.08e-5 against 1.099e-3 at 0-47.

This reproduces and tightens the in-tree 2026-08-29 certification of the same
scope (`2026-08-29-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-moe27-q8-32-production.json`: 450
rows, four categories, mean KL 1.197e-4, top-1 449/450, `code` mean 4.57e-5) on
3.4x more rows and a different harness. The certified scope holds on the current
profile stack.

## Changes

- `scripts/execution_profile_q8_wmma_prefill_layers_gate.py` (new): whole-model
  gate for the route. Constructs the named production profile first, then flips
  `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_WMMA_LAYERS` per arm, because the profile binder writes
  that variable as `""` during its own pass and any pre-construction value is
  silently inert. Teacher is the same profile with the selector cleared, so the
  measured drift is the changed route's own contribution. Reports full-logit
  trajectories, per-scope gates, and same-schedule repeat determinism.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-16-q8-wmma-dense-prefill-layers-gate/`: both
  artifacts and the write-up.
- `docs/REFACTOR.md`: recorded the binder-ordering hazard on the existing
  `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_WMMA_LAYERS` bridge entry.

| top-1 | 1543/1548 = 0.99677 | 384/387 = 0.9922 | 0.99 |
| repeat determinism | 3/3 identical | 3/3 identical | exact |
| outlier rows | 0 | 1 | none |

Per category at 32-47: `code` 2.08e-5 / top-1 1.0, `general_en` 1.25e-4 / 0.98966,
`general_ja` 5.04e-5 / 0.99742, `mixed_ja_en` 3.62e-5 / 1.0. `general_en` carries
the tail and all five top-1 misses, matching the 2026-08-29 category ordering.

Independent evidence that the route is live at the leaf: on the same
`layers.8.attn_qkv` packet the exact coltile route and the WMMA route produce
outputs differing elementwise by max 2.65e-3 with mean signed 1.2e-7 and no
constant shift, which is the f16 rounding signature and not a scale/offset bug.

Both artifacts record `untracked_dirty` from an unrelated untracked
`docs/superpowers/` directory, so `measurement_valid` is false on provenance
grounds even for the passing arm. The 0-47 artifact's `provenance.command` is a
character list rather than a command list (the gate joined argv into a string
where the schema wants a sequence); the gate is fixed and the 32-47 artifact
carries a correct `command`.

## Open question: the prefill row is nearly unaffected

At both scopes the prefill row's distribution is nearly unchanged (32-47: KL
3.54e-9, teacher NLL delta -6.4e-11; 0-47: KL 1.09e-11, NLL delta 5.2e-11) while
its logits are not identical (max absolute logit delta 1.86 and 1.34, on entries
whose probability is negligible). All 12 prefill rows sit within 3.3e-8 of the
teacher distribution while the decode rows carry essentially all of the measured
drift, and the asymmetry reproduces at both scopes and all 12 cases. Two
readings are open and neither is measured: the prefill perturbation is real but
its effect on the prefill's last-token distribution is small and the drift is
amplified by the decode state, or part of the prefill path is not taking the
candidate arithmetic (for example a captured MoE graph replaying the teacher's
kernels) and the measured drift is a lower bound. A counted kernel census per
owner is the required next evidence before either reading is adopted.
