# PN3 P3-LAQ1 GEMV-leaf mechanism rejected (PN4 binding failure)

## Summary

Rejected the P3-LAQ1 GEMV-leaf mechanism (software-pipelined / improved
Q4_T16 c1 dense GEMV for linear-attention QKV/gate) after a clean PN4 RED
attempt on gfx1151. Two bit-exact T0 variants were implemented and neither
flips any of the three leaf ceilings:

- `vecq` (word-load uint32 qword reads): bit-exact vs local32 and pack8 but
  ~10% slower on both shapes -> the kernel is not load-instruction-bound.
- `tile16` (one block computes all 16 tile columns, killing the 2x read
  amplification): bit-exact per column but only +5% on attn_qkv and -7% on
  attn_gate (256 blocks under-utilize the 80-SIMD machine) -> not memory-
  BW-bound either.

The leaf is at ~350 GB/s effective (attn_qkv ~0.029 ms burst) and is at its
practical limit for the byte-scattered Q4_T16 layout; pair 0.046 ms vs the
0.042 ms RED target stays RED even with the best variant.

Root-cause evidence: the 2.29 ms/token `gdn_input_projections` stage is
**dispatch-bound, not kernel-bound**. Single-launch (burst=1, realistic
per-launch host+sync) measures attn_qkv 0.062 ms + attn_gate 0.044 ms =
0.106 ms/layer (~3.2 ms/token) vs 0.045 ms/layer pipelined (burst=50,
1.34 ms/token). The model's 72 us/layer stage wall sits between the two and is
dominated by ~30 us/kernel per-launch dispatch (~780 launches/token -> ~23
ms/token of dispatch exposure). A GEMV leaf can therefore never win the
complete wall; the sound next mechanism is launch-count reduction of the c1
linear-attention block (P3-LA2).

## Changes

- Reverted the candidate implementation (`vecq`/`tile16` kernels, launchers,
  extern "C" exports, host wrappers, and registrations) in
  `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_t16_selected_gemv.{hip,py}` to the
  committed PN3 state.
- Preserved the failure fixture `tests/test_pn3_laq_gemv_red.py`: bit-exact
  guard stays live; the three leaf perf ceilings are marked xfail with the
  rejection reason.
- Declared the next mechanism (P3-LA2, launch fusion of
  attn_norm->attn_qkv->attn_gate) in
  `docs/QWEN36-35B-ZBOOK-PRODUCTION-NUMERICS.md` (PN3/P3-LAQ1 REJECTED +
  P3-LA2 declaration).
- Evidence artifact:
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-17-zbook-qwen36-pn3-laq1-rejected.json`.

## Validation

- All three variants bit-exact vs local32 owner and pack8 control on both
  attn_qkv (2048->8192) and attn_gate (2048->4096) with random Q4_K T16 tiles
  (gfx1151, HIPENGINE_HIP_ARCH=gfx1151).
- Timing (burst=50 median): attn_qkv local32 0.0288 / vecq 0.0312 / tile16
  0.0289 ms; attn_gate local32 0.0160 / vecq 0.0170 / tile16 0.0179 ms.
- burst=1 (realistic dispatch): attn_qkv 0.0617, attn_gate 0.0438 ms.
- `tests/test_pn3_laq_gemv_red.py`: 1 passed (bit-exact guard), 3 xfailed
  (perf ceilings, rejection reason).
