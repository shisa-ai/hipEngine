# R12 outcome: whole-model d4x2 MMQ REJECTED at the canonical envelope; route stays default-off

## Summary

The whole-model d4x2 MMQ candidate is **REJECTED** at the canonical envelope and
stays default-off. The 48-layer accumulation of two-plane truncation error fails
every bar: mean KL 1.598e-3 (bar 1e-3), p95 8.703e-3 (5e-3), p99 2.108e-2
(2e-2), max 7.179e-2 (5e-2), top-1 0.98293 (bar 0.99, with code/general_en at
0.97189 on the 0.97 scope floor), 17/996 flips and top-5 overlap degrading to
2/5.

The same probe design passed the R11 packet (mean 4.03e-4), so the drift is
genuinely the route's rather than harness noise. Design conclusion: the
dense-linear gap to the newer engine (~0.5-0.8s at code-p4096) is the price of
the three-plane exactness chain, and closing it requires bounded-drift arithmetic
that the current bars reject at whole-model scope.
