# Scope GR wave-scale selection to its runner

## Summary

R0419 is complete. The profile publishes `gr_wave_scale` on its generator and
runner, production True and strict False; late clones inherit it. The retired
process selector is ignored. The rows>256/four-branch fused GR guard, kernel
arithmetic, wide/IU8 precedence, independent DPP selection and its wave-scale
fallback are unchanged. This is a control migration, not arithmetic promotion
or a performance claim.

## Changes

- Forward the field through scalar runner layer/head calls and both attention
  and FFN reads of dense QSA, prefill QSA and GDN. Mixed-target passes forward
  their batch owner's field through attention, FFN and head reads too.
- Captured scalar GR segments only execute at one row. Ordinary batch decode
  is bounded at 64 rows. Neither reaches the rows>256 selector, so no graph
  cache key change is needed. The mixed-target pass is eager and carries the
  field explicitly. MTP draft scalar calls and its `decode_rows=True` append
  use scalar GR semantics and cannot reach this selector.
- Add exception-safe `gr_wave_scale(runner, enabled)` diagnostic scope. The
  campaign and state gate bind the owning runner, record its publication, and
  close GR wide, IU8 and DPP while comparing the original parent and wave
  composite. Those prerequisite overrides are recorded and restored. Without
  closing the preemptors, a wave-scale comparison can count neither named
  leaf; this correction does not reinterpret historical benchmark results.
- Preserve journey-localize's strict GR-family, dense-family and split-prefill
  controls explicitly. Its dense family includes `GR_`, unlike the previous
  Q8 wave-scale migration. The three K320 diagnostic scripts publish their
  existing wave prerequisite on their actual runner.
- Retire the ENVS entry and remove the completed ledger unit. The sweep
  manifest maps each original requirement to tests and this entry.
- The original ledger recorded promotion using exact gate/mixed outputs,
  logits/state/KV and full normal-model trajectories, with positive prefill
  and request-wall results. It also recorded small-row order reversals and
  explicitly excluded them from small-row promotion. Those historical claims
  are not refreshed here: the registered composite and its original scope
  remain unchanged, with the parent composite and unfused chain intact.
- Reaffirm the three unchanged DP4A layer-list graph-key reads as NOT-DEBT:
  forwarding changed the enclosing function fingerprint, not their capture
  identity purpose. Separate DP4A selector cleanup stays open.
- Unrelated dirty audit decisions were left untouched and unstaged. Subagent
  launch returned a storage error twice, including without parent context;
  no new child appeared in the run listing. Implementation proceeded locally.
