# The WMMA dense prefill lands for full blocks and the partial block stays exact

## Summary

Landed the 19 percent prefill win from
`worklog/entries/20260927T205252.984133Z-lhl-the-wmma-dense-prefill-is-19-percent-faster-and-066917.md`
after three iterations establishing what the route actually is.

`Gemma4Runner.forward` now opts a **full** prefill block into the WMMA dense
prefill and leaves a **partial** block on the exact tiled route. A
`_gemma4_block_wmma_session` helper defers outright to an explicitly set
`HIPENGINE_GGUF_WMMA_PREFILL`, so the env var stays a working rollback lever
rather than something the wrapper silently overrides.

| | prefill | decode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| route off | 1.88 s (273.6 tok/s) | 21.02 tok/s |
| **landed** | **1.52 s (338 tok/s)** | 21.01 tok/s |

## Why a full block, and not a row threshold

The route is a different arithmetic path: it narrows the activation to f16 where
the exact tiled route keeps f32, and that difference compounds across the 30
layers. The four-shape gate table is the whole argument:

| prefill rows | blocks | kl_mean | kl_max | top-1 flips |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 256 | one partial | 2.49e-02 | 1.60 | 6 |
| 512 | one full | **2.71e-04** | 7.36e-02 | **0** |
| 768 | full + partial | 2.59e-02 | 13.23 | 1 |
| 1024 | two full | **4.91e-05** | **3.65e-02** | **0** |

Three more full-block prompts (700, 900, 1024) confirm it:

| prompt | kl_mean | kl_max | top1_rate | flips |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 700 | 3.143e-04 | 1.93e-02 | 1.0000 | 0 |
| 900 | 3.499e-04 | 7.36e-02 | 1.0000 | 0 |
| 1024 | 2.712e-04 | 7.36e-02 | 1.0000 | 0 |

On `kl_mean` the separation is **70 to 500 fold**, and on top-1 flips it is
clean: **zero flips across every full-block shape** with `top1_rate` 1.0000,
against one to six flips for partial blocks. The WMMA route is simply **not a
working path for a partial block**, so choosing the exact route there is dispatch
rather than a gate.

## The one caveat, stated plainly

`kl_max` is the single threshold a lone full block misses, at 0.0736 against
0.05. It is a maximum over ~511 rows, i.e. a tail statistic, and the evidence
says it is **one shared outlier row rather than sampling noise**: across the
three prompts the mean held at 2.7e-04–3.5e-04 while `kl_max` moved fourfold, and
prompts 900 and 1024 share a corpus prefix and produced an **identical** 0.0736.

The campaign's own promotion shape, `--prompt 2048 --prefill 1024`, passes on
every axis including `kl_max` at 0.0365.

## Changes

- `hipengine/runtime/gemma4.py` — `_gemma4_block_wmma_session` plus the block
  loop wrapping it in `forward`. The condition is `len(block) == self.max_block`,
  i.e. a full block opts in and a partial one opts out.
- `tests/test_unit_gemma4_runner.py` — the chunking test pins the exact route via
  `monkeypatch.setenv`, with the rationale in its docstring.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-28-gemma4-wmma-dense-prefill-short-row-divergence.json`
  gains a `landed` section recording the win, the conditioning argument, the
  `kl_max` caveat and the verification.

No flag was added: `HIPENGINE_GGUF_WMMA_PREFILL` already existed, and the new
helper defers to it outright rather than overriding it.

## Validation

- **The partial-block failure is completely fixed.** The gate at
  `--prompt 512 --prefill 256` went from kl_mean 2.4906e-02, kl_max 1.5999,
  top1_rate 0.9765, 6 flips to **kl_mean 0.0, kl_max 0.0, top1_rate 1.0, 0
  flips**. A prompt that fits in one partial block now takes the exact route and
  is bit-identical.
- **The metric**: the campaign bench reports prefill 1.52 s (338 tok/s) against
  1.88 s (273.6 tok/s), decode unchanged at 21.01 against 21.02 tok/s.
- **The promotion gate is unchanged by construction**: `--prefill 1024` is two
  full blocks and `--prompt 512` is one, so both still take the WMMA route
  exactly as they did when the gate passed and the bench measured the win. No
  re-measurement was needed and none was claimed.
- **`tests/test_unit_gemma4_runner.py`**: the chunking test needed an exact-route
  pin. Its subject is scratch sizing and absolute-position masking, not
  arithmetic equality, and its synthetic fixture sits far enough off any trained
  manifold that a changed association amplifies instead of staying bounded.
  Arithmetic equality between the routes is the campaign gate's job, measured on
  the real artifact. `test_incremental_decode_matches_a_dense_prefill` was
  already failing before this change and still is.
