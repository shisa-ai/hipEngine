# W7900: the d1 packets do have observability, they just predate the grouping counter

## Summary

Two sentences in `20260830T042451...-7aab29` (written ~20 minutes earlier, same session) are wrong and
this supersedes them. It claimed the `he-d1-*` packets "carry no `resident_observability` block at
all" and called them d1 packets. They are 80-cell C1-C8 matrices - the directory names say `d1`, the
contents sweep widths 1-8 x 10 prompts - and every cell *does* carry
`resident_observability.routes` with `counts`, `fallback_reasons`, `last_physical_group_plan` and
`recent_completed`. The real reason the grouping counter is absent from them is commit lineage:
`native_full_prefill_groups` was added by `eb17bb589` at 12:09, and `he-d1-cap8` was produced by
`982e01cad` at 12:02. A packet cannot contain a counter that does not exist yet.

## Changes

Nothing but this note. No artifact needed correcting: the sentence was in prose, and the corrected
counter table in `7aab29` reads the C1-C8 packet (`13fa01e50`, 12:13, after the counter landed), which
is the right source either way.

## Validation

Read directly from the packets, `cells[0].ar.resident_observability` key set:

| packet | producer commit (time) | widths | routes block | grouping counter in counts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| /tmp/he-d1-cap8 | 982e01cad (12:02) | 1-8 | present | absent, predates eb17bb589 |
| /tmp/he-d1-post | 97a0b8c4a (11:31) | 1-8 | present | absent, predates eb17bb589 |
| /tmp/he-c1c8-cap8 | 13fa01e50 (12:13) | 1-8 | present | present, 0 at C1 rising to 151-176 at C8 |

So the lesson generalizes past this counter: before concluding a packet is missing telemetry, diff the
producing commit recorded in its `source.commit` against the commit that introduced the field. Both of
today's "missing telemetry" scares were lineage, and one of my two wrong sentences came from reading
the first place keys appear (`telemetry.route_counters`) instead of the place they live.

Separately, `/tmp/he-d1-post` carries `status: failed, passed: false`, which looks alarming and is not
evidence of a regression. Its verdict comes from `mtp_engaged: false` in 80 of 80 cells while the MTP
arm has a rate in 80 of 80; all three equality gates (`ar_self_exact`, `mtp_self_exact`,
`ar_mtp_equal`) pass in all 80 cells and `correctness.passed` is true in all 80. It is an
expectation failure - the run was supposed to show MTP engaging and did not - on a 11:31 build, and no
committed artifact, README row or changelog line cites it. It is not an MTP measurement either: a
packet that reports no engagement cannot support an acceptance or speedup claim in either direction,
which is the same reason the K3 rows are drawn from the promote-final packet rather than this one.

## Next

- If grouping ever needs to be shown at C1 specifically, re-run the d1-style script on any build at or
  after `eb17bb589`; the counter will be there. Do not conclude from these three packets that grouping
  is unobservable at C1 - it is observable and is 0 there by definition, one request cannot group.
- Someone should decide whether a packet whose only failing gate is `mtp_engaged` should be marked
  `status: failed` at all, or `status: complete` with a failed expectation. The current encoding makes
  an unengaged MTP run and a broken run look identical in the field everyone reads first.
