# QSA head-tile row-scale screen

## Summary

The QSA head-tile result changes with row count. At rows=256 and selected
stride/count 2051/2050, the shipped head-quad variant measured 5.7291 ms,
head-pair 6.2360 ms, and head-1 14.1886 ms. Outputs remained bit-identical.
Thus head-pair was faster at the small rows=32 screen but 8.8% slower at the
larger representative screen. The tile choice is shape-dependent; no global
replacement is justified.

## Changes

- Added `benchmarks/results/2026-10-12-qwen4exp-cross-engine-gap-qsa-head-tile-rows256.json`.
- Preserved the synthetic/non-claim status and exact geometry in the artifact.

## Validation

- The run completed under TheRock ROCm 10/gfx1151.
- All three variants were bit-identical.
- The result was measured with one warmup and three repetitions.

## Next

Compare this shape-dependent behavior with the external attention launch
geometry and continue with stage-level QSA attribution rather than promoting a
single head tile.
