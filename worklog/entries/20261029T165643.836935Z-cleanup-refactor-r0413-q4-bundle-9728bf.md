## Summary

runner instead of writing `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q4_BUNDLE_PREFILL`.
Production stays on, strict stays off. The old exact grouped owner remains
registered and selectable through an exception-safe runner scope. The lead's
accepted prefill/decode tradeoff is unchanged; this is not a new performance
