# PF-1 comparison-basis predeclaration: frozen decision rule

## Summary

The PF-1c blocker asked the profile owner to choose between (a) ratifying a
"whole-model trajectory basis" and (b) restoring a "single-transition
admission basis". Source reading shows the choice is not available: **both
bases are the same basis.** This entry freezes the decision rule *before* the
reconciliation measurement is run, per the
[2026-08-31 accuracy review](../../docs/PRODUCTION-ACCURACY-POLICY-REVIEW-2026-08-31.md)
requirement that controls, metrics, and decision rules be fixed before
candidate identities are re-evaluated. It contains no measurement.

## Established before this entry (source reading only, no new numbers)

1. **The trajectory basis is the calibrated basis.** The harness behind the
   2026-08-16 threshold calibration —
   `scripts/execution_profile_gdn_calibration.py` calling
   `scripts/gguf_gdn_semantic_gate.py:118 _run_teacher_forced_candidate` —
   runs `reset() -> prefill() -> 24 forced steps` with the candidate carrying
   its own KV and recurrent state. `--decode-steps` defaults to 24.
2. **The August-basis harness is durably in-tree, not a /tmp one-off.**
   `scripts/qwen4exp_layer2_profile_gate.py` is a committed Qwen4Exp
   production-profile gate over the same 18-prompt suite; its docstring states
   "18 prompts x prefill-last plus 24 c1 rows" (450 rows) and its
   `_strict_trajectory`/`_candidate_trajectory` use the identical pattern. It
   produced the 2026-08-31 layer-2 rejection.
3. **450 rows = 18 prompts x 25**, matching the accuracy review's scope table
   (code 150 rows, other categories 100). The 2026-08-29 Q8 MMQ artifact
   reports exactly `rows: 450`.
4. Therefore the only material difference between the 2026-08-29 admission
   packets and the 2026-09-03 PF-1 gate runs is the **prompt fixture**, not
   the comparison basis.
5. **The admission suite is 39-71 tokens per prompt** (measured by
   tokenization, `scripts/build_qwen4exp_admission_suite_fixture.py`), while
   `QWEN4EXP_Q8_MMQ_PREFILL_POLICY` admits MMQ only at `rows >= 64` for every
   registered shape
   (`hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q8_0_mmq_prefill.py:104`).
   Decode steps are single-row. Only 2 of 18 prompts reach the threshold.

## Frozen decision rules

**Rule A - binding basis.** The binding comparison basis for Qwen4Exp
production numerics is the existing `EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` section 6/6.3
strict-teacher trajectory: strict supplies teacher tokens, each profile owns
its own KV/recurrent state, 24 decode transitions, at least three repeats.
This is a restatement of normative policy, not a new bar. PF-1c options (a)
and (b) are both withdrawn: (a) would "ratify" what is already binding, and
(b) rests on a single-transition basis that never existed.

**Rule B - route coverage.** A numerical packet is admissible evidence about a
route only if it exercises that route. Every future production-numerics
artifact must record **route-engagement coverage**: the fraction of compared
rows whose trajectory dispatched the candidate route at least once. A packet
whose coverage is below 50% of compared rows is recorded as
`route_vacuous_for_scope` and is binding evidence for **neither** admission
nor rejection of that route.

**Rule C - what the pending reconciliation may conclude.** The 18-prompt
reconciliation run resolves exactly one question, and the verdict is fixed now:

- If incumbent-vs-strict KL is exactly `0.0` on the sub-threshold prompts and
  non-zero only on the two prompts at/above 64 tokens, then the 2026-08-29
  `numerical_gate` block is **route-vacuous** under Rule B, and its
  `kl_mean 1.01e-4 / 450-of-450 top-1 / three bit-exact categories` are
  retired as evidence *for the Q8 MMQ route*. This is a statement about the
  fixture's reach, not a defect claim against the shipped route.
- If sub-threshold prompts show non-zero divergence, the `min_rows` reading in
  point 5 is wrong, this entry's premise fails, and the whole PF-1 basis line
  is re-opened rather than closed.

**Rule D - candidates are not re-judged here.** d4x2 and the P3 shape row are
*not* re-adjudicated by the reconciliation run. Re-judgement requires a
route-covering fixture; the canonical p512/p1024/p4096 fixture is the only one
available that satisfies Rule B, and on it both already failed binding limits.
Their rejections stand. This predeclaration may not be cited to admit them.

**Rule E - underpowered scopes are flagged, not relaxed.** Per-scope verdicts
computed over fewer than 25 rows (the 12-row transition scopes) remain
**binding as implemented** — section 10 forbids moving a budget without an
explicit calibrated policy decision. They are additionally annotated
`underpowered_scope` in artifacts so the recalibration campaign recommended by
the 2026-08-31 review has the evidence it needs. No threshold moves in this
unit.

**Rule F - single-plane d4 is a representation question, not a drift
question.** `gguf_q8_0_mmq_prefill.hip:6,35,342` records that the d4 block
(`float d4[4]` + 128 int8, one FP32 scale per 32 elements, K-major) is a port
of llama.cpp's `block_q8_1_mmq` D4 layout; `d4x2`/`d4x3` add residual
re-quantization passes that llama.cpp does not have. That llama.cpp ships the
single-plane form is a real and relevant datum, but it does not enter the
same-quant implementation-drift envelope: under section 5 an activation
representation change of that magnitude is T3-adjacent. If single-plane is
ever revisited it goes through section 6.2 BF16-relative and section 6.4 task
gates as a declared representation configuration with its own strict fallback
— never through the strict-teacher drift limits, under which it cannot and
should not pass.
