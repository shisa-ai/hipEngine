# Reject Qwen4Exp shared Q8 activation pair

## Summary

Rejected and removed shared activation packing for the production-MMQ shared
expert gate/up pair. The mechanism is bit-exact and reduces the complete
projection-pair GPU window, but it does not improve counterbalanced p508 or
p1024 wall and therefore fails the campaign's explicit complete-role/shape
retention rule.

## Changes

- Reversed the candidate runtime helper, dispatch branch, env binder, tests, and
  refactor trigger from `0c40e2dea`; the committed checkpoint remains history.
- Added compact rejection evidence and updated the P3 campaign/changelog.
- Kept the existing singleton production-MMQ gate/up projections unchanged.

## Validation

- Exactness:
  - mocked contract performs one quantization and two projection/correction/
    risk-reset passes;
  - gfx1151 rows64 K2560/N640 outputs for two distinct raw-Q8 weights are
    F32-bit exact to two singleton production-MMQ launches;
  - both p508 runs and code-p1024 are cross-route/repeat exact.
- Alternating HIP-event rows508 complete gate/up projection pair:
  `1.134→1.077 ms` (`1.0525x`); host submission also falls 0.0463→0.0362 ms.
- Whole model:
  - ten combined p508 pairs: `0.998807x`, 95% CI
    `[0.997112,1.000503]`;
  - code-p1024: `0.999726x`, 95% CI `[0.998049,1.001403]`.
- Candidate source patch is hash-pinned in the artifact and reversed cleanly.

## Next

Pursue a larger operation-complete shared gate/up+SiLU or shared
down+sigmoid-gate+combine boundary; do not reintroduce the pack-only route.
