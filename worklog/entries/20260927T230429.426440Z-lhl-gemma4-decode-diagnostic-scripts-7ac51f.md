# the decode diagnostics that produced the census and the pack8 route

## Summary

Three diagnostic probes were written while attributing the decode step and were
still untracked. They are the instruments behind the decode findings that are
already committed, so the evidence chain did not resolve without them. This unit
commits them unchanged.

## Changes

- `scripts/gemma4_gemv_route_probe.py` (115 lines): counts which raw GGUF
  K-quant GEMV symbols the decode path actually launches. `launch_gguf_linear`
  applies `_pack8_decode_dispatch` before resolving, so the *contract* variant
  (`gemv_bf16_bf16_out`) is not necessarily the kernel that runs; this wraps
  the one choke point every Q8_0/Q5_K/Q6_K GEMV goes through and reports what
  actually launched. It is what established that the gemma4 decode expert
  projections take the selected family rather than a pack8 one.
- `scripts/gemma4_expert_selected_bw.py` (197 lines): times the Q4_K gate/up
  and Q5_1 down selected GEMVs at the artifact's own shapes and reports achieved
  weight-read bandwidth, so the expert share of decode has a per-kernel starting
  point. It is the source of the 410 -> 290 us pack8 comparison.
- `scripts/gemma4_dense_q8_0_decode_bw.py` (186 lines): the same for the dense
  Q8_0 decode GEMV at the artifact's six dense shapes plus the lm head.

No production code changed. All three are diagnostic only: they time kernels and
count launches, and none is reachable from a user surface.
