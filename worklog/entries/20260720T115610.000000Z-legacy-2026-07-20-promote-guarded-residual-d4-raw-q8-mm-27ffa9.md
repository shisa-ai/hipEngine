## Changes


- Task #27 corrected its source premise before implementation. Same-model qwen-kernel evidence is roughly 829 tok/s baseline / 1,153 tok/s grouped on XTX, not the 2.6–2.9k path; those larger same-Q3 rows belong to llama.cpp HIP/Vulkan. Registered the clean pinned llama.cpp HIP `1ebf790cda38d827559548f67b0469189690cc8c` lineage for `ggml-cuda/{quantize.cu,mmq.cuh,mma.cuh}` in `docs/source_lineage.json`; both focused drift checks report zero changed/dirty sources. The changed premise is llama.cpp's D4 `block_q8_1_mmq` activation layout plus padded raw-Q8_0 128x128 K256 integer-MMQ dataflow, not another exact 8x8/32x2/scale-broadcast schedule.
- RED first imported a missing `gguf_q8_0_mmq_prefill` module; the all-queued correction test then defined the exact output boundary. Source-faithful one-pass MMQ was 73–89% faster at 4K production projection shapes but failed all-258-projection model quality (`KL 6.76`, top-1 changed). A tail-only policy remained fragile: even tail-1 failed one of 18 text workloads after eight decode steps (`KL max 0.09854`). Unguarded residual D4x2/D4x3 also changed full-model logits. The D4x3 audit localized only about 0.0239% BF16 projection outputs to rounding-boundary mismatches, motivating a sparse exact-repair queue rather than a layer heuristic.
- Added `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q8_0_mmq_prefill.{hip,py}` and registry-driven runtime integration. BF16 activations are represented by three serial residual D4 planes; the MMQ leaf emits BF16 and queues outputs within absolute `1e-5` of a BF16 rounding boundary; a 128-thread exact raw-Q8 reduction recomputes only queued indices. The quant-axis policy admits `(K,N)=(2048,8192)` from 32 rows and `(2048,4096)` from 48 rows through 4,096. Boundary repair made `(4096,2048)`, short-K width-2048, and width-512 shapes slower, so they retain exact 16x4/8x4/8x2/pack8 fallbacks. Session pointers are context-local and the risk counter clears asynchronously on the launch stream. No backend/quant conditional, weight sidecar, duplicate resident weights, or host synchronization was added.
- Threshold and quality gates are explicit. `3e-6` failed 14/18 text workloads and 4K (`KL 0.905857`, top-1 failed); a later wide-only `6e-6` screen still failed `explain_concept_rot` (`KL max 0.434622`, top-1 4/9). Both are rejected. The actual `1e-5` default passes all 18 fixture-derived/rotated text workloads across prefill plus eight decode steps: all 162 full-logit positions are bit-exact, KL max `0`, top-1 minimum `1.0`, and max logit difference `0`. Final GPU1 tests report `19 passed` for the primitive/policy module, `44 passed` for dispatch, and `2 passed` for the real Q3 mixed-64/all-row/full-logit module. `python3 scripts/check_fixtures.py` passes all seven CPU-reference fixtures; focused Ruff, compileall, JSON parsing, and `git diff --check` pass.
- Final post-hardening matched GPU1 wall gates are positive and exact: mixed-pattern 512 moves `760.411 -> 837.417 tok/s` (+10.13%) and mixed-pattern 4K moves `743.906 -> 831.393` (+11.76%). The official cached command with one warmup gives five-run repeated-512 median `848.543 tok/s` (0.22% stdev, +9.60% vs retained 774.185) and three-run repeated-4K median `828.003 tok/s` (0.13% stdev); matched mixed 4K is +12.17% versus retained 741.180. Earlier same-source repeated/mixed pairs were also all positive, but the rollup uses these final context-local/same-stream-clear measurements. GPU0/W7900 was untouched and remains unverified.
- Final cache-only selected-region trace `/tmp/q3-mmq-post-async-4k-profile-20260720-204755/q3-mmq-post-async-4k_kernel_trace.csv` records 250 each of residual quantize, guarded MMQ, and sparse exact correction. They total `5.685/602.924/207.578 ms`; 540 exact-Q8 fallbacks total `753.045 ms`, moving dense Q8 `2,052.066 -> 1,569.232 ms` (-23.53%). Total kernel sum falls `5,350.508 -> 4,815.413 ms` (-10.00%) and span `5,534.073 -> 4,973.718 ms` (-10.13%). MMQ is local256/VGPR216/dynamic-LDS57,856B/scratch0; quantizer and correction are VGPR24/scratch0. D4x3 reuses `bulk_prefill_scratch.linear_qkv_f32`; only the bounded int32 queue persists, adding exactly 16/128 MiB and moving tracked peak to `15.821/17.080 GiB` at 512/4K.
- Updated the kernel catalog, Q3/GGUF status docs, refactor ledger, benchmark rollup/changelog, and `benchmarks/results/2026-07-20-gpu1-q3-guarded-d4x3-mmq-prefill.json`. Task #27's first changed-algebra tranche is complete, but ~3,000 tok/s remains explicitly unmet; do not weaken `1e-5` or re-admit losing shapes. The independent roadmap now proceeds to #28 row-shaped GGUF target execution, while any future prefill reopening needs another genuinely new algebra/layout premise and its own memory/correctness contract.
