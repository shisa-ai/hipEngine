# DFlash2 economics attribution correction: MTP B3 acceptance is 2.85 tokens/cycle, not 3.85 verify rows

## Summary

A review of the DFlash2 campaign's conclusions found that the central MTP
number in the Economics record was a misread of the comparator artifact, and
that two supporting mechanisms were unsupported. The campaign **decision is
unchanged** — DFlash2 is not promoted on gfx1151, best point B3 8.85 tok/s =
0.66x AR against exact MTP B3's 1.7845x — but the recorded *reason* was wrong
and is corrected here. No measured value changed; only attribution.

### The misread

`benchmarks/results/2026-08-17-gfx1151-qwen38-27b-q4ks-exact-native-b3.json`,
`b3` block: `cycles` 87, `accepted_draft_tokens` 161, `proposed_draft_tokens`
248, `target_forward_rows` 335, `stage_seconds.target_verify` 9.663 s,
`stage_seconds.proposal` 0.206 s.

- `3.85` = 335/87 = **verify rows per cycle**. The campaign recorded it as MTP's
  accepted tokens per cycle, then divided it by the 4-row block to produce a
  "0.96 per-row acceptance" — the same error twice.
- MTP B3 accepted tokens per cycle = (161 + 87)/87 = **2.851**; per verify row
  = 2.851/3.851 = **0.740**.
- DFlash2 B3 `mean_acceptance` 2.799 is the same quantity (produced tokens per
  cycle including the bonus; `scripts/dflash2_gguf_cycle.py:486`), over 4 fixed
  rows = **0.700** per row.

So the acceptance gap is **~2%, not ~27%**: DFlash2's drafter is at parity with
MTP on this lane. MTP additionally proposes adaptively (248 drafts over 87
cycles = 2.85 of a 3-draft budget), which is why it verifies 3.85 rows instead
of 4 — a mechanism DFlash2 lacks, not a quality difference.

### What the deficit actually is

| | rows/cycle | tokens/cycle | per verify row | verify ms | draft+select ms | cycle ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Exact native MTP B3 | 3.85 | 2.85 | 0.740 | 111 | 2.4 | 118 |
| DFlash2 B3 | 4.00 | 2.80 | 0.700 | 166 | 96 | 264 |

The whole 2.7x is cost: ~96 ms/cycle of drafter+select MTP does not pay, plus
~55 ms of verify MTP does not pay for the same rows.

### Two unsupported mechanisms, withdrawn

1. **"Verify is O(N^2) in block rows (causal block attention)."** Decode here is
   weight-bandwidth-bound and at the roof: Q4_K_M is 17,106,775,008 bytes, one
   sweep at the 221 GB/s practical read (`docs/ROOFLINE-gfx1151.md`) is 77.4 ms,
   and measured AR is 74.6 ms/token (~1.0 sweeps). Sweeps per cycle: MTP 1.52,
   DFlash2 4-row 2.14, DFlash2 8-row **8.01** — one full weight re-read per row.
   The 4->8 row step adds 26 causal query-key pairs over a few hundred KV
   tokens, i.e. microseconds against 620 ms; the attribution is off by ~3 orders
   of magnitude. The real cause is a dispatch admission bound:
   `hipengine/runtime/gguf_linear.py:150,159` cap the dense-BF16 and
   resident-pack8 rowtiles at 4 rows. This is corroborated in-tree by the D4
   rowtile-8 experiment (620 -> 310 ms) that was reverted as AR-divergent on
   `code_lru_cache` and never root-caused.
2. **"The 166 ms verify is shared with MTP, not DFlash2-specific."** False on
   three counts: different target file (`Q4_K_S` 16.12 GB vs `Q4_K_M` 17.11 GB),
   different harness (`qwen36_dense_gguf_suite.py` with reusable target graphs
   vs `dflash2_gguf_suite_smoke.py` per-cycle Python with explicit syncs), and
   DFlash2-only 5-layer hidden-state capture inside the timed region
   (`scripts/dflash2_gguf_cycle.py:417-422`). Token budgets also differ (25 vs
   40).

### Consequence for the "airtight ceiling"

The arithmetic was right, the causality inverted. At DFlash2's own 166 ms
verify, matching MTP B3 needs 23.853 x 0.166 = 3.96 tokens/cycle (above the B3
cap of 4.0) — hence "no operating point". At **MTP's** verify economics the
requirement is 23.853 x 0.111 = **2.65**, and DFlash2 already delivers 2.80.
The bound is set by the verify implementation, not the drafter. The B-sweep's
B3 optimum is likewise optimal only against a verify that re-reads weights per
row above four; with amortization the optimum would move to the deep chain,
which is exactly where DFlash2's longer accept length (3.49 at 8 rows) would
pay.

### Cross-lane reconciliation, downgraded

The 2026-08-20 entry used `~/sm120-tuning/QWEN38-27B.md` (FP8-BLOCK / PRO 6000 /
vLLM) to argue the gfx1151 loss is hardware economics that does not transfer.
That path **does not exist on `strixhalo`**, the host this campaign ran on, and
the repo is not among the declared read-only peers in `AGENTS.md`; it cannot
carry a claim here until re-cited with host identity and a reachable path. Its
second pillar — "hipEngine's MTP is near-cap, a very high bar" — is refuted by
the correction: at 2.85 tokens/cycle hipEngine's MTP B3 is slightly *below* the
cited vLLM MTP3 datapoint (3.04).

### Reproducibility gaps found on this host (2026-08-22)

Not invalidating, but recorded: the drafter snapshot
(`models--z-lab--Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2/snapshots/50307d4c...`), `~/dflash`,
`Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_S.gguf` and `~/sm120-tuning/` are all absent. All three
DFlash2 artifacts also carried `"gpu": "AMD Radeon Pro W7900-compute-lane
(gfx1151 resident)"` — the wrong machine (W7900 is gfx1100) — where every other
artifact on this host says `"AMD Radeon 8060S Graphics"`.

Process lesson worth carrying: a derived metric read out of an artifact counter
must be checked against the counter's definition before it becomes load-bearing.
`target_forward_rows` and accepted tokens are both ~4 at B3, which is precisely
why the misread survived four worklog entries and two doc consolidations.
