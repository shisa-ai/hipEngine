# DE-1 layer bisection: the warp256 gate/up flip localizes to a 13-layer early group, the 5 excluded layers CREATE it, and a margin figure was misattributed

## Summary

Two results, one of which corrects a claim this worker committed four minutes
earlier.

**1. A margin figure was misattributed, and the correction is recorded here
because the superseded entry is immutable.** Entry
`20260930T022601.837322Z-lhl-qwen4exp-de1-dual-vs-strict-323db5` (commit
`b15bb6bc5`) states that the step-63 flip is "at step 63 of 83 with margin
0.954472". That figure is **the strict teacher's** top-1-vs-top-2 margin, not
the candidate's. The probe derives it from `ref`:

```python
for i in range(ref.shape[0]):
    order = np.argsort(-ref[i])
    step_margins.append(float(ref[i][order[0]] - ref[i][order[1]]))
```

The **substance survives** - the candidate did not jitter across a tie, it
picked a different token on a step the teacher resolved by a wide margin - but
the **attribution was wrong** and is corrected in
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-30-qwen4exp-de1-dual-vs-strict/README.md`,
`docs/QWEN4EXP-OPTIMIZATION-LEDGER.md` (DE-1), and `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`.
The candidate-side evidence for that step is the arm-measured 6.7x KL ratio
(0.261188 vs 0.039213) from `step_kls` / `incumbent_step_kls`, which is sound.

**2. The flip localizes to a 13-layer early group, and the 5 excluded layers
turn out to be load-bearing in the opposite direction from the design intent.**
A 9-tier bisection on the single case that carries the outlier
(`heldout_mixed_review`, 82 steps, `--packet dual --teacher`, both arms against
the strict teacher) splits the dp4a layer scope and reads the top-1 flip:

| tag | layers | n | max_kl | flip? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `t0_none` | (none) | 0 | **0.00000** | no |
| `t1_hi` | [31,48) | 17 | 0.11596 | no |
| `t1_lo` | {0,2,5,6,8,9,10,11} ∪ [13,31) | 26 | 0.24654 | **YES** |
| `t2_a` | {0,2,5,6,8,9,10,11} ∪ [13,22) | 17 | 0.13123 | **YES** |
| `t2_b` | [22,31) | 9 | 0.25861 | no |
| `t3_a` | {0,2,5,6,8,9,10,11} ∪ [13,18) | 13 | 0.19748 | **YES** |
| `t3_b` | [18,22) | 4 | 0.05319 | no |
| `t_all48` | all | 48 | 0.10379 | no |

The three flip carriers intersect at exactly
**`{0,2,5,6,8,9,10,11} ∪ [13,18)` = 13 layers**, and every set disjoint from it
is clean.

`t0_none` is a **perfect control**: with dp4a on no layer both arms are the
exact chain, and it measures `max_kl = 0.00000` with zero flips. That confirms
the harness isolates the dp4a/warp route and nothing else.

**The counter-intuitive result is `t_all48`.** The production scope (43 layers)
carries the flip, and adding back the 5 layers it excludes (1,3,4,7,12) removes
it. So the exclusion set is not protecting against the flip - it is what
produces it. This is the opposite of the intent recorded for that exclusion.

**A second finding, which limits how the bisection may be used:** `max_kl` is
**not monotone** in the layer set (0.00000 at n=0, 0.05319 at n=4, 0.25861 at
n=9, 0.19748 at n=13, 0.11596/0.13123 at n=17, 0.24654 at n=26, 0.10379 at
n=48). The 9-layer `t2_b` reproduces nearly the full 0.26 magnitude with **no
flip at all**, and the 48-layer arm has a *lower* max than either. So the
max-KL outlier does **not** localize by layer-set bisection; only the top-1
flip does. Any claim of the form "layer L carries the max-KL tail" is not
supported by this evidence.

## Validation

- 9 bisection tiers, each `--packet dual --teacher` on
  `heldout_mixed_review` at 82 decode steps against the frozen strict-teacher
  `.npz` (`/tmp/r11-18case-strict-teacher.npz`), artifacts under
  `/tmp/r11-layer-bisect/`.
- The control is exact: `t0_none` gives `max_kl = 0.00000` and zero flips.
- Layer lists were read back from each artifact's own
  `dp4a_layers_override` field rather than from the driver, so the table above
  is what ran, not what was intended.
- **Trap confirmed while setting up the bisection:** the runner tests
  `(not dp4a_layers or gate_layer in dp4a_layers)`, so an **empty**
  `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q4_DP4A64_LAYERS` means *no filter* (every layer), not
  *none*. Expressing "none" as `""` enables dp4a on all 48 layers and measures
  incumbent max KL **0.19363** vs strict, **3.2x** the 43-layer scope's 0.06017.
  `--dp4a-layers none` now means no layer.
- Limits: one model, one quant, one case, one host (gfx1151, not the gfx1100
  default hardware), one profile. The bisection is a **single-case**
  localization; it does not establish the full-suite envelope at any scope, and
  a scope that is flip-clean on this case is not thereby admissible.
