# Confirmed: the wmma gate measures a decode-path side effect, not the prefill route

## Summary

The prediction from the previous entry is confirmed, and the finding is larger
than the discrepancy that led to it.

## The isolated measurement

`artifact-canonical-codep512-d1.json`: a **single** canonical case
(`code-p512`) for **one** decode step, so each row is one row and nothing is
averaged.

```
strict_logits_sha256    = 93042f1a4d162b7568a2...
candidate_logits_sha256 = 1b4e5c0fa5d3bd266c4a...    (different)
```

| row | rows | mean KL | max |
|---|---|---|---|
| `shape=prefill_last` / `transition=prefill_to_c1` | 1 | **5.8014e-12** | 5.8e-12 |
| `shape=c1` / `transition=steady` | 1 | **1.0184e-04** | 1.02e-4 |

The post-prefill output is identical to f32 precision; the entire drift sits on
the single decode step.

**The prediction was made before the run**, which is what makes this evidence
rather than a story fitted afterwards: if the arms differ in decode-path
selectors rather than in the WMMA prefill route, the prefill row must be flat and
the decode row must not be.

## What it means

The canonical `wmma` gate does not measure a prefill route. Its recorded
`candidate_chain` is `wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out` and its surface is
`post-binder HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_WMMA_LAYERS`, but the quantity that moves is
reached through `use_wmma` on the decode path - `f_rowtile`, the rows-2-8 native
batch-decode session, `use_rowtile`. The prefill route contributes nothing
measurable here.

So **the 2026-09-16 certification and its bit-identical HEAD re-run measure a
decode-path side effect.** They are reproducible and not meaningless, but they
are not evidence about the WMMA prefill route, and every artifact says they are.

## Open, with a named explanation and a test

The heldout run shows the **opposite** pattern:

| run | prefill row | decode rows |
|---|---|---|
| canonical (512 tok, 1 or 128 steps) | 5.8e-12 | 1.02e-04 |
| heldout (39-71 tok, 24 steps) | 2.06179e-03 | 1.99214e-04 |

The heldout prefill moves **ten times more** than its decode rows. No decode-path
effect can produce that, so something engages on the heldout prefill that does
not engage on the canonical one - despite both having `rows > 1` and the same
`in_features`.

Candidate explanation, **unverified**: dispatch order.
`_dispatch_can_use_wmma_prefill` requires the variant to start with `prefill_`,
and the exact leaf `coltile8_rowbatch4_f32_f32_out` fails that at every row
count. If the canonical prefill arrives carrying the coltile leaf and the heldout
prefill arrives carrying a `prefill_*` variant, the route engages in one and not
the other.

The test: record the variant the dispatch actually carries at
`_wmma_prefill_dispatch` for both protocols. Neither run does that today, which
is why this is stated as open rather than resolved.
