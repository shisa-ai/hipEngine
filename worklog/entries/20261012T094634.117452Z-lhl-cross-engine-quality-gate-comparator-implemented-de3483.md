# Cross-engine quality-gate comparator implemented and self-checked

## Summary

`scripts/cross_engine_quality_gate.py` implements the G0-G4 characterization
ladder of docs/CROSS-ENGINE-PROTOCOL.md over the shared task battery, with the
hipEngine strict profile as the G1/G2/G4 teacher. The machinery is
unit-tested and validated end-to-end by a live strict-teacher battery run and
a self-score.

## Decisions

- The battery is the committed 18-prompt set (mtpbench suite 10 + GDN
  category heldouts 8), rendered with the canonical material recipe
  (`[<id>]\n<role>: <content>` sections, no chat template); the recipe string
  is recorded in every artifact and must match before scoring. Free
  trajectories are exactly 32 greedy steps with EOS fed back, matching the
  internal layer2 battery protocol.
- Comparison bases: exact token ids for hipEngine, llama.cpp-family
  (n_probs top-N), and kyojin (return_token_ids); **decoded text is the
  primary basis for gufo** because re-encoded ids are merge-unreliable at BPE
  boundaries (the general_ja-p1024 lesson). G1 reports per-prompt
  first-divergence position (char index on the text basis).
- G2 is measured only on aligned common-prefix positions: engine top-k rows
  are comparable to teacher top-k rows only while the engine's greedy ids
  have matched the teacher so far. Metrics: top-1 agreement and KL over the
  union of the two top-20 lists, renormalized (logprob-or-prob tolerant
  parsing across llama.cpp fork schemas). Engines without a logprob surface
  record `available: false` (gufo by design, per the 2026-10-04 finding).
- A provenance gate mirrors the canonical compare: an engine missing a
  battery prompt must declare it disqualified with a reason, else scoring
  refuses the silent drop. G4 verdict is `characterized` only at full
  coverage, `partial_coverage` otherwise.
- hipEngine-mode artifacts record strict/production profile, register the
  builtin profile plans explicitly (registration is not an import side
  effect), and capture top-20 logprobs on the first repeat only.
