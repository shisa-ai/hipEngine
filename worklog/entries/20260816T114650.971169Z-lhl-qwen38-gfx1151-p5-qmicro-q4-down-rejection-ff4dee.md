# Reject qmicro Q4 dense down ownership

## Summary

Rejected sole qmicro-Q4 ownership for the 32 Q4 dense FFN-down weights. The
candidate saves 44,564,480 tracked/resident bytes and its actual-weight c1 and
native-row residual leaves are exact and positive, but the complete 512/128
production graph regresses both decode and prefill in an A/B/A gate.

## Changes

- Implemented and RED/GREEN-tested direct compact-metadata c1/rows2-4 residual
  producers, direct shared-B WMMA below 4K, and bounded 4K metadata expansion.
- Scoped materialization to only gfx1151 Qwen3.8 H5120/N5120/K17408 Q4
  `layers.*.ffn_down` tensors, with standard-T16 fallbacks for all misses.
- Removed the complete transient implementation and tests after the request
  gate failed; only this decision entry, the compact rejection artifact, and
  campaign history remain.

## Validation

- Nineteen focused planning, backend-capability, real-model materialization,
  dispatch, c1/native residual, and cooperative-WMMA checks passed. Synthetic
  and actual-weight outputs were BF16-bit exact.
- Actual Q4 down layers 8/32/47 improve fused c1 residual 1.0047-1.0065x; rows
  1/2/3/4 aggregate leaf speedups are 1.0072x/1.0081x/1.0124x/1.0076x.
- Complete 512/128 production A/B/A: candidate decode medians 12.43310 and
  12.45127 tok/s versus clean control 12.45720; candidate mean is 12.44219
  (-0.1205%). Candidate prefill mean is 399.4079 versus 399.8383 (-0.1076%).
  All final token IDs are exact, outputs finite, teardown zero, and candidate
  tracked peak is 42.5 MiB lower.
- Evidence:
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-16-gfx1151-qwen38-27b-q4-qmicro-down-rejected.json`.
