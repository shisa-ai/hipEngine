# Correct the flash lookahead defect's provenance: in-unit regression, not shipped

## Summary

Corrects a claim in
`worklog/entries/20261008T162238.312050Z-lhl-gemma4-gemma4-flash-global-decode-900c2e.md`
and the sentence that repeated it in `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md` (since edited).
Both said the flash kernel's V-pipeline lookahead defect "was live before this
unit started" and had been "corrupting every flash result from the third slice
row onward on the short-context decode route that is still shipped". **That is
false.** The defect was introduced by this unit's own perf ladder and fixed
inside the same unit; no shipped or pushed state ever carried it.

What the history actually shows:

| commit | date | state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `a0fc5b16a` | 2026-09-30 | ships the sliding-layer flash route with a **correct** 2-deep pipeline (`jn = jj + 2` is right for a 2-deep pipe) |
| `37ba3b3da` | 2026-10-08 09:06 | "deepen Gemma flash V pipeline": pipe goes 3-deep, lookahead left at `jj + 2` → **defect introduced**, rows from `jj == 3` accumulate against the previous row's weight |
| `308c4c38c` | 2026-10-08 10:22 | still carries it |
| `b9e0f3f0d` | 2026-10-09 01:53 | fixes the lookahead to `jj + 3` and promotes the global route |

`git log -S "jn = jj + 2"` lists only `a0fc5b16a` (add) and `b9e0f3f0d` (remove),
which is what made the defect look older than it is: `37ba3b3da` changed the
pipeline depth without touching that string. The commit range is what settles
it, and so does the artifact record:

- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-30-gemma4-d2-flash-gate.json` and
  `...-d4-flash-gate.json` name their candidate's
  `attention_source_sha256.hip = e352656b7718657ac9f35e59517b991d474d4c02eaf4b2d093e21b09be43f922`,
  which is byte-for-byte `a0fc5b16a:...gemma4_attention.hip` — the correct
  kernel. Their baseline's `3fb6a454...` is `7ddffcfa6`, the pre-route kernel.
- The 2026-10-08 promotion gate's candidate is
  `0739b640...` = `39cf7af2b` = the **fixed** kernel, and its baseline
  `d5ef7906...` is the `308c4c38c` worktree carrying only the lookahead fix.
  Both verdicts therefore rest on correct arithmetic.
- The branch is 70 commits ahead of `origin/gemma4`; none of this work is
  pushed.

So the defect lived in exactly two unpushed commits. It was caught by the flash
battery, which is what the unit's own record shows: `tests/test_gpu_gemma4_flash_decode.py`
went 44 failed / 16 passed → 60 passed across the fix. The battery exists at
`a0fc5b16a` (added with the route) and at `37ba3b3da`, so the regression was
in-unit and detected in-unit.

## The shipped sliding-layer route does have a gate

Task 52 asked whether the shipped short-context flash route has ever been
gated. It has. The route's own unit
(`worklog/entries/20260930T064925.880868Z-lhl-performance-959d7b.md`, punchlist
row D2) froze a baseline at `--prompt 2048 --prefill 1024` — 1023 rows, scored
keys 1025-2047 — and stated its contract explicitly: "agreement to float
precision vs the single kernel in the unit battery, production KL/top-1 bars at
`scripts/gemma4_teacher_forced_gate.py gate`". The `d2` and `d4` artifacts are
that contract met, against the **two-phase split** the route replaced:

| artifact | mean KL | max KL | top-1 flips | rows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `d2` | 7.75e-6 | 6.70e-3 | 0 | 1023 |
| `d4` | 5.16e-6 | 3.22e-3 | 0 | 1023 |

Limits are mean 1e-3 and max 5e-2, so both clear the bars by two to three orders
of magnitude. `promotion_qualified: False` in those artifacts is a constant the
evaluator writes for every verdict (`scripts/gemma4_teacher_forced_gate.py:255`,
"Category/task/isolation gates are separate"), not a finding.

What had never been measured is the **cumulative** question: the production
short-context decode route set against the *exact* chain. That measurement is
`worklog/entries/20261008T174856.609217Z-lhl-gemma4-gemma4-short-route-vs-exact-f6ba87.md`
and it does not pass: mean KL 5.38e-3 against a 1e-3 bar.
