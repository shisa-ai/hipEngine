### The two normative findings are now in the contract

The closure records carry the two constraints that any later optimization must
respect, because they were previously only in worklog entries and `docs/REFACTOR.md`:

1. **`chain_exact` is necessary but not sufficient** -- the `threads=64` decode gate+up
   change was 3.8x on that kernel, moved 5 of 17920 outputs by one ulp, kept
   `chain_exact` true and the suite green at 126 passed, and still dropped the ten-seed
   suite from 58/60 to 57/60.
2. **The speed target is not met** -- RTF 1.201 vs 1.336 is ~1.11x, not the 2-10x the
   objective anticipated, and zeroing the entire LM stage yields only 0.77 RTF ~=
   1.74x, so no single-stage optimization reaches 2x.

Recording (2) inside the contract is deliberate: it is the one objective clause that is
not satisfied, and a reader of the document should not have to reconstruct that from
worklog entries.
