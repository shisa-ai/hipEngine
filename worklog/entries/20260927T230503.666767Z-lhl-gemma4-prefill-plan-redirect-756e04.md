# the gfx1151 prefill attribution redirects the prefill plan to attention

## Summary

`docs/campaigns/GEMMA4-26B-A4B-OPTIMIZATION.md` had two gfx1151 prefill status
sections, both of which are about the *grouped expert routes* — they record what
was fixed, not what remains. Neither carries a gfx1151 route attribution, so the
remaining prefill gap had no target. This unit adds one and records what it
changes.

At 2048 tokens over a 7.536 s / 271.8 tok/s baseline: **routed experts 40.7%
(3.070 s) and prefill attention 30.5% (2.295 s)**. The router, the normalization
and rotary chain, and the MoE elementwise chain each save nothing measurable.

Two things follow, and both are in the section:

1. **Attention is the better-scoped target.** It is one kernel, its geometry is
   fixed by the artifact, and its mechanism is a missing query tile rather than a
   missing route. Isolated, it runs at 286 to 363 GFLOP/s — under 1.3% of this
   part's fp32 FMA peak — because the grid is `tokens * num_heads`, one block per
   query row, so K and V are read once per query row instead of once per tile.
2. **The dense share of prefill is unmeasured, not zero**, and the section says
   so. The `no_dense_q8` and `no_all_gemv` arms both save about -1%, which cannot
   be right: skipping every GEMV cannot save less than skipping the experts alone
   at 40.7%. The arms hook funnels the prefill leaves do not use. Recording a
   wrong arm as a zero share would have been the more damaging error.
