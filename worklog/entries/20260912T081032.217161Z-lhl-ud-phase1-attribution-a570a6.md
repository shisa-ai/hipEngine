# Attribute the UD MTP deficit to the IQ small-row verifier owner

## Summary

Phase 1 of `docs/UD-GFX1151-OPTIMIZE.md`. The hypothesis is confirmed at the
kernel level: at the B1-B3 verifier shapes, UD's IQ-family quants run the
strict per-row GEMV (`gguf_iq_dense_strict`) because the dense-IQ decode owner
is `rows == 1` and the dense-IQ prefill owner has `min_rows == 8`. Plain's
Q4_K/Q5_K/Q6_K/Q8_0 use the weight-amortized rowtile family and never launch
that kernel.

On physical GPU1 (RX 7900 XTX, gfx1100), the production-matching native target
graph leaf measures:

| Artifact | rows | host ms | kernel ms | kernel share | `gguf_iq_dense_strict` ms/step | % of kernel |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| UD-Q4_K_M | 4 | 67.32 | 62.03 | 0.921 | 31.99 | 51.6% |
| plain-Q4_K_M | 4 | 35.90 | 31.50 | 0.877 | 0.00 | 0.0% |
| UD-Q4_K_S | 4 | 72.25 | 67.06 | 0.928 | 45.63 | 68.0% |
| plain-Q4_K_S | 4 | 34.55 | 29.74 | 0.861 | 0.00 | 0.0% |
| UD-Q4_K_M | 2 | 58.27 | 53.47 | 0.917 | 26.99 | 50.5% |
| plain-Q4_K_M | 2 | 31.62 | 27.36 | 0.865 | 0.00 | 0.0% |

UD/plain at rows 4 is 1.88x (K_M) and 2.09x (K_S) on host wall and 1.97x /
2.25x on kernel time. The paired production artifact reports the same direction
and ordering (UD/plain MTP 0.560x K_M, 0.516x K_S, i.e. plain/UD 1.79x and
1.94x), so the leaf is representative. The static rank-2 MAC mix explains the
ordering: 38.53% of UD-Q4_K_M and 60.61% of UD-Q4_K_S rank-2 MACs land in the
rows 2-7 dead zone, against 0.00% for both plain controls.

Decision gate: **verifier device work dominates** (kernel share 0.861-0.928,
host residual 4.3-5.3 ms/step), there is **no BF16 expansion or repack work** in
the verifier windows, and the loss is **shape-scoped** to the B1-B3 rows. The
selected action is to optimize the responsible kernel family with a
verifier-sibling small-row IQ owner (Phase 2).

## Validation

- **Device identity.** Every run set `HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES=1` with
  `ROCR_VISIBLE_DEVICES` unset and `rocminfo` reported `AMD Radeon RX 7900 XTX`
  (`gfx1100`). Physical GPU0 (W7900) was not touched.
- **Leaf representativeness.** With the production graph bucket the leaf
  reproduces the paired ordering. An earlier arm that used
  `device_accept_commit=False` and `bulk` attention measured UD *faster* than
  plain (110.9 vs 138.4 ms) and was rejected as unrepresentative; it is
  recorded here rather than in the artifact.
- **Kernel-level confirmation.** `gguf_iq_dense_strict` appears 1080 times in
  the 8 UD windows at rows 4 (135 launches/step, 237 us/call, grid up to
  278528, block 128) and zero times in every plain window. Plain's largest
  families are `q4_k_t16_dense_dual_rowtile_silu_gemv` (512 calls, 8.85 ms),
  `q6_k_t16_qmicro_planar_gemv_rowtile_col8` (520, 7.77 ms) and
  `q4_k_t16_dense_rowtile_gemv` (1088, 6.44 ms).
- **No expansion.** No dequantize, expand, requantize, repack, or materialize
  kernel appears inside any measured verifier window.
- **Verifier row cap.** Rows 5-8 raise `rows must be 2, 3, or 4` from the fused
  rounded add/RMSNorm (`hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/fused/gguf_ops.py`) and
  the device accept/commit kernels, with and without
  `--native-device-accept-commit`. The production native caller therefore
  reaches B1-B3 only.
- `python3 -m pytest tests/test_scripts_gguf_mtp_verifier_rocprof.py
  tests/test_scripts_gguf_route_mix.py`: 18 pass.
- `python3 scripts/worklog.py check`: valid.

## Next

- Phase 2: classify every retained prefill owner for verifier transfer. The
  concrete candidate this attribution selects is a rows 2-4 IQ verifier
  sibling: the existing dense-IQ W4A16 prefill owner is bit-exact against the
  strict GEMV on the shipped stack and its `min_rows` floor is a measured
  crossover, so the work is a small-row geometry, not new arithmetic.
- Do not lower `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_PREFILL_POLICY.min_rows` as the fix: the recorded
  crossover sweep measures the prefill owner at 0.59x (2 rows) and 0.68x
  (4 rows) against the strict GEMV on real shapes, and 1.58x at 8 rows. A new
  small-row kernel is required.
- Not captured in this unit: c1/c2/c4/c8 concurrency widths (the census is a
  single-request leaf), rows above 4 (unsupported), and DRAM/L2 traffic
  counters (no memory-counter pass was run).
