# Pair wide Inkling NVFP4 prefill outputs

## Summary

The grouped NVFP4 kernel reuses activation loads across two adjacent output rows for wide aligned matrices. Each row keeps its own weights, ordered accumulator, scale and destination. Narrow or uneven shapes keep one row per wave.

## Changes

- Share the exact one-row and two-row implementations through a compile-time output count. Select two rows only for at least 2,048 outputs aligned to a complete paired workgroup. No registry, route layout, arithmetic, or capability change.
- Extend the exact fixture across the output-width threshold and its alignment condition. The fixture is also valid on the parent because the output contract did not change.
