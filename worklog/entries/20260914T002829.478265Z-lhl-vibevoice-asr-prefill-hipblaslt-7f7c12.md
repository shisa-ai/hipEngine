# VibeVoice prefill via hipBLASLt fp16: faster than torch E2E

## Summary

Prompt-prefill GEMMs of the VibeVoice Qwen2 backbone now route through
hipBLASLt fp16-in/f32-out with persistent fp16 weight copies; hipEngine
is faster than the torch GPU lane end to end. Follow-up to
`20260914T001300.445618Z-lhl-vibevoice-asr-prefill-gemm-probe-862984.md`.

## Changes

- `hipengine/runtime/vibevoice_qwen2.py`: `_prefill_gemm_lt` (per-shape
  hipBLASLt problem + zero-workspace algorithm cache), persistent fp16
  copies of the 6 GEMM weight sets per layer (host-side bf16-value ->
  fp16 conversion, +9.2 GB on the 133 GB unified memory), prefill
  rewiring: q/k/v GEMMs emit f32 directly (drops the bf16 intermediates
  and three bf16_to_f32 casts), o/gate/up/down GEMMs emit f32 with
  bf16_to_fp16/f32_to_fp16 input casts; fixed a pre-existing per-call
  leak of the positions buffer.
- Registered strict fallback: the WMMA/naive dense_prefill chain stays
  in-tree (`_prefill_gemm`) as the registered unfused/strict path.

## Validation

- gfx1151, 11 s real speech, best of 3, two independent runs:
  hipEngine 4.250/4.249 s vs torch GPU 4.320/4.392 s -> 0.98x/0.97x.
  Prefill 146 rows: 0.76 -> ~0.30 s (gate GEMM 8.55 -> 3.91 ms,
  down 5.32 -> 4.05, kv 0.46 -> 0.12, q tie). Decode unchanged at
  66-78 ms/token. hipBLASLt rates 4.6-5.1 TFLOPS vs WMMA 2.3.
- Numerics class unchanged (fp16 operands, fp32 accumulate - the WMMA
  kernel already staged half_t). Greedy chain 16/16 exact vs the torch
  fixture; 10/10 gpu suites; transcript content identical to torch
  (End timestamp 11.0 vs 11.07, benign bf16 divergence).
- Artifact `benchmarks/results/2026-09-14-gfx1151-vibevoice-asr-e2e-faster-than-torch.json`;
  README + CHANGELOG rollups updated.

## Next

- Front-end residual (0.345 vs ~0.30 s torch) and decode floor
  (bandwidth-bound) are the remaining levers; both marginal. Next major
  items per docs/MODEL-VIBEVOICE-ASR.md: batched/longer-audio workloads,
  beam/temperature sampling support, and the multi-speaker head.
