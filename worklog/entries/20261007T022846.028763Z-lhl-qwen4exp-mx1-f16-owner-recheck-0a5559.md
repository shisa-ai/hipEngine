# MX-1 F16 down owner is the fastest down kernel; gate/up stays refuted

## Summary

`MX-1` is the ledger's F16 routed-expert route (Q4_K/Q5_1 decoded to F16,
F16 WMMA, F32 accumulate, no repair pass). Its full route and its gate/up half
were already refuted on numerics (`2026-09-30-qwen4exp-mx1-full-route/gate.json`,
`2026-09-30-qwen4exp-f16-compact-gate-up/gate.json`) and its gate/up half on
performance (`2026-10-04-qwen4exp-moe-f16-compact-owner-ab/gate-up-3arm.json`).
The down half's main-kernel cost was never measured.

This unit re-runs the registered-owner comparison on the current tree and adds
the missing down arm. The gate/up result reproduces; the new down result has
the opposite sign: **the F16 down owner is the fastest registered down owner in
8 of 8 cases, median -59.45% against the A/B's pair2 primary.** The A/B primary
is not the production down owner - production runs the iu8-risk kernel via
`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q51_IU8_EXACT=1` - and against that owner the F16 down is
**-42.29% median** (range -49.54% to -32.71%). So the down half is not why the
full route loses, and a **down-only** F16 variant (F16 down, gate/up left exact)
is the only MX-1 configuration with a favorable main-kernel measurement whose
numerical status is unmeasured.

Nothing is promoted. No default, kernel or dispatch changed. The down-only
variant is diagnostic until it passes the layer2 numerical/task gate.
