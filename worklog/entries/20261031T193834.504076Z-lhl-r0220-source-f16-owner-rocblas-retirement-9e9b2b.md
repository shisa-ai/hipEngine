# Retire the resident source-F16 rocBLAS handle and solution policy

## Summary

`90b1424ee` moved bounded source-F16 prefill onto a K16 consumer that owns its
own FP32 WMMA accumulator and publishes BF16 directly, but left the resident
owner holding a rocBLAS handle and a solution-index policy it no longer read.
This unit removes that resident plumbing. Owner construction now builds only
the dequant library and the transient planes, so the source-F16 route neither
loads rocBLAS nor version-queries a build to decide whether it may run.

The public direct kernel wrapper `_launch_t16_f16_rocblas` keeps its
`rocblas=` and `solution_index=` keyword parameters, and the raw-Q6 diagnostic
producer keeps acquiring rocBLAS and issuing its FP16 GEMM. No kernel, no
registered variant, no arithmetic, no source-F16 scratch plane, aliasing or
in-place rule changed.

## Changes

- `hipengine/runtime/gguf_linear.py`: dropped `T16F16RocblasPrefillSession.rocblas`
  and `.solution_indices_by_gemm_shape`, their `__post_init__` int32/shape
  validation, and the `solution_index()` accessor; the host launch no longer
  forwards `rocblas=`/`solution_index=` to the wrapper. The class docstring now
  states that an owner carries no rocBLAS handle or solution policy.
- `hipengine/runtime/qwen35_gguf_runner.py`: removed the `_t16_f16_rocblas`
  field, its `Rocblas.load()`/`set_workspace(0, 0)` acquisition, the
  version-prefix-gated solution-index read, both owner kwargs, the teardown
  `close()`/synchronize pair and the reset assignment, plus the now-unused
  `Rocblas` import. `_t16_f16_rocblas_prefill_library`, the scratch planes and
  `_gguf_t16_f16_rocblas_prefill_policy` are untouched.
- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/__init__.py`: removed the retired
  `GGUF_T16_F16_ROCBLAS_SOLUTION_VERSION_PREFIX` and
  `GGUF_T16_F16_ROCBLAS_SOLUTION_INDICES` capabilities and their `__all__`
  entries. They had one consumer (the runner block above); gfx1151 never
  defined them.
- Tests: `test_unit_qwen35_gguf_prefill_scratch_liveness.py` gains the
  RED-first owner test and asserts the retired field is absent;
  `test_unit_gguf_source_f16_accumulation.py` gains the raw-Q6 diagnostics
  guard; `test_unit_gguf_linear_dispatch.py`, `test_unit_gguf_source_f16_row_spans.py`,
  `test_unit_gguf_resumable_layer_outer_prefill.py` drop the dead constructor
  kwargs, the solution-index assertions and the `Rocblas` monkeypatch;
  `test_unit_gfx1151_backend.py` keeps the transfer-audit ledger rows as an
  explicit `retired` exemption instead of editing the archived audit document.
- Stale-test repair outside the source set:
  `test_unit_qwen4exp_dense_split_route.py` and
  `test_unit_qwen4exp_dense_wide_dispatch.py` read the dispatch chain from
  `launch_gguf_linear`, which `ac6e4b063` turned into the source-F16
  span-binding wrapper. They now read `_launch_gguf_linear_impl`; their
  assertions are unchanged. The repair restores coverage of the implementation
  behind the source-F16 span-binding wrapper.
