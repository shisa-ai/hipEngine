# Retain default-off W1 Q6 shared4 candidate

## Summary

W0 measured Q6 as the largest R32 family: 515.4 ms and 113.2 GiB fetched per
cycle, or 26.69 resident sweeps. Before writing a new kernel, screened the
existing four-wave shared-weight WMMA bodies already retained for bulk prefill.
They are exactly the B-stationary tile machinery W1/Y1 requires: one workgroup
decodes a Q6 slab into LDS and shares it across four 16-row wave ranges.

The existing bodies are strongly positive at the binding rows. At R32,
standard Q6 K5120/N10240 improves **5.331→0.930 ms (5.73x)** and planar
K17408/N5120 improves **10.340→2.115 ms (4.89x)**. Narrow planar
K5120/N1024 improves only **1.36x**. The bodies are not BF16-bit exact to the
current R8-chunk/direct parents (maximum BF16-value delta 0.0078125), so this is
classified T2 rather than relabeled exact.

A one-canonical-prompt C8/K3/R32 strict-teacher smoke passed hard distribution
and repeat gates over 24 rows: mean/p95/max KL
`5.23e-5/2.11e-4/6.71e-4`, **24/24 top-1**, three deterministic candidate
repeats. The smoke is intentionally measurement-invalid as a production gate;
full canonical+heldout C6/R24 and C8/R32 packets remain required.
