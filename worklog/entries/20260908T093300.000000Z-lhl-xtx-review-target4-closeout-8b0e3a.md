# Review target 4 closed: layer-outer DMS prefill, hidden reuse, 232,448 tokens

## Summary

Review target 4 (adapt the layer-outer shared-BF16-oracle prefill to DMS)
is complete. Three units close it: the layer_outer DMS prefill mode itself
(22ded5522: shared-oracle two-pass prefill, per-layer pack with dense-plane
release, no dense pool), the hidden-plane reuse the task anticipated
(6c6d1ad4a gate + 307e7633f adoption after a byte-identical-logits A/B at
73,728, -723 MiB whole-card), and the capacity ladder (published as
benchmarks/results/2026-09-08-rx7900xtx-dms-int8-hidden-alias-ladder.json).

## Acceptance evidence

- 72K eager-path equality, pre-merge, same card (GPU1): dense_pool and
  layer_outer runs produce identical decode outputs and identical packed
  stores — tokens [8998, 150071, 151733, 200076, 34481, 15303, 17, 15];
  1,393,439,696 store bytes; 2,621,504 live rows; compression 1.799956.
- 72K eager-path equality, post-merge, cross-card: dense_pool on the W7900
  (GPU0) and layer_outer on the XTX (GPU1) produce identical decode outputs
  and identical packed stores — tokens [279, 1534, 557, 40050, 436, 5168,
  11, 852]; same store bytes/rows/compression. This simultaneously
  establishes cross-card gfx1100 determinism at this width (a prerequisite
  recorded in docs/DMS-ANALYSIS.md for the 232K no-evict reference arm).
- 256K-class attempts under the whole-card sampling protocol, fresh process
  per point: 261,888 OOM and 262,144 validation-capped on the two-plane
  route; the alias-era ladder passes 200,704 / 216,064 / 220,672 / 224,256 /
  232,448 (largest passing prompt, 99.75% of card at 62 MiB headroom).
  234,496 hit the wrapper's 2700 s bound with 116.1 MiB headroom remaining
  — recorded as timeout/unqualified, not a pass or an OOM; it may fit.
  No alias-era OOM is established (the 176,128 OOM was two-plane code).
- Full-context quality at 232K remains explicitly out of scope: DMS discards
  history; fitting a prompt does not establish quality. The quality bar and
  ladder are defined in docs/DMS-ANALYSIS.md.

## Findings recorded for follow-up

- GPU0-only layer_outer bug: on the 48 GB W7900 the device-memory-class
  prefill config grows the full-attention layer chunk (4096+) beyond the
  bulk scratch's row-scaled buffers (sized from the row-capped 1024-row
  budget), and for_chunk's positions copy fails closed
  (ValueError: copy size 32768 exceeds device buffer size 8192). Invisible
  on the 24 GB XTX where the two coincide. Blocks the W7900 no-evict
  reference arm until fixed (clamp the layer chunk to the scratch budget or
  size the scratch to the layer chunk in layer_outer mode).
- Speed-kernel merge flips near-tie greedy argmax at some widths: identical
  stores/decisions, different tokens at 73,728 ([8998...] pre-merge vs
  [279...] post-merge) while 139,264 matched. Greedy-token agreement across
  kernel changes is length-dependent; the long-horizon trajectory equality
  task covers this.
- The dense_pool (eager) route at 73,728 no longer fits GPU1 alongside an
  external llama-server (23.86 GiB, launched externally at 18:27 on the
  XTX with the same model at -c 114944 BF16 KV); the equality rerun OOMed
  at weight load. Contention, not a regression.
