# Q8 WMMA prefill layer scopes: recoverable time, coverage, bisect
## Summary

The f16 WMMA dense Q8_0 prefill route at its maximal scope is the **largest
single recoverable-time family measured in this model**, and the scope that
currently passes the numerical envelope captures about a third of it.

On `code-p4096`, gfx1151, fallback median 23.840 s:

| Scope | Median s | Saved s | Speedup | Numerics |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| layers 0-47 | 16.799 | +7.041 | 1.419x | fails mean and p95 marginally |
| layers 32-47 | 21.405 | +2.436 | 1.114x | passes every calibrated gate |

`+7.041 s / 1.419x` is ahead of `Q8_IU8_WMM`'s `+6.614 s / 1.388x`, which held
rank 1 in the 2026-09-16 nine-family ranking. The route was absent from that
ranking because its selector is a layer list rather than one of
`PRODUCTION_ARITHMETIC_RECOVERY_FLAGS`, so nothing had ever measured it. The
numerical boundary between layer 0 and layer 32 is therefore withholding about
**4.6 s**, the largest identified block of unclaimed prefill time in the tree.

This inverts the concern that opened the unit. The worry was that the gate had
certified a route that buys nothing, since `docs/REFACTOR.md` records the
selector as superseded for the named production profile by the MMQ stack. The
route in fact buys more than anything else measured.

### Recoverable time tracks route bytes

`scripts/qwen4exp_q8_route_coverage.py` reads the selector's own filter
(`quant_key == "gguf_q8_0"` plus a `layers.` slot path) off the GGUF index. The
route's candidate set is 499 Q8_0 tensors, 7.68 GiB, all inside blocks. Layers
32-47 own 36.8% of those bytes and recover 34.6% of the time, so the two shares
agree to 2.2 points and the untested boundaries are predictable: 28-47 owns
51.4% (~3.6 s), 24-47 owns 55.2% (~3.9 s).

Two exclusions in that coverage map remove open hypotheses rather than just
describing the model. The MoE experts are Q4_K on 47 of 48 layers, so the expert
GEMMs are excluded by the quant filter and the gate README's "a captured MoE
graph replaying the teacher's kernels" explanation is not needed to account for
that path not moving. And `output.weight`, the largest Q8_0 matrix in the model
at 248320x2560, is excluded by the slot-path filter along with `token_embd`.

The Q8_0 roles are periodic with period 4: layers at 3 mod 4 carry separate
`attn_q`/`attn_k`/`attn_v`/`attn_output` while the other 36 carry fused
`attn_qkv` plus `attn_gate` and `ssm_out`. Bisect boundaries must be multiples
of 4 or depth and attention-role composition are confounded.

### Two corrections to how the committed arms were read

The two gate arms ran different prompt mixes — 12 cases over four categories
against three `code` cases — so figures quoted across them confound scope with
mix. On the controlled code-to-code basis the max absolute logit delta grows
**1.0028 to 3.1656 (3.2x)** and max KL 69.5x, while the 0-47 arm's minimum
decode margin is *wider* than the 32-47 arm's (0.0308 against 0.0021). Adding
layers 0-31 therefore enlarges the perturbation itself rather than exposing more
near-ties. The `1.86 / 1.34` pair in the original write-up is the prefill row of
two different case sets, not a scope comparison.

The "part of the prefill path is not taking the candidate arithmetic" reading is
refuted by the committed artifacts and needs no kernel census. In the 32-47 arm
the prefill row carries the *largest* `max_abs_logit_delta` of any row in the
run (1.861 against 1.409 for c1); a bypassed route returns a zero delta. The
near-zero prefill KL is a confidence artifact: `prefill_last` has
`strict_margin_min` 14.67 against `c1`'s 0.0021. What remains open is coverage,
which the static map above bounds.

### `dense_wide256`

The missing link is a dispatch route, not evidence. The kernel is registered in
`hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q8_0_dense_wide.py` and pulled into
gfx1151 through that backend's module list, but nothing in `hipengine/runtime/`
or `hipengine/dispatch/` references it, so no dispatch path can select it.
Per-shape bit-identity would not change that. Its own evidence already measures
it at 6.74x the production dispatch on the `layers.8.attn_qkv` packet, against
`wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out`'s 3.9x, so it is the larger prize once reachable.

### Certified scope moved from 32-47 to 28-47

Layers 28-47 passes the full 12-case protocol: mean KL `9.342e-5` against the
`1e-3` limit (10.7x headroom), p95 `4.063e-4`, top-1 1544/1548, three identical
trajectory hashes, no scope failures, `hard_gates_passed` and
`eligible_for_automatic_admission` both true. It is the first arm in this
campaign with `measurement_valid: true` and no qualification blockers, because
provenance now separates documentation-only dirt from execution-affecting dirt.

The layer axis is **not monotone on tail metrics**: at the deeper scope
`general_en`'s max KL falls from `1.362e-2` to `5.848e-3` and its top-1 rises
from `0.98966` to `0.99225`, and `general_ja`'s max KL falls from `4.077e-3` to
`2.025e-3`. Category means are ordered across all seven measured scopes, which
is what the screens read, but a scope rejected on a tail metric alone needs its
own arm rather than an inference from a neighbour.

### Sample size: a screen may not judge a rate gate

A 132-row screen of layers 32-47 returned FAIL on the per-scope top-1 floor for
a scope the full 1548-row gate had already certified at `0.99677`. At screen
size 33 rows per category means one miss scores `32/33 = 0.9697` against the
`0.97` floor.

The deeper problem is not small-n. A rate gate tested against the value it
equals is near a coin flip at any reachable sample size: a scope whose true miss
rate sits at the `1%` limit fails the `>= 99%` test 38-48% of the time from 132
to 1548 rows, and the canonical fixture caps at 12 cases x 129 transitions =
1548 rows, so no larger run exists to buy the power back. Continuous statistics
do not have that problem — screen mean KL tracked the full arm within 8-15% on
both control scopes.

This is now normative in `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` 6.4: a reduced run may
report every metric but may decide only the continuous ones, must inflate its
mean KL by a measured rather than assumed screen-to-full ratio, must carry a
control scope with a known full-gate verdict, and may never admit a profile.

### Screen series and what remains reachable

Mean KL is monotone across all seven measured scopes, from `5.81e-5` at 32-47 to
`1.356e-3` at 8-47, putting the mean-KL boundary between layers 12 and 16. So a
contiguous scope cannot reach layers 0-7, and the roughly +2.09 s those layers
carry needs a non-contiguous scope to claim.
