## Summary

bg-75's queue item died in one second on the category oracle's c4 guard, but its third command - the
AR-arm submodule decomposition - produced the recomputation that `20260830T135632…` ordered. The
committed decomposition artifact published "AR admission is slower than the peers at C1-C7 and slower
than laurent at C8". That ladder (302/584/877/1172/1457/1746/2033/2320 ms) is 292 ms per lane times
