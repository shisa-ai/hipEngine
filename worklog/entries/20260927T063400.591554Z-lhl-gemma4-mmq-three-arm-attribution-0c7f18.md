# Three-arm attribution: the MMQ divergence is near-tie sensitivity

## Summary

The MMQ expert gate/up route's short-prefill divergence is a near-tie sensitivity
on rows the probe corpus creates deliberately, not a defect that one arm has and
the other does not. Adding llama.cpp as a third arm shows the anchor splitting
between our two arms rather than condemning either, and shows all three engines
agreeing exactly wherever the rows are confident.

`worklog/entries/20260927T062830.732548Z-lhl-gemma4-mmq-prompt-not-length-845b23.md`
established that the divergence follows the prompt rather than the length and
that the probe cannot attribute it, because it compares two of our own kernels
and so reports disagreement without establishing which arm is correct. This
closes that gap with an independent implementation of the same quantization.

## Changes

- `scripts/gemma4_mmq_arm_attribution.py` (new): feeds byte-identical prompt ids
  to hipEngine with the MMQ route off, hipEngine with it on, and llama.cpp's
  `llama-server` on the same artifact, GPU, context and KV dtype, and reports
  each arm's greedy continuation against the anchor. Records the top-2 logit
  margin per length, because a flip at a near-tie is a different finding from a
  flip at a confident row.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-27-gemma4-mmq-gate-up-three-arm-attribution.json`:
  the measurement, its positive control, and its caveats.
- Repointed every script that named a path this box does not have. The GGUF
  literal moved from `/mnt/nvme1/models` to `resolve_artifact()` in
  `gemma4_llamacpp_compare.py`, `gemma4_moe_dispatch_probe.py`,
  `gemma4_real_generate.py`, `gemma4_real_layer_dump.py` and
  `gemma4_real_probe.py`, so one resolver answers for all of them.
  `gemma4_llamacpp_reference_bench.py` now defaults to the pinned comparator at
  `~/llama.cpp/llama.cpp-hip` instead of the removed `/mnt/nvme1/llama-gemma4`.
  Both llama.cpp-facing scripts now auto-detect the amdgpu card through
  `select_card()` instead of hard-coding the dGPU's `0000:10:00.0`, which this
  box does not have.

## Validation

- Positive control, frozen corpus, `--lengths 96 512 1024`: llama.cpp, fp32 and
  MMQ all return the same greedy token at all three lengths (870, 13718,
  43424), with fp32 top-2 margins 12.36, 12.31 and 13.46. The 96-id point is the
  same length that disagrees under the probe corpus, so length alone does not
  predict the outcome. A harness defect would not spare these rows.
- Probe corpus, `--lengths 16 24 96`: the arms disagree at all three lengths and
  the anchor matches fp32 at 16 (236778), the MMQ route at 96 (236800), and
  neither at 24 (llama 236771, fp32 236800, MMQ 236812). fp32 margins are 1.56,
  5.95 and 3.14 against 12.31 to 13.46 on the frozen corpus, which is what
  `probe_corpus` documents itself as producing.
- The tokens are genuinely different characters rather than spelling variants:
  236771 is `'0'`, 236778 `'2'`, 236800 `'3'` and 236812 `'4'`, single-digit
  tokens in one contiguous block. The divergence is real at the token level.
- Reproduced: the probe-corpus run was repeated and returned identical tokens
  for all three arms at all three lengths.
- All seven touched scripts compile; the only remaining `/mnt/nvme1` literal in
  `scripts/` is the deliberate fallback candidate inside `resolve_artifact`.
- llama.cpp's `llama-server` was built for gfx1100 and runs on this box's
  gfx1151 integrated GPU.

## Next

- The campaign's recorded evidence is reproduced and now anchored: 1024 ids on
  the frozen corpus is 7.335e-07 against the 5e-2 bar, with llama.cpp agreeing on
  the token. No promotion decision needs to change on account of the short-length
  divergence, but a promotion row should state the corpus it was measured on,
  because the same length is exact on one and a decision flip on the other.
- The low-margin rows stay unresolved at the level of which single arm is right
  on any one row. Resolving them needs an oracle with exact arithmetic rather
  than a third approximate implementation; the CPU streaming reference
  (`hipengine/kernels/cpu_reference/gemma4_streaming.py`) is the candidate, and
  it is the only comparator in the tree that can arbitrate logit magnitudes
  instead of top-1 identity.
