# Define ZBook Qwen3.6 35B gfx1151 campaign

## Summary

Defined a separate Qwen3.6-35B-A3B campaign for the HP ZBook Ultra G1a
Radeon 8060S at its measured 60 W STAPM, 60 W fast-PPT, and 45 W slow-PPT
limits. The plan freezes model identity, makes BF16-relative quantization
quality precede speed work, requires a fresh same-file hipEngine/llama.cpp
HIP/Vulkan baseline, and admits no ROCmFPX port before a reconciled local
profile gives one mechanism a >=1% complete-request ceiling. No benchmark was
run and no performance row is promoted by this unit.
