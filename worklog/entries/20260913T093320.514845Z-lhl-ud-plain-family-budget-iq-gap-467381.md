# The UD-versus-plain kernel gap is entirely the IQ family

## Summary

Two rocprofv3 verifier-window censuses on the same host, model shape and
protocol put UD-Q4_K_M at 435.74 ms total kernel time (36.31 ms/step, 1032
kernel calls/step) against plain Q4_K_M at 351.98 ms (29.33 ms/step). UD
carries 83.8 ms of extra kernel time, and every millisecond of it is the IQ
family, which has no plain counterpart because plain Q4_K_M uses only the
K-family quants:

| UD-only owner | ms/step | share | calls/step | us/call |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gguf_iq4_xs_local32_gemv` | 11.373 | 31.32% | 117 | 97.20 |
| `gguf_iq_dense_strict` | 3.215 | 8.85% | 11 | 292.25 |
| `gguf_iq4_nl_local32_gemv` | 0.777 | 2.14% | 7 | 110.97 |

That is 15.37 ms/step of UD-only kernels. UD wins back 9.2 ms/step on Q4_K
(4.95 against plain's 14.16), 5.0 on Q6_K (2.53 against 7.52) and 1.0 on the
norm tail (`gguf_norm_fixed5120_wave256` 0.675 for 1536 calls against plain's
`gguf_rounded_add_rmsnorm_bf16_f32_weight` + `gguf_add_rmsnorm_bf16_f32_weight`
at 1.657 for 1524), so the IQ family is both the whole deficit and the whole
remaining opportunity.

## Changes

None. This is a budget measurement, not a code change. One plan bullet added
to `docs/UD-GFX1151-OPTIMIZE.md` recording the split and the three ranked next
steps with the pair counts that bound each.

## Validation

Both censuses are the same protocol (`scripts/mtp_verifier_rocprof.py`-style
verifier window, block-verify rows 3, 12 steps after warmup) on the W7900
(gfx1100):

- UD: `/tmp/phase8/census-i13-base.json`, avg_kernel_ms 36.3115
- Plain: `/tmp/phase8/census-plain.json`, avg_kernel_ms 29.3314

Per-kernel `ms_per_step` is `total_ms / steps`, so the two tables are directly
comparable.

## Next

Ranked by measured size, with the bound that decides each:

1. **IQ4_XS local32 dual-SiLU at the verifier row envelope.** IQ4_XS has 30
   `ffn_gate`/`ffn_up` pairs (60 of its 117 tensors). The dual kernel is
   already written and documented bit-exact with the unfused
   single/single/`silu_mul` chain, but it is `template<int WAVES>` with no
   `ROWS`, its `extern "C"` rejects `rows != 1`, and `gguf_linear.py` gates
   the pair on `rows == 1`. The single owner's `ROWS` sibling pattern is the
   template to copy. **Open question that must be measured first:** the
   `ROWS = 4` dual needs `acc_a[4][8] + acc_b[4][8] + xv[4][8]` = 96 float
   registers, and the single owner already measures 168 VGPRs at `ROWS = 4`,
   so the dual may spill. Measure the isolated `ROWS = 2` and `ROWS = 4` dual
   against `2 x` the single at rows=3 before touching dispatch.
2. **Q5_K T16 dense dual rowtile SiLU.** Q5_K has only 12 gate/up pairs, so
   this is bounded at roughly 0.8 ms/step even at the Q4 dual's 762 GB/s.
3. **IQ4_XS T16 repack.** Largest ceiling (1.53x on 11.37 ms/step) but the
   largest unit: a new `GGUF_IQ4_XS_T16_*` layout, CPU packer and inverse, a
   new owner, registration and gates.
