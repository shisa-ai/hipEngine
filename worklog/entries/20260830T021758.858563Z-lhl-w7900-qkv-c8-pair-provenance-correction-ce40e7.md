# W7900 Qwen3.8: the QKV C8 pair is re-derivable; the correction to it was itself wrong

## Summary

An hour ago I recorded that the QKV rows16 artifact's "+0.7% shipping C8 AR" pair
(45.62 -> 45.94) "is not reproducible from retained outputs". That clause was wrong and
this entry supersedes it. Both packets exist and the quoted numbers match their
`summary` blocks exactly: `he-server-rep2-512.json` gives `summary.8.ar.tok_s` = 45.618
(2026-08-30T00:29:10Z, floor 512, fused owner off) and `he-server-rep2-16.json` gives
45.936 (00:32:31Z, shipped floor 16, owner on), both widths [1,8] x 10 prompts on the
same protocol. I had grepped the wrong pair of packets.

The substitution I made in its place was the worse error: `he-uneq-srv-33.json` vs
`he-uneq16-shipped.json` (46.062 -> 45.999, -0.14%) is **not** the promotion contrast.
Floors 33 and 16 both admit the unequal owner at every width in this protocol (prefill
rows are either <= 8 decode rows or >= 33 prompt rows), so that pair is a
dispatch-identical **control**, not a floor-axis A/B. My "-0.14%, therefore C8 is
neutral" conclusion read a control as a treatment.

The conclusion survives, but for the right reason and stated differently. One
candidate draw of +0.70% against one dispatch-identical control draw of -0.14% at the
same width and protocol puts same-protocol packet drift near 0.8 points, which agrees
with the previously recorded observation that four same-state server packets spread
about 1%. A single packet per arm therefore cannot separate a 0.7% topline gain from
packet drift or arm order, and C8 AR is recorded as unresolved. What the promotion
rests on is unchanged: byte-identical arithmetic over 20/20 packed trajectory hashes in
both measurement orders, and the order-controlled +1.81% packed prefill crossover.
