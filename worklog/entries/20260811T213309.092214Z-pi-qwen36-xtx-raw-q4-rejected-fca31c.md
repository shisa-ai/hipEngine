# Reject Qwen3.6 XTX sole-raw Q4 rung

## Summary

Rejected the predeclared sole-raw Q4 rung on RX 7900 XTX before runtime integration. Repeated one-weight raw-Q4/Q8_1 screens were cache-resident and optimistic; distinct same-role pools larger than cache made every tested rows-4 raw family slower than exact compact T16. The exact T16 fused tile4 pressure candidate also lost. Production code, registry, materialization, and defaults remain unchanged.

## Changes

- Audited llama.cpp `c8e03ce8122b` CUDA/HIP MMVQ and Vulkan integer-dot MMVQ/MMQ source arithmetic.
- Screened disposable raw-Q4/Q8_1 col1/2/4/8, Vulkan stored-Q8-sum arithmetic, fused gate/up+SiLU, raw prefill, and exact compact-T16 tile4 candidates on GPU1.
- Added `benchmarks/results/2026-08-12-qwen36-27b-xtx-raw-q4-rung-rejected.json` and updated `docs/QWEN36-27B-GGUF-7900XTX.md` P6.4-P6.6 with the no-keep decision.

## Validation

- Cache-cold rows-4 raw/T16 pools, each exceeding approximately 96 MiB, measured raw regressions of 65.73% `attn_gate`, 17.90% `attn_output`, 29.18% `attn_q`, 25.17% `attn_qkv`, and 12.53% `ffn_down`; commands and raw hashes are in the compact artifact.
- Fused K5120/N17408 rows4 measured exact T16 129.413 us, correct raw split 166.988 us, and disposable raw dual 174.145 us. The split route passed KL 1.65e-8/top-1 1.0; the losing dual body also failed top-1 and was discarded.
- Exact compact-T16 tile4 was BF16-bit exact but measured 158.702 us versus tile8 144.799 us, a 9.60% regression.
- Raw all-Q4 residency can save only 291,635,200 bytes / 0.2716 GiB, less than every remaining AR and MTP external-memory gap.
- `python3 -m json.tool benchmarks/results/2026-08-12-qwen36-27b-xtx-raw-q4-rung-rejected.json`, `python3 scripts/worklog.py check`, and `git diff --check` are the publication gates for this unit.
