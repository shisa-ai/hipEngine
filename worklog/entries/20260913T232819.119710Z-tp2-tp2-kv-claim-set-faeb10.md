# TP2 Packet 1: per-rank KV pool geometry and aggregate claim rollback

## Summary

Packet 1 asks for the N-rank plan *and composite KV pool/claim set*. The plan
existed; the pool did not. `hipengine/distributed/kv.py` now resolves the
per-rank KV share and provides the all-or-nothing claim path, with no allocation
of its own so it is fully testable without a device.

Two decisions carry the design:

1. **The KV head partition is the weight planner's partition.** A rank's KV
   heads come from the same `partition_groups` call that splits `attn_k`/
   `attn_v`, so the pool geometry cannot drift from the shard plan, and an
   impossible degree fails with the planner's own message. Qwen3.8-27B at N=3 is
   refused for exactly that reason (4 KV heads, 16 GDN groups).
2. **Admission is all-or-nothing.** `claim_all` allocates in rank order, and any
   failure releases every earlier buffer in reverse order before raising. A
   partial admission would leave one rank holding memory the others never got,
   which is precisely the failure mode a distributed request path must not have.

Resolved geometry for Qwen3.8-27B `Q4_K_M` (16 full-attention layers of 64,
`head_count_kv=4`, `head_dim=256`, bf16 planes, 16 B/token/layer of
`KVLiveSpans`):

| Group | Context | KV heads/rank | Plane/rank | Spans/rank | Total/rank |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| N=1 | 8192 | 4 | 512.0 MiB | 2.00 MiB | 514.0 MiB |
| N=2 | 8192 | 2 | 256.0 MiB | 2.00 MiB | 258.0 MiB |
| N=1 | 32768 | 4 | 2048.0 MiB | 8.00 MiB | 2056.0 MiB |
| N=2 | 32768 | 2 | 1024.0 MiB | 8.00 MiB | 1032.0 MiB |

So a TP2 rank holds half the pool of a TP1 process at the same context, which is
the capacity side of the Packet 3 decision.

  partition at N=1/2/4 plus replication when ranks exceed heads, N=3 refusal with
  the planner's message, layer-type counting, per-token/plane/spans arithmetic,
  linearity in context, every geometry refusal, claim binding to the plan
  (including a world-size mismatch), the free-memory check with and without a
  reserve, misordered/incomplete claim sets, all-or-nothing rollback (failure on

  `/models/gguf/Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M.gguf` for N=1/2 at 8192 and 32768 context.
