# IQ4_NL localization fails: the cost is not per-tensor

## Summary

Investigated the blocker recorded in `018bc80eb`. The hypothesis was that a
small number of sensitive IQ4_NL tensors caused the numerics cost and that
excluding them would recover the gate while keeping most of the +22% prefill.
**The hypothesis is false**, and the way it was tested was itself wrong in a way
worth recording.

IQ4_NL stays held out of the default policy.

## Changes

- `gguf_linear.py`: `_iq_dense_mmq_prefill_dispatch` honours an optional
  `shapes` allowlist, mirroring the planar-Q6 integer policy. Retained - it is
  how any future subset gets re-tested in one line.
- `scripts/gguf_iq4_nl_mmq_layer_probe.py` (new): per-layer divergence probe,
  carrying a caution about how to read it (below).
- `hip_gfx1151/__init__.py`, `docs/REFACTOR.md`, artifact: the negative result
  and the rejected subset recorded so the attempt is not repeated.

No default changed.

## What was measured

The seven IQ4_NL tensors in K_M sit at layers 1/2/3 (`ffn_down`, 5120x17408),
21 (`attn_qkv`, 10240x5120) and 27/27/50 (`ffn_gate`/`ffn_up`, 17408x5120).
Shape maps one-to-one onto those groups in this file, so the dispatch can
discriminate them.

**Per-layer probe** (worst prompt): divergence is seeded at layer 1 (4.9e-04)
and reaches 3.5e-01 by layer 63, with no single dominant injection - the large
jumps at layers 6 and 50 include non-IQ4_NL sites, so most of the growth is
amplification of an early seed through 62 downstream layers.

**Shape bisect, on divergence from the current default**: all seven 1.548e-01,
only the early `ffn_down` 1.102e-01 (71%), the other four 2.258e-02 (15%).
That pointed clearly at the three early tensors.

**The same subset, measured against the teacher**: prefill 140.3 tok/s (+10%
instead of +22%) and mean **0.0013632** - no better than routing all seven
(0.0013457), and worse than not routing IQ4_NL at all (0.0010796). It recovers
only top-1 (162/162).

| arm | prefill | mean | p95 | max | top-1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| no IQ4_NL (default) | 127.2 | 0.0010796 | 0.0051188 | 0.0354152 | 162/162 |

## Why the bisect misled

It optimized **divergence from our own default**, which is not the gate
objective. Moving away from the incumbent says nothing about agreement with the
teacher - a change can diverge from the current default while landing no
further from, or even closer to, the reference.

Against the teacher the cost is essentially **binary**: routing any IQ4_NL
through the MMQ costs about +0.00027 mean regardless of which tensors are
routed. It is not attributable to particular tensors, so no subset fixes it,
and the best remaining one (`attn_qkv` alone, 29.5 MB of 330) could not be
worth the dispatch complexity.

**Lesson: bisect retention decisions on the gate metric, not on distance from
the incumbent.** The probe script now carries that caution at the top.

## Decision

IQ4_NL remains out of `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_MMQ_PREFILL_POLICY`. Kernel support,
registration and tests stay; the rejected subset is recorded in
`_GGUF_IQ_DENSE_MMQ_IQ4_NL_HELD_OUT` so it is not retried.

Remaining hypotheses, both untested: the cost is intrinsic to Q8_1 activations
on these tensors, or IQ4_NL wants a finer activation quantization than the
shared DS4 plane. If neither holds, delete the support rather than leave it
unrouted - trigger recorded in `docs/REFACTOR.md`.
