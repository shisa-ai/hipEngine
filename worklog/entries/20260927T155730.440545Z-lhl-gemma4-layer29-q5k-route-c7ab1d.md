# Layer 29's fast Q5_K route exists and is wired only for Qwen

## Summary

Chased why layer 29 is 110.89 ms in a single launch, and the answer is that
the fix is already in the tree.

The profile's launch counts identify the layer: 29 calls of the q4_k fused MMQ32
gate/up route against 1 call of `gguf_q5_k_selected_grouped_row4_bf16_kernel` at
110.89 ms. Gemma 4 26B-A4B's MoE layers are architecturally uniform, so the one
q5_k layer is a quant difference rather than a shape difference, and its gate_up
costs about **17x** what a q4_k layer's does - 110.89 against 189.24 / 29 =
6.53 ms.

The gate is `gemma4_project_experts_gate_up_mmq`, which returns False unless
`weight.spec.quant_key == "gguf_q4_k"`. That is capability-correct rather than a
defect, because the two routes are different designs: q4_k quantizes activations
to DS4 Q8_1 and runs MMQ32, while the q5_k route is iu8-WMMA with a 3-plane
residual int8 activation and a risk+repair pass, and its wrapper takes
`risk_count_ptr`, `risk_indices_ptr`, `max_risks` and `risk_multiplier`, which the
q4_k route has no equivalent of.

**The fast q5_k route already exists and is already registered.** Its module
docstring says it is the production-enveloped iu8-WMMA risk+repair machinery
adapted from `gguf_q4_k_selected_prefill.py` to Q5_K weights, and that the sparse
exact repair reproduces the strict row4 parent bit-exactly. Both halves are
exposed: `gguf_q5_k_selected_dual_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill_bf16_bf16_out` and
`gguf_q5_k_selected_dual_sparse_exact_repair_bf16`.

What is missing is the gemma4 wiring, and the size of that job is the useful
qualifier. The q5_k halves are not driven from a small reusable helper: their only
caller is `hipengine/runtime/qwen4_exp_runner.py`, a Qwen-specific runner of several
thousand lines that owns the risk buffers and sequences the risk pass against the
sparse repair. Nothing under `gemma4_experts.py` reaches them, so gemma4 falls back
to the strict row4 parent for the one layer whose weights are Q5_K. Mirroring the
route is therefore a port of that sequencing, not a flag flip - which is why this
entry stops at the finding.

If that route can run at the same order as the q4_k fused one, it is worth
roughly 100 ms, about **5% of the prefill** - the largest single remaining item in
the profile, ahead of the attention work just landed.
