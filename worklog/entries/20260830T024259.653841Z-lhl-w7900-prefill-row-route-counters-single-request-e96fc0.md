## Summary

The prefill submodule is now measured **protocol-comparably** to the published
peer row, and it is the one submodule that does not come close: hipEngine is
**146.5 -> 154.8 tok/s** (flat) against llama.cpp current **200.9 -> 424.1** and

**single request**, never a wave.

## Validation

Convention reverse-engineered from the peer packet, then validated: per width,
`mean(numerator_tokens) / mean(wall_seconds)` over the 10 one-token cells, where
`numerator_tokens = prompt_tokens x width`. It reproduces the published current-HIP
prefill row exactly at all eight widths (200.946, 239.658, 259.036, 281.828,
323.043, 366.213, 374.207, 424.072), so the same arithmetic applied to our one-token
arm is a like-for-like number, not a new metric. Width cancels for us because our
cell rate is already `lanes / wall`.

| C | hipEngine | current | Laurent | HE/current | our wall ms | their wall ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 146.546 | 200.946 | 195.803 | 0.73x | 306.4 | 223.4 |
| 4 | 152.425 | 281.828 | 274.520 | 0.54x | 1178.3 | 637.3 |

Flat 146.5 -> 154.8 across an 8x wider wave is the serialized-prefill signature;
the peers scale 2.11x over the same range.

Direct route evidence (`scripts/gguf_pair_route_count_probe.py`, widths 1-8, one
process, counters keyed `(name, group_size, rows)` on `prefill_batch_native`): the
group-size field is **1 in every recorded call across the whole run** and rows take
only the per-prompt values 35, 36, 39, 43, 46, 48, 60, 67 - never a sum of them
(all eight prompts together would be 374 rows). The engine never forms a multi-request
prefill group, which is the mechanism behind the flat row and matches the guard
reading in the previous entry rather than contradicting it.

Packet caveat stated rather than hidden: this one-token arm self-reports
`status: failed, passed: false` because I passed `--expected-mtp-widths 8`, and K3
cannot engage at `max_tokens 1` (0/80 cells engaged, 80/80 content-exact on
`ar_self_exact`/`mtp_self_exact`/`ar_mtp_equal`). The older one-token packets pass
with `expected_mtp_widths: []`, so the flag was the only cause and the AR walls are
sound. A clean re-run without the flag is queued (`bg-47`) and the README row waits
for that packet rather than publishing from a failed-verdict run.
