# Width-map section: record ROW_TILE=16/32 prediction and the sparse-ladder D3 rule

## Summary

Four amendments to the `CONCURRENCY2.md` "Width-adaptive GEMM selection"
section written in f8f829e2b, prompted by in-flight uncommitted work that adds
`Q4_T16_DENSE_ROWTILE_CASE(16)` and `CASE(32)` and raises the
`launch_q4_dense_rowtile` cap from 8 to 32.

The substantive addition is a **standing, falsifiable prediction recorded before
the measurement**: extrapolating the measured rowtile slopes, a direct
`ROW_TILE=16` should beat `2x8` by ~1.24x on gate/up and ~1.40x on down (one
saved weight-read pass), while `ROW_TILE=32` should regress because `acc[32][8]`
is 256 accumulator VGPRs before operands against a 256-VGPR wave32 limit and
should spill to scratch. The earlier section text said only that the family
"caps near M~8-12", which reads as a blanket discouragement; the corrected
reading is that 16 is a real but small win (~7% of a c16 step once the FFN is
~24-30% of it) and 32 is predicted negative. This does not move the priority
order: there is no c>8 execution path to plug a 16-row kernel into until D2
(group decomposition) exists.

The second substantive addition is a **sparse-ladder rule under D3**. A launcher
accepting `{2..8, 16, 32}` while returning `hipErrorInvalidValue` for 9-15 and
17-31 reopens exactly the coverage cliff that cost `c5` 4x throughput. D3 is now
explicit that a width may be covered either by its own instantiation or by a
declared D2 decomposition, and that widening a launcher cap is incomplete until
the shape check, dispatch gate, interior-width decomposition rule, and coverage
test move with it.
