# TP2 Packet 0: the break-even projection is negative on the eager path

## Summary

**No-go.** The break-even projection that justified proceeding to Packet 3 was
wrong in three independent ways, and correcting them flips the decision. TP2 now
projects **0.62-0.68x** against the faster matched single-GPU arm, i.e. 32-38%
*slower*, and the measured collective cost is **2.2-4.5x** over budget.

The three defects, each of which alone was enough to make the old verdict wrong:

1. **The collective cost was measured on a structure that cannot carry a layer
   dependency.** The old input was 3.66-4.48 ms/token, from a chain ladder in
   which the reductions did not consume their predecessors. In a dependent chain
   - every reduction in its own group, its result feeding the next layer - a
   20 KB fp32 all-reduce costs **177.8 us**, not 28.6-35 us, so 128 reductions
   is **22.76 ms/token**
   (`benchmarks/results/2026-09-14-w7900-tp2-dependent-reduction-chain.json`).
   The deferrable structure's 37.3 us per step is real but is not a per-layer
   cost; the ratio between the two structures is 4.8x.
2. **One group was counted as two independent results.** The old projection
   computed a complete TP2 time *per rank* and compared each against its own TP1
   row. A TP2 decode step is one synchronized group: its time is the sum of
   `max` over ranks at each dependency boundary,
   `max(fixed) + max(rank weight reads) + collective`, and it is compared
   against the *faster* arm. The old form let the faster arm ignore the slower
   rank it would wait for. The bandwidth term also divided where it should have
   multiplied by the weight share, overstating rank-weight time by the square of
   the share (at a 20% fixed share: 349 GiB/s implied instead of 546).
3. **The baselines were not a matched pair.** The W7900 row came from a
   512-prompt / 128-decode / INT8-KV run; the XTX row was labelled "8192
   context" and had **no committed source artifact at all**. The plan requires
   matched same-host controls on one protocol, so both arms were re-measured
   here, one GPU at a time, on one revision (`14f2b5c72`) and one protocol
   (512-prompt / 128-decode / INT8 per-token-head KV / fp32 scales / eager decode
   / persistent session / public AR profile): **W7900 29.575 tok/s**,
   **XTX 35.355 tok/s**. Both are higher than the figures they replace (the
   retained W7900 row was 27.89 on an earlier revision).

The decision is settled by a bound that needs no shard-kernel measurement: even
if rank-local weight reads cost **nothing**, the group would still pay 22.76 ms
against a 28.28 ms baseline, or **1.24x** - below the plan's 1.3x target. This is
the plan's own stop rule ("Stop full-model expansion if the optimistic bound
cannot beat the faster TP1 arm"), so the pre-Packet-3 shard-kernel gate does not
have to be satisfied to answer the question. The gate remains unmet and is
recorded as such.

The limiting rank-weight term is the W7900's 8.646 GiB shard at its own implied
bandwidth (436.5 GiB/s); the baseline is the XTX's 28.28 ms/token. Even a shard
kernel 3.6x faster than the bandwidth model implies would need 1566 GiB/s of
read bandwidth on a card whose peak is 864 GB/s, so the projection cannot be
rescued by kernel work: the exposed collective count or cost has to change.

## Changes

- `scripts/tp_break_even.py` - replaced the per-rank projection with the
  synchronized group model; fixed the implied-bandwidth term to divide by the
  weight share; made the workload protocol a required part of every `--tp1`
  spec; made the collective input come from a measurement
  (`--dependent-chain-artifact` or an explicit `--marginal-us`, no default);
  added the optimistic bound, the protocol-match check, the pre-Packet-3 gate
  record, and a `certified` verdict that is withheld when any of them fails.
- `tests/test_unit_scripts_tp_break_even.py` - rewritten for the group model: 29
  tests, including hand-computed group maxima, the bandwidth-share regression,
  protocol-mismatch and gate withholding, the dependency-check refusal for a
  non-dependent marginal, and the optimistic bound.

## Notes and blockers

- Packet 3 (full-model integration) does not start from this projection. If TP2
  is pursued on this host, the next unit is a cheaper collective structure, and
  the measurement that would reopen the question is a dependent-chain marginal
  low enough that `baseline - collective` is positive at a realistic fixed
  share: under 9.61 ms/token at a 0% share, under 5.07 ms at 30%.
- The `--graph-replay-decode` fallback defect should be fixed independently: an
  unadmitted KV layout must record a disabled reason and fall back to eager
  decode rather than raise, which is what the flag's own help text promises.
- The pre-Packet-3 shard-kernel gate stays unmet and recorded as such; it would
  matter only for the magnitude of a projection that currently has the wrong
  sign.

- **The collective is the binding term, and its levers are structural.** Fewer
  exposed reductions (fuse the two per block, keep partials in registers across a
  boundary), cheaper per-reduction groups, or overlap with independent work.
  Faster shard kernels cannot close a 2.2-4.5x gap.
- **The pre-Packet-3 shard-kernel gate is unmet and now moot for a go decision.**
  It matters for the *magnitude* of a positive projection, not for this sign.
- **Discovered while measuring the control: `--graph-replay-decode` now defaults
  to true, and an INT8 KV layout crashes instead of taking the documented
  fallback.** `capture_qwen35_gguf_decode_graph` raises "GGUF decode graph
  requires BF16 KV or the admitted tail4_hadamard_group32 layout", but
  `_decode_graph_disabled_reason` does not consult that admission, so the help
  text's "record the disabled reason and fall back to eager decode" does not
  happen for `int8_per_token_head`. Repro: the command above without
  `--no-graph-replay-decode`. The matched pair therefore passes
  `--no-graph-replay-decode` explicitly; at the retained 2026-09-13 baseline's
  commit that was the default, so the arm reproduces the same configuration.
  This is a separate defect from the break-even work and is not fixed here.
- No engine exists yet, so no TP speedup is claimed in either direction; the
  projection is a projection. The corrected artifact supersedes every earlier
  break-even number, including the 1.7-1.9x and 1.23-1.80x rows.
