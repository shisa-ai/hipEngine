# Retain unregistered GR row-reuse comparisons as a checked diagnostic reference

## Summary

`docs/REFACTOR.md` R0034 asked whether the three unregistered widened-row-reuse GR
up exports are worth keeping for a different register-resource class or cost
model. **Decision: retain them intentionally**, as a named diagnostic reference,
with one recorded reopening reason and no dispatch exposure. This is a retention
decision, not a promotion and not a re-measurement: no arithmetic, default,
registry key, profile or selector changed, and no new timing or numerical claim
is made here.

Retention is not rubber-stamping the status quo. The three exports are the only
on-tree instantiations of this kernel body at `ROW_BATCH != 4`, so they are the
only evidence that the parameterization compiles and reproduces the parent's bits
at another width - including partial groups at the grid edge - and the only
apparatus a different register-resource class could compare against without
first reconstructing the instantiation. Retiring them would delete what the
recorded reopen condition depends on, leaving a condition nothing in the tree can
clear.

## Reopening reason

Reopen only with one of these, and only by re-running the recorded screen and
census rather than by argument:

1. A target whose register-resource class keeps the 130-VGPR (`ROW_BATCH` 8) or
   256-VGPR (`ROW_BATCH` 16) instantiations at the parent's resident-wave count,
   i.e. a machine class where the recorded occupancy collapse does not occur.
   The recorded census gives 72/130/256 VGPR and 28/15/8 waves per CU for widths
   4/8/16 on gfx11 (`register-census-gr.json`); that gap is the premise.
2. A body change that lowers the accumulator cost of the retile
   (`acc[ROW_BATCH][4][2]`), which changes the register cost the negative was
   attributed to.

If either premise is exercised and refuted - a census on the new class still
shows the occupancy collapse, or the body change removes the register pressure
without recovering the width - retire instead: remove the three exports, the
three wrappers and the `_row_batch` plumbing together, after re-checking shared
instantiations as recorded below. That is the same gate R0034 described, now
with a stated trigger on both sides.

## What retention does not mean

- Not dispatchable: the family is registered under `linear+gr_gated_mean` for the
  width-4 variants only (`coltile2_branch4_rowbatch4_f32_exact`,
  `..._wave_scale_f32_exact`, `..._wave_scale_dpp_f32_exact`,
  `..._wave_scale_dpp_k320_full_rows_f32_exact`), and no registry key resolves to
  any of the three widened wrappers;
  `tests/test_gpu_qwen4exp_gr_row_batch8.py::test_row_batch_is_not_reachable_from_dispatch`
  scans every registered key to keep it that way. The three are nameable through
  the module, which is what a screen or a diagnostic needs, and unselectable from
  dispatch, which is what production needs. Those are different properties and
  this decision keeps both explicit. Excluding them from dispatch says nothing
  about whether the primitive can be called: the exports are callable by name and
  that is the property this decision preserves.
- Not a numerical re-qualification: the recorded bit-exactness is the strict
  parent-parity contract for a retile whose per-output reduction order is
  unchanged. It is not a production profile gate and does not admit anything.
- Not a fresh measurement: every number referenced above is the 2026-10-04
  artifact. Nothing was timed, rebuilt or re-measured in this unit.
- Not permanent by default: the retirement branch above is real. What is
  withdrawn is retirement *because the evidence row is a single host*, which is
  the reasoning `AGENTS.md` forbids.

## Changes

- `tests/test_unit_qwen4exp_gr_row_batch_contract.py` (new, unit tier, CPU only):
  the host half of the retention contract. It replaces `_cached_fn` with a
  recorder so nothing is built or launched, and asserts that (a) all three
  exports remain in `__all__`, callable and distinct from the parent, (b) each
  named wrapper resolves exactly its own `hipengine_<name>` symbol, (c) each
  refuses `_no_gate=True` without resolving anything, (d) the pinned width cannot
  be overridden (`_row_batch=4` is a duplicate keyword, so no caller can re-point
  a diagnostic at production), and (e) the unspecialized entry still defaults to
  the rowbatch4 production symbol, so retention did not become promotion.
- Parent removed completed R0034 from the ledger and updated the adjacent
  non-registration comment to point here instead of the removed backlog item.
  No executable kernel, registry or wrapper behavior changed.

## Shared-instantiation check

Before choosing retention, the removal R0034 described was sized:

- `_row_batch` (the keyword, not the unrelated `_row_batch` helper in
  `quant/gguf_iq_dense.py` or the rocblas-prefill geometry loops) appears only on
  the base GR up wrapper and the three widened wrappers. No production caller
  passes it.
- In the `.hip`, the three widened names are three exclusive `Q8_GR_SCALE_ENTRY`
  invocations; the macro, `launch_q8_gr_up_scale` and the template are shared
  with the production entries at the default `ROW_BATCH = 4`, so no production
  instantiation is exclusive to them.
- The recorded census lists exactly six GR up instantiations - three production
  (plain, wave-scale, no-gate at width 4) and the three widened arms - so no
  other symbol shares the widened code.
- No script, runner, profile or env selector names the three wrappers;
  `scripts/qwen4exp_q8_wave_scale_screen.py` reaches them only through its
  generic `--gr-parent`/`--gr-candidate` symbol overrides.

Retirement would therefore be bounded and safe. It is still the wrong call now,
for the reason in "Summary": the exports are the only way to exercise the width
parameter and the only apparatus the reopen condition can use.
