# Cross-engine harness completion: gufo live smoke, ignore_eos, and case-intersection compare

## Summary

The cross-engine harness (the `openai` mode of
`scripts/qwen4exp_canonical_ar_bench.py`) is live-validated end-to-end against
the pinned gufo v0.12.0 server on the real four-shard `UD-Q4_K_XL` payload,
and `compare` now supports the case-intersection basis that text-driven
engines require.

## Decisions

- **`general_ja-p1024` is disqualified for every text-driven engine** (gufo,
  kyojin): the fixture's exact ids at that case's tail are
  `... 60, 198, 198, 248046` (`a_plan]` + two separate newline tokens +
  `<|im_end|>`), but detokenized-then-retokenized text merges the two newlines
  into one token 271 (`\n\n`), giving 1023 ids — a BPE merge-boundary
  artifact of decode/encode at that boundary, not an adapter or tokenizer
  bug. The fixture stays pinned (every prior row was measured on it), so the
  case is excluded per protocol for engines that must be driven by text, and
  the exclusion is recorded in each artifact's `disqualified_cases`.
- `compare --allow-case-intersection` compares over the case intersection
  with a provenance gate: an engine that did not measure a compared-suite
  case must declare it disqualified with its reason, otherwise the comparison
  refuses the silent drop. Engines that measured cases outside the
  intersection get their aggregates recomputed to the comparison basis
  (`summary_basis: compared_case_intersection`), so no engine is compared on
  a different aggregation basis than its peers.
- gufo v0.12.0's `/v1/completions` accepts `ignore_eos`, so the gufo payload
  now sends it and the decode leg is the full 128 transitions, exactly like
  the llamacpp leg. The 400 fallback was generalized: any optional field
  (sampling switches, `ignore_eos`) a pinned engine rejects falls back to
  the minimal payload; `cache_prompt`/`return_token_ids` are never silently
  stripped because they carry protocol semantics.

## Changes

- `scripts/qwen4exp_canonical_ar_bench.py`: gufo payload gains `ignore_eos`;
