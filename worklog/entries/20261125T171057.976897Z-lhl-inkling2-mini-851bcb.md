# Read Q5_K and Q8_0 experts of Inkling prompt blocks through the grouped WMMA kernel

## Summary

The mainline `UD-Q4_K_M` file keeps NVFP4 experts in its first 20 layers and Q5_K gate/up with
Q8_0 down experts in its last four. The grouped WMMA kernel read one layout, so the prompt blocks
of those four layers went through the FP32 tiles: 14.1 ms for a 256-row block's gate/up and 3.7 ms
for its down, against 1.3 and 1.3 ms for an NVFP4 layer. In the `production` prefill trace on the
W7900 they were 577 of 3,382 ms of kernel time at 2,057 prompt tokens (17%) and 164 of 950 ms at
575 (17%).

The kernel now reads Q5_K and Q8_0 as well. An NVFP4 weight is exact in FP16 and comes in one part;
a Q5_K or Q8_0 weight is not, so it comes as a high and a residual FP16 part, and a tile takes three
products: activation high by weight high, activation residual by weight high, and activation high by
weight residual. The product of the two residual parts is below FP32 rounding and is left out. The
sum is the FP32 reference to its own rounding, so the two-token outputs after 47, 575 and 2,057
prompt tokens are unchanged.

launch):

## Changes

- `quant/gguf_nvfp4_selected_wmma.hip`: `selected_wmma_kernel<PARTS, FORMAT>` with
  `dequant16_q5_k` and `dequant16_q8_0`. A Q8_0 row is not word aligned, so `load16_even` reads
  it with aligned words and byte permutes and loads the last two bytes alone. The NVFP4 entries and
  their arithmetic are unchanged; four entries named for Q5_K and Q8_0 join them.
- `gguf_nvfp4_selected_wmma.py`: `launch_wmma(..., fmt)` and the registry entries
  `moe_linear/gguf_q5_k/selected_wmma_f16_parts_f32_out` and `moe_linear/gguf_q8_0/` of the same
  name, on both HIP backends.
- `Inkling2GGUFDeviceProjections` keeps the grouped launch of each layout the backend registers
  (`_wmma_by_quant`), and the device-routed block and the host-routed block take the launch of
  each bank.
- The plan has two scopes, `prompt_block_ge_192_rows_top8_q5_k_gate_n2304_m2304` and
  `prompt_block_ge_192_rows_top8_q8_0_split_down_n1152_m2304`: `strict` selects the FP32 tiles,
  `fast` and `production` the grouped kernel.

## Validation

  dequantized NumPy reference in FP64 for Q5_K at 512 and 2,304 inputs and Q8_0 at the same, seven
  experts, 150 rows, eight routes each; every output within 2e-5 of its magnitude bound, and the
  rows past the last route untouched. The NVFP4 cases are as before.
