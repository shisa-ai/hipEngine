teacher-forced trajectory rows, 3 repeats, d4x2 candidate + d4x3 incumbent

## Decision

PF-1d stays **blocked** on two named prerequisites:

1. **State-determinism forensics**: localize the first-run mismatch
   (candidate-basis: which runner buffer persists across prefill calls and
   differs on first x3-after-x2 use). Until resolved, no MMQ-chain admission
   (plane flip, P3 shape row, or attn_output row) can pass a same-schedule
   gate — this also re-opens a question about the incumbent's own
   state-repeat evidence under reordering.
2. **Comparison-basis alignment**: reproduce the 2026-08-29 admission basis
   (single-transition, 450 rows) in this harness — prefill-final logits plus
   forced-step rows without decode compounding — so d4x2 can be judged on the
   scale under which the incumbent was admitted.

No promotion, no rejection: d4x2's numerics are neutral and its speedup
(1.38x isolated) stands, but the binding determinism prerequisite gates it.
