# prefix-cache cost attribution: which phase pays

## Summary

Added per-phase wall-clock accounting to the GGUF resident loop and ranked the
prefix-cache cost by measurement instead of by inspection. The ranking is not
close: the **serial c1 suffix prefill pays 6.2-6.9 s on a hit turn** (33.8-35.3
ms per suffix token), the **snapshot capture pays 0.18-0.38 s on every eligible
turn including misses**, and everything else the task asked about -
`_prefix_source_for`'s flush and refresh-all-rows work, the `_processed_tokens`
tuple build, the workspace lease reservation, and the state restore - is
**0.1-5 ms per turn, i.e. negligible**. The task's candidate list was ordered by
plausibility; the measurement inverts it: admission bookkeeping is free, the
suffix prefill is the entire cost. rocprofv3 confirms the mechanism: the hit
route issues **+329,029 kernel dispatches** (~1,679 per suffix token) against
~0.4 ms/token for the batched prefill kernels, and the capture's cost is a
**malloc storm** (~54 `hipMalloc` calls per capture at ~3.2 ms each), not the
state copies, which are ~25 us each.

One route difference found on the way is directly useful for the fix: the three
sampled fixture lanes reuse 2,560-8,704 tokens with 172-189 token suffixes and
record **no `suffix_prefill` phase at all**, with turn walls 3-5 s lower than the
greedy lanes' equivalent hits. A cheaper reused-suffix route already exists in
this tree; finding it is the first step of the optimization.

### Ranked cost (median per turn; hit turns are the three greedy cumulative lanes)

| rank | code path | hit turn | miss turn | mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `_prefill_native_chunk` serial c1 suffix loop | **6242-6912 ms** | 0 | ~1,679 extra dispatches per suffix token vs ~0.4 ms/token batched |
| 2 | `_capture_prefix_snapshot` → `capture_prefix_state_snapshot` | 135-297 ms | **257-1415 ms** | ~54 `hipMalloc` per capture at ~3.2 ms, plus one device sync |
| 3 | `_processed_tokens` + per-step aligned probe | 22-64 ms | 18-55 ms | ~128 calls per turn at 0.15-0.5 ms each |
| 4 | `clone_prefix_state_from_snapshot` (restore) | 1.2-1.6 ms | - | D2D copies only, ~25 us each, no malloc |
| 5 | `_prefix_source_for` lookup (flush + refresh others + match + resolve) | 0.4-2.3 ms | 0.5-1.5 ms | flush is 0.01 ms; refresh-others 0.0 ms |
| 6 | KV pool admission (`admit_with_shared_prefix` / `allocate`) | 0.1 ms | 1.8-4.8 ms | - |
| 7 | packed workspace lease (configure time, mode independent) | - | - | 42,949,672,960 pool-plane bytes |

The four hypotheses the task named, answered directly:

- **`_prefix_source_for` admission work**: 0.4-2.3 ms per turn. `lookup_flush_packed`
  is 0.01 ms and `lookup_refresh_others` is 0.00 ms - both are noise next to a
  10-30 s turn. Refuted as a driver.
- **Snapshot capture cost**: real, 0.18-0.38 s per capture on the greedy route
  (up to 1.4 s on the sampled route), and paid by *every* eligible turn because
  the prompt-boundary refresh runs whether or not anything was reused. This is
  the miss-path overhead.
- **`_processed_tokens` tuple building**: 0.15-0.5 ms per call, ~128 calls per
  turn, 0.02-0.06 s per turn. Small, but it is the largest per-decode-step cost.
- **Workspace lease reservation**: not per request. The packed workspace lease is
  taken once in `configure_engine_loop` (42,949,672,960 pool-plane bytes) and is
  identical in both modes. It matters for *capacity and contiguity*, not for
  per-turn cost.
- **c1-suffix path that bypasses packed/graph decode**: this is the cost. Same 4
  requests, same prompts, same outputs: radix 632,638 dispatches / 29.0 s device
  time at 45.8 us per dispatch, off 303,609 / 24.0 s at 78.9 us per dispatch.
  The suffix route runs the small single-token GEMV family (`q8_0_t16_dual_split_gemv`
  29,250 vs 12,930 dispatches; `q4_k_t16_selected_dual_s` 39,000 vs 17,240) where
  the batched route runs `*_prefill_wmma` over the whole prompt.

### Route difference worth keeping

| lane | turn | reused | suffix tokens | `suffix_prefill` | turn wall |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cumulative-growing_history | 1 | 2048 | 189 | 6392 ms | 12.0 s |
| cumulative-medium_repo | 1 | 8192 | 183 | 6242 ms | 13.3 s |
| cumulative-small_repo | 1 | 2048 | 196 | 6912 ms | 14.5 s |
| fixture-growing_history | 2 | 2560 | 172 | **0 ms** | 11.1 s |
| fixture-medium_repo | 2 | 8704 | 187 | **0 ms** | 8.6 s |
| fixture-small_repo | 2 | 2560 | 189 | **0 ms** | 12.6 s |

All six rows report `native_compact_prefill=False` and reuse a comparable volume,
so the sampled/processed-argmax lanes prefill their suffix through a route that
never enters the serial loop. That route is the template for the fix.

- No arithmetic changed: this unit only reads `perf_counter` and adds counters.
  The instrumentation was in place for the phase arm, so the reported phase times
  are the same code path the product runs.
- rocprof caveat: call counts are exact, per-call durations are profiler-inflated,
  and the two profiled arms ran while other jobs shared the host, so only counts
  and kernel families are quoted from them. The absolute phase times come from the
  unprofiled engine counters.

## Next

- Identify the sampled-lane suffix prefill route and reuse it for greedy rows.
  That is the whole optimization: 6.5 s per hit turn, ~90% of the measured cost.
- Capture-side: the snapshot buffers are allocated and freed per capture
  (~54 `hipMalloc` at ~3.2 ms plus a device sync). A per-session snapshot buffer
  pool removes the mallocs and would cut 0.18-0.38 s per eligible turn - which is
  paid on misses too. Ledgered in `docs/REFACTOR.md`.
- The miss-path overhead measured by the A/B (+2.07 s in the second position) is
  larger than the phase table accounts for (~0.3-0.4 s). A same-session off/radix
  A/B with the phase counters live would localize the rest; the counters are in
  `/ready` now, so that is one run.
