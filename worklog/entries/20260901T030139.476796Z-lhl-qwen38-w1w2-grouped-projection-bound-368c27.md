# Reject grouped projection W1/W2 bound

## Summary

Inventoried same-input groups: 48 linear-attention Q6-Q4 qkv/gate pairs, 16
full-attention mixed Q4/Q4/Q6 q/k/v triples, and dense Q4 gate/up. The runtime
already attempts pair/triple/pair-silu owners; mixed quant groups fall back.
Fusing them cannot avoid distinct weight reads and only saves activation reads,
launches, and host gaps.

At current-head C8/R32 the median target pass is 51.22 ms host and 49.02 ms
summed kernels across 61 dispatches. Even deleting the entire non-kernel gap is
only 2.21 ms/pass (4.31% of target, much less of complete wall). Dominant Q6
device work remains. This cannot cover the remaining ~20% wall gap.

## Changes

Emitted a compact topology and upper-bound artifact. No code/default change.

## Validation

Bound is computed from the current-head baseline rocprof target windows and
source-level pair/triple call topology. It deliberately over-credits all host-
kernel gap to grouping.

## Next

Do not build cross-projection grouping. The credible W1 dataflow is within-
tensor persistent/M-loop reuse that reduces Q6 device time without multiplying
dispatches.
