# The wmma gate's drift is the rowtile's, named and measured

## Summary

The `wmma` gate's drift is now attributed to a named selector on real weights,
by measurement rather than by elimination - and getting there exposed two bugs in
my own summariser of the same shape as the misattributions it was built to catch.

## Attribution, measured

| selector | teacher rate | candidate rate | differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `q4k_rowtile` | **1.0** | **0.775128** | yes |
| `wmma_prefill` | 0.0 | 0.264 | yes |
| `native_batch_decode_session` | 0.0 | 0.0 | no - ruled out |

`teacher_route_engaged = False` (its `wmma_prefill` count is exactly 0),
`selectors_observed_in_one_arm_only` empty, so the attribution rests on both arms
being observed rather than on absence of evidence.

The 0.775128 is the finding: the candidate leaves `q4k_rowtile` **inactive for 792
launches** where the teacher had it active. Those are rows the WMMA route refuses,
so they are decode-path work - and the canonical prefill row is bit-identical
(5.8014e-12) while the decode row moves (1.0184e-04). The gate's drift is the
rowtile's, reached through a flag named for the route.

KL is unchanged by this analysis, which is the check that the fix moved
attribution rather than measurement.

## Two bugs in my own check

1. `teacher_route_engaged` was derived from total engagement across every
   selector. `q4k_rowtile` is always active in the teacher arm and correctly so,
   which made the flag unconditionally true and the verdict read "the teacher arm
   also engaged the route" on a run whose route count was zero.
2. The differs-comparison collapsed each arm to a boolean, so 1654/1654 and
   2730/3522 both read "active at least once" and the difference being hunted was
   discarded by the comparison itself.

Both fixed: the route flags come from the route's own selector, and the
comparison uses rates (comparable across arms with different repeat counts),
reporting `selector_states` with both rates. Two tests added.

Both are the same shape as the four misattributions this packet already carried -
a true-valued field narrower than its name. Seven instances now, three of them
mine, which is why the artifact is read through `selector_states` and the
per-selector counts rather than the verdict string.

## What is not established

That the rowtile difference is **harmful**. It is 1.0184e-04 KL on the decode
path - inside the broad smoke floor, not through the production profile gates.
Whether `q4k_rowtile` should remain bound to `use_wmma` at all is a separate
question from whether the gate is named correctly. It is the naming that was
wrong.
