# Measure IU4 prefill control baseline

## Summary

Completed punchlist B3: the missing current-owner dense-prefill control for
Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_S layer-0 gate/up+SiLU. Across M64/128/256/512/1024 the
operation-complete pack8 owner measures 3.869/3.937/4.170/8.240/16.234 ms,
32.06-34.56 effective payload GB/s, and 21.89-23.59 executed TFLOP/s. The
256-row block owns padded work at M64/M128 and one complete weight sweep per
row block. All rows reach only 39.75-42.84% of the measured 55.066-TFLOP/s F16
WMMA roof while remaining far below the practical memory roof, confirming that
bulk prefill is the correct arithmetic/dequantization surface for R7.

## Changes

- Added `scripts/qwen38_iu4_prefill_gate_up_leaf.py`, a control-only actual-
  weight harness that losslessly repacks Q4_K to pack8, runs two independent
  activation matrices in 15 counterbalanced HIP-event pairs after three paired
  warmups, and reports padded executed work, weight sweeps, payload bandwidth,
  F16-roof fraction, hashes, determinism, finiteness, and teardown.
- Added pure RED/GREEN metric tests, including the 256-row padding and repeated-
  weight-sweep contract.
- Added the compact B3 diagnostic artifact and updated `MTP-IU4.md` plus the
  benchmark changelog. No IU4 candidate or runtime route ran.
