# Classify Q5 Q6 WMMA decode projections in packed traces

## Summary

The packed profiler classified dense projections only when the kernel name
contained `gemv`. Q5/Q6 padded-WMMA decode kernels and Q5's output cast were
therefore emitted as `other`/unclassified. This hid 71.7 ms at c8 and 21.4 ms
at c5 from the family rollup and encouraged the invalid stage labels corrected
in `20260820T050808.468391Z`.

  `dense_projection`, alongside GEMV kernels.
- Pin Q5 WMMA, Q6 qmicro WMMA, and cast names in the focused profiler test.
