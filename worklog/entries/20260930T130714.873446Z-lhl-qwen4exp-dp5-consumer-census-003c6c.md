# DP-5's consumer census: the HC down projections are 64% of the cast, so the epilogue-first order is confirmed

## Summary

DP-5's named prerequisite is done. The row asked for a consumer census to confirm the HC
mix epilogue feeds the most dense consumers before the F16 write is moved into it. **It
does: the two HC down projections are 83.861 ms = 64.0% of the cast total**, the largest
single block, more than the other ten roles combined. DP-5's whole 131.001 ms is one
kernel, `q8_0_f32_to_f16_kernel`, under 12 roles on layers 16-47.

## Validation

- The cast total reconciles exactly with the row's own priced figure: the 12 role
  entries for `q8_0_f32_to_f16_kernel` sum to **131.001 ms**, matching the
  `131.001 ms of a 15,741.963 ms kernel union (0.83%)` already in the row.
- Layer scope read from the trace, not assumed: the `hc_*_down` roles carry
  exactly 32 layers each, first 16 last 47.
- Each HC down role's three stages were read from the same window and are
  mutually consistent: producer 64 launches, cast 128, consumer 128.
- Read-only re-read of a committed artifact; no GPU, no capture, no kernel.
- `python3 scripts/worklog.py check` passes.

## Why the census was worth doing before the build

The row had already priced the row at 131.001 ms and already named its intended first
move. What it did not have was evidence that the intended move was the biggest one - and
the census shows it is 64.0% of the total, so the build can be scoped to two roles
instead of twelve and still capture most of the row.

It also turned up two things the row did not state: the cast exists **only on layers
16-47** (32 layers, the promoted scope), and the producer it would be folded into is
already the model's largest kernel at 21.39% of the union.

## The estimate is flagged as an estimate

87.3 ms for the whole row and 55.9 ms for the HC down pair are derived from a 4/6
traffic ratio, not measured. The cast could be launch-bound (0.331 ms per launch on
`hc_ffn_down`), which would make the recovery larger, and the producer's epilogue is not
free, which would make it smaller. The A/B decides.
