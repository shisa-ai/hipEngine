# Audit historical Vulkan configuration axes

## Summary

Rebuilt and audited both historical Vulkan comparators. Graphics queue and
explicit fit-off are entitled configurations for both lanes.

## Changes

- Rebuilt upstream at `f1793c1c4` and Nathan source at `ad914eb` with Vulkan.
- Ran queue off/on, no-repack, and fit-on exact code-p512 arms under auto clocks,
  each with one warmup and three measured repetitions.

## Validation

- Upstream queue-on improves decode 2.23% with flat prefill; Nathan improves
  decode 2.31% with a 0.40% prefill tradeoff.
- Upstream no-repack is neutral. Nathan no-repack loses 1.02% prefill.
- Fit-on loses 8.86% upstream and 1.79% Nathan prefill.
- Upstream outputs are exact across arms/repetitions. Nathan has the same four
  position-dependent hashes in every arm: configuration-invariant but still
  nondeterministic and diagnostic.
