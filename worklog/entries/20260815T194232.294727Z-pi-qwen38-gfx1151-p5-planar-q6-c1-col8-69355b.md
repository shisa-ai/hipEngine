# Screen Qwen3.8 gfx1151 planar-Q6 c1 col8

## Summary

Reject the existing exact planar-qmicro col8 rowtile body as a serial-c1 owner
for Qwen3.8's remaining largest unclosed decode family. The recovered retained
post-Q5 graph assigns **1,965.469 ms / 18.91%** of selected kernel wall to
planar-Q6 BF16/F32 consumers; the current c1 owner is local128, 80 VGPR,
512-byte LDS, and zero scratch. The col8 candidate is bit-identical but
regresses every actual role and projects **-13.495 ms / -0.130%** selected
wall, so the direct 16-column c1 owner remains.

## Changes

- Temporarily extended only the explicit planar-qmicro col8 wrapper to
  instantiate the already-registered rowtile body at `ROW_TILE=1`; production
  remained on the 16-column c1 wrapper. The transient instantiation and test
  expansion are removed after rejection.
- Preserve each output's K ownership, FMA stream, wave32 reduction, ordered
  wave-0..3 block reduction, and BF16/F32 store. The sole change is two
  independent eight-column owners over the same byte-neutral T16 tile.
- RED-first is not practical for output values: before the selector change the
  explicit wrapper deliberately falls back to the bit-identical direct c1
  owner. Extend both BF16 and F32 exactness fixtures and require named-kernel
  tracing to prove the new route actually executes.
- Kernel-lineage audit is blocked before filtering by the absent optional
  `/home/lhl/amd-gpu-tuning/reference/atlas` checkout; no external body is
  copied.
