# Ledger rule 9: three prefill-window denominators are in use, and a falsified provenance hypothesis

## Summary

The ledger quotes shares of "the window", but **three different prefill-window denominators
are in use**, spanning **44%**:

| window | source | used by |
|---:|---|---|
| **22,698.6 ms** | `role-analysis.json` at `725794c3f` - the §8 rule-1 **1x authority**, 9,652 kernel rows | MR-1, QA-5 |
| **16,319.9 ms** | the 2026-09-28 `qwen4exp-current-attribution` capture at `2789c19d6` | reconciliation capture X-3's status names |
| **15,742.0 ms** | that same capture's family-taxonomy union (excludes time outside the taxonomy) | HC-7 |

**This is a reading hazard, not a data error.** Every row is internally consistent and each
states its own basis. But a share quoted against one base is not comparable to a share quoted
against another, and the ledger did not say so anywhere.

## My opening hypothesis was falsified

I began this investigation expecting a **provenance error**: MR-1 and QA-5 attribute a
"window 22,698.6 ms" to *X-3*, while X-3's status names commit `2789c19d6`, whose packet has
`window_ms = 16,319.89`. That looked like a citation pointing at the wrong capture.

**It is not.** §8 rule 1 states plainly: *"The 1x authority is `role-analysis.json`
`exact_role_kernels` (`2026-09-17-qwen4exp-per-role-cost`)"* - and that is exactly the file
carrying 22,698.6 ms and 9,652 rows. So **MR-1 and QA-5 are correct**, and X-3's action
correctly cites the rule. The 2026-09-28 capture is the *reconciliation* capture, not the
ranking authority, which is why its window differs.

I am recording the falsification because the "wrong window" reading is the natural first
hypothesis and the next reader will form it too. Checking the declared authority **before**
writing anything up is what settled it.
