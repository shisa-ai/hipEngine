# Add deterministic SPECDEC2 K0 policy planner

## Summary

Completed SPECDEC2 S1 with a deterministic pure pre-mutation planner. It selects
per-request K or K0 from an immutable composed capability and typed request
semantics, then emits one validated `SpecRequestPlan` with honest proposal and
target real-row decomposition. No provider/target owner or resource is opened by
planning.

The policy conservatively selects stable K0 reasons for missing capability,
unsupported sampling/mode, context/output room, group claim miss, circuit
breaker, unavailable physical/graph/eager route, or an unsupported composed
shape. Qualified eager execution is used when graph replay is unavailable but
eager is declared.
