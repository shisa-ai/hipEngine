# UD gfx1100 port: dense-IQ W4A16 policy and Q5 T16 role coverage on the W7900

## Summary

Route-plan item F executed on the W7900 (GPU0, gfx1100). Two ports landed:
the four-quant W4A16 dense-IQ prefill policy (`GGUF_IQ_DENSE_PREFILL_POLICY`,
previously gfx1151-only) and the item-2 Q5 dense role coverage
(`GGUF_DENSE_Q5_T16_H5120`, the e35a032bd role set, also gfx1151-only until
now). The Q5 flag was the dominant one: a rocprof census of the pre-port run
showed the raw-K coltile rowbatch owner taking 74% of the 512-token prefill
GPU budget because the UD files' Q5_K tensors never left the raw layout on
gfx1100. With both ports the materialization plan is identical between the
two HIP backends (61.3% optimized bytes on K_M, zero raw Q5 tensors) and the
UD-vs-plain comparison on the W7900 reaches the campaign's zbook end state.

The W4A16 kernels themselves needed no port: the module lives in
hip_gfx1100/quant and registers on both backends already.

The zbook campaign ended at 0.51x prefill / 0.61x decode — gfx1100 now
matches that state, so the remaining gap is campaign-wide (Q3_K mean-gate
blocker, item C decode), not a gfx1100 port gap.

W4A16 policy A/B at max-layers 8: 80.2 → 136.8 tok/s prefill (route engaged),
decode unchanged as designed.

Post-port prefill census (rocprofv3, 512/8): W4A16 IQ4_XS 26.1%, Q5 T16 WMMA
16.4%, Q4 T16 8.1%, IQ4_NL W4A16 1.7% — all optimized owners. Q3_K strict
10.2% is the deliberate mean-gate holdout.

Measurement caveat recorded: the first four-arm sweep ran while the peer GPU1
job was active and the UD arms degraded badly under that host contention
(decode 3.73 contended vs 15.52 clean at the same tree state); the plain arms
were contention-insensitive (856.2 vs 868.7 prefill). UD arms were re-measured
on a quiet host and the artifact uses those numbers.
