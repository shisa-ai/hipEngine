# Promote the dense-wide Q8 prefill route to layers 13-47

## Summary

**`PRODUCTION_Q8_DENSE_WIDE_PREFILL_LAYERS` moves from `range(16, 48)` to
`range(13, 48)`.** The dense-wide Q8 prefill route now covers three more layers.

**Why this is admissible:** the scope passes every binding production gate on the
12-case canonical protocol - `hard_gates_passed = true`, `kl_mean` **6.229e-4**
(limit 1e-3), `kl_p95` **3.158e-3** (5e-3), `kl_max` **2.606e-2** (5e-2), top-1
**0.99354** (0.99) - and all four categories clear their 0.97 floors (code
0.99225, general_en 0.98708, general_ja 1.0, mixed_ja_en 0.99483).

**It is also numerically stronger than the scope it extends.** The previously
deepest passing scope, 14-47, scores top-1 0.99225 and `kl_max` 3.979e-2, both
**worse** than 13-47's 0.99354 and 2.606e-2. So this is not a speed-for-quality
trade against the passing frontier - it improves both axes.

**Speed, three-arm A/B/C at `code-p4096`, chunk 1024**, one process per arm,
1 warmup + 3 repetitions, baseline on both sides:

| arm | scope | prefill tok/s | decode tok/s |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| A | 16-47 | 268.30 | 17.955 |
| B | **13-47** | **277.70** | **18.088** |
| C | 16-47 | 272.54 | 17.891 |

Same-code drift A against C **+1.58%**; pooled effect **+2.69% prefill** and
**+0.92% decode**.

## Changes

- `hipengine/generation/qwen4_exp_profiles.py`:
  `PRODUCTION_Q8_DENSE_WIDE_PREFILL_LAYERS = tuple(range(13, 48))`.

## Validation

**Gate** (`scripts/execution_profile_q8_wmma_prefill_layers_gate.py --route
dense_wide --layers 13-47 --decode-steps 128 --repeat-runs 3 --execution-profile
production --prefill-chunk-size 1024 --require-cached-build`): every binding gate
passes with 1.6-1.9x margin, `hard_gates_passed` true. Artifact
`benchmarks/results/2026-10-04-qwen4exp-dense-wide-13-47-frontier/`.

**Frontier located by testing every point, not by monotone search.** 11-47 fails
`kl_max` at 1.39x, 12-47 fails top-1 by one row, 10-47 is worse than 12-47 on
top-1, and 14-47 and 16-47 pass. The failure axes differ between neighbouring
scopes, which is why the boundary could not be bracketed.

**Harness sanity check:** 12-47 was re-run and reproduced the recorded ladder
figures exactly (`kl_mean` 6.4056e-4 against 6.406e-4, `kl_max` 3.2486e-2 against
3.249e-2, top-1 0.98966 against 1532/1548).

**Speed caveat, recorded as a limit rather than a blocker.** The +2.69% carries a
signal-to-drift ratio of only **1.7x**, against a scope ladder that declined a
+1.69% effect at 1.2x. It is retained because arm B is temporally between A and C
and exceeds **both** (277.70 against 268.30 and 272.54) - a monotone session trend
cannot produce that - so the effect is bounded below by **+1.89%** and above by
**+3.50%**. The within-arm spread is 0.06-0.08%. **A headline number should not be
quoted from this at 1.7x; tightening it needs more repetitions, not a different
design.** This does not block promotion because the quality gate is the binding
contract and it passes on its own terms.

**Limits:** one host (gfx1151 Strix Halo, Radeon 8060S, not the gfx1100 default
hardware), one model, one quant, one profile, one chunk size; the speed claim is
one case while the gate is the 12-case protocol; 13 layers' worth of KV rounding
changes, so generated IDs are not expected to match the strict parent and are not
required to.

## Next

**Rollback is the one-line constant.** If a longer-context or multi-category suite
regresses, restore `range(16, 48)`.

**The larger lever remains the arithmetic.** Extending to 8-47 is worth **+8.52%**
prefill and is gated by drift from layers 8-13 - the same layers this promotion now
covers three of. `benchmarks/results/2026-10-04-qwen4exp-dense-wide-scope-frontier/`
records the mechanism: the drift enters through decode via the rounding of
projections feeding the KV cache. The next unit is to compare the wide route's
kernel arithmetic against its coltile-chain parent for the layers that fail, and
try a more accurate accumulation there.
