# Probability residual screen fails and occupancy control exposes warm decode denominator

## Summary

Retaining the softmax probability's BF16/FP16 residual in a second P*V WMMA
operation does not repair the four saved strict-teacher chains. Full-only
residual max KL on mixed summary is 0.216658; both full and sliding residuals
reach 0.430080, with top-1 agreement 0.953125 on that case. Neither arm passes
all per-case bars. The prototype remains external; no shipped kernel changed.

Selecting both strict attention variants in the production-created runner
reproduces the saved strict-created-runner logits bitwise on all four chains:
zero maximum absolute difference and KL. This controls for other runner settings
and supports attention arithmetic as the cause within this diagnostic. Replacing
only one half was insufficient; probability rounding is not the sole explanation.

A one-wave occupancy control calibrates MeanOccupancyPerCU correctly: 0.02498
against its 1/40 = 0.025 maximum; forty waves give 0.9996 against 1.0. The active
metric reports 2.0 for forty waves, consistent with WGP rather than CU active
normalization on this device. That interpretation is not yet a hardware contract.

Removing LDS conflict/stall counters from the current-kernel group does not fix
the impossible warm decode reading. Raw counters show its denominator problem:
GRBM_GUI_ACTIVE is 461,182 over 1.602 ms, versus 4,356,933 over 1.881 ms for the
first decode dispatch. The derived warm occupancy cannot be used: it exceeds
128 launched waves / 40 CUs. Prefill counters instead replicate approximately
6.5-6.6 waves/CU across groups, with GRBM active cycles consistent with about
2.4 GHz over the measured dispatches. They are useful diagnostic estimates, not
proof of a resource ceiling or of a speedup available from raising occupancy.
