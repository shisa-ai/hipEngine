## Changes

`hipengine_gguf_q4_k_quantize_f32_q8_1` and routed
`HIPENGINE_RESIDENT_MTP_DRAFT_Q6_TOP1_DP4A=1` through direct
`rmsnorm_f32 -> f32 q8_1 quantize -> q6 dp4a top1`, skipping the BF16 cast before
