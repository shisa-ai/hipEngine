Stated assumptions, so a miss is interpretable: (a) grouping leaves the decode component
untouched - supported because generated ids are identical with and without grouping;
(b) admission inside a 24-token run behaves like admission in the one-token packet -
this is the weakest link, since a 24-token run overlaps prefill with other lanes'
decode, and is the specific reason the prediction could undershoot or overshoot;
(c) no scheduler-thrash effect at the widths where the grouped row itself dipped (C5,
C8).

If `bg-58` lands materially below this, the overlap assumption (b) is the first thing to
doubt, and the grouped admission wall should be re-measured inside the D24 packet rather
than imported from the one-token arm.
