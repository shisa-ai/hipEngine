# XTX capacity: Q4_K_S is memory-dominated by Q4_K_M on gfx1100

## Summary

Packet 3's bytes comparison (throughput/quality still open) measured both Q4_K_M
and Q4_K_S through the allocation-ledger harness on the XTX (BF16 KV, 3072
context, N=1, one cold in-process server each). **Q4_K_S loses on every memory
axis despite its smaller file**: planned resident 16.168 vs 15.992 GiB
(+0.176), load sysfs 20.562 vs 18.429 GiB (+2.133), request peak 21.3 vs
19.0 GiB-class (+2.061). The explanation is the variant's quant mix: Q4_K_S
stores 8 `ffn_down` (170.00 MiB each), 3 `attn_qkv` (100.00 MiB each) and 1
`attn_v` (10.00 MiB) as BF16 **inside the GGUF** (1.631 GiB dense_bf16
residents vs 0.078 in Q4_K_M), shifts mass from Q6 (4.112 -> 0.971 GiB) to Q4
T16 (10.049 -> 11.814 GiB), and carries ~2 GiB more tracked load-stage
staging. For the 24 GB capacity campaign Q4_K_M is the retention candidate;
Q4_K_S buys no capacity on this stack.
