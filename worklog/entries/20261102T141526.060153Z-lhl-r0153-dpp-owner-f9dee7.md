# Add owner-bound Q8 DPP reduction transport

## Summary

Carry an explicit Q8 DPP reduction choice on generator/current and late runner
owners, entering it around each prefill chunk. Keep unbound diagnostic process
inputs until their callers migrate; no profile/default or kernel changes.

## Changes

- Add restoring `q8_dpp_reduction_enabled(owner, enabled)` owner scopes,
  constructor/clone inheritance and a context-local linear launch session.
- Selector and dispatch-cache identity now consume the same effective choice.
  Explicit booleans canonicalize to `0`/`1`; unbound callers retain raw-string
  memoization and the existing default-on behavior. Both plain DPP and its
  checked complete-row sibling still follow the one family decision.
- Tests cover nested/missing/distinct scopes, exception restoration, late
  inheritance, prefill-chunk transport and cache separation/reuse with opposing
  process input. Public checks add an owner interface alongside the maintained
  legacy campaign interface and compare final dispatches, logits and teardown.
- Campaign/state/layer-gate/generic diagnostics and profile publication are
  follow-ups, not silently changed by this transport addition.
