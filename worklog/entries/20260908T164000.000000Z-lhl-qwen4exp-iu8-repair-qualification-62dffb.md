# R4a: qualify the iu8 repair criterion at the production multiplier

## Summary

Executed review punchlist R4a for the promoted exact iu8-risk+repair
selected Q4 gate/up route. Built the durable production-multiplier-4 test
suite (bit-identity to the pair2 parent at exactly 4.0 across skewed/empty
experts, 16-row tile tails, cancellation-heavy, tie-targeted,
wide-magnitude, subnormal-adjacent, deep-subnormal and huge-magnitude
activations; nonfinite contamination; risk-queue overflow boundedness;
deterministic repair) and a calibration sweep script over activation scales
2^0..2^-130.

Two genuine criterion holes were found and fixed (RED before, GREEN after):

1. **Nonfinite activations silently dropped.** The iu8 staging computes its
   block amax with fmaxf, which ignores NaN, and the activation-to-int8
   conversion of Inf collapses to zero; all-NaN rows produced exactly-zero
   finite outputs that the boundary-distance criterion (INFINITY for
   nonfinite) never queued. The parent propagates NaN bit patterns, so the
   route's exactness claim failed for nonfinite inputs.
2. **Tiny activation scales break the Kahan bound.** At activation scales
   below ~2^-100 the fp32 intermediates enter the subnormal range and the
   Kahan/eps bound collapses with them while the true iu8 error grows:
   escapes begin between 2^-100 and 2^-110 (2-8 BF16 ulps) and reach ~100%
   of outputs (sign-crossing garbage) by 2^-115. Real activations never
   approach this regime, but BF16 represents it, so the contract must hold.

Fix (`62dffb872`): a row-level at-risk flag in the risk kernel's staging
phase — any nonfinite activation value or a raw 32-element subblock amax
below 2^-80 marks the row; flagged rows have every output queued and the
sparse exact repair restores the parent's arithmetic. The floor keeps a
>=2^20 margin over the measured onset and never fires on real activations
(risk counts unchanged at scales 2^0..2^-80). An earlier version of this
edit had an indexing bug (expert-relative local_row instead of the
tile-relative r on tail tiles, reading past the 16-entry shared flag array);
caught by the skewed-fixture tests before any commit.
