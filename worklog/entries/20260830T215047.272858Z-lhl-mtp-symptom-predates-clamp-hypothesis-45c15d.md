# a superseded artifact already recorded the MTP non-packing symptom the clamp would cause

## Summary

While tracing the four-request MTP clamp I found that a prior decomposition had
already recorded the symptom that clamp would produce, plus a second independent MTP
defect. The successor artifact superseded the AR rows but names the MTP arm as not
recomputed, so the mechanism description is available while fresh numbers are not.

## Changes

- None. Read from
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-c1c8-submodule-decomposition.json`.

## Validation

Its findings, quoted in substance:

- "MTP cycles do not pack across lanes", costing a flat ~85-107 ms per lane-cycle, so
  one wave cycle costs 107 ms at C1 and 680 ms at C8. That is per-lane serialization,
  which is what a grouping ceiling looks like from the outside.
- A separate defect: the native GPU-accept path loses about 40 percent of accepts.
- On the K3 arm at C8, our admission alone (2311.9 ms) exceeds the peer's entire
  24-token C8 wall (2026.7 ms current, 1899.6 ms Laurent).
- For AR, admission scales 302.4 to 2320.7 ms across C1-C8 (7.68x) against the peer's
  223.4 to 847.0 (3.79x), because our admission does not overlap requests. Every AR
  cell we lose is admission while our decode step is ahead (34.1 vs 38.3 ms at C1,
  81.9 vs 105.3 at C8).

Why this matters for #13: the clamp found at qwen35_gguf_mtp2.py:1029 caps requests per
group at four and lines 1392/1405 refuse a fifth. Cycles not packing across lanes is
the expected external shape of exactly that. So the ordering is: symptom recorded
first by measurement, candidate mechanism found later by reading. Neither is proven
without a group-size count at C5-C8.

Two honesty notes. The artifact is marked superseded, and its MTP headline of about
31 tok/s does not match the current matrix rows (47.960 at C5, 55.860 at C8), so its
absolute MTP numbers are stale and only its mechanism descriptions should be reused.
The successor artifact, which is the one to trust for AR, explicitly lists the MTP arm
as not recomputed.
