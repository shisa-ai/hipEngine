# Decode campaign validation and publication — the final rollup

## Summary

The decode campaign is closed: K_M 26.20 -> 29.98 tok/s (+14.4%), K_S
25.53 -> 29.49 (+15.5%), four-arm decode ratio 0.769x/0.836x ->
**0.848x (K_M) / 0.804x (K_S)**, pure-kernel decode 35.23 -> 30.26 and
36.16 -> 30.02 ms/tok, launches 898 -> 831 and 918 -> 839 per token;
prefill unchanged. Both artifacts pass the tokenized category gate on
the final stack (K_M max 1.484e-2, K_S max 2.014e-2, top-1 99.91%).

## Measurement caveats (recorded, not hidden)

- The plain arms crash under rocprofv3 on the XTX (JIT-compile
  interception faults, reproducible); their W7900 censuses read
  30.48/28.73 ms per token, but the matching W7900 UD runs came out
  inflated past wall (profiler serialization on the slower card with
  the 831-launch stack), so the cross-arm pure gap is published from
  wall, not mixed-card traces.
- The campaign-start census keyed kernels on a truncated name, merging
  the whole direct-GEMV family under the Q5 label: the "7.60 ms / 113
  launches" was the family total; the Q5 dense singles are 69 launches.
- plain K_M sweeps crashed twice on GPU faults/OOM before the clean GPU1
  run (the passing runs are the published ones).
