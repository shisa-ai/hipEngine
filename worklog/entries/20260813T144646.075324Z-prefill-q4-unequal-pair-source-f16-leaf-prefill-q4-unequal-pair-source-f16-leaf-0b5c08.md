# Retain Q4 unequal-pair source-F16 leaf

## Summary

Retained a runtime-unwired changed-arithmetic leaf for the 24 Q4/Q4
linear-attention QKV+gate pairs. The candidate replaces the current exact
unequal-output dual-WMMA operation with one shared activation cast, exact
adjacent-pair-owned source-F16 production for both sole-resident T16 weights,
rocBLAS FP16 GEMMs, and BF16 publication.

## Changes

- Added
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-13-qwen36-27b-q4-unequal-pair-source-f16-leaf-retained.json`
  with all raw-source hashes, counterbalanced samples, quality gates, and the
  fail-closed runtime contract.
- Added the benchmark changelog entry. Production dispatch, package policy,
  memory ownership, decode, MTP, and public toplines remain unchanged in this
  leaf unit.

## Validation

- Actual `blk.8.attn_qkv.weight` and `blk.8.attn_gate.weight`, K5,120 with
  N10,240+N6,144: selected confidence medians improve XTX **1.075x/1.051x**
  and W7900 **1.120x/1.025x** at M512/M1024, with **91/92** pairs winning.
  Across the initial and confidence runs, **120/124** pairs win.
- The early XTX M512 order-sensitive losses do not reproduce after stabilization:
  15 warmups plus 31 counterbalanced pairs improve **1.0749x**, all **31/31**.
- Both output matrices are finite. Top-1 versus the exact unequal-Q4 owner is
  **94.92-96.00%**, max abs is **0.027344**, and mean abs is at most
  **0.00115254**, passing the kernel quality gate.
- The candidate uses only the existing 27-MiB transient planes and adds no
  persistent or workspace bytes. The proposed route remains model/shape/row
  bounded to M512-M2047; M2048/4K, decode, MTP, peers, standalone calls, and
  all misses remain exact.
- JSON syntax, source hashes, worklog schema, and diff hygiene pass.
