# TP2 uneven split: 0.44/0.56 is retained and passes the production gate

## Summary

This entry corrects the framing of
`20260918T151851.718167Z-lhl-tp2-uneven-split-gate-blocked-2a2557.md`. That entry
correctly reported that the balance solve's optimum (`0.417145/0.582855`, widths
7168/10240) fails the production max-KL ceiling, but it left the impression that
the uneven split as a whole is uncertified and that the even split is the only
option. A split sweep answered that: **the retained uneven split is 0.44/0.56
(widths 7680/9728) and it passes the full production numerical envelope over the
complete suite depth**, with 100% top-1 and zero flipped rows.

Sweep, all at 18 prompts / 831 teacher-forced positions / 3-sweep determinism /
`tp2` scored against both single-GPU controls (`score_arm(tp1_control, tp2_arm)`):

| split | widths (r0/r1) | mean KL | p99 KL | max KL | top-1 | flips | gate |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| even (default) | 8704/8704 | 2.32e-04 | — | 0.0121 | 100% | 0 | pass |
| 0.417 (solve optimum) | 7168/10240 | 3.272e-04 | 5.353e-03 | 0.069946 | 99.64% | 3 | **fail** |
| 0.43 | 7424/9984 | 3.206e-04 | — | 0.100556 | 99.52% | 4 | **fail** |
| **0.44 (retained)** | **7680/9728** | **2.430e-04** | **3.334e-03** | **0.014126** | **100%** | **0** | **pass** |
| 0.45 | 7936/9472 | 2.278e-04 | — | 0.015672 | 99.88% | 1 | pass |

The retained 0.44 run in full
(`benchmarks/results/2026-09-18-w7900-tp2-uneven-split-044-gate-passed.json`):
831 rows, mean **2.430e-04** (bar 1e-3), p95 **9.087e-04** (bar 5e-3), p99
**3.334e-03** (bar 2e-2), max **0.014126** (bar 5e-2, 3.5x margin), top-1
**100%** (bar 99%), **0 flipped rows**, and 100% top-1 with 0 flips in *every*
category (code 272 rows, general_en 164, general_ja 168, mixed_ja_en 227).
Determinism: 3 sweeps, per-row match, no mismatched sweeps, no non-finite rows.
The two single-GPU controls are byte-identical to each other
(`teacher_cross_check`: max_abs_diff **0.0**, digest `b898c101…` on both), and
reset/reuse state boundaries are bit-exact. Wall 540 s. No `--horizon` was
passed, so the run scored every position in every prompt — deeper than the D=42
comparison horizon that `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` 6.5 declares.

Why 0.44 rather than the solve optimum: the max-KL tail is **not monotone in the
move**. 0.417 breaches at 0.0699, 0.43 breaches *worse* at 0.1006, and 0.44 comes
back inside the envelope at 0.0141. That is the ULP-route-noise regime the
2026-09-18 horizon work already characterized: the tail is a realization, not a
designable quantity, so the split is chosen from measured passing candidates and
the largest passing move (0.44, which is also the one with 100% top-1 and zero
flips) is the one retained.

Status of the split: **opt-in, not default.** The even split stays the shipped
TP2 configuration until a clean-revision certifying run exists; the 0.44 boundary
is reachable through the shard-fraction option and its widths are admitted to the
pair+SiLU allowlist so it keeps the fused route.
