# Unblock the Q8_0 wide-row dense prefill route at its certified scope

## Summary

`dense_wide256` could not run in any configuration. Its own gates, its layer
scope and its registration were all correct, and none of them were the reason:
the f16 WMMA prefill rewrite runs **earlier in the launch chain** and changes the
ABI out from under the selector, and the promoted production default binds that
route to the same layers the wide route was scoped to. The selector returned on
its first condition — `dispatch.abi != "raw"` — before its gates, its scope or
its registration were consulted.

This unit makes the wide route claim the rewritten parent inside its own scope,
makes every remaining decline name the blocking family, repairs the gate script
that could not run, and gives the launch census sight of both dense Q8_0
families.

## Root cause, and how it was confirmed

`_wmma_prefill_dispatch` (`hipengine/runtime/gguf_linear.py`, earlier in
`launch_gguf_linear`) rewrites a claimable parent to `wmma_<parent>` under ABI
`wmma_raw`. `_q8_dense_wide_dispatch` ran after it and accepted only the three
exact coltile parents under ABI `raw`. With
`PRODUCTION_Q8_WMMA_PREFILL_LAYERS = range(16, 48)` bound by the production
binder for `gguf_ud_q4_k_xl`, the two scopes were disjoint in the worst way:

| Layer window | Parent the wide selector received | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16-47 (the certified window) | `wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out` / `wmma_raw` | declined on the ABI check, silently, before any diagnostic |
| 0-15 | `coltile8_rowbatch4_wave_scale_f32_f32_out` / `raw` | passed every gate, then declined on the layer scope |

Evidence, all from the instrumented run's own output on disk
(`HIPENGINE_DW_DEBUG=1`, `code-p512`, wide enabled at 16-47): 134
`DW-GATES-OK` lines and **zero** `DW-KEY` lines. The gates passed and the
registration was never reached, which is why registering the family changed
nothing and the "not registered" warning never fired. The 134 lines are exactly
the layers 0-15 dispatches; layers 16-47 never reached the first print.

## Changes

- `hipengine/runtime/gguf_linear.py`: `_q8_dense_wide_claims_parent` accepts the
  WMMA-rewritten parent (`wmma_<exact parent>` under `wmma_raw`) as well as the
  exact parents. Both families are f16-operand WMMA, so this is the wide
  kernel's own arithmetic class, and the layer scope remains the authority on
  where the route runs. The selector is also restructured so that "enabled,
  eligible shape, unclaimable parent" emits a `RuntimeWarning` naming the
  blocking variant instead of returning quietly; the registration warning is
  kept.
- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q8_0_prefill.py` and
  `.../gguf_q8_0_dense_wide.py`: both families now call
  `launch_census.record_launch`. The census previously recorded only from
  `gguf_k_gemv`, so it could not see either family — see the correction below.
- `hipengine/kernels/launch_census.py`: docstring states the per-module coverage
  boundary and that an empty row means "not counted", not "did not run".
- `scripts/execution_profile_q8_wmma_prefill_layers_gate.py`: the half-finished
  `--route` edit raised `NameError` on `SELECTORS`/`_select_route`, so the gate
  could not start at all. Completed with a `Route` table: `wmma` (unchanged
  bindings, so the certified figures stay reproducible) and `dense_wide`, whose
  candidate arm clears the WMMA selector so the comparison carries the
  candidate's arithmetic only.
- Tests: `tests/test_unit_qwen4exp_dense_wide_dispatch.py` (the chain
  composition, the scope not widening, the diagnostic naming the blocker, the
  chain-order contract), `tests/test_unit_q8_dense_prefill_route_gate.py` (route
  table contract), `tests/test_unit_gguf_launch_census_coverage.py` (both
  families record).

  quality verdict, and the wall times from these runs are not a performance
  result (single repetition, model load in between, no interleaving).

## Correction to a retained claim

The 2026-09-17 promotion entry (`20260916T192306.422564Z-lhl-q8-wmma-1647-promotion-b024aa`)
reports "2144 launches of `coltile8_rowbatch4_wave_scale` and **zero
`wmma_prefill` launches**" on the new default and concludes the +4.542 s "does
not come from the Q8 dense linear route". That census could not see the WMMA
family: `record_launch` was called only from `gguf_k_gemv`, and the 134 roles it
did see were exactly the layers the WMMA route had not claimed. The default arm
re-run in this unit, with the census fixed, records 264 roles on
`hipengine_gguf_q8_0_wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out` at layers 16-47 — the family the
entry reported as never launching. The entry's *timing* and *numerical*
evidence stand; its attribution of the win away from the dense Q8 linear route
does not.
