# DP-1's reopening route is closed by measurement - the staged f32 FMA tile fails at 0-15

## Summary

Returning to the objective (work through the ledger's remaining candidates), the
top-ranked open §4 row is **`DP-1` - "Layers 0-15 on the exact chain: 3262.5
ms"**, the largest single measured cost in the prefill section.

**`DP-1` is closed as a scope change**, and its cell names exactly one way to
reopen it:

> "Reopening needs an arithmetic change, not a scope change. The matrix cores
> take only f16/bf16 operands, so the wide tile's operand precision cannot be
> improved in place; **the remaining route is an LDS-staged f32 FMA tile that
> keeps exact arithmetic and takes the wide tile's weight-traffic advantage.**"

**That route was built and measured. It fails at 0-15 on three independent
criteria.** The ledger presented it as still remaining, and the result appeared
in neither §7 of the ledger nor the benchmark changelog - so this is a registry
correction as much as a rejection.

## The measurement

`staged_f32_bm128_bn64_bk32` at layers 0-15, teacher
`coltile8_rowbatch4_f32_f32_out`, WMMA and dense_wide cleared in both arms:

| criterion | measured | limit | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `kl_mean` | **1.17743e-3** | 1e-3 | **FAIL** |
| `kl_p95` | **6.12058e-3** | 5e-3 | **FAIL** |
| top-1 | **0.986434** | 0.99 | **FAIL** |
| rows over review | 7 | 0 | |

Protocol verified canonical before citing it: 12 cases, 1548 rows, 128 decode
steps, chunk 1024, 3 repeat runs, canonical fixture `42b562bd8e9644be…`,
determinism passed on all 12 prompts. Layers **0-11 are worse** (mean 1.274e-3,
10 rows over review).

**The same mechanism passes at 16-47**: mean 3.236e-4, p95 1.629e-3, max
1.610e-2, top-1 0.99483, no rows over review, `status: complete`.

## The substantive point

The mechanism is the one `DP-1` asked for - it "keeps the staging and tiling and
does the multiply in f32 FMA on f32 operands, so the only arithmetic difference
from the exact chain is the summation order" (kernel docstring). **Its own
summation-order drift is enough to fail at 0-15 while passing at 16-47.**

So the boundary is **which layers are converted, not the tile**. That is a
sharper statement than "0-15 cannot be moved": the arithmetic is admissible at
16-47 and inadmissible at 0-15, which points at the recurrent/GDN block in the
lower layers interacting with the reassociation rather than at the tile design.

## Two implementations, kept distinct

The ledger's route and the `HC-1` work are different kernels and I checked
before writing:

- `staged_f32_bm128_bn64_bk32` - the gate's `staged_f32` route, **measured
  here**, and the one that fails at 0-15.
- `coltile2_branch4_rowbatch4_staged_f32` - the GR-up-specific sibling
  (`HC-1`, `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_GR_STAGED`), registered default-off, whose
  docstring records that it "has not crossed the GR prefill-boundary KL screen".

Both are LDS-staged f32 FMA. Only the first has a 0-15 result, so the rejection
is scoped to it and the §7 row says so.
