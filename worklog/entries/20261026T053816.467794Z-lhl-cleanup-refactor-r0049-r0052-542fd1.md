### R0052 flash decode fallback -- keep, section rewritten

The wrapper's fallback is reachable and the recorded removal condition would
have broken a caller:

- `_decode_flash_bf16` (`gemma4_attention.py:1241`) asks `flash_request_slices`
  (`:1266`) and `flash_serves` (`:1274`) before requesting flash, and delegates
  to `gemma4_attention_staged_bf16` on rejection.
- The layer selector (`gemma4_layer.py:880`) is one caller, and nothing it
  admits reaches the fallback -- that half of the entry was correct.
- The MTP assistant head calls the long-row wrapper **directly**, bypassing any
  selector: `hipengine/runtime/gemma4_assistant.py:730` (single row) and `:1277`
  (packed rows) launch `gemma4_attention_decode_flash_long_bf16` for every
  draft row. Its geometry comes from the assistant artifact, so a packing
  `flash_admits` (`gemma4_attention.py:579`) does not implement -- anything but
  head_dim 256/ratio 2 or head_dim 512/ratio 8 -- reaches the wrapper with no
  route decision but this one. Dropping the check would put that path on the
  launcher's class-kernel fallback (`_launch_prefill`, `:1036` sets
  `flash = flash and request_slices > 0`), the 225 ms-vs-73 ms regression the
  section measured.
- The unit battery already pins the fallback for a shape no selector can admit:
  `tests/test_unit_gemma4_decode_attention_selection.py:580` drives
  `head_dim=128` to the staged chain.

The recorded blocker was also mis-costed: the admission is two ctypes calls the
launcher already makes on the same launch, so moving it one frame earlier is
cost-neutral per launch; the reason to keep the check is the second caller.
Section rewritten as a keep record. No code change.
