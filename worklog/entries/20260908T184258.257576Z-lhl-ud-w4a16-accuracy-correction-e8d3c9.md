# Correction: W4A16 does not recover the mean

## Summary

Supersedes the accuracy claim in
`20260908T182230.589531Z-lhl-ud-iq-w4a16-prefill-72d4f7.md` and commit
`0bf5e5dc7`, which stated that W4A16 "reaches the strict GEMV's accuracy".
**That is not supported.** The leaf measurement it rested on could not resolve
the two paths, and the model-level gate shows W4A16 landing with the integer
MMQ, not with the strict GEMV.

W4A16 is therefore **dominated**: 12% slower end to end with no model-level
mean benefit. The integer MMQ remains the production owner, now for a stronger
reason than before.

## Changes

No code change. The policy was temporarily routed to W4A16 for measurement and
restored; `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1151/__init__.py` is byte-identical to
`0bf5e5dc7`.

## What was measured

End-to-end, published sweep protocol, 512/128, and the context512 teacher gate:

| arm | prefill | mean | p95 | p99 | max | top-1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| two-quant MMQ | 127.2 | 0.0010796 | 0.0051188 | 0.0217009 | 0.0354152 | 162/162 |
| four-quant MMQ (production) | 171.9 | 0.0014707 | 0.0077991 | 0.0253607 | 0.0297407 | 161/162 |
| four-quant W4A16 | **150.9** | **0.0014384** | **0.0037423** | 0.0325024 | 0.0408521 | 161/162 |

W4A16's mean is 0.0014384 - level with the integer MMQ's 0.0014707, **not** the
two-quant 0.0010796 that the leaf result predicted.

## Why the leaf test was wrong

It compared each GPU path against an f64 host reference and reported GEMV
1.66e-03, W4A16 1.67e-03, integer MMQ 5.60e-03, which read as "W4A16 is at
GEMV parity".

Both kernels write **bf16**. Rounding a random f32 vector to bf16 and back
gives a relative norm error of **1.66e-03** - exactly the two numbers reported.
The leaf comparison was measuring the output format, and had **no resolving
power** below that floor. Only the integer MMQ was far enough above it
(5.60e-03) to be distinguished.

The model-level gate does not have this problem: errors accumulate across 64
layers, so differences well below one bf16 ULP per site still register.

**Lesson: a leaf comparison cannot validate a change whose expected effect is
smaller than the output dtype's rounding. Either compare in the accumulation
dtype, or go straight to the model-level gate.** This is the second time in
this campaign that a cheap proxy pointed the wrong way (the first was
bisecting IQ4_NL on divergence-from-default rather than teacher KL).

## What this says about the mean regression

Routing IQ3_S and IQ4_NL off the strict GEMV costs about +0.00036 mean
**regardless of the activation format**: an int8 path and a path with 25x more
accurate activations land within 0.00003 of each other. So the regression is
not an activation-precision effect, which is consistent with the earlier
finding that it is not attributable to particular tensors either.

The remaining explanation is accumulation order - the strict GEMV sums 128
strided f32 accumulators through a wave32 shuffle tree, while both GEMM routes
accumulate over K-tiles in the WMMA accumulator. That is untested.

W4A16 does have a different error *shape*: its p95 of 0.0037423 is the best of
all three arms, better even than the two-quant policy, while its p99 and max
are the worst. That is a real difference, just not the one that was claimed.

## Decision

Production is unchanged: four-quant integer MMQ at 171.9 tok/s. W4A16 stays
registered and unrouted, but its retention rationale is now weaker than
`docs/REFACTOR.md` records - it was kept as "the only route that reaches
exact-path accuracy at GEMM speed", and it does not. The ledger entry and the
artifact are updated to say so, and the removal decision is the human lead's.

## Validation

Policy restore verified byte-identical to `0bf5e5dc7`. The bf16 floor was
verified numerically (1.66e-03 for bf16 rounding alone). No suite was rerun
because no production code changed.

## Next

If W4A16 is kept, the open question is whether accumulation order explains the
residual, which would be tested by an f32-output variant compared against the
GEMV in f32 rather than bf16.
