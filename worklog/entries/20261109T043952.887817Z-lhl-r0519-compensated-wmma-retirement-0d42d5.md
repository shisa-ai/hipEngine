# Retire compensated Gemma WMMA candidate (R0519)

## Summary

Retire the compensated Gemma 4 WMMA MoE prefill candidate (former
`HIPENGINE_GEMMA4_MOE_PREFILL=wmma` / `wmma_plain`). The lead chose retirement
after a gfx1100 A/B confirmed the promotion premise is stale: the pin is a ~4x
prefill regression and the compensated owner never engages on the T16-converted
layout. Close R0519. The exact comparison modes, auto dispatch, and the plain
WMMA down fallback are preserved.

## Changes

- `gemma4_experts.py`: remove `wmma`/`wmma_plain` from `_PREFILL_MODES` and
  `_prefill_route_flags`; remove the gate_up dual-WMMA forward branch and the
  `compensated` threading; delete `gemma4_project_experts_wmma_dual` and the
  `_WMMA_PREFILL_COMP_VARIANT` / `_WMMA_DUAL_PREFILL_VARIANT` /
  `_WMMA_DUAL_PREFILL_COMP_VARIANT` constants. Keep the plain down owner
  `gemma4_project_experts_wmma` (the auto/mmq fallback behind the T16 down owner)
  and `_WMMA_PREFILL_VARIANT`.
- `gemma4_gguf_device.py`: drop the retired-pin split-layout exception; the fused
  Q4_K gate_up stack is tiles-only for every mode.
- Tests: rewrite `test_unit_gemma4_moe_prefill_routes.py` for the post-retirement
  selector (retired values resolve to auto; plain down variant stays registered);
  remove the three split-layout loader tests and rework
  `test_default_expert_residency_does_not_duplicate_raw_weights` to assert
  tiles-only residency across modes.
- Census harnesses: drop the removed `gemma4_project_experts_wmma_dual` wrap/leaf
  from `gemma4_prefill_census.py` and `gemma4_prefill_kernel_census.py`.
- `docs/ENVS.md`: mark `wmma`/`wmma_plain` retired (resolve to `auto`).

## Validation

- gfx1100 A/B (W7900, UD-Q4_K_XL, production, same-process best-of-3,
  `/tmp/refactor-gfx1100/r0519-gfx1100-ab.json`): auto 3227/4207/4060 tok/s at
  256/1024/2048 vs wmma 913/974/962 (0.28/0.23/0.24x). auto routes
  gate_up_fused_t16 + down_t16; wmma routes selected_gemv (compensated owner never
  engaged). Confirms the gfx1151 verdict on this host.
- Post-retirement production smoke: long-prompt generation still engages
  gate_up_fused_t16 + down_t16 (29/29, plus one gate_up_iu8), matching the
  pre-retirement route; short prompt correctly uses the selected_gemv fallback.
- `tests/test_unit_gemma4_moe_prefill_routes.py`, `test_unit_gemma4_gguf_device.py`,
  `test_unit_gemma4_expert_route.py`, `test_unit_gemma4_staged_prefill_integration.py`,
  `test_unit_gemma4_decode_attention_selection.py`: 275 passed.
- Docs gates pass; census scripts compile.
