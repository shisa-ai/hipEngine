# The exact-iu8 risk multiplier is a 2.11% prefill dial: 4.0 repairs 0.712% of rows, 1.0 repairs 0.209%

## Summary

Reattributing the prefill (`20261003T224929`) put the routed MoE block at 40.6% and,
inside it, a 1370 ms `_risk_` repair pass - 8.6% of the whole prefill. That pass
exists to serve a Kahan-bound criterion, and
`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q4_IU8_RISK_MULT` sets the bound. Its docstring says the
default is **4.0**, that the **screened floor on actual weights is between 0.5 and
1**, and that 4.0 is chosen for a ">=4x margin". So the pass is deliberately
over-inclusive, and how much it is over-inclusive had never been measured.

**Measured, `code-p4096`, production profile, 1504 `q4_iu8_exact:expert_gate_up`
calls per point, 9,856,614,400 outputs per point, three wall repetitions each:**

| `RISK_MULT` | risk rows | repair rate | max rate | over-capacity calls | wall (3 reps) | tok/s | vs 4.0 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| **4.0** (default) | 70,167,220 | **0.7119%** | 0.8057% | 0 | 17.0430 / 17.0034 / 16.9939 | 240.75 | - |
| 1.0 | 20,621,412 | **0.2092%** | 0.2389% | 0 | 16.6418 / 16.6523 / 16.6723 | 245.92 | **-2.11%** |
| 0.5 | 11,074,348 | **0.1124%** | 0.1281% | 0 | 16.5641 / 16.5645 / 16.5647 | 247.27 | **-2.64%** |

Within-point spread is 0.2-0.3% at 4.0 and 1.0 and 0.004% at 0.5, so the 2.11% and
2.64% deltas are roughly 7x and 100x their own noise. **`over_capacity_calls` is 0
at every point**, so the repair buffer is never the constraint and the incidence is
the whole story.

**The rate is linear in the multiplier and the wall is not.** 4.0 -> 1.0 cuts the
repair incidence 3.40x but the wall only 2.11%, and 4.0 -> 0.5 cuts the incidence
6.33x for 2.64%. On the recorded 1370 ms share, a proportional repair pass would
have saved 968 ms (5.7% of the prefill) at 1.0; the measured saving is 358 ms.
**So the repair kernel carries a large component that does not scale with the
number of flagged rows** - it is launched over `risk_capacity = compact * 2 * ffn`
regardless of `risk_count`, so its fixed scan is paid at any incidence. That is a
separate and still-open lever, and it is why the multiplier alone cannot recover
the whole 1370 ms.

## Why this is not promoted on the measurement alone

The wall win is real and cheap - one environment constant, no kernel change, no
owner change - but it is an **arithmetic** change: it decides which outputs the
exact repair serves, so it is not bit-exact and it needs the production gate rather
than the wall.

The prior evidence cuts both ways and is worth stating precisely, because it is
easy to read the wrong half of it:

* **Against:** `repair_free_gate_up` (`20260930T`,
  `results/2026-09-30-qwen4exp-repair-free-gate-up/`) removed the repair entirely
  and was **rejected**. Every aggregate gate passed (mean KL 5.278e-4, max 2.469e-2,
  top-1 0.99556) but it failed **per-scope**: `prefill_last` / `prefill_to_c1`, 18
  rows, mean KL **2.159e-3 against a 1e-3 limit**, p95 7.776e-3 against 5e-3, p99
  2.131e-2 against 2e-2. The conclusion recorded there is that the repair "is not
  decorative" - at a 0.71% incidence, per-element bounded drift accumulates across
  48 layers and lands on the prefill path.
* **For:** that experiment repaired **nothing**, which is below the multiplier's
  screened floor. This candidate repairs **29% of what production repairs** and
  keeps the criterion, which the docstring's own screening places *inside* the
  range where BF16 flips are still caught. The two are not the same change, and the
  `repair_free_gate_up` result does not by itself bound this one.

So the honest position is that this is genuinely undetermined and the gate is the
only thing that settles it - which is exactly the case `AGENTS.md` describes when
it says a candidate that is not bit-exact must be re-reviewed under the production
gates before any rejection. **The gate is running; nothing is promoted by this
entry.** If it passes, the 1.0 point is the cheapest one that keeps the criterion
and the 0.5 point becomes the next question; if it fails on `prefill_last` the way
`repair_free_gate_up` did, then the multiplier is a dial the envelope has already
pinned and the remaining lever is the repair kernel's fixed component, not its
incidence.

## Validation

- Three points x 1504 calls x 9,856,614,400 outputs, one case, one profile.
- `over_capacity_calls: 0` at every point, so incidence and not buffer size is
  being measured.
- Three wall repetitions per point; within-point spread 0.2-0.3%.
- No kernel, default, flag or owner changed by this unit. The candidate is
  reachable only by naming it in the gate.

## Limits

One host, one GPU architecture (gfx1151, not the gfx1100 default hardware), one
model file, one quant, one prompt case (`code-p4096`), production profile. The
`code-p4096` wall here is this harness's prefill wall (17.014 s at the default) and
is **not** comparable to the canonical-benchmark row in `20261003T224929` (16.0 s,
different harness, different warmup protocol); only the three points within this
table are mutually comparable. The 1370 ms repair share is from a 2026-09-16
capture and is used only to show that the wall saving is smaller than a
proportional model predicts.
