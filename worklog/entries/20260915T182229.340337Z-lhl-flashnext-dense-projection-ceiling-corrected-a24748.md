# Measured roofline overturns the dense-projection ceiling: 57.7% not 22.1%

## Summary

Supersedes the ceiling claim in
`worklog/entries/20260915T182013.566728Z-lhl-flashnext-dense-projection-tuning-rejected-5aa8e2.md`.
That entry concluded the dense GGUF projection is capped at **22.1% of FP32 peak**
by its instruction mix. That is wrong. A probe kernel holding the projection's
exact instruction mix (40 FMA + 105 integer per iteration) sustains **16467
GFLOP/s, 57.7% of the measured FP32 peak**. The projection runs at 2650 GFLOP/s,
**16.1% of its own mix ceiling**.

The error was a modelling assumption, not a measurement: counting issue slots one
per cycle assumes no co-issue, and RDNA3 co-issues integer and FP32 work. The
measured ceiling is more than 2x the inferred one.

What survives from the superseded entry: the tile-shape family is genuinely
exhausted, the kernel has no spills, and occupancy is at the register-only
maximum. What does not survive is "therefore tuning cannot help". It can — about
6x of headroom exists in memory latency and scheduling, without changing the
arithmetic.

## Measured roofline (gfx1151, Radeon 8060S, 40 CU at 2.9 GHz)

| Probe | GFLOP/s or GB/s | Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Datasheet FP32 peak | 29696 GFLOP/s | 100% |
| Register-resident FMA, measured | **28521 GFLOP/s** | 96.0% of datasheet |
| 40 FMA + 105 integer, measured | **16467 GFLOP/s** | 57.7% of measured FMA peak |
| Stream copy, measured | **211.1 GB/s** | 82.5% of 256 GB/s datasheet |
| Dense projections, measured in-model | 2650 GFLOP/s | 16.1% of mix ceiling |

Two consequences:

- The datasheet peak is real on this part, so percent-of-peak has an honest
  denominator and the 14.8 TFLOP/s figure corrected in the superseded entry stays
  corrected.
- The instruction mix is not what limits the projection. A kernel with that mix
  reaches 57.7% of peak, and the projection reaches 16.1% of that.

## Open and flagged, not asserted

**The byte accounting for `attn_qkv` does not close.** Per 1024-row launch it
reads 27.85 MB of weights, 10.49 MB of activations and writes 41.94 MB of F32
output — 80.3 MB in 17.32 ms, or 4.6 TB/s. That exceeds both the 211 GB/s
measured stream rate and the 32 MB MALL, so those bytes cannot all be crossing
DRAM inside the measured window. Either the working set is more cache-resident
across repetitions than the byte model captures, or the effective traffic is
smaller than the buffer sizes suggest. Until a `rocprofv3` memory-counter capture
runs, the 16.1% should be read as "16.1% of the mix ceiling on a kernel whose
memory behaviour is not yet characterized", not as a pure latency-hiding gap.

The probe kernel that reaches 57.7% has no memory traffic in its loop, so it is
an upper bound the projection cannot reach without solving its own memory access.
The size of the gap is established; its attribution is not.

## Validation

- `hipcc -O3 --offload-arch=gfx1151` build of the probe source succeeds.
- Register-resident FMA probe reaches 96.0% of the datasheet rate, which is the
  check that the probe itself is not the limiter.
- Stream copy lands at 82.5% of datasheet, inside the 75–85% band
  `docs/ROOFLINE.md` expects for a well-written streaming kernel.
- `scripts/sync_benchmark_readme.py --check` passes.

Diagnostic only. No performance claim retained and no hipEngine rate changed.
