# Account for lazy QSA WMMA scratch in context admission

## Summary

R0462's capacity planner omitted the lazy contiguous F16 K/V pair and QSA
selection mask. Context admission now reserves their upper bound alongside
activation staging. Execution arithmetic and allocation behavior are unchanged.

## Changes

- Add a CPU-safe sizing helper in `loading/qwen4_exp_scratch.py` and consume it
  in chunk-aware context admission. The shared prefill owner holds one pair,
  not one per QSA layer; resident capacity multiplies this reserve per runner.
- Preserve the base allocation census: these buffers are lazy and not part of
  the constructor/repair-queue allocation recipes.
- Reserve independently of profile selection, as for activation staging.
  The existing scratch floor and explicit reserve are unchanged.
- Keep R0462 open for its selector and gather conditions. No kernel changes.
