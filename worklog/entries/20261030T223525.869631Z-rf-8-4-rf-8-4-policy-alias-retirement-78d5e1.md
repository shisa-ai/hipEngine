# RF-8 retire gfx1151 policy aliases

## Summary

Deleted the 372 explicit policy re-export aliases and their 289 `__all__`
entries from `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1151/__init__.py`. Every remaining
policy read now resolves through the package's declared owner list
(`POLICY_MODULE_NAMES` / `_POLICY_MODULES`) or `backend_package_capability`.
`TARGET_ARCH` is retained as architecture identity; it is the only explicit
policy-owner assignment left in the initializer. No kernel wrapper, registry
mapping, ordinary import, function body, or `__all__` entry outside the
policy aliases was removed. No module `__getattr__` proxy,
wildcard re-export, or new registry was added.

## Exact deleted inventory

The pre-edit initializer had 373 explicit `NAME = _policy_x.NAME` assignments.
372 were deleted; 1 (`TARGET_ARCH`) was kept. The 289 deleted `__all__`
entries are exactly the deleted names that were listed there; `TARGET_ARCH`
and the two registrar names remain (`register_backend_kernels`,
`register_gfx1151_kernels`), for a final `__all__` of 3 entries.
