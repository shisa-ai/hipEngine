# Qualify frozen W8192 DMS on source-disjoint 128K

## Summary

The frozen epoch-20 exact-budget W8192 sidecar passes a fresh, source-disjoint
four-category 128K semantic and capacity gate. All policy bytes are identical to
the sealed 32K qualification. This closes the campaign's long-context semantic
gate while retaining 3.750 GiB of BF16 payload savings at c1 128K. DMS remains
default-off pending lifecycle/soak, sampled memory, and performance evidence.
