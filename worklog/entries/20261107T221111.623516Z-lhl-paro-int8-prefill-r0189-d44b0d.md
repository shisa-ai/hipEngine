# Keep PARO INT8 prefill capacity controls; re-home R0189

## Summary

- **R0281-PARO-INT8-PREFILL:** keep the four `HIPENGINE_QWEN35_INT8_PREFILL_*`
  operator capacity controls (attention mode, streaming minimum tokens,
  low-memory total GiB, oracle reserve MiB) as supported configuration. They are
  documented in `docs/ENVS.md` with explicit invalid-value errors, and they tune
  when `auto` picks streaming INT8 versus the transient BF16 oracle. Moving them
  into the resolved prefill plan has no product benefit. This applies the lead's
  2026-11-07 keep-supported-config pattern (R0067, R0211/R0212), and it is
  reversible.
- **R0189:** packed-PARO transaction slab design is feature/storage work and moves
  to the `docs/PLAN.md` backlog under the lead's re-home decision.
