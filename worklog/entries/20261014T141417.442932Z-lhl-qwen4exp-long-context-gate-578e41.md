# Qwen4Exp long-prompt production gate

## Summary

The owner asked for a long-prompt gate "that makes sense" in place of the
open calibration item in EXECUTION-PROFILES section 6.1. Fixed KL limits do
not work at 2K+ tokens: any non-bit-identical change gives about 2e-3 mean KL
against `strict`. The gate now binds on two axes that do work there: the
candidate against a round-off floor (`strict` reruns with one BF16 ulp
flipped in one prompt-embedding element, three seeds), and retrieval answers
at 8K and 16K tokens scored against stored answers. `production` passes:
mean/p95/max KL 2.13e-3 / 8.28e-3 / 3.46e-2 against a floor of 5.03e-3 /
2.24e-2 / 0.116, top-1 98 against 97, retrieval 16/16 for both arms.

Decisions taken while calibrating (mine; revisit if the owner disagrees):

- Control size. A probe with 16 flipped embedding elements gave mean KL
  3e-3 to 3e-2 and 21/25 top-1 on one prompt, wider than any route; four
  flips were still wide. One flip is the smallest change a single rounding
  of the BF16 residual stream can make, so the gate uses one.
- Median, not largest, control for mean and p95, so a candidate is held to a
  typical rounding flip rather than the worst of three. Max uses the largest
  control (the tail statistic of 100 rows is itself noisy), top-1 the lowest.
- Controls run only on the 2K prompts. `strict` prefills at about 63 tok/s
  (127 s at 8K, 261 s at 16K measured), so controls at 16K would triple a
  gate that already takes 25 minutes; the 16K prompts carry the retrieval
  axis with one `strict` pass.
- Retrieval is gold-forced (one prefill per arm) rather than free-running, so
  answer formatting cannot fail an arm whose numbers are right.
