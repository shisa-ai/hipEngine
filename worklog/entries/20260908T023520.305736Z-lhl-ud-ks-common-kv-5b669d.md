# K_S common-BF16-KV diagnostic and artifact prose correction

## Summary

Two corrections accepted on the K_S teacher-gate summary, then the suggested
common-KV-precision diagnostic computed from existing captures: comparing
both arms to the saved BF16-KV teacher (matching hipEngine's KV storage)
instead of the default F16-KV teacher substantially reclassifies the mean
excess as a KV-precision mismatch. Labeled diagnostic; the normative F16-KV
gate stands unchanged.

- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-08-zbook-ud-ks-context512-teacher-gate-corrected.json`:
  prose correction. The raw IQ2_XS arm's mean KL (0.0010448) is slightly
  worse than the expanded arm's (0.0010213); raw improves top-1 (161 vs 160),
  p95, and maximum (0.020106 vs 0.020704) only. The 0.020704 worst row
  belongs to the expanded arm; the raw worst is 0.020106
  (heldout_general_en_incident step 6). Numeric per-arm fields were correct;

  both arms vs the BF16-KV teacher on the 158 valid rows (prompt 7 steps 5-8

Interpretation (diagnostic, not a gate): the mean-gate excess against the
F16-KV teacher is substantially the hipEngine-BF16-KV vs teacher-F16-KV
precision mismatch - against the KV-matched teacher both arms drop under the
0.001 mean gate, and the expanded arm passes all five gates on the valid
rows. The raw IQ2_XS arm retains a real tail/top-1 deficit: worst row
heldout_code_rate_limiter step 1 KL 0.0307 (the same row already flagged as
the K_M raw Q5/Q6 arm's one above-threshold row in the 2026-09-07 common-KV
artifact), and top-1 misses at code_lru_cache step 8 and the known
general_ja_explain step 4.

The four stale BF16-KV teacher rows were not recaptured; none is a
worst-case candidate, and recapture is optional follow-up.

## Decisions

- The normative F16-KV gate and this diagnostic are both retained; teacher
  selection is not a gate criterion and nothing is promoted on this basis.
- The closeness of the mean to the threshold is not treated as noise and no
  gate is relaxed. The objective remains implementation fidelity.
