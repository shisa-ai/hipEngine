# Graph-route teacher divergence at the 1024 split boundary is a gate-session finding, not a serving one

## Summary

While qualifying the eager staged linear-attention route
(`20260918T235100.743824Z-lhl-staged-linear-rows-long-default-447f0a`) the
long-context gate's graph-eligible packet showed that the cached native target
graph, not the eager chain, is the route that fails teacher equality near the
split-K threshold: at cycle ends 1,016-1,022 it submits **zero split-K calls
against a required split count of 4** and diverges from the serial-exact teacher
by up to **9.27 absolute logit delta, KL 6.16 and top-1 agreement 0.667**, with
identical numbers for the staged and row-wise linear-rows settings, while the
eager route is byte-exact at the same ends. Because gfx1151 declares a
65,544-token graph context limit, the open question was whether serving reaches
those ends. It does not reproduce there: six synthetic shapes landing at
**993-1,030 prompt tokens**, with MTP engaged on 96.9-98.4% of output tokens,
are **exact on generated ids against their own true-AR arm** (1.34x-1.53x). The
graph route still needs a logits-level review at the ends whose buckets it does
serve, but nothing in the promoted route or in the default serving path depends
on that: the promotion is inert on a cycle the graph serves, and the two
packets that reach the bad ends are the gate's own.

## Changes

No source change. The packet artifact gains the `graph_band_serving_probe`
block and a corrected graph caveat.

## Validation

- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-19-gfx1151-qwen38-staged-linear-rows-long-verifier-route.json`,
  `graph_band_serving_probe` (six shapes with per-shape rate, coverage and
  identity) and the graph caveat in `caveats`.
- The gate's graph-submitted rows are in the two band artifacts behind the same
  packet (`band1024-staged`, `band1024-rowwise`): 22 of 31 and 12 of 17 direct
  cases submitted the graph, every one of them failed teacher equality, and
  `end1020-b2` is the worst case at max abs logit delta 9.272, KL 6.164, top-1
  0.667 with `split_k_calls` 0 and `required_split_count` 4. The same case's
  eager run is exact (0.0 delta, KL 0.0) on both linear-rows settings.
- Serving probe protocol: one server process, staged-linear admission on, BF16
  KV, candidate budget 3, `HIPENGINE_MTP2_MAX_CONTEXT_TOKENS=8192`, 64 decode
  tokens, two runs per shape, and a true-AR arm selected by the request's own
  `speculative_mtp` field against the same process
  (`/tmp/lc-numerics/phase6.sh`, logged at `/tmp/lc-numerics/graphband-*.json`).

## Limits of the serving result

The serving probe compares **generated ids**, which is a weaker instrument than
the gate's teacher comparison: the gate's divergence is in logits, and a logit
delta only becomes a token difference when it flips an argmax. A 32-token exact
match on six shapes with high MTP coverage is evidence that serving is not
silently wrong at these prompt sizes, not a proof that its graph route is
teacher-exact there. Closing it needs the same teacher comparison driven through
a serving-shaped session, or the graph's own bucket geometry tied to the ends it
serves.

## Next

- Give the cached native target graph a teacher-equality packet of its own at the
  cycle ends it serves, starting from `end1020-b2` and its neighbours, and decide
  whether the zero-split-K substitution is a bucket-geometry bug (the graph
  replaying a single-pass attention kernel over a span that needs split-K
  reduction) or a capture-time staleness.
- Until then, treat a graph-substituted cycle as unverified at the split
  boundary: the eager route is the only route with teacher equality there.
