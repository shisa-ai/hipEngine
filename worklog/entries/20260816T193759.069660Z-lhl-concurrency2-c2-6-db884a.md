- Moved native resident runner preparation/global session allocation to model-load time before `EngineService`, keeping submit commands O(1) and outside the 30-second control timeout.

- Added a compact blocked diagnostic artifact with full commands, model/hardware identity, correctness outcomes, and unsupported comparison scope.
