# Layer-outer cancellation-cleanup gate (CPU)

## Summary

One of the reviewer's outstanding P3 packet gates, closed at the CPU level:
cancellation cleanup. A mid-executor failure (simulated cancellation after
the per-layer reset, before the state sync) leaves every session with
cleared per-layer keying and empty oracle buffers; the public wrapper's
finally releases any acquired pair even when the executor raises, so a
later call cannot inherit per-layer pairs or stale owners.
