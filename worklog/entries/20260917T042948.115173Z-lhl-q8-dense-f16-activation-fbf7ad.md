# Hoisting the wide Q8_0 prefill activation conversion

## Summary

The promoted wide-row route converts its activation from f32 to f16 inside the K
loop, once per column block, and re-reads the f32 activation the same number of
times. This unit adds a real conversion kernel plus an f16-input tile variant,
and measures the composite — conversion pass, then matmul — against the promoted
route on the shapes the shipped default actually launches.

**Measured: 1384.7 ms → 794.1 ms over the route's 1056 launches in a 4096-token
prefill, a 590.6 ms saving (42.6%), with bit-identical output.** The packet's
per-shape times match the shipped default's own capture to 1.4%, so the saving
is engine-representative; it is 3.4% of the 17.2 s prefill wall.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q8_0_dense_wide.hip` — `x_t`
  templated activation ABI. The f32 load path is unchanged in instruction
  content: its inline conversion moved into a `__forceinline__`
  `wide_load_act8(const float*)` with the same two `float4` loads and eight
  `static_cast<half_t>` conversions. New `wide_load_act8(const half_t*)` and a
  `q8_0_f32_to_f16_kernel` with its `hipengine_gguf_q8_0_f32_to_f16` export. Two
  new exports: `..._dense_wide256_f16in_f32_f32_out` and
  `..._dense_wide128x128_f16in_f32_f32_out`.
- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q8_0_dense_wide.py` — `f32_to_f16`
  wrapper (range-checked before any library or runtime work) and the f16in
  variants in `_VARIANTS`.
- `scripts/qwen4exp_dense_wide_f16_activation.py` — the packet. Per admitted
  shape it times the f32-input tile, the conversion pass, the f16-input tile and
  the composite, checks bit-identity between the two ABIs, and aggregates over
  the census's launch counts.
- `tests/test_unit_qwen4exp_dense_wide_f16_activation.py` — export/wrapper
  symmetry, activation ABI against the variant name, registry presence, the
  converter's range check, and the admitted-shape table against the launch
  census.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-17-q8-dense-f16-activation/` — `artifact.json`
  (primary, 8 rotating weight buffers), `artifact-l2-resident.json` (1 buffer,
  diagnostic) and a README.

## Validation

- `uv run pytest tests/test_unit_qwen4exp_dense_wide_f16_activation.py` — 5
  passed. The census test caught a wrong per-role layer count in the first
  draft of the admitted-shape table (16 against the measured 24).
- Bit-identity: `max_abs_diff_f32in_vs_f16in == 0.0` for all 24
  shape/repetition-count configurations. Both ABIs put the same f16 bytes in
  LDS, so this is the same arithmetic, not a close approximation.
- Calibration against the engine (same commit, `code-p4096`, 1056 wide
  launches): engine 1404.2 ms, packet 1384.7 ms, 1.4% apart. Two roles differ by
  more than 10% — `attn_k`/`attn_v` (the engine runs the tail instantiation,
  512 of 2112 trace rows) and `shared_gate` — and both are small in absolute
  terms.
- Cache sensitivity: with one weight buffer the packet reports 1291.7 ms for the
  f32 arm, 7% optimistic. The 8-buffer run is the reported one.
- The conversion pass runs at memory speed (0.171 ms for 37.7 MB at K=6144,
  0.276 ms for 62.9 MB at K=10240) and is 2-4x smaller than the saving on the
  same shape.

## Next

- The route is **not wired into the runner**, so 590.6 ms is per-launch timing
  summed over census launch counts, not a prefill wall. Wiring needs a scratch
  buffer, one conversion per distinct live activation, invalidation on producer
  writes, and the strict fallback left registered; then an interleaved A/B on
  `code-p4096`.
- Not numerically gated beyond bit-identity between the two ABIs. The change is
  arithmetic-preserving, so the applicable gate is the profile's own, not a new
  calibration.
- This is smaller than the two opportunities the refreshed attribution ranks
  above it (dense scope below layer 16, ~2.5 s; MoE gate/up plus down, 4.61 s of
  gap). It stays cheap because it is exact.
