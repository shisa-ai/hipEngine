# the prefill/decode divergence is the MMQ gate-up route's prefill-only selection

## Summary
`test_incremental_decode_matches_a_dense_prefill` has been failing with an
identical `max abs diff 4.417` since 2026-09-28 00:36 and was recorded three
times as "pre-existing, worth its own investigation, not fixed here". It is now
bisected to a single commit and causally confirmed:

**`940255881 feat: the fused MMQ gate/up route ships on, and the flag rolls it
back`** flipped `gemma4_moe_gate_up_mmq_enabled()` on by default. That route is
selected through `gemma4_moe_prefill_route_enabled(lanes, num_experts)`, which
returns `lanes >= _PREFILL_MIN_LANES_PER_EXPERT * num_experts` -- so a 4-token
prefill takes the int8-dp4a MMQ gate/up projection and decoding those same four
tokens one at a time takes the fp32 grouped route. The two produce different
logits, and the model is not self-consistent between how many tokens are in the
batch.

**This is a batch-composition invariance violation**, which `AGENTS.md` names as
one of four distinct contracts alongside control/ownership, deterministic
repeatability, and arithmetic equality. It is not a numerical-accuracy
complaint about the MMQ route: the route passed its own teacher-forced gate at
worst-case KL 0.0013 against a 0.05 bar. The defect is that the *same* tokens
get *different arithmetic* depending on the batch they arrive in.

That matters beyond the test: speculative decoding drafts and verifies in
different batch shapes by construction, so an MTP loop on this branch would
draft with one arithmetic and verify with another.

## Changes
- **`hipengine/runtime/gemma4.py`**: the assistant head's read side of the
  backbone. `Gemma4SharedKV` (a read view: two buffer addresses, live position
  count, geometry, and no write offset), `Gemma4Runner.shared_kv(layer_index)`,
  `Gemma4Runner.layer_count`, and `Gemma4Runner.hidden_state(row)`.
- **`_last_rows`**: `hidden_state` indexes the most recent forward's rows, not
  the accumulated position count. A prefill of 512 followed by a decode of 1
  leaves one valid row; indexing 513 would hand back stale hidden state from
  before the decode, which for the head means a draft step conditioned on the
  wrong token's activations. `reset()` clears it.
- **`tests/test_unit_gemma4_runner.py`**: a GPU-guarded test that runs a real
  prefill then a real decode and checks the view tracks what the backbone wrote,
  including that the live count is the *new* position rather than the total.
- **`tests/test_unit_gemma4_runner_lifecycle.py`**: two CPU-only tests for the
  same accessors through the fake allocator, so a non-ROCm runner covers the
  index arithmetic and the out-of-range refusals.

- **Mechanism read from the source**: both the gate/up and the down projection
  take the MMQ branch only inside
  `gemma4_moe_gate_up_mmq_enabled() and gemma4_moe_prefill_route_enabled(lanes=lanes, num_experts=num_experts)`,
  and that predicate is `lanes >= _PREFILL_MIN_LANES_PER_EXPERT * num_experts`.
- The three earlier entries that recorded this failure cited the identical
  `4.417`, and `20260923T043353` had cited the same test as *passing*

  long-standing limitation.

  the exposure itself: no kernel changed and no performance claim is made.

## Next
- **The divergence is a decision, not a patch.** Restoring invariance means
  either the decode path also takes MMQ (needs the route to be sane at one lane
  per expert, and its own gate) or the prefill path stops taking it (loses the
  measured 1.27x on the 512/128 prefill). Both are arithmetic-promotion calls
  under `docs/OPTIMIZATION.md`, so this reports the defect and leaves the choice
  open rather than picking one.
- **The assistant head's forward** is the next unit and is fully specified. Its
  four blocks read backbone layers 28/29 through `shared_kv`, its input is the
  backbone's embedding, and its rope configs are the bound layers' own.
