# Prefill parity push: wide-N 64-column owners, IQ tails, dual vectorization, double buffering

## Summary

Follow-on run over the remaining prefill levers after the first
cooperative W4A16 owner (+20%). The decisive discovery: that owner's
decode-stubbed "46.6 TFLOPS ceiling" was the 32-column structure's
activation-traffic limit, not the hardware. Doubling the columns per
block (64) halves the redundant x re-reads and amortizes each
a-fragment load over twice the WMMA work; the cooperative IQ4_XS owner
then measures 63-72 TFLOPS. The same wide-N treatment was applied to
all three prefill single families.

## Changes

- Q5 8r2w64 (4-out-tile instantiation of the shared Q5 single), routed
  at rows >= 129: 1.0-1.6x across the real Q5 shapes, up to 58 TFLOPS.
- Q4 shared-B w64 (4 out-tiles, 8 waves, 2 row-tiles), routed at
  rows >= 129: 1.1-1.8x across the real Q4 shapes, up to 65 TFLOPS.
- IQ4_XS coop64 + IQ4_NL routed through the 64-column cooperative
  owner (IQ4_NL gained an explicit fast path: one 18-byte block per
  32-element sub-segment with d hoisted and a 16-entry LDS codebook,
  2.0-2.2x the one-wave owner).
- Q3_K and IQ3_S routed through the 32-column cooperative owner (the
  hi+lo split path needs two slabs and cannot widen): 1.4-1.5x.
- All single and dual T16 kernels: padded 272-half slab strides
  (32 B row alignment) and vectorized half16 b-fragment loads
  replacing sixteen 2 B scalars per fragment - removing the 16-way LDS
  bank conflict the 256-half stride maps every lane onto.
- The cooperative W4A16 hi-only path now double-buffers its decode:
  128-K units across two alternating LDS slabs, the decode of unit
  u+1 issued before the WMMA phase of unit u. Bit-exact; 62-72
  TFLOPS vs 61-69 single-buffered. (The block-local scale-group index
  is now explicitly masked - the unit-based k_base made the
  global/group distinction matter.)

## Measurements

Census (K_M, 512-row prefill wall): 870.7ms at run start ->
558.1ms final. Owner deltas: IQ4_XS singles 345.8 -> ~145, Q5
singles 162.0 -> 117.2, Q4 singles 137.2 -> 100.7, IQ4_NL 20.4 -> 9.9,
Q3_K 26.0 -> 18.0, IQ3_S 18.3 -> 13.8.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| UD-Q4_K_M | 720.4 | 930.8 (+29.2%) | 26.1 (unchanged) |

K_M prefill parity vs plain Q4_K_M (982.8 tok/s): 0.60x at the
campaign start -> 0.947x final.

## Evaluated and declined (with numbers)

- IQ4_XS gate/up pair fusion on the coop structure: a 64-column dual
  needs two 34.8 KB slabs (69.6 KB > the 64 KB LDS budget); a
  48-column dual forfeits much of the wide-N gain. Estimated 5-13ms
  (1-2% E2E) for the remaining shape - not taken this run. The Q5/Q4
  duals already exist and were vectorized instead.
- Further decode-phase work: decode-stubbed coop64 measures 74.3
  TFLOPS vs 63-72 real; the double buffer recovered part of that
  spread and the rest is ALU/issue-bound decode throughput that the
  LDS budget cannot hide further at 64 columns.
- Attention (the last objective item): the non-linear share is 66ms
  of the 558ms wall (~0.5ms/layer across the 96 linear-attention and
  32 full-attention layers). Full attention at 512 rows dispatches
  AOTriton (vendor-tuned); the remainder is per-layer norm/RoPE/
  residual chains - a kernel-fusion campaign, not a single lever, and
  out of this run's scope. The prefill flight recorder is host-side
  submission timestamps only, so GPU-side attribution would need a
  profiler; recorded as the open item.

## Validation

  real shapes; IQ4_NL, Q3_K, IQ3_S at 512 rows).
