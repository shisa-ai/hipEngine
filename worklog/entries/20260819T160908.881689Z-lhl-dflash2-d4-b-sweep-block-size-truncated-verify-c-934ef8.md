# DFlash2 D4 B-sweep: --block-size truncated verify chain; B3/B5/B7 vs AR and exact MTP B3

## Summary

Root-caused why the DFlash2 "B-sweep" never moved the needle: `main()` in
`scripts/dflash2_gguf_cycle.py` force-clamped `--block-size` back to the
drafter config block_size (8), so every claimed B run actually verified the
full 8-row chain. After separating the *forward* block size (fixed at config
8 — the native drafter kernels are sized to it) from the *verify chain*
length (CLI block_size), a true B ∈ {3,5,7} sweep is measured on the full
10-prompt mtpbench suite (gfx1151 resident Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_M, same-session
AR, exact MTP B3 23.85 tok/s = 1.78x AR from the 2026-08-17 closed
campaign).

**Decision: B3 is the DFlash2 optimum (7.70 tok/s avg = 0.575x AR, 100%
AR-exact on all 10 prompts), but it is still ~3.1x below exact MTP B3
(23.85 tok/s = 1.78x AR). DFlash2 stays a diagnostic; not promoted.**
## Changes

- `scripts/dflash2_gguf_cycle.py`: fixed `--block-size` handling. The
  override that reset CLI block_size to `drafter.config.block_size` was the
  bug that made the "B-sweep" verify 8 rows every time. The batch cycle now
  runs the drafter forward at the config block size (`fwd_bs`, required by
  the fixed-size native scratch buffers) and truncates the draft chain to
  `block_size - 1` before the batched chain verify.
- `scripts/dflash2_gguf_suite_smoke.py`: added `--block-size` passthrough
  (clamped to [2, config block_size]) so the full-suite sweep can vary B.

## Validation

Full 10-prompt mtpbench suite, B = 3/5/7, max_new_tokens 40, native batched
chain verify, `ar_agree = 1.00` on every row (all outputs are exact greedy
AR predictions — no arithmetic drift).

| B (block) | verify rows | mean acceptance | mean tok/s | ratio AR (AR=13.4) | vs MTP B3 (1.78x AR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 (4) | 4 | 2.80 | 7.70 | 0.575x | ~3.1x slower |
| 5 (6) | 6 | 3.30 | 4.26 | 0.32x | ~5.6x slower |
| 7 (8) | 8 | 3.49 | 3.58 | 0.27x | ~6.7x slower |

code_markdown_table 3.08 / 4.00, general_en_plan 3.64 / 4.74,

Why B3 beats B5/B7 despite lower acceptance: the verify cost scales with rows
(~170 ms at 4 rows vs ~620 ms at 8 rows, measured in this tree); the
acceptance cap at B3 (root+3 drafts) only binds on the few 4.0-4.4-acceptance
prompts, so the 2.6x verify saving dominates. Even at the optimum B3 the
drafter forward + selector (~130 ms/cycle) plus per-draft acceptance ~0.38
(versus MTP ~0.95) keep DFlash2 far behind MTP B3. The remaining theoretical
ceiling (8-row verify at ~310 ms via rowtile-8) was already reverted as
AR-divergent in the D4 measurement entry.
