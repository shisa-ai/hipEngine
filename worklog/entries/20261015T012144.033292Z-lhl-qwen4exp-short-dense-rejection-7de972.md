# Qwen4Exp short-prompt dense route gate rejection

## Summary

The profile-owned short-row dense route was fully exercised by the 18-prompt Qwen4Exp layer2 gate. It is **not admitted to production**: the route's arithmetic exceeds the binding `prefill_last` mean-KL envelope even though top-1 remains 18/18 on that scope.

## Changes

- The route remains implemented and explicitly selectable with `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_DENSE_WIDE_SHORT_ROWS=1` for future research.
- Removed that selector from the production/fast profile binder so the failed arithmetic-changing route cannot become a default.
- Preserved exact fallback behavior for rows 1-256 in the shipped profile.
- Gate artifact: `benchmarks/results/2026-10-15-qwen4exp-short-dense-gate/gate.json`.

## Validation

- Full layer2 gate: `measurement_valid: true`, 18 prompts, 450 teacher-forced rows, 3 repeats.
- Overall: mean KL `6.6579e-4`, p95 `3.3262e-3`, p99 `8.4265e-3`, max `2.2908e-2`, top-1 `447/450`.
- `prefill_last`: mean KL `2.2846e-3`, top-1 `18/18`; this exceeds the `1e-3` binding mean-KL limit.
- Focused route/profile/allocation tests: all passed.

## Next

Do not promote unchanged. Reopen only with a numerically different short-row implementation or a separately approved envelope decision; the current candidate is rejected for production admission.
