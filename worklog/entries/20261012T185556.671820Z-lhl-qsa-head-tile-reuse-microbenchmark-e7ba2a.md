# QSA head-tile reuse microbenchmark

## Summary

The existing QSA head-tile microbenchmark ran successfully under TheRock ROCm
10 on gfx1151 after exporting the compiler path. On a representative synthetic
geometry (`rows=32`, selected stride 257, 256 selected positions, 24 query
heads, 2 KV heads), all three variants were bit-identical. The head-pair
variant was fastest at 0.1903 ms, 1.389x the shipped head-quad variant at
0.2643 ms; head-1 was 0.3705 ms. This rejects the simple assumption that more
K/V reuse is always faster for this geometry. It is a hipEngine kernel-shape
finding, not yet an external-engine explanation or a production promotion.
