# gfx1100 IU4 instruction screen (W7900 + 7900 XTX)

## Summary

Ran the in-tree `mtp_iu4_roofline` instruction screen on both gfx1100 devices to
test whether the gfx1151 IU4 capability transfers to RDNA3. **The 2× IU4 WMMA
lane transfers: `v_wmma_i32_16x16x16_iu4` compiles and executes on gfx1100 and
sustains 1.981× (W7900) / 1.989× (7900 XTX) IU4-vs-IU8 WMMA**, matching the
gfx1151 1.98–2.00× finding. **The tiny-M DOT8 advantage does NOT transfer:**
DOT8/DOT4 is only 1.188× (W7900) / 1.169× (XTX) on gfx1100, versus the 2.01×
measured on gfx1151 (56.83 vs 28.25 TOP/s). On gfx1151 the DOT4 lane measured
anomalously low (28 TOP/s, ~half its WMMA roof), which is what produced the 2×
DOT8 headline; on gfx1100 DOT4 is already efficient, so DOT8 has little
arithmetic headroom to add.

Implication: dense prefill (WMMA, wide M) is the surface worth profiling on
gfx1100; tiny-M B1–B3 verifier decode (DOT lane, memory-bound) is *weaker* on
gfx1100 than on gfx1151 and not worth an IU4 sidecar.

## Validation

- ISA verification: `isa_all_expected_seen: true` on both gfx1100 builds
  (all six expected mnemonics incl. `v_wmma_i32_16x16x16_iu4` and
  `v_dot8_i32_iu4`).
- Best-median TOPS (same harness/params as the gfx1151 doc, 65536 iters,
  9 samples, 3 warmups):
  - W7900: IU8 WMMA 122.1 → IU4 WMMA 242.0 (1.981×); DOT4 52.3 → DOT8 61.2
    (1.188×).
  - 7900 XTX: IU8 WMMA 134.3 → IU4 WMMA 267.0 (1.989×); DOT4 56.3 → DOT8 66.2
    (1.169×).
- Diagnostic-only (`performance_claim: false`); clocks/power not pinned,
  shared worktree disclosed by the driver.
