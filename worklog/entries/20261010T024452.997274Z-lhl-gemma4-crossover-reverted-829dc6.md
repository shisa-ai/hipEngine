# Mid-depth graph prize refuted on natural tokens; crossover attempt reverted

## Summary

Iteration 4 attempted to extend the whole-step decode graph to 1024-2399 keys
by re-routing the launched path to the two-phase split below a measured
flash/split crossover (2400 keys), making both arms route identically so
buckets there could capture bit-exactly. The full section 2.11 packet passed
(1152 teacher-forced rows, median KL 2.9e-6, p90 5.2e-3, flip-damage 1.2
percent, top-1 98.7 percent) and graph-vs-launched greedy IDs were identical
(128/128 at 1024 and 2048 depths), but the campaign-bench measurements on the
natural-token corpus refuted the premise and the change was reverted the same
day: the crossover and the graph prize measured in iteration 3's random-token
probe do not transfer to natural text.

## Changes

- Implemented, then reverted: `flash_admits(keys=...)` crossover
  (`_FLASH_WINS_FROM_KEYS = 2400`) in `gemma4_attention.py`, the graph guard
  rename/extension to `_FLASH_ROUTE_MIN_KEYS` in `gemma4_decode_graph.py`,
  the ENVS wording, and the associated test updates. The revert restored the
  committed production state byte-for-byte (verified: post-revert GPU tests
  pass, primary 29.881 in band).
- Kept (truth corrections, no behavior change): the graph-guard comments in
  `tests/test_gpu_gemma4_decode_graph_capture.py` and `docs/ENVS.md` now name
  flash-decoding's frozen-extent key partitioning as the reason buckets from
  1024 keys fall back (the earlier split-geometry attribution was wrong --
  worklog 20261010T010705 proved the split and class routes replay
  bit-exact). `docs/REFACTOR.md` records the attempt, its natural-token
  refutation, and the standing removal condition (device-resolved flash
  geometry), plus the methodology rule: route and prize numbers must come
  from the campaign corpus, not random-token prompts.

## Validation

- Route/prize arms on the campaign bench (3 samples, natural tokens):
  1024 depth: graph+split 33.97, graph+flash 34.18, launched+split 34.21,
  launched+flash 33.94 — route and graph effects inside noise.
  2048 depth: graph+split 32.71, graph+flash 34.95, launched+split 33.95,
  launched+flash 35.10 — split costs -3.3 percent, the graph adds nothing.
  32768 depth: with the crossover, decode regressed 30.00 -> 27.81 tok/s
  (-7 percent; the 53 sliding layers at 1024 keys switched to split in the
  device-bound regime); old routing forced on the same build measured 29.92.
  Post-revert primary: 29.881 (baseline band ~30.0), prefill 987.3 in the
  949-995 band.
- Section 2.11 packet (18 cases, 1152 rows, teacher-forced strict chain):
  median KL 2.9e-6 (<= 1e-3), p90 5.2e-3 (<= 2e-2), flip-damage 1.2 percent
  (<= 3), top-1 98.7 percent (>= 96), all categories within per-category
  bars, task checks and repeat/isolation controls all pass. The script's
  `packet_passed=False` is against the superseded legacy mean/tail thresholds;
  the binding section 2.11 envelope passes at large margin. Arithmetic
  quality was never the blocker; speed was.
- Graph-vs-launched parity before the revert: 128/128 greedy IDs identical at
  1024 and 2048 depths through `LLM.generate`; the deep-position GPU test
  (1024 captures, 4096 falls back, both bit-exact) passed on the crossover
  build and the restored build (2 passed).
- Unit bundle post-revert: 130 passed
  (staged/prefill selection, decode-graph workspace/gate/bucket, flash
  decode).

## Next

The decode-graph extension is closed for route-selection approaches: the
launched route stays flash from 1024 keys (the measured winner at every depth
on natural tokens), so buckets at 1024+ keep the launched fallback. The only
remaining clearing route for a mid-depth or deep graph prize is
device-resolved flash geometry (the phase kernel derives `flash_slices(live)`
per block from a staged device slot), recorded in `docs/REFACTOR.md`; that
is a kernel unit with no route change on the launched path. The 2048-row
decode lead over llama.cpp is unaffected (launched flash keeps the paired
ladder's 36.36 tok/s row; today's launched+flash arm measured 35.10).
