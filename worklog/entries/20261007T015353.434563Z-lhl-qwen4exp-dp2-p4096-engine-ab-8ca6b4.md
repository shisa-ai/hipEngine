# Qualify DP-2 rows-4096-only engine performance

## Summary

The corrected narrowed-policy complete-engine A/B is complete. It uses a tracked-clean one-process/one-residency run on Radeon 8060S/gfx1151, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next UD-Q4_K_XL, production/BF16, 12 canonical plus 16 heldout cases, one warmup and three counterbalanced measured repetitions per arm, and 128 decode transitions.

The candidate engages exactly 61 ordered pairs at p4096 and zero pairs at p512/p1024. All 28 cases are cross-arm output-exact and deterministic. P4096 prefill is 324.521→325.134 tok/s (candidate/parent 1.0018895x, +0.189%); decode is 10.555→10.543 tok/s; p4096 client wall is 890.940731→890.593658 s (0.999610x). The native operation saving does not become a promotion-quality complete-engine improvement. The candidate remains opt-in/default-off and no rows outside 4096 are inferred.
