# Make the thinking policy part of batching and streaming

## Summary

The policy-aware dispatch in a9ed26755 fixed the isolated blocking case and left
two ways for a request to be served under the wrong policy. Both were reported
against that commit and both reproduced before the fix.

- **Coalescing dropped a hard request's budget.** `_group_key` keyed on the route,
  the plan fingerprint, and the sampling params, but not on the thinking policy,
  while `_run_group` dispatches the whole group with `group[0].thinking_policy`.
  The policy is not visible in the sampling params -- the request path leaves them
  untouched and the dispatch applies the policy -- so a hint request and a hard
  request with identical sampling and plan fingerprint produced equal group keys
  and merged. Hint-first cleared both budgets; hard-first enforced both.
- **Streaming ignored the policy.** `_stream_single` passed its sampling straight
  to the engine and never called `_mtp_dispatch_sampling`, so an MTP hint stream
  kept the budget the blocking path clears. Hard streaming already preserved
  enforcement, which is why the two transports disagreed only under `hint`.

## Changes

- `hipengine/server/api.py`: `_group_key` includes `item.thinking_policy`, with a
  comment recording why the sampling key cannot stand in for it.
- `hipengine/server/api.py`: `_stream_single` resolves its route first and applies
  `_mtp_dispatch_sampling` at the same dispatch boundary the blocking path uses
  when that route is the MTP batch route.
- `tests/test_unit_server_mtp_policy_batching.py`: drives the real batcher with a
  recording fake. Mixed-policy requests must not coalesce, parametrized over both
  arrival orders, with the per-request budget checked on both sides of the group;
  same-policy requests must still coalesce into one dispatch; streaming must apply
  the policy for both `hint` and `hard`; and blocking and streaming must agree on
  the cap for each policy.

## Validation

- RED before the fix: both arrival orders of the coalescing test failed, and both
  `hint` streaming cases failed (the `hard` cases already passed, matching the
  report). GREEN after: 16 passed in the two dispatch/policy files.
- `.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_integration_server_api.py
  tests/test_unit_server_mtp_policy_batching.py
  tests/test_unit_server_mtp_dispatch_sampling.py
  tests/test_unit_server_mtp_refusal.py`: 695 passed.
- `.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_integration_generation_engine_service.py`:
  33 passed.
- The reproduction in the report was a CPU-only caller-level probe; these tests
  drive the same batcher and streaming entry points the server uses, and the
  recorded sampling is what the engine callable received.
