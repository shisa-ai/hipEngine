# Measure matched near-256Ki Gemma 4 prefill and decode

## Summary

The valid near-256Ki endpoint is measured and paired at 262015 prompt tokens plus 128 greedy outputs in a 262144-token context. On zbook/Radeon 8060S, llama.cpp is 3.99x faster in prefill (227.86 vs 57.15 tok/s) and 1.31x faster in decode (7.21 vs 5.51 tok/s).
