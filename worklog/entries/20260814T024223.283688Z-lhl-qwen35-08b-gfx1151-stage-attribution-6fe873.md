# Qwen3.5 0.8B gfx1151 stage attribution

## Summary

Completed the HIP half of the bounded Qwen3.5-0.8B module-attribution lane on
Radeon 8060S / gfx1151. rocprofv3 kernel timestamps were all zero and
`hipEventElapsedTime` produced near-zero or large negative intervals, so the
single allowed mechanical repair uses profiling-only same-stream
`wall_clock64()` markers. Q4/Q8 prefill ledgers reconcile 99.51%/98.81% of
instrumented wall and eager decode reconciles 96.59%/97.58%.

Q4 prefill is dominated by linear-attention projections (43.79%); its QKV/gate
and alpha/beta paths explicitly report `fallback`. Q8 prefill is dominated by
GDN (38.70%). Projection GEMVs own 60.1% of Q4 decode and 53.2% of Q8 decode.
This admits one bounded D08-P1 Q4 projection-route repair after the Vulkan
M3/M4 ledger closes; no arithmetic tuning is admitted yet.
