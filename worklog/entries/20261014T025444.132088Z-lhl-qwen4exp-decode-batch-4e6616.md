## Decisions

- **Three reduction-order decode routes are in the UD production plan**:
  the row-split GDN decode step and the two warp-per-expert routed-expert
  kernels. The expert kernels were rejected on 2026-09-30 on an 18-case
  stress suite whose 0.05 max-KL bar the then-default plan also failed; the
  binding layer2 gate passes with them, so they are selected now and the old
  record has a pointer to this decision.
- **The DP4A gate/up owner stays on 43 layers.** Widening it to 48 gave +2.2%
  decode but top-1 445/450 against the 0.99 floor; the commit was reverted
  (`d76d3fe3e`, revert `5f25a10f2`). Top-1 is now the tightest bar for
  decode work: 446/450 leaves two rows.
- **The fused one-row read keeps the separate norm launch.** Rebuilding the
  norm in every block of the down launch was bit-identical but 1.4% slower;
  three launches instead of six is the kept form.
- **Batched MTP verification is not wired.** At 1.97 steps for four rows,
  plus rollback and replay of the accepted prefix on a partial accept, it
  only beats autoregressive decode at high draft acceptance. The prototype
  provider mode stays out of the tree until two prerequisites are measured:
  a small-row verify pass well under two steps (the two-row pass at 1.77
  steps points at small-row kernel selection) and a replay that re-runs only
  the GDN recurrence from saved per-layer conv rows instead of the whole
  body.
