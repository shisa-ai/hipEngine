# The semantic GEMV rejection was right: correcting a false negative

## Summary

The entry `20260915T162000.000000Z-lhl-vibevoice-tts-synthesize-surface-and-quality-correction-7b31c4`
claimed the semantic encoder's `rows=1` GEMV dispatch is quality-neutral, on the
grounds that a ten-seed A/B produced bit-identical outcomes with the dispatch on
and off. **That claim is false and this entry supersedes it.** The A/B harness
labels its arms `wmma` for threshold 0 and `gemv` for threshold 2 — so `wmma` means
the dispatch is *off* — and the artifact it was compared against had also been
produced with the dispatch off. Both arms were off, so they agreed trivially.

The dispatch was re-applied, measured, and re-rejected on the quality gate. It is
reverted again.

## The measurement that settles it

Ten seeds, fresh synthesis plus scoring, one arm per dispatch state:

| Arm | Request-runs passed | Failures |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| GEMV **off** | **58/60** | `two-2turn` seed 20260919 (WER 0.1176); `two-long` seed 20260918 (attribution) |
| GEMV **on** | **54/60** | `two-2turn` seeds 20260916 (0.1176), **20260917 (0.6471)**, 20260919 (0.1176); `two-long` seeds 20260918, 20260920 (attribution), 20260922 (no transcript) |

The runaway reproduces the original three-seed rejection exactly: `two-2turn` seed
20260917, WER **0.6471**, **5 repeated 4-grams**, **11.73 s** of output at duration
ratio 1.725, and a broken speaker attribution. `two-long` seed 20260922 is a hard
failure — the transcript does not parse and the request produces no usable turns.

So the rejection recorded in `bb727729f`/`c08b828da` was correct, and the reason it
gave was correct. The stale-audio bug it also identified was real, but fixing that
bug does not rehabilitate the dispatch.

## Why the error was possible

The isolated stage win is large and real, which made the neutral result plausible:

- `encode_chunk_streaming("semantic", ...)` per chunk: **55.22 ms tiled -> 18.42 ms
  GEMV, 3.00x** (isolated A/B in one process).
- Pooled RTF over five runs with the dispatch on: **1.127 / 1.136 / 1.163 / 1.151 /
  1.146, median 1.146**, against 1.337 at the kept revision and 1.336 for the torch
  lane. `chain_exact` and `negative_conditions_match` are true on all five.
- The numerical perturbation at the boundary is small: max 0.0625 absolute,
  3.7e-3 relative, about one bf16 ulp at the signal's 16.875 scale. The full
  vibevoice suite is green with the dispatch on (**126 passed, 11 skipped**) and
  the exact generated-token chain gate passes.

Everything except the generated-audio quality suite said this was a win. The
lesson is that the two-speaker trajectory is chaotically sensitive to a one-ulp
perturbation, so "small perturbation" is not "safe perturbation" on this path, and
the quality suite is the only instrument that sees it.

## Guard against recurrence

- The rejected arm's artifact is retained at
  `benchmarks/results/2026-09-15-gfx1151-vibevoice-tts-quality-gemv-on.json`, with
  its variant recorded in the artifact rather than only in a harness label.
- `docs/REFACTOR.md` now records the measured 3.00x stage win alongside the
  rejection, and states that any future A/B on this path must assert the arm's
  threshold in the artifact rather than trusting a `wmma`/`gemv` label.
- `benchmarks/README.md` names the dispatch in the quality section so the gate that
  rejects it is visible from the scoreboard.

## Next

The dispatch is closed unless a mechanism removes the trajectory sensitivity. The
semantic stage remains the second-largest at ~1.0 s of a ~4.5 s warm request, so
any further work there must be validated by the quality suite before it is kept —
the correctness suite is not sufficient evidence on this path.
