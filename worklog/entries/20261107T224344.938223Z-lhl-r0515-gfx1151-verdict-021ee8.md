## Validation

Radeon 8060S, gemma-4-26B-A4B-it UD-Q4_K_XL. Each gate uses
`scripts/gemma4_teacher_forced_gate.py --slices 1 --wmma-full-slices 1`, with the
reference captured under `HIPENGINE_GEMMA4_MOE_GATE_UP_MMQ=0` and the default as
candidate.

- Frozen corpus at 512 and 2048 ids, probe corpus at 16, 24, 64, 128 and 1024 ids:
  every gate passed with kl_mean = kl_max = 0.0 and zero top-1 flips. The probe-1024
  chain includes 24 rows with margin < 0.01 and 53 with margin < 0.05.
- Routes were distinct per arm: the reference recorded `grouped_row4`, the candidate
  `gate_up_iu8`, one per scored row.
- Before the `--wmma-full-slices 1` pin, the gate failed only on
  `no_wmma_full_launches_observed`, an attention-split requirement unrelated to this
  MoE lever. The metrics were already KL 0.
- Single prefill forward via `runner.forward(ids[:L])` on the probe corpus, L = 16,
  24, 64, 128, 512: final-row logits bit-identical, same argmax.
- Leaf counts at L <= 128: with the lever on, `gemma4_project_experts_gate_up_mmq` is
  entered in all 30 layers but serves only one, the Q5_K layer through the exact
  `gate_up_iu8`. The 29 Q4_K layers decline MMQ32 and take the exact ladder. The
  recorded 2026-09-27 short-prefill divergence (benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md) belongs to
  the Q4_K MMQ32 leaf, which was not exercised.
- `scripts/gemma4_mmq_prefill_length_probe.py` also reports KL 0 at every length, but
  its route column is empty and its "route used" check does not recognise
  `gate_up_iu8`. Its route attribution is incomplete; it is not evidence that MMQ32 ran.
