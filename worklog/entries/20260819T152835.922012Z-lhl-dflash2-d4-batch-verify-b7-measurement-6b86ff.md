# D4: B7 batch verify measured vs exact MTP B3 — not promoted, blocker recorded

## Summary

D3.1 (chain-batched verifier at B=7) is implemented and AR-exact, and the D4
measurement is complete on the full 10-prompt mtpbench category suite. **The
promotion rule is NOT met: DFlash2 B7 is decisively below exact MTP B3.** Mean
DFlash2 acceptance is 3.49 tokens/cycle on the full suite, but the 8-row
verify costs ~620 ms/cycle (Q4_K_M, gfx1151), giving ~3.6 tok/s ≈ **0.27x AR**,
vs exact MTP B3 at **23.85 tok/s (1.78x AR)**. The blocker is fundamental and
recorded below: DFlash2's per-draft acceptance (~0.38) is far below MTP's
(~0.95), so the B7 chain mostly computes rejected rows, and the 8-row verify
cost (2.2-4x MTP B3's 4-row) cannot be amortized. DFlash2 B7 stays a
diagnostic; the rowtile-8 optimization that cut the verify to ~310 ms was
found to diverge from AR on `code_lru_cache` and was reverted.

## Decision / blocker

**Not promoted.** DFlash2 B7 stays a diagnostic with a recorded blocker.

Blocker (concrete, fundamental): DFlash2's per-draft acceptance on this target
is ~0.38 (r@1 0.72-0.82), far below MTP's ~0.95. With block size 8, a cycle
accepts only ~2.6 drafts on average, so the 8-row verify mostly computes
rejected rows. The 8-row verify (Q4_K_M) costs ~620 ms (native, correct path)
vs MTP B3's 4-row ~160 ms — 4x more for LOWER acceptance (3.49 vs 3.85). The
acceptance/cost ratio is worse than MTP at every measured block size; the
reference blog's 4.8 DFlash2 acceptance is not reached on this Q4_K_M target
(selector r@16 0.87-0.97, but the greedy chain still falls to MTP-level
acceptance at 2.6-3.6 tokens/cycle).

## Rowtile-8 finding (invalidated path)

The Q4T16 dense rowtile 8-row extension (switch cases 5-8, rows<=8 gates) cut
the 8-row native verify from ~620 ms to ~310 ms (N2 graph) and DFlash2 B7
throughput from ~3.6 to ~6.9 tok/s on good prompts, but **diverged from AR on
`code_lru_cache`** (native verify row 7 wrong: native=436 vs AR=43072; 9/10
prompts exact, lru_cache 31/40). All rowtile kernels are numerically exact vs
per-row decode in isolation (K/Q/V/attn_output/ffn_gate/up/down across all
full-attn layers, rows 2-8, maxdiff 0). The divergence is a verify-path
integration issue (native full-attn staged chain uses the K sidecar rowtile at
rows 5-8) not reproduced by isolated kernel tests; capping `_q4_t16_sidecar_
decode_variants` at rows<=4 makes native exact again. **Reverted** to restore
the strict AR-equality gate; the rowtile-8 path is recorded as invalid until
the verify-path interaction is root-caused.
