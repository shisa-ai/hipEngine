# PMC classification: AR decode attention at DRAM roof; verify row-sharing is the only attention headroom

## Summary

Campaign §11.1 open question answered: the shared-row Muse AR decode
attention is **DRAM-bandwidth-bound at long context**, so further AR
decode-attention kernel work cannot pay there. The one attention-side
candidate with real headroom is **multi-row verify sharing** (tok>1), which
benefits DFlash verify.

Method: isolated shared-row WMMA kernel probe
(`scripts/muse_glimmer_decode_attn_scale.py`, 32Q/2KV/head-128 bf16,
split_keys=256, n_blocks=128) across contexts 4K/32K/131K/260K and verify
rows 1/5. rocprofv3 three-pass counters (FETCH_SIZE | SQ_INSTS_VALU
SQ_WAVES | GRBM_GUI_ACTIVE SQ_BUSY_CYCLES); rates computed from calibrated
FETCH_SIZE + unprofiled 50-iter device-synchronized walls against a 220 GB/s
practical sustained-read roofline.

| ctx | rows | ms | K/V MB | eff GB/s | x floor |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 4096 | 1 | 0.084 | 4.2 | 50 | 4.38 |
| 32768 | 1 | 0.152 | 33.6 | 221 | 1.00 |
| 131072 | 1 | 0.682 | 134.2 | 197 | 1.12 |
| 262144 | 1 | 1.299 | 268.4 | 207 | 1.06 |
| 32768 | 5 | 0.435 | 33.6 | 77 | 2.85 |
| 131072 | 5 | 1.496 | 134.2 | 90 | 2.45 |
| 262144 | 5 | 2.856 | 268.4 | 94 | 2.34 |

- **tok=1, >=32K**: 197-221 GB/s = 90-100% of the practical roofline;
  FETCH_SIZE ~= one K/V read (2-6% over the byte model, no re-fetch).
  Bandwidth-bound; no kernel-level headroom.
- **tok=1, 4K**: latency/launch-bound (4.4x floor) but absolute cost tiny
  (~1.1 ms/step over 13 NoPE layers). Not worth chasing.
- **tok=5**: K/V fetch amortized to ~1x model at every context, yet
  2.3-2.9x the single-row floor (77-94 GB/s effective) — NOT
  bandwidth-bound. Residual is per-row compute/occupancy in the multi-row
  path. Upper-bound saving if row sharing reached the 1-row floor:
  13 NoPE layers x (ms5-ms1) = 3.7 ms/verify at 32K, 10.6 at 131K, 20.2 at
  260K. Queued behind the target-prefill TTFT lever.

## Validation

- FETCH_SIZE within 2-6% of the K/V byte model at all eight points
  (calibrated counter, KB units).
- Unprofiled walls authoritative for rates; PMC-mode dispatch timestamps
  underestimated the 32K tok=1 kernel (0.115 vs 0.152 ms, implying an
  impossible 312 GB/s) — recorded as a caveat, not used.

## Caveats

- SQ_INSTS_VALU / SQ_WAVES / GRBM_GUI_ACTIVE / SQ_BUSY_CYCLES absolute values
  are unreliable on this rocprofv3/gfx1151 pairing (SQ_WAVES=19 for a
  256-workgroup grid; GRBM_GUI_ACTIVE non-monotonic in duration; 40 instance
  rows per dispatch). Relative VALU scaling (~row count) is consistent.
  Classification rests on FETCH_SIZE + unprofiled durations + roofline
  arithmetic, not activity-counter utilization.
- Isolated synthetic one-NoPE-layer geometry; per-model x13 NoPE layers.
