# GR staged-f32: the traffic win is separable from f16 operand rounding

## Summary

Two Qwen4 ledger items (HC-1 hyper_connection, DP-1 dense_projection scope) were
gated on the same recorded conclusion: the promoted `dense_wide256` tile is
4.59x faster than the production fused GR-up kernel **and** fails the numerical
gate, and the two facts were recorded as one, so a route wanting the traffic win
was said to need "a changed arithmetic". Measured separation: the traffic
mechanism is free and the precision cost is a separate, optional 1.9x.

A prototype that keeps the wide tile's LDS staging and tiling and replaces the
WMMA multiply with f32 FMA on f32 operands takes the GR up projection from
6.5387 ms to 2.7380 ms (**2.388x**) with a relative-L2 deviation of **7.442e-08**
from the exact production kernel - about 1.25 f32 ulp, the summation-order floor
for K=320 - while the wide arms sit at 4.709e-05, 633x larger.

## Next

Three findings worth carrying forward, in the order they matter:

1. **The wide arms' error is entirely the weight operand.** `wide_f32in` and
   `wide_f16in` report identical error to every printed digit (relative L2
   4.709e-05, max abs 7.327e-05) because both stage the dequantized weight tile
   as f16 halves; converting only the *activation* to f32 buys nothing. Any
   future attempt to close the wide tile's gate by widening one operand should
   start from this.
2. **The residual 1.92x is arithmetic, not memory.** `staged_f32` 2.7380 ms
   against `wide_f16in` 1.4252 ms at identical tiling. That difference is the
   price of the f16 rounding and nothing else.
3. **The down projection is an occupancy defect, not a mechanism failure.**
   At rows=1024, K=10240, N=320 the prototype's grid is 40 blocks for 40 CUs,
   one wave with no latency hiding. A smaller BM would restore parallelism at
   the cost of weight re-reads. Unmeasured; do not cite the 0.859x as evidence
   against the mechanism.

Open, and deliberately not claimed here: `staged_f32` is a prototype whose
arithmetic is not bit-exact with the parent, so promoting it into the
hyper_connection route requires the full `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md`
production gate (mean/p95/p99/max KL, top-1 by category, determinism,
isolation, task quality) plus a registered strict fallback. That gate has not
been run. The next unit is to run it on the GR-up path, or to fix the down
projection's tiling first if the route needs both halves.
