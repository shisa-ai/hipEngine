# Extend NextN graphs with attention schedule bindings

## Summary

Extend proposal graphs beyond the fixed short-context cap while matching the
ordinary BF16 draft attention schedule at every chain depth.

## Changes

- Bind each graph to per-depth context caps, split counts and kernel identities.
  Context buckets stop at page edges and any non-page-aligned policy transition.
- Preserve cached-only rejection on binding drift, bounded graph ownership and
  eager fallback after capture failures.
- Separate proposal graph eligibility from target verifier graph eligibility in
  the public greedy serving route. Sampled proposal behavior is unchanged.
