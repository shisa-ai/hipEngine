# Deepest passing scope is 13-47, three layers beyond the certified scope

## Summary

The boundary ladder found a passing scope deeper than the certified one:

| Scope | Mean KL | p95 KL | p99 KL | Max KL | Top-1 | Verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 12-47 | 6.593e-4 | 3.469e-3 | 9.765e-3 | 3.482e-2 | 0.98837 | fail (top-1) |
| **13-47** | **5.167e-4** | **2.716e-3** | **8.329e-3** | **2.504e-2** | **0.99419** | **pass** |
| 14-47 | 4.367e-4 | 2.109e-3 | 6.332e-3 | 1.835e-2 | 0.99225 | pass |
| 15-47 | 4.453e-4 | 1.961e-3 | 6.538e-3 | 6.349e-2 | 0.99548 | fail (max) |
| 16-47 | 3.236e-4 | 1.629e-3 | 4.843e-3 | 1.610e-2 | 0.99483 | pass |

**13-47 is the deepest passing scope**, three layers beyond the certified 16-47,
with roughly 2x headroom on every gate.

**I predicted this wrong.** I said 12-47's top-1 failure implied near-zero margin
for anything in that band. 13-47 has 1.94x headroom on the mean and 2.0x on max.
A neighbouring scope's failure did not bound the next one's margin - which is the
same non-monotonicity this packet keeps producing, now demonstrated on the
pass/fail boundary rather than within the failing region.

15-47 is the sharpest instance yet: it passes mean, p95 and p99 comfortably and
fails only on `max_kl` (6.349e-2 against 5e-2), while both 14-47 (1.835e-2) and
16-47 (1.610e-2) pass max with room. One layer's difference moves the worst case
by 3.5x.

## The result does not yet establish production value

Every scope in this packet is measured with **WMMA cleared in both arms**. So
these rows certify *staged on the named scope with no WMMA anywhere*. Production
is not that configuration: the f16 WMMA route owns layers 16-47, so a staged
scope of 13-15 would only ever replace the **exact chain** on 13-15, with WMMA
still running from 16.

The marginal cost of that composition cannot be read off this table. Adding
WMMA's 16-47 drift (3.80e-4 mean) to the staged 13-15 increment would be a
summed-KL budget, and this packet's own ladders show that is invalid.

So I added a second route, `staged_f32_over_wmma`, whose **teacher is current
production** (WMMA@16-47) rather than the exact chain, and whose candidate adds
staged at the named scope on top. It is running at 13-15 and 13-14.

Wiring verified before launch: the staged dispatch claims layers 13 and 15 and
**declines 16 and 20**, which is the property that makes the composition
meaningful - if it also claimed 16-47 it would contend with WMMA rather than
supplement it.

## Estimated value, if the composition passes

3 layers x 8.985 ms per layer-step (family total 38.9055 -> 29.9208 ms at rows
1024) x 4 chunks of 1024 rows = ~108 ms against a ~16,300 ms request, or
**~0.66% of prefill**. That is an estimate from the kernel packet, not a measured
end-to-end figure, and it is small enough that it needs to be measured rather
than argued before anything is promoted.
