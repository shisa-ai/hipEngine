# TP2 sustained D128 authorized horizon is 42-45 positions, not 128

## Summary

The single-prompt result is a suite property, and it has a number. Across the
whole 18-prompt teacher suite (2,304 teacher-forced positions per arm, one
revision, one host), **three materially different implementations satisfy the
full production numeric envelope on every prompt up to D=42** — hipEngine TP1
token-serial (no TP2 code), TP2 token-serial (shipped), and TP2 rank-local bulk
prefill — and TP2 token-serial reaches **D=45**. Beyond that they all breach on
the *same four prompts at the same decode depths*: 43/46/43 on
`heldout_mixed_review`, 51/51/51 on `mixed_ja_en_review`, 80/80 on
`heldout_mixed_summary` (TP2 arms only), 83/83/83 on `mixed_ja_en_translate`.
The reference arm reproduces itself exactly (max KL 0.0 on all 2,304 positions,
18/18 prompts), so the depths are set by the reference trajectory's own logit
geometry, not by any candidate's arithmetic. `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md`
already carries the concept ("A D120 diagnostic fails the absolute tail ceiling
at max KL 0.08574; therefore no long-horizon authorization transfers from this
result"); this entry supplies the measured horizon for the TP2 gate.

Per-arm, teacher distance over the suite:

| arm | schedule | horizon | prompts breaching | positions > 0.05 | mean KL | worst prompt mean | worst max KL | min top-1 |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `tp1-bulk` (self-check) | bulk | - | **0/18** | 0/2304 | 0 | 0 | 0.0000 | 1.0000 |
| `tp1-serial` | token-serial | **42** | 3/18 | 5/2304 | 6.912e-04 | 7.241e-03 | 0.6092 | 0.9844 |
| `tp2-serial` (shipped) | token-serial | **45** | 2/18 | 3/2304 | 3.688e-04 | 2.334e-03 | 0.2686 | 0.9844 |
| `tp2-bulk` (candidate) | bulk-tp2 | **42** | 4/18 | 5/2304 | 4.771e-04 | 2.558e-03 | 0.2608 | 0.9844 |

Counting every envelope statistic (mean/p95/p99/max and the 0.99 top-1 bar), the
shipped TP1 token-serial route fails **5 of 18** prompts, the shipped TP2 route
**4 of 18**, and the bulk candidate **5 of 18**. TP2's shipped route is the best
of the three on the suite mean and the fewest breaching prompts; the bulk
candidate buys a marginally lower worst max (0.2608 vs 0.2686) at the cost of
three positions of horizon and two more breaching prompts.

Mechanism: the positions carrying large KL are the flat ones. Across the 2,304
reference rows the teacher's top-1 probability has median **0.9951**, while the
27 rows with KL > 0.01 have median **0.7988** (max 0.9421) and a median top-2
logit gap of 1.66 against a suite median of 5.73. Flatness is necessary but not
sufficient — 712 rows are below 0.9 top-1 probability and only a few of them
carry a large KL — because a flat row only converts a residual difference into
KL where the two implementations still differ. All arms keep top-1 agreement
>= 0.9844 (two rows in 128 at worst).

Consequence, stated as the open decision rather than a conclusion: the sustained
gate's **D=128 is past every route's authorized horizon**, so its verdict at that
depth is not a TP2 discriminator. The options are (a) run the sustained gate at a
declared horizon of D<=42 (any route) or D<=45 (the shipped TP2 route), which the
existing authorization framework already supports, (b) keep D=128 and read the
numeric envelope beyond the horizon as diagnostic while scoring same-schedule
parity, or (c) leave the gate as it is and record that no route qualifies at
D=128 on this host. Nothing is promoted either way: `decode_partial_dtype` stays
`bf16` and token-serial stays the shipped prefill route.

## Changes

- `scripts/tp2_prefill_schedule_failure_probe.py`: suite mode (`--prompts all`),
  `--store-rows`/`--compare-rows` so the two TP2 schedules can be compared across
  the suite with one session resident at a time, per-prompt and suite rollups, the
  full identity (`source_sha256`) in the artifact, and the effective
  `prefill_schedule`/`prefill_use_bulk` per arm.
- `scripts/tp2_prefill_schedule_suite_rollup.py` (new): merges the per-arm passes
  into one artifact and derives the authorized horizon, the per-prompt first
  breaching depth, the per-prompt envelope table and the reference flatness
  statistics. It refuses to merge passes that name different teacher captures or
  different source revisions.
- `scripts/tp2_resident_control.py`: `prefill_schedule` is now a property that
  reports the route the adapter will actually drive, so the TP1 token-serial
  reference arm is no longer labeled `bulk` in a comparison artifact.
- `tests/test_unit_scripts_tp2_prefill_schedule_suite_rollup.py` (new) and
  additions to `tests/test_unit_scripts_tp2_prefill_schedule_failure_probe.py`
  and `tests/test_unit_tp2_sustained_quality.py`: CPU-only coverage for the
  envelope/breach logic, the horizon search, the per-prompt merge, the
  cross-revision and cross-teacher refusals, and the schedule labeling.
- `benchmarks/README.md`, `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`,
  `docs/QWEN38-27B-GFX1100-TP2.md`: the sustained section now carries the horizon
  and the implementation-independence of the breaching depths.

## Validation

(W7900 rank 0 + RX 7900 XTX rank 1, gfx1100), model
