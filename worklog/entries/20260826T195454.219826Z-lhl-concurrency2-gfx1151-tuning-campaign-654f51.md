# CONCURRENCY2-GFX1151 tuning campaign plan

## Summary

Froze the gfx1151 MTP tuning campaign plan after reviewing the current
hipEngine evidence and six external Strix Halo projects. The plan names three
measured gaps: G1 — public serving C1/B3 (12.940 tok/s, 1.4337x AR) sits ~39%
below our own direct-leaf exact B3 (21.157528 tok/s, 1.8095x AR); G2 —
CONCURRENCY2 MTP is flat at 12.6-13.6 tok/s across c1-c8 while AR scales
(indicative quant-mismatched row: 10.9 -> 29.8 tok/s); G3 — physical SPECDEC2
acceptance collapses from 80.48% at C1 to 18.43% at C2/C4.

The "llama-like MTP was much faster" recollection resolves to the
accuracy-traded `llama-compat` lane (gfx1151 B2 69.50 tok/s / 1.2776x, W7900
122.67 tok/s) plus the exact direct-leaf B3 result — neither is the qualified
serving route. This campaign tunes the exact route only.
