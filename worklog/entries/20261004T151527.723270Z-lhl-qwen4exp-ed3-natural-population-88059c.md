# ED-3 answered: the synthetic expert-population model understates the natural prompt by 1.9x

## Summary

ED-3's evidence cell states the defect exactly: *"The historical population probe measured
203 of 512 active experts on average using **synthetic token IDs 4000..5023**, not a natural
code-p4096 prompt."* Measured on the natural prompt, the answer is that the model is wrong
by **1.9x at the same token count**, and by 2.2x over the full prompt.

| input | tokens | assignments | active of 512 | share |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| synthetic IDs 4000..5023 (historical) | 1,024 | 10,240 | 202.96 | 39.6% |
| **natural code-p4096, first 2 chunks** | 1,024 | 10,240 | **385.48** | **75.3%** |
| **natural code-p4096, full prompt** | 4,096 | 40,960 | **446.48** | **87.2%** |

The first two rows are exactly comparable: same token count, same top-10 routing, same model,
same per-layer structure. Delta **+182.52 experts (+89.93%)**. Across the 48 MoE layers the
range is 354-493 active at 4,096 tokens.

**The row's other stated fact is confirmed exactly.** ED-3 records "43 Q5_1 layers, five Q8_0
(2, 4, 30, 46, 47)". The captured per-row quant triplets reproduce that split precisely, and
the population separates by quant as the row anticipated: `gguf_q5_1` (43 layers) at 388.4
active @1,024 tokens and 451.7 @4,096; `gguf_q8_0` (5 layers) at 360.6 and 402.0. The Q8_0
layers are lower but still ~1.8x the synthetic figure, so a pooled number would not have
hidden the split here.

## Changes

No engine, kernel, dispatch or default change. This is a measurement unit, so there is
nothing to keep or revert.

**What the number implies, marked as inference:** the MoE is 25.3% of the prefill (gate/up
16.38% + down 8.92%, from the stall census `20261004T150839`). If 75-87% of experts are
active rather than ~40%, the expert-weight traffic behind the selected MoE kernels is close
to the **full** matrix, so the headroom a selected/sparse route can have over a dense one is
much smaller than the 40% model implies. That is an inference from the population, not a
measured traffic figure — the census *times* are measured and unaffected.

**Why the synthetic input was pathological, not merely different:** with 10,240 assignments
spread over only 203 experts, the routing was strongly concentrated. That is what a
repetitive degenerate token sequence produces. A natural prompt spreads the same number of
assignments over 385 experts. The historical probe did not measure a slightly different
population; it measured a different regime.

The case is confirmed natural rather than assumed: its `source_prompt_ids` are four real code
prompts and its tokens begin 248045/846/198, not synthetic IDs in the 4000..5023 range.

**Limits.** Counts, not throughput measurements. One case, one model, one quant, one host
(gfx1151, not the gfx1100 default hardware). Natural-task routing is qualified for **this
prompt family only** — a different category (`general_en`, `general_ja`, `mixed_ja_en`) may
have a different population, and the category-heldout requirement in `docs/BENCHMARK.md`
applies before any tuning is based on this. The synthetic arm is the recovered fixture, not a
re-run; its own artifact records its provenance limits.
