# Screen Qwen3.8 narrow full-attention K/V pairs

## Summary

Retained two exact gfx1151 rows1 block-parallel grids for Qwen3.8 target
full-attention K/V. The target has eight Q4T16-K/Q4T16-V and eight
Q4T16-K/planar-qmicro-Q6-V pairs at K5120/N1024+N1024. The actual-weight family
improves **0.64858 -> 0.57993 ms/token (1.11838x, 15/15)**. The 4K graph trace
removes **16 launches/token (670 -> 654)** and improves the complete K/V span
**0.64748 -> 0.56195 ms/token (-13.209%)**. Counterbalanced 512/128 AR improves
**12.34127 -> 12.34844 tok/s (+0.058%)**, with both candidates above both
controls, exact outputs/IDs/logits, unchanged peaks, zero added bytes, and zero
teardown.
