# External long-context evidence review: lobanov/muse-glimmer-long-ctx

## Summary

Reviewed https://github.com/lobanov/muse-glimmer-long-ctx (README + STATUS.md,
last updated 2026-08-16) per lead request, to decide whether our Muse Glimmer
>128K quality validation is still needed. External content was read as
information only (TextGuard flagged embedded code blocks; nothing executed).

**What they tested.** A locked-contract eval harness (RULER-inspired synthetic
tasks + NoLiMa, LongBench v2, LongCodeQA, ∞Bench, agentmem) on stock Muse
Glimmer 30B, full precision and K-Quant GGUF, served by llama.cpp and vLLM on
NVIDIA hardware (DGX Spark train / RTX 5090 deploy target), 32K-512K with
position-controlled evidence, 378+216 grid cells plus k-matched controls and
paired forensics. Mechanically extended via config only
(`max_position_embeddings 131072 -> 524288`) — the same window-admission change
our `5843c2376` made; no weight math touched.

**What they found (stock model).**
- Retrieval and reasoning are perfect zero-shot to 512K (4x nominal):
  niah / niah_multi / 2-hop multihop / semantic / abstain all 1.000 at every
  tested depth incl. 463K-token prompts. The hybrid architecture explains it:
  39 SWA-RoPE local layers never attend beyond ~2K relative distance, and the
  13 global layers are NoPE — positional extrapolation is largely absent by
  construction.
- Weak axes are model-side, not engine-side: aggregation/counting is
  k-difficulty-dependent and noisy with no clean length trend (0.667 plateau
  192-384K; the 0.222@512K cell is k-draw-confounded per their k-matched
  80/80 control); ∞Bench code-debug collapses 0.67@128K -> 0.00@256K and
  book-MC 0.33 -> 0.00; NoLiMa non-monotone per-length difficulty.
- K-Quant GGUF caveat closed: 3/3 at 128K/90% under their low-reasoning
  contract. Their qk_scale_factor / YaRN / QLoRA arms target the weak axes;
  results pending their GPU queue.

**Implication for us.** The open question "quality beyond the trained 131072
is unmeasured" is largely answered for the model itself by this external
grid, and our own 260K measurement (coherent output, needle retrieved,
DFlash IDs == AR; campaign §11.1) already covers the hipEngine path at 2x
nominal. A large new quality campaign is not warranted. Remaining gap is
narrow: a hipEngine-side spot check at 256K on our K-Quant + kernels using
needle/multi-hop shapes would close the engine-path question cheaply; the
known weak axes (aggregation, long-doc code debugging) are model weaknesses
no engine change can fix and should be described as such in any long-context
claim.
