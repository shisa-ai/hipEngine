# CORRECTION: drafter forwards are bandwidth-bound (5% dispatch gap), quantization worth pursuing

## Summary

Corrects `worklog/entries/20261110T082914.227025Z-lhl-qwen38-27b-specdec-35b1e7.md`, which wrongly inferred "dispatch-bound, quantization ~3% upside" from effective bandwidth alone. rocprofv3 kernel-sum vs wall shows the DSpine forward is **bandwidth-bound**: kernel-sum 43.2 ms vs wall 45.6 ms = **only 5% dispatch gap**. One kernel, `dflash_dense_bf16_to_bf16_wmma_kernel`, is **39 ms = 86%** of the forward, reading weights at ~71 GB/s because rows=16 does not saturate the WMMA kernel. Quantization therefore cuts the dominant cost: Q8 ~halves that 39 ms, Q4 ~quarters it -> potentially **-50-75% of the forward**, not ~3%. Quantization is worth pursuing (lead approved (a) then (b)).

## Validation

- DSpine forward: wall 45.6 ms; kernel-sum/forward 43.2 ms; dispatch gap ~5%. Top kernel `dflash_dense_bf16_to_bf16_wmma` 39.0 ms/fwd (86%), `dflash_dense_bf16_to_f32_wmma` 3.0 ms, sliding attention 0.78 ms, norms/glue <0.5 ms.
- The earlier "28% of peak => dispatch-bound" inference was invalid: low effective bandwidth here is low-efficiency bandwidth execution (small-row WMMA), not launch gaps. kernel-sum ~= wall proves the time is in the kernels, reading weights.
