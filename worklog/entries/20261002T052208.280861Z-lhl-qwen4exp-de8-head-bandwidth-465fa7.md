# DE-8 closes: the head is at its own family's read rate, no lever at c=1

## Summary

DE-8 records the historical `lm_head` cost of 3.25 ms/token and asks for it to be priced -
*"Price GEMV versus argmax in `X-4`; only screen weight reuse or stable k=1 reduction if
material."* `X-4` priced it at **2.980 ms/step, one kernel per step, 5.50% of decode kernel time
and 6.04% of the 49.3 ms wall**, the second largest single role in the decode split. This is the
screen that follows, and its answer is that **neither named screen has a target**.

**The head reads 675.4 MB per step and runs at 226.7 GB/s.** `root.lm_head` maps to
`output.weight` (Q8_0, 248320 x 2560 = 675,430,400 B); a dense k=1 GEMV must read all of it once
per step, so 2.980 ms is 226.7 GB/s.

**The decisive comparison is internal to the same capture, host and kernel family.** Four sibling
Q8_0 decode GEMV families, with byte traffic computed from the same GGUF header:

| family | MB/step | ms/step | GB/s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **`linear:root.lm_head`** | **675.4** | **2.980** | **226.7** |
| `linear:attn_qkv` (36 kernels) | 1,002.7 | 4.658 | 215.3 |
| `linear:attn_gate` (36) | 601.6 | 2.890 | 208.2 |
| `linear:ssm_out` (36) | 601.6 | 2.842 | 211.7 |
| `linear:attn_output` (12) | 200.5 | 0.947 | 211.8 |

Sibling range 208.2-215.3 GB/s, median 211.7. **The head is 1.071x the sibling median and 1.053x
the fastest sibling - the fastest of the five.** At the sibling median rate it would cost 3.190 ms
and at the fastest sibling rate 3.137 ms, both *slower* than the 2.980 ms it takes.
