# a kernel trace independently confirms the down MMQ at minus 23 percent with the gate up kernel as a flat control

## Summary

The down MMQ fix has now been measured by three instruments that do not share a
mechanism: the HIP-event probe that found the defect, the campaign's public-path
loop metric, and `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace`. This unit adds the third and closes
the kernel verification tier in `docs/OPTIMIZATION.md` section 8, which asks for a
trace showing the kernel ran under its expected name with a plausible duration.

**All three agree, and the trace carries its own control.** The Q4_K gate_up
kernel, launched the same 58 times against the same rows, moves 0.2 percent while
the down moves 22.9 percent.

## The trace, before and after

`rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` over two 512-token prefills, 58 dispatches per kernel
(29 layers x 2 calls). Before is the run recorded in
`20260928T081118.644190Z-lhl-gemma4-expert-down-mmq-outlier-31796e.md`; after is
this unit.

| kernel | before ms/call | after ms/call | delta |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `q5_1_mmq_ds4_selected_prefill_kernel` | 5.353 | **4.128** | **-22.9%** |
| `gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_q8_1_ds4_mmq32` (control) | 5.410 | 5.400 | +0.2% |
| `gguf_q8_0_prefill_wmma_kernel<32>` | 6.550 | 6.667 | +1.8% |
| `gemma4_attention_prefill_kernel` | 5.783 | 5.847 | +1.1% |

The two arms are separate processes on the same host in the same session. The
three kernels that were not edited ran **1.1 to 1.8 percent slower** in the after
arm, so the run drifted slightly against the change; the down projection ran 22.9
percent faster inside that drift. That is the useful part of the control: it is
not that the edited kernel improved while everything else held, it is that
everything else got marginally worse and the edited kernel improved anyway.

## Three instruments, one number

| instrument | measures | down kernel delta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HIP event pair on the compute stream (`scripts/gemma4_expert_mmq_wrapper_split.py`) | the leaf call in place, per layer | **-24.9%** |
| `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` (this unit) | device-side dispatch duration | **-22.9%** |
| `scripts/gemma4_campaign_bench.py --prompt 512 --output 128` (whole step) | end-to-end prefill | **-6.6%** |

The two kernel-level figures are 5.353 to 4.128 against 5.391 to 4.051 ms per
layer, which is 22.9 against 24.9 percent: a 2-point spread on a change of about
a quarter, from instruments that disagree about what "duration" means (host-side
event brackets include launch overhead, the trace measures device time). The
end-to-end figure is smaller because the kernel is 15 percent of the step, and
15 percent of 23 is the 3.5 percent the probe's own wall-clock showed alongside
its 4.1 percent headline.
