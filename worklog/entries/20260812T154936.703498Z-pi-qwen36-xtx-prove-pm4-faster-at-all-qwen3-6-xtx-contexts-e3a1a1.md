# Prove PM4 faster at all Qwen3.6 XTX contexts

## Summary

Retained PM4 as superior to HIP graph for Qwen3.6-27B Q4_K_M private-c1
128-transition replay windows at all three campaign contexts on the RX 7900
XTX. PM4 won all 15/15 counterbalanced paired samples: median speedups were
+1.412% at 512, +1.747% at 1K, and +1.588% at 4K. Recorded output fields were
exact, native fallback and unretired-submission counts were zero, and tracked
allocation ownership returned to zero after close.
