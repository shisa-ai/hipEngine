# The MMQ route degenerates on a long continuation where the anchor does not

## Summary

The MMQ expert gate/up route collapses a healthy varied continuation into a
repeated single token at 96 prompt ids, while both the fp32 arm and llama.cpp's
`llama-server` on the same artifact keep producing varied output. This is the
route's first anchored defect: it is a disagreement with an independent
implementation rather than with our own fp32 kernel, so it does not depend on
assuming which of our two arms is correct.

The measurement also shows why the campaign's earlier probes understated it.
Comparing only the first greedy token misses this class entirely: at 96 ids the
route's first token is *identical* to llama.cpp's, and the divergence appears
only in the tokens after it. Every earlier comparison in this campaign, including
`scripts/gemma4_mmq_prefill_length_probe.py` and the recorded decision-stability
probes, scored one token at a time.

`worklog/entries/20260927T063400.591554Z-lhl-gemma4-mmq-three-arm-attribution-0c7f18.md`
concluded from first tokens alone that the divergence was near-tie sensitivity
with neither arm systematically wrong. That conclusion is corrected here: the
first-token data was right about what it measured and too narrow to support the
generalisation. Confident rows still agree across all three engines, so the
low-margin framing for the flips survives, but a flip that then self-reinforces
into a loop is a distinct and worse failure than a flip.

## Changes

- `scripts/gemma4_mmq_arm_attribution.py`: `--text`/`--text-file` to put an
  arbitrary prompt to all three arms, and the continuation length is now the
  comparison rather than a four-token afterthought.
- `docs/ENVS.md`: the flag row said the route is "off by up to 2.036 KL with
  greedy decision flips at 7 of the 13 prefill lengths", which conflates lengths
  over the 0.05 bar with lengths that change the decision and is corpus-specific
  besides. It now records 7 over and 2 flipping on the frozen corpus, 9 over and
  6 flipping on the probe corpus, the same length being bit-exact under one and
  a flip under the other, and the 96-id degeneration as the concrete cause.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-27-gemma4-mmq-gate-up-three-arm-attribution.json`:
  the continuation chains and distinct-token counts.

## Validation

- Probe corpus, 16-token continuations, all three arms, at 16/24/96 ids:
  - 96 ids: llama 10 distinct of 16, fp32 10 of 16, MMQ 3 of 16. The MMQ chain
    is `236800, 236800, 236743, 236832, 236832, 236832, 236832, 236743, 236832,
    236832, 236832, 236832, 236743, 236832, 236832, 236832`.
  - 16 and 24 ids: all three arms degenerate, so those lengths are the prompt
    rather than the route and carry no attribution. The 96-id length is the only
    clean case and it is decisive.
- The route's first token at 96 ids equals llama.cpp's (`236800`), which is why
  a first-token comparison reports agreement there.
- Control: a complete 181-token Python function has all three arms agreeing on
  the first token at margin 16.77, and the frozen corpus agrees at 96/512/1024
  with fp32 margins of 12.3 to 13.5. The harness is not producing the effect.
- Mechanism: the record already measures the route's own error as normalised max
  2.09e-2 and mean 1.09e-3 on a projection of mean magnitude 611.6, which is the
  int8 activation-quantization step. A ~2% perturbation at a position the model
  is uncertain about moves the argmax, and the moved argmax then persists,
  because the loop is self-consistent rather than random.

## Next

- The route stays off, and now for a reason an outside implementation supports
  rather than an inference from our own two kernels. `docs/REFACTOR.md`'s removal
  condition stands unchanged: the probe exits 0, then the teacher-forced gate.
- Any future qualification of this route must score continuations, not first
  tokens. A route that agrees on token 1 and loops by token 5 passes every
  first-token gate in the campaign.
- The low-margin rows remain unresolved at the level of which arm is right on
  any individual row. Resolving them needs exact arithmetic rather than a third
  approximate implementation; the CPU streaming reference is the candidate.
