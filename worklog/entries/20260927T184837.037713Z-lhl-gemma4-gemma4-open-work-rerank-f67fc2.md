# Re-rank the Gemma 4 open work against the current base

## Summary

The campaign's "Recommended order" was written against a 709 ms prefill and a
3761 tok/s comparator. Both are gone: the base is now **582 ms** of prefill wall
for 1024 tokens on the W7900 (measured today) and llama.cpp measures **4377** on
the same GPU on the matched basis. Re-ranked by traced cost against that base,
the order changes materially.

**The two largest gaps are both matmul efficiency, not the attention work the
list led with.** MoE expert matmuls are 150.8 ms behind llama.cpp and dense Q8_0
projections 62.5 ms, against 44.6 ms for attention. The doc's item 5 --
"single-plane int8 MMQ with llama.cpp-sized tiles", filed as longer term -- owns
the second-largest gap, and the 704-wide-down argument that was used to exclude
the MoE half covers only the *down*, not the gate_up.
