# AR decode wins every width, AR admission loses every width: one defect explains the published table

## Summary

Per-submodule parity verdict for the published Qwen3.8-27B `Q4_K_M` C1-C8 rows, computed from the
committed decomposition artifact (`2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-c1c8-submodule-decomposition.json`) rather
than from the headline table. Each cell is ours / best-peer.

```
AR admission ms/lane  C1 302.4/229.3  C2 292.2/194.1  C3 292.4/177.5  C4 293.1/163.6
                      C5 291.4/142.0  C6 291.1/125.4  C7 290.5/122.0  C8 290.1/105.9   lose x8
AR decode ms/step     C1 34.1/38.6    C2 42.3/42.6    C3 48.0/79.1    C4 53.1/122.7
                      C5 60.4/113.2   C6 67.1/105.5   C7 75.0/104.2   C8 81.9/105.3    WIN x8
AR published tok/s    C1 22.1/21.7    C2 30.8/35.4    C3 36.3/30.8    C4 40.1/27.8
                      C5 42.1/36.5    C6 43.8/45.8    C7 44.7/52.5    C8 45.7/59.3     win 4, lose 4
MTP published tok/s   C1 31.6/32.7 ... C8 31.4/101.1 (ours flat ~31 across all widths)  lose x8
```

So the objective is not eight problems, it is two:

1. **Admission does not amortize across the wave.** Ours is flat at 290-302 ms/lane from C1 to C8;
   peers fall 229 -> 106. Eight lanes therefore cost us 8x the peer admission. This single defect is
   why C2/C6/C7/C8 are lost even though decode is faster at every width - C3-C5 wins are decode
   masking admission. Root-cause hypothesis to test first: `GGUF_C2_PACKED_PREFILL_MAX_ROWS = 8` was
   declared today, but a flat per-lane cost is what an unbatched prefill looks like, so the grouped
   route is probably not engaged in the server bench despite being reachable in code.
2. **MTP per-row verify cost is 3.21x AR marginal** (fit: AR 27.6 ms fixed + 6.71 ms/row, R2 .9972;
   MTP 21.4 ms fixed + 21.51 ms/row, R2 .9951 - our fixed cost is *lower*, marginal per row is the
   problem). Localization needs the 8-vs-32-row verify trace, currently blocked by the `--block-rows`
   bug in `gguf_mtp_verifier_rocprof.py --mode block-verify`.
