# Judge Inkling candidates on their own prompt blocks in the profile gate

## Summary

The profile gate took the prompt block width from the `strict` generator and used it for the
candidate's captures, while its isolation worker took the width from the candidate's own generator.
The two agreed while every profile read 64-token blocks. After `fast` moved to 256-row blocks
([entry](20261125T023627.416022Z-lhl-inkling2-mini-91a27c.md)), the gate at `1979a63b0` failed its
isolation section on the `fast_resident` arm: 18 of 25 requests bit-identical, and the seven that
differed were exactly the prompts longer than 64 tokens. The primary had captured the candidate
through 64-token blocks; the worker ran 256. The other five sections passed.

- **So the row did not judge the candidate's public schedule.** Its prefill block-end rows were
  64-token blocks, and the mismatch was between two schedules, not two processes.
- **Checked first that the model is not the cause.** In fresh processes the same prompts captured in
  opposite orders give bit-identical logits, equal to the worker's rows; poisoning 6 GB of freed
  device memory before a block leaves a 68-token block unchanged.
- **Fix.** The gate reads the width from the candidate's profile
  (`Inkling2GGUFGenerator.prefill_schedule`, a classmethod that loads no weights), runs the teacher
  on the candidate's blocks so its rows land on the candidate's block ends, and refuses a candidate
  whose generator reports another width. The teacher's arithmetic does not change.
