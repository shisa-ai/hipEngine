# Prefill deficit at C1-C3 re-measured on the current tree: real, worst at C2

## Summary

Task #12 says small-row prefill is slow, "1.52x deficit at ~45 tokens". Re-measured on
the current tree
with a same-protocol repeat pair, the deficit is **real**, largest at **C2 at 0.681x
(1.47x slower)**,
and confined to C1-C3: C4-C8 match or win. The published prefill row it rests on came from commit
`802c0e310`, far behind HEAD, so this replaces an unrepeatable premise with a measured one.

## Changes

- No code change. This is the premise check that has to precede any prefill work.

## Measured

Prefill rate = `prompt_tokens_per_lane x lanes / admission_ms`, which is exactly how the
published row
reproduces (`44.9 / 0.302991 s = 148.2` against the README's 149.072). One-token packets `pf-r1` /
`pf-r2` at commit `cac9b449c`, long arm from the K3 pair, 10 prompts x widths 1-8, 44.9 prompt
tokens/lane, both `status: complete`:

| C | r1 | r2 | spread | mean | vs current | vs laurent |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 148.2 | 146.6 | 1.1% | 147.4 | **0.734x** | 0.753x |
| 2 | 167.4 | 158.9 | 5.2% | 163.1 | **0.681x** | 0.705x |
| 3 | 227.8 | 234.4 | 2.9% | 231.1 | **0.892x** | 0.914x |
| 4 | 298.5 | 278.1 | 7.1% | 288.3 | 1.023x | 1.050x |
| 5 | 320.3 | 315.7 | 1.4% | 318.0 | 0.984x | 1.006x |
| 6 | 374.9 | 358.9 | 4.4% | 366.9 | 1.002x | 1.025x |
| 7 | 396.7 | 431.6 | 8.4% | 414.1 | 1.107x | 1.125x |
| 8 | 417.3 | 399.2 | 4.4% | 408.2 | 0.963x | 0.962x |

- C1, C2 and C3 exceed their own spread, so the deficit is not noise. C4-C8 sit inside or near it,
  except C7 where the win is plausible but only 1.5x the 8.4% spread.
- Magnitude correction for the task title: **1.47x at C2**, not 1.52x, and the shape is a
  *fixed cost*,
  not a rate problem: admission is 302.99 ms for 44.9 tokens at C1 (6.75 ms/token) against
  107.6 ms per
  lane at C8 (1.35 ms/token). The peer C1 equivalent is about 223 ms, so the target is
  roughly **80 ms of
  per-request fixed cost**, which is also why batching to C4 mostly hides it.
- Prefill variance is materially higher than decode: 7.1% and 8.4% spreads at C4/C7 versus
  0.08%-1.06%
  for K3 at the same widths. Any future prefill claim needs a repeat, not a single packet.
- Peer columns are carried forward from `benchmarks/README.md`, not re-measured here.
