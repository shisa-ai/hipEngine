# Attribute sampled GR-wide primitive error to operand rounding

## Summary

The sampled F32-input GR-wide primitive closely follows its F16-rounded operand
oracle. Its error against the original F32 operands is dominated by operand
rounding on these samples, rather than a large accumulation defect. This does
not prove the full-model drift has only that cause. R0028 is still open; no
kernel, arithmetic, threshold or default changed.

## Changes

- Saved matched-input primitive diagnostics, source/host/model/command and
  original report/script hashes in
  `audit/refactor-sweep/2026-11-01-r0028-primitive.json`.
- Replaced the ledger's unperformed primitive-capture action with the observed
  rounding finding and the next repair experiment.

## Validation

- The artifact records the exact command. A strict owner with wide enabled
  prefills canonical `code-p1024`, capturing the first three real GR-up wide
  calls at rows1024/K320/N10240. Each sample compares eight evenly spaced rows
  and 64 columns against float64 accumulation of actual F32 operands and of
  those operands rounded to F16. Q8_0 weights use the existing CPU dequantizer
  in `tests/test_gpu_gguf_q8_0_dense_wide.py`.
- The three maximum absolute errors against the F16-simulated oracle are
  0.000033647128475422505, 0.00002921688610513229 and
  0.00002072792631224729. Against original F32 operands they are
  0.007001387215989752, 0.0093177937165283 and 0.003960018526148801.
  The respective operand-rounding differences are 0.006997016563030201,
  0.009290596860902056 and 0.0039659256146560296.
- Sampled outputs are finite and teardown has zero active allocations/current
  bytes. No repeat, task, full-output primitive or all-layer pass is claimed.
