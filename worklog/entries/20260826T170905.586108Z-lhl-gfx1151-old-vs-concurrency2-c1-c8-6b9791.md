# Measure gfx1151 OLD versus CONCURRENCY2 c1-c8

## Summary

Measured the requested same-host gfx1151 logical-c1 through logical-c8 OLD
versus frozen-CONCURRENCY2 server curve. The primary metric is median aggregate
generated completion tok/s over p512/d128 SSE with one warmup and three measured
runs per width. Production-FP16 CONCURRENCY2 is **10.1% faster at c1** and
**104.6% to 260.1% faster at c2-c8** than OLD's exact route.

| Route | c1 | c2 | c3 | c4 | c5 | c6 | c7 | c8 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| OLD | 9.915 | 4.919 | 6.807 | 8.885 | 9.658 | 11.190 | 12.598 | 14.366 |
| CONCURRENCY2 | 10.921 | 17.713 | 22.656 | 25.492 | 26.680 | 27.832 | 28.720 | 29.392 |
| Difference | +10.1% | +260.1% | +232.8% | +186.9% | +176.2% | +148.7% | +128.0% | +104.6% |
