# Gemma 4: a low-margin probe chain, and the MMQ gate/up defect it found

## Summary

The campaign recorded an evaluator gap: its frozen chain is eight sentences
cycled, so its rows are near one-hot and the top-1 bar cannot see a
reordering-class divergence. Closing that gap produced a **probe chain** and, on
its first use, a finding that changes the pending decision rather than informing
it.

Measured on the strict arm, the frozen chain has **no row below 0.20 top-1
margin at all** - median margin 1.0000, all 1023 rows above 0.20. Its top-1 bar
is not nearly blind; it is exactly blind. The probe chain carries 247 rows below
0.20 and 77 below 0.05.

Pointed at the fused MMQ expert gate/up route, the probe chain reports
`kl_mean` 0.384, `kl_max` 10.86 and **229 top-1 flips of 1023 rows**, where the
campaign's recorded frozen-chain evidence reports 1.438e-04, 0.0651 and 0 flips.
The route replaces only the prefill's gate/up projection, so a single forward of
N ids reproduces all of it, and the length sweep shows the recorded number was
taken at the route's best case: deterministically over the binding 0.05 `kl_max`
bar at **7 of the 13 prefill lengths from 16 to 1024 ids** where it runs, up to
**2.036 at 64 ids with a greedy decision flip**, against 7.3e-07 at 1024 ids.

This is not a marginal arithmetic path and not a tail effect. The route is wrong
at the short-prompt lengths production uses, and the flag that keeps it off was
justified in the source, `docs/ENVS.md`, `docs/REFACTOR.md` and the campaign
document by text that argued the opposite - that the breach was a near-certain-row
tail effect on 2 of 1022 rows that the already-shipped split breached too. Both
halves of that are now measured false: the default path is bit-identical to the
strict arm, and the breaching lengths have top-1 flips.

  chain, plus `require_single_pass` on `exact_prompt_ids` so a corpus too short
  for the target raises instead of silently cycling back into predictable text.

  `docs/ENVS.md`, `docs/REFACTOR.md` and the campaign document, each now naming

## Validation

- **RED/GREEN for the new math.** `TestMarginReport` was written first and failed
  on `ImportError`; two of its expectations were then wrong rather than the
  implementation (a rolled flat row keeps its argmax, and the moderate row lands
  in the mid band), and both were corrected on the measurement.
  `tests/test_unit_gemma4_teacher_forced_gate.py`,
  `tests/test_unit_gemma4_campaign_bench.py` and
  `tests/test_unit_gemma4_teacher_forced_routes.py`: 40 passed.
- **The harness agrees with the record.** Re-running the MMQ route on the frozen
  chain reproduces the campaign's recorded `kl_mean` 1.438e-04, `kl_max` 0.0651,
  zero flips and 3 rows over 1e-3 exactly, so the divergence below is not a
  harness difference.
- **The probe chain is deterministic and paired.** Seeded, and the gate refuses a
  mismatched `chain_kind` or a mismatched prompt id list.
- **The default path is exact.** KL exactly 0.0 on all 1023 rows of both chains,
  0 flips, including all 77 close-margin rows, with 30690 split launches observed
  and `selections` [2] against the strict arm's [1] and 0.
- **The defect is deterministic.** Three arms per length are bit-identical at
  every length, so it is an indexing or arithmetic difference and not a race.
- **The route actually ran.** `gate_up_mmq32` launches are counted per length and
  reported in the probe record; the route is absent below 16 ids, which is the
  lane threshold, and KL is exactly 0.0 there.

## Next

- **The mechanism is unidentified and that is the next unit.** The route
  quantizes activations to DS4 Q8_1, whose error is per-row and should not vary
  by six orders of magnitude with the row count, so that bound does not explain
  it. The productive next step is to correlate the divergence with per-expert row
  counts and tile remainders, then read
  `gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_q8_1_ds4_mmq32_fused_prefill_compact32_bf16_bf16_out`
  against `qwen35_moe_mmq32_tile_map`. The leaf's only unit coverage is 4 experts
  over 10 rows in `tests/test_unit_gemma4_expert_route.py`, which never reaches
  128 experts, so a geometry-parameterized test is part of the fix.
- The prefill campaign's other numbers are unaffected: the Q4_K row batch, Q5_K
  row4 and Q8_0 tile work are bit-exact and were measured against the default
  path, which this unit found to be bit-identical to strict.
- The probe chain is now available to every future changed-arithmetic candidate
  on this model, which is the class that most needs it.
