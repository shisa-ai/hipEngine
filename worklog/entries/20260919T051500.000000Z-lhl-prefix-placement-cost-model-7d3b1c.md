# Prefix-cache placement cost model

## Summary

Page placement decides which prefill route a cache hit gets, and the two routes
are far apart. A contiguous shared allocation keeps the slot-local prefill; a
gapped one drops to the packed paged route at **6.5 ms/token against 0.39
ms/token** for the full prefill it replaces. Admission now prefers contiguity
and declines a gapped hit whose suffix is longer than the paged route can pay
for. The wide retained working set goes from +9.5% to **-11.0%** wall; the
default retention is unchanged at -18.6%.

## What the measurement showed

Phase attribution across the two served runs isolated one phase:
`suffix_prefill` was 5.8 s at the default retention and **125-135 s** at
retained 16, while `prefill_batched` fell 21 s. Per-turn records named the
culprit: one lane spent **114,195 ms** prefilling a 6,186-token suffix on an
8,234-token prompt, against ~14,700 ms for a comparable lane that prefilled
8,698 tokens from scratch.

Three hypotheses were tested and rejected before the right one:

- **Per-chunk quadratic import.** Prefilling the 6,186-token suffix in 1 chunk
  costs 40.1 s and in 25 chunks 33.9 s, so chunking is not the driver.
- **The whole-history KV import.** Stubbing
  `_copy_session_packed_kv_segments` changes the same shape by **0.1%**.
- **Shallow hits.** The per-turn records show the opposite: hits reuse 91-96%
  of their prompt.

What remained is the route itself. `_gguf_device_kv_contiguous_base_row`
returns None for a gapped block table, which clears
`slot_local_full_prefill` and sends the suffix through the packed paged route.
At the default retention the allocator's adjacency preference already placed 15
of 16 hits contiguously, which is why that configuration never paid it; at
retained 16 the pinned retained pages fragment the arena and 8 of 18 placements
were gapped.

## Changes

- `hipengine/generation/qwen35_gguf.py`: shared admission asks for a
  contiguous placement first and falls back to a gapped one instead of
  refusing. Counters `contiguous_admissions` / `gapped_admissions` are
  exposed in the prefix telemetry.
- `hipengine/generation/qwen35_gguf.py`: a gapped placement is accepted only
  when the suffix is at most `HIPENGINE_GGUF_PREFIX_GAPPED_SUFFIX_MAX` tokens
  (default 512); past that the hit is declined with
  `gapped_suffix_exceeds_paged_budget` and the request takes the full prefill.
- `tests/test_unit_gguf_prefix_cache_runner.py`:
  `test_gapped_placement_declines_a_hit_whose_suffix_exceeds_the_paged_budget`
  drives a pool that refuses contiguous placement and pins both sides of the
  threshold.

## Validation

- Crossover, Qwen3.5-0.8B, gapped placement forced, against the miss the hit
  replaces: 2048+512 **0.63x**, 4096+512 **0.87x**, 2048+768 **1.07x**,
  4096+768 **1.22x**. The 512-token default sits below both crossings.
- Served multi-turn A/B, zbook / gfx1151, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B `UD-Q4_K_M`:

  | config | wall vs off | tok/s | hits | contiguous/gapped | suffix_prefill |
  | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
  | default retention | **-18.6%** | 20.80 | 16/27 | 15/1 | 5.9 s |
  | retained 16, before | +9.5% | 15.46 | 18/27 | - | 125.3 s |
  | retained 16, after | **-11.0%** | 19.03 | 14/27 | 10/4 | **6.8 s** |

  Retained 16 declines 3 hits as `gapped_suffix_exceeds_paged_budget`, which
  is what removes 128 s of paged suffix prefill.
- `tests/ -k "prefix or kvcache or device_kv or pool"` — the only failure is
  the pre-existing `test_unit_grammar_masks` `llguidance` import.

## Next

- The paged prefill route being several times slower per token than the
  slot-local one is the real prize. Closing that gap would make every hit pay
  regardless of placement and let the retained working set grow; the cost model
  here is a guard around it, not a substitute.
- `GlobalDeviceKVPool.shrink_idle` still returns 0, so a grown pool never
  returns capacity.
