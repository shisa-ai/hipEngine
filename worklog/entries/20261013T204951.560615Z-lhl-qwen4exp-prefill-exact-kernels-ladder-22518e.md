# Qwen4Exp exact prefill kernels and 1K/8K/16K ladder

## Summary

Two bit-identical prefill kernels and a long-context benchmark ladder for
`qwen4_exp_gguf` / `hip_gfx1151` / `gguf_ud_q4_k_xl`, `production`, Radeon
8060S / gfx1151 (machine `55ea6c509d0b49eea8de7094a1023668`), chunk 4096,
canonical protocol (one warmup, three repetitions, compact capture, warm PLE
cache), tracked-clean `1a5f0069a`.

- Canonical 12-case: prefill 769.2 / 932.9 / 1108.4 to 779.4 / 977.9 /
  1190.3 tok/s at p512/p1024/p4096 (+1.33% / +4.82% / +7.39%), decode
  20.94 / 20.18 / 20.59, output token ids identical in 12/12 cases.
- New 1024/8192/16384-token fixture, first measurement: prefill 978.7 /
  1158.8 / 1133.1 tok/s, decode 20.18 / 20.53 / 20.43, 12/12 deterministic.
- Kernel time per code-p4096 prefill: 3657 to 3409 ms (gufo 2373).

Commands:
`scripts/qwen4exp_canonical_ar_bench.py hipengine --model-root <UD-Q4_K_XL>
--prefill-chunk-size 4096 --capture-mode compact --ple-cache-mode warm
--execution-profile production --output <json>`, and the same with
`--fixture benchmarks/fixtures/qwen4exp_canonical_ar_p1024_p8192_p16384.json`.

## Decisions

- **Both kernels are defaults in every profile with no selector.** They are
  bit-identical to their parents (bitwise GPU tests), so there is nothing to
  gate; the parents stay for other geometries and for the tests.
- **The fused epilogue was measured before and dropped.** On 2026-10-13 an
  earlier version measured +2.1% at code-p4096 and -0.6% at code-p512 in a
  two-case A/B and was not kept. The 12-case run shows every case positive
  (p512 +1.3%); the earlier p512 reading was noise.
- **A/B results not acted on:** `HIPENGINE_GGUF_Q8_DENSE_WIDE_F16_ACT=0`
  (F32 activations converted while staging, no separate conversion kernel)
  is 15% slower at both code-p512 and code-p4096;
  `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_DENSE_F16_PAIR=1` is +0.4% at code-p4096 with
  identical tokens and stays off pending a 12-case run.
- **Regression protocol.** `scripts/qwen4exp_rate_regression_check.py`
  compares two artifacts of one fixture on one host (3% rate tolerance,
  determinism, optional output identity). The two artifacts of this unit are
  the baselines; `benchmarks/HARNESSES.md` records the procedure.
