# The MTP cycle is a fixed weight stream: 78.7 ms fixed, 7.9 ms per verified row

## Summary

Task #31 step 3. The previous entry measured *where* the c=1 cycle goes (target
verify window 110.67 ms/cycle = 98%) and left one question: inside that window,
how much is per-row work and how much is fixed per-cycle cost. The candidate
budget sweep answers it without a profiler, because the number of verified rows
is exactly `budget + 1`:

| budget | verify rows | target ms/cycle | phase sum | observed wall | ITL ms | accepted/cycle | tok/s/request |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 | 94.52 | 95.68 | 95.73 | 50.49 | 0.87 | 19.81 |
| 2 | 3 | 101.78 | 103.19 | 101.67 | 38.65 | 1.61 | 25.87 |
| 3 | 4 | 110.35 | 112.13 | 109.39 | 35.71 | 2.08 | 28.01 |

Marginal target cost per added verify row: **+7.92 ms**. Fixed intercept
(target at zero rows): **78.69 ms**. So 71% of the budget-3 verify window does
not depend on how many rows are verified, and an added row costs ~8 ms.

The phase sum tracks the independently observed decode wall within ~2% at every
budget (95.68/95.73, 103.19/101.67, 112.13/109.39), so the accounting is closed
across the sweep: no cost hides outside the instrumented phases at any budget.

## Interpretation, and its limit

The fixed 78.7 ms sits within noise of a plain c=1 AR step on this model
(~83 ms ITL from the 2026-09-19 baseline), and the marginal row is ~8 ms. That
is the signature of weight-streaming-bound work: the forward pass pays for
streaming the model once, and extra rows reuse that stream cheaply. So the lever
is *passes*, not host code and not per-row cost.

Stated precisely, the sweep separates **fixed per cycle** from **marginal per
row**. It does not itself separate device-busy from synchronization; "fixed" is
not "device-busy". The weight-streaming reading is an inference from the AR-step
cost, and a marker/profiler run on this route is still the direct measurement if
that distinction ever matters for a decision. For the decisions pending now it
does not: the host-side phases are 2.1 ms of a 112 ms cycle (#31 steps 1-2), and
the per-row cost is 7.9 ms.
