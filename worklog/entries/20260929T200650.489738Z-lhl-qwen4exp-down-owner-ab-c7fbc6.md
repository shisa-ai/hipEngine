# The expert-down owner is not the fastest registered one - and its repair is the open question

## Summary

`moe_linear` / `prefill_rows_ge512_k640_exact_grouped_q5_1_down` has three registered owners.
They had never been put in an arms pair. Measuring them finds that the production default is
well justified against its own fallback, but that **a registered owner no manifest entry
selects is faster on the main kernel in all 12 cases, median -50.4%**.

That is not a promotion candidate as it stands, and the reason is concrete: the winning arm
is an approximate-then-repair design whose **repair pass is not timed here**, while the
production default is exact and has no repair at all. The -50.4% is the whole owner cost for
the production default and only part of it for the wmma owner. Sizing the gap from recorded
numbers puts the route-level effect at roughly **5.4%**, an order of magnitude below the
main-kernel figure, on two uncertain terms.

So this unit's product is a **measured main-kernel win plus a named, bounded experiment**,
not a speedup.

## The repair gap, sized with every term labelled

| term | value | provenance |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| q5_1 down main-kernel total | 1,482.958 ms | `q51_fast_ms`, repair-rate artifact |
| q5_1 repair | 661.21 ms | `q51_repair_ms`, **the artifact marks this an explicit inference** |
| wmma main kernel scaled by this packet | ~741 ms | 1,482.958 x (1 - 0.504) |

`1,482.958` against `~741 + 661.21 = ~1,403 ms` is roughly **80 ms, ~5.4% of the route**.

**The repair figure is the weaker term.** It assumes the q5_1 route carries the q4_k risk
rate; that rate has never been observed for q5_1, and the artifact says so verbatim. The
risk rate is a property of the arithmetic and activations rather than of the main kernel, so
it may or may not transfer.

## Limits

- **No correctness gate was run.** No output was compared between any two owners. Nothing
  here says the wmma owner is numerically acceptable; it is by construction an approximate
  path that depends on the repair this packet does not exercise.
- **Weights are synthesized** at the real shapes and byte layout - representative access
  pattern and byte counts, not real values. Populations come from the harness's own count
  synthesizers, so there is **no real-router population**.
- `max_risks=0`: no risk queue writes, no repair, no packing, no engine work. Main kernel
  only; not a complete-owner, route or engine rate.
- One host, one geometry, one quant, one process.
- The `1,482.958` and `661.21` figures come from a different measurement context than this
  packet and are used only to size the repair gap. The ledger records three prefill-window
  denominators spanning 44% (22,698.6 / 16,319.9 / 15,742.0 ms), so the two are not treated
  as interchangeable and the resulting ~5.4% is an estimate, not a measurement.
- `single-512` repeats at 4.53% drift, the largest in the sweep. `single` is degenerate and
  included only to bracket parallelism behaviour.

## Validation

- Gate/up parity confirmed before removing the old harness: +21.23% (new) vs +21.24% (old)
  at balanced-1024, 3 reps.
- 12 cases per route, all arms in one process on identical buffers, primary arm repeated
  last as a drift control.
- Host identity matches the ED-1 artifacts' recorded host.
- Ledger table integrity checked after both edits: the six rows stay contiguous; `--numstat`
  is `2 1`.
- No default, dispatch or kernel changed. No pytest run applies; the change is a new
  diagnostic harness plus artifacts plus ledger and changelog updates.

## Next

- **The one experiment that matters: measure the wmma owner end-to-end including its repair,
  at real routing, with the q5_1 risk rate observed rather than assumed.** Only that can turn
  ED-4 into a promotion candidate. If the route-level effect stays near 5%, it is still worth
  having because it needs no arithmetic change to the exact owner - but it needs the
  production numerical gate before any promotion.
- The gate/up route's owners were measured in the previous unit and its production default
  wins 12 of 12, so that route is closed; this route is the one with an open candidate.
- Do not cite the -50.4% as a route or engine saving anywhere. It is a main-kernel figure
  with the winner's repair excluded.
