# The census hid the ledger's largest lever: MX-1 was classified closed while its own cell said the build was open

## Summary

The §11 open-candidate census exists to answer the campaign's question — "has every
candidate in this ledger been run through?" — and its conclusion was that the
campaign is not exhausted but that **"the rows that carry measured millisecond-scale
value are already closed"**.

**That conclusion is false, and the row that falsifies it is the largest lever in the
ledger.** `MX-1` (the competitor route: F16 routed experts with no repair) was
classified **closed**. Its own status cell says:

> **MX-1 is NOT promoted**: this is an operand-error budget and does not replace the
> production mean/p95/p99/max KL, top-1, determinism, isolation or task gate; **the
> candidate build and the production gate remain open**

and its final append closes with "**The pricing does not decide whether to build** - it
decides what the build must beat."

The census read the row's *first experiment* (the operand-admissibility budget,
complete across all 144 expert tensors) as the whole row. What is finished is the
budget; what remains is the build — and the build's price was already recorded in the
same cell: **the repair is 1,219.978 ms = 7.75% of the 15,741.963 ms prefill union**,
with a stated acceptance target (the F16 gate/up+down must come in at or below the
4,280.901 ms the iu8-risk owners currently take). `EG-3` names `MX-1` as the **sole**
remaining path to that cost.

**7.75% is larger than every other open lever**: `GD-4`'s ~5.5% ceiling, `DP-6`'s
1.090% scope, `DP-5`'s 0.832% cast, and `DP-8`'s measured 0.6%.

## Method — a contradiction sweep, not another positional reading

Every classification in that census is a judgement over a long prose cell, which is
the same kind of reading that had already failed four times in the section. So it was
re-checked mechanically in the one direction that matters: **every row the table
called closed was searched for an explicit statement, in its own cell, that something
of *its own candidate* remains open.**

Signals: "remains open", "remain open", "still open", "not promoted", "not yet built",
"the candidate build".

Thirty-two of the thirty-six closed rows contain no such statement. **Four do, and all
four were misclassified:**

| row | the sentence in its own cell | disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `MX-1` | "**the candidate build and the production gate remain open**"; "**The pricing does not decide whether to build**" | **actionable — the largest measured lever (7.75%)** |
| `DE-1` | "**Unit (2) — the exact-activation warp256 variant — REMAINS OPEN and is NOT licensed by this result.**" | actionable (unit 2 only; the dp4a promotion is landed) |
| `DE-5` | "**Still OPEN:** no mechanism inside `reset()` is isolated — `state.zero()`, the per-attention KV memsets, `index.reset()` and the `_ple_hash_states` clear are all candidates and none is individually excluded." | actionable |
| `MT-1` | "Remaining work is composing it into a verifier body pass and measuring the COMPLETE pass"; "**does NOT cover DE-1** (MoE expert GEMV pair, 11.3 ms/token)" | actionable |

**Two closed rows pass the sweep and stay closed, and they show why the sweep keys on
the row's *own* candidate rather than on the words.** `X-4` says "**Still OPEN**: the
stable wall and serving-wrapper attribution remain open **under X-10/DE-5**" — the
residual is explicitly delegated, both named owners are now in the actionable set, and
the row's own named next step is done. `X-6` says "**RT-3's blocker is separate and
still open**" — that blocker belongs to `RT-3`, whose cell states a terminal conclusion
(premise falsified). In both cases the sentence names *another row's* work.

**A crude second reading, recorded because it shows the instrument's limit.** Taking
the census's rule literally — "closed only when the cell states a terminal conclusion"
— and testing whether the *end* of each cell states one reports **30 of 36 closed rows
as violations**. That is not 30 misclassifications: most cells end with an evidence or
limits line ("Evidence: `benchmarks/results/...`", "One model, one quant, one host"),
not a status token. The rule is about the cell *containing* a terminal conclusion for
its own candidate, not about its final words. Recorded because it is the **fifth
positional reading in this census to look decisive and be wrong**.
