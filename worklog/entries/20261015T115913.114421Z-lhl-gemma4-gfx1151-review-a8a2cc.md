# gfx1151 regression review of the pulled gfx1100 Gemma 4 range

## Summary

Reviewed the pulled range `f4259f19d..875f51dfc` (11 commits, the W7900
verify-block/flash campaign) for gfx1151 impact on the Strix Halo host
(AMD RYZEN AI MAX+ 395, Radeon 8060S, therock ROCm 10 wrapper from
docs/ENVS.md). **No gfx1151 regressions found.** Nothing in the range touches
`kernels/hip_gfx1151/` itself; all gfx1151 reach is through the shared gfx11
kernel bodies (compiled native for gfx1151), the shared runtime, and dispatch.
Unit tier 248 passed; GPU battery on the gfx1151 device 173 passed, 4 skipped
(the whole `test_gpu_gemma4_expert_chunk8.py` module — it needs the real
backbone artifact, which is absent on this host).

Division of the range for gfx1151:

- **Flows automatically** (no port): lm-head 8-row chunking (`e8cc517a9`,
  runtime), small-row dense GEMV dispatch for 2-8-row T16-tile blocks
  (`eba4d1e55`, bit-identical), expert chunk8 siblings (`5823df24c`, production
  "auto" MoE route, changed arithmetic), draft-head attention via the
  flash_long launcher in every profile (`65605f17b`), verify-contract adapter
  fix (`82cdb84a3`, test-only), kernel/variant registrations (additive).
- **gfx1100-plan-keyed, does not reach gfx1151**: the `gemma4_flash_long`
  decode-attention selection (`df70f7793`) and the decode-scope behaviors it
  enables (multi-row verify-block flash from 256 keys, one-row verify taking
  decode variants, `step_like`). The gfx1151 plan (`gemma4_gguf_profiles.py`)
  declares only prefill-attention scopes; its decode steps keep the prefill
  request's own one-token routing, whose measured lead on Strix Halo is the
  staged singleton. Adopting the gfx1100 decode scope wholesale could regress
  gfx1151 decode, so this is a measure-first decision, not a mechanical port
  (the flash-long worklog itself leaves gfx1151 "unmeasured").
