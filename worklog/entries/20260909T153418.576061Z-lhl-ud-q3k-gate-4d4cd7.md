# Q3_K-over-W4A16 gate investigation: +14.3% prefill, blocked on the envelope

## Summary

Impact-list task 2, run on GPU1 (7900 XTX). The Q3_K-over-W4A16 route (UD
route-plan item B, blocked on the zbook at a 6.1% mean overage) was
re-investigated with a production-config teacher-forced probe, an
operand-precision analysis, and a null-controlled methodology.

**The upside is confirmed and large: prefill 477.1 -> 545.1 tok/s (+14.3%)
in production config.** The route stays unrouted: over three seeds the
per-context mean KL is 1.13e-3 / 1.15e-3 / 2.77e-4 (2/3 over the calibrated
1e-3) and seed 7 breaches the 5e-2 absolute max-row ceiling at 5.82e-2 -
consistent with and worse than the zbook record.

## Findings

- **Operand precision is ruled out for Q3_K at the weight level**: Q3_K's
  fp16 rounding error (1.30e-4 mean rel on a real tensor) is the *smallest*
  of all the quants - better than IQ4_XS (1.59e-4), which passes in the
  shipped four-quant arm.
- **No defect at the element level**: W4A16-vs-strict per-element mean rel
  error at 512 rows is 2.94e-3 for Q3_K vs 1.92e-3 for IQ4_XS (both at the
  bf16-output floor) - only 1.5x, while Q3_K's byte share is the largest
  remaining. The mean delta is share x noise volume.
- The zbook's int8-vs-fp16 equality plus the above points at accumulation
  structure as the noise source, but the hi/lo fp16 split addresses operand
  rounding only and no lever is identified that provably clears the binding
  ceiling. The unblock remains the recorded human decision: envelope
  re-calibration for UD scope (the 1e-3 was frozen on a 2026-08-16
  Qwen3.5 calibration; hipEngine strict itself sits at 0.001271 vs the
  llama.cpp teacher) or a task gate.

## Methodology notes

- The first probe round ran with use_wmma_prefill=None (not production
  config); all recorded numbers are production config. A null control
  (same policy in both arms) measures exactly 0.
- The local32 decode probe had a candidate-capture bug after the policy
  was emptied (the candidate arm became a no-op, KL exactly 0); fixed with
  an explicit candidate policy. Its production-config numbers: means
  8.0e-4 / 9.0e-4 / 1.6e-4 (all pass 1e-3) but seed 23 fails top-1 at
  98.44% - mixed failure modes, unrouted stays correct.
