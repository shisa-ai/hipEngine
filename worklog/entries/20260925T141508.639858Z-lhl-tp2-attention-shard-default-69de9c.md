# head-sharded attention becomes the TP2 default

## Summary

Head-sharded attention is now what a `mode="tp2"` session runs by default on the
graphed schedule with the device-side reduction; `attention_shard=False` selects
the replicated route. It is promoted on the production numerical envelope
measured on **both** prefill routes (756 rows per arm, no breaching prompt,
bit-identical determinism across two processes) plus a same-session
interleaved c=1 A/B that separates the two routes: **prefill +7.55% / +5.71%,
decode +10.77% / +11.00%, and 3.327 GiB less session VRAM per rank (-21.8%)**.
The one part of the route that is *not* promoted with it is the profile's own
status, which stays `diagnostic_only`: task quality and BF16-relative are
unqualified for the TP2 route as a whole, so this records the envelope and the
route choice, not a full production qualification.

## Changes

- `hipengine/distributed/tp2_generate.py`: `attention_shard` resolves from
  `(mode == "tp2" and schedule == "graphed" and reduce_mode == "device")`. The
  reduce-mode term is not cosmetic: head sharding needs the device reduction to
  sum each rank's attention-output partial, and `reduce_mode="host"` is the
  documented transport opt-out, so tying the default to `"device"` keeps that
  opt-out working instead of turning it into a construction error. An explicit
  `attention_shard=True` with the host reduction still raises.
- `scripts/tp2_c1_bench.py`: `--attention-shard` threaded through the cell, plus
  the `tp2-serial-shard` arm so the token-serial prefill route (a different
  reduce call site, `_enqueue_layer` rather than the bulk path) has a control.
- `scripts/tp2_prefill_schedule_failure_probe.py`,
  `scripts/tp2_resident_control.py`: route flags for the envelope arms.
- `tests/test_unit_distributed_tp2_generate.py`: the fixture's layer map is a
  dataclass because the substitution rewrites it with `dataclasses.replace`;
  `FakeShardGroup.reduce_device_payload` records the bulk prefill's attention
  reduction; `_graphed_session` pins the route explicitly so the schedule tests
  keep describing the route they assert; new
  `test_graphed_attention_reduce_takes_a_second_slot_per_layer` asserts the
  default's slot layout (attention reductions take the second half of the slot
  space, the unsharded slots are not renumbered).

## Validation

- `tests/test_unit_distributed_tp2_generate.py`: 79 passed. Wider bundle
  (`-k "distributed or shard"`, 200+ nodes): 0 failures.
- Envelope, 18 product prompts x 42 steps x 2 ranks = 756 rows per arm, against a
  same-revision TP1 teacher (3 bit-identical sweeps, empty identity diff):

  | arm | prefill | mean KL | p95 KL | p99 KL | max KL | top-1 | flipped |
  | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
  | tp2-bulk | bulk | 8.370e-05 | 3.955e-04 | 7.547e-04 | 3.691e-03 | 99.47% | 4 |
  | tp2-bulk-shard | bulk | 1.034e-04 | 5.083e-04 | 9.799e-04 | 8.461e-03 | 99.60% | 3 |
  | tp2-serial | token-serial | 9.442e-05 | 4.411e-04 | 1.029e-03 | 4.955e-03 | 99.74% | 2 |
  | tp2-serial-shard | token-serial | 1.106e-04 | 4.831e-04 | 9.776e-04 | 1.357e-02 | 99.74% | 2 |

  Bars: mean 1e-03, p95 5e-03, p99 2e-02, max 5e-02, top-1 0.99 global / 0.97
  per category. Worst margin 3.7x, worst category 0.9940, no prompt breaches.
  The sharded arms flip no additional rows while carrying a higher KL tail
  (max 8.461e-03 and 1.357e-02 against 3.691e-03 and 4.955e-03) - recorded, not
  explained away; it is the shape of boundary-rounding noise at near-ties.
- Determinism: two independent processes, bit-identical KL curves (42 values per
  prompt) and bit-identical last-row sha256 on 18/18 prompts for both bulk arms.
- c=1 cell (512-token prompt, 128 decode tokens, 3 resident repeats, interleaved
  twice): prefill 982.16/989.80 -> 1056.27/1046.27 tok/s, decode 37.51/37.53 ->
  41.55/41.66 tok/s, session VRAM 15.279 -> 11.952 GiB per rank.
- Uneven 0.44/0.56 split + shard: 1023.65 tok/s prefill, 43.44 tok/s decode,
  10.829 / 12.391 GiB per rank.
- `scripts/sync_benchmark_readme.py --check`: synchronized.
