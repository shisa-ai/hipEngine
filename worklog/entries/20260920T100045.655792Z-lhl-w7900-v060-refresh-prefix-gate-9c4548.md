Both GGUF models pass the C1 graph/eager gate on all 18 category/heldout
prompts with exact generated IDs, final logits, and full state. Laguna S 2.1
is not present locally and was not measured.

**Prefix gate — gfx1100 does NOT reproduce the gfx1151 result.** Of the eight
category/heldout scopes, only the two `completed`-lifecycle `general_ja`
scopes pass all eight binding terms. All six `active`-lifecycle scopes fail the
binding `scheduler_state_exact` term with 120 final-state component mismatches
(15 `kv.key` + 15 `kv.value` + 45 `linear.conv` + 45 `linear.recurrent`); the
prefix boundary state is exact in every scope. `heldout-code` additionally
exceeds the `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` §6.1 mean-KL (`2.264e-3` vs `1e-3`)
and max-KL (`0.2753` vs `0.05`) ceilings. Numerical top-1 is 1.000 in four
scopes, 0.9844 in three, overall `0.99414`.

This is refreshed evidence, not a causal optimization claim. It is a **BF16-KV
qualification only**: the gate publishes no KV policy and the direct `LLM`
path auto-resolves BF16 (`hipengine/llm.py:1246`). It does **not** validate the
server-default `int8_per_token_head` KV path, whose scale-plane clone/share and
admission behavior remains a separate, un-gated contract.
