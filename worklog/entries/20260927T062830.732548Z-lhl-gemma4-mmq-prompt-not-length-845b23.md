# MMQ gate/up divergence tracks the prompt, not the length

## Summary

The MMQ expert gate/up route's short-prefill divergence is a property of the
prompt's routing, not of the prompt length, and the probe that reports it cannot
say which of its two arms is wrong. Running the full length sweep under both of
the probe's corpora shows the same length being exact under one corpus and
flipping the greedy decision under the other: 96 ids is 1.66e-06 under the
frozen corpus and 1.614 with a flip under the probe corpus, and 384 ids is
2.56e-06 versus 3.142 with a flip. The record's claim of greedy decision flips
at 7 of 13 prefill lengths conflates the count of lengths over the KL bar with
the count that actually change the decision; under the frozen corpus 7 lengths
are over the bar but only 2 (24 and 64) flip.

This also removes the last untested structural condition in the route's leaf.
Eight candidate mechanisms are now eliminated, so the open question is no longer
the kernel's arithmetic but which arm of the comparison is correct.

## Changes

- `tests/test_gpu_gemma4_expert_route_true_geometry.py`: added a power-law
  skewed count pattern at the real 512-lane short-prefill width. The previous
  pattern peaked at exactly 32 rows, i.e. exactly one 32-row MMQ tile, so no
  expert ever crossed a tile boundary and the multi-tile map path was never
  exercised. The new case asserts the head exceeds 32 rows so it cannot pass
  vacuously. All eight combinations now pass: fragmented and skewed counts, a
  gaussian and an outlier activation profile, and scratch capacity factors 1
  and 8.
- `scripts/gemma4_mmq_prefill_length_probe.py`: records and prints both arms'
  greedy argmax. A two-arm comparison can report disagreement but not
  attribution, and this makes the divergence attributable against an
  independent reference instead of being assigned to whichever arm runs second.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-27-gemma4-mmq-gate-up-short-prefill-divergence.json`:
  records the eight eliminated mechanisms, the two-corpus sweep, and the
  corrected flip count.

## Validation

  tests/test_gpu_gemma4_expert_route_true_geometry.py -q` -> 8 passed.
- Full probe sweep under both corpora, `--repeats 2`:
  - frozen: 13 lengths, 7 over the 0.05 bar, 2 decision flips (24, 64).
  - probe: 13 lengths, 9 over the bar, 6 decision flips (16, 24, 32, 96, 192,
    384).
  - 1024 ids, the length the recorded `kl_max` 0.0651 was taken at, is 7.3e-07
    under the frozen corpus and 0.035 under the probe corpus, so that figure is
    corpus-specific rather than representative.
- Every length is deterministic across repeats in both corpora, with the route
  confirmed running (58 or 116 launches depending on length).
- `python3 scripts/docs/check_all.py` -> all 4 gates passed; `python3
  audit/audit.py check` -> ok.
