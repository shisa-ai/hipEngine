# Target Qwen4Exp Q8_0 dense prefill MMQ

## Summary

Profiled the current strict pp508 (chunk 512) prefill into kernel families
and microbenched the in-tree raw-Q8 MMQ128 against the resident Q8_0 dense
owner. The Q8_0 dense family - not the MoE experts - is the single largest
prefill bucket, and the existing MMQ128 body is 6.4-7.3x faster than the

plus a policy registration under the production gate.

require-cached, load copies excluded; 8.330 s prefill kernels vs llama.cpp HIP
1.798 s):

- Q5_1 selected grouped down: 1.812 s / 43 (llama q5_1 MMQ 0.382 s)
- Q4_K selected dual gate/up: 1.796 s / 47 (llama q4_k MMQ 0.554 s)
- GDN prefill: 1.008 s / 36 (llama GDN 0.085 s - separate algorithmic target)
- dense F32 GEMV: 0.232 s / 264; attention 0.155 s; glue small.

Microbench (best-of-20, device-synchronized, D4 quantize included in MMQ path):

- ssm_out 512x6144x2560: coltile best 9.34 ms vs MMQ 1.27 ms (7.3x, 12.7 TFLOPS)
- attn_q 512x2560x12288: 20.33 vs 3.17 ms (6.4x)
- attn_qkv 512x2560x10240: 16.35 vs 2.27 ms (7.2x)

Projected: Q8_0 family 2.46 -> ~0.35 s removes ~2.1 s of the 8.33 s pp508
kernel budget (~25% prefill wall at p508). The 2026-07-15 gfx1100 MMQ
rejection is superseded here: it compared against a Q8T16 FP16-WMMA owner on
W7900/Qwen3.6; that owner is not resident in this configuration.
