# Scope register-state GDN prefill to its runner

## Summary

R0415's control migration is complete. Production publishes
`gdn_register_prefill=True`, strict publishes False, and the retired process
selector is ignored. The Hk16/Hv48/D128, rows>=2 and registry guards, kernel
arithmetic, serial strict fallback and decode routes are unchanged. This is
not a performance claim or arithmetic promotion.

## Changes

- Publish the choice on the generator and runner and inherit it in late clones.
  Forward it through prefill's complete GDN layer into the token mixer. The
  decode call uses rows=1 and does not enter this prefill selection; batch and
  mixed target modules do not call this scalar layer/mixer selection.
- Add exception-safe `gdn_register_prefill(runner, enabled)` diagnostic scope.
  Campaign and state-gate arms now name the runner, record its choice and
  restore it. Close tiled/peer preemptors and unset the wave-norm variant during
  this serial parent/register comparison so the named kernel actually runs.
- Preserve the layer2 evaluator's explicit register request over its strict
  base through `CandidateSpec.gdn_register_prefill`. Journey-localize's named
  strict and GDN-isolated arms, including split prefill/decode trajectories,
  carry the corresponding runner choice rather than deriving it from a
  removed environment key.
- Retire the ENVS entry and map all original requirements in the sweep manifest.
  Move the remaining decode optimization follow-up to the bottom of REFACTOR
  with a profiling and full comparison clearing action. Preserve the lead's
  prefill-first decision, accepted resource review, adverse historical decode
  result and rejected barrier schedules. No decode optimization is claimed.
