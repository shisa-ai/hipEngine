# Revert the 16-slice split: it is faster but worsens a binding gate metric, and the teacher-forced gate never tested the split

## Summary

Reverts the 16-slice decode-split policy landed earlier in this iteration
(commit 74a6f13a7). It is genuinely faster - 45.8427 -> 48.2630 tok/s at the
metric row and 42.7662 -> 45.9349 at 4096p - and it passes the production gate
against its own incumbent. It is reverted anyway because it makes a binding
correctness metric worse: the teacher-forced rows over the production `kl_max`
bar go from 1 to 2 and the worst value from 0.0556 to 0.1508. Fixing the
evaluator is what exposed this, and that fix is kept.

## Changes

Kept: `scripts/gemma4_teacher_forced_gate.py` and
`tests/test_unit_gemma4_teacher_forced_gate.py`. `capture_chain` fed the prompt
one token at a time from an empty cache, so key counts ran 1, 2, 3, ... 1023 -
one token below the decode split's 1024-key entry threshold - and
`decode_slices` returned 1 for every row. The gate was comparing the
single-kernel path with itself and reporting `kl_max` of exactly 0.0.
Instrumented directly: 30 calls per forward (one per layer), keys `[1]` at
position 0 through `[5]` at position 4, `slices=1` at every one.

The evaluator now takes `--prefill` (ids pushed through the cache in one forward
before scoring) and `--slices` (pin the split for one arm; 1 selects the strict
path). A baseline records both, and `gate` refuses to compare arms whose scored
key range differs. The override is reversible within one process - the
RED-first test for it caught that the first version was not, since
`force_slices(None)` returned early and left the patch in place.

Reverted: `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/gemma4/gemma4_attention.py`,
`tests/test_unit_gemma4_attention_routing.py` and
`tests/test_gpu_gemma4_attention_decode_parity.py`, restored to the pre-change
policy (4 slices for a 256-wide head; the doubling rule capped at 4 for a
512-wide head).

## Validation

Three arms against one frozen strict baseline, prompt 2048 with a 1024-id
prefill so every scored row runs at key counts 1025-2047 and the split is
engaged on all of them; 1023 paired rows each:

| arm | kl_max | bar | other limits | top-1 | verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| candidate 16 vs incumbent 4 | 0.005212 | 0.05 | all pass | 1.0, 0 flips | PASS |
| incumbent 4 vs strict | **0.055589** | 0.05 | all pass | 1.0, 0 flips | **FAIL** |
| candidate 16 vs strict | **0.150817** | 0.05 | all pass | 1.0, 0 flips | **FAIL** |

`kl_mean` 9.2e-05 and 2.1e-04 against 0.001; `kl_p95` 4.0e-06 and 3.5e-06
against 0.005; `kl_p99` 4.1e-05 and 3.8e-05 against 0.02. Rows over the bar: 1 of
1023 at 4 slices (row 864), 2 at 16 (rows 602, 864). On every outlier the teacher
is near-deterministic and the decision never moves - row 864: top-1 6605 -> 6605,
teacher p_top1 0.995465 against the split's 0.999999, max|delta logit| 18.2; row
602: top-1 6605 -> 6605, teacher p_top1 0.988141 against 0.999995, max|delta
logit| 20.0 at 16 slices - so the divergence is in the tail, and KL from a
0.995-peaked distribution to a slightly sharper one is dominated by the residual
spread over the remaining 262143 tokens.

- `env -u HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES ROCR_VISIBLE_DEVICES=1 .venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_unit_gemma4_*.py tests/test_gpu_gemma4_*.py -q` -> 410 passed, exit 0
- `env -u HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_unit_gemma4_teacher_forced_gate.py -q` -> 14 passed
- `python3 scripts/docs/check_all.py` -> all gates pass except the pre-existing, unrelated `published command drift` failure
- `python3 scripts/worklog.py check` -> clean

Kernel-level parity does not bound this failure: the parity suite passes at 16
slices (f32 within 1e-4, bf16 within one ulp) and 128 greedy positions give
identical token ids at 4 and 16 slices. The outlier is a tail effect at a
near-tie, which is the class of divergence a parity test on typical shapes will
not see - which is exactly why the production gate exists and why its coverage
defect mattered.

## Next

- **Campaign-level blocker, escalated rather than decided here:** the two-phase
  decode split, at both 4 and 16 slices, so as shipped since 2026-09-26, fails
  the production `kl_max` bar against the strict path on 1-2 rows of 1023 with
  top-1 unchanged. Either the split's tail behaviour on near-tie rows is fixed,
  or the applicability of the max-KL bar to near-deterministic rows is
  re-derived with the lead, or the strict path returns as the default. The
  slice table is worth +5.3% at the metric row and +7.4% at 4096p whenever that
  is resolved, and the earlier entry's acceptance is corrected by this one.
- The `max|delta logit|` of 18-20 on the outlier rows is far above f32
  association error and is the thing to understand first.
