## What was decided

DE-2's status cell offered two routes to the cost split inside its measured
remainder: *"a scoped marker **or a name-to-kernel join** that this capture
cannot supply."* **The join route is struck. It is structurally impossible, not
merely unavailable.**

Re-running the X-4 attribution's own selection
(`_read_marker_windows(..., "qwen4exp_decode_live4097_rep")` +
`_filter_kernels_by_windows` over
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-29-qwen4exp-x4-decode-split/rocprof/x4full_kernel_trace.csv`)
reproduces the published `total_kernel_ms_per_step` of 54.2181 ms/step exactly
(3 windows, 5,313 selected kernels), and shows:

- **5,313 of 5,313 selected rows (100.00%) carry a `qwen4exp_role:` name.**
- **0 rows retain a real kernel name.** Distinct real kernel names in the decode
  subset: 0.

`_annotate_kernel_roles` rewrites `kernel` to the role for every in-window row.
The decode subset therefore has zero kernel-name identity to join on. A scoped
marker is the only route to a sub-family split, and DE-2 should stop naming the
join as an alternative.

DE-2's price is unchanged: the bracket [1.36, 2.544] ms/step and the <=4.69%
ceiling stand, and DE-2 still ranks ~4x below DE-1.

## Reading hazard found while checking

`x4full_kernel_trace.csv` (and its byte-identical `x4decode_` twin) is the
**whole capture - 18,941 rows, 15,309 ms - and it contains the prefill** as well
as the 3 decode windows (5,313 rows, 163 ms). A per-kernel census over the raw
CSV silently measures the prefill.

The failure is quiet because the contaminated number lands near the prefill's
own and reads as corroboration:
`qwen35_router_logits_f32_token_tile_dense_exact_kernel<4>` totals **616.5 ms
whole-trace and 0.0 ms decode-only** (0.0 because the in-window rows are
renamed). An early pass in this session read ~617 ms for that kernel from the
raw CSV and briefly treated the whole-trace census as a decode measurement.

Recorded as ledger §8 rule 11, alongside rule 10 (read a status cell to its end
before working the row - DE-2's cell is 9.5 KB and its conclusions are appended
last, which is what produced the stale premise this iteration started from).
