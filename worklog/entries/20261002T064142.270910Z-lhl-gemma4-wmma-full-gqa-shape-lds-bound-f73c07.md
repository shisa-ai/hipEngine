# the GQA-aware full-layer decode shape is shared-memory bound, and the Q tile is what binds it

## Summary

Task #27 asks for a tiled GQA-aware decode attention for the full geometry. The
tiled part landed as `58810c616`: dividing the key walk is worth 3.07x on the
kernel at 262,144 keys and 1.51x end to end. The GQA-aware part does not fit,
and this records why so the next attempt starts from the arithmetic instead of
rediscovering it.

The change it needs is `kGqaHeads` 4 to halve the KV re-reads, or 8 to remove
them. `kColumns` is `kQueryRows * kGqaHeads` and the Q tile is staged in shared
memory at `kQStride`, so both scale with it, and LDS is
`(kColumns * kQStride + kKBatch * kKvStride) * 2` bytes against a 65,536-byte
limit:

| `kGqaHeads` | `kColumns` | LDS | against 64 KB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 (shipped) | 32 | 48.75 KB | fits |
| 4 | 64 | 81.25 KB | 1.3x over |
| 8 | 128 | 146.25 KB | 2.3x over |

## Validation

- The LDS expression is the launcher's own
  (`hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/gemma4/gemma4_attention_prefill_wmma_full.hip`,
  the `shared_bytes` computation) and the constants are the kernel's, so the
  three rows are arithmetic on the shipped code rather than a model of it. The
  `kGqaHeads` 2 row is confirmed by the kernel running at 262,144 keys.
- No measurement is claimed for `kGqaHeads` 4 or 8. They cannot be launched, so
  there is nothing to measure.

## Next

- **The Q tile is what binds it, not the KV tile.** At `kGqaHeads` 4 the Q tile
  alone is `64 * 520 * 2` = 66.6 KB, the entire budget before any K or V is
  staged. Shrinking `kKBatch` from 16 to 8 and dropping the Q padding still
  leaves 74.9 KB, so this is not a tuning margin that a different tile shape
  recovers.
- Clearing it means tiling head_dim so Q fits, which puts the dimension loop
  outside the key loop and re-reads KV once per dimension tile — reintroducing
  exactly the 4x the shape was meant to remove. Holding Q in registers instead
  is 128 VGPRs of Q on top of 128 of accumulator, at the ISA maximum of 256.
  Neither is free and neither was built.
- **The cheaper lever is L2, not LDS.** The 4x is shareable rather than
  unavoidable: the 4 sibling gqa_tiles of a kv_head walk the same key range and
  are adjacent in `blockIdx.z`, but `blockIdx.x` varies fastest, so the
  concurrent window — about 20 blocks against 128 — sits inside one gqa_tile for
  its whole duration and the band is streamed four times in sequence. Making the
  gqa_tile the fastest-varying axis would put the siblings in flight together
  and let L2 serve three of the four reads.
- That reorder was **not attempted**, and the reason is scope rather than
  difficulty: it moves the grid layout for the prefill as well, and the prefill
  is the topline, so it needs a prefill measurement and not only a decode one.
  It is the right next experiment for #27 and it is bounded.

## Open question

- Whether the L2 argument is real is unmeasured. The inference is from the grid
  order and the concurrent-block count, not from a counter: a
  `rocprofv3 --memory` trace of the split walk would say directly whether the
  537 MB per layer is DRAM traffic or L2 traffic, and that is the measurement
  that would decide whether reordering is worth a prefill regression risk. It
  should be taken before the reorder is written.
