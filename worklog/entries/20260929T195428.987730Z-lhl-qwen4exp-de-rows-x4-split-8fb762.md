# Seven DE rows updated with X-4's decode split, and DE-2's shared expert has no role at all

## Summary

Seven `DE-*` rows were parked "after `X-4`". That dependency is now satisfied, so each gets
the device split it was waiting for rather than a pointer.

| row | was | now (ms/step, share of 54.218) |
|---|---|---|
| DE-1 routed experts | 11.3 ms/token | **`moe:expert_gate` 24.550, 45.28%** |
| DE-3 dense/linear | 24.73 ms/token | **23.166, 42.73%** |
| DE-7 attention/GDN | "X-4 must include them" | **5.022, 9.26%** (+ `decode_boundary` 0.508, 0.94%) |
| DE-8 head | 3.25 ms/token | **`linear:root.lm_head` 2.980, 5.50%** |
| DE-5/DE-6 runtime | unmeasured | **~65% host/launch** (~99 ms/step non-kernel) |

## The substantive findings

**DE-1's cost has more than doubled against its anchor** (11.3 ms/token -> 24.550 ms/step,
45.28%), and it is a per-expert GEMM family spread over 48 layers rather than one large
kernel: the largest single role is 1.33% and the rest cluster at 0.5-0.6, so **no layer
dominates** and only a whole-family lever can matter.

**DE-3's hyper-connection members are 6.911 ms/step = 12.75%**, a share the row's action does
not mention - `hc_ffn_down` 1.771, `hc_attn_down` 1.768, `hc_attn_up` 0.998, `hc_ffn_up`
0.995.

**DE-8 is the second largest single role in the whole split** - one output projection per
step, behind only the 48-layer expert-gate family.

**DE-2's shared expert has NO role in the decode capture at all**: 740 roles, 30 families,
zero `shared_*` entries. **This is a marking gap, not evidence the block is free** - prefill
marks `linear:layers.*.shared_gate` / `shared_up` / `shared_down` separately (117.4 / 84.9 /
116.2 ms there). The decode path either fuses the shared expert into `moe:expert_gate` or runs
it under a role the harness does not push. **DE-2 needs its own decode marker before it can be
ranked, and it cannot be priced from this capture.**

**DE-5/DE-6**: decode is **not kernel-bound** - the unprofiled wall is median 153.0 ms against
54.2 ms of kernels, so ~65% is host/launch, roughly **99 ms/step of non-kernel time**.
