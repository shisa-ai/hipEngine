Captured the required selector-unset RX 7900 XTX M512 prefill trace after the
Q5 recurrent-output promotion. The Q5 arithmetic window is halved and the
fresh residual selects a new exact same-input Q4T16 operation boundary rather
than reopening rejected singleton source-F16, GDN, attention, or launch-count
