The controller blend at the loop re-entry matches the host expression to 6.2e-06, and zeroing the resident arena before the traversal leaves the end-to-end error unchanged at 6.065. The blend and arena history are both cleared, and the branch dumps that produced the 3.379 reading are shown to be an unreliable reference.

- The blend is exact, so the loop re-entry's attention inputs, query, messages, restored hidden state and controller rows are all verified and the remaining error is not in any of them.
