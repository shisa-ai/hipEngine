# Gemma 4 D5: fuse the rows=1 router into one launch

## Summary

D5 part 1: the rows=1 router runs as **one launch**. The chain
`gemma4_router_prescale_bf16` → `qwen35_router_logits_bf16_f32w` →
`qwen35_router_select` → `gemma4_expert_weight_scale_f32` (four launches and
1.0094 ms/token across 30 layers on the decode census) is replaced at
`tokens == 1` by `gemma4_router_topk_fused_bf16`: every block recomputes the
prescaled row into shared memory with the chain's exact bf16 rounding, dot
products its own expert row, and the last block to arrive runs the identical
select + per-expert-scale math over the global logits (the cooperative-output
pattern from `moe/router.hip`). Selection is by shape, the same dispatch rule
as the token-tile logits variant; the four-launch chain stays what runs for
`tokens > 1` and is the strict unfused route under the composite (its three
primitives — `router_prescale`, `router_logits`, `router_select` — are
registered keys).

**This is new information against a rejected direction, not a retry of it.**
The P8 prescale-fold entry (2026-09-28) rejected fusing prescale into the
logits at **rows=512 prefill**, where the two-kernel pair runs at full GEMM
efficiency and the fused kernel cost 34% more (0.1223 vs 0.0913 ms). The
decode case is the other regime: four sequential launches of 6.3 / 16.6 /
8.3 / 2.4 µs where dispatch dominates, so the question is whether one launch
beats 33.6 µs per layer, and only the census can answer it. The prior
entry's mandated sentinel coverage check is in the new oracle test: the
logits and weight buffers are sentinel-filled before the launch and asserted
fully overwritten after, so a partial-write kernel cannot pass on agreement
alone.

Teacher-forced gate (W7900, baseline `gate_base_mmq.npz`, `--prompt 2048
--prefill 1024`): **passed**, `kl_max` 0.000814 against the 0.05 bar,
`kl_mean` 6.31e-06 against 1e-3, top-1 1.0, 0 flips, 0 rows over limit —
eighteen times more margin than the t16 landing's 0.01522 at the same bars.
Artifact `benchmarks/results/2026-09-30-gemma4-d5-fused-router-gate-w7900.json`.

Census (`--gpu 1 --tag d5r` against the same-lane `t16` tag from the same
day): the four-stage router costs **1.0095 → 0.6944 ms/token (−31%) at
90 → 30 launches per token** — `moe.route_glue` 1.1619 → 0.8814 (select and
expert_scale no longer run as kernels) and `moe.router_gemm` 0.6886 →
0.6944 across half the launches. The real costs are recorded too:
`runtime_copy_fill` **+0.087 ms/token** for the 30 arrival-counter memsets
(67 → 97 launches) and `dense.q8_0` +0.114 at unchanged launch counts,
which appears at both prompts and is run-to-run noise rather than a
consequence of this change. Decode device busy moves 15.7719 → 15.7067
ms/token and the census wall span 19.69 → 19.39 (**−0.30/token**, the
dispatch the fusion removes); topline decode **57.03 → 57.45 tok/s at
1024/128 (+0.7%) and 50.92 → 51.35 at 4096/128 (+0.8%)** with sample
ranges separated, prefill flat at both prompts as the `tokens == 1` branch
requires. Artifact `benchmarks/results/2026-09-30-gemma4-scoreboard-d5r.json`.
