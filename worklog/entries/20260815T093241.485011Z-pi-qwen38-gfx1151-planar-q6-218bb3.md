# Qualify Qwen3.8 gfx1151 role-qualified planar Q6

## Summary

All-planar Q6 is mathematically exact and cuts prefill wall, memory, and the
profiled AR kernel sum, but is not retainable: complete 512/1K/4K host-wall AR
falls 0.14-0.19%, while the full natural suite falls 0.15% true AR, 1.70% B1,
and 0.48% B3 (B2 rises 0.44%). A counterbalanced actual-weight leaf screen
localizes the blocker to K5120/N10240 `attn_qkv` c1: planar is 8.72% slower
with 0/11 paired wins. The repaired gfx1151 policy keeps exactly one standard
Q6T16 resident for those 24 tensors while planar remains the sole payload for
32 down, 8 narrow-V, and the untied root. This preserves the 49,479,680-byte
memory reduction and exact outputs without a shadow copy.

## Changes

- Admit the existing exact planar-qmicro Q6 decode, verifier, residual, prefill,
  and top-1 leaves on gfx1151; the changed-math F16 and Q8 activation routes
  remain excluded.
- Add backend capability
  `GGUF_DENSE_Q6_T16_QMICRO_PLANAR_EXCLUDED_SLOTS=("attn_qkv",)` and propagate
  the role exclusion through the generic GGUF materialization planner. No
  backend branch was added to model, engine, or dispatch code.
- Add RED/GREEN policy and actual Qwen3.8 plan/materialization tests, plus
  `scripts/qwen38_q6_planar_actual_operation_gate.py` for actual wide, narrow,
  root, prefill, residual, top-1, CPU-quality, ownership, and teardown checks.
