# Surya vision compute-time admission

## Summary

`check_vision_capacity` bounded a vision grid by bytes only, so the checkpoint's
own `max_pixels` ceiling (256x256, 65536 patches) was admitted at 502 MB of score
tile while one forward there is 1.70e14 FLOPs — 93% of it bidirectional
attention, 161.4 s estimated and about 2.7 minutes measured on gfx1151. Nothing
downstream could serve that page, and the rejection only happened later, if at
all. There is now a second declared budget: a per-request vision wall clock,
estimated from the plan the runner will execute.

The estimate is a plan model, not a patch-count table, because the byte budget
and the time budget are not independent. It has three measured terms — the
linear GEMMs, the quadratic attention, and the per-tile key/value re-read — so it
follows `max_vision_scratch_bytes`: the A4 page estimates at 43.4 s under the
default 512 MiB budget and 195.7 s under an 8 MiB one. That is the property that
lets admission reason about both budgets at once.

## Changes

- `hipengine/runtime/surya.py`: `vision_flops(spec, n)` (pure geometry, returns
  `(linear, attention)`) and `vision_forward_seconds(spec, n, tiles)` (three
  measured rate constants, calibrated against the retained tiling sweeps).
  `DEFAULT_MAX_VISION_SECONDS = 120.0`. `SuryaGpuRunner` takes
  `max_vision_seconds` (default 120 s, `None` = unbounded) and gains
  `vision_time_seconds(grid)`, `vision_patch_ceiling(seconds=None)` (bisection on
  the monotone estimate, re-planning the tile at each candidate), and a third
  check in `check_vision_capacity` that names the estimate, the attention TFLOPs,
  and the largest patch count the budget would admit. The byte-budget check is
  unchanged and still runs first; the free-memory check still runs last.
- `hipengine/generation/surya_gpu.py`: `max_vision_seconds` constructor kwarg and
  advertised attribute; `_generate_ocr` rejects a page whose estimated vision
  forward cannot finish inside the request's remaining `deadline_at`, raising
  `GenerationDeadlineExceeded` before any device work. Without it the deadline
  was only observed at the next stage boundary, i.e. after the client's timeout.
- `hipengine/generation/deadline.py`: `generation_deadline_remaining(deadline_at)`
  (companion to `generation_deadline_expired`), exported from
  `hipengine.generation`.
- `hipengine/llm.py`: `LLM(vision_max_seconds=...)`, forwarded by
  `_factory_capacity_kwargs` only to factories that declare it, so the budget is
  reachable through the public entry point like the two score-tile budgets.
  `math.inf` is how a caller disables it (`None` at the `LLM` layer means "use
  the generator default", matching the existing budget semantics). The
  registered `make_surya_generator_gpu` factory declares the parameter by name,
  which is what makes the forwarding reach the runner rather than be dropped.
- `docs/MODEL-SURYA.md`: the three-check admission and the time budget, plus
  `LLM(vision_max_seconds=...)` in the configurable-budget list.
- `docs/REFACTOR.md`: two open edges of this boundary — the CPU-reference
  generator has no vision admission at all, and an over-budget page surfaces as
  the runner's `SuryaGpuRuntimeError` rather than a request-level error.

## Validation

Calibration, from the retained artifacts (no new measurement; the constants are
fitted to the existing sweeps, 165 rows across seven artifacts, and every number
below is a row of one of them):

| patches | tiles | measured | estimate | ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 256 | 2 | 28.07 ms | 26.21 ms | 0.934 |
| 1024 | 8 | 136.00 ms | 134.07 ms | 0.986 |
| 4096 | 32 | 881.59 ms | 1004.23 ms | 1.139 |
| 6400 | 34 | 2131.13 ms | 2050.19 ms | 0.962 |
| 34320 | 108 | 43318.30 ms | 43378.91 ms | 1.001 |
| 34320 | 358 | 49602.21 ms | 49015.97 ms | 0.988 |

Those are the shapes the planner picks: -6%/+14%. Across the 33 A4 query-block
rows the per-tile term is fitted to, the band is -11%/+3%, worst case the raw
325-row budget, which is not a multiple of 32 and therefore a shape wavefront
rounding keeps the planner from choosing. Measured shapes the planner never
picks fall outside the band in the same direction and by under a second: the
unbudgeted dense tile is 194.01 ms against 129.36 ms at 1024 patches, 1039.95
against 920.80 ms at 4096, and a 512-row tile at 4096 patches is 1667.07 against
939.64 ms. Admission only sees planner-chosen shapes. The estimate is an
admission gate, so the contract asserted in the tests is the stated band rather
than exactness.

Budget behaviour on the retained grids: A4 (34320 patches, 108 tiles) 43.38 s
and the 65536-patch ceiling 161.42 s (410 tiles, 158.3 TFLOP of attention), so
the 120 s default admits the largest page the benchmark suite measures with 2.8x
headroom and rejects the ceiling; `vision_patch_ceiling()` = 56856 patches,
which matches a brute-force scan of the estimate at every budget from 1 s to
200 s. Every fixture page in `tests/fixtures/surya` re-preprocesses to a grid
whose estimate is 0.03-43.38 s, so no existing test, transcription gate, or
reported row is affected by the default budget.

Gates:

- `tests/test_surya_generation_contract.py` 49 passed (42 pre-existing plus 7
  new: the calibration band, the plan sensitivity and monotonicity, the
  ceiling-grid rejection, the raise/disable paths, the ceiling inversion, and
  the `LLM` forwarding rule). These build the runner through `__new__` with a
  stubbed `mem_get_info`, so the admission contract is CPU-testable with no
  checkpoint and no device.
- `tests/test_surya_gpu.py` full file: 42 passed, 0 failed
  (`HIPENGINE_HIP_ARCH=gfx1151`, Radeon 8060S). Includes the rewritten
  `test_gpu_vision_admission_bounds_the_page_by_memory_and_time` (the ceiling
  passes the byte budget and is rejected on time; a raised or disabled budget
  admits it on the same device) and two new generator tests that assert vision
  never ran: an over-time page, and a page whose estimate exceeds the remaining
  deadline (phase 2 pins the estimate to isolate the new check from the
  pre-existing expiry checks, which raise the same error). The factory-forwarding
  test was extended for the new parameter and re-run with the final source (13
  focused nodes, 13 passed) after the full-file run, which had collected the
  pre-extension test file.
- `tests/test_qwen4exp_context_capacity.py`, `tests/test_qwen4exp_chunk_default.py`
  and `tests/test_llm_generate.py` unchanged and passing: the
  `_factory_capacity_kwargs` opt-in assertions still hold because an unset value
  is not forwarded.
- `python3 scripts/worklog.py check`: 19 pre-existing errors, unchanged.

No benchmark was re-run and no retained row changes: the vision time budget is
admission policy, not a kernel or plan change, and every measured page (largest
A4, 43.4 s) stays admitted under the default. `benchmarks/README.md` and
`CHANGELOG.md` are therefore untouched. `scripts/surya_vision_tiling_cost.py`
calls `vision_forward` directly rather than through admission, so it can still
measure shapes the planner and the budget would reject.
