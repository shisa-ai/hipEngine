# Drain and release streamed draft siblings after failure

## Summary

R0281-MTP-DRAFT-EXECUTOR failure handling waited for workers but discarded their
successful draft results, leaving sibling snapshots unreachable by caller cleanup.

## Changes

- Collect every submitted future even after a worker or submission failure.
- Before propagating failure, release snapshots in every successful draft result.
- Continue snapshot cleanup after an individual cleanup error and attach its note
  to the original failure. Preserve successful ordered results and pool lifetime.
- Do not claim worker reuse or resident scheduler improvement; ledger stays open.

## Validation

- Regression at each failed slot first reproduces missing sibling cleanup, then
  passes after implementation.
- Focused chunk policy and live-tier stream-draft/verifier fixture bundle passes
  (`/tmp/mtp-draft-drain.xml`). These are host fakes, not fresh public GPU evidence.
- No arithmetic, dispatch default or native resident scheduler change.

## Next

- Cover submission and cleanup failures explicitly and continue compatibility
  lifetime/public lifecycle work before closing the ledger row.
