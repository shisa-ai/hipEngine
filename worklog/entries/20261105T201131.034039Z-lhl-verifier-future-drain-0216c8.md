# Drain every submitted compatibility verifier future

## Summary

Compatibility chunked verification now collects every submitted future even when
submission or an earlier worker fails, matching the draft-side failure discipline.

## Changes

- Preserve the first submission/worker failure, collect every launched result,
  and annotate additional worker failures without serial retry after progress.
- Pool shutdown remains per-call and precedes propagation; no worker reuse or
  arithmetic/default change is introduced.
- Keep R0281-MTP-DRAFT-EXECUTOR open for its separate owner-lifetime choice and
  full public matrix. This repair does not establish a pool-reuse performance win.

## Validation

- RED: both deterministic future-collection cases fail before implementation:
  `/tmp/verifier-drain-red.log` (early worker error and partial submit failure).
- GREEN: complete chunk-policy unit file passes (`/tmp/verifier-drain-green.xml`).
- Existing live-tier generation fixtures for verifier chunks above four slots
  pass (`/tmp/verifier-drain-live.xml`). These use fake sessions, not real weights.
- Failure injection is an internal future-ownership contract; no GPU/public
  workload can deterministically inject these submission/result failures. Public
  compatibility lifecycle validation remains separately required by the ledger.

## Next

  results and shutdown before stream/resource teardown. Do not reuse old throughput
  comparisons as evidence for worker reuse.
