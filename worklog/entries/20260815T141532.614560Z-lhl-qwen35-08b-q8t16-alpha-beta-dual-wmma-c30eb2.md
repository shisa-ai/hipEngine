# Qwen3.5-0.8B Q8T16 alpha-beta dual WMMA prefill

## Summary

Retained D08-X8. A 64-thread two-wave WMMA owner shares each exact activation
tile across the eighteen Q8T16 alpha/beta pairs. The actual-pair leaf is
byte-exact and 5.010x, saving 1.691 ms; paired Q4/Q8 core pp512 improves
3.64%/2.45% and public pp512 improves 5.05%/2.13%.

## Changes

- Added `gguf_q8_0_t16_dual_prefill_wmma_kernel<32>` and a raw-pointer wrapper
  under `(linear_pair, gguf_q8_0_t16_v1,
  t16_dual_wmma_prefill_bf16_bf16_out)`.
- Wave 0 owns alpha and stages one 1-KiB BF16-to-F16 activation tile; wave 1
  owns beta. Both preserve singleton weight decode, K order, WMMA accumulation,
  and BF16 output boundaries.
- Added gfx1151 model/quant/request and exact-shape capability policy for
  Qwen3.5-0.8B Q4_K_M/Q8_0 rows512/K1024/N16+N16. All misses retain two
  singleton WMMAs; the temporary env selector restores that fallback.
- Added focused GPU, registry, dispatch, backend, model-scope, and cumulative
  role tests plus catalog/campaign/refactor/benchmark evidence.
