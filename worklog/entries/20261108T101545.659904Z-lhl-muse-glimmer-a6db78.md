# Muse Glimmer 256K DFlash and AR kernel profile

## Summary

Muse Glimmer runs at 260,000 prompt tokens with `max_sequence_length=262144`.
That is about 2x the artifact's trained 131072 positions, reached through the
over-length override from `5843c2376`. The output stays coherent: the model
retrieved a needle planted at 25% depth (`needle_found=True`) and reasoned
about it correctly. DFlash greedy output equals AR token-for-token at 260K.
At this length, though, **DFlash is slower than AR**: 5.41 tok/s against
6.69 tok/s, or 0.81x. Verify attention scales linearly with context and is
now 75% of the verify wall time. This confirms that the §11 #1 lever
(shared-K/V verify attention) in `docs/campaigns/MUSE-GLIMMER.md` is what
decides whether DFlash is worth running at agentic context lengths.

Setup: gfx1151 (Radeon 8060S), Muse Glimmer 30B Q4_K_M GGUF. Coding prompt
with a needle, 256 generated tokens, r=1, greedy. A resident llama-server
held 20.8 GB VRAM, but its CU occupancy was 0. Command:

```
rocprofv3 --kernel-trace --marker-trace --output-format csv -d <out> -- \
  .venv/bin/python -u scripts/muse_glimmer_context_profile.py \
  --contexts 260000 --max-tokens 256 --max-context 262144 --needle --ar-steps 16 --output <json>
```

The run used the scratchpad original of `scripts/muse_glimmer_context_profile.py`,
which has the same logic. Phase rows come from
`scripts/rocprof_marker_breakdown.py`.

What the profile shows:

- **Attention is linear in context; everything else is flat.** From 126K to
  260K the context grows 2.06x. AR attention grows 2.02x and verify
  attention 2.19x. The GEMVs, the launch gaps, and the draft phase do not
  change, and the draft phase cannot grow because it is window-bounded.
- **Verify attention costs ~1.36 ms per 1K context per cycle, against
  ~0.25 ms for AR.** With the 5-row verify block, that is about 5.4x AR's
  per-token attention cost. The extra comes from the rows not sharing K/V
  reads. The right fix is a shared-row kernel, not a larger budget.
- **Inferred break-even:** fitting both per-cycle costs as linear in context
  (DFlash ≈ 144 ms + 1.36 ms/1K; AR ≈ 84 ms + 0.25 ms/1K per token) at the
  measured 2.75 tok/cycle puts the crossover near 128K. This is inferred
  from a fit, not measured.
- **The 126K speedup from the earlier sweep overstated the real gap.** That
  sweep reported 7.40 AR tok/s against 9.38 DFlash tok/s (1.27x), but its AR
  row predates `45433fe8b`. Before that commit, the greedy sampler spent
  ~11 ms/token on history counting: 135 ms/token wall against a 116 ms
  step. At 260K the AR wall (149.5 ms/token) equals the step, which
  confirms the fix is in. Re-deriving 126K with the fix gives roughly
  1.1x (inferred). That matches the 1.10x projection in §11.
- **No position failure above the trained length.** The 13 global layers are
  NoPE, and RoPE on the SWA layers is window-relative. No YaRN or
  position scaling is needed. The KV for 262144 positions fit alongside
  the llama-server's 20.8 GB.
- **Prefill is the agentic-UX problem.** 260K costs ~29 min of AR prefill.
  DFlash adds ~2 min of drafter inject on top (16,689 inject calls of
  7.2 ms). Each request currently resets the session.
