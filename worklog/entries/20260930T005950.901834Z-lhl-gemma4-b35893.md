# Align the BF16 teacher rows; Gemma4 control-smoke packet passes on registered ids

## Summary

Two fixes land the Gemma4 control-smoke packet and its BF16 teacher
comparison on the registered row set.

**Packet.** The prompt pin moved from chat-template text to the campaign
recipe's exact token ids (`exact_prompt_ids`, chained), so the packet
prefills the registered `--prefill 1024` of the frozen corpus and scores
the band rows `1024..1823` (inside the campaign-verified `1024..2047`
span); auto width resolves to 2048 from the ids pin. `limit 1` ×
`decode-steps 800` gives 801 rows. The schedule builder grew a
`step_offset` so multiple prompts in one scenario cannot collide on
physical slots (the template assumed one prompt).

**Teacher.** The earlier BF16 figures (610 rows, mean KL 0.0102/0.00947)
were aligned one row position early: `gemma4_teacher_logits.cpp` fed
`prompt + teacher[:-1]` and read logits from `prompt_len - 1`, so teacher
row r predicted `label[r]` while packet capture rows predict `label[r+1]`.
Repeated chain tokens masked the shift because the manifest's argmax
diagnostic compared against the same wrong reference (97.2% "agreement").
Array-level check on the new packet made it unambiguous:
`argmax(P[i]) == label[i+1]` at 100% while `argmax(T[i]) == label[i]` at
99.75%. The tool now feeds every label and reads from `prompt_len`, and
the finalize diagnostic checks next-label argmax over `rows - 1`.

Separately, a 2849-token physical ubatch at 27 offloaded layers exhausted
the ROCm pool (`alloc of 280.35 MiB failed`, two attempts, both empty
GPUs); `n_batch` still holds the whole batch (llama asserts
`n_tokens_all <= n_batch`) while `n_ubatch` is capped at 2048 — per-token
logits are row-independent of that grouping.
