# Qwen4Exp exact Q5 logical256 physical64 decode

## Summary

Promoted a second exact selected-Q5_1 decode contraction: 64 physical threads
materialize all 256 logical partials and reconstruct the original reduction.
Counterbalanced graph decode improves 13.077 to 13.302 tok/s (+1.69%) on
zbook/gfx1151 with exact full logits and generated IDs.

## Changes

- Added and registered `selected_gemv_logical256_t64_bf16_bf16_out`.
- Reconstructed strict stride128 as two independent 64-lane additions, then
  retained the original stride64/32 and wave32 tail.
- Made physical64 the selected-Q5_1 default; retained t128 and t256 fallbacks.

## Validation

- `HIPENGINE_HIP_ARCH=gfx1151 uv run pytest -q tests/test_qwen4_exp_q5_1_selected.py -x`
  — 3 passed with CPU-oracle/t128/t256 BF16-bit identity.
- Counterbalanced one-residency `S/C/C/S/S/C`, p1 + 8 warm + 32 measured:
  median 2.446997 to 2.405650 s, 13.077253 to 13.302016 tok/s; every full-logit
  SHA and generated ID exact.
- `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` recorded the expected
  `q5_1_selected_gemv_logical256_t64_bf16_bf16_kernel`; 1,806-launch matched Q5
  cycle-wall fell 444.698654 to 362.525158 ms (-18.48%).
- Evidence:
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-29-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-exact-q5-logical256-t64-decode.json`.
