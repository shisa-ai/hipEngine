# Correction: D3 down is outside the t16 family (conflated Q5_1 with Q5_K)

## Summary

Supersedes the third "Next" bullet of
`20260930T011029.944678Z-lhl-gemma4-t16-decode-screen-e59361.md`, which said
iteration 39's "Q5_1 not t16-eligible" reading "looks wrong" because
`repack_gguf_q5_k_tile16` and Q5_1 selected t16 wrappers exist in-tree. That
was a conflation: those are **Q5_K** artifacts, and Gemma 4's down projection
is quantized **Q5_1** (`_MMQ_DOWN_QUANT_KEY = "gguf_q5_1"` in
`gemma4_experts.py`). Iteration 39's claim stands, for two independent
reasons:

1. **No Q5_1 t16 family exists.** `hipengine/quant/` has
   `repack_gguf_q4_k_tile16*`, `repack_gguf_q5_k_tile16`,
   `repack_gguf_q6_k_tile16*` — nothing for Q5_1, and
   `gguf_t16_selected_gemv.py` registers `gguf_q5_k_*` / `gguf_q6_k_*`
   wrappers, no `gguf_q5_1_*`.
2. **The down geometry fails the t16 guard regardless.** Down reads
   in_features = intermediate = 704 per output, and `_check_direct_common`
   requires `in_features % 256 == 0` (704 % 256 = 192). Q5_1's own 32-elem
   blocks are what let the production kernel serve 704; the K-quant t16
   machinery cannot.

Do not re-probe D3 with the t16 screen. Its options stay as the row states
(mmvq-style kernel on Q8_1 activations, or a new 16-byte-aligned plane
repack). The screen applies to Q4_K gate_up (landed, iteration 136) and may
apply to **Q5_K** weights elsewhere (e.g. layer 29's Q5_K gate_up, in 2816
✓ out 1408 ✓ — a Q5_K-armed variant of the screen would settle that).
