# TP2 Packet 1: RCCL HIP graph capture support, replay parity, and the capture-size limit

## Summary

Packet 1 asks for a graph-disabled correctness baseline and a RCCL capture
support test before any graph path is designed. Both are now measured on the
target host (W7900 + RX 7900 XTX, ROCm 7.2.4, RCCL 2.27.7, HIP 7.2.53211).

- **Capture works.** With communicator creation outside capture and each rank's
  whole chain captured on that rank's own stream, all 40 probes across chain
  depths 1/4/8/16/24 (rows 1 and 4, fp32 and bf16, all-reduce and broadcast)
  captured, instantiated, launched, and replayed.
- **Replay is bit-identical to the graph-disabled path.** Receive buffers are
  poisoned with `0xFF` before the replayed run and compared afterwards, so a
  graph that silently did nothing cannot pass. 40/40 match.
- **Replay is 20-30% faster and much less variable than eager enqueue.** A 24-op
  20 KB all-reduce chain is 1046-1194 us eager against 724-880 us replayed
  (-26 to -39%). One-row all-reduce p50 lands in a 202-205 us band across every
  captured case, against 219-334 us eager: capture removes host enqueue jitter,
  which is most of the run-to-run variance the screening numbers carry.
- **Capture size is bounded, and the limit is low.** 49 captured nodes (a 24-op
  chain plus its producer/consumer memsets) works; a 32-op chain at 65 nodes
  faults the device with `Memory access fault by GPU node-1 ... Page not present
  or supervisor privilege` and aborts the process. A TP2 decode step's 36
  collectives therefore cannot be one graph; the graph path needs at least two
  captured groups per step.

A chain ladder also replaces the two-point marginal as the per-token estimate. A
20 KB fp32 all-reduce measures 398 us at depth 1, 827 us at depth 16 (28.6 us per
op) and 1388 us at depth 32, so 36 collectives per token is about 1.3-1.5 ms
rather than the 1.0-1.25 ms a single chain_1/chain_4 pair suggested.

## Changes

- `hipengine/core/hip.py`: named capture modes
  (`HIP_GRAPH_CAPTURE_MODE_GLOBAL/THREAD_LOCAL/RELAXED`) and
  `stream_begin_capture` defaulting to relaxed.
- `scripts/tp_collective_bench.py`:
  - `_capture_graph_probe` captures one chain per rank, instantiates, poisons the
    receive buffers, replays, and compares the replayed bytes with the
    graph-disabled result; it records node counts, replay latency and host launch
    cost, and reports `captured: false` with the error instead of failing the run
    when capture is unsupported.
  - `_poison_recv` / `_snapshot_recv` make the parity check meaningful.
  - `--graph-chain-depth` and `--graph-iterations` drive the probe; the docstring
    records the 49/65-node capture limit.
- `benchmarks/results/tp2_graph_capture_probe.json`: the retained probe artifact
  (40 captures, 0 errors).

## Validation

- `python3 scripts/tp_collective_bench.py --devices 0,1 --rows 1,4
  --dtypes fp32,bf16 --ops all_reduce,broadcast --enqueue single --root-order
  forward --chain-depth 1,4,8,16,24 --iterations 30 --warmup 8
  --graph-chain-depth 1,4,8,16,24 --graph-iterations 15 --mode collective` —
  40/40 captured, 40/40 `replay_matches_eager`, 0 errors.
- Depth ladder on one shape (rows 1, fp32, all-reduce, 10 iterations) to locate
  the capture limit: chain 8 = 17 nodes OK (-20.7%), chain 16 = 33 nodes OK
  (-23.7%), chain 24 = 49 nodes OK (-40.9%), chain 32 = 65 nodes **faults**
  (reproduced twice).
- Eager chain ladder, same shape: depth 1 398 us, depth 16 827 us, depth 32
  1388 us.
- `uv run pytest tests/test_unit_scripts_tp_collective_bench.py` — 12 passed.
- `uv run pytest tests/test_integration_benchmark_readme_sync.py` — 11 passed.

## Environment note

After a device fault the next RCCL process hung once during communicator setup
with no output (killed after 900 s); a device health probe and every later run
passed, and `rocm-smi` showed 0% VRAM use with no orphaned process. No restore
was performed. Recording it because a fault-induced hang can be mistaken for a
deadlock in the transport.
