## Finding

- **The AR row in the objective table is pre-grouped-prefill and already obsolete.** It lists hipEngine
  at C6-C8 as 42.49 / 43.57 / 44.34 against peers at 45.53 / 51.91 / 58.74. Measured AR on this build is
  71.72 / 77.25 / 78.27, and `20260830T135632…` records the same thing from its own packets (C8 79.96 vs
  58.74, a win; 5/10 cells). So AR C6-C8 is no longer the deficit; treating it as one re-litigates a
  fixed defect. The independent reproduction here is AR C8 78.27 against that entry's 79.96, a 2.1%
  spread, which is the repeat-noise scale established today (C1/C2 moved +1.95%/+1.68%).
- **The live gap is MTP throughput saturating, not MTP being flat.** The published K3 row shows
  hipEngine pinned near 30-31 tok/s at every width while peers scale to 94.7 / 101.1. That flatness is
  also gone: our MTP arm scales 31.82 -> 54.86. What replaced it is a ceiling - the MTP arm stops
  improving after C3 (54.60 at C3, 55.58 at C4, 54.86 at C8, peak 55.58) while AR keeps climbing to
  78.27. So MTP is a 1.43x win at C1 and a 0.70x loss from C6 onward, and the loss is not a decline in
  MTP - it is MTP standing still while AR advances.
- Framing the target as "MTP must reach peer absolute rates at C5-C8" is therefore the wrong question
  first. The prior question is why the speculative route stops amortizing past ~55 tok/s when the
  non-speculative route on the same server reaches 78.3: our own MTP route loses to our own AR route by
  30% at C6-C8, independent of any peer. Candidates the packets cannot separate: verifier block cost
  growing linearly in lanes x draft width (K3 means 4 rows per lane, 32 rows at C8), draft/verify
  serialization between lanes, or acceptance collapsing as lanes grow.
- The acceptance hypothesis is now **eliminated, for free**: `acceptance.by_width[w].scopes.all` is
  identical at every width - draft acceptance 0.7889447236180904, and per-position 0.930 / 0.773 / 0.645
  for positions 1/2/3, with cycles exactly 71 x width (71, 142, … 568). Mean accepted draft tokens per
  cycle is 1256/568 = 2.21 at every width. So lanes do not degrade acceptance; the ~55 tok/s ceiling is
  cost-side (verifier block cost and/or draft-verify serialization), which is what the blocked
  verifier-block trace exists to separate.
- Consequence: with acceptance constant at 2.21 accepted draft tokens per cycle, MTP *should* beat AR by
  a similar factor at C8 as it does at C1 (1.43x). It delivers 0.70x instead. The gap is therefore
  per-cycle cost growing with lanes on the speculative route only - the sharpest statement of the
  remaining objective.
