# Run the Gemma 4 expert down prefill through WMMA by default

## Summary

A `rocprofv3` kernel trace of W7900 prefill at `6aec43d09` put one kernel,
`q5_1_selected_grouped_prefill_staged_bf16_bf16_kernel<4, 4, 256>`, at 59.3% of
prefill kernel time at 512 tokens and 60.1% at 8192 (8.75 and 17.97 ms a call).
It is the exact staged owner of the Q5_1 expert down projection, which the
unpinned default fell to because only a pinned `wmma`/`mmq` mode enabled the
WMMA down. The unpinned default now runs the down through the grouped WMMA
owner, in the Q5_1T16 tile form the tensor already carries, behind a default-on
lever.

Measured on AMD Radeon Pro W7900, Gemma 4 26B-A4B `UD-Q4_K_XL`, production,
BF16 KV, 128 outputs, one warmup and three samples,
`scripts/gemma4_campaign_bench.py`, same tree before and after:

| prompt | prefill tok/s before | after | ratio | decode tok/s before | after |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 512 | 1253.2 | 3164.7 | 2.53x | 70.38 | 70.75 |
| 8192 | 1194.3 | 2936.1 | 2.46x | 69.22 | 70.20 |
| 32768 | 1086.4 | 2335.8 | 2.15x | 53.86 | 53.62 |
| 65536 | 970.8 | 1874.3 | 1.93x | 41.67 | 41.60 |

Public generated ids are equal before and after at every length and
`public_path_parity` is true on all rows. Against the saved 2026-10-06
llama.cpp rows, which were not re-measured, prefill is 1.00x at 512, 0.95x at
32768 and 1.05x at 65536.

## Validation

- Section 2.11 packet on W7900, WMMA down against the exact grouped owner on
  one shared runner (`--candidate-kind down_wmma`, 18 cases, 1,134 rows): median
  row KL 2.1e-6 against 1e-3, p90 5.8e-3 against 2e-2, flip-damage 0.97% against
  3%, top-1 98.41% against 96%; all 18 cases passed with repeats and isolation
  clean.
- Generated-history screen (`scripts/gemma4_generated_history_gate.py`), the
  screen the int8 MMQ down failed, production against strict over 610 rows:
  route on 5 top-1 flips and max KL 0.114; route off 6 flips and max KL 0.214.
  Both arms fail that script's single-chain mean/tail bars, which section 2.11
  replaced for routing models, so the reading is the comparison between arms.
- Teacher-forced captures before versus after: zero top-1 flips over 1,023,
  4,095 and 527 rows, max KL 1.2e-3, 3.2e-2 and 5.4e-3. Decode routes and
  launch counts are identical.
- `pytest` default unit tier: one failure,
  `test_unit_gemma4_fused_wmma_route.py::test_forward_uses_fused_gate_up_and_exact_down_on_the_requested_stream`,
  which pinned the old default; rewritten as three lever cases, the file passes
  9/9. The broad run was not repeated.
- No RED-first test for the route change: the experiment that measured it came
  first, and the tests were written with the lever.
- One small test run used GPU1 while GPU0 held the packet; every measurement is
  from GPU0.
