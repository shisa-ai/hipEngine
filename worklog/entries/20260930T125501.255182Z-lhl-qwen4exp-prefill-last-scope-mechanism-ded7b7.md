# The `prefill_last` scope is the prefill's own output, and its two artifacts are one route effect

## Summary

Task #5 was opened after the X-7 long-form heldout run (`2cca23c6c`) to localize a
suspected defect at the 16-47 prefill-to-c1 transition, on the reading that
`shape=prefill_last` and `transition=prefill_to_c1` both failing - on `dense_wide`
at 1.438974e-3 and on `wmma` at 2.06179e-3, same scopes, same host, top-1 1.0 at
that scope - meant two different routes agreeing on *where* a defect was.

That reading is wrong, and the correction is larger than the task.

**The row is not a transition.** `_trajectory_rows(result)` returns
`result.logits`, and `_strict_trajectory`'s `trajectory[0]` is
`runner.prefill(prompt_ids, capture_logits=True)`. So the row labelled
`transition=prefill_to_c1` is **the prefill's own last-token logits**, and `shape`
and `transition` are two names for it: in the long-form artifact the two
`by_scope` entries carry identical `rows`, `kl_mean`, `kl_p95`, `kl_max` and
`top1_agreement` (4 / 1.4390e-03 / 3.677e-03 / 4.181e-03 / 1.0), and
`scope_failures` lists it once per dimension. The long-form run reports **one**
failing scope, not two.

**The disagreement is five orders of magnitude, and it is not a route.** Scanning
every artifact in `benchmarks/results/` carrying a `prefill_last` scope (49 of
them), 43 report it at 1e-8 or below - float round-off - and six report it above
1e-6, all of them heldout/admission/natural fixtures. The canonical 12-case gate
reports **4.4145e-09** for `dense_wide` and for `wmma` alike; the long-form run
reports **1.4390e-03**. `benchmarks/results/2026-09-29-qwen4exp-prefill-scope-
discrepancy/README.md` had already found the inversion for `wmma` on the admission
suite, falsified its own dispatch-order explanation with the engagement recorder
(the route engages in both protocols: 264 canonical launches, 328 heldout), and
recorded *"why the heldout prefill row moves"* as an open question with a named
test. It was never resolved. `dense_wide` on the long-form suite reproduces the
inversion, which removes the remaining discriminators: not the route, and not
prompt length - the long-form suite's 512-token prompt is the same row count as
canonical `code-p512`.

**The mechanism: the route's per-logit perturbation is the same in every protocol
and only its projection onto the distribution differs.** It replaces the exact
chain's f16 operands at 16-47 and moves individual logits by ~0.4-0.6 whatever the
prompt; KL is distributional, so the same perturbation reads as 1e-12 where the
changed logits sit in the flat part of the softmax and as 1e-3 where they sit near
the argmax. That corrects the record in both directions: the canonical row's
flatness is not evidence about the route (its logits move by 0.4 - the 2026-09-29
packet read the flatness as *"the route runs and contributes no measurable
numerical difference"*, which is true of the distribution and false of the
logits), and the long-form failure is not a transition defect (there is no
transition mechanism in the row).

## Changes

`scripts/qwen4exp_prefill_last_row_probe.py` plus
`scripts/run_qwen4exp_prefill_last_row_probe.sh`: one process, production profile,
chunk 1024, three repeats per arm, both arms built from one generator and
differing only in `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_DENSE_WIDE` + `..._DENSE_WIDE_LAYERS=
16-47`. It reports three things per subject: the same-arm repeat deviation (the
noise floor), the route effect on the prefill's own logits, and the route effect
on the first decode step.

Five subjects, chosen as the exact protocols whose artifacts disagree: canonical
`code-p512` (512 tokens), admission `code_merge_intervals` (40) and
`heldout_code_rate_limiter` (55), long-form `long_mixed_ja_en_acceptance` (512)
and `long_general_ja_retention_review` (424).

The ledger's `X-7` row now owns this scope explicitly, with a note that a future
reader should extend `X-7` rather than open a new row; `DE-7` is decode
attention/GDN *cost* (ms/token) and owns nothing here. `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`
carries the rollup. `benchmarks/README.md` deliberately unchanged - this is a
measurement of the gate's own scope definitions and of a default-off-then-promoted
route's arithmetic, not a change to a shipped topline result.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| canonical `code-p512` | 512 | bit-identical | KL **1.3277e-12** | KL 2.1902e-05 |
| admission `code_merge_intervals` | 40 | bit-identical | 0 (bit-identical) | 0 (bit-identical) |
| admission `heldout_code_rate_limiter` | 55 | bit-identical | 0 (bit-identical) | 0 (bit-identical) |
| longform `long_mixed_ja_en_acceptance` | 512 | bit-identical | KL **1.0885e-03** | KL 9.6647e-06 |
| longform `long_general_ja_retention_review` | 424 | bit-identical | KL **1.5955e-04** | KL 1.7305e-06 |

**Two 512-token prompts, one route, one process: 1.3277e-12 and 1.0885e-03.**

The probe reproduces both recorded artifacts from one process: 1.3e-12 against
the canonical artifact's 4.4e-09, and 1.1e-03 against the long-form artifact's
1.439e-03 four-case mean. Neither run was measuring something the other could not
see.

| subject | `prefill_last` KL | `prefill_last` max abs logit delta |
|---|---|---|
| canonical `code-p512` | 1.3277e-12 | **4.0587e-01** |
| longform `long_mixed_ja_en_acceptance` | 1.0885e-03 | **4.4500e-01** |
| longform `long_general_ja_retention_review` | 1.5955e-04 | **6.2800e-01** |

Nondeterminism is excluded rather than assumed: every same-arm repeat in every
arm and subject is bit-identical (worst deviation 0.0 across all subjects, arms
and steps), and the long-form artifact's own `repeat_determinism` passes 3/3.

The two admission subjects are a **negative control**: `dense_wide` declines at
`rows <= 256`, so the route cannot run there and the probe correctly measures
nothing. They are reported as such rather than as zeros about arithmetic.

**Why the scope exceeds its limit is the gate working.** It is 4 rows, one per
prompt, against `kl_mean_max` 1e-3, with no averaging across the 96 steady rows to
absorb one sensitive prompt, where the canonical protocol applies the same limit
to a 1536-row scope. The long-form suite's prompts are natural long-form tasks
whose transition distribution is sharper, so the same arithmetic that reads as
1e-9 on the canonical suite reads as 1.4e-3 here. That is the intended behaviour
of a per-scope gate and the reason the long-form protocol exists, and it is the
first measurement attributing the sensitivity to the route's f16 operands rather
than to a transition, a decode-path selector, or nondeterminism.

**A false alarm checked and discarded, recorded because it was reasonable.** Five
canonical 16-47 artifacts across three route labels share one full-matrix
candidate digest (`65370710c0b9`): the 2026-09-16 wmma-family run, the 2026-09-17
`dense_wide` certificate, and a 2026-09-29 `wmma` run with `dense_wide` cleared.
Since `strict_logits_sha256`/`candidate_logits_sha256` hash the whole concatenated
float32 matrix (`_matrix_sha256`), and this probe measures a 0.4 logit delta from
binding `dense_wide`, identical digests looked like a false pass. It is not one.
The 2026-09-17 certificate was created at 20:45 UTC, 81 minutes *after*
`04f1dde42` added `_dense_wide_key` to the dispatch cache key, so its cache key was
correct; and `9a30a93c2` (which added the `dense_wide` recorder) does not touch the
cache key, so the certificate's absence of a `dense_wide` census entry is the
documented recorder gap and not a replay. The identity is the arithmetic: the two
wide-row f16 routes produce bit-identical results at 16-47, which is what RT-1
recorded today from four artifacts. The resolution is only visible from the
timeline.
