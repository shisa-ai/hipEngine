# Survey Qwen3.8 Flash-Next Strix Halo engines

## Summary

Published a matched Strix Halo engine survey that separates speed, static
implementation fidelity, autoregressive repeatability, MTP equivalence, test
coverage, production-promotion guards, and absolute model/quant quality.
Static logits are broadly plausible across the tested forks, but fast
multi-step paths fail: Nathan is non-repeatable on every canonical AR case,
apepojken on four of twelve, EngramHalo on one p4096 case, and every fast
external MTP lane fails full repeated AR equivalence.
