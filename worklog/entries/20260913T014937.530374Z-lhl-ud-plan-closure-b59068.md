`docs/UD-GFX1151-OPTIMIZE.md` is complete through Phase 7 for both published UD
artifacts, within a declared and measured scope of **width c1** and the
**4-95 token context bucket**. Automatic MTP admission is live there.

### Phase 7: promotion, within scope

Both tiers meet all six completion conditions. The promotion is the populated
pin plus the serving evidence rows; it is not blanket enablement:

- c2 measured **1.0418x** — the speculative advantage collapses when two
  requests are in flight, because the server path's own AR rate nearly doubles
  while MTP barely moves.
- c4 has no entry in `GGUF_SPECDEC2_MTP2_PHYSICAL_WIDTH_DEPTHS['production']`.
- c8 is policy-admitted but fails every request with `HIP error 2: out of
  memory` at capacity 8.
- Above 1023 tokens the adapter refuses MTP and the verifier stops batching.

## A harness bug found while producing the artifact

`scripts/ud_mtp_paired.py` hardcoded its `candidate_mode` note as "the U6
admission pin is empty … this artifact is diagnostic evidence". That was true
while the U6 records were incomplete and false the moment they completed: the
same artifact reported `pin_complete: true` with no open items while calling
itself diagnostic. The note is now derived from
`u6_gate.gated[*].pin_complete`, so it cannot drift again, and it states that
the in-process grant is kept only so the measurement cannot depend on admission
state, where it is a no-op.

## What remains open, with its blocker

14 items in the plan stay unchecked. None is an unfinished phase; each names
what is missing. The two that matter most:

- **Budget 4 is correct but uneconomical.** Rows 5 is the only newly reachable
  production shape and costs **18.26 ms/row against 12.79 at rows 4** (91.32 vs
  51.15 ms/step), because above four rows the IQ family leaves the rows 2-4
  local32 verifier sibling and reverts to `gguf_iq_dense_strict`, which then
  owns 59.5-61.8% of verifier time. Extending the sibling's policy to rows 5-8
  is the follow-up. `max_candidate_budget` stays 3.
- **Batch-composition invariance at c2/c4/c8** and the long-context points
  (4096, 32768) are unmeasured. Deterministic replay passes; composition needs
  the multi-request caller, and the long-context route above 1024 falls through
  to the per-row strict loop rather than the batched verifier.
