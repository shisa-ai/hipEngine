# T16 expert-major route measured fastest gate/up owner: -32.90% median

## Summary

The 4-arm gate/up owner A/B measures the T16 expert-major compensated route for
the first time, and it is the **fastest registered owner**:
 runs at
**-32.90% median vs the production iu8 risk owner** (faster in 8/8 cases,
range -40.55% to -30.66%), against a maximum repeat drift of 0.2613% - a
126x-noise result. Absolute medians: balanced 512/1024/4096/10240 at
5.848/5.997/6.032/8.770 ms against the iu8 owner's 8.517/8.641/8.881/14.691;
sparse 4096/10240 at 2.852/6.485 ms against 4.494/10.912. The other three arms
reproduce their committed dispositions exactly (j32 +114.82%, grouped
+152.25%, F16-compact +138.51% medians), validating the harness.

The prize structure: the T16 route is a **bit-lossless weight repack decoded in
registers** (avoiding the LDS-footprint doubling that refuted F16-compact),
F16 WMMA with F32 accumulate plus a **compensated accumulate correction**, and
**no repair by construction** - so the gate/up GEMM saving (~33% of the
2,779.7 ms gate/up cost, ~917 ms) STACKS with the repair removal it makes
unnecessary (573.2 ms), for a combined ~1,490 ms of the 12,385 ms p4096
prefill kernel time (~12%). This is the largest single in-envelope lever
measured in the campaign.
