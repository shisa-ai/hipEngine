# DE-1 route removed: no scope clears the envelope at an acceptable cost, and the warp is a rebate on a price it cannot pay

## Summary

DE-1's warp256 MoE decode route is **deleted**, and this entry is the record of
why. The row's removal condition was written into `docs/REFACTOR.md` on
`20260930T` as "localize the flip and test a narrower scope; promote and delete
the flag if a scope clears the envelope, delete the flag and the route if none
does." No scope clears the envelope at an acceptable cost, so the second branch
applies.

**The scope space, exhausted.** All rows: full 18-case suite, both arms against
the strict teacher, 1494 rows over 18 cases at 82 decode steps, one harness.
The production default is the 43-layer dp4a scope.

| scope | envelope | net cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 43L (production) | fails max KL **0.261188**, 5.2x over | baseline |
| 48L (all) | fails mean+max (max 0.128120) | - |
| 35L | fails mean+p99+max (max 0.104060) | - |
| **30L** | **passes every bar, 1.84x-3.10x margin** | **+1.426 ms/step, +3.10%** |
| 17L | fails max (0.115957) | - |

The 30-layer scope is the only one that passes, and it passes *well*: mean
0.000428 vs the 0.001 bar, p95 0.001611 vs 0.005, p99 0.009040 vs 0.02, max
0.027240 vs 0.05, top-1 0.995314 vs 0.99, all four categories above their 0.97
floor. It also **dominates the current production default on all five axes**
(mean 2.46x better, p95 2.32x, p99 2.69x, max 2.88x, top-1 0.995314 vs
0.994645). Its 7 top-1 flips are near-ties; the largest flip KL is 0.00161, 31x
below the max bar.

**But it costs a measured +1.426 ms/step = 3.10% of decode kernel time.** Both
arms valid (`token_exact`, `state_exact`, lifecycle closed), per-kernel join
over the decode ROCTX windows: 46.061 -> 47.486 ms/step. The decomposition
closes: **-2.079** (dp4a owner 5.415 at 43L -> warp 3.336 at 30L) **+ 3.556**
(13 more layers on the exact route, 1.485 -> 5.041) **= +1.477** against the
measured +1.426.

**The correction that decides the framing: the warp is not what costs time.** At
a fixed 30-layer scope, adding the warp *saves* **0.493 ms/step** against the
same scope without it. So the +1.426 is the price of the scope reduction and the
warp is a partial rebate on it. There is no configuration in which the warp pays
for the numerics it needs, and no configuration in which it is admissible at a
scope that keeps the production cost.

**The gate that had retained this flag never covered the failing case.** Its
docstring cited qualification "at 996 shared-chain teacher-forced rows (all bars
pass with 2.6-4.4x margin, top-1 99.50%)". That fixture does not contain
`heldout_mixed_review`, which is exactly where the warp fails. The ledger's own
action cell had predicted this gap in advance: "Also qualify the 996-row
canonical citation in `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` 6.1, since that fixture does
not contain `heldout_mixed_review`." A gate that omits the failing case cannot
clear it. This is the second green-on-red fixture-coverage gap in this row - the
first was the flip-clean single-case bisection tier that failed max on the full
suite.

**What the production default actually does, for contrast.** It **passes** the
admission gate with 4-8x margin: mean KL 2.44e-4 vs 1e-3, p99 3.67e-3 vs 2e-2,
max 6.14e-3 vs 5e-2, top-1 0.9911, determinism 3/3, isolation clean. The 18-case
suite it fails is already recorded as a stress fixture whose bars cannot rank
arms, because it fails both arms at the production scope. So the scope reduction
is numerical insurance against a fixture that is not the admission basis, priced
at 3.10% of decode.

**Two prior figures are corrected by this work and must not be reused:**

1. **The 2.0x/5.8x and 2.792 ms/step warp figures were microbenchmark rates.**
   The warp's real edge over dp4a **at the same scope** is **0.442 ms/step**
   (3.778 proportional - 3.336 measured), not 1.948. An earlier projection in
   this work stream scaled the microbenchmark by layer count and predicted
   -0.266 ms/step; the measurement is +1.426. The sign was right by accident and
   the magnitude was wrong by 5x.
2. **The step-63 flip's 0.954472 margin is the strict teacher's**, not the
   candidate's - the probe derives `step_margins` from `ref`. Corrected in the
   results README, the ledger and the changelog; the candidate-side evidence is
   the arm-measured 6.7x KL ratio at that step.

**Also recorded, and it constrains any future use of the bisection:** `max_kl` is
**not monotone** in the layer set (0.00000/0.05319/0.25861/0.19748/0.11596/
0.13123/0.24654/0.10379 at n=0/4/9/13/17/17/26/48). The 9-layer `t2_b`
reproduces nearly the full 0.26 magnitude with **no flip at all**. The max-KL
outlier does not localize by layer-set bisection; only the top-1 flip does.
