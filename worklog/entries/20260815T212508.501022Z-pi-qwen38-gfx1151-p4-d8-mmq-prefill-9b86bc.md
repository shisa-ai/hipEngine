# Reject gfx1151 Qwen3.8 dense D8-MMQ prefill

## Summary

Rejected a materially new byte-neutral transfer of the retained Laguna
D8-Q8_1 dual-interleaved Q4T16 MMQ128x32 dataflow to the Qwen3.8 dense
K5120/N17408 gate/up+SiLU boundary. The complete candidate is 2.99-3.09x slower
than the retained dual-WMMA+SiLU owner at rows 512/1024/4096 and wins none of
21 counterbalanced pairs. It also changes most BF16 hidden outputs, although
finite outputs, small diagnostic error, and teardown pass. Production remains
unchanged.

## Changes

- Instantiated only existing tracked kernels in an isolated `/tmp` leaf:
  D8-Q8_1 activation packing, the dual-interleaved Q4T16 MMQ128x32 projection,
  and primitive BF16 SiLU.
- Repacked immutable `blk.0.ffn_gate.weight` and `blk.0.ffn_up.weight` both as
  ordinary Q4T16 controls and as one dual-interleaved payload. The candidate
  pair is exactly byte-neutral at 103,055,360 bytes.
- Compared the operation-complete candidate against the retained exact
  BF16-input dual-Q4T16 WMMA+SiLU owner on all three binding rows.
- Added compact rejection evidence at
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-15-gfx1151-qwen38-27b-q4-d8-mmq-prefill-rejected.json`
  and updated the campaign/changelog.
- RED-first does not apply because no production behavior changed. The leaf
  compares actual model weights and records finite/error/argmax diagnostics;
  decisive performance rejection stops before a model-level quality gate.

## Validation

- Two warmups plus seven counterbalanced HIP-event pairs per row measure
  control -> candidate **6.851030 -> 21.171640 ms** at M512,
  **13.753523 -> 41.140911 ms** at M1024, and
  **54.238129 -> 164.292740 ms** at M4096, for **0/21** candidate wins.
- The 64-layer projection is **+916.519/+1752.793/+7043.495 ms**, or an
  estimated **+61.457%/+57.862%/+57.207%** complete-prefill regression against
  the current retained-path walls. No full-model A/B is warranted.
- Candidate residency is byte-neutral, but operation-complete transient
  workspace is 56.754/113.508/454.033 MB at M512/M1024/M4096.
- All outputs are finite and allocation ownership returns exactly to zero.
  Row argmax agreement is 98.535-99.219%, relative RMSE is 0.748-0.749%, and
  hidden-softmax max KL is below 4.77e-9, but outputs are not BF16-bit exact;
  these hidden diagnostics are not a substitute for the model-logit gate.
- Raw leaf SHA-256 is
  `87efb77cbb3292ecba6491da8286c5e93c191d633bb148cdc44a87fbd5f491db`;
  leaf script SHA-256 is
  `e6ceb97fb262a40d1b73648c79ea837d1de5bf1227a76e3f85709f056eb2f0d2`.
