# MTP request capacity is clamped to four everywhere in the GGUF MTP2 provider

## Summary

Task #13 asks why explicit K3 falls from 55.780 tok/s at C4 to 47.960 at C5 while
llama.cpp scales to 94.735. Read-only tracing found a systemic ceiling: the GGUF
MTP2 provider clamps request capacity to 4 at six separate sites, and request
grouping refuses to grow past the provider's max_requests. That is a candidate
root cause for the whole gap, and it is scheduling rather than kernel speed.

This is a hypothesis with a mechanism, not a confirmed cause. The link I have not
verified is whether the Qwen3.8 explicit-K3 C1-C8 packet routes through this
provider: the qwen38 profile modules do not import it directly, while
qwen35_gguf.py does.

## Changes

- None. Read-only tracing of hipengine/generation/qwen35_gguf_mtp2.py.

## Validation

The clamp sites:

- line 1029 sets max_requests to min(4, max(1, owner.capacity)), so it binds no
  matter how large owner.capacity is.
- lines 1214, 1238 and 1345 repeat the same min(4, max(1, capacity)) expression.
- lines 586 and 3903 use min(4, capacity). At 586 an outer max(len(ids), ...)
  rescues it, which is why that site is not the bug.
- line 1063 admits widths (1, 2, 4) filtered by max_requests.
- lines 1392 and 1405 gate grouping on whether the group plus the missing requests
  exceeds provider.executor.max_requests, so a fifth request cannot join.

The consistency signal that makes this worth testing: our explicit K3 at C5-C8
(47.960, 49.020, 55.225, 55.860) is below our own AR at the same cells (61.881,
71.226, 74.903, 78.667). Speculative decode slower than plain autoregression is the
signature of a partly-speculating batch paying verify overhead while the remainder
runs unshared. Two alternatives are excluded: _MTP_SERVING_TARGET_BATCH_MAX_SLOTS = 4
gates only the packed-prefill helper at qwen35_gguf.py:2983, and the capacity at
line 581 is rescued by its outer max.

## Next

1. Confirm the route. Does the explicit-K3 C1-C8 packet construct this provider, and
   what is owner.capacity at C5-C8? If capacity is at least 8, line 1029 is binding
   and the clamp explains the whole shape of the curve.
2. Measure per cell at C5-C8: requests actually sharing one MTP group, verify rows
   issued per accepted token, and spec-off or partition events. If grouping stays at
   four, the mechanism is proven.
3. Only then change the clamp, and require the full gate: content-exact 80/80 on the
   C1-C8 protocol plus the same-packet K3 rows. Raising the constant without the
   frontier sizing at line 1030, where max_frontier_rows is max_requests times
   max_candidate_count + 1, would be a correctness bug rather than a fix.
