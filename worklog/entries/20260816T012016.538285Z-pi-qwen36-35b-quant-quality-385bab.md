# Qwen3.6 35B cross-format quantization quality

## Summary

Established a durable quantization-quality area and completed the first exact-artifact Qwen3.6-35B portable comparison on the 60/60/45 W ZBook. The 90-row BF16-teacher suite uses all ten canonical code/English/Japanese/mixed prompts, the six-train/four-heldout split, exact official token IDs, nine fixed continuation contexts per prompt, and full-vocabulary logits.

On matched ROCmFPX HIP `0d313da1849f`, local Q4_K_M is mean/p95/max KL `0.013713/0.067523/0.269131` and `92.222%` BF16 top-1. ROCmFP4 STRIX_LEAN is 15.96% smaller and reaches `97.778%` top-1, but mean/p95/max KL is `0.045984/0.205053/1.272484`; its paired 10,000-sample prompt-block mean-KL delta 95% CI versus Q4 is `[+0.00690,+0.07168]`, and Japanese mean KL is `0.135091`. It fails the frozen `+0.005` KL/category margin and is quality-traded, not Q4-equivalent.

The downloaded PARO quant is independently healthy: its bundled canonical 129,921-token payload reports KL `0.027939`, top-1 `92.856%`, and PPL `6.6090` versus BF16 `6.5590`. The exact checkpoint's hipEngine output is finite and its copied logits agree with its own sampler, but it gets 0/90 BF16 top-1 with mean KL `12.072252`. This is an implementation correctness blocker, not quantization loss. Native ParoQuant Transformers loaded all 94,596 tensors but its external ROCm rotation kernel failed with `HIP error: invalid device function` on gfx1151.
