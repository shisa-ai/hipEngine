# iu8-WMMA dense Q8_0 linear promotion (T1 production route)

## Summary

Promoted the dense iu8-WMMA route for Q8_0 F32/F32 linears to the
production profile. This is the reopened #13 lane resolved by subsumption:
instead of reviving the old prepacked-MMQ attention-gate candidate (whose
determinism blocker was already fixed by the later state work, and whose
perf sat at ~1.01x with a CI through 1.0), the R5 iu8-WMMA kernel was
registered as a generic `linear` variant and a dispatch rule rewrites the
retained F32 coltile family (`prefill_f32_f32_out` /
`coltile8_rowbatch4[_wave_scale]_f32_f32_out`) for prefill-sized rows.
The route captures the attention-gate (2560->6144) and shared-expert
down (640->2560) projections - 84 calls per routed chunk. The raw MMQ
keeps the shapes where its 128-row tiles win (verified: my 16-row kernel
loses to the MMQ on 2560->10240, so the dispatch ordering is correct);
the exact wave-scale coltile stays the fallback and sub-256-row path.

Packet:

- Operation-complete screen on real weights: 2.47-2.64x (attn-gate),
  3.23-3.45x (shared-expert down) versus the wave-scale coltile parent;
  drift ~1e-6 absolute (rel p50 ~2e-7).
- Teacher-forced full-model logits (9 fixture cases, double-run): KL
  versus the incumbent <= 5.1e-8, top-1 agreement 100% everywhere, argmax
  gaps 15-30, bit-deterministic.
- Full-suite same-residency A/B (72 samples, chunk 1024, both arms
  carrying the five promoted routes): prefill geo-mean **+7.9%**, every
  case winning (1.068-1.087); decode neutral (p4096 TG wiggle on two
  cases matches the known #16 variance signature).
- Focused tests: dispatch gating (default-off, sub-256 rows, odd-K
  geometry keep the parent) plus the kernel suite.

Two real dispatch bugs were found and fixed during qualification: (1)
the linear resolve cache did not key on this route's flag, so before/
after arms re-used the first arm's resolved kernel (the initial logits
probe silently measured the parent for most cases, exposing the bug via
impossible zero-drift-with-nondeterminism cells); the cache key now
includes the flag. (2) the first launch of a process could silently
fall back past the route because the variant was registered lazily
after the dispatch decision; the rule now ensures registration before
deciding.
