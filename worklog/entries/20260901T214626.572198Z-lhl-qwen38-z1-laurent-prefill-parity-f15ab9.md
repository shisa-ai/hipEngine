# Refresh Laurent prefill parity at C2 and C8

## Summary

Ran pinned Laurent Vulkan `c28d538df` and current hipEngine under the same
17,106,775,008-byte Qwen3.8 `Q4_K_M` model, ten-prompt suite, one-output
boundary, greedy/no-cache settings, physical host, and barrier-released C2/C8
schedule. The authoritative comparison uses prompt tokens divided by
barrier-to-last-completion complete wall for both engines.

hipEngine/Laurent measures **178.660/196.824 prompt tok/s at C2 (-9.23%)** and
**283.540/297.325 at C8 (-4.64%)**. hipEngine prefill passes all exactness and
route checks; Laurent generates one token for every request with unique-char
fraction 1.0 and zero repeated word trigrams.

Laurent's internal `prompt_ms` reports 212.057/308.340 tok/s. The earlier
211.888/305.847 leaderboard targets used that internal timer and were not
matched to hipEngine's complete wall. They remain valid Laurent internal
measurements, but not an apples-to-apples engine boundary.
