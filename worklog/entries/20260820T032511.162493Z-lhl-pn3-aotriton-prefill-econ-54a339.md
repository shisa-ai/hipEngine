# Aotriton prefill economics on gfx1151 (35B-A3B)

## Summary

Clarified where AOTriton is actually used and quantified the attention share of
prefill on the gfx1151 35B-A3B lane. Aotriton is **prefill-only** (batched
rows>=512, the 10 full-attention layers); decode attention is 100% native HIP
(`paged_attn_decode_int8_*` for full-attn, GDN recurrent for linear-attn).

## Measured (8060S / gfx1151, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q4_K_M, prefill 512 bulk)

- whole 512-token prefill: 4.36 s (108.5 ms/layer avg over 40 layers).
- aotriton full-attention wall in situ (10 layers, serialized upper bound):
  1.06 s = 105.7 ms/layer = **~24% of prefill wall**; native layers (GDN +
  MoE GEMM) are 76% and dominate prefill.

## Prior crossover evidence (gfx1100/W7900, 2026-05-16 threshold sweep)

Native prefill attention is faster than aotriton below 512 tokens (-3.5% to
-17% at 32-256); aotriton wins at >=512 (+6% to +256%). The 512 threshold is
that measured crossover. The gfx1151 crossover is unmeasured.

## Conclusion / implication

"Beating aotriton" is a bounded prefill lever: even 2x on attention saves
~0.5 s of a 4.4 s prefill, and prefill is ~25% of an end-to-end 512+512 run.
The larger attention lever on gfx1151 is the native decode path (P3-FULLATTN,
full-attention paged_attn_decode ~2.4 ms/tok of the 26 ms decode wall), where
there is no aotriton to beat.

Open follow-ups (not started): (a) measure the gfx1151 native-vs-aotriton
prefill crossover to see whether the 512 threshold can drop; (b) commit
P3-FULLATTN (decode paged_attn_decode tuning) as the active candidate.
