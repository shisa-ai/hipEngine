Both one-wave and two-wave candidates reconstruct the baseline stride64/stride32/16..1 sum association and pass all **7** Laguna KV tests bit-for-bit with complete `KVLiveSpans` metadata/lifecycle behavior.
- The direct wave32 transfer is slower in the measured SWA family: clean-D1 local128 -> dirty candidate is **4.212 -> 4.274 ms/token (+1.49%)** at the canonical 69-token regime and D0-local128 -> candidate is **27.823 -> 29.016 ms (+4.29%)** at the 512-token physical window. It removes LDS/barriers but serializes four head dimensions per lane; local32/VGPR32/LDS0/scratch0 does not offset that loss of width.
- A two-wave 64-thread reconstruction uses two dimensions per lane and 512 B LDS to reproduce the exact stride64/32 tree. It is neutral short (**4.210 ms, -0.034%**) and still slower at 512 (**28.638 ms, +2.93%**). The separate no-profiler d32 values (`26.785 -> 26.553/26.543 ms`) are classified as dirty-run noise because the kernel-specific short/physical-window gates fail and profiled child wall regresses.
- No category run was warranted after the predeclared family screen. Both candidates and their backend selectors/tests were removed; local128 remains default. Future SWA work must add token/score parallelism or online/split reduction rather than only remap the 128 head dimensions.
