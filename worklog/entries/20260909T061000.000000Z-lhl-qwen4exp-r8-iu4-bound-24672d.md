# R8: direct-Q4 IU4 grouped-prefill feasibility bound

## Summary

Ran the R8 bounded feasibility screen's instruction-level control first,
per the plan's precision-matched requirement. The gfx1151
`v_wmma_i32_16x16x16_iu4` intrinsic compiles and lowers to a single
hardware instruction (half-width i32x2 fragments versus iu8's i32x4).

Measured throughput control (hipEvent microbenchmark, 160x64 threads,
2M dependent wmma instructions per lane path, identical instruction
counts): **iu8 98.9 ms vs iu4 52.3 ms - the iu4 lane runs at 1.892x
the iu8 rate**.

Verdicts:

1. **Precision-matched integer split** (`q8 = low4 + 16*high4`, two iu4
   products per iu8 product): 2 / 1.892 = **0.945x** - the arithmetic
   control loses ~5% before recombination/correction overhead. Rejected
   as a speed route, exactly as the experiment plan's cancelation
   analysis predicted (slightly worse than cancellation on this
   hardware).
2. **Residual 2-plane IU4** (the separate numerical hypothesis): the
   arithmetic ceiling is 3/2 x 1.892^-1 = **1.06x** versus the current
   3-plane iu8 chain. A <=6% ceiling on the expert-GEMV subowner cannot
   fund the numerical risk (4-bit residual planes versus 8-bit, with the
   BF16-publication envelope precedent - the Q5_K bundle - already
   sitting within 18% of rejection at 8-bit-adjacent precision) or the
   qualification cost. Rejected per the stop criteria.

The bounded screen closes without a kernel build: no complete-chain win
is possible within the measured arithmetic envelope. The old
dense-prefill IU4 gains remain non-transferable to the Flash-Next MoE
owner, consistent with the pinned MTP-IU4 history review.
