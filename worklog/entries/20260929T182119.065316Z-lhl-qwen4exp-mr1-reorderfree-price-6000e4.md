# MR-1 priced: the reorder-free share is 0.0145%, 114x below the rejected lever

## Summary

MR-1 ("Router logits producer, 617.73 ms, reduction-structure-bound") records that the
warp-per-token reduction wins 2.16x and +1.65% end to end but **fails top-1**. Its stated
next step is: *"First try structures preserving the existing reduction: fold the
shared-expert gate row into the router GEMM (gufo computes both in one GEMM), or remove a
pass. **Price the reorder-free share first.**"*

**Priced: the reorder-free share is 0.0145% of the window.**

## The "row" is a vector, read from the GGUF

| tensor | shape | type | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `blk.N.ffn_gate_inp.weight` | (512, 2560) | F32 | the router GEMM |
| `blk.N.ffn_gate_inp_shexp.weight` | **(2560,)** | F32 | **a vector - one output row** |
| `blk.N.ffn_gate_shexp.weight` | (640, 2560) | Q8_0 | the shared expert's own gate projection |

So folding the "row" adds **one output row** to the router GEMM.

## The price

From X-3's attribution (window 22,698.6 ms):

| item | kernel | ms | share |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| router logits producer | `qwen35_router_logits_f32_token_tile_dense_exact_kernel` | 617.805 | 2.722% |
| **the shared-expert gate row** | `dense_gemv_out_kernel` | **3.286** | **0.0145%** |

617.805 ms matches the row's stated 617.73 ms, which is a useful cross-check that I am
looking at the same kernel the row means. The 3.286 ms is the whole
`linear:layers.*.shared_expert_gate` role across all 48 layers.

**The fold can save at most 3.286 ms = 0.0145%, and that is an over-estimate** - the MAC
still happens, just inside the router's kernel. The genuine saving is the launch and one
activation read.

## Verdict

**114x smaller than the rejected lever's +1.65%.** The reorder-free path cannot recover
that win, and MR-1's pricing step is answered: do not build the reorder-free router
restructuring expecting a meaningful end-to-end gain.

This does **not** re-open the rejected warp reduction. MR-1 already records that only the
tested warp reduction is rejected and that other reordered implementations would need
their own production gate. That stands. What is closed is the *reorder-free* branch -
which is exactly why the row asked to price it first.

## Validation

- The "row is a vector" claim is read from the GGUF tensor shapes, not assumed from the
  name. Note this took three attempts: `scan_gguf` on the first shard returned
  `tensor_count: 0`, and the tensors are a tuple of `GGUFTensorInfo` (not a dict, and the
  attribute is `shape`, not `dimensions`). The model is split across 4 shards and all four
  must be scanned.
- The router kernel identity is cross-checked against MR-1's independently recorded
  617.73 ms; the measured 617.805 ms agrees.
- I distinguished `ffn_gate_inp_shexp` (the 1x2560 row, 3.286 ms) from `ffn_gate_shexp`
  (the 640x2560 shared-expert gate projection, 271.004 ms in the `shared_gate` role) -
  conflating them would have overstated the lever by 82x.
- Ledger IDs unchanged (70).
- **Attribution, not an experiment.** No kernel was written or run.
