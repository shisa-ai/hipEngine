# IQ3_S dense MMQ, and the four-quant policy retained

## Summary

Adds IQ3_S to the dense raw-IQ integer-MMQ route and, having measured the
combined arm, un-holds IQ4_NL. `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_MMQ_PREFILL_POLICY` now routes
every quant whose K32 groups are expressible as `scale * int8`.

**Prefill 127.20 -> 171.86 tok/s (+35.1%)**, decode unchanged. Campaign
cumulative on the published protocol: **22.1 -> 171.9 tok/s (7.78x)**, gap to
the plain `Q4_K_S` file on the same host **13.3x -> 1.72x**.

This **reverses** the IQ4_NL hold-out from `018bc80eb`, deliberately and with
the trade recorded.

## Changes

- `gguf_iq3_s_grid.h` (new): the 2 KB IQ3_S grid, extracted from
  `gguf_iq_dense.hip` and now included by both kernels so there is one copy
  rather than two. `gguf_iq_dense.py`'s `TABLE_HASH` covers it.
- `gguf_iq_source_mmq_prefill.hip`: `iq3_s_expand_group32`, the QTYPE==2 arm,
  `IQ3_S_BLOCK_BYTES`, and the extern "C" entry.
- `gguf_iq_source_mmq_prefill.py`, `quant/__init__.py`: IQ3_S dense launcher,
  registration, exports.
- `hip_gfx1151/__init__.py`: IQ3_S and IQ4_NL policy entries, with the measured
  trade in the comment; `_GGUF_IQ_DENSE_MMQ_IQ4_NL_HELD_OUT` deleted.
- `docs/REFACTOR.md`: the hold-out entry replaced by a retained-trade entry
  with a per-quant rollback trigger.

**IQ3_S expansion.** One 4-bit scale per K32 group; each group is eight grid
lookups of four values with a per-value sign bit. Grid magnitudes are odd
1..15, so the signed residual fits int8 with wide margin. `words[w]` byte `b`
carries element `4*w + b`, so the grid slot is exactly `w`.

Mean regresses 36% and p95 52% against the two-quant policy. The **maximum
improves** to 0.0297407 - the best of the four arms, and better than the
previous default. Both binding gates pass with margin (max 0.0297 against 0.05,
top-1 100%); the strict mean/p95/p99 thresholds were already failing, the
pre-existing campaign state.

Retained under the standing instruction to take production-correct wins.
Rollback is per quant: delete a policy entry. IQ4_NL buys +22%, IQ3_S +8.3%.

## Why the earlier hold-out was reversed

`018bc80eb` held IQ4_NL out because its mean was the worst measured and the
cost looked concentrated. Two things changed:

1. **Localization failed** (`82b6447f9`). Excluding the three early `ffn_down`
   tensors - which a single-prompt probe blamed for 71% of the
   divergence-from-default - measured no better against the teacher
   (0.0013632 vs 0.0013457). The cost is not attributable to particular
   tensors, so there is nothing to fix by exclusion.
2. **IQ3_S shows the same pattern**, which makes it systematic rather than an
   IQ4_NL quirk: it is a mean/tail-shape property of the shared Q8_1 DS4
   activation plane. And the combined arm has the **best maximum of all four**,
   so the tail is not degrading as the mean rises.

## Next

Q3_K, IQ2_S and IQ2_XS remain on the strict GEMV: they carry a scale per 16
elements and their residual integers reach +/-128 and +/-1333, so routing them
needs a per-16-scale MMQ contract - a kernel redesign, not an expansion
function.

The open improvement is a finer activation quantization than the shared DS4
plane, which would likely recover the mean regression recorded above.

K_S was not separately gated; it carries 15 IQ3_S tensors against K_M's 4, so
its gain should be larger. All zbook rates are provisional pending the desktop
gfx1151.
