# Reconcile final long-row untracked memory growth

## Summary

Closed the apparent long-row untracked-memory growth as monolithic AOTriton
scratch scaling, not a second owner. Under the final 8-MiB ROCr reserve,
whole-device-minus-tracked residual grows from 322.165 MiB at 512 to 551.144
MiB at 4K. Fresh traces show the live attention allocation grows from 70.5 to
300 MiB over the same shapes. After subtraction, all 512/1K/4K/8K remainders
fit within a 7.003-MiB band.

- Added diagnostic artifact
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-15-qwen36-27b-final-untracked-gap-reconciliation.json`.

## Validation

- Fresh cap8 cached `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace --scratch-memory-trace` at 512
  records 16 `attn_fwd` launches with scratch metadata 1,504 bytes, grid
  2048x24, and 16 x **70.5-MiB** allocation/free pairs.
- The same trace at 1K records grid 4096x24 and 16 x **141-MiB** pairs. The
  retained 4K trace records metadata 3,200 bytes, grid 8192x24, and 16 x
  **300-MiB** pairs. Final 8K remains two 4K chunks with one such allocation
  live at a time.
- Final cap8 whole-device-minus-tracked values are
  **322.165/395.284/551.144/547.282 MiB**. Subtracting the corresponding
  **70.5/141/300/300 MiB** scratch leaves
  **251.665/254.284/251.144/247.282 MiB**, a **7.002-MiB** span. The long-row
  observed growth versus 512 is **228.979/225.117 MiB**, matching the
  **229.5-MiB** scratch growth within **0.521/4.383 MiB**.
- The prior same-route 8K stage timeline independently shows the first layer-3
  full-attention window raising sampled VRAM **300.754 MiB**, from
  **16.795914 -> 17.089619 GiB**.
- Artifact JSON, source SHA-256, and all derived residual/delta arithmetic are
  checked before commit. Configured compact-peer verify and routing/liveness
  guard are rerun for the multiloop record.
