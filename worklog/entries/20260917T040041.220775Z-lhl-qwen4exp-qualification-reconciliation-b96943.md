# Qualification record reconciliation for the promoted wide route

## Summary

The review asked for the full-stack qualification record to be reconciled
against the local numerical gate, because the wide route's gate is
candidate-local: its teacher is the production profile with this route's
selectors cleared, not the named strict profile, and its
``strict_logits_sha256`` field does not make it one. The reconciliation is now
in `docs/QWEN4EXP-STATUS.md` §4 as a per-field table against
[`EXECUTION-PROFILES.md`](../docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md) §9, plus the three
retained artifacts that touch qualification for this model.

Result: the promoted scope has a full-protocol numerical envelope (1548 rows, 3
scopes, no failures), determinism (3 identical trajectory hashes), a measured
performance delta, and an end-to-end launch-census identity check. It has
nothing recorded for four §9 fields — isolation/graph-eager/lifecycle verdicts,
task quality, BF16-relative non-inferiority, and whether generated-ID equality
is binding or diagnostic — and its teacher is not §6.1's named-strict
comparison.

The three artifacts that could have filled those fields do not:

- `2026-08-27-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-heldout-logits.json` is 8 predeclared
  category-heldouts with top-1 8/8 and mean KL 9.87e-3, but against a llama.cpp
  oracle (`bea3b12da…`) at the broad-floor thresholds (max KL 0.05, top-1 0.90),
  on the `zbook` host (`87c566d30a…`, not this campaign's `55ea6c509d…`), dated
  2026-08-27 — before `49ffa3cb5` zeroed the recovery flags and before both route
  promotions. Its own caveat records that the merged 18-prompt diagnostic is not
  reported as passing (`mixed_ja_en_review` KL 0.089147).
- `2026-09-03-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-pf1-admission-suite-reconciliation.json`
  carries the 18-prompt shape with context and incumbent-relative blocks but is
  `measurement_valid: false`, `status: invalid_or_screen_only`, with one
  qualification blocker, on the zbook host.
- `2026-09-05-framework-qwen4exp-qsa-fullsuite-audit.json` is the closest thing
  on this host — canonical 12 cases, `cross_mode_output_exact: true`,
  `within_mode_deterministic: true`, clean teardown, and a follow-up comparing all
  128 logits plus recurrent and complete paged K/V bytes — but it qualifies the
  QSA h256 route at manifest `b3afcd55…` and records a decode finding it does not
  waive. It is not evidence about the dense route.

Recorded as a gap in the record rather than a failed check: the route stays
promoted on its numerical and performance halves, and §5 item 2 is what would
close the table.
