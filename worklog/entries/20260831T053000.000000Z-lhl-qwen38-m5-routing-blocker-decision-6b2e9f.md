# M5 whole-batch routing: economics measured; server-route demotion needs a frozen-protocol amendment

## Summary

Sub-group interleaving at physical widths 5-8 costs 0.74-0.80x of own
AR (frozen 24-token protocol, `/tmp/m5-routed-24tok.json`: C5-C8 mtp
28.03/31.38/31.80/34.17 vs AR 36.48/39.23/42.19/46.11, engaged 6/6).
Routing over-width batches to one full-batch AR decode would recover
+25-36%, but it removes the spec cycles the frozen C5-C8 engagement
contract requires, so the production route change is a named
protocol-amendment decision, not silent scope.

## Measurements

- Engine-tick probe at C8 (frozen protocol): the server admission route
  caps explicit-MTP groups at 4 (`server_mtp_batch_max_active_requests`
  = capability bound), so C8 executes as two alternating MTP-4 batches;
  the partitioner only ever sees 4-row due items (186 calls, all
  (4 -> 4)). The wide-batch AR fallthrough is not reached on the server
  surface.
- The engine-surface M5 policy (`GGUF_SPECDEC2_MTP2_BATCH_ROUTE_ABOVE_REQUESTS`,
  production: 4) is implemented and seam-tested where wide due items do
  reach `_lower_wide_all_spec_due_item`; it is measured inert on the
  server-bench surface because of the upstream cap, and it cannot lift
  any C5-C8 number above own AR (route ceiling = AR arm).
- An earlier interim "+67-90%" reading was a protocol artifact
  (`--max-tokens 512` instead of the frozen 24: longer runs amortize
  prefill differently **and** exceed the spec context eligibility,
  yielding engaged 0/6). It is retracted; the frozen-protocol reruns
  above are the record. Same-session AR arms matched m4 within +-4%.

## Blocker (named)

C5-C8 production demotion to full-batch AR must be implemented at the
admission-route layer (server `_route_request_cap` group formation /
`llm.py` submission grouping: when pending spec-route width > bound,
route the whole submission plain-AR). The frozen bench contract
requires engaged spec cycles at expected MTP widths, so the campaign
owner must first amend C5-C8 engagement semantics (or re-freeze those
cells at their AR-route rates, which remain 10-12% under the 1.15x
gates regardless - those cells still belong to M2's verify-cost wall).
