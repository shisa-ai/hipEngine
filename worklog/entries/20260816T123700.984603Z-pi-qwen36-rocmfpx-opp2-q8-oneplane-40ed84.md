# Reject one-plane Q8_1 after operation-complete screen

## Summary

Rejected one-plane Q8_1 activation arithmetic for the Qwen3.6-35B-A3B
small-M path. The quantized dot is often much faster and gate/up projection
metrics look excellent, but SiLU(gate)*up amplifies the error beyond the
operation-complete KL gate. Projection-only quality would have produced a
false admission.

## Changes

- Added
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-16-qwen36-35b-gfx1151-rocmfpx-opp2-q8-oneplane-rejected.json`
  with c4/c8 random- and unique-expert screens at exact model dimensions.
- Made no runtime or registry change; the candidate stops at the diagnostic
  leaf.

## Validation

- Geometry: hidden `2048`, expert intermediate `512`, `256` experts, top-k `8`;
  c4 uses 32 routed rows and c8 uses 64.
- Gate/up projections versus floating T16 are top-1 exact in all four cases,
  with mean KL no worse than `0.000128`.
- Operation-complete SiLU output fails:
  - random c4 mean/max KL `0.3999/11.6902`, top-1 `93.75%`;
  - random c8 `0.01083/0.69315`, top-1 `98.4375%`;
  - unique c4 `2.2799/54.2090`, top-1 `93.75%`;
  - unique c8 `1.1404/54.2090`, top-1 `95.3125%`.
- Quantize-plus-dot SiLU speed ranges from `1.0228x` to `1.8348x`; speed does
  not override the KL failure, and unique c4 is below the 5% speed screen too.
- Both runs use deterministic synthetic Q4_K weights/activations at exact
  production dimensions. This is a conservative leaf/operation screen, not a
  same-checkpoint full-model quality result.

## Next

  oracle; never admit this route from projection-only metrics.
