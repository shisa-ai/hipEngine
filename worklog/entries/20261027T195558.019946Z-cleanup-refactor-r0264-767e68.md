# Separate Laguna long attention enablement from full score debt

## Summary

Move R0264 full-score diagnostic retirement to the bottom with its actual
caller and shared-policy dependencies. Do not remove the long-attention
capability, which also enables the bounded production route.

## Changes

- Inspect `hipengine/runtime/laguna_gguf_runner.py`: long route selection
  requires `prefill_long_attention_hipblaslt`, then chooses bounded or full
  score based on `prefill_block_attention_hipblaslt`. Both capabilities are
  on for gfx1151. The long capability/setter is not exclusive old-path debt.
- Full-score lazy owner remains reachable through
  `scripts/laguna_long_context_profile.py` block-route comparison and the
  dedicated `scripts/laguna_attention_long_context_hipblaslt_ceiling.py`.
- `LagunaAttentionHipblasLt` owns short, full-score and bounded execution;
  `LagunaSwaAttentionHipblasLt` subclasses it. Algorithm override/count API
  is used by both global and SWA ceiling harnesses, not ceiling-full-score
  alone. Any retirement must split callers and preserve shared functionality.
- Defer diagnostic retain-versus-retire decision and exact migration work;
  preserve bounded route, rolling SWA, short attention and generic spans.
- Historical original entry: full-score 128K improvement 22.088% with 4.298 GB
  scratch; bounded successor improvement 12.521% and 96.655% scratch reduction
  to 143,753,216 bytes. These are old results, not rerun measurements. Exact
  source remains in `1acd9c2fd:docs/REFACTOR.md` under the original heading
  and sweep SHA-256 `c37bbbd58d7b3f493e62ee33a175a3ac28872f0f256ef89ee177df1cd6e1d21d`.
  No runtime, arithmetic or allocation change in this unit.
