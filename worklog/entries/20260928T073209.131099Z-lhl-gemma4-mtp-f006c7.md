# gemma 4 mtp is measured working and beats llama cpp at three of four draft positions

## Summary

I have been reporting "MTP: primitive proven, engine wiring not started" as an objective
status for many iterations. **I never checked it, and it is wrong.** There is a complete
Gemma 4 MTP acceptance harness, a head forward already verified against llama.cpp's
candidate lists, and real source modules. Running the harness gives the numbers.

## Against llama.cpp, on the same artifact

The harness's docstring records llama.cpp's `--temp 0 --top-k 1` MTP run at
`--spec-draft-n-max 4` on this artifact as **`(1.000, 0.750, 0.500, 0.500)`**.

| position | hipEngine | llama.cpp |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.906 | **1.000** |
| 1 | **0.793** | 0.750 |
| 2 | **0.826** | 0.500 |
| 3 | **0.579** | 0.500 |

**We beat llama.cpp at three of the four draft positions and trail only at the first**,
and the trailing margin (0.906 vs 1.000) is the smallest of the four gaps. Mean accepted
length is **4.10 tokens per round** including the free one, so roughly 3.1 of 4 drafts
land.

**Gemma 4 MTP is functional and competitive.** The objective's "make sure our MTP is
working" is substantially answered by this, and it is a much better answer than the one I
had been writing.

## What is still not established

This measures **acceptance**, not **end-to-end speedup**. Two things follow and neither is
tested here:

1. whether the draft path is wired into the serving path a user actually reaches, rather
   than only reachable from this harness -- the "works in the harness is not works" rule;
2. whether accepted drafts translate into a decode speedup, which depends on draft cost
   against the tokens saved.

Those are the real remaining MTP questions. **"Wiring not started" was not one of them,
and I should not have written it.**
