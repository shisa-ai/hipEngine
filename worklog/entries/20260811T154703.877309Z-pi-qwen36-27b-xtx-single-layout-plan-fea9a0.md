# Plan Qwen3.6-27B XTX single-layout campaign

## Summary

Created the RX 7900 XTX continuation plan for dense Qwen3.6-27B Q4_K_M. The
campaign rejects a capacity-specific slow fallback and instead makes one
physical device payload per logical weight a package-default gfx1100 invariant.
It must fit the 23.984 GiB XTX, retain W7900 performance, and beat clean
same-commit llama.cpp HIP and Vulkan in prefill, context-matched AR decode,
compatible natural MTP, and whole-device peak VRAM.

The decisive residency audit found that the 288 rank-2 Q4 matrices currently
occupy 13.037109375 GiB pack8 plus 10.0494384765625 GiB T16. Keeping T16 as the
sole first candidate projects the published 512/4096/MTP tracked peaks from
29.786/32.611/30.477 GiB to 16.749/19.574/17.440 GiB before remaining
NextN/Q8/root duplicate cleanup. These are arithmetic projections, not XTX
measurements.
