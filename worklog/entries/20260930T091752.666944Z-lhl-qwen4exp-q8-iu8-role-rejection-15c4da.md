# Q8 iu8-WMMA dense route rejected by role-isolated evidence: all 14 claimable roles fail alone

## Summary

**The generic dense Q8 iu8-WMMA route is rejected. Every role family it can
claim fails the canonical numerical envelope on its own, so no role set can be
promoted and the route has no promotion path.** This closes the question the
previous entry opened and satisfies the reopen condition `docs/REFACTOR.md`
"Non-GR Q8 Diagnostic Scope" named: role coverage expressed through a dispatch
control.

The prior state was a boolean (`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_IU8_WMM`) that claimed
every eligible Q8_0 prefill linear. Its two rejections each closed an *arm*:
`20260915T015835` rejected the unchanged generic switch ("not role-isolated
non-GR work") and `20260915T022059` rejected the non-GR arm while recording that
it "does not implicate every projection" and asking for "identical operands by
non-GR role". A boolean cannot express that, so re-running it could only
re-measure the whole route that already failed.

The immediately preceding entry (`20260930T034024`) added the missing control,
`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_IU8_WMM_ROLES`. This entry records what it measured.

### What the result establishes

- **All 14 claimable roles exceed `kl_mean` and `kl_p95` individually.** The
  failure is a property of the iu8 arithmetic, not of one projection, so no
  subset of roles can be salvaged by narrowing.
- **The failure is not localizable, and the tail is non-monotonic.**
  `shared_gate` alone (3456 calls, 12%) reproduces the full route's top-1
  exactly (0.98718) while producing a *worse* max (0.062865 vs 0.031511). This
  is the effect `20260915T022059` warned about.
- **Tiny projections still break the envelope.** `ple_key` and `ple_value` are
  one tensor each (72 calls, 0.25%) and both fail; `ple_value` has the worst max
  in the sweep (0.115324, 2.3× the limit).
- **Three roles are unreachable by this route.** `expert_down`, `hc_attn_up` and
  `hc_ffn_up` claim zero calls. `expert_down` shares its `(640, 2560)` shape with
  `shared_down`, which does claim, so the difference is the parent variant, not
  geometry. Their arms are reported inert, never as passes.

### Controls

- **Reproduction.** The full route reproduces `20260915T022059` on mean and p95
  (0.001394 vs 0.0013826; 0.007240 vs 0.0069630). Its max and top-1 differ
  (0.031511 vs 0.1051165; 0.98718 vs 0.99103) because this arm is broader: it
  includes eligible GR-down, and `candidate_dispatch_calls` is 28656 against that
  rejection's 21744 non-GR calls plus 6912 excluded GR reads.
- **Route-off control.** The three inert arms are mutually identical to 1e-12 on
  mean, max and top-1, and **pass every binding threshold**. They are the correct
  route-off control because the candidate inherits from
  `production_gr_up_restore` and so runs production defaults for every flag it
  does not name, unlike `production_conservative` which zeroes all conservative
  flags. Their pass isolates this route's arithmetic as the cause.

### Method notes

- The scope is part of the dispatch-resolution cache key. Without that, two
  roles of identical shape share a key, the first resolution is replayed for the
  rest, and the scope becomes an artifact of cache-fill order. The same omission
  is documented for the WMMA layer scope, where it produced bit-identical arms,
  KL exactly zero and `measurement_valid: true`.
- The gate records only `candidate_spec.environment`, which does not include the
  role scope. Each arm's scope is therefore recorded in `summary.jsonl` and
  `ANALYSIS.txt`, not inside the per-arm JSON.
- `analyse.py` re-derives each verdict from the raw numbers rather than trusting
  `quality.hard_gates_passed`, so a gate-revision change cannot silently relax
  the envelope. It also classifies `candidate_dispatch_calls == 0` as inert
  rather than pass; an arm that claims nothing is the route-off control wearing a
  role name.
