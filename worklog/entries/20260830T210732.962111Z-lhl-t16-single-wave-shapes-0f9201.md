# single-wave band extended to two more dense shapes

## Summary

Sweeping every dense shape that still fell through to the 256-row shared-B tile shows the win is
shape-dependent, so the band was extended by measurement rather than by generalisation. `(5120, 10240)` and
`(5120, 12288)` are bit-identical and 1.02x-1.28x faster on single-wave at rows 2-128 and are now promoted.
Four shapes are recorded as **measured losses** - `(17408, 5120)` at 0.77x-0.83x, `(5120, 6144)` and
`(6144, 5120)` at 0.75x-0.85x, `(5120, 1024)` at 0.63x-0.92x - so they cannot be re-opened by a future
source reading. End to end the first-token wall moved **252.92 ms to 230.49 ms (-8.8%)** on the same build.

The sweep also names the last big prefill kernel gap: the FFN down projection.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/__init__.py`: `GGUF_Q4_T16_PHYSICAL_SINGLE_WAVE_SHAPES` is now
  `{(5120,17408), (5120,10240), (5120,12288)}`. The comment records the measured per-shape ratios, the row
  band, and explicitly that the omitted shapes are measured losses rather than unmeasured gaps.

- `benchmarks/results/2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-t16-single-wave-shapes-accepted.json` (new) and one changelog

## Next

- The down projection `(17408, 5120)` is the last large dense-prefill gap: 1.23-1.37 ms per launch at suite
  rows, 1.7x the gate/up cost on the same 51.5 MB of weights, because `out_features = 5120` gives the shared-B
  grid only ~107 column blocks against 512 compute units. No registered leaf beats it, so closing it needs
  split-K or column-resplit kernel work with its own RED gate; that is roughly 2x on a launch that is as
  expensive as the one this campaign just fixed.
- `(5120, 10240)` still wins at 144 and 160 (1.14x / 1.11x). The band was left at 128 because that is the
  region measured and claimed uniformly; widening it is a separate measurement with its own packet.
- A retained-packet run for this shape extension is still owed before the numbers are quoted as a scoreboard
  movement; the first-token A/B is a sub-window claim, consistent with how the gate/up band was retained.
