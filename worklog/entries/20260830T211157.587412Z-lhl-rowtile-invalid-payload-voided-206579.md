# rowtile speedups on the down projection are void, ULP gate added

## Summary

Task #11 asked for a split-K kernel to fix the FFN down projection's parallelism gap. Before writing one I
swept the rowtile leaves directly, and they timed **7.8x-14.3x faster than shared-B** on `(17408, 5120)`
(0.071 ms versus 1.014 ms at row 2). That number is **void**: the harness reports a worst BF16 distance of
**~1e7 ULP** from the owner for every rowtile measurement, because those leaves need sidecar buffers this
harness does not supply. The same trap had already contaminated two figures I published earlier this session
(`7.28x` and `7.32x` at row 6), so the headline correction here is bigger than the finding: **the
down-projection still has no validly measured win from any registered leaf, and #11 genuinely needs new
kernel work.**

## Changes

- `scripts/gguf_t16_prefill_row_sweep.py`: arms are added for `dense_rowtile` and `rowtile_col4`, the
  dispatcher arm is renamed `c1_rowtile_dispatch` to stop it being mistaken for the sidecar leaf, and any arm
  whose output diverges from the owner now gets `output_diverges_from_owner: true`, has its ratio dropped, and
  prints `DIV ulp=...` instead of a speedup. Verified: at `(17408,5120)` row 6 the three rowtile arms print
  `DIV ulp=2295726080` while `smallm` shows a real `1.33x`.
- `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`: the `7.28x` row-6 rowtile claim is retracted in place, and row-6 routes are
  described as left untouched for control neutrality rather than as a preserved win.
- No engine change. Both promotions from this session stand, because they were ULP 0 by construction.

## Validation

Host `epyc`, W7900 gfx1100, `91b9e3d90`, canonical `repack_gguf_q4_k_tile16` payload, 20 reps, both passes:

| rows | shared-B ms | dense_rowtile ms | reported | ULP vs owner | verdict |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2 | 1.015 | 0.071 | 14.30x | 11,730,944 | void, wrong buffers |
| 4 | 1.015 | 0.094 | 10.80x | 2,243,756,032 | void, wrong buffers |
| 6 | 1.011 | 0.118 | 8.55x | 2,295,726,080 | void, wrong buffers |
| 8 | 1.013 | 0.130 | 7.82x | 2,271,739,904 | void, wrong buffers |
| 16+ | 1.014-1.665 | rejected | - | - | `dense Q4T16 rowtile requires rows in 2..8` |

Valid content from the same sweep, where ULP is 0: `smallm` measures **0.755-0.763 ms at rows 2-16 against
shared-B's 1.013-1.014 ms (1.33x-1.34x, bit-exact)** on `(17408, 5120)`; single-wave is 0.76x-0.85x, i.e. a
confirmed loss on this shape; and the dispatcher itself is 1.00x-1.06x versus shared-B at rows 32-192, which
is why rows 33-192 stay on shared-B.

Why the reachability argument kills the smallm result for now: rows 2-8 are owned by the sidecar
`dense_rowtile` selection (`_q4_t16_sidecar_decode_variants`, `_Q4_T16_DENSE_ROWTILE_MAX_ROWS_BY_QUANT =
{q4: 8, qmicro: 4}`), which this harness cannot measure validly, and rows 9-16 do not occur in this suite -
widths cap at 8 and K3 verify cycles are 4 rows. Declaring a band for rows that the workload never admits is
scoreboard decoration, not a win.
