# Three-engine comparison: strix-llama.cpp, llama.cpp, hipEngine on gfx1100

## Summary

Built `halo-box/strix-llama.cpp` from scratch and measured it against upstream
`llama.cpp` and hipEngine on the same artifact, both GPUs, one at a time.

**The fork buys nothing here, and costs a little decode.** It is explicitly the
gfx1151 (Strix Halo) fork, so that is the expected result. Prefill is level
within ±0.7%; decode is consistently behind upstream by 2.3%/3.0% on llama-bench's
warm `tg128` row and 6.7%/8.5% at a 1024-token context.

**The Gemma 4 gap to llama.cpp is wider than the campaign doc records.** Measured
today on a matched basis, hipEngine is at **40.2% (W7900) / 39.7% (XTX)** of
llama.cpp prefill, not the 47.6%/48.2% the doc's `3761`/`4124` comparator gives.
hipEngine's decode at a 1024-token context is **55.3% / 53.8%** of llama.cpp, a
comparison the doc had never recorded.

## Measurements

All today, 2026-09-27, `gemma-4-26B-A4B-it-UD-Q4_K_XL.gguf`, 1024 prompt tokens,
**bf16 KV** (hipEngine's `kv_storage_dtype` default), `-fa on`, both GPUs idle,
sequential. Prefill basis is a pure prompt forward in every engine.

llama-bench `tg128`, warm short context, bf16 KV: strix 74.56 ± 0.16 / 86.57 ± 0.36;
upstream 76.35 ± 0.13 / 89.21 ± 0.21. At f16 KV both rise (strix 77.71 / 90.78,
upstream 78.83 / 91.59), so bf16 KV costs llama.cpp ~4% decode; the bf16 rows are
the matched basis and the f16 rows are a sensitivity check.

## Next

- **Two open caveats on the 40% figure.** The cause of `3761` -> `4377` cannot be
  attributed: the old build will not start here (`GGML_NATIVE=ON` from a
  higher-ISA machine) and `8cfc315` is in neither local clone. And the harness
  basis is not interchangeable -- `llama-cli`'s `Prompt` rate is
  request-start-relative (`n_prompt_processed / (t_prompt_last - t_start)`) and
  reads only ~2500 for the same prompt, so it must never be quoted against
  hipEngine's `prefill_s`.
- The fork's decode deficit is unexplained. The one fork commit that could have
  moved Gemma's attention routing, `cfe6bb1`, narrows the WMMA guard to
  `ne[0] <= 128 || ne[0] == 256`; Gemma 4's head sizes are 256 and 512, both on
  the same side of that guard as before, so it is not the cause. Not worth
  chasing on hardware the fork does not target.
- The `tg128` column must not be used as hipEngine's decode comparator: the
  context lengths differ. Only the decode@1024 column is matched.
