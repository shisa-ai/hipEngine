# Gemma 4 q4_k decode re-route: selection point located, candidate closed at iteration cap (G3/G4)

## Summary

Campaign iteration 15 (final, cap=15) was assigned to locate the q4_k decode
re-route's variant selection point. **Located, with definitive evidence — and
the candidate is closed, not executed.** The re-route that the G2/G3 notes
framed as a dispatch wiring item is, at its actual selection point, a
multi-part kernel campaign stacked behind four independent gates. None of the
gates can be cleared correctly inside the final iteration, and clearing the
first would contradict its recorded cause.

## The selection point

Decode MoE linears choose their kernel in
`qwen35_gguf_runner.py::_try_run_post_attention_moe_c1_compact_gemv`
(called at line 10272) which resolves through
`_resolve_compact_moe_gemv_kernels` (line 37996) using
`_COMPACT_MOE_Q4_DUAL_GEMV_KEYS` (gate+up) and `_COMPACT_MOE_DOWN_GEMV_KEYS`
(down). The trace's `gguf_q4_k_selected_prefill_out` (9.15 ms/step, 58
launches) and `q5_1_selected_gemv_bf16_bf16` (5.15 ms/step, 58 launches) are
the legacy per-row selected fallbacks this path never leaves.

Four stacked gates, each independently fatal:

1. **`HIPENGINE_GGUF_COMPACT_MOE_C1` defaults off** (line 10273,
   `_env_flag(..., False)`). `docs/ENVS.md:292` records its cause: *"Diagnostic
   fallback — Forces the older compact c=1 MoE decode scheduler; current
   retained default uses direct selected T16 kernels instead."* Reviving the
   C1 scheduler as production means flipping a documented default-off
   diagnostic; per AGENTS "Product Defaults" that needs the cause removed and
   the retained route justified, not silently overridden.
2. **No Q5_1 down kernel exists.** `_COMPACT_MOE_DOWN_GEMV_KEYS` covers
   `gguf_q5_k`, `gguf_q6_k` and the `*_t16_v1`/`*_x8_v1` variants — never
   `gguf_q5_1`, in any table (`_COMPACT_MOE_DOWN_KEYS` too). The artifact's
   down experts are Q5_1 (header evidence below), so
   `_resolve_compact_moe_gemv_kernels` returns `None` at `down_key is None`
   regardless of (1). Clearing this needs a **new compact grouped GEMV decode
   kernel** for raw Q5_1 (port of `gguf_k_selected_pack8_gemv.hip`'s pack8
   structure onto the legacy `dequant_q5_1` arithmetic from
   `qwen4_exp_q5_1.hip:950`), plus wrapper, registration and table entry.
3. **Alignment guard:** `qwen35_gguf_runner.py:37598` requires
   `expert_ffn % 256 == 0`. Gemma 4's MoE FFN intermediate is **704**
   (704 mod 256 = 192) → hard `return False` even after (1)+(2). The 256
   bound traces to the compact scheduler/silu tile expectations inherited
   from the qwen35 shapes; relaxing it requires validating those kernels at
   ffn=704 — untested tile territory, its own unit of work.
4. **Safety/route interplay:** the retained route is T16 decode-repack
   (`HIPENGINE_GGUF_DECODE_REPACK` default 1), but no T16 variant exists for
   Q5_1 down either, so this artifact cannot reach the retained route; it runs
   raw legacy kernels by capability, not by choice.

## Artifact evidence (GGUF header, via `hipengine.loading.gguf.scan_gguf`)

- `blk.N.ffn_gate_up_exps.weight`: **Q4_K**, logical (128, 1408, 2816) —
  fused gate+up, 128 experts, per-expert out 1408 = 2 x 704, in 2816 = hidden
  (blk.29 is Q5_K — one straggler layer, would resolve `None` and fall back
  per-layer even with everything else fixed).
- `blk.N.ffn_down_exps.weight`: **Q5_1**, logical (128, 2816, 704) — in 704
  is the MoE FFN intermediate that fails gate (3).
- Dense/shared `ffn_gate/up/down`: Q8_0 (separate path, not in these tables).
- `gate_weight = layer.weight("ffn_gate_exps")` works because the loader
  synthesizes views over the fused tensor (contiguous per-expert halves), so
  the dual-key lookup `("gguf_q4_k", "gguf_q4_k")` itself would have matched.

## Why this iteration logs instead of reverting

No source change was made: the work was locating and verifying the selection
point (the iteration's stated hypothesis), which succeeded. The candidate is
recorded so a future attempt starts at the right line instead of re-deriving
it: the order would be (a) decide C1-vs-retained-route at the product level,
(b) author the Q5_1 compact down kernel with an oracle against
`qwen4_exp_q5_1_selected_gemv_bf16_bf16`, (c) validate/relax the 256 guard at
ffn=704 with tile-shape tests, (d) promote through the frozen teacher-forced
gate — changed arithmetic against the legacy kernels, same gate the
attention-split candidate failed (see
`20260924T111306.244799Z-lhl-gemma4-1bb76d.md`).
