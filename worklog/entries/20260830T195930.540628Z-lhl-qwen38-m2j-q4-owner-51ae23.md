# M2j Q4 rows2-16 owner promotion retained

## Summary

The M2i hip-API/copy/kernel trace of the production C8 bound-4 configuration
named the accept-window owner precisely: during the median ~196 ms accept-
upload wait the GPU is **98 % busy**, and 112 ms of the ~191 ms busy window is
`gguf_q4_t16_dense_wmma_prefill_bf16_kernel<false,1,3>` - the one-row-tile
smallm owner running the R16 verify pass at ~90 GB/s effective weight
bandwidth. The 16-column low-VGPR sibling (already admitted from rows 17)
measures 1.19-1.65x faster at rows 2-16 on every one of the six physical
Qwen3.8 shapes, and on the N5120 down-projection the 32-thread shared-B2W2
sibling is faster still (0.716-0.727 ms vs 0.772-0.775 ms). All siblings are
bit-exact vs shared-B by the failing-closed microbench harness.

The wrapper now routes **rows <= 16 on the six physical shapes** to low-VGPR
(shared-B2W2 for `(17408, 5120)`), superseding the smallm band {6,8,12,16};
the smallm launcher stays registered under its explicit variant for rollback
and inventory, and rows >= 17 bands are untouched.

Full frozen-protocol run at the production bound: **48/48 cells exact /
engaged / budget-conformant**, MTP tok/s versus the M0 re-freeze:
C1 **7.841 -> 13.759 (+75.5%)**, C2 25.85 -> 28.125 (+8.8%), C3 29.57 ->
30.537, C4 **29.54 -> 34.201 (+15.8%)**, C5 **18.74 -> 26.059 (+39.1%)**,
C6 28.31 -> 31.885 (+12.6%), C7 29.56 -> 32.395 (+9.6%), C8 29.04 -> 33.491
(+15.3%). AR arms are within drift of the re-freeze, so the lift is entirely
the verify/proposal target-path owner. MTP/own-AR flips to >1 at C1 (1.237);
C4 reaches 1.138 vs its 1.15 gate (34.201 vs 34.596, -1.14% remaining).

This also reshapes two campaign items: the M3 C1 deficit shrinks from -63% vs
its 18.191 comparator to -24% (13.759 measured), and the M2 accept-window
story closes as "GPU genuinely busy in Q4 verify owners" - host-side
explanations are fully excluded.
