# Declare exact Q5_1 M2 hierarchical reduction and record RED

## Summary

Declare the first `halo-prefill-match` optimization after the fresh p4096
operation-role profile. The operation-complete MoE role is the largest owner at
23,749.123 ms. Within that role, the exact Q5_1 prefix-down M1 kernel is the
largest individual strict symbol at 6,961.936 ms across 200 launches.

The M2 candidate is **T0**. It preserves each M1 logical-lane FP32 partial and
the strict stride-128→1 addition tree. M2 may fold the stride-128 addition into
the two accumulators already owned by one physical thread, use shared memory
only for the cross-wave stride-64 and stride-32 steps, and use wave shuffles
for the unchanged stride-16→1 steps. No input, prompt, token, expert ID, or
candidate ID affects dispatch or arithmetic.

The registered strict fallback for this optimization is
`selected_grouped_prefill_compact_rowbatch8_out8_expertgrid64_m1_bf16_bf16_out`
under `KernelKey("hip_gfx1100", "moe_linear", "gguf_q5_1", ...)`. The older
logical256 owner remains registered behind M1. If M2 cannot match M1 output
bits at both the sparse 16-row anchor and production 512-row chunk, the T0
candidate is rejected rather than reclassified.
