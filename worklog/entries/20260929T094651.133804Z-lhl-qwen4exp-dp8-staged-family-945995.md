# Staged-f32 across the dense family, and a withdrawn extrapolation

## Summary

The previous unit measured the staged-f32 tile on one dense tensor. This one
extends it to two more shapes and, more importantly, **withdraws an
extrapolation the previous packet made**.

Measured against the leaf the engine actually selects, means over five layers at
rows=1024:

| tensor | shape (out x in) | leaf ms | staged ms | speedup | time | max abs |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| attn_qkv | 10240x2560 | 16.1753 | 13.1121 | **1.2336x** | -18.94% | 1.049e-05 |
| attn_gate | 6144x2560 | 9.8217 | 7.3819 | **1.3305x** | -24.84% | 1.335e-05 |
| ssm_out | 2560x6144 | 9.2511 | 7.2360 | **1.2785x** | -21.78% | 2.241e-05 |
| **total** | | **35.2481** | **27.7300** | **1.2711x** | **-21.33%** | |

The prototype is the fastest variant in the sweep at **all 15 (layer, tensor)
cases**, across one square-ish, one wide-N and one tall-K shape. The gain is not
shape-specific.

## The withdrawal

The previous packet said a family-wide 23.6% would be "~6.8% of prefill" and
labelled it an upper bound. **That figure is withdrawn.** The scaling it rested
on does not close:

- the three measured tensors total 35.25 ms at rows=1024;
- x 16 exact-chain layers (0-15) = 564 ms; x 48 layers = 1692 ms;
- the attribution puts the **entire** `dense_projection` family at
  **4553.007 ms of a 15741.963 ms prefill**.

Any scaling consistent with that 4553 ms total implies a row count or per-step
multiplicity this measurement does not establish. The per-tensor speedups are
solid; the prefill share is not derivable from this data.

A **units error** in the same packet is also corrected: the `(+22.5%)`-style
labels gave the ratio's excess ("x faster"), not the time reduction, which is
18.94% for that mean. Both packets now separate the two.

## Next

Unmeasured and needed before any family claim:

1. **The three `ffn_*_shexp` Q8_0 suffixes** (640x2560, 640x2560, 2560x640). Same
   command, three more runs.
2. **A relative-error figure.** `max_abs_diff` 1.0e-05 to 2.2e-05 is consistent
   with f32 summation-order drift and far too small for f16 operand rounding, but
   that remains an inference.
3. **Natural activations.** The synthetic standard-normal control is right for
   comparing variants on identical operands; it is not a real activation range.
4. **A correct per-step model of the forward pass** before anyone converts these
   per-tensor numbers into a prefill share. The failed scaling above is the
   evidence that the naive model is wrong.

The promotion question is unchanged: the output is not bit-identical, so it
needs the `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` production gate plus a registered strict
fallback. That gate has not been run.

Task #3 remains open.
