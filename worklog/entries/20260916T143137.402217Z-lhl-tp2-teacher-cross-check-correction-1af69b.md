# Correction: teacher bit-identity was asserted without a direct comparison

## Summary

The previous unit (`ac5066de4`) claimed the two TP1 teacher controls were
"bit-identical to each other" on the strength of their **identical aggregate
metrics** against the shared TP2 student. That inference is invalid: equal
aggregate KL/top-1 numbers do not prove the underlying logit arrays are equal
(for example, a softmax-invariant shift leaves every aggregate identical while
the bytes differ). The retained artifact
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-16-w7900-tp2-teacher-coverage-broad.json` stores only
aggregate summaries and per-row hashes of the **student**, and the raw teacher
arrays were not saved, so bit-identity cannot be proven from the retained
evidence. The claim is withdrawn here and in the prose; the harness now measures
it directly for future runs.

This entry corrects `20260916T142717.674895Z-lhl-tp2-teacher-controls-device-ownership-1ffd16.md`;
that committed entry is immutable and is not amended.

## Changes

- `scripts/tp2_teacher_coverage_broad.py`
  - `_trajectory_digest(rows)` — order-sensitive sha256 over the exact float32
    bytes of a trajectory, length-prefixed per row.
  - `cross_teacher_check(a, b, ...)` — direct control-vs-control comparison:
    validates shapes against expected positions/vocab, rejects non-finite rows,
    reports `byte_identical`, `differing_rows`, `max_abs_diff`, per-side digests,
    and the cross-teacher KL/top-1 envelope.
  - `evaluate_gates(..., teacher_cross_check=...)` — fail-closed: missing
    cross-check, shape mismatch, non-finite rows, cross-teacher envelope breach,
    or `byte_identical is False` all fail the run. Bit-identity is now a measured
    gate, never inferred from aggregates.
  - `main()` computes and records `teacher_cross_check` in the artifact.
- `tests/test_unit_scripts_tp2_teacher_harness.py` — 7 new tests (60 total):
  - `test_cross_teacher_check_proves_byte_equality`
  - `test_equal_aggregates_do_not_prove_byte_equality` — two teachers whose
    softmax is bit-identical (one entry far below the max, underflowing to 0)
    give **exactly equal** `score_arm` aggregates against the student while
    `cross_teacher_check` reports `byte_identical=False`.
  - `test_cross_teacher_check_rejects_shape_and_nonfinite`
  - `test_evaluate_gates_requires_teacher_cross_check`
  - `test_evaluate_gates_rejects_non_byte_identical_controls`
  - `test_evaluate_gates_rejects_cross_check_nonfinite`
  - `test_coverage_main_fails_when_controls_differ` — end-to-end `main()` with
    softmax-shifted rank-1 teacher: both per-control envelopes pass at mean KL
    0.0 yet the run fails on `not byte-identical`.
- `benchmarks/README.md`
  - Removed "the two TP1 controls agree bit-identically"; now states the
    aggregate metrics agree exactly and that this artifact records no direct
    byte comparison, so bit-identity is not asserted.
  - Marked the `~2.2 ms/token` TP1 timing denominators unqualified at the point
    of claim: they predate the 2026-09-16 device-ownership fix, and the
    teacher-forced correctness rerun does not re-measure timings.
- `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`
  - Corrected the 2026-09-16 entry (bit-identity not asserted; harness now
    measures it; timings not requalified).
  - Annotated the 2026-09-15 compiled-exchange entry where it claimed "both TP1

  - Named the new run's `model sha256` (full-file hash) distinctly from the older
