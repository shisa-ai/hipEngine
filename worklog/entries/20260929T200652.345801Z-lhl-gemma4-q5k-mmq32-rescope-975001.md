# P3 scope correction: MMQ32 is the real leaf

## Summary

The previous entry
(``20260929T200423.614760Z-lhl-gemma4-q5k-gateup-rowbatch-owner-fd59e9.md``) is
superseded: it added a Q5_K ``expertgrid64`` grouped-rowbatch owner on the belief
that layer 29's Q5_K gate_up had no grouped owner. It did have one, and that was
never the problem.

Three corrections, each verified:

1. **Gate_up is not missing an owner.** ``gemma4_project_experts_grouped_dual``
   (``gemma4_experts.py:1149``) selects from
   ``[_GROUPED_DUAL_AMORTIZED_PREFILL_VARIANT, _GROUPED_DUAL_PREFILL_VARIANT]``.
   Running that exact loop for ``quant_key="gguf_q5_k"`` hits
   ``selected_dual_grouped_rowbatch8_out4_amortized_bf16_bf16_out``, registered
   all along. P3's own row agrees: gate_up *runs*
   ``gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_grouped_rowbatch`` at 21.9 ms / 1024.
2. **P3 is a performance gap, not a missing leaf on that axis.** The row's
   evidence is 21.9 ms against llama.cpp's 2.6 ms through ordinary MMQ.
   ``gemma4_project_experts_mmq_dual`` measures its int8 leaf at 2.07x the BF16
   WMMA owner (2.00x even paying the packing cost).
3. **The genuine missing leaf is the MMQ32 tile loader, on the axis I first
   tested.** ``_mmq_dual_route`` rejects everything but
   ``_MMQ_DUAL_QUANT_KEY = "gguf_q4_k"``. After importing
   ``gguf_q4_k_q8_1_selected_prefill``,
   ``(hip_gfx1100, moe_linear, gguf_q4_k, selected_dual_q8_1_ds4_mmq32_prefill_compact32_bf16_bf16_out)``
   resolves, and the identical ``gguf_q5_k`` key raises ``MissingKernelError``.

The interim retarget of the RED test to ``expertgrid64`` (iteration 91) was a
false negative: registration is import-time and lazy, and ``gguf_q4_k_q8_1_selected_prefill``
was never imported, so ``resolve`` reported nothing for a key that existed. This
is the third time that trap produced a wrong conclusion in this campaign.
