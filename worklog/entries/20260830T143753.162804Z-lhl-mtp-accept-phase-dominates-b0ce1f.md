# MTP cycle breakdown: the accept phase, not the verifier, inflates with width

## Summary

Task #17 was framed as "fix per-row MTP verify cost so K3 stops plateauing". The per-cycle
telemetry in
`/tmp/he-parity/he-d24.json` says the verifier is **not** the phase that inflates with width. Per
lane-cycle, the MTP cycle decomposes as (ms):

| C | proposal | target | provider_upd | **accept** | sel_commit | readback | sum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5.2 | 21.6 | 1.2 | **30.6** | 0.4 | 0.0 | 59.0 |
| 2 | 3.8 | 28.7 | 4.3 | 61.9 | 0.9 | 0.0 | 99.6 |
| 3 | 4.1 | 48.0 | 4.5 | 65.2 | 1.3 | 0.0 | 123.2 |
| 4 | 4.5 | 34.3 | 4.8 | **104.8** | 1.8 | 0.0 | 150.2 |
| 5 | 4.6 | 31.5 | 4.0 | 90.7 | 1.5 | 0.0 | 132.3 |
| 6 | 4.4 | 33.3 | 4.7 | 90.4 | 1.5 | 0.0 | 134.3 |
| 7 | 4.5 | 41.1 | 4.7 | 87.6 | 1.6 | 0.0 | 139.6 |
| 8 | 4.7 | 35.5 | 5.6 | **105.1** | 2.2 | 0.0 | 153.1 |

Growth C1 to C8: **accept 3.44x**, target 1.64x, provider_update 4.7x but only 5.6 ms absolute,
proposal 5.2 to 4.7 ms (flat), selected_commit 0.4 to 2.2 ms, candidate readback 0.0 throughout. So
accept is 52% of the accounted cycle at C1 and 69% at C8, and 2.96x the target phase by C8.

## Changes

- No code change. This redirects what #17 should optimize, before GPU time is spent on the
  wrong phase.

## Finding

- `accept` is host-side work, not a kernel. The timers are `accept_started` at
  `hipengine/generation/qwen35_gguf_mtp2.py:2842` and `:2967`, closed at `:2891` and `:3000`, and
  accumulated per row at `:3097` (`row.mtp2_accept_ms`). The window spans loops over `rows` and
  `states`, a `cancelled_request_ids()` call, and
  `_commit_deferred_packed_verify_states_batch_device`. Per-row host work per cycle is exactly what
  produces near-linear growth with resident lanes, which is the `w^0.76` exponent measured in
  20260830T143605….
- Corroborating shape evidence inside the same packet: at width 8 the MTP `groups[0]` record reports
  `logical_c: 1`, `group_count: 1`, `physical_slot_extent: 1`, `physical_rows: 1`,
  `active_rows: 1` and
  a single `request_ids` entry, with `provider_groups: 0`. The MTP cycle is therefore organized one
  logical lane at a time even though the server is holding eight lanes.
- The target phase behaves correctly: 35.5 ms per lane-cycle at C8 with 1.64x growth over 8
  lanes. That
  is a batched verify. Optimizing the verifier kernel would be aimed at 23% of the accounted
  cycle while
  leaving the 69% phase untouched - which is why this entry exists.
- Honesty constraint on these numbers: the phase totals do **not** partition wall time. Their sum is
  153.1 ms per lane-cycle at C8 while the wall-derived figure from 20260830T143605… is 61.6
  ms, a 2.5x
  overshoot, because phases are accumulated per row on the host and overlap each other and
  the device
  (accept plausibly absorbs waiting on other lanes). Use these numbers for **shares and
  growth factors**,
  never as absolute rates or as a budget. The rates in 20260830T143605… come from
  `wall_seconds` and stay
  authoritative.
