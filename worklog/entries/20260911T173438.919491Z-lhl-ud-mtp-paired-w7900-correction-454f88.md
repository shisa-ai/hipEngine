# Correction: the W7900 UD decode gap is GPU-side in two local32 kernels, not eager-protocol host overhead

## Summary

The paired-measurement entry
[`20260911T173154.215773Z-lhl-ud-mtp-paired-w7900-76a4c6.md`](20260911T173154.215773Z-lhl-ud-mtp-paired-w7900-76a4c6.md)
attributed the W7900's ~2x UD-vs-plain AR gap to "host-side per-launch overhead
exposed by eager decode", and inferred that the UD MTP ratios were inflated
relative to a graph-replay protocol. **That attribution is wrong and is
withdrawn here.** The measurement it came from is unchanged and still stands.

## What refuted it

The W7900 is slow on UD decode **under graph replay too**, where there is no
host work in the measurement. The campaign's own censuses, which the earlier
entry cited selectively, contain a W7900 arm:

| census | device | launches/token | pure us/token |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `wudkm` | W7900 UD-Q4_K_M | 831.2 | **46,913** |
| `ud-k_m` | XTX UD-Q4_K_M | 831.2 | **30,263** |
| `wudks` | W7900 UD-Q4_K_S | 839.0 | 34,428 |
| `plainkm` | XTX plain Q4_K_M | 796.3 | 30,478 |

Identical launch counts, 1.55x pure-kernel gap on UD only. Files:
`benchmarks/results/final-decode-campaign-2026-09-11/{wudkm,wudks,ud-k_m,plainkm}-census.json`.

## Where the gap actually is

Per-kernel, W7900 vs XTX (us/token):

| kernel | W7900 | XTX | ratio | launches/token |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `q5_k_t16_dense_single_local32_gemv_kernel` | 13,380.2 | 3,485.7 | **3.84x** | 98.8 |
| `gguf_iq4_xs_local32_gemv_kernel` | 8,120.2 | 2,436.1 | **3.33x** | 33.9 |
| `q4_k_t16_dense_single_local32_gemv_kernel` | 5,310.6 | 4,370.1 | 1.22x | 99.8 |
| `gguf_iq4_xs_local32_dual_silu_kernel<2>` | 3,237.4 | 2,843.5 | 1.14x | 19.4 |
| `qwen35_paged_full_attn_decode_context_tensor` | 2,534.2 | 2,315.1 | 1.09x | 15.5 |

Every other kernel is 1.06-1.23x, which is ordinary device variation and also
**excludes GPU contention**: contention would inflate the whole census, not two
kernels. Those two kernels alone carry ~94% of the 16,650 us/token total gap
(9,894 + 5,684 of 16,650).

## Consequence for the paired MTP numbers

The paired ratios themselves are unaffected — they are within-arm and each arm's
AR and MTP ran in the same session on the same device. What changes is the
interpretation:

- The UD MTP ratios are **not** inflated by host-overhead amortisation. UD's AR
  baseline is slow because two GEMV kernels are 3.3-3.8x slower on this device,
  which is a GPU cost that MTP also pays.
- Why the UD MTP ratio (2.00x) exceeds the plain ratio (1.72x) is therefore
  **not established** by the withdrawn argument. It is an open question.
- Nothing in the measurement section of the paired entry is retracted.
