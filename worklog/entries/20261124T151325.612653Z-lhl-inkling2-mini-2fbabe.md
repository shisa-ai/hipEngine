# Dispatch/bandwidth ceiling probe on the W7900 dev lane

## Summary

Measured the launch/dispatch side of the Inkling 2 GGUF execution plan on the
W7900 dev lane with a new self-contained probe, `scripts/inkling2_dispatch_probe.py`
(hipcc-built no-op kernel; in-tree `gguf_q8_0_gemv_f32_f32_out` for the
weight-streaming ceiling). Nothing here ran the model. Numbers below are from
`--output` captures on 2026-11-24 (epyc, W7900, GPU at idle clocks at the
start of each run; treat absolute numbers as lower bounds and the run-to-run
spread as large on the small shapes).

Launch-chain (dependent no-op launches from Python, default stream):

- 48 launches: ~6.0 us/launch (median total 0.29 ms)
- 1,300 launches: 5.5-5.6 us/launch (median total 7.2 ms)
- 2,000 launches: 5.5-5.6 us/launch (median total 11.2 ms)
- An earlier run measured 3.7-4.9 us/launch; spread across runs is ~1.5x.
- Captured as one HIP graph and replayed, the same chains give ~5.6 us/launch
  (1,300 and 2,000) - no speedup over eager on this part (~1.0x). One earlier
  run showed eager ~5 us vs graph ~2.8-3.3 us (1.3-1.5x); reproducibility of
  that delta is unproven. Treat graph capture as launch-neutral here until
  remeasured with locked clocks / under sustained load.
- hipEventRecord + hipEventSynchronize: ~18 us (idle stream).
- hipStreamSynchronize on an idle stream: ~0.5 us.

Interpretation: the plan's ~1,300-launch prefill figure x ~5.5 us is ~7 ms of
pure dispatch per token-step on this host - dispatch is a first-order term,
not a rounding error. Graph capture is not a free fix on gfx1100/ROCm 7.2.

Q8_0 GEMV (rows=1, `gguf_q8_0_gemv_f32_f32_out`, 30-iteration warmup then 50
reps; GB/s = weight bytes / time):

- 2304x2304: 5.64 MB weights, 2.0-2.6e-05 s/call, 221-295 GB/s (L2-resident; not a DRAM figure)
- 3072x3072: 10.0 MB weights, 3.9-4.4e-05 s/call, 229-256 GB/s (also L2-resident)
- 2304x200704 (vocab-head shape): 491 MB weights, ~1.60 ms/call, ~307 GB/s (exceeds L2; DRAM-side)

The 200704-wide head is the only shape too big for L2 and shows ~307 GB/s
against a 576 GB/s peak (~53%). The two smaller shapes are L2-resident and
their numbers reflect cache residency, not DRAM bandwidth.

Not measured / open:

- The pm4/aql submission transports in `hipengine/core/pm4/` were not exercised;
  the plan calls them out as unmeasured. They are graph-submission paths, not a
  drop-in eager-launch replacement, and were out of scope for this probe.
- Clocks were not locked (no root assumption); idle GPU at measurement start.
- This says nothing about Strix Halo, where the plan expects ~8-11x faster
  launch overhead; gfx1151 numbers must be measured there.
- Q8_0 head bandwidth is kernel-dependent; other kernels may do better. This
  bounds what the current in-tree kernel achieves, nothing more.
