# Screen paired output rows in Inkling NVFP4 prefill

## Summary

Reusing activation loads for adjacent output rows improved the grouped NVFP4 projection on both GPUs at full Inkling expert width. Narrow synthetic outputs were mixed or slower. Every result matched the unrolled one-output-row kernel bit-for-bit.

## Changes

- Scratch only. Compute two adjacent output rows per wave, each with its own weights, accumulator and lane reduction. Share the activation loads and mask the second store for an odd final row. Keep the shipped four-way block-loop unroll and route layout in both arms.
