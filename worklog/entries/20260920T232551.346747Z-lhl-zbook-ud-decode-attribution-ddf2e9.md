# zbook UD vs plain Q4_K_M decode kernel attribution and the gfx1151 dense-IQ policy gap

## Summary

Task #5 measured UD-Q4_K_M decode 32-33% behind plain Q4_K_M on zbook and left the cause
unattributed. This unit attributes it by rocprofv3 kernel census and reaches a single
decision: the lever is the **undeclared dense-IQ decode policy on `hip_gfx1151`**, not the
strict kernel's inner loop and not the UD artifact.

UD spends **61.64 ms/token, 53.42% of its 115.39 ms pure decode, in
`gguf_iq_dense_strict_kernel`** across four compile-time templates, at 130.78
launches/token. The dominant template runs at **442.1 us/launch**. Plain has **zero**
launches in this family. The second-ranked item is the Q5_K resident direct GEMV at +20.22
ms/token and +84.3 launches/token; third is Q4_K single local32 at +7.21 ms/token.

The mechanism is a one-constant gap, not a port. `_iq_dense_decode_dispatch`
(`hipengine/runtime/gguf_linear.py:8151-8184`) reads
`backend_package_capability(backend, "GGUF_IQ_DENSE_DECODE_POLICY", {})` and returns the
original dispatch unchanged when the quant has no entry. `hip_gfx1100` declares that
constant with all six dense-IQ quants mapped to `local32_gemv_bf16_bf16_out`; `hip_gfx1151`
declares it nowhere, so `backend_package_capability` hands back the `{}` default. The
local32 owners are **already registered on `hip_gfx1151`** — `KernelKey(hip_gfx1151,
linear, gguf_iq4_xs, local32_gemv_bf16_bf16_out)` and its five siblings all resolve, as
does `local32_pair_silu_bf16_bf16_out`. Nothing needs writing; the policy needs declaring.

The decision this entry records is **route the family, do not tune it**. No routing policy
was changed in this unit, so no post-change rate is claimed.

A quality question gates that work and is recorded here rather than left implicit:
`hip_gfx1100` declares `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_PREFILL_STRICT_SLOTS` and
`GGUF_IQ_DENSE_DECODE_STRICT_SLOTS`, which pin specific UD-Q4_K_M slots back to the strict
GEMV because the fast owners' accumulation-order tail breached the calibrated max-row
ceiling on the tokenized category suite. `hip_gfx1151` declares neither table. Enabling the
decode policy there without applying those pins would admit exactly the route gfx1100 had
to pin.

Artifact: `benchmarks/results/2026-09-21-zbook-ud-vs-plain-q4km-decode-attribution.json`
(`performance_claim: false` — it sizes a lever, it does not assert a post-change rate).

## Validation

**Protocol.** `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` over `graph` mode of the fixed driver: 512-token
prefill, 4 eager warm steps, graph capture, 8 warm replays, a 0.5 s GPU idle gap, then 32
measured graph-replay steps. The census windows the 32 trailing `advance_decode_position`
occurrences, so graph-launch plumbing is excluded and only the graph's own kernels are
counted. Pure-kernel totals sum per-kernel device time; wall time is measured separately by
an unprofiled warm run of the same driver.

**Harness agreement.** The unprofiled warm run of this driver measures **11.669 tok/s**
(plain) and **8.085** (UD). The task #5 sweep measured 11.635 and 7.877 at the same 512/128
shape. The profiler protocol reproduces the baseline arms to 0.3% and 2.6%, so the census
describes the same execution the task #5 row measured.

**JIT policy.** The warm build ran outside the profiler against a pinned
`HIPENGINE_COMPILER_VERSION_FILE`; the profiled run then required the cached artifacts and
spawned no `hipcc`/`clang`. Both arms are cache hits, so neither census contains compile
time.

**Provenance.** Both traces have recorded sha256s. Both arms ran on zbook
(`machine_id 87c566d30a5645cf8d12ed7ef6b6e1e8`, gfx1151 / Radeon 8060S), the same lane as
the task #5 pair. rocprofv3 1.3.5.

**What the numbers are.** Launch counts are per decode token. The census is a pure-kernel
attribution with full provenance; it is a diagnostic, not a benchmark row, and
`performance_claim` is false.

## Next

1. **Land the gfx1151 dense-IQ decode policy** — the dominant item, 53% of UD's pure decode.
   The kernels are registered; the work is the constant plus its correctness gate. Two
   things must be settled first, both recorded in the artifact:
   - **The strict-slot pins.** `hip_gfx1100` needed
     `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_DECODE_STRICT_SLOTS[("MOSTLY_Q4_K_M", "gguf_ud_q4_k_m")]` (four slots)
     and `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_PREFILL_STRICT_SLOTS[...] = ("layers.0.ffn_up",)` because the
     unpinned route breached the calibrated max-row ceiling. `hip_gfx1151` has neither
     table. Whether the same pins transfer, or whether gfx1151's different prefill variant
     assignment makes the prefill pin unnecessary, is an open question — not an established
     fact. `hip_gfx1151`'s `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_PREFILL_POLICY` puts all seven quants on the
     one-wave owner while gfx1100 refines four to coop32/coop64, so gfx1151 may legitimately
     not run the Q3_K hi+lo-split path the prefill pin exists for. Verify, then decide.
   - **The production-referenced accuracy gate.** Admitting a local32 owner requires it. The
     gfx1100 record for this owner is 2.5-2.9x the strict GEMV per launch on every real
     shape of this artifact, and 22.76 vs 17.57 tok/s end-to-end with the route enabled
     (2026-09-09, W7900). **That factor is gfx1100 evidence and must not be transferred as a
     gfx1151 rate.**
   `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_VERIFY_POLICY` is likewise undeclared on gfx1151, so MTP verifier rows 2-4
   take the strict path too; no verifier arm was profiled here.
2. **The Q5_K role spread** — +20.22 ms/token, +84.3 launches/token. UD's 131 Q5_K tensors
   span 9 slot roles and 7 shapes, where plain's 48 are all one shape. About 44 take
   `q5_k_t16_dense_tile8_gemv_kernel` at 110 us/launch; about 84 take
   `qk_t16_selected_direct_gemv_kernel<u16,5>` at 240 us/launch. Whether the slower owner is
   *required* by those roles or is a selection gap is open — the same "why is it ~2.3x"
   study the 2026-09-11 campaign left open on gfx1100.
3. **Fused duals on the UD path** — plain runs
   `q4_k_t16_dense_dual_local32_silu_gemv_kernel` at 62.0 launches/token for 32.40 ms/token;
   UD runs no fused dual at all and pays `silu_mul_separate_out_kernel` (62.0 launches/token)
   plus a separate residual add (62.0 launches/token). A
   `q5_k_t16_dense_dual_silu_gemv_kernel` exists in
   `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_t16_selected_gemv.hip` and is documented
   bit-exact with the unfused chain; whether UD's gate/up pairs can use it, or the registered
   `local32_pair_silu_bf16_bf16_out` for IQ4_XS, was not tested.
4. **Do not re-derive this.** The attribution table is in the artifact; the family deltas are
   additive and reconcile to the measured wall time.
