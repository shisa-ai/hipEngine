# R13 ladder step 1: two-plane MoE gate/up iu8 - NEGATIVE screen (T1 drift + repair explosion)

## Screen (in-situ, off/on/off/on per case, production runner, env Q4_IU8_PLANES)

| case | p3 warm prefill | p2 warm prefill | speedup | exactness |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| code-p512 | 1719 ms | 1665-1685 ms | ~2.1% | max dlogit 0.968 (NOT bit-exact) |
| code-p4096 | 14562 ms | 14245 ms | ~2.2% | max dlogit 1.698 (NOT bit-exact) |

Both arms repeat-deterministic. The 2-plane variant flags more rows for
the sparse exact repair, and the repair volume grows enough to consume
most of the WMMA saving (expected ~5.4% prefill at constant repair;
measured ~2.2%). Worse, the output is NOT bit-exact: the Kahan-bound
risk criterion was calibrated for the three-plane residual chain, and
two-plane escapes fall below the flag threshold - the same failure mode
the original flip screens guarded against at multiplier 4.0.

**Verdict: rejected at the screen.** T1 drift with ~2% gain does not
justify envelope qualification (R12's dense-legs precedent shows the
accumulated drift lands well above the bars). The kernel entry stays
default-off (HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q4_IU8_PLANES=2) with this negative
recorded.

## Consequence for the R13 ladder

The iu8 chain's repair makes plane-count reduction self-defeating: the
exactness machinery that makes 3-plane T0 is what a 2-plane variant
needs more of. The remaining R13 levers, re-ranked by expected value:

1. **Small-expert fast path** (the 32.75% padding waste): inherently T1 -
   WMMA cannot pack different experts into one A-fragment (B differs
   per expert), and sub-16 tiles cannot reduce WMMA work below the
   16-row fragment, so only a dp4a/scalar sub-16 path recovers it.
   Needs its own flip screen + envelope if promoted.
2. **Legacy-compact layers (964 ms)**: the P2 stop boundary left layers
   0-26 on the legacy kernel after measured screen failures - recorded
   negative, do not retry unchanged.
3. **Router (617 ms at ~0.84 TF/s per layer-chunk)**: the P3
   counter-last projection+top-10 fusion was rejected for prefill speed
   (0.882x) - do not retry unchanged; a different mechanism (e.g.
   MMQ-owned router projection at the 512-wide N) would be a new
   bounded screen.
4. Repair elimination and plane reduction both require admitting
   bounded-drift arithmetic through the production gates first - the
   same policy decision R12 surfaced.
