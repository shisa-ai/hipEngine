# R13 closure: the 6x MoE gap is exactness-structural; remaining levers are analytically negative or policy-blocked

## Summary

R13's mechanism analysis closes the 6x MoE prefill gap as **exactness-structural**.
Pair-share by expert-count bucket (10.81M pairs, 1,152 captures) shows c<=16
experts hold only 12.9% of pairs (c<=2: 0.9%) and contribute at most 15.6% of total
padded gate/up rows, so half of the 32.75% padding waste lives in the 17-63 range
as tail-tile residue - structurally locked behind WMMA's 16-row fragment
granularity plus per-expert B (different experts cannot share an A-fragment).

The dp4a small-expert path is **rejected analytically**: eliminating ALL c<=16
padding saves <=15.6% of gate/up row-work, while the R11 warp256 dp4a gate/up runs
at ~1.16 TF/s versus this kernel's ~16.2 TF/s WMMA (~14x), a large net loss at any
cutoff. A fully T0-optimized MoE block lands around 4.0-4.3 s, still ~4x off the
external 1.0 s; the remaining ~3x is the exactness chain, and every measured
attempt to relax it (decode pair R11, dense MMQ R12, MoE iu8 R13) fails the
calibrated envelope by 1.6-3x over the mean bar.

contributes at most 15.6% of total padded gate/up rows. The other half
of the 32.75% padding waste lives in the 17-63 range as tail-tile
residue - structurally locked behind WMMA's 16-row fragment granularity
plus per-expert B (different experts cannot share an A-fragment).

**dp4a small-expert path: rejected analytically.** Eliminating ALL

## Disposition

R13's engineering ladder is exhausted at the T0 boundary. The decision
now required is policy, not engineering: either (a) a model-class
recalibration of the 6.1 envelope (the strict-referenced measurement
already shows the seven-promotion production stack exceeds the frozen
bars on this model - routed separately since the R11 window), or (b) an
explicit bounded-drift admission path with per-route envelopes. Until
one is chosen, further MoE kernel work has no admissible target.

Screen scripts, distribution artifacts, and the negative records are
committed; the p2 kernel entry stays default-off.
