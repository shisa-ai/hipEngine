# Qwen4Exp fast profile small-matrix F16 GEMM for indexer and router

## Summary

A new F16-operand WMMA GEMM for small uncompressed-weight projections takes
the `fast` profile from 684.8 / 754.4 / 754.7 to **707.3 / 811.4 / 825.0
tok/s** prefill at p512/p1024/p4096 on the canonical 12-case protocol
(Radeon 8060S / gfx1151, `UD-Q4_K_XL`, chunk 4096), 12/12 cases positive,
decode unchanged. That is 2.31x / 2.46x / 2.46x the same boot's `production`
run.

## Decisions

- **A new in-tree kernel, derived from `dense_prefill_wmma_out_kernel`.**
  The indexer projections (BF16 weights) and the router (FP32 weights) ran
  4096-row products through scalar FP32 kernels at well under 1 TFLOP/s.
  `dense_f16_gemm_f32_out_kernel` keeps the same LDS staging and fragment
  layout, takes FP32 activations and FP32 or BF16 weights, writes FP32, and
  accepts any output width.
- **Indexer first, then router.** Indexer alone: 707.8 / 788.5 / 794.2, with
  generated tokens unchanged on the two cases checked. Router added: neutral
  at p512, +2.9% at p1024, +3.9% at p4096. The router rounds its operands to
  F16, so expert selection can change on near-ties; the 18-prompt battery
  moved from 12/18 to 13/18 strict-exact with top-1 agreement still 1.00.
- **96-column floor.** The 4-column hyper-connection inject is much slower
  on a 128-column WMMA tile at 512 rows (682 to 592 tok/s), and the
  48-column GDN gate projections are mixed (+1.5% at p4096, -1.8% at p512),
  so both stay on the scalar GEMV. A narrower column tile is the follow-up.
- **Dispatch cache key.** The linear launcher caches resolved dispatches;
  the new selector is part of the key so a bind-time change cannot serve a
  stale dispatch (the first sweep measured no change for exactly that
  reason).

## Measured

Commands, per-case rows, the column-floor sweep, quality score and
attribution are in
`benchmarks/results/2026-10-13-qwen4exp-fast-indexer-f16-gemm/README.md`.

- Quality against the strict teacher: 13/18 strict-exact, aligned top-1
  1.00, top-20-proxy mean KL 0.056, max 1.22.
- `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace`, code-p4096, indexer-only composition, per
  prefill: kernel total 5145 ms; attention 1167, dense 1281, routed experts
  698, GDN 587 plus 155, router and reduce 448, hyper-connection elementwise
  about 580. The new kernel ran 24 times for 30.3 ms.
