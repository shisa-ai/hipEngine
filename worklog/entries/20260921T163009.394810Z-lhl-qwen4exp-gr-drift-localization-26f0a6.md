# Hyper-connection drift localized: the production gate is a cliff at 5e-9 prefill KL

## Summary

The GR (hyper-connection) family's model-visible drift is localized by stage and
magnitude, and the resulting decision re-targets the arithmetic route instead of
closing it. **Stage:** the Q8_0 projection boundary, and nothing else - on actual
model-produced inputs the strict projection plus the standalone sigmoid and
gated-mean kernels reproduces the fused production output bit-exactly in all six
GR-up captures, so the epilogue is not a drift source. **Magnitude:** 1.12e-7 to
3.69e-7 relative L2 (1-3 fp32 ulps), max absolute difference below 5e-4 over the
full arrays. **Propagation:** the gate outcome is a *cliff* at a prefill-boundary
logits KL of about 5e-9, not a gradient. Across five measured routes the
prefill-boundary KL spans 9.8x while the gate mean KL spans only 4.25x, because
everything above the cliff saturates at 1.31e-3 - 1.61e-3. The decisive pair:
GR-down + compensated accumulation improved the prefill-boundary KL **5.1x**
(4.32e-8 -> 8.48e-9) and the gate metric did not move at all (mean KL 1.310e-3 ->
1.316e-3, top-1 identical at 769/780). **Decision:** the arithmetic route is
re-targeted to a cliff-crossing screen - the prefill-boundary KL, measured with
one prefill per arm and no decode - where the best candidate sits 1.9x above the
passing bracket, so a further ~2x reduction is the concrete next step. FP64 MSE
is an unreliable proxy for that screen (it improved 16.9-67.4x in one correction
whose screen moved 5.1x better, and 1.76-4.60x in another whose screen moved
1.15x *worse*). Fusion/tiling is retained as the path that needs no numerical
gate, since the production default is already bit-exact against the strict chain.
