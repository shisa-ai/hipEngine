### Measured corrections to the reviewer's Nov 9-10 estimates

The reviewer re-ran nothing; their projections were estimates. Three were tested this session:

1. **P0.1 (EAGLE Q8) - confirmed and shipped.** Reviewer projected 18-19 tok/s; measured 17.99 (n2) then 18.61 (n3). Their "3 drafts will probably win" prediction held post-Q8.
2. **P0.2 (DSpine refine "cheap +13%") - optimistic.** The cheap part (verified-rows-only) nets only **+1.4%** (noise). Profiling showed the **host chain walk was 50% of the refine** (25.7 ms), not the head projection as the reviewer assumed; rows-only fixes that. The **bandwidth-bound head projection (~18 ms, 2.5 GB BF16 at ~139 GB/s, ~54% of peak) dominates** and does not shrink with rows. The real ~+10% lever is Q8-ing the head, which is **kernel work**: the Q8 T16 WMMA prefill (`gguf_q8_0_t16_prefill.hip`, `..._wmma_nwave_kernel`) accumulates f32 but hardcodes bf16 stores + f16 inputs; a f32-out variant needs a new instantiation + repair-logic check + OPTIMIZATION.md gates. Alternative: reuse the target's resident quantized lm_head (also removes the 5 GB host copy, P1.7).
3. **P1.4 (verify per-row) - correction accepted.** The earlier campaign conclusion "verify is at its bandwidth floor" (`aec0fce7d`) was too strong. The reviewer's argument: ~13 ms per extra verified row (AR 94 ms/1 row -> 132 ms/4 rows) implies **per-row compute**, not bandwidth amortization (a bandwidth-bound kernel reuses weights across rows, so extra rows would be nearly free). This **reopens the shared verify ceiling**. Needs per-kernel profiling at 1/2/3/4/6/8 rows to confirm; reconcile with `aec0fce7d`'s "8 rows ~ 6 rows" observation.
