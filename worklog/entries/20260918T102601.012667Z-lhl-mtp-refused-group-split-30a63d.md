# Refused MTP due groups: the one-row-cycle split loses even at perfect depth-3 acceptance

## Summary

Task #3 asked whether a *targeted* split of a refused due group (only when a row
crosses the provider's 1023-token window or fails its target profile) keeps the
win that a blanket split was measured to lose at C5-C8 (`0.74-0.80x` of own AR,
`20260831T053000`). Measured on gfx1151 with two protocols, three arms, and an
AR control per concurrency:

- **The split loses at c=2 and c=4, and loses more than the whole-group AR route
  it replaces.** pair protocol: MTP output share rises 1.8% -> 69.3% (c=2) and
  2.1% -> 73.9% (c=4), while decode falls 17.08 -> 14.17 and 18.63 -> 14.65 tok/s
  end to end, and 21.33 -> 17.51 / 24.68 -> 16.94 excluding TTFT.
- **Acceptance is not the limit.** The healthy row in the c=2 pair accepted depth
  3 on all 162 of its cycles (318 accepted drafts, 2.96 emitted tokens per cycle,
  99.8% MTP coverage) and still cost **111.1 ms per token against 92.1 ms** for
  the same row in the whole-group AR arm, and 91.8 ms for the pure-AR control. At
  c=4 the same row cost 117.6 ms against 92.7 ms. A one-row cycle and the
  co-resident row's AR step serialize inside the tick, so the split replaces one
  batched AR decode per tick with N provider cycles plus N AR steps and cannot
  amortize the cycle's fixed cost.
- **The targeted variant is the same mechanism over a shorter window, and it is
  measured too.** The split's post-crossing window *is* the window a targeted
  split would open (healthy row cycling beside the refused row's AR steps): in it
  the healthy row decoded at 9.0 tok/s against 10.9 tok/s when the group stayed
  autoregressive. So the decision is to keep the current coupling, not to
  narrow it.
- **Separate, larger finding: the route's own cost is prefill.** Both
  speculative arms sit ~17% below their AR controls on these long-prompt
  protocols because the speculative route's TTFT is 3.4x the AR arm's (11.9-13.0
  against 3.5 s on ~840-token prompts). A refused speculative row pays provider
  activation during prefill and then decodes at the AR rate; that, not grouping,
  is the lever that matters for coverage-economics on this model.

## Changes

- `hipengine/generation/engine_loop.py`: a refused due group keeps its shipped
  route (one batched AR decode). `_compatible_speculative_subset` names the rows
  a decomposition would serve, `_run_split_refused_group` serves them as one-row
  cycles with the refused rows autoregressive in the same tick, and
  `_record_refused_reasons` carries each refused row's own plan reason to its AR
  emission. The split route is behind `HIPENGINE_SPEC_MTP_SPLIT_REFUSED_GROUPS`
  (default `0`), so the shipped behavior is unchanged and the rejected arm stays
  reproducible.
- `docs/REFACTOR.md`: the flag's removal condition and the measurement that
  rejected it.
- `tests/test_unit_specdec2_engine_loop.py`: the refused group stays
  autoregressive by default; the flag serves every capable row separately; a row
  the provider refuses on its own still decodes autoregressively.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-18-gfx1151-qwen38-mtp-refused-group-split-rejected.json`.
