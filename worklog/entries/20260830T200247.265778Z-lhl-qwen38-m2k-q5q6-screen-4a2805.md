# M2k Q5/Q6 verify-owner screens find no promotion

## Summary

M2i named ~65 ms per R16 verify pass in Q5/Q6 rowtile owners, so the M2
"Q4-owners-above-R12" reopening was extended one quant up: a sibling screen
(`.worklog/tmp_m2k_owner_bench.py`, synthetic actual-layout repacks, median
of 25, failing-closed within bit-exact families) compared the admitted
Q5/Q6/Q6-planar rowtile owners against every WMMA sibling at rows 2-16 on
the three verify shapes.

No strict promotion exists and the retained owners win where they run:

- Q5 (6144,5120): rows <= 8 the admitted col8 rowtile wins outright
  (0.178 ms vs low-VGPR WMMA 0.227). True-R16 col8 (current, 0.319 ms) loses
  to `wmma_prefill_lowvgpr` (0.226 ms) but the WMMA family is
  association-different (max abs 4.0), so it is production-profile-only for a
  ~3.6 ms/pass saving (<1% of the C8 cycle). Not worth an E-series gate now;
  recorded as a reopen candidate if a production-numerics unit is ever run for
  other reasons.
- Q6 standard (5120,10240): rows 2-4 the bit-exact col8 rowtile wins; rows
  8-16 plain WMMA is 1.1-1.15x faster (0.347 vs 0.365-0.391 ms) but again
  association-different for a ~1-2 ms/pass saving. Rows 12-16 currently chunk
  R8+R4/R8+R8 - the TRUE_ROWTILE R12 lane (M1's E2 screen) already covers the
  bit-exact route.
- Q6 planar (17408,5120)/(5120,1024): the admitted col8 rowtile wins at rows
  2-8 (0.36/0.04 ms vs >=0.43 for every WMMA sibling); rows 12/16 have no
  current-owner single sweep in the harness (chunked policy), and plain
  low-VGPR WMMA (0.81/0.13 ms) loses to the chunked planar route (19.2 ms /
  58 launches ~0.33 ms each).

Conclusion: the M2j low-VGPR effect was specific to the Q4 WMMA family
(smallm one-row-tile was the loser); Q5/Q6 R2-16 owners are already the
measured winners within the bit-exact family, and the association-different
WMMA alternatives save <5 ms/pass combined. M2's remaining C4-C8 gap lives
elsewhere (proposal residue, accept-interval Q4 share now 40%, and the
C5-C8 split-cycle economics M5 owns).
