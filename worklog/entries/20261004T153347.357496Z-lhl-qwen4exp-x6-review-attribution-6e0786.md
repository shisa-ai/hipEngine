The signature is a hash of every mismatch row's `(request_id, scenario_step, kl)` triple. **All four

## What that establishes

**The over-boundary row is a property of the shared baseline, not of the route or the chunk.** It is
present at chunk 1024, so the 1024→4096 step does not create it; it is present on `wmma`, so the
`dense_wide` route does not create it; and it is present in the "at head" run, so it is a HEAD
property rather than an artifact of either change. That matches X-6's own reasoning by identity —
*"its teacher movement follows by identity since both gates clear every selector for the teacher
arm"* — and it is now confirmed directly rather than by that argument.

**This is exactly what the review needed.** X-6's cell already recorded the re-gate's shape: hard
gates pass with **2.1x–2.3x headroom** on the tightest three, `top-1` **improves** 0.993540 →
0.994186, and one row crosses the review boundary where none did. The open question was whether that
row was a route regression. **It is not: it is unchanged by both the route and the chunk size, so
the movement X-6 observed is in the shared baseline and the row is inherited, not introduced.**
