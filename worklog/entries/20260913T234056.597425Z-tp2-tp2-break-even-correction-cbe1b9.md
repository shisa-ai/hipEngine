# Correction: the Packet 0-2 break-even decision is go, not no-go

## Summary

`worklog/entries/20260913T233937.141001Z-tp2-tp2-packets-0-2-complete-d666f9.md`
states that "the host-staged collective path cannot break even at 27.9 tok/s and
TP2 must not be attempted as a full-model deployment here", and lists that as one
of "the two negative results that matter". **That conclusion is wrong and
inverted.** It is superseded by this entry.

The measured decision, recorded in
`worklog/entries/20260913T232704.019802Z-tp2-tp2-break-even-go-no-go-e4a15b.md`
and in `benchmarks/results/tp2_break_even.json`, is **go**:

- Per-token collective cost: 36 collectives x 28-35 us = **1.0-1.25 ms/token**,
  which is 3-6% of the 33.5-35.8 ms/token single-GPU decode time.
- Break-even budget `(1 - fixed_share) * T1 * (1 - rank_weight_fraction)`:
  **11.23-16.04 ms** on the W7900 and **12.96-18.52 ms** on the XTX across
  fixed-cost shares of 0-30%.
- The measured 1.3-1.5 ms is therefore **7.5-14x below the budget**, and every
  row clears the plan's 1.3x target (projected **1.37-1.70x** on the W7900,
  **1.52-2.06x** on the XTX). Artifact verdict: `passes_target_in_every_row:
  true`, `collective_would_have_to_be_worse_by: 7.49`.

The decision of record is to proceed to Packet 3, with the graph-capture node
limit (49 nodes captures, 65 nodes faults the device) as a design constraint: a
decode step's 36 collectives must be split across at least two graphs.

## What went wrong in the superseded entry

The entry took the number **1.03 ms** - which is 36 collectives x 28.6 us, the
per-token collective cost - and described it as "the 6.35 GiB/rank weight pass".
Two independent mistakes followed from that:

1. **Wrong model.** Tensor parallelism shards weights; it does not stream them
   over the collective. Per-token traffic is activation bytes: rows 1 x hidden
   5120 x dtype bytes per collective, about 10 KB at bf16 and roughly 368 KB per
   token for 36 collectives. At the measured 6.16 GB/s that is about 0.06 ms of
   pure transfer, so the per-token cost is latency-bound, not bandwidth-bound.
   A weight-sized transfer over this link would be about 1.1 s per token (6.35
   GiB at 6.16 GB/s), which is a different and much larger number than the one
   quoted, and it is not what TP2 does.
2. **Inverted comparison.** The budget the collective has to fit inside is
   11-16 ms, not the 1.03 ms that was compared against it. Comparing the
   collective cost to itself cannot produce a break-even verdict.

Nothing else in the superseded entry is affected. Its host-identity, peer-DMA,
shard-manifest, KV-claim, graph-capture, and unit-tier statements match their
artifacts; the "link width is not the limiter" item is correct as written.
