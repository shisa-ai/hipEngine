# At the qualified c=1 budget-3 cell, no_provider is one bounded activation-wait event per request

## Summary

`no_provider` has two very different magnitudes, and the qualified cell sits at
the small end. At c=1, production profile, the qualified budget 3, four 4-turn
conversations with output spans on:

- `mtp_requests=16` of 16 (100.0%), `mtp_output_share=99.1%` (2030 of 2048 output
  tokens), `routes: effective={"speculative_mtp": 16}`, and
  `speculation coverage: never_entered=0 ... ended_inside=16`. MTP engages and
  stays engaged.
- Decode 213.44 tok/s aggregate, 53.36 -> 79.30 -> 76.03 -> 78.01 tok/s across
  turns 0-3, ITL 36 ms average, ttft median 1751 ms.
- `refusal cost: events=16 spanned_ar_tokens=16 by_reason={"no_provider": 16}`
  out of 18 autoregressive tokens, with `attribution: reconciled=16/16
  span_proofs=16 fallback_events=16`.

That is exactly one refusal event per request spanning one AR token each: the
activation wait before the provider has the prompt's hidden rows. Sixteen of
eighteen AR tokens across 16 requests is a bounded startup cost, not a routing
failure, and it is fully attributed (`span_proofs=16` of 16).

The contrast with the c=8 beyond-1024 run is the whole point: there `no_provider`
covered 248 of 256 AR tokens with no rejection warning and MTP admitted. The same
reason code therefore means "waiting one cycle for activation" at one end and
"never admitted for the row's whole lifetime" at the other. Any MTP claim has to
say which one it is measuring.

## Changes

- None. One measurement, serving as the #25 activation-waiting attribution and
