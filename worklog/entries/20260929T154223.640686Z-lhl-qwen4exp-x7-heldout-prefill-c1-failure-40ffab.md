## The evidence is complete and it fails

Route `wmma`, layers 16-47. The candidate and teacher hashes differ, so the route
engaged - checked first, before reading any KL, because an identical pair is the
failure this packet has already recorded twice. All seven coverage checks pass.

Then: `hard_gates_passed: False`. The aggregate is fine (mean KL 2.74e-4, top-1
0.99556) but two per-scope gates fail:

| failing scope | top-1 | mean KL | limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `shape=prefill_last` | 1.0 | **2.06179e-3** | 1e-3 |
| `transition=prefill_to_c1` | 1.0 | **2.06179e-3** | 1e-3 |

Both fail on **KL, not top-1**, and both are the same row population: the drift
concentrates at the **prefill->c1 transition**, at **2x the limit**. One row
exceeds the 0.02 review boundary; 2 top-1 mismatch rows;
`requires_outlier_review: True`, `eligible_for_automatic_admission: False`.

## Why the heldout suite found what canonical missed

The canonical fixture is 512/1024/4096-token prompts over 128 transitions; the
heldout suite is 39-71-token prompts over 24. The shorter prompts put
proportionally more weight on the prefill->c1 transition, which is exactly where
this route's drift concentrates. The canonical protocol passed and the heldout
protocol did not, which is the value of heldout evidence rather than a box ticked.

## Two errors of my own, recorded rather than edited away

1. **I claimed the suite "cannot engage" the route**, on a 256-row floor. Wrong:
   I read the floor from `_q8_iu8_wmma_dispatch`, a *different* route. The f16
   WMMA route declines only at `rows <= 1`, and `_raw_k_staged_f32_dispatch` has
   no row guard at all. The run settled it. So my check was producing a false
   **refusal** - the mirror image of the false pass it was built to prevent - and
   both directions are silent. Floors are now per-route, read from each dispatch's
   own guard, and the check discriminates: the same suite qualifies `wmma` and
   `staged_f32` and does not qualify `dense_wide`.
2. **I emitted `category_heldout_qualification_claim: true` on this failing
   run.** Collected is not passed, and the field name promises the second. The
   claim now requires complete coverage **and** passing quality; evidence
   availability is reported separately as `category_heldout_evidence_complete`.

Both were caught by reading the artifact instead of the summary line, and the
same was true of the earlier over-claim that "every quality gate passed" - the
aggregate did, the per-scope gates did not.
