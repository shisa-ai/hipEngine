# gemma4 gfx1151: hardware counters put the prefill attention walk at 4 percent of the DRAM roofline, not 105

## Summary

`rocprofv3 --pmc FETCH_SIZE` on the Radeon 8060S measures the prefill attention
kernel's actual DRAM traffic. It is **4.0% of the 238.5 GB/s roofline** at a
256-token chunk over 65,536 keys, not the 105-118% recorded for the prefill walk.

**This is a 25x opportunity on the dominant cost of a long-context prefill**, and
it is the largest headroom found in this campaign. The 995 s a 262,144-token
prefill takes is essentially all this kernel, and the kernel is neither
DRAM-bound nor WMMA-bound.

## The counter is calibrated, not trusted

`FETCH_SIZE` is documented as "the total kilobytes fetched from the video memory
... with all extra fetches and any cache or memory effects taken into account",
which is exactly the quantity in dispute, so it was checked against a case whose
answer is known before being used.

The decode shape reads each KV band exactly once, and at `tokens=1, keys=65536`
that is `65536 * 2 kv_heads * 512 dims * 2 bytes * 2` = **268,435,456 bytes**.

    gemma4_attention_decode_wmma_full_kernel   262,313.375 KB  =  268,608,896 bytes

**0.06% high**, the excess being the mask, the output tile and the combine pass.
So `FETCH_SIZE` is real DRAM bytes in KB, and the decode shape's byte model is
right. The same measurement independently confirms the decode walk's roofline
percentages: 268.6 MB in 1.24 ms is **216 GB/s, 91% of the roofline**.

## The prefill, measured

`scripts/gemma4_prefill_kernel_bw.py` under `rocprofv3 --pmc FETCH_SIZE`, one
chunk, one full layer, all-ones mask, 65,536 keys:

| tokens | grid | ms | DRAM fetch MB | GB/s | % of 238.5 roofline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 8192 | 6.51 | 271.8 | 41.8 | 17.5% |
| 64 | 512 | 19.59 | 527.5 | 26.9 | 11.3% |
| 256 | 512 | 75.10 | 708.6 | 9.4 | 4.0% |

The byte model these replace said 256 tokens x 65,536 keys x 4,096 bytes =
**68.7 GB**; the counter says **708.6 MB**. The model over-counts by **97x**.

The fetch count grows sub-linearly with the row count -- 271.8 MB at 16 rows,
708.6 MB at 256 -- so at 16 rows the chunk fetches almost exactly the unique band
once (268.4 MB) and at 256 rows it fetches 2.6 bands. L2 is absorbing most of the
16-row blocks' sharing, which is the opposite of what the "reads each band once
per query-head tile" reasoning predicted.

## What this means

- The recorded claim that the prefill walk runs at **105-118% of the DRAM
  roofline** is **wrong by a factor of about 25**. It came from the byte model,
  not from a measurement.
- The prefill attention kernel is not memory-bound and not tensor-bound: 4.0% of
  DRAM and, at the WMMA count the source documents (1.5x ideal for the
  head-dimension split), about 1.2% of the part's 54.6 TFLOP/s BF16 rate.
- Something else is the binding cost, and it is worth roughly a 25x speedup on
  the prefill if it can be found. The grid is 512 blocks of 128 threads on a
  96-CU part at the largest point and 8,192 at the smallest, so the shape of the
  launch changes by 16x across the sweep while the achieved rate *falls* 4x --
  more blocks per unit of work is worse here, which points at per-block work and
  the online-softmax serial chain rather than at parallelism.
- **The decode path is not implicated.** Its byte model is confirmed to 0.06% by
  the same counter, so the decode split and thread-count results, and the
  end-to-end decode gains, stand as measured.

## Changes

- `scripts/gemma4_prefill_kernel_bw.py` docstring records the counter table and
  the calibration, so the file carries the corrected reading rather than the

## Validation

- Calibration: decode shape, `tokens=1, keys=65536`, 262,313.375 KB against
  268,435,456 bytes expected.
- Every point is two launches of the shipped entry point; the two agree to 0.5%
  (e.g. 708.6 MB and 708.6 MB at the largest point, 9.4 GB/s both).
- The profiling run is a fresh sweep, not a re-read of the earlier probe output.
- Probe: `env -u HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES PYTHONPATH=. HIPENGINE_HIP_ARCH=gfx1151
  rocprofv3 --pmc FETCH_SIZE -d <dir> -- .venv/bin/python
  scripts/gemma4_prefill_kernel_bw.py --reps 1 --keys 65536 --tokens 16 64 256`

## Next

- **Find the binding cost of the prefill attention kernel.** It is at 4% of DRAM
  and ~1% of WMMA, so it is neither. `MemUnitStalled` and the occupancy counters
  are the next measurement; the source's own note that the output-dimension split
  "issues 1.5x the ideal WMMA count" and the per-batch online-softmax serial chain
  are the two named suspects.
- Re-derive the sliding prefill kernel's numbers the same way. It shares the
  tile structure, so it may share the problem.
- `docs/ROOFLINE.md` and the model cards carry roofline-percentage claims that
  came from byte models. They need the same counter treatment before any of them
  is quoted again.
