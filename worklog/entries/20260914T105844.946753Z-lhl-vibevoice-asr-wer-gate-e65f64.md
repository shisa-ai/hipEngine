# VibeVoice WER gate: hip 0.02% vs torch 0.05% on diverse 50-clip subset

## Summary

Open-ASR-Leaderboard-style WER gate on a diverse LibriSpeech
test-clean subset; hipEngine shows no degradation vs the torch bf16
reference and this gate becomes the acceptance test for the Q4 quant
runner.

## Changes

- scripts/vibevoice_asr_wer.py: spread clip selection (one utterance per
  chapter round-robin; the first run had been a single-speaker subset),
  per-clip WER records, structured-output extraction.
- Artifact
  `benchmarks/results/2026-09-14-gfx1151-vibevoice-wer-gate-50clips.json`
  plus the earlier single-speaker parity artifact.

## Validation

- 50 clips across 23 speakers (2.1-27.2 s), matched frozen-request
  protocol, greedy: torch bf16 0.05%, hipEngine 0.02%; transcripts
  differ on 23/50 only at punctuation/possessive level; per-clip WER
  differs on 9/50 and hip matches or beats torch in all nine,
  including 260-123440 where the torch lane produced a fully wrong
  transcript (WER 1.0) and hip scored 0.0.
- Both numbers are far below the model card's published 2.20% row; that
  row is full-set and not subset-comparable (easy-skewed 50-clip
  subset, possibly different audio/chunking protocol). The gate claim
  is hip-vs-torch parity under identical inputs, which passes.
