# Verify attention LDS stride fix: multi-row 5-22% faster, bit-exact

## Summary

Task #28. The shared-row decode/verify attention kernel staged K/V tiles at
the same 256 B row stride that caused the 16-way k_frag bank conflict in the
prefill mt kernel (fixed earlier). Applied the same arithmetic-neutral fix:
`KV_STRIDE = HEAD_DIM + 2` (260 B) in `glimmer_shared`, used by the tile
declarations, staging writes, and k_frag/v_frag reads.

Row-count scaling probe (production split_keys=64/n_blocks=64, one NoPE-layer
geometry) before -> after:

| ctx | rows | before ms | after ms | speedup |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 32768 | 4 | 0.293 | 0.248 | 1.18x |
| 32768 | 8 | 0.536 | 0.429 | 1.25x |
| 131072 | 4 | 1.006 | 0.912 | 1.10x |
| 131072 | 8 | 1.994 | 1.575 | 1.27x |
| 262144 | 4 | 1.957 | 1.766 | 1.11x |
| 262144 | 8 | 3.894 | 3.028 | 1.29x |

Multi-row verify attention 5-22% faster (tok8 -21/-22% at 131K/260K);
single-row decode unchanged (memory-bound). The remaining tok5 residual
(~1.9-2.1x the single-row floor) is WMMA-issue throughput at 5 active waves,
not LDS — no further arithmetic-neutral lever identified. This also closes
the first target of task #29's LDS-stride audit (the decode/verify kernel).
