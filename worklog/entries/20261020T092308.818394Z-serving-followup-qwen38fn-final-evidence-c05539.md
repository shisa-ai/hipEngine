## Validation

Both packets use the same physical gfx1151 Radeon 8060S host, machine ID
`55ea6c509d0b49eea8de7094a1023668`, UD-Q4_K_XL, BF16 KV and the recorded
production manifest. Exact commands, environment, source identities and raw
artifact hashes are included in each packet. Local full outputs live under
`/tmp/qwen38fn-serving-followup/`.

- Full scheduler capture: 2,790 speculative rows, 630 AR-peer rows, 630 true-AR
  rows and 558 dynamic rows; repeats do not enlarge these denominators.
  All four numerical gates fail. All repeat, source, manifest and capture
  consistency checks pass; 11,664 consumed pass rows align exactly.
- The separate direct composition packet has identical complete candidate-logit
  hashes for batch2/4/8 and mixed2/4/8, with all repeats identical. The
  neighbor-isolation packet passes. These do not override numerical failures.
- Direct timing uses 18 category/heldout prompts, 32 forced-token steps, one
  warmup and three measured repetitions at c1 and batch1/2/4/8. Pooled step
  medians in milliseconds, baseline -> candidate:
  c1 40.456 -> 40.427; batch1 42.679 -> 42.512; batch2 49.835 -> 49.626;
  batch4 57.881 -> 57.641; batch8 75.551 -> 75.537. Baseline then candidate
  process order is not counterbalanced, so these small differences are not
  strong causal speedup evidence. Per-group/repeat ratios are preserved;
  width8 includes regressions up to about 0.6%.
- Eight-step caller profiles show H2D copies reduced from 320 to 40 at both
  widths 1 and 8. D2H copies stay 8 and 16, respectively. Python `memcpy`
  time includes device synchronization and is not pure transfer cost.
- Candidate PLE staging totals about 1 ms at width1 and 4 ms at width8 across
  eight steps; hashing totals below 1 ms and about 3 ms, respectively.
  These rounded profiler values do not justify a new hashing/gather rewrite.
  Preserve the immediate-use pinned-buffer contract and make no additional
  PLE optimization in this campaign absent a new measured bottleneck.
