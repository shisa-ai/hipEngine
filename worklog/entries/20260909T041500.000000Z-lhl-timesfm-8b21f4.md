# TimesFM scatter constant folding + custom-GEMM negative result

## Summary

Two outcomes: (1) the warp-per-head qkv_norm_scatter kernel was
transcendental-bound — softplus(per_dim) and the q_ln/factor scale are
per-layer constants and are now folded into a precomputed qscale vector at
decoder init, removing two transcendentals per output element; (2) a custom
WMMA small-GEMM kernel for the AR shapes was implemented, measured no
better than the shape-autotuned rocBLAS solutions, and removed (negative
result). Wall metric 0.0877 -> 0.0844 s median (-3.8%, MAD 0.9 ms).

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/timesfm/timesfm.hip`:
  `qkv_norm_scatter_kernel` takes `qscale` (=
  `q_ln * log2(e)/sqrt(D) * softplus(per_dim)`) instead of `q_ln` +
  `per_dim`; the per-element `softplus_` and `factor` multiplies are gone.
- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/timesfm/timesfm.py`: wrapper ABI updated.
- `hipengine/runtime/timesfm_decode.py`: per-layer qscale computed at init
  (D2H read of q_ln/per_dim, host float32 math, upload; kept in the
  fp16-weights lifetime pool); strict-path layer entries unchanged.

## Measured

- qkv_norm_scatter: avg 65.7 -> 44.4 us over 96 calls/decode (rocprof);
  total ~6.3 -> ~4.3 ms per decode.
- Custom small-GEMM (removed): 32x1280x3840 62.2 us (158 GB/s) vs
  autotuned rocBLAS 49 us; 32x1280x1280 36.5 vs 39.2 us; 4-tile ILP
  variant 90 us (parallelism starved). Flat vs rocBLAS overall — the
  weight-read floor needs a split-K dataflow, not a wave-per-tile kernel.
- Wall: 5 runs 0.0828-0.0908, median 0.0844.

## Validation

- `python3 scripts/timesfm_gpu_bench.py --check`: fp32 strict parity +
  fp16 production gate pass.
- `python3 -m pytest tests/test_timesfm_gpu_decode.py -q`: 3/3.
