# Repair Gemma split workspace ownership after campaign review

## Summary

Replaced the process-global split-attention cache with explicit layer-owned
scratch. The old cache overwrote each allocation on growth and had no teardown;
`DeviceBuffer` has no freeing destructor. This is an ownership repair, not a
performance promotion or resolution of the split's recorded numerical failure.

## Changes

- `Gemma4AttentionScratch` grows geometrically, retains allocations while kernels
  may reference them, isolates streams and owners, and synchronizes used streams
  before freeing. Runtime/device migration is refused rather than reusing a
  foreign pointer.
- `Gemma4LayerScratch` owns and closes attention scratch. Direct wrapper callers
  without reusable scratch get temporary scratch released after stream completion,
  including partial launch failure.
- Seven CPU cases cover growth, reuse, owner/stream isolation, allocation failure,
  device/runtime mismatches, layer teardown, and temporary-wrapper cleanup.
- Review follow-ups remain: scalar pair-load alignment/bounds, empty slice outputs,
  evaluator route engagement and provenance, and unsupported campaign closures.
  The iteration-45 residual arithmetic is not a measured re-profile; unrelated
  Qwen fusion/graph results do not reject Gemma-specific candidates.
