# Launch the bounded Q8_0 dense-wide tile at every row count on gfx1100

## Summary

`wide_launch` in `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q8_0_dense_wide.hip` chose between two
instantiations of `q8_0_dense_wide_kernel`: a bounded one for a shape with a partial tile, and a
"tail-free" one when the rows and the output columns divide the tile. Which of the two is faster at
full tiles depends on the architecture, and on the W7900 the tail-free one is the slower at every
full-tile shape measured. Without the predication the compiler keeps more values live: the 128 by
128 tile takes 256 VGPRs and 320 bytes of scratch in its tail-free form, against 224 and none in
its bounded form, and the 64 by 128 tile 232 against 176.

**Dense tiles, K2304 by N3072** (W7900, best of 20; before is the tail-free form, after the
bounded one):

| Tile | 1,024 rows | 2,048 rows |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 64 x 128 | 280 -> 270 us | 705 -> 416 us |
| 128 x 128 | 527 -> 221 us | 985 -> 458 us |
| 128 x 256 | 831 -> 375 us | 1,545 -> 616 us |

The bounded form is what a shape with a partial tile always ran, so those shapes do not change:
1,000 rows took 228, 247 and 354 us and 2,000 rows 408, 498 and 625 us before the change. Other
shapes at 1,024 rows, 128 by 128 tile: K2304 by N1024 548 -> about 140 us, K2304 by N384 275 ->
101 us, K3072 by N2304 589 -> 216 us.

**The hyper-connection up projection** of the same file (`gr_up_mean_launch`, a 128 by 128 tile)
made the same choice. At K2560 and hidden 2560: 1,024 rows 1,937 -> 677 us and 2,048 rows
4,305 -> 1,435 us; 1,000 rows (661 and 671 us) and 2,000 rows (1,310 both) are on the side of the
boundary that did not change.

**Strix Halo is the other way round.** The same A/B there, the committed launcher against a
launcher that always chose the bounded form, put the bounded form behind at every full-tile point:

| Shape, tile | 1,024 rows, tail-free | 1,024 rows, bounded | 2,048 rows, tail-free | 2,048 rows, bounded |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| K2304 by N3072, 128 x 128 | 659 us | 801 us | 1,517 us | 1,717 us |
| K2304 by N3072, 128 x 256 | 739 us | 897 us | 1,369 us | 1,617 us |
| K2304 by N3072, 64 x 128 | 753 us | 864 us | 1,503 us | 1,462 us |
| K2304 by N1024, 128 x 128 | 255 us | 312 us | 524 us | 622 us |
| K2560 by N10240, the up projection | 1,910 us | 1,994 us | 3,938 us | 3,949 us |

The 1,000 and 2,000 row points of that run are within 4% of each other. So the launcher reads the
device architecture: on `gfx1100` every tile launches in its bounded form; elsewhere the full
tiles keep the tail-free form, which is what they ran before. The host pass of this `hipcc`
(ROCm 7.2.4) defines no `__gfx1100__` macro, so the choice is made from `gcnArchName`, read once
per device.

Both instantiations run the same arithmetic on every fragment they store. The outputs of nine
full-tile cases saved on the W7900 before the change, three tiles at 256, 1,024 and 2,048 rows, are
bitwise the same after it.
