# DP-9 identity resolved from the rocpd symbol table; it tempers the completion audit's ranking

## Summary

The completion audit committed in `5c2d554ec` ranked `DP-9` **up**, on a shape-level
inference: five of the decode `linear` family's six largest launch signatures shared one
`wg=128, VGPR=48, LDS=512` shape totalling 34.9% of decode, and the audit named the
identity resolution as the row's first experiment.

**That experiment needed no re-run, and its result partly retracts the ranking.**

The `x4full` stem carries a rocpd symbol table (`rocpd_info_kernel_symbol_*`) keyed by the
same `Kernel_Id` its `--kernel-rename`d kernel trace uses. The rename is why the decode
kernel names were thought unavailable: it replaces each kernel's name with its enclosing
ROCTX role, which is the only way to attribute the graph-replayed kernels at all. But the
rename only touches the trace's name column, not the DB's symbol table. Joining the two
recovers the ROCTX role **and** the real kernel name from a single stem, with no clock
mismatch and no new profile.

The decode's true top three:

| calls | ms/step | share | wg | grid | kernel |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| **497** | **23.846** | **44.55%** | 128 | 5120 | `gguf_k_pack8_prefill_out_kernel<float,float,8>` |
| 47 | 13.968 | 26.10% | 64 | 40960 | `gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_silu_logical128_t64` |
| 43 | 5.420 | 10.13% | 64 | 163840 | `q5_1_selected_weighted_sum_logical256_t64_bf16` |

**One kernel is 44.55% of decode**, and it is the `pack8` GEMV family - the family `DE-3`
already measured and optimized. It serves every dense slot (`root.lm_head`, `attn_q`,
`attn_qkv`, `attn_gate`, `hc_*`), and its name says `prefill` while it runs 497 times per
decode step.

## Why this tempers the audit's own ranking

`DE-3` measured this exact family at `out=320` running 40 workgroups at **92.4 GB/s (42% of
the 221 GB/s roof)** and every shape at 2560 or more at **90.8-102.9%**, then widened the
block 128 -> 256 at the two starved shapes for -32.4% and -19.4% of kernel time. Its family
figure was **24.005 ms/step**, matching the 23.846 measured here.

So the dominant decode owner is **already near roof at wide shapes and already fixed at
narrow ones**. `DP-9`'s target is real and large, but its first step must be to subtract what
`DE-3` captured. The less-examined part is the other kernel pair's `grid=163840` /
`grid=40960` `expert_gate` shapes.

The audit's ranking was made on a shape-level inference and stated as such. The symbol join
is what made the correction possible. Leaving the stronger claim standing would have been
the more flattering write-up and the less accurate one.

## Validation

- **The symbol join is exact, not heuristic.** It maps `Kernel_Id` -> `kernel_name` from the
  rocpd table; 702 symbols resolved and every one of the 1,771 in-window dispatches got a
  name.
- **The join reproduces the known per-kernel figures.** The resolved
  `gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_silu_logical128_t64` is 47 calls / 13.968 ms/step against `DE-1`'s
  independently recorded 47 kernels / 14.013 ms/step; `dense_gemv_out_kernel<float,float,false>`
  resolves at 264 calls / 1.959 ms against `DE-1`'s 1.363 ms/step for the router GEMV. The
  44.55% pack8 figure is consistent with `DE-3`'s 24.005 ms/step family measurement.
- **The 53.524 ms total is the `x4full` rep2 window** against 54.173 ms for the `x4decode`
  window - the same repetition captured by two profiler runs, within 1.2%.
