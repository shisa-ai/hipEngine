Why the earlier split was wrong but the ratios were not: cycle wall is
`tokens per cycle / per-lane rate`, so the K3-to-AR ratio equals
`tokens per cycle / cycle cost in AR steps` identically -- modelling tokens per cycle
moved both sides of that quotient and cancelled. The ratio (1.05x at C1, 0.32x at C8)
was and is correct; only the attribution between yield and cost was wrong. The
position-acceptance model under-counted because proposals per cycle are fewer than
the budget near a run's end (packet: 20 draft tokens over 7 cycles at C8) and because
`acceptance.by_width` denominators are per-position, not per-cycle.

Recomputed ceiling for wave packing at C8, same crude four-pass model: the wave pays
4 step-equivalents (3 draft rounds + one 32-row verify) = 328.8 ms and yields
8 x 2.674 = 21.4 tokens, i.e. **65.1 tok/s against the measured 31.6 = 2.06x**
headroom (the entry I am correcting said 1.49x using the wrong 1.93 yield). Against
Laurent's 101.07 that is still **0.64x**, so packing alone does not overtake the peer
rows -- it closes roughly two thirds of the gap and acceptance must supply the rest.

Revised order across the open tasks: grouped prefill first (peers 2.7x ahead at C8 on
a submodule where the fix is guard relaxation if the runner probe fuses), then K3 wave
packing (2.57x of the measured 8.23-step cycle), then acceptance (1.27x, and the only
route past the peer rows).
