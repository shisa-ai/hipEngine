# Correct DMS eligible-history budget semantics

## Summary

Corrected the target-compression budget before real labels were generated. The
prior implementation applied CR to total sequence length and then clamped at the
protected window, which labels every eligible key for short/moderate contexts
such as T768/window256/CR4. FastDMS semantics instead compress eligible history
while always retaining the grace window.

## Changes

- `build_eviction_labels` now retains
  `protected_count + ceil(eligible_count / target_cr)` rows and evicts the
  lowest-use remainder independently per KV head.
- Added `target_historical_live_count` to label statistics.
- Aligned compact admission estimates to the same protected-plus-compressed-
  history formula.
- Updated hand-checked fixtures and added a T768/window256/CR4 regression:
  257 protected, 511 eligible, 128 historical retained, 383 evicted, 385 total
  live, and zero protected violations.
- Updated `KVCACHE.md`. This entry supersedes only the budget interpretation in
  the earlier oracle-label worklog; capture/QK math and tie semantics stand.

## Validation

- No real label artifact existed before the correction; the 30,720-token FP32
  capture is unaffected.
- Focused label and compact admission fixtures pass with exact expected counts.
