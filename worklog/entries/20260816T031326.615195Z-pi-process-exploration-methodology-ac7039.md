# Adopt guarded optimization exploration methodology

## Summary

Adopted an optional methodology for broader optimization exploration after
repeated bounded kernel tuning showed a risk of converging on local minima. The
methodology broadens hypothesis generation and allows structural candidates to
mature while explicitly preserving hipEngine's correctness, anti-gaming,
heldout, lifecycle, and production-promotion contracts. This process unit makes
no performance claim.

## Changes

- Added `docs/PROCESS-EXPLORATION.md` with opening criteria, declared
  generalization envelopes, an immutable evaluation firewall, separate
  discovery/qualification/confirmation surfaces, three-to-five-family
  hypothesis beams, bounded structural maturation, stagnation/advisor resets,
  a layered E0-E5 loop, autonomy checkpoints, thin recordkeeping, and explicit
  exit states.
- Distinguished legitimate architecture/model/quant/shape specialization from
  forbidden prompt-, token-, candidate-, fixture-, scorer-, or timing-aware
  shortcuts. Conditional data/weight/adaptive policies require a frozen
  development set, untouched evaluation, complete overhead, and a safe
  fallback.
- Updated `docs/README.md` to index the methodology and add it to the common
  reading path for less-bounded optimization searches.

## Validation

- Re-read `docs/PROCESS-EXPLORATION.md` end to end and checked it against
  `AGENTS.md`, `docs/BENCHMARK.md` Anti-gaming, `docs/TESTING.md`, and the

- Verified all local Markdown links resolve, ran `git diff --check`, and ran

- Docs/process-only unit; no GPU benchmark or test suite is required.

## Next

- Pilot the methodology on one freshly profiled, material kernel/runtime owner:
  freeze its evaluator and product envelope, seed three genuinely different
  mechanism families, set a finite structural maturation budget, and evaluate
  both search diversity and false-promotion rate in addition to retained speed.
