# X7 attribution: the host path costs 0.655 ms per decode step, and softcap is 81% of it

## Summary

Punchlist X7 (`runtime / sampling`) said its cost was **unknown** and named the
work: "`Gemma4Runner._forward_block` copies vocabulary logits to the host and
applies NumPy softcap; `next_token` selects on the host. Attribute those costs
directly." X5 separately recorded its own status as "unknown until
host/timeline attribution", so this measurement was the stated prerequisite
for two rows.

Three host operations sit on every decode step, all at the real vocabulary
width (262144 x float32 = 1 MiB):

| operation | mean | p50 | p95 | where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| device->host logits copy | 114.70 us | 112.40 | 119.92 | `gemma4.py:907` |
| copy + observe (synced) | 112.36 us | 111.59 | 115.91 | same |
| `np.tanh(x/c) * c` softcap | **528.23 us** | 515.68 | 657.82 | `gemma4.py:915` |
| `np.argmax` (`next_token`) | 14.07 us | 13.96 | 14.44 | `gemma4.py:935` |
| greedy path as written | 14.39 us | 14.28 | 15.04 | same |

**Host total per decode step: 654.99 us = 0.655 ms**, which against a 24.88 ms
decode step (40.2 tok/s at 4096) is **2.63%** — of which the transfer is
**0.45%** and NumPy softcap + argmax **2.18%**.

The finding that matters is that **the transfer is not the cost**. X7's wording
leads with the copy, but `np.tanh` over 262144 elements is 81% of the host
total, roughly 4.6x the copy. If the path is made device-resident, moving
softcap alone is worth about eight times what moving the copy is. `next_token`'s
argmax at 14 us is negligible and is not worth moving on its own.

Two smaller observations from reading the path:

- The softcap allocates a fresh array every step
  (`logits = (np.tanh(logits / cap) * cap).astype(np.float32)`), so a
  device-resident move would also remove a per-step allocation.
- The unsynced copy returns before the DMA completes, so its 114.70 us is not
  what a host sampler must wait for; the synced 112.36 us is. They are within
  noise of each other here because the copy is only 1 MiB.

## What this does and does not settle

It settles the *cost* half of X7 and unblocks X5's stated prerequisite. It
does **not** make the move: the Check also requires checking "softcap
rounding/ties, supported sampling semantics, route selection, and request
isolation", and X7 explicitly requires preserving "an explicit host-logits path
for diagnostics and numerical gates". Softcap rounding is a changed-arithmetic
question of the same family as task #33, so moving it to the device needs the
same care, not just a stopwatch.

## Changes

- `scripts/gemma4_host_logits_cost.py` (new): times all three operations at
  the real vocabulary against a real device buffer, reporting mean/p50/p95/max
  and the share of a caller-supplied decode step. Run it with
  `--decode-ms` to express the percentage. No production code changed.
- `docs/campaigns/GEMMA4-26B-A4B-PUNCHLIST.md`: X7's Evidence now carries the
  measurement and the softcap-is-the-prize conclusion; its Est. gain moved from
  `unknown` to `0.655 ms/token recoverable (2.63% of decode)`, and Status to
  `measured 2026-09-29; device-resident move not started`.
