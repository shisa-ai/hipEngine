# TP2 versus TP1 and llama.cpp's tensor split, measured in one window

## Summary

The question was two-sided: how the TP2 route compares to our own single card,
and how llama.cpp's tensor parallel compares on the same host. Both were open.
The recorded llama.cpp comparison was from 2026-09-20 and its hipEngine side
predated the device-side MLP reduction and the head-sharded attention default, so
every hipEngine number in it had moved.

One host (`lhl-w7900-xtx`: W7900 46064 MiB + RX 7900 XTX 24560 MiB, gfx1100),
one model (`/models/gguf/Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M.gguf`), 512-token prompt, 128 decode
tokens, f16 KV on both engines, c=1, three repetitions per arm, prefill and
decode measured separately, all seven arms back to back in one window.

| arm | prefill tok/s | decode tok/s |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| llama.cpp TP=2 `-sm tensor -ts 1/1` | 1332.1 ± 11.8 | 40.68 ± 1.77 |
| hipEngine TP2, bulk prefill + head-sharded attention (default route flags) | 1066.3 | 41.64 |
| llama.cpp TP=2 `-sm layer` (pipeline) | 978.7 ± 1.8 | 29.22 ± 0.12 |
| llama.cpp TP=1, XTX | 1057.9 ± 2.2 | 37.14 ± 0.09 |
| llama.cpp TP=1, W7900 | 938.0 ± 1.3 | 30.37 ± 0.06 |
| hipEngine TP1, XTX (this harness prefills token-serial) | 36.57 | 34.90 |
| hipEngine TP1, W7900 (same) | 30.07 | 28.78 |

**Decode is level with llama.cpp's tensor split.** 41.64 against 40.68 is inside
llama.cpp's own ±1.77 spread, up from the 39.17 that the 2026-09-20 recording
measured (a 5.85% deficit then). The head-sharded attention default moved it.

**Prefill is 20.0% behind**, 95.8 ms to remove from a 480 ms prefill, against the
28.33% deficit at the 2026-09-20 recording.

**The two engines scale differently, and that is the actionable part.** Over each
engine's own best single card, llama.cpp's TP=2 gains **1.26x prefill and 1.10x
decode**; hipEngine's TP2 gains **1.08x prefill and 1.19x decode**. Our decode
scaling is the stronger of the two and our prefill scaling is the weaker one. Our
TP2 bulk prefill (1066.3) is also only 1.008x llama.cpp's *single* XTX card
(1057.9): on prefill the second card currently buys us almost nothing, where it
buys llama.cpp a quarter.

**Against our own TP1**, same host and shape: TP2 decode is 1.45x the W7900 and
1.19x the XTX; TP2 bulk prefill is 1.21x and 1.08x against the single-card
bulk-prefill controls; and on the token-serial route both engines ship by
default, TP2 is 1.46x and 1.20x.

## Validation

Protocol. llama.cpp is the `llama.cpp-rdna3-opt` RDNA3 fork at build `15995a1`,
HIP backend, via `llama-bench -p 512 -n 128 -r 3 -ngl 999 -ctk f16 -ctv f16 -fa
auto` with the split flags varied per arm. hipEngine is revision `0f495a4d9`
through `scripts/tp2_c1_bench.py` at the same shape and repetition count.

`-sm row -ts 1/1` is absent because it **fails to load this model**:
`llama_bench: error: failed to load model`. The row split is the other
tensor-parallel-shaped mode in this build, so `-sm tensor` is the only working
multi-GPU TP on this pair.

**llama.cpp's default multi-GPU mode is a loss on this pair.** Its pipeline split
measures 0.93x single-card prefill and 0.79x single-card decode, so a llama.cpp
user on two gfx1100 cards who does not select `-sm tensor` gets less than one
card.

Limits.

- The single-card **bulk-prefill** TP1 controls are from an earlier revision
  (`results/2026-09-14-*-512-128-matched-tp1.json`, 878.42/29.57 W7900 and
  988.10/35.35 XTX). This harness's TP1 arms prefill token-serial (30.07/36.57
  above), so the token-serial-vs-token-serial ratio is the cleanest same-route
  TP2-vs-TP1 statement in this window and the bulk comparison carries the
  revision caveat.
- The prefill deficit is if anything understated: llama-bench's `pp512` projects
  the prompt's output rows where this route uses `logits_rows=1` (last row only),
  worth a few percent of a 384 ms prefill in llama.cpp's favour.
- llama.cpp's recorded TP=2 prefill figure of 1474.6 still does not reproduce:
  1332.1 now, on the same build, with the TP=1 companion reproducing within 0.4%
  (938.0 against a recorded 941.8).
- The route this session ships for TP2 prefill is still token-serial by default
  (`bulk_prefill=False`); 1066.3 is the bulk candidate, whose promotion is
  blocked by the recorded Q6_K `attn_qkv` dispatch divergence.
