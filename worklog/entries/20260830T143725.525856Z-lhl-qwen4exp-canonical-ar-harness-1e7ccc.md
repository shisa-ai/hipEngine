# Add canonical Qwen4Exp exact-token benchmark harness

## Summary

Replaced the future Qwen4Exp closure shapes with a category-balanced exact-token
p512/p1024/p4096 plus tg128 protocol. Added the committed 12-case fixture and a
durable driver for synchronized hipEngine production or managed
llama.cpp-compatible servers. The first output belongs to prefill and decode
covers exactly 128 subsequent autoregressive transitions on every engine.
