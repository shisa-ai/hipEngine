# Retain sole-T16 Q4 narrow attention F16 prefill

## Summary

Extended the model-scoped bounded Q4 source-F16 prefill policy from dense FFN
down to the 1,024-output full-attention K/V projections. The route remains
byte-neutral and prefill-only. A complete quality gate and counterbalanced
same-session A/B on both gfx1100 boards retain the small exact component win;
absolute benchmark rows remain diagnostic because their thermal drift is larger
than this increment.

## Changes

- Added Q4 shape K=5120/N=1024 at row anchors 512/768/1024/1280/4096.
- Split Q4 in-place activation admission from Q4 shape admission. FFN down keeps
  its dead-activation reuse; K/V use the existing aliased activation plane.
  This fixes the RED smoke where treating every admitted Q4 shape as in-place
  overwrote the shared full-attention normalized input and produced non-finite
  logits.
- Added a focused shape-scoped in-place dispatch fixture. Exact fallback remains
  for rows below 512, all wider attention projections, gate/up, decode, MTP, and
  peer backends.

## Validation

- Actual-weight K and Q4-V screens win at all measured anchors. K speedups are
  **2.27x/2.03x/1.56x/1.77x/1.24x** at M512/768/1K/1280/4K; Q4 V is
  **2.15x/2.03x/1.52x/1.64x/1.22x**.
- Complete 10-prompt/four-category/330-transition quality gate passes with
  minimum per-prompt top-1 **96.97%**, max KL **0.0156330**, deterministic
  repeats, 1,120 asserted Q4 and 1,280 asserted Q6 candidate dispatches.
- Counterbalanced seven-pair full-prefill A/B versus FFN-only, one resident
  session per board/shape:
  - RX 7900 XTX 512/1K/4K: **+0.326%/+0.172%/+0.079%**, with 6/7, 7/7, and
    6/7 paired wins.
  - W7900 512/1K/4K: **+0.123%/+0.203%/+0.315%**, with 6/7, 5/7, and 4/7
    paired wins.
- Standard XTX 512/128 remains finite and deterministic at **803.388 prefill /
  33.511 decode tok/s**, with the tracked peak unchanged at **15.605 GiB**.
  Standard absolute rows are not promoted over the higher retained FFN-only
  medians because sub-percent load/thermal drift exceeds this increment.
- Focused CPU/HIP owner bundle passes **100 passed, 9 skipped**; `ruff` and
  `git diff --check` pass.
