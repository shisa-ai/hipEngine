## Summary

Iteration 14 landed E6 step 2c: the (IQ4_XS gate, Q5_K up) family —
**3 layers** (combo 23, 4), the largest of the 8 remaining mixed combos

share).

**The lever:** side A unchanged (IQ4 local32 split-K chain, raw); side
B a new exact emulation of the E4a tile8 single's 4-group chain: each
hardware wave emulates single-waves `g = wave, wave+WAVES, ...` with
identity lane mapping (single thread (g, lane) owns k = g·32+lane and
+128 per superblock), the single's fmaf order and shfl-tree reduce per

via shared memory. Third template instantiation `<WAVES, false, true>`,
new extern-C symbol, new wrapper (gate-first = geometry order, no
reorder), new route block.

**The screen taught the unit's main lesson.** First cut: side B
serialized in wave 0 only → **bit-exact but 0.90× — rejected on the
gate**. Wave-distributed restructure → **bit-exact and 1.03×** (573.6
vs 588.1 µs/layer at (5120,17408) rows=1) → shipped. But the
production census shows pair3 at 575 µs/launch against a production
chain portion of ≈512 µs/layer — the isolated screen's chain (588)
overstates what the chain costs inside the running graph (two runs:
decode pure_us +110.7 / +95.3 µs/tok, +0.11–0.13%, inside the ≤0.62%
recorded CV band). Future pair screens should budget chain cost from
per-kernel census µs, not only the isolated A/B, and treat an isolated
win under ~1.05× as in-band rather than decisive on wall time.
