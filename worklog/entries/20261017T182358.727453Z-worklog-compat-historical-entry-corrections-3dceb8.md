# Repair worklog validation of historical invalid entries

## Summary

`python3 scripts/worklog.py check` rejected eight malformed committed entries: five recorded `base_commit` as a short abbreviation, and four lacked required headings, with one entry having both defects. Their bytes cannot change, so validation now reads an optional immutable correction record under `worklog/corrections/` that binds a specific exemption to one entry path and to the sha256 of that entry's commit-tree bytes. New entries are unaffected: with no bound record they fail the same rules as before.

## Changes

- `scripts/worklog.py` parses `worklog/corrections/*.json` with a fixed field set (`schema`, `path`, `sha256`, `defects`, `reason`, `base_commit`, `superseded_by`), reads records from the Git index like entries, and gates their index state as immutable tracked content.
- Two defects can be bound. `abbreviated_base_commit` resolves the recorded abbreviation to the full commit hash and must extend the recorded prefix. `missing_required_sections` permits missing non-summary headings and empty section bodies while preserving heading order, and requires a tracked `decision` or `checkpoint` entry that names the corrected path.
- A record is not applied, and the entry still faces the full strict schema, when its sha256 does not match the entry's commit-tree bytes, when the entry is untracked or not yet committed in HEAD, or when the record is unnecessary because the entry already passes strict validation. Unknown, unsorted, or unpaired defect lists and misnamed record files are rejected.
- `worklog/README.md` documents the record format, what each defect relaxes, the authoring recipe, and the limits: no new entry may use the route, and historical entries stay byte-identical.
- `tests/test_integration_worklog.py` covers both repairs through `check` and `render`, sha256 mismatch, untracked and new-entry records, unnecessary records, structural supersession, committed-record immutability, record schema strictness, and rejection of malformed new entries while corrections exist.
- Eight records under `worklog/corrections/` carry the repair: five resolve an abbreviated `base_commit`, four relax missing headings, and one of those does both. Four `decision` entries, one per structurally corrected entry, carry the missing sections; this unit entry records the mechanism.

## Validation

- `python3 scripts/worklog.py check` on the staged tree reports 11,642 valid entries and no correction error; before this unit the same command failed on all eight historical entries.
- `python3 scripts/worklog.py render` writes 11,644 entries to `.worklog/WORKLOG.md` in the shared tree (11,642 indexed plus two valid unstaged MTP entries), including the corrected historical entries with their original bytes.
- `.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_integration_worklog.py tests/test_unit_worklog_port_legacy.py -q` passed: 61 integration tests and 17 unit tests. Parent review added a RED/GREEN regression rejecting a superseding entry that itself requires a correction; structural supersession must terminate at a strictly valid entry.
- `python3 scripts/docs/check_all.py` passed all four documentation gates.
