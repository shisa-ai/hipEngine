# Correcting the estimate: gemma4's materializer has no resident-repack machinery

## Summary

A correction to my own previous entry's estimate, found by looking for the place
to make the change rather than assuming it existed.

**Gemma 4's materializer has no resident-repack machinery at all.** A grep for
`_RESIDENT_REPACKS`, `LAYOUT_` constants and `repack` in
`hipengine/loading/gemma4_gguf_materialize.py` returns **nothing**. By contrast
`qwen35_gguf_materialize.py` carries a full `_RESIDENT_REPACKS` mapping -
`LAYOUT_Q4_K_PACK8` through the q5_k/q6_k/q8_0 entries - and
`laguna_gguf_materialize.py` defines its own `LAYOUT_Q4_K_PACK8`.

So part one of the fix is **not a declaration line to add**. It is either porting
resident-repack materialization to Gemma 4's loader or producing the pack8 layout
for its expert tensors by another route. The previous entry called it "declare the
q4_k pack8 layout", which understated it.

I am recording this before implementation rather than discovering it mid-change,
because the difference between "add a mapping entry" and "bring a materialization
capability to a second loader" is the difference between a small unit and a
substantial one, and the plan's sizing should be honest.

**The ordering argument is unaffected.** Whatever produces the layout, the weight
must carry it before any route change can be correct - that is still the safety
property, and a lookup change alone still reads garbage.

## Validation

- The absence is established by grep over the named file for the three markers
  the sibling materializers use, not by reading part of it.
- The presence in the siblings is established the same way, in two independent
  materializers.
- **Not yet established:** whether Gemma 4's loader materializes tensors at all in
  a way that could accept a repack step, or whether its expert weights are handed
  to the kernel in their raw GGUF layout. That is the first question of the
  implementation unit, and it decides whether part one is a port or a new path.
