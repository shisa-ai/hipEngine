# Gemma 4: the Q8_0 dense prefill tile space was never swept

## Summary

The Q8_0 dense projection route is 717 ms of a 3003 ms prefill (23.9%, the second
largest entry in the closed profile) and its tile shape had never been swept -
only three of the family's legal shapes had ever been instantiated. `col4 row16`
against the carried `col16 row4` is **2.32 against 3.18 ms at in 2816/out 2112
(1.37x)** and **4.10 against 5.19 ms at in 2816/out 4096 (1.27x)**. The 512-token
prefill is **2.993 -> 2.770 s (1.081x)**, the largest single prefill win of this
campaign, and it is bit-exact so it ships with no flag and no gate.

The lever is the row tile, not the column tile: a block re-reads its weight bytes
once per row tile, so at `col4` the shape goes row1 7.90, row2 4.49, row4 3.18,
row8 2.42, row16 2.32 ms. That is the third route in this campaign where a
carried launch shape turned out to be the wrong one, and the second where the
fix was bit-exact and free.

## Validation

- **Geometry from the route, not assumed.** A live 512-token forward with the
  registered kernel wrapped recorded all eight shapes: in 2816 out 2112 over 60
  calls, in 2816 out 2048 over 50, in 2112 out 2816 over 30, in 2816 out 4096
  over 25, in 4096 out 2816 over 25, in 2816 out 8192 over 5, in 2816 out 1024
  over 5, in 8192 out 2816 over 5. Together 1684 GFLOP over 717 ms, 2349
  GFLOP/s, so the chosen shape had to hold across all eight rather than at one
  favourable geometry - and it was the best at both geometries swept.
- **Bit-exactness.** The reduction is per-(row, column): the k-loop stride is
  `blockDim.x` and the shuffle tree and four-wave sum are unchanged by the tile
  shape, so the whole space is bit-identical. Measured: 0 of 1,081,344 and 0 of
  2,097,152 bf16 outputs differ.
- **The branch boundary was measured, not assumed.** Rows 8 favours `col8 row8`
  by 1.9x over any 16-row tile, because a 16-row tile half-fills at 8 rows. The
  change is therefore made only in the branch the route's own geometries take,
  and every row count that branch can see was measured: 32 (0.188 against
  0.242), 64 (0.265 against 0.415), 128 (0.535 against 0.589), 512 (2.304
  against 2.375). The short and narrow branch was left alone rather than changed
  unmeasured.
- **Tests.** `tests/test_unit_gguf_gemv_decode_dispatch.py` 24 passed and
  `tests/test_gpu_gguf_q8_0_mmq_prefill.py` 20 passed.
- **The intended route ran.** The public path was re-run with the new symbol
  wrapped: all 205 calls resolve to `tile4x16` across the same eight geometries,
  so this is the shipped route rather than a silent fallback.
- **Public A/B.** The same timing script in two git worktrees, one warm-up and
  five timed 512-token forwards each: 2.993 -> 2.770 s medians (minima 2.972 ->
  2.765). The -223 ms public delta exceeds the -167 ms the kernel-level ratios
  predict, which is consistent with the same change also cutting activation
  reloads.
