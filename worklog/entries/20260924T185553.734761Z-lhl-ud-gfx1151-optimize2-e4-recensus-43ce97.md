  time and the non-kernel per-launch cost is within 1.1 µs between arms.
  Row batching / graph-capture work (the campaign's launch-overhead

  boundaries + one non-boundary), strict fallback, symbol verified in a

  not alone prove an owner rewrite wins; that is what the paired A/B gate
  decides.
