# gfx1151 Qwen3.6-35B-A3B INT8 decode KV blocked on the mixed_v1 control

## Summary

Screened an INT8 decode KV copy for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on gfx1151 under the
declared production numerical gate. **Blocked on quality**: the gufo-matching
`int8_per_token_head` + fp16-scale layout passes 512/8 and 1024/8 with zero
BF16 mirror and fails 4096/16 on the harness control `mixed_v1` alone (mean KL
**0.1597** > 0.05, max 2.6939, top-1 0.9412). All ten natural category prompts
pass at every shape, at 4096/16 with 100% top-1 and mean KL <= 1.09e-3.

The decision does not rest on the gate alone. The lane's premise was that KV
width drives the decode gap that widens with depth; measured directly, the INT8
route is worth **+1.44% / +1.50% / +1.59%** against the BF16 graph route at
depths 0 / 4096 / 16384 - real but flat - and the net owned-session memory
reduction at 18,688 rows is **146,761,728 B (0.64%)**. A clean pass would not
justify re-cutting a gate that closed the gfx1151 27B lane. No registry entry
was added, `auto` still resolves to BF16, and no production default changed.

The screen also produced a second, independent result: the "decode gap widens
with depth" reading is an artifact of the **eager** decode route. Ratios against
the comparator's published headline are 1.203x/1.204x/1.277x for BF16 eager but
**1.205x/1.207x/1.206x for BF16 graph** and 1.187x flat for INT8 graph, and the
comparator value is a single headline rather than a per-depth measurement.
