# one layer of thirty costs 12 percent of gemma4 prefill, and the ablation could not see it

## Summary

The 2048-token Gemma 4 prefill is now accounted to 98.9 percent kernel by kernel, and
the accounting names a defect that both prior attributions missed: **layer 29 of thirty
costs 606.6 ms, 11.7 percent of the step, because UD-Q4_K_XL quantizes it differently
from its siblings and it falls off both fast expert routes.** Its gate/up is `gguf_q5_k`
where the other 29 layers are `gguf_q4_k`, and its down is `gguf_q8_0` where the other 29
are `gguf_q5_1`. Each of the two falls onto an fp32 grouped fallback that is 14.1x slower
per layer-block.

This also corrects two numbers this session had been working from, both of which were
wrong in the same direction — they understated the work left and misdirected it.

## Changes

- `scripts/gemma4_prefill_kernel_census.py` (new): times every kernel the decoder layer
  launches, in place, with a HIP event pair per call. The expert block is timed whole and
  through its leaves, so the glue is a measured difference. Sums only the top-level
  leaves — the expert leaves are nested inside the block, and adding them counts it twice.
- `scripts/gemma4_prefill_dense_probe.py` (new): times `launch_gguf_linear` grouped by
  `(quant, rows, in_features, out_features)`. This is the funnel the route ablation's two
  catch-all arms never reach, which is why 30 percent of the step had no name.
- `scripts/gemma4_attention_prefill_bench.py`: the "all 30 layers" total summed all three
  cases, but its two sliding-window rows are the same 25 layers under two window settings.
  At 2048 it printed 4111.6 ms where the model's 30 attention layers are 2180.8 ms. It now
  sums only the rows the model has and labels the `window=0` row as a probe.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-28-gemma4-gfx1151-prefill-kernel-census.json` (new): the
  census, the layer-29 finding, the named fix and the corrections.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-28-gemma4-gfx1151-prefill-attention-length-scaling.json`:
  `superseded` block. Its `the_prize` shares came from the pre-Q5_1-MMQ 7.536 s baseline
  and its first `not_established` entry read the double-counted bench total.

| prefill attention | 1993.9 | 38.0% |
| routed experts (whole block) | 1980.2 | 37.8% |
| dense projections + lm head | 1077.2 | 20.6% |

The accounting closing at 98.9 percent is the evidence that the event timing is not
inflated by host stalls; the residual is host time, and it is 1.1 percent rather than the
30 percent the ablation implied.

## The defect

Four of the 120 expert-block calls are layer 29, and they take the fallbacks:

| route | quant | layers | calls | total ms | ms/call |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gate_up_mmq` | `gguf_q4_k` | 29 of 30 | 116 | 626.81 | 5.404 |
| `down_mmq` | `gguf_q5_1` | 29 of 30 | 116 | 619.11 | 5.337 |
| `rows` -> `grouped_row4` | `gguf_q5_k` | **layer 29** | 4 | **436.80** | **109.199** |
| `rows` -> `grouped_prefill` | `gguf_q8_0` | **layer 29** | 4 | 169.77 | 42.443 |

Siblings cost 10.74 ms per layer-block; layer 29 costs 151.64 ms — **14.1x**, and 606.6 ms
of a 5.242 s step. Calls are 4 per layer because the prefill runs as 4 blocks of 512 rows.

The gate/up cause is in the code and it is real rather than a soft guard.
`gemma4_project_experts_gate_up_mmq` routes `gguf_q5_k` to the iu8-WMMA route, which
declines on `intermediate % 128`; Gemma 4's expert intermediate is 704 and 704 % 128 = 64.
The kernel walks columns as `blockIdx.x * 128` (`gguf_q5_k_q8_1_selected_prefill.hip:194`)
and its host launcher rejects a half whose `out_features` is not a multiple of 128
(`:601`). The fused gate|up block is 1408 columns — 11 x 128 — **but the gate/up seam sits
at column 704 and 704 / 128 = 5.5, so a 128-wide block straddles the seam.** This is the
same partial-trailing-block shape the Q5_1 down MMQ route already handles on its K axis,
which is why the fix looks bounded: tile each half separately so a half's last block is 64
columns wide. The kernel's own argument guard already accepts 16-multiples.

The fallback is slow by construction, not merely untuned. Layer 29's gate/up is 32.5 GFLOP
in 109.2 ms — 0.30 TFLOP/s against 6.0 TFLOP/s for the Q4_K MMQ at the same shape — and
its 271 MB of weights move at 2.5 GB/s. Neither the FLOPs nor the bytes explain the time,
which is the same barrier-bound reading the expert-down artifact recorded for the staged
grouped kernel.

## Two corrections

**The ablation's `no_experts` arm does not work.** It reports 0.579 s saved, 11.1 percent.
The arm patches `gemma4_project_experts_rows` and `gemma4_project_experts_selected`, but
the default path reaches `gemma4_project_experts_gate_up_mmq` and
`gemma4_project_experts_down_mmq` directly, so the arm only skips the fallback. The
measured expert share is 1980.2 ms, **37.8 percent — 3.4x what that arm reports.** Both
the prefill-attention length-scaling artifact and this unit's own starting hypothesis had
read that number, which is why this unit started looking at attention when the expert
block was equally large.

**The attention bench double-counted.** Its three cases are the 25 sliding-window layers
under `window=1024`, the same 25 under `window=0`, and the 5 full layers. Summing all
three counts those 25 layers twice and adds a probe. The length-scaling artifact read the
double-counted 4111.6 ms as an isolated bench 1.79x slower than the in-situ 2295 ms, and
concluded its absolute milliseconds do not transfer. With the double count removed the
isolated total is 2180.8 ms, 1.05x the in-situ figure, so they do transfer.

## Validation

- Census measured with `env -u HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES ROCR_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0
  HIPENGINE_HIP_ARCH=gfx1151 PYTHONPATH=. python3 scripts/gemma4_prefill_kernel_census.py
  --prompt 2048 --repeats 3`, three independent runs at 5.242, 5.230 and 5.218 s.
- The layer-29 route assignment was confirmed by a separate probe recording
  `(route, quant, return)` per expert-leaf call: `gate_up_mmq` returns `False` for
  `gguf_q5_k` in 0.006 ms, `down_mmq` returns `False` for `gguf_q8_0` in 0.006 ms, and the
  four fallback calls are the only slow ones.
- The artifact geometry was confirmed against the file's tensor table: `blk.29` is the
  only `ffn_gate_up_exps` that is not Q4_K and the only `ffn_down_exps` that is not Q5_1.
- The census's first revision printed 134.5 percent of the step because it summed the
  expert leaves inside the expert block. The committed version sums top-level leaves only.
- No arithmetic changed: this unit adds measurement scripts and a bench-script fix. The
  only code path touched is the bench's reporting, which no runtime path imports.
