## What the numbers mean, and what they do not

**The ratios are within-arm, and that is the claim.** Each ratio is that
artifact's MTP rate over that same artifact's own AR rate, measured by the same
suite on the same host in the same session. No cross-host or cross-protocol
comparison is being made.

**The UD/plain ratio in this protocol is confounded, so it is not reported as a
result.** UD AR is 0.505x plain AR here (15.362 vs 30.442), while the recorded
UD decode campaign measured 0.848x. Both facts are real and they are not in
conflict:

- The campaign measured **graph-replay** decode on **GPU1 (the XTX)**, per its
  own rollup header: "Four-arm decode (GPU1 XTX, medians of 3)".
- This suite's AR path is **eager** (no decode graph).
- The campaign's census shows the two artifacts are **not** compute-different:
  UD K_M 30.263 ms/token pure vs plain K_M 30.478, at 831 vs 796 launches per
  token. Nearly identical kernel time and launch counts.

So the ~2x UD AR gap is host-side per-launch overhead exposed by eager decode,
not slower kernels. The MTP path is *not* equally exposed, because its proposal
is graph-captured (`runtime.proposal_graph.replays: 175`, `last_status:
replay`). MTP therefore amortises host overhead that AR pays per launch, which
is why the UD ratio (2.00x) exceeds the plain ratio (1.72x) — the UD AR
baseline is inflated. **Under a graph-replay protocol the UD ratio would be
lower.** That is an inference from the census plus the protocol difference; UD
pure-kernel time was not re-measured on the W7900.

## U6 items 5 and 6

The run supplies their evidence but does not close them, by design:

- Item 5 (control): **binding part passes.** GPU/CPU acceptance agreement holds
  on all 40 MTP rows, the AR denominator is present, every arm beats its AR
  baseline, and both repeats are bit-identical per prompt for AR and MTP. The
  exactness part is recorded and not binding: UD-Q4_K_S differs from its own AR
  reference on `general_ja_plan` in both runs (2 of 20 rows), the already
  localised 0.0096-logit near tie between the single-row AR route and the 4-row
  verify route. Under the production profile `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md`
  section 4.1 permits this. What is still missing is the section 6.1 calibrated
  teacher-forced KL / per-category top-1 gate, which free-running generation
  cannot produce.
- Item 6 (envelope): the run declares what was measured — c1, natural25,
  budget 3, W7900/gfx1100. `context_max` stays undeclared, so the pin stays
  empty, because c2/c4/c8, long context and width transitions are unmeasured.
