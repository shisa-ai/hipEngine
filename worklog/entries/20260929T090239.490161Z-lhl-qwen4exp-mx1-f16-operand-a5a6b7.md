# MX-1 F16 routed experts: operand error 8-9.5x below the BF16 floor on all 48 down layers

## Summary

MX-1 is the competitor's routed-expert design: decode the quantized expert
weights to F16, cast the activation to F16 once, run F16 WMMA with F32
accumulate, and drop the iu8 risk/repair pass. Its first experiment is a
numerical packet, because the route changes arithmetic. **The route is
admissible by quant.** Decoding the real expert weights to F16 and casting the
real activations to F16 costs 8.0x to 9.5x less than the BF16 rounding the path
already applies, on real natural-input activations, for every one of the 48
expert_down layers and every expert quant in the model, with no F16 overflow.

This closes MX-1's first experiment and leaves the kernel build and the
production gate open. No runtime, kernel or default changed.

## Why the packet comes first

The two expert families hold **4610.1 ms of the 12973 ms gap** against the
comparator (`expert_gate_up` 2640.6 and `expert_down` 1969.5, 35.6% together),
and a further **1239.8 ms of iu8 exact-repair** sits inside our two expert rows
with no comparator equivalent. That makes the pair the largest combined lever,
and it is the one family pair where the comparator's mechanism is a known
design rather than an inference. But it changes arithmetic, so the operand
question has to be answered before a kernel is written.

The comparison basis is the **BF16 publication floor already in the path**, not
an arbitrary tolerance: the expert output is published to BF16 downstream, so an
operand change far below that rounding cannot move the model beyond what the
current path already does.

## Result

Part A, every expert tensor in the model, 8 experts sampled per tensor:

| Quant | Tensors | Max relative | RMS relative |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Q4_K | 94 | 4.880e-4 | 2.068e-4 |
| Q5_1 | 43 | 4.880e-4 | 1.843e-4 |
| Q5_K | 2 | 4.880e-4 | 2.067e-4 |
| Q8_0 | 5 | 4.815e-4 | 1.977e-4 |

The maximum relative error is 2^-11 = 4.88e-4, exactly the F16 representation
limit and identical across quants, so the error is F16's rather than the
quant's. Weight magnitude peaks at 0.5624 against the F16 maximum 65504: **no
overflow**, all finite after the cast.

Part B, expert_down on the real `general_en-p4096` natural-input capture
(10,240 expert-sorted rows, K=640, N=2560, 512 experts, all 48 down layers):

| Quant | Layers | Candidate RMS | BF16 floor RMS | Ratio |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Q5_1 | 43 | 5.12e-6 to 5.50e-6 | 4.44e-5 to 4.79e-5 | 0.115 to 0.116 |
| Q8_0 | 5 | 5.67e-6 to 6.52e-6 | 4.55e-5 to 5.23e-5 | 0.124 to 0.125 |

Worst ratio 0.125. Activation magnitude peaks at 13.44 against 65504: no
overflow. The capture is the real `SiLU(gate)*up` intermediate from the
production route, not a synthetic distribution, and the BF16 decode is verified
exactly (`activation_bf16_exact: true`) — an earlier revision of this packet
decoded those bits as F16 and produced a wrong answer until that was caught.
