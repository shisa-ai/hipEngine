# The campaign gated against the wrong reference

## Summary

Every numerics decision in this campaign was made against an **independent
llama.cpp teacher**. `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` section 6 says the production
gate compares `strict` against candidate `production` on the same artifact, and
section 7.1 says an external engine "is a comparison oracle, **not** the
definition of hipEngine strict bytes".

Re-scored against the correct reference, using captures already on disk, the
ranking **inverts**: the shipped four-quant integer-MMQ default breaches the
binding absolute max-row ceiling, and W4A16 - which I had just concluded was
dominated - passes every threshold.

## Changes

No code change. Re-analysis of existing captures plus a recorded decision.

## The measurement

Reference: the pre-MMQ capture with every IQ tensor on the strict dense GEMV,
same production config and same forced tokens, so the comparison isolates the
IQ routing change and subtracts the hipEngine-versus-llama.cpp codec gap that
every arm carries.

| arm | mean | p95 | p99 | max | top-1 | 6.1 verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| two-quant MMQ | 0.001038 | 0.003479 | 0.014385 | 0.049730 | 99.4% | FAIL (mean) |
| **four-quant MMQ (shipped default)** | 0.002110 | 0.006797 | 0.024044 | **0.170390** | 100.0% | **FAIL (max, 3.4x the ceiling)** |
| **four-quant W4A16** | **0.000827** | 0.004547 | 0.012475 | **0.023513** | 100.0% | **PASS on every metric** |

Limits: mean 1e-3, p95 5e-3, p99 2e-2, max 5e-2 absolute ceiling, top-1 >= 99%.

Row detail: the shipped default has **one row over the 5e-2 absolute ceiling**
(position 91, KL 0.1704) and two over the 2e-2 review line. W4A16 has **none**
over the ceiling and one over the review line. At the default's worst row
W4A16 scores 0.0003 - 570x better - so this is a real failure of that route at
that position, not noise.

For scale, hipEngine strict against the llama.cpp teacher is mean 0.001271.
That gap is common to every arm and is what the teacher-referenced numbers were
carrying; it is why every arm looked like it "failed" mean against a 1e-3 limit
that was never meant for that comparison.

## Why this went unnoticed

The teacher comparison is a legitimate quality diagnostic and the campaign
inherited it from earlier UD units. It is not the admission gate. Because the
common codec gap dominates, teacher-referenced numbers compress the differences
between arms and, for max, reorder them: against the teacher the four-quant MMQ
had the **best** max of all arms (0.0297) and W4A16 the worst (0.0409). Against
strict those are 0.1704 and 0.0235.

This is the third cheap-proxy error in this campaign, and they share a shape:
bisecting IQ4_NL on divergence-from-default rather than teacher KL; validating
W4A16 at the bf16 output floor; and now scoring admission against an external
oracle rather than strict. **Check what the reference actually is before
trusting a comparison.**

## Consequence

By `AGENTS.md` - "never land a production win that fails any binding
mean/tail/category/task threshold" - the shipped four-quant integer-MMQ default
should not stand as-is. W4A16 passes the envelope at 150.9 tok/s against the
default's 171.9, a 12% throughput cost.

The policy is **not** changed in this entry: the human lead selected the
current one explicitly, and did so on this campaign's teacher-referenced
numbers. The decision is theirs with the corrected data.

## Validation

Re-analysis only; no production code changed and no suite rerun. The reference
capture is the committed pre-MMQ default arm, and forced-token equality with
each candidate is asserted in the comparison.
