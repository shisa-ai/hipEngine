# Decode lever 3: Q5 dense local32 single owner - the study, then the owner

## Summary

The Q5 dense decode singles (113 launches, 7.60 ms/tok on K_M; 68 on
K_S) moved from the direct T16 GEMV to a local32 owner: K_M 27.70 ->
29.98 tok/s, K_S 28.41 -> 29.49, prefill unchanged.

## The study (why the direct GEMV costs 2.3x)

`qk_t16_selected_direct_gemv_kernel<u16,5,...>` (74.5 us/launch, 7.60
ms/tok over 113 K_M launches) decodes every weight with the per-MAC
unpack inside the K loop: for each (k, column) it re-reads and
re-decodes the per-column d/dmin (two fp16 decodes) and the superblock
scale/min bytes (two byte loads) - four redundant memory operations
plus two fp16-to-f32 conversions per MAC. The rocprof trace
(trace-unrouted.csv) shows the structural cost: VGPR 128, LDS 512 B,
128-thread blocks (launch_bounds(128,2)); the Q4 local32 comparator
runs VGPR 96, LDS 0, 32-thread blocks at 45.3 us/call.

The microbench (microbench.py, synthetic T16 tiles, real shapes)
isolates the access pattern from everything else - same tile layout,
same arithmetic class: the local32 rewrite measures 2.3-2.6x per
launch on GPU1 (91.8->39.6 us at 5120x6144, 191.5->77.5 at
17408x5120) and 1.6-2.1x on GPU0. The layout comparison answers the
study's second question: the T16 repacked layout needs no change -
the QL nibble window is the same aligned u32 per lane-row the Q4
owner reads, and the Q5 high bit adds exactly one u8 per lane-row;
a raw-layout repack would buy nothing the tile does not already give.

## The owner

`q5_k_t16_dense_single_local32_gemv_kernel` - the retained Q4 local32
geometry (one superblock of eight contiguous K per lane, 32-thread
blocks, launch_bounds(32,8)) applied to the Q5 tile: the unpack
(per-column d/dmin, the lane's superblock scale/min bytes) hoisted per
256-block, the nibbles as one aligned u32 per lane-row, the fifth bit
as one u8. Routed via GGUF_T16_C1_VARIANTS_BY_QUANT_SHAPE (the
designed rows==1 shape-keyed override), so rows>1 keeps the
direct/rowtile/WMMA family and the Q5 gate/up dual condition (which
keys on the direct variant) is untouched.

Two integration defects caught by the evidence process: (a) the
consumer-surface variant swap broke the native-batch rowtile
promotions (they key on the direct variant name) - reverted to the C1
table mechanism; (b) the registration used variant
"dense_single_local32_bf16_out" (missing the second _bf16) so the C1
dispatch silently fell back to the direct kernel - the route only
became live after the name was fixed, and the first routed trace is
the proof it fires.

## Validation

- Real-repack A/B (repack_gguf_q5_k_tile16 over synthetic raw Q5_K):
  rel <= 5e-4 vs the direct GEMV, corr >= 0.9999
  (test_q5_t16_local32_single_matches_direct_gemv).
- Routed rocprof trace (trace-routed.csv): the local32 kernel serves
  all Q5 decode singles (3036 calls, 53.5 us/call in-situ, VGPR 96,
  LDS 0); the direct kernel is gone from the decode window.
- Dedicated probe (scripts/gguf_q5_local32_decode_gate.py - the
  C1-table arm swap, since the IQ-policy probe cannot gate this
  route; natural self-generated + the 18 tokenized category fixtures,
  pooled campaign predicate), 3 seeds x both artifacts: ALL PASS.
  K_M pooled mean 8.9e-5, max 2.70e-2, top-1 99.92%; K_S mean 6.7e-5,
  max 8.1e-3, top-1 99.92%. The category half is seed-independent
  (fixed fixtures, incumbent-greedy extension).
- Decode (GPU1 XTX, 512/128, median of 3): K_M 27.70 -> 29.98 tok/s
  (+8.2%), K_S 28.41 -> 29.49. Prefill unchanged (901/891).
- Campaign cumulative: K_M 26.20 -> 29.98 (+14.4%), K_S 25.53 ->
  29.49 (+15.5%); the remaining gap to plain decode is ~4.0 (K_M) /
  ~1.1 (K_S) ms/token.
- Focused battery green (test_gguf_q5_k_t16_dense,
  test_gguf_gemv_decode_dispatch, test_gguf_iq_local32_decode,
  test_gguf_q4_k_t16_dense, test_gguf_ud_dense_t16_roles);
  test_gguf_linear_dispatch segfaults at test 34 both with and
  without this change (pre-existing, same crash point at HEAD).
