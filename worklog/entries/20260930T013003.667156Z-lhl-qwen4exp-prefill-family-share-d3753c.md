# Correction: GD-3's ceiling is ~5% of prefill, not 1.1% - and prefill is 74% MoE+GR

## Summary

The GD-3 entry committed immediately before this one (`8ba915864`) states the
recurrence lever is "bounded at **1.1%** of prefill". **That number is wrong by
4.5x.** This entry corrects it and records the measurement that caught it.

I traced the p512 prefill (`rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` over
`qwen4exp_profile_gap.py --mode prefill --case-id code-p512`, one repetition,
production profile, cached build, chunk 1024) to answer GD-4's cost gate - how
much prefill the whole GDN family occupies - and the trace answered both
questions at once.

**5699 dispatches, 3914.468 ms total kernel time** (about two passes: 3914/2014
≈ 1.94).

| family | calls | total ms | share |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **moe** | 384 | 1554.168 | **39.70%** |
| **gr** | 654 | 1338.149 | **34.18%** |
| other | 3231 | 314.040 | 8.02% |
| qsa | 288 | 309.376 | 7.90% |
| **gdn** | 72 | 194.487 | **4.97%** |
| linear | 662 | 117.020 | 2.99% |
| attention | 48 | 49.856 | 1.27% |
| norm | 200 | 16.178 | 0.41% |
| conv | 146 | 13.625 | 0.35% |

**Prefill is 73.9% MoE + GR.** Four individual kernels are 67.7% of all kernel
time: `gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill` (21.77%),
`gguf_k_prefill_out_coltile_rowbatch` (18.05%),
`q8_0_gr_up_sigmoid_mean_coltile2_branch4` (15.68%),
`q5_1_selected_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill` (12.23%).

## The error and its cause

GD-3's 1.1% came from "9 certified GDN layers x 4.961 ms/1K = 22.7 ms at p508
against today's 2014 ms p512 prefill".

**9 is the numerically certified layer subset (global layers 35-47), not the
number of GDN layers in the model.** The model has **48 layers = 36 GDN + 12
full attention**, established three independent ways from the same trace:

- GDN recurrence dispatches: **72** = 36 layers x 2 passes.
- Router dispatches: **96** = 48 layers x 2 passes.
- Conv dispatches: **144** = 2 convs x 36 GDN layers x 2 passes.

**Corrected: 36 x 2.54 ms at p512 = 91.4 ms of 2014 ms = ~4.5%**, matching the
measured 194.487/3914.468 = **4.97%** directly. The recurrence lever is bounded
at **~5% of prefill**, not 1.1%.

**The direction of GD-3's conclusion survives** - its cited baseline still
matches no registered route, and gufo's quoted 65 ms is still ~13x slower than
our production route - but its cost gate was overstated by 4.5x.

**GD-3's second paragraph is simply wrong and I am withdrawing it.** It says
"the GDN family's cost is overwhelmingly *outside* the recurrence - the
recurrence is ~3% of the family". The family is 194.487 ms and the single
recurrence kernel `qwen4_exp_gdn_prefill_f32_kernel<true, true>` accounts for
**all** of it. There is no hidden 97% to redirect to. The GDN-adjacent stages
are small and separately classified: conv 13.625 ms (0.35%), norm 16.178 ms
(0.41%). So GD-4's ceiling is the same ~5% plus those two, ~5.5% - it is the
more complete version of the same idea, not a separate prize.

## Bonus: hardware evidence for GD-3's mechanism

The trace records dispatch resources, which is better evidence than the
inference-from-roof-distance GD-3 had to rely on. The recurrence runs at
**`VGPR_Count` 256** (the hardware maximum), `SGPR_Count` 128, `LDS_Block_Size`
0, `Scratch_Size` 36, workgroup 128, grid 6144. At 256 x 32 lanes x 4 B = 32 KB
against a 64 KB VGPR file per SIMD that permits **2 waves per SIMD** out of a
64-wave-per-CU maximum, ~12.5% occupancy. **So "latency-bound" is right and the
cause is register-file capacity, not issue width** - the same conclusion QA-2
reached independently for QSA from a static VGPR census.

## Validation

The measurement is reproducible from the trace:

```
rocprofv3 --kernel-trace --output-format csv -d <dir> -o prefill -- \
  python3 scripts/qwen4exp_profile_gap.py --model-root <model> --mode prefill \
  --case-id code-p512 --repetitions 1 --execution-profile production \
  --compiler-version-file <ver> --require-cached-build --prefill-chunk-size 1024 \
  --output <artifact>
python3 scripts/qwen4exp_family_share.py --trace-dir <dir>
```

The corrected share was cross-checked two ways: the family share computed
directly from the trace (194.487/3914.468 = 4.97%) and the layer-count
arithmetic (36 x 2.54 ms / 2014 ms = 4.5%). They agree to within the
pass-count rounding.
