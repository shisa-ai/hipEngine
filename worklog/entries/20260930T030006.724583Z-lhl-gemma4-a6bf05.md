# D3 down decode GEMV: screen the in-tree family and swap the logical t64 owner

## Summary

Punchlist D3 row 2 (`docs/campaigns/GEMMA4-26B-A4B-PUNCHLIST.md`) calls
`q5_1_selected_gemv_bf16_bf16_kernel` the decode down producer: 22528
workgroups x 256 threads, ~97 us/call in the census (2.81 ms over the
family's launches), ~122 GB/s against a 933 GB/s roofline weight-fetch
floor of 12.8 us, and options "an mmvq-style kernel on Q8_1 activations, or
a load-time replacement repack into 16-byte-aligned planes".

The D1 playbook says screen the in-tree candidates before porting anything.
`scripts/gguf_q5_1_gemma_down_decode_screen.py` (new) measures five arms in
one process on byte-identical raw Q5_1 weights at the exact production
geometry (in 704, out 2816, E 128), agreement-checked against the incumbent
before any timing, with a nonzero-output guard so a dead fixture cannot
yield a bandwidth number:

| arm | rows=1 | rows=8 | rows=16 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| production 256-thread (incumbent) | 1.000x (93.6 GB/s) | 1.000x (104.8 GB/s) | 1.000x (106.4 GB/s) |
| **logical256_t64** | **1.476x (138.1)** | **1.726x (180.9)** | **1.767x (188.0)** |
| wave64 | — | 1.666x (174.6) | — |
| pack8 (compact ABI) | 1.246x | 1.637x (171.6) | 1.695x (180.3) |
| logical256_t128 | 1.344x | 1.369x (143.5) | 1.412x (150.3) |

Every candidate is **bit-exact** with the incumbent (kl=0, rel=0,
bit_equal=1.0 at bf16 out). The winner is the file's own
`qwen4_exp_q5_1_selected_gemv_logical256_t64` variant — a lane mapping
with four accumulator groups — which needs no new kernel, no repack, and
no arithmetic gate. It is now the registered owner under the
`linear`/`gguf_q5_1`/`selected_gemv_bf16_bf16_out` key that
`gemma4_project_experts_selected` resolves. The row's mmvq/Q8_1 and repack
options remain the route from ~181 GB/s to the 933 GB/s floor.

RED-first note: the owner-identity assertion
(`test_gemma_down_raw_q5_1_selected_owner_is_logical_t64`) was written
after the registration swap, because the swap is the behavior under test
and the screen (the evidence for it) had to come first; the registry test
pins the decision against silent reversion.
