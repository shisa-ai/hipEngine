# gemma4 gfx1151: cumulative long-context result after the decode routing, split and thread fixes

## Summary

Three units landed on the decode path today and this is the end-to-end result at
the campaign's longest context, measured on the same device, artifact and harness
as the recorded ladder:

| context | decode before | decode after | ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4,096 | 25.59 | 28.49 | 1.113x |
| 8,192 | 16.83 | **29.41** | **1.748x** |
| 32,768 | 25.98 | 27.29 | 1.050x |
| 262,144 | 14.79 | **15.56** | **1.052x** |

The 8,192 point is the headline and it is a *shape* change, not a percentage: the
recorded ladder collapsed to 16.83 tok/s there and recovered to 25.98 by 32,768.
That dip was the strict decode kernel's super-linear cost, and decode is now flat
across the range instead of collapsing in the middle.

At 262,144 the prefill dominates wall time (995.08 s of a 1003.24 s run), so the
decode number there is the one that moved and the wall-clock number is not the
decode's.

## Where the 262,144 gain came from, and why it is less than predicted

The walk went 6.42 -> 5.16 ms per full layer (1.244x), which predicted about
10% off a 67.6 ms decode token. The measured saving is 3.84 ms, or 5.2%. **The
prediction is not reconciled and is recorded as unreconciled rather than
explained away.** The walk is five full layers against a ~33.9 ms non-attention
floor, so the floor absorbs proportionally more than the linear estimate
assumed; whether the floor itself moves with the attention kernels' occupancy is
not measured.

## Next

- The walk is at **87.2%** of the DRAM roofline at 262,144 keys, from 70.1%. The
  probe that established the residual is the grid rather than the KV layout
  reached 97% at 1024 x 256, so the remaining ~13% is the walk's per-tile
  structure, and it is worth about 4% end to end. Untested.
- **The prefill's walk arithmetic does not reconcile with its measured time and
  this is an open question.** Five full layers at 262,144 keys, 4,096 bytes per
  key per layer, over the causal triangle is 7.0e14 bytes, which at the measured
  238.5 GB/s roofline is about 2,950 s. The prefill measures 995 s. Either the
  per-key byte model is wrong or the roofline is being beaten by a factor of
  three; the earlier claim that the prefill walk runs at 105-118% of the roofline
  came from the same model and inherits the same doubt.
- The decode's non-attention floor is ~33.9 ms of a 262,144-key token, about 53%
  of the 63.75 ms measured here. It is characterized in the campaign doc's decode
  attribution (dense Q8_0 GEMV 13.78 ms at 135 GB/s, expert GEMV 14.04 at 96,
  host floor 8.12) with a recorded direction -- fewer and larger launches -- and
  MTP named as the larger lever.
