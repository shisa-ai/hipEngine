# Loop metric ledger correction: the kept 367.934 was the rejected candidate's measurement

## Summary

A metric-ledger bookkeeping error in the pi-multiloop run
qwen4exp-5x/run-20261008-160400, found and corrected.

**The error:** iteration 7's keep recorded canonical_prefill_tok_s
367.934 as the loop's current metric, but that measurement was taken
with HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_T16_GATE_UP=1 - the T16 gate/up CANDIDATE
configuration, which iteration 9's layer2 gate then REJECTED (the
prefill_last scope at 1.69x mean) and which remains default-off. The
rejected candidate's measurement was carried as the loop's
"Current/Best: 367.934" through twenty subsequent iterations, while
every honest default-path verify reads ~340.

**The honest default-path state, measured twice on 2026-10-09 across
different thermal states (throttled 341.36, cooled 339.76, 12/12
deterministic both):** the production default is ~339.8 tok/s
aggregate, within noise of the loop's 338.12 baseline - because the
session's three promotions (dense Q8, K320 GR-up, tiled16_multi GDN)
landed BEFORE the loop baseline was set, and every subsequent session
candidate either landed default-off or was rejected at its gate.

**What remains true regardless of the correction:**
- The three promoted defaults and the validated MoE decode graph are
  real, promoted, and predate the baseline.
- The X-7 package is real: T16 gate/up +6.53% (12/12 positive, gate
  rejected only at prefill_last 1.69x with top-1 18/18) and T16 down
  +9.46% (12/12 positive, rejected only at 1.53x with top-1 18/18) -
  both measured as same-residency candidate A/Bs with their env flags
  named, both default-off, ~+16% combined behind the owner's X-7
  decision.
- The public scoreboard (benchmarks/README.md) carries none of the
  erroneous figure; the STATUS doc quotes the candidates as candidates
  with their flags named. The error was confined to the loop's own
  metric ledger and the session's conversational summaries.
