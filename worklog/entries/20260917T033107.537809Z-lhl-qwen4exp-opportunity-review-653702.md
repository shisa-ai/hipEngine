# Correct Qwen4Exp opportunity accounting and rank next experiments

## Summary

Reviewed the recent Framework Qwen3.8-Flash-Next UD-Q4_K_XL optimization
records and implementation without subagents or new GPU runs. The wide-route
A/B and candidate-local numerical result remain supported. Several deductions
in the status tracker were not: invalid-value timing was presented as a working
activation path, separate savings were added, wall deltas were subtracted from
kernel gaps, and local qualification was described too broadly.

This decision corrects conclusions in
[the preceding status refresh](20260917T031723.972815Z-lhl-qwen4exp-status-refresh-c30775.md),
not its immutable evidence. Historical benchmark/campaign records are unchanged.

## Changes

- `docs/QWEN4EXP-STATUS.md`: withdraw the mixed-basis residual/closed-gap
  accounting and the claim that the four-flag combination contains the shipped
  wide route. Report each measured wall experiment separately.
- Identify 2.494 s GR and 0.354 s GDN as sums of independent arms, not combined
  measurements. GR-up and GR-down both fail p95 as well as mean KL; earlier
  P4-up/compensated-down attempts are already rejected in `docs/REFACTOR.md`.
- Identify the 1.303 ms activation probe as invalid-value, timing-only evidence.
  The source candidate README explicitly says it reads F32 bytes as F16. Its
  layer-8 geometry is outside the promoted 16-47 scope, and it excludes a real
  conversion pass. It supports an experiment, not an end-to-end speed claim.
- Record the teacher actually constructed by
  `scripts/execution_profile_q8_wmma_prefill_layers_gate.py`: production with
  dense selectors cleared. The artifact's strict-named digest is not proof of
  named-strict execution. The local gate does not itself provide task/heldout,
  BF16-relative or serving-isolation qualification. Missing applicable evidence
  must be linked or obtained; this review does not infer a runtime failure.
- Preserve matching numerical summaries and trajectory digests, but correct
  the assertion that the complete WMMA/wide artifacts differ only in scenario
  name. Route, provenance, manifests and timestamps also differ.
- Explain conversion/fusion classification boundaries, retire the withdrawn
  pwilkin family number, distinguish the earlier 13.9% throughput pair from
  the later profiled comparator table, and distinguish census-covered linear
  launches from a full per-prefill GPU census.
- Rank correct F16 activation materialization, routed MoE gate/up/down/repair,
  hyper-connection, QSA, MoE reduction and dense scope extension. Current
  baseline/attribution and qualification accounting are prerequisites. Old
  family milliseconds are indicators, not promised additive recovery.
