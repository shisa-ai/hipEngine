# Keep the device-built expert schedule off layers whose experts are not NVFP4

## Summary

The clean-worktree gate rows of ce61e3b55 failed. The cause is a defect in that commit; this change
fixes it.

- **What failed.** From a clean worktree of ce61e3b55, `fast` with device routes and resident
  decode, and with the decode graph, no longer pass the gate on 256-row blocks: over 687 rows
  strict's top-1 holds on 663 and 26 rows change it with damage (3.78%, limit 3%), all in long
  context (159 rows: p90 KL 6.98, largest 15.8, strict's top-1 on 135). The other eight categories
  carry the numbers of b266a65a7. `fast` without the flags is unchanged (median 6.8e-5, p90 4.6e-3,
  fails the code category as before).
- **Cause.** The model file keeps higher-precision experts in its last four layers: layers 20 to 23
  have Q5_K gate/up and Q8_0 down experts, the other 20 have NVFP4. The host-routed block picks its
  kernel by the bank's layout. The device-routed block did not check the layout and ran the NVFP4
  grouped kernel on Q5_K and Q8_0 bytes. Those are the last four visits of every block; on the
  2,057-token bench prompt 32 of 384 block visits wrote rows of magnitude 1e7 where the tile path
  writes 8.
- **Why the earlier evidence missed it.** The model test counted calls and compared one NVFP4 layer.
  The working-tree bench gave `fast`'s 128 tokens at 575 prompt tokens, and I put the parting at
  2,057 down to rounding. I have not established why the wrong rows at 575 tokens leave the tokens
  unchanged.
- **The fix.** `block_device_routes_supported` and the explicit call require raw NVFP4 banks, the
  condition the host-routed block already uses (`_wmma_banks`). The four visits keep the
  host-routed block.

**Working-tree bench with the fix over ce61e3b55** (`fast`, 256-row blocks, resident traversal and
device routes, 128 outputs, five repetitions, W7900, mainline `UD-Q4_K_M`):

| Prompt tokens | First token | Over host-routed blocks, same flags | Over public `fast` | Over strict | Decode |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 575 | 1.329 s (1.325 to 1.335) | 1.14 times (1.518 s) | 1.14 times (1.509 s) | 7.4 times (9.804 s) | 29.26 tok/s |
| 2,057 | 5.587 s (5.575 to 5.605) | 1.16 times (6.496 s) | 1.13 times (6.300 s) | 6.7 times (37.447 s) | 26.47 tok/s |

- The 128 greedy tokens are `fast`'s at both depths.
- The 1.29 and 1.30 times recorded for ce61e3b55 included the wrong path and are withdrawn.
- At 47 prompt tokens the flagged arm's tokens are not plain `fast`'s from token 37 on. That is the
  decode arm's own arithmetic: b266a65a7 with the same flags gives the same tokens (the hash strict
  has at that depth), and no block of that prompt reaches the device path.

## Changes

- `hipengine/loading/inkling2_gguf_device.py`: `_wmma_banks` names the layout condition;
  `_wmma_rows`, `block_device_routes_supported` and `project_experts_block_device_routes` share it,
  and the explicit call names the layouts it refused.
- `tests/test_gpu_inkling2_device_block_routes.py`: the public route calls the device-routed block
  exactly once per visit whose experts are NVFP4 in every full block and never for the others; a
  visit with other experts is refused by name; the greedy tokens of the public route equal those of
  the same prompt with host-routed blocks.
