# UD-U1 repair 4: admission certificates record slot scope and effective plan contract

## Summary

U1 finding 4: the admission certificate recorded only
(backend, operations, fingerprint, stamp, covered_slots).  A partial preflight
(e.g. `slot_filter=("root.output_norm",)`) therefore produced a certificate
that `certificate_covers_artifact` accepted as full-artifact coverage, and a
contraction-enabled certificate was indistinguishable from an uncontracted
one.  Certificates are now scoped to the exact checked slots and the exact
effective resident/operation contract, and the compat API verifies both.

## Changes

- `hipengine/loading/qwen35_gguf_admission.py`:
  - New frozen `Qwen35GGUFPlanContract`: requested operations, slot filter,
    effective decode_repack (after veto/environment resolution), repack_veto,
    effective `contract_f32_linear` (after inference), and every dense-T16 /
    qmicro / planar override flag with its excluded-slot list.  Exports a
    nested `as_dict` for audit trails.
  - `Qwen35GGUFAdmissionReport` and `Qwen35GGUFAdmissionCertificate` gain
    `slot_filter` and `plan_contract` fields; `certificate()` copies them
    verbatim.
  - `certificate_covers_artifact` gains `slot_filter` and `plan_contract`
    checks: a full-artifact certificate covers any subset of slots; a
    slot-filtered certificate covers only uses inside its checked subset
    (an unspecified check means the full artifact is intended and fails for
    filtered certificates); an intended plan contract must equal the
    certificate's recorded contract exactly, and a legacy certificate without
    contract metadata fails closed.  Subset/debug loading remains supported
    (full cert → partial use).
- `tests/test_gguf_ud_admission.py`: four new tests — one-slot certificate
  does not cover full/different use but covers its exact subset and is
  covered-by the full certificate; empty-slot-filter certificate covers only
  the empty use; plan-contract binding (contraction flip, implicit repack
  veto, legacy no-contract fail-closed); contract records effective
  operations/slot scope and round-trips through `certificate()`/`as_dict`.

## Validation

- GREEN: `tests/test_gguf_ud_admission.py -q` → 52 passed; policy,
