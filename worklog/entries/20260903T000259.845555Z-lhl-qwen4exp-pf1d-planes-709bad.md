# PF-1d plane candidates: d4 rejected at unit level, d4x2 admitted to gate

## Summary

Built and measured the PF-1d activation-plane candidates at all seven policy
shapes: single-plane d4 (the 2.40x lever) fails unit numerics decisively and
is rejected without a whole-model gate run; two-plane d4x2 is numerically
tight at the earlier-measured 1.38x and proceeds to the production numerics
gate as the remaining PF-1d lever.

Verdict: **d4 (2.40x speed lever) fails unit numerics decisively** (top-1
99.0-99.6%, max_abs 1.5-3.4) — no whole-model gate run is justified; this also
explains why the production profile selected the three-plane chain. **d4x2 is
the viable candidate**: top-1 512/512 at every shape, mean_rel at the d4x3
chain's level (1.2-1.6e-4 vs <1e-4), max_abs 0.006-0.011 (vs <2e-3), at the
earlier-measured **1.38x** isolated speedup (MMQ family 6.93 -> ~5.0 s/req at
p4096 projection).
