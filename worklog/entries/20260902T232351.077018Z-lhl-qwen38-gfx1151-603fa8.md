# qwen38 C1 R4 wmma floor fix

## Summary

Fix the production explicit C1 MTP collapse named in the 2026-09-03 refresh:
the B1 rows>1 transferred verify owner is now floored off for packed shapes
below 8 rows, so width-1 (and any R<=4) verify passes keep the per-row GEMV
owners that measured healthy (18.168 tok/s, 1.582x AR, acceptance 0.7889,
AR equality 10/10).

## Decisions

- Floor constant `MTP_SERVING_TARGET_WMMA_PREFILL_MIN_ROWS = 8` in
  `hipengine/runtime/gguf_linear.py`: R8+ is the measured-healthy retention
  scope (R8/R12/R16/R20-R32); R4 measured broken. Explicit env opt-in (1/on)
  still defeats the floor for bisection; opt-out (0/off) forces GEMV
  everywhere. Profile scoping unchanged (strict/fallback keeps GEMV).
- Clamped at the two verify entry points in `qwen35_gguf_runner.py`
  (`verify_target_blocks_batch` via `layout.rows`, `verify_target_block` via
  `len(input_token_ids)`), where the packed row count is known; job-level
  resolution and the mtp2 job builder are unchanged. Plain prefill WMMA sites
  are out of scope (large rows, not verify).
- Removal condition: the floor disappears only together with an exact
  small-row owner passing a C1 AR-equality RED gate (also recorded in
  docs/REFACTOR.md).

## Changes

- `hipengine/runtime/gguf_linear.py`: min-rows constant,
  `mtp_serving_target_wmma_prefill_allows_rows()`, `packed_rows` kwarg on
  `mtp_serving_target_use_wmma_prefill()`.
- `hipengine/runtime/qwen35_gguf_runner.py`: clamp both verify entry points.
- `tests/test_mtp_serving_target_wmma_transfer.py`: RED-first additions
  pinning the floor, the R8+ retention scope, env escape hatch, and runner
  consumption.
