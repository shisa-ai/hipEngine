The planner decision is APPROVED, not awaiting a parent answer: selected-call

cannot turn the production paired intent into a singleton; a genuinely

argument captures. Singleton certificates cannot authorize paired calls.

  selected calls include every ordered partner's complete source/resident/

### Boundary and qualification limits

These are production **caller-interface** contracts, not a mirror of every
optimized leaf. An internal alternative can share one only with identical
supplied operand, output/state ownership, shape and row semantics. Arithmetic
selection remains governed by existing profile/variant policy; a baseline key
does not prove a changed caller boundary. Optional raw/T16 selected DP4A
adapters with unrepresented external conversion/workspace semantics refuse
admission; mandatory X8 adapters are explicitly bound. Baseline prefill GDN
refuses FP16 state rather than borrowing its F32-state contract. These are
concrete fail-closed limits, not new runtime numerical restrictions or new
profile authorization. Complete entry consumption of these intents remains
F1; the actual LLM automatic profile/FP16-state binder hole remains F5.
