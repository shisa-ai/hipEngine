# Retraction: DE-11's kernel sum is post-promotion, so its remainder figures stand

## Summary

**I introduced an error into `DE-11` and this entry retracts it.** Worklog
`20261004T100141` recorded a "third correction" claiming `DE-11` subtracts a
pre-promotion kernel sum from a post-promotion wall: the kernel sums
(`46.1927` at p512, `47.1298` at p4096) were attributed to `X-4`'s
`2789c19d6` capture dated 2026-09-29, the walls to the 2026-10-04 thermal
packet, with `DE-1`'s dp4a promotion and `DE-3`'s block-size lever (both
2026-09-30) landing in between. It then adjusted the kernel sum by
`-8.660 ms/step` and concluded the p512 remainder was ~10.1 ms/step rather than
1.47, and that the launch/transition lever was available at short prompts.

**Both halves of that are wrong, and the artifact said so.** The kernel sums come
from
`benchmarks/results/2026-10-04-qwen4exp-thermal-protocol-and-llamacpp-basis/bench-decode-rep-trace.json`
- a **2026-10-04** artifact whose own `note` field reads "decode-rep window of
`qwen4exp_canonical_ar_bench.py hipengine --case-id`, under rocprofv3" - and
**its `families` breakdown contains
`gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_q8_1_dp4a_silu_logical128_t64`, which is `DE-1`'s
promoted owner and cannot appear in a pre-promotion capture.**

**So the kernel sum and the wall are the same configuration, the subtraction is
sound, and `DE-11`'s original figures stand: ~1.47 ms/step at p512 and ~10.56 at
p4096, growing ~7x.** Withdrawn with the correction: the ~10.1 ms/step p512
figure, the `-8.660 ms/step` promotion adjustment, and the claim that the
launch/transition lever is available at short prompts.

**What still stands, and why it is independent.** The fourth correction in
`DE-11` - that the decode wall tracks kernel time 1:1, measured as 1.545 ms/step
of wall for `DE-3`'s 1.416 ms/step kernel lever at `code-p512` - does not use the
kernel sum at all, only a wall difference between two arms of one interleaved
A/B. It is unaffected. The same is true of the graph-launch refutation
(0.0004 ms per launch). Both are recorded in
`benchmarks/results/2026-10-04-qwen4exp-wall-tracks-kernel-time/`.

**Method note, recorded because this is the failure mode to avoid.** The
retracted claim was an **inference from commit dates and an assumption that
`DE-1`'s and `DE-3`'s savings are depth-independent and additive** - not a
measurement. The artifact that refutes it was already in-tree and committed, and
its own contents (a kernel name in a `families` dict) were the decisive evidence.
The check that would have prevented the error was to read the artifact's
provenance and contents **before** reasoning about dates. Two prior iterations in
this same campaign had already been retracted for the same class of mistake -
asserting a mechanism from correlated evidence instead of from the artifact.
