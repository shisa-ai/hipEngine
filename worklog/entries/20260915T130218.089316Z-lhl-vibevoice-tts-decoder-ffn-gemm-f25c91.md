# VibeVoice-TTS decoder FFN: batched GEMM instead of the per-output GEMV (4.8x decode)

## Summary

`decoder.decode` was the second-largest stage of the warm request (1.303 s of
5.474 s, 25 frames at 52 ms each) and the acoustic tokenizer only holds 687 M
parameters, so the kernel was reading 1.28 GiB per call at an effective 25 GB/s
against the ~231 GB/s the bf16 backbone reaches. The cause was the launch shape,
not the arithmetic volume: the ConvNeXt FFN linears called
`dense_gemv_out_bf16`, whose grid is `(out_features, rows)` with one 256-thread
block per output element. At `rows=3200` a `32 -> 128` projection launched
409,600 blocks whose 256-wide tree reduction is about 100x its 32-FMA count.

Dispatching those two linears to the batched GEMM at `rows >= 8` takes
`decoder.decode` from **51.27 ms to 10.65 ms** in a same-session A/B (**4.81x**)
and the decode stage from **1.303 s to 0.343 s**.

## Evidence

Microbenchmark of the two kernels on the decoder's own FFN shapes, minimum of
five reps, one block-set each:

| rows | in | out | per-output GEMV | batched GEMM |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3200 | 32 | 128 | 2195.2 us | 38.1 us |
| 1600 | 64 | 256 | 2414.3 us | 54.6 us |
| 800 | 128 | 512 | 2492.7 us | 85.5 us |
| 200 | 256 | 1024 | 1293.0 us | 82.1 us |
| 40 | 512 | 2048 | 609.8 us | 89.0 us |
| 8 | 1024 | 4096 | 330.1 us | 150.0 us |
| 1 | 2048 | 8192 | 86.2 us | 428.7 us |

The last row is why the threshold exists: at one row the GEMV's grid is one block
per output and it wins by 5x.

Isolated A/B in one process, same session, same minute, threshold neutralized to
`10**9` for the "before":

```
OFF (per-output GEMV)  decoder.decode 51.27 ms   semantic 39.61 ms
ON  (batched GEMM)     decoder.decode 10.65 ms   semantic 38.29 ms
```

Session bench, same protocol as the retained row:

| | pooled RTF | warm | LM | diffusion | decode | semantic |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| before | 1.642 | 5.474 s | 1.587 s | 1.390 s | 1.303 s | 1.013 s |
| after (median of 5) | 1.381 | 4.603 s | 1.547 s | 1.383 s | 0.343 s | 1.135 s |

The five after-runs measured 1.316, 1.381, 1.408, 1.431, 1.434. Decode is stable
across all of them (0.343-0.355 s); the LM, diffusion and semantic stages moved
together by about 8% between the two measurement sessions, which is host drift,
not a regression from this change - the isolated A/B above shows the semantic
encoder unchanged and the decoder 4.8x faster in the same minute.

## This is a production-route change, not a bit-exact one

The batched GEMM reassociates the reduction. On the decoder's FFN shapes it
differs from the GEMV on 6-27 of 409,600 elements, at up to 4 bf16 ULP and 1.95e-3
absolute. Two gates were run against that:

- **The 27-token constrained chain stays exact** (`chain_exact` true,
  `negative_conditions_match` true) on every run, including the retained artifact.
  The reassociation does not flip a token.
- **The generated-audio quality suite is unchanged**: 17 of 18 request-runs pass
  over three seeds, with the same single `two-2turn` seed-20260915 failure as
  before the change. No new intelligibility, duration, level, silence,
  repetition or attribution failure appeared.

`tests/test_live_vibevoice_tts_decoder_gpu.py` pins both the boundary and the
bound: `_ffn_linear` selects the GEMV below `_FFN_GEMM_MIN_ROWS` and the GEMM at
or above it, and the two agree within 4 bf16 ULP, on under 0.1% of elements, and
within 0.02 absolute on each of the five multi-row FFN shapes.

## One documented invariant had to be weakened

`decode_bulk` was bit-identical to per-frame streaming, because the old kernel's
arithmetic did not depend on the row count. The FFN dispatch does depend on it, so
a segment of 8 or more frames now runs the GEMM at its stage-0 rows where
per-frame streaming runs the GEMV. Measured bulk-vs-streaming RMS relative error
is 5.8e-3 (single) and 6.7e-3 (two). The test keeps exact equality for segments
below the threshold and uses a declared 1e-2 RMS bound above it, against the 2e-2
the GPU-vs-oracle parity test allows. `decode_bulk` is not on the session path
(`decode()` calls it with one row); its other caller is
`scripts/vibevoice_tts_decoder_rocprof_smoke.py`.

## Next
