# GD-1 is closed: its two columns agree, and the census's own audit invented the contradiction

## Summary

`GD-1` was the one row the §11 census audit left actionable while stating that it
defined no experiment. The audit's premise was that the row's two columns
contradict: Evidence reads *"Inside noise, not additive"*, First-bounded-experiment
reads *"Do not re-screen unchanged"*, so either the effect is inside noise and the
row should be closed, or it is real and the prescribed experiment is not an
experiment.

**Reading the source of the two figures shows the columns agree, and the audit's
premise was the error.** Both come from
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-16-disabled-family-recoverable-time/README.md`, a
nine-family sweep on `code-p4096` whose fallback median prefill is **23.648 s**:
rank 5 `GDN_COLWARPS_PREFILL` at 23.420 s (**+0.228 s, +0.96%**) and rank 6
`GDN_PEER_PREFILL` at 23.522 s (**+0.126 s, +0.53%**). That artifact's own limits
section settles the Evidence column:

> "The differences at ranks 5-9 (0.066-0.228 s) are within the run-to-run spread
> this project has already measured, and should be read as 'no material effect'
> rather than as a ranking."

So *"Inside noise"* is a **sourced reading**, not a hedge, and *"do not re-screen"*
is the correct action for it. There is nothing to correct in either column and
nothing to run.

## The arithmetic that looked like a contradiction compared two denominators

A first pass at this row argued the deltas must be wrong because they are
inconsistent with the campaign's measured GDN family share: +0.228 s and +0.126 s
sum to 0.354 s, against a whole-family 194.487 ms of 3914.468 ms at p512 (4.97%),
which would make the two deltas 182% of the entire family.

That comparison is invalid and the check is worth recording. The deltas are
**wall** deltas on a **23.648 s p4096 prefill**; the family figure is a **kernel
share** of a **3.914 s p512 sum**. Different shapes and different denominators.
0.228 / 23.648 = **0.96%** of that wall, which is exactly what a ~20% improvement
inside a ~5% family produces. Both statements describe the same effect and there is
no contradiction to settle.

## The row's real defect: it names a kernel for a gate's measurement

`GDN_COLWARPS_PREFILL=1` is the **gate** that admits the tiled16 owner, not a
request for the columnwarp kernel. The runner consults
`qwen4_exp_gdn_tile16_prefill_selected` only inside `if colwarps_prefill:`, and that
predicate returns the **tiled16** route for the envelope shape, using the columnwarp
parent only for non-envelope shapes and for the
`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_GDN_TILE16_PREFILL=0` opt-out
(`hipengine/runtime/qwen4_exp_runner.py:286-308`; its docstring records the tiled16
owner's own canonical A/B at +0.49%/+0.47%/+0.37% at p512/p1024/p4096).

So the +0.228 s is "the gate plus its tiled16 owner versus the recovery default's
serial route". The columnwarp *kernel* the row is named after is a measured **loss**
against that owner: GD-3's route screen puts `columnwarps` at 7.610 ms per 1K tokens
against `tiled16`'s 4.961, i.e. **1.53x slower**.

**No candidate remains.** Both flags are production-ON today, which the section-7
row already records, so `GD-1` describes the live default's own wall rather than a
proposed change.
