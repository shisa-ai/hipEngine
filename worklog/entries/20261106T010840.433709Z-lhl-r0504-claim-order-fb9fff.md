# Preserve activation claim when a short prompt follows a chunked opener

## Summary

Fix claim ordering discovered while inspecting R0504 overlap prerequisites.
A long claim-opening chunk must exclude later short openers too, not just
other long openers. Co-activation remains unimplemented and R0504 stays open.

## Changes

- Defer every subsequent claim opener after selecting an opener whose prompt
  spans multiple chunks. Deferred cursors are unchanged.
- Preserve short-before-long and short-before-short serial execution: a short
  prompt closes its activation before the next selected row runs.
- No provider-slot capacity, arithmetic or concurrency policy changed.
