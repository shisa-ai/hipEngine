# The norm-family census totals are microseconds per step

## Summary

`worklog/entries/20260913T073710.202482Z-lhl-ud-norm-fixed5120-rowslab-8c2632.md`
and the rollup it produced label the `add_rmsnorm` family totals as "ms/step".
They are **microseconds per step**. The family figures are 0.770 -> 0.329
ms/step, not 770.07 -> 328.56 ms/step, and the whole-verifier delta is
**-0.4464 ms/step**, not -446.7 ms/step. The percentages, the per-call figures,
the bit-identity proof, the gate, and the paired rates are unaffected and stand
as recorded.

## Changes

- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-13-ud-gfx1100-norm-fixed5120-row-slab.json`:
  `add_rmsnorm_family_us_per_step` plus the derived `_ms_per_step`,
  `whole_verifier_delta_us_per_step` -446.4, and a `unit_note` carrying the
  cross-check.
- `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`, `benchmarks/README.md`,
  `docs/UD-GFX1151-OPTIMIZE.md`: family figures restated in both units, and the
  true-AR explanation now uses the measured 64 launches per step at
  16.9 -> 5.9 us (about -0.7 ms on a 30.5 ms single-row step) rather than an
  unverified 127-launch count at the verifier's per-call figure.

## Validation

The follow-on unit for `gguf_rounded_add_rmsnorm_bf16_f32_weight` recomputed the
census from the raw `kernel_trace.csv` and the cross-check failed: the family
saving is 770.07 - 328.56 = 441.5, and the whole-step delta is
37.067 - 36.6206 = 0.4464 ms. The two agree only if the family totals are in
microseconds. Independently, `scripts/gguf_mtp_verifier_rocprof.py` emits
`summary.avg_kernel_ms` (36.6206, milliseconds) and
`summary.buckets[*].ms_per_step` / `top_kernels[*].ms_per_step` in the same
unit, so a 64-call family at 5.13 us per call is 0.328 ms/step, not 328 ms.

`scripts/sync_benchmark_readme.py --check` passes after the edits.
