# atlas AR serves here, atlas MTP does not; harness no longer scores empty responses

## Summary

Atlas is usable on this host as an **autoregressive** yardstick and not as a
speculative one. Its MTP path fails at every context length tried; its AR path is
healthy.

With `NUM_DRAFTS=4`, atlas starts, logs `Speculative decoding: ENABLED (4
drafts/step)` and `MTP gate: throughput-arbitrated (K=4)`, answers `/v1/models`
with `max_model_len: 262144`, and then fails every real request:

    ERROR verify_dflash_step: decode_verify_dflash: SSM MTP intermediate buffers not allocated (need K-1 ...)
    ERROR sequence: free_sequence: gpu.synchronize after zero_slot(0): cuStreamSynchronize ...
    ERROR phase_start_prefills: Prefill start error: cuMemsetD32Async failed: status 901

The visible symptom is one short burst of content followed by every later request
completing in ~1 ms with zero content tokens. It reproduces at `MAX_SEQ_LEN`
262144 and 32768 and at `MAX_BATCH` 8 and 3, so it is neither a context-length nor
a batch-size limit -- the batch-8 `KV OVERCOMMIT` warning was a real but separate
problem, and fixing it did not fix this.

With `NUM_DRAFTS=0` the same binary is healthy: four requests at 32K gave 29/28/29/28
content deltas, ~700 ms TTFT, ~2.2 s decode, zero errors, median 14.53 tok/s.

A likely cause is the ROCm version: atlas's published Strix Halo numbers were taken
on ROCm 7.13 and this host has 7.15.26333. Chasing that is atlas's work rather than
hipEngine's; the goal is to fix hipEngine, and a yardstick that needs its own
debugging is not worth more budget than the record in
`benchmarks/atlas-agent/README.md`.

**Harness defect found and fixed by this.** The broken arm reported a median of
~260 tok/s, because a request that returns no content completes in ~1 ms and the
rate divides by that near-zero elapsed time. `http_1to1_bench.py` now treats a
response with no content as an error, so empty output is visible as a failure
rather than as an implausibly fast engine. That figure must never be reported.

Consequence: a best-vs-best **MTP** comparison cannot be made against atlas here.
The viable options, in order of value, are AR vs AR at 256K with `NUM_DRAFTS=0`
(clean, fully matched except quant), hipEngine MTP against atlas AR (explicitly not
a speculation comparison), or a different MTP-capable yardstick such as llama.cpp,
whose protocol side is largely covered by `scripts/mtp-bench.py`.

## Changes

- `benchmarks/atlas-agent/README.md`: a "Status on this host" section recording
  what was needed to get atlas running at all (ROCm build, conda ROCm SDK, the
  HIP-to-CUDA shim on the loader path, and `MAX_BATCH` bounded by the KV pool) and
  what does not work, with the failure text and the recommended alternatives.
- `benchmarks/atlas-agent/http_1to1_bench.py`: an empty response is now an error.

## Validation

`ENGINES=atlas ARMS=single OUTPUT_LEN=32 PROMPT_LIMIT=2` at `MAX_CONTEXT` 262144
and 32768: both produced `deltas=3` then `deltas=0` and four
`cuMemsetD32Async failed` lines. The same invocation with `NUM_DRAFTS=0` at 32768
produced 29/28/29/28 deltas, 0 errors, and `Scheduler started (batched mode,
max_batch=3, mtp=false)`. Build and environment detail in
`worklog/entries/20260920T091519.652205Z-lhl-atlas-agent-harness-ee07c7.md`.
