# EVIE matched-protocol torch comparison

## Summary

Closed the review's protocol-fairness finding with a matched comparison:
identical inputs (real ColQwen3_5Processor outputs — 8 seeded pages, 8
20-token padded queries), preprocessing outside timing on both engines,
full 8x8 MaxSim scoring, best of 3.

## Results

Performance (totals): torch fp32 3990.8 ms / bf16 1299.5 ms; hip fp32
7601.7 ms / fp16 2134.1 ms. At matched precision torch leads (1.9x fp32,
1.64x production) — the reference batches all 8 pages into one forward;
hipEngine v1 is single-page sequential. The earlier "fp16 beats torch
fp32" headline is cross-precision, now labeled as such in
MODEL-EVIE.md.

Retrieval quality: hip fp32 matches torch fp32 scores to 1e-4; hip fp16
is closer to the fp32 teacher than torch's own bf16 (max rel 0.23% vs
3.5%) and preserves the fp32 argmax ranking 8/8 (torch bf16 6/8,
Kendall tau 0.91).
