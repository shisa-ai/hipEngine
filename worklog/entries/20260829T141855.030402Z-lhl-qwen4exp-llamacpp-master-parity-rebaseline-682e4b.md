# Re-baseline llama.cpp master and profile parity gaps

## Summary

Opened the llama.cpp parity campaign. Updated both same-host llama.cpp trees
to merged master `17252c769` (qwen4exp support merged via #27742 plus the
graph-splits reduction #27880), rebuilt HIP and Vulkan Release binaries with
the recorded flags, and re-ran the exact matched llama-bench protocol on the
pinned UD-Q4_K_XL artifact. Master is 8.0-8.5% faster than the old PR-branch
baseline on HIP and Vulkan decode jumps 28% (tg32 18.7→24.1). Profiled
master's pp508 with rocprofv3 and built the per-kernel-family comparison
against hipEngine's final production profile, identifying WMMA-vs-dp4a as the
MoE matmul root cause. Surveyed forks (ik_llama.cpp, ROCmFP4 forks) and the
public Strix Halo reports: the 50-65 tok/s decode claims are ROCmFP4-quant
DFlash2/MTP speculative forks, not same-quant no-spec comparisons.

## Validation

- Warm two-pass llama-bench HIP: pp508 298.6/294.1, pp1006 307.2/318.9,
  tg32 17.31/17.07 tok/s; Vulkan: pp508 333.8/332.1, pp1006 310.5/308.4,
  tg32 24.04/24.13 tok/s. Commands and parameters recorded in the artifact.
- Family comparison (pp508 kernels): llama 1.693 s/5121 launches vs hipEngine
  6.548 s/3684; MoE+dense matmul 1258 vs 5527 ms, GDN 86.0 vs 750.7 ms, QSA
  14.5 vs 141.5 ms, elementwise hipEngine-favoring ~126 vs ~275 ms.
- Root cause verified in source: `common.cuh` defines `AMD_WMMA_AVAILABLE` for
  RDNA3/RDNA4 HIP builds; `mmq.cuh` `use_mma_data_layout()` routes mul_mat_q
  through WMMA fragments on gfx1151, while hipEngine MMQ kernels are dp4a.
- `python3 scripts/worklog.py check`, `scripts/sync_benchmark_readme.py
  --check` pass.
