# Promote unequal Q4 linear-attention prefill

## Summary

Promoted the exact unequal-output Q4T16 linear-attention QKV/gate leaf as the
model-scoped Qwen3.6-27B gfx1100 bulk-prefill default. It improves every
required full-engine median on both gfx1100 boards without adding storage or
changing decode/MTP ownership.

## Changes

- Registered the leaf under `linear_pair/gguf_q4_k_t16_v1`, added a nested and
  cache-safe request context, and admitted only dense Qwen3.6-27B
  `MOSTLY_Q4_K_M`, rows >=512, K5120/N10240+N6144.
- Kept the two exact Q4T16 singleton projections as the registry-miss,
  small-row, peer-backend, and model/shape fallback; no environment selector,

- Updated the kernel catalog, refactor trigger, campaign scorecard, root and
  benchmark rollups, changelog, and compact retained artifact.
