# RT-1 generator-chunk win reproduces at n=2: -5.74%/-5.80%, 183x separation

## Summary

`worklog/entries/20261003T190751.024622Z-lhl-rt-1-genchunk-serving-loop-6593b2.md`
reported the generator-chunk serving-loop win at n=1. Repeated independently: the
effect is stable and the arms are completely separated.

| arm | prefill wall rep1 / rep2 | within-arm spread | peer TTFT | peer ITL median | peer max gap |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| `g1024_l1024` | 15.992 / 15.998 s | 0.005 s (0.03%) | 16.854 / 16.864 s | 49.19 / 49.24 ms | 147.9 / 155.5 ms |
| `g4096_l1024` | **15.075 / 15.070 s** | 0.005 s (0.03%) | 15.770 / 15.777 s | 49.09 / 49.15 ms | **75.1 / 71.9 ms** |
| `g4096_l4096` | **15.001 / 15.072 s** | 0.071 s (0.47%) | 15.717 / 15.796 s | 49.17 / 49.10 ms | 70.5 / 73.3 ms |
| **delta** | **-5.74% / -5.80%** | | -6.43% / -6.44% | -0.2% | **-49% / -54%** |

**Separation is far beyond RT-1's own bar.** Within-arm spread is 0.005 s (0.03%)
for both arms, the arms **do not overlap** - the slowest `g4096_l1024` run (15.075 s)
is 5.74% faster than the fastest `g1024_l1024` run (15.992 s) - and the median delta
is **183x** the larger within-arm spread, against the **5.70x** RT-1 required for the
original -4.94% claim.

**The cap adds nothing on top of the chunk.** At repetition 2, `g4096_l4096`
(-5.79%) and `g4096_l1024` (-5.80%) are indistinguishable, and `g4096_l4096` is the
*more* variable arm (0.071 s vs 0.005 s spread). That is the same conclusion the
earlier packet's cap-only arms reach from the other direction.

**The peer's worst gap halves in both repetitions** (147.9/155.5 -> 75.1/71.9 ms)
while its ITL median moves -0.2%, so the earlier "no fairness cost, and the peer
benefits" finding is not a single-run artifact.
