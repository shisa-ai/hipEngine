# Merged-lane layer_outer capacity reaches 172,288 prompt tokens

## Summary

Merged the GPU0 speed campaign (90b9aa510: staging-upload hoist, in-place
finalize, wave6 gfx1100 producer, chunked keep-scan INT8 append,
wave-grouped INT8 producer) into the capacity lane (merge eb3242a7a,

729 CPU + 35 GPU tests green on the merge), committed the previously
uncommitted layer_outer prefill change as 22ded5522, then re-validated
capacity on the merged tree.

Merge validation at 139,264 tokens: whole-card peak moved by 57,344 bytes
(memory-neutral), greedy tokens identical across the merge on all eight
decode steps, logits sha256 differ (kernel evaluation-order change, same
standard as the speed campaign's verification). Single-session diagnostic
decode timing at 139,264 context: 469.09 -> 47.10 ms/step mean
(steady-state 43.6-44.4 ms). No throughput claim is made
(performance_claim false; capacity/finiteness/ownership scope only).

GPU1 idle baseline verified per run, source clean at eb3242a7a):

Largest observed passing prompt: **172,288** (+50.2% over the layer_outer
pre-merge 114,688; +133.6% over the 73,728 campaign baseline). 176,128 is
the smallest tested OOM; intermediate sizes unqualified. 172,800 was
started and cancelled by operator choice before completion — unqualified,
not evidence of either outcome. Proven maximum: false.

Marginal slope is non-constant (store grows sublinearly as the DMS
compression ratio improves with context): 45,346 B/token (73,728-139,264),
33,899 B/token (139,264-168,704), 80,191 B/token (168,704-172,288, the
near-full tail). Linear extrapolation is not a capacity claim.

Tracked ledger reconciled in bytes at 139,264 (weights 16,401,463,296 +
store 2,501,602,448 + oracle 571,473,920 + hidden 2x1,428,684,800 +
token buf 1,116,160 = 22,333,025,424; residual unaudited 1,660,667,912;
tracked peak 23,993,693,336). The whole-card-minus-tracked delta
(423,340,904 B) is recorded without attribution.
