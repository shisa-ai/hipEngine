# MX-1's F16 gate/up owner is 1.54-2.42x the iu8-risk main kernel, so the repair it removes cannot pay for it

## Summary

`MX-1` (F16 routed experts, no repair pass) was refuted on numerics on 2026-09-30:
the full route fails the production envelope *worse* than its own gate/up half, and
two distinct operand classes were measured failing. What was never measured is the
half of the row's acceptance target that is not numerics - **whether the F16
replacement main kernel can come in at or below the iu8-risk main kernel it would
replace**. The row's target is `F16 gate/up + down <= 4,280.901 ms` (the iu8-risk
expert owners) out of a 1,219.978 ms repair ceiling.

`scripts/qwen4exp_moe_owner_ab.py --route gate-up` now puts the three registered
owners of `moe_linear` / `prefill_rows_ge64_exact_grouped_q4_gate_up` in one arms
pair for the first time: the production default
`selected_dual_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill_bf16_bf16_out`, its declared fallback
`selected_dual_grouped_rowbatch8_out4_expertgrid64_bf16_bf16_out`, and the F16
route `selected_dual_wmma_prefill_compact_bf16_bf16_out` (what
`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_EXACT_GROUPED_Q4=0` selects).

**The F16 owner is slower than the production iu8-risk owner in 8 of 8 cases**,
main kernel only, real Q4_K geometry, 15 HIP-event repetitions, 2 primed launches,
same host:

| distribution | rows | padded rows | iu8-risk ms | F16 compact ms | F16 vs iu8-risk |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| balanced | 512 | 8096 | 8.5398 | 20.6290 | **+141.6%** |
| balanced | 1024 | 8192 | 8.6486 | 20.8694 | **+141.3%** |
| balanced | 4096 | 8192 | 8.8984 | 21.2436 | **+138.7%** |
| balanced | 10240 | 13696 | 14.7203 | 31.5391 | **+114.3%** |
| sparse | 512 | 2048 | 2.2562 | 5.3758 | **+138.3%** |
| sparse | 1024 | 2048 | 2.2867 | 5.4838 | **+139.8%** |
| sparse | 4096 | 4096 | 4.5086 | 9.3266 | **+106.9%** |
| sparse | 10240 | 10240 | 10.9214 | 16.8084 | **+53.9%** |

Median **+138.5%**, range **+53.9% to +141.6%**. The iu8-risk owner's own repeat
drift is 0.22% max, so the separation is 240x the noise.

**The most favourable case already exceeds the entire prize.** MX-1's prize is the
repair, 1,219.978 ms = 28.5% of the iu8-risk GEMM time and 22.2% of the
routed-expert total. The smallest gap measured is +53.9%, and applied to the
gate/up half alone (2,779.713 ms) that is **+1,498 ms - more than the 1,219.978 ms
of repair the route exists to remove, before the down half is counted at all**. The
median case is +138.5% = +3,850 ms, 3.2x the prize.

**And that comparison is biased in the F16 owner's favour**: `max_risks=0` means the
iu8-risk owner is never charged for writing its risk queue, which if anything
understates it. So the negative survives the direction of the bias that matters.

Combined with the 2026-09-30 numerics refutation, `MX-1` is now closed on **both**
halves of its own acceptance target: it fails the production envelope, and it could
not have paid for itself if it had passed. The row's remaining route is not a
kernel.

The fallback `grouped_rowbatch8` owner reproduces its recorded loss (+19.6% to
+394.9%, median +157.8%), which is the control that the harness change did not
disturb the existing comparison.

No kernel, default, flag or dispatch changed. Nothing promoted.

## Validation

- `scripts/qwen4exp_moe_owner_ab.py --route gate-up`, 8 cases (2 distributions x 4
  row counts), 15 repetitions per case, medians of per-launch HIP event pairs,
  `max_abs_repeat_drift_pct` 0.2229.
- The tile passed to the F16 arm was read from the production call site rather than
  chosen, which matters because a smaller `tile_m` would have manufactured this
  negative.
- The two pre-existing arms reproduce their archived values (iu8-risk primary
  unchanged; `grouped_rowbatch8` slower in 8 of 8, median +157.8% against the
  recorded +19.61% to +389.24% range), so the added arm did not perturb the harness.
