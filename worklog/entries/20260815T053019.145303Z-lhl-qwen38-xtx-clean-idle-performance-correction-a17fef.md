# Correct Qwen3.8 XTX performance after idle-GPU rerun

## Summary

A human report that another process may have occupied the GPUs was correct in
substance: the previously published Qwen3.8 natural25 AR denominator was badly
contaminated. Strictly serial RX 7900 XTX reruns admitted with both GPUs idle
and no KFD PIDs measure production at 35.287 true-AR and 62.440 native-B3 tok/s,
not 21.679/60.925. The corrected same-run MTP ratio is 1.7695x, not 2.8104x. A
matched clean pre-fusion control measures 34.007/62.167, retaining fused SiLU
for 3.764% AR and 0.439% B3 wins with every category non-regressive.
