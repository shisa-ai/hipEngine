# QSA attention-section sizing: 147 launches/step, 12 per layer

## Summary

Final sizing for the decode dispatch-gap line's remaining big seam, from the
committed code-p4096 trace: the attention-like sections (QSA sparse attention
across the model's 12 QSA layers plus the GDN decode mixer) own **147 launches
per decode step** - 4.80 ms/step of kernel time and 0.67 ms/step of
intra-section gaps. The QSA chain runs **12 launches per layer**: norm_rope,
score, topk_expand, split_norm_rope, write_paged_kv, the three separate
sparse-attention passes (scores, coefficients, values), and gate_context.

The deep-fusion target for the next multi-session build: the three
sparse-attention passes are one attention computation split three ways - the
natural fusion candidate is one kernel (or two), taking the chain from 12
toward 8-9 launches per layer and removing their shared-load traffic and node
dispatches. Expected value is again in the ~1% class per seam, consistent with
the router-chain fusion's measured resolution limit: the decode dispatch-gap
prize needs several such fusions or the whole-step graph.
