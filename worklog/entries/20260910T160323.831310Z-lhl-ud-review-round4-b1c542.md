# Review round 4: per-tensor Q3_K bisection - one strict slot qualifies K_M at 3.4% cost

## Summary

The reviewer's recommended path - per-tensor attribution before any
threshold change - was executed and it recovered most of the speed. The
seven Q3_K tensors of UD-Q4_K_M were bisected leave-one-out on the failing
prompt: no single tensor is the sole carrier, but `layers.0.ffn_up` (the
earliest Q3_K tensor) accounts for ~85% of the mean and ~92% of the
max-row KL. The shipped policy now pins exactly that slot to the strict
GEMV (stamp-keyed `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_PREFILL_STRICT_SLOTS`); the other six
keep W4A16. K_M passes the tokenized 18-prompt category gate on seeds
7/11/23; the four-arm re-measure reads UD K_M 904.1 vs plain 980.7
(0.922x, ~3.4% pin cost) and K_S 893.2 vs 944.5 (0.946x, unchanged - no
K_S row in the table). Both artifacts are now quality-qualified with
performance claims.

  `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_PREFILL_STRICT_SLOTS` declaration, keyed

  (single launch, IQ4_XS dual gate) thread the weight's spec slot path.
- `qwen35_gguf_runner.py`: `_iq_dense_mmq_strict_slots()` resolves the
  artifact identity (file type + UD preset key, the same policy identity
  the dense-pair tables use) at session open; plain controls (preset None)
  can never match a UD row.
- Three regression tests in `tests/test_gguf_iq_dense_mmq_route.py`:
  pinned slot stays strict / unpinned routes, no session -> no pin, and the
  table's stamp keying.

## Measurement-methodology notes (recorded for future rounds)

- The GPU latched SCLK at 0 MHz mid-marathon (power-gate stuck; recovered
  via `rocm-smi --gpureset -d 1`); prefill was unaffected but it muddied
  several intermediate decode numbers. The sweep's decode protocol needs
  `--graph-replay-decode` - it defaults to eager decode for the gguf
  engine, which measures ~12 tok/s and is NOT comparable to the published
  graph numbers (~26).
- Decode/prefill numbers reproduce the published four-arm exactly once the
  GPU is settled (ed8188871 build re-measured 936.7 prefill vs the
  published 936.4).

## Next

The Q3_K speed recovery lever: a precision-preserving owner (strict
accumulation association with shared decoded weights, split-K with a
deterministic strict-order merge, compensated residual accumulation, or a
higher-precision decoded-weight representation). Cooperative geometry
alone cannot help - coop32 vs one-wave is bit-exact. Recovering the
~28ms/slot pin cost and the remaining Q3_K strict gap is the campaign's
next optimization target; GDN (~35ms) and norm/RoPE fusion are separate,
smaller campaigns.
