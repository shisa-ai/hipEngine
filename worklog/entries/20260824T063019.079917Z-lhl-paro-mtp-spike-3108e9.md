# Retain explicit PARO MTP provider-contract spike

## Summary

Implemented and retained the bounded `docs/MTP-PARO.md` S2/S3 provider repair
behind `HIPENGINE_MTP_PROPOSER_TARGET_CONTRACT=1`. The spike corrects target-
hidden observation/reseed and replaces the private cap65536 F16 scorer with the
borrowed target full-vocabulary W8A16 scorer. Current exact D24 and strict D64
evidence strongly favors repairing the stitched provider rather than starting a
clean-slate implementation. The spike remains explicit/default-off until its
parity, repeat, lifecycle, registered-profile, and fast-verifier gates close.
