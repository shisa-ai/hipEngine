# the prefill step is 100 percent kernel-busy, and the unaccounted time was never a gap

## Summary

A `rocprofv3` kernel trace of one 512-token prefill step ends the budget arithmetic and
**corrects the central claim of the previous two entries.**

**The sum of kernel durations is 697.4 ms against an unprofiled wall of 679.2 ms --
busy/wall = 1.027.** The step is essentially 100 percent kernel-busy. **There is no 36.2 ms
of idle time and there never was.** The "unaccounted" time in the step budget was
un-instrumented kernels, and the "in-situ penalty" that `20260928T184000` attributed to
inter-launch gaps and `20260928T190000` then bounded at the wrapper is not gap time at all.

## The correction

`20260928T184000` computed 687.7 - (287.7 + 189.8 + 173.97) = 36.2 ms and read the
remainder as idle time; `20260928T190000` then tried to attribute it to host dispatch. **The
remainder is other kernels**, and the trace shows where:

- **The MoE plumbing alone is 51.6 ms** -- router logits 14.7, group compact 11.4, the ds4
  activation pack 10.5, gather packed hidden 9.6, weighted accumulate 5.1 -- none of which
  any of the three budget terms counted.
- **Everything outside MMQ, attention and the dense prefill is 88.9 ms, 13.1 percent of the
  step**, against the 5.3 percent the subtraction implied.

**Two of the three named terms were also wrong.** The trace puts attention at **134.7 ms**
where the budget used **173.97** -- a 39 ms over-count, presumably from the census folding
attention-adjacent kernels into that term. The dense term is close (184.2 against 189.8).
So the budget's own terms carried more error than the residual it was chasing.

## What the readback finding still says

`20260928T184000` also suspected a host round-trip. **That is falsified independently**: the
expert wrapper split times `device->host readback` at **1.353 ms over 30 calls, 0.045 ms
each, 0.20 percent of the step.** There is no readback stall to find.

## What is actually left

**The dense in-situ rate gap is real kernel time, and its cause is still open.** The trace
does not explain why the isolated harness measures `4096 -> 2816` at 1.507 ms and the in-situ
probe measures 1.792 for the same shape and tile. Bandwidth is ruled out (24.6 percent of
peak), drift is ruled out (1.4 to 4.4 percent), launch overhead is ruled out (18 to 20
microseconds), gap time is ruled out by this trace, and the readback is 0.20 percent.

**The remaining candidate is that the two measurements are not the same work.** In situ
`launch_gguf_linear` is the funnel for the whole dense call -- if it includes an activation
quantization or pack step that the isolated harness does not perform, the 19 to 23 percent
is that step's cost and not a rate difference at all. The trace supports this: the MoE path
has a **separate 10.5 ms `gguf_q8_1_mmq_ds4_pack_bf16_kernel`** row, so packing is
instrumented separately where it is known about, and the dense funnel's own pack would be
inside its 184.2 ms.

**That is a hypothesis, not a measurement, and it is the next one to test**: count the
kernels the dense funnel launches per call, or time `launch_gguf_linear` with the pack
disabled.
