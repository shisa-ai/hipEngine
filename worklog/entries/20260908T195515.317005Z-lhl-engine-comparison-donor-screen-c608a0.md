# Screen nasone32 donor optimizations from the engine comparison

## Summary

Review-and-test pass over the Optimization Review list in
benchmarks/results/2026-09-08-rx7900xtx-engine-comparison.md (nasone32
fork `7dc2f0cb` vs hipEngine `6c01f1f1c`). Mapping verdicts: adaptive-depth
(rejected with evidence in the comparison itself), already-represented
items, RDNA3.5-only guards, MoE items and future-TP items need no action.
Two items are testable now: (1) nasone32 K-quant load reuse
(`efa4e86410c07723deaa458bdadd8c08f1029928`, vecdotq.cuh VDR bumps) and
(2) chunked GDN (`4169fbbf5`, prefill side). Item (1) is screened first.

Key dispatch fact established while mapping the baseline: with
`HIPENGINE_GGUF_DECODE_REPACK=1` (the comparison protocol), dense Q4_K
rows=1 decode dispatches `dense_single_local32_bf16_bf16_out` on T16
tiles (rows 2-8: `dense_rowtile16_w2` owners), not the raw pack8 float
GEMV. The shaped benchmark therefore carries four arms: T16 production,
raw pack8, dp4a control, dp4a VDR.

## Decisions

- The donor VDR idea ports cleanly onto the in-tree q8_1-dp4a Q4_K
  idiom: new leaf-screen kernels
  `gguf_q4_k_q8_1_dp4a_vdr_gemv.{hip,py}` with an unamortized control
  (per-4-pack header/scale/min re-decode) and a VDR variant
  (subblock-hoisted metadata, activation packs reused across 8 columns).
  Same thread mapping and f32 evaluation order, so the RED contract is
  bit-exact ctl/vdr equality.

## Validation

- RED observed: module/kernel absent, test collection failed before
  implementation; green after (9 nodes).
- Full 4-arm benchmark on GPU0 (W7900) and GPU1 (RX 7900 XTX), 7
  samples, burst 16, interleaved arm order, rows 1/2/4, actual
  Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_M weights (ffn_down 5120x17408, recurrent_qkv
  10240x5120, attention_v 1024x5120, attention_q 12288x5120).
  Result: the retained T16 production owner wins 12/12 cases on both
  cards. At rows=1 the VDR dp4a kernel is 1.11-1.44x slower than
  production (T16 rows=1 times sit at/near the DRAM-bandwidth floor of
  each projection's weight bytes); at rows=4 up to 2.92x slower. Inside
  the q8_1-dp4a class the amortization is a large real win:
  0.24-0.50x of the unamortized control (-50..-76%), and it beats the
  raw pack8 float fallback at every shape. ctl/vdr stayed bit-identical
  in every benchmark case (asserted in the script).
- rocprofv3 --kernel-trace smoke (cached-only child, compiler-version
  file): `q4_k_q8_1_dp4a_ctl_gemv_kernel` 19.2 us VGPR80 and
  `q4_k_q8_1_dp4a_vdr_gemv_kernel` 9.0 us VGPR112, both WG128 on the
  512x256 fixture, plausible durations consistent with the leaf bench.
- Artifact:
  benchmarks/results/2026-09-09-gfx1100-qwen38-q4km-vdr-load-reuse-rejected.json
  (both card runs merged, verdict and correctness fields embedded).
- Verdict: REJECTED as a dense-decode replacement; no route or dispatch
  change. The kernel and its bit-exact A/B harness are retained as
  leaf evidence for future integer decode routes. Diagnostic value:
  the nasone32 TG advantage is NOT attributable to Q4_K projection
  GEMVs - our T16 owners are at the bandwidth floor at rows=1.
