# Review nasone32 RDNA3/7900XTX fork; add E8 experiments

## Summary

Source-level review of the previously unreviewed
[nasone32/llama.cpp-RDNA3-7900xtx-opt](https://github.com/nasone32/llama.cpp-RDNA3-7900xtx-opt)
fork, requested after its Reddit-style feature list claimed a set of
Flash-Next optimizations (MoE MMQ sizing, channels-major GDN, QSA sparse
decode, TOP_K wave32, GPU MoE expert cache, lazy PLE direct reads, adaptive
MTP, plus multi-GPU allreduce/P2P/Q8-wire and DFlash2 tensor split).

Reviewed at pinned tip `1a6b9351` via a read-only blob-less clone; no
external runtime, weights or kernels were built or executed. MIT license,
so commit bodies are referenceable under the campaign's lineage rules
(unlike Halogen's EULA). All its performance rows are author-reported on
two RX 7900 XTX (gfx1100), ROCm 7.14, PP8192, `UD_Q3_XXL` Flash-Next /
Q8_0 27B under tensor parallel with RAM offload - none is a comparator
row for our UD-Q4_K_XL all-resident single-GPU fixture, and per campaign
rules no headline rate is credited without reproduction.

Findings (full mapping table in the review doc):

- **Directly relevant, new evidence:** `ed11a0d2f` (RDNA3.5 D=256
  fattn-tile config: `nbatch_K` 128->64, occupancy 3->4) targets exactly
  our QSA geometry (24q/2kv/D256) on our silicon family; QSA prefill is
  1.331 s versus Vulkan's 0.646 s. And `47ff3777a`/`9a764c613`/
  `7dfa528e3` (size routed-MoE MMQ tile J from typical expert width /
  tokens-per-expert) address the same tile-underfill problem our routing
  histogram shows (9-12 median active rows/expert), strengthening E1 with
  a concrete RDNA3 reference implementation.
- **Already covered or superseded on our side:** gathered QSA decode
  (#28213 - our ordered-v2 route is 0.179 ms/layer bit-exact), MoE
  expert cache (all-resident payload; the author disables it for PP),
  lazy PLE direct reads (a host-offload/mmap pathology; our PLE owner is
  15.7 ms of 16.5 s), skip-graphs-for-prefill (corroborates our neutral
  advancing-graph finding), fused gate+up+GLU (we already fuse
  gate/up+SiLU).
- **Out of current scope, recorded for later lanes:** internal HIP
  allreduce + P2P without RCCL, Q8_0 inter-GPU wire compression, fused
  allreduce+residual, DFlash2 tensor-split (dual-GPU lane); adaptive MTP
  draft depth (W7900 MTP lane).
- **Below re-rank threshold:** TOP_K wave32/hybrid (router decode owner
  0.33 ms/token), channels-major GDN (GDN already 0.59x the comparator).
- `mmvdq` dequant-float matvec and mmvq short-K item-split noted as
  references for R6's decode GEMV target.
