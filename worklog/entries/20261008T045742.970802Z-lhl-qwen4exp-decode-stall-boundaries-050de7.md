# Decode stalls localized to layer-section boundaries

## Summary

Stall decomposition of the p4096 decode gap mass completes the localization: the
depth-growing remainder is **~52 stalls/step of 130-190 us** (one per
layer-section boundary) totaling 6.38 ms/step, on top of a small-gap floor that
grows only 4.17 -> 5.15 ms/step. Every big stall sits at a sub-chain transition -
attention/GR/norm -> MoE expert burst, MoE -> dense projection - i.e., exposed
Python section preparation that appears once the host falls behind enough for
the launch queue to drain at each boundary. This resolves the build-identity
A/B tie: intra-section host work overlaps device execution and is invisible;
only boundary-exposed preparation moves the wall at depth.

## Validation

- Both counterbalanced trace arms agree; 52 stalls/step at ~150 us matches the
  ~48-layer cadence; p512 shows only 2.9 stalls/step (0.49 ms), confirming the
  stalls are the depth-grown component rather than the small-gap floor.
