## Summary

The band extension to rows 4-128 (commit `502b8c282`) was reverted after the
retained-cell re-measurement produced a **GPU hardware hang** in a diagnostic
cell. The four product-route cells re-measured green and faster before the
hang; the three `--generation2-diagnostic` cells all failed.

The diagnostic failures are the specdec2 physical engine

`HW Exception by GPU node-1 reason: GPU Hang` during the C7/K3 MTP arm; the
device recovered after the process died (rocm-smi healthy at 0% use).

## Root-cause hypothesis (unverified)

The standalone L1 screen validated bit-exactness only at rows
{4, 6, 12, 16, 24, 32, 33, 48}; the in-engine specdec2 frontier row counts
20 (C5/K3) and 28 (C7/K3) were **never validated**. The row64 kernel or its
launch path is suspected incorrect at row counts outside the screened set.
The diagnostic cells' 0/10 engagement (without hang) at rows 20/4 is
consistent with the engine's device-chain qualification refusing mismatched
output; the C7/K3 hang is a direct kernel-safety failure.
