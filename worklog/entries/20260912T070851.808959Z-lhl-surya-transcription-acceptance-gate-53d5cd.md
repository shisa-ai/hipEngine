# Surya full-page transcription acceptance and teacher-forced KL gate

## Summary

Two gates replace "reproduce the oracle's greedy ids" for the Surya lane.

**Transcription acceptance.** The checkpoint's real full-page HTML prompt
(`FULL_PAGE_HTML_PROMPT`) is run over seven representative documents and scored
against the text actually drawn on each fixture. Measured fp32 on gfx1151, all
seven pages reach a natural EOS with recall 1.000, exact 1.000, CER 0.0000, zero
reading-order violations, and (for the table page) a 4x8 grid with 32/32 cells
and a matching header. The test asserts exact id parity with a captured torch
fp32 protocol oracle on six pages, a bounded coordinate drift on the seventh,
and the ground-truth bars on all seven, plus a starved-budget case that must
report truncation and omissions rather than pass a prefix.

**Numerical gate.** `scripts/surya_numerical_gate.py` measures the declared
production envelope (`docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` section 6) over teacher-forced
full-vocabulary rows: mean/p95/p99/max row KL, top-1 globally and per page, and
the BF16-relative comparison. The teacher's own greedy chain is forced into
every arm. The 512 MiB default budget is a no-op at these page sizes, so the
measurement forces the vision-attention reassociation with a 12 MiB budget
(4-10 tiles per page, including partial tiles).

## Results

Vision-attention tiling versus the dense path, 4562 teacher-forced rows over all
seven pages: **mean/p95/p99/max KL `0.000e+00`, top-1 `100%`, zero rows above
the `2e-2` review bar.** The tiling partitions queries rather than keys and
reduces over the same full key range, so it is arithmetic-neutral rather than a
reassociation — stronger than the earlier "agree within 1e-4".

BF16-relative (teacher = torch fp32, 4562 rows):

| arm | mean KL | p95 | p99 | max | top-1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| hipEngine dense | 7.157e-04 | 1.947e-03 | 1.988e-01 | 7.706e-01 | 0.99868 |
| hipEngine tiled | 7.157e-04 | 1.947e-03 | 1.988e-01 | 7.706e-01 | 0.99868 |
| torch bf16 | 2.429e-04 | 1.114e-03 | 1.219e-02 | 3.386e-01 | 0.99605 |

The two hipEngine arms are identical to the last digit, confirming the tiling is
arithmetic-neutral end to end. The hipEngine-fp32-versus-torch-fp32 drift is a
cross-implementation difference (different GEMM kernels and reduction orders),
not a production-versus-strict comparison, so the envelope's tail bars are a
stricter-than-required reading here. Its top-1 is higher than bf16's
(0.99868 vs 0.99605) while its KL tail is larger, and the tail is concentrated
on the degraded scan page where the teacher distribution is flat. Rows above the
review bar carry top-5 overlap, the teacher's probability and logit margin, and
the flip flag in the artifact, as the profile requires.

## Parity caveat: the degraded scan's bbox digits

Six of the seven pages reproduce the torch fp32 protocol oracle's greedy ids
exactly. On the degraded scan the HIP lane differs on **21 of 616 ids (3.4%)**,
all of them bbox coordinate digits: the parsed labels and texts are identical
and the worst coordinate delta is **4 of 1000**. This is intrinsic fragility of
that page, not a HIP-specific defect — torch bf16 against torch fp32 differs on
15 ids there and even changes the decoded text, and the scan carries all 16 of
the HIP-versus-torch rows above the review bar, with teacher probability margins
of 0.002-0.099 and top-5 overlap 3-5.

The acceptance test therefore splits: `EXACT_ID_CASES` (ja, mixed, dense, table,
blank, long) assert full id equality, and `COORDINATE_CASES` (scan) assert equal
finish reason, a coordinate-diff fraction under 5%, identical block labels and
texts, and a worst bbox delta under 8 of 1000. The ground-truth transcription
bars apply to all seven. The numerical gate's `bf16_relative` block carries an
explicit note that it is a cross-implementation diagnostic rather than the
binding production-versus-strict comparison, and every row above the review bar
records top-5 overlap, the teacher's probability and logit margin, and the flip
flag.
