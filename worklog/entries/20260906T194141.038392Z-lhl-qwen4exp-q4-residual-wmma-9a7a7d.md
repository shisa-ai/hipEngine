# Evaluate two-plane Q4 WMMA weight reconstruction

## Summary

Evaluate a T2 kernel-only Q4 gate/up WMMA experiment. Two FP16 weight
planes (high plus residual) aim to reduce the representation error of the
single-plane WMMA path while retaining its matrix throughput. This is
not T0; no exact-output or runtime-promotion claim. Numerical agreement
improves substantially,but all tested raw consumers are slower than exact
pair2. Retain solely as a diagnostic reference for a new cooperative loader.

  prefill at K2560/N640. Existing grouped pair2 is the strict fallback.
