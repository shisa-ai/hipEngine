# VibeVoice-ASR 200-clip comparative WER: Q4_K_M 2.011% vs bf16 2.097% vs torch 2.808%; Q4 statistically indistinguishable

## Summary

The 20-clip and 50-clip subsets were point estimates. This is the comparative
run at a sample size with a usable interval: 200 LibriSpeech test-clean
utterances, round-robin across all 40 speakers and their chapters, 1744.4 s
of audio, three lanes over one shared request cache, greedy decoding
everywhere, Whisper-normalized jiwer micro-average.

| lane | WER | clips scored | malformed | s/clip |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| torch-GPU bf16 (reference) | 2.808% | 197/200 | **3** | 4.35 |
| hipEngine dense bf16 | 2.097% | 200/200 | 0 | 4.71 |
| **hipEngine Q4_K_M** | **2.011%** | 200/200 | 0 | **3.48** |

**Q4_K_M is statistically indistinguishable from dense bf16.** Paired per-clip
over the 200 clips both lanes scored: 190 identical, 3 worse, 7 better, mean
paired difference -0.343 pp, 20000-sample bootstrap 95% CI [-0.964, +0.048] —
includes zero. This is the answer the earlier subsets could not give: the Q4
backbone's 1.52x prefill and 1.50x decode cost no measurable quality.

Both hipEngine lanes land within 0.1 pp of the model card's published
LibriSpeech test-clean row (2.20%) on this subset, and both beat the torch
reference lane. The subset is fixed at 200 clips, not the full 2620, so the
absolute number carries subset variance; the ordering and the Q4/bf16
equivalence do not.

## Findings

**The torch reference lane fails the transcript schema at 1.5%.** Three of its
200 generations were rejected by the strict evaluator (two missing the JSON
array opener, one truncated at the 256-token cap). Two of the three are
front-truncated — the output begins mid-object at
`assistantContent":"Ojo examined..."` and `assistant2.17,"End":2.17,...` — so
the model skipped the array opener rather than the harness eating it; the
prefix stripping leaves `[{"Start":...` intact when it is present. The
hipEngine lanes scored `ok` on all three of the same clips.

This matters for reading the table: torch's 2.808% is over 197 clips while
both hipEngine lanes are over 200. Excluding its malformed clips if anything
flatters the torch lane, so the ordering is safe, but the exact gap is not a
like-for-like 200-clip comparison.
