# Retract the GPU0 UD MTP measurements and pin the device

## Summary

Every rate I published today from the paired UD/plain MTP measurement and from
the W7900 UD census is withdrawn. Both were taken on HIP device 0 with
`HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES` unset, on a two-gfx1100 host where another worker owned
that card and was tracing on it. The device was never declared and never
verified, so the numbers are not evidence. This supersedes
`20260911T173154.215773Z-lhl-ud-mtp-paired-w7900-76a4c6` and
`20260911T173438.919491Z-lhl-ud-mtp-paired-w7900-correction-454f88`; both are
committed and stay as written, so the correction lives here.

The root cause is a process error, not a measurement error. I ran
`rocm-smi --showuse` once, saw GPU0 at 0%, and treated that one-second snapshot
as ownership. A snapshot cannot see a bursty job, a job between phases, or one
about to start. On a shared box the device must be declared and checked, never
inferred.

## Changes

**Withdrawn — rates.** All four AR/MTP pairs and their within-arm ratios
(UD-Q4_K_M 15.362 -> 30.735 tok/s, 2.0007x; UD-Q4_K_S 15.271 -> 27.626,
1.8091x; plain Q4_K_M 30.442 -> 52.342, 1.7194x; plain Q4_K_S 31.222 -> 54.433,
1.7434x) and the 0.505x UD/plain AR ratio derived from them.

**Withdrawn — the device-gap attribution.** The claim that the W7900 is 1.55x
slower than the XTX on UD decode (46,913 vs 30,263 us/token pure at 831.2
launches/token), localised to `q5_k_t16_dense_single_local32_gemv_kernel`
(13,380 vs 3,486, 3.84x) and `gguf_iq4_xs_local32_gemv_kernel` (8,120 vs 2,436,
3.33x). The specific error in that entry was the inference "contention is
excluded because every other kernel is within 1.06-1.23x". That reasoning is
backwards: a competing process running the same kernels is exactly what
inflates a few kernels non-uniformly while leaving the rest near 1.0x. The
observation is consistent with contention, and the census was read as a clean
device property while the device was not mine.

**Not withdrawn.** Contention changes timing, not identity:

- Token-ID exactness and determinism: all four arms repeated bit-identically
  per prompt, and UD-Q4_K_S differed from its own AR reference on
  `general_ja_plan` in both runs.
- The 0.0096-logit near-tie localisation (AR rank-1 99720 at 21.181459 vs
  rank-2 211768 at 21.171841, with the MTP verifier picking 211768). This is a
  logits comparison against a single-row AR control, not a rate.
- The U6 draft/verifier state-disjointness result (62 draft device buffers
  intersecting neither the target session's 188 nor the verifier journal's 381).
  A pointer-set comparison is a property of the allocation, not of the clock.

**Code.** `scripts/ud_mtp_paired.py` now refuses to run without an explicit
`--device-index`, exports `HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES` from it, fails the run if the
suite's reported `device_name` does not contain `--expect-device`, and records
both in the artifact. `--invalidate <reason>` marks an artifact's rates
unusable without discarding the run. The earlier revision of this harness had
no device argument at all, which is what allowed the mistake.

**Records.** `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md` carries a dated retraction naming the
withdrawn numbers and what survives. `benchmarks/README.md` no longer publishes
the table or the census comparison; it states that the measurement is withdrawn
and no rate is published pending a GPU1 re-run. `docs/UD-QUANTS.md` gains the
same note at the U6 phase split, where it tells a reader the paired run may
proceed.

**Artifact.** `benchmarks/results/paired-ud-plain-mtp-c1-natural25-b3.json` is
kept as a marked-invalid record with
`invalidation.invalid = true` and the reason, and a `device` block recording
that no device was declared.
