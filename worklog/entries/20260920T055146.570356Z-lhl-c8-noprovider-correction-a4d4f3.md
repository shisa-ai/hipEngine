# The c=8 no_provider is the documented context bound, not the capture fix - my attribution was wrong

## Summary

**Corrects the causal claim in
`worklog/entries/20260920T053446.597740Z-lhl-noprovider-fixed-by-capture-wiring-064033.md`.**
The confirmation run it asked for has been done, and it disproves the attribution.
The conclusion that the c=8 `no_provider` is not a defect survives; the reason I
gave for it does not.

The run: the t17 reproducer on current HEAD `8072599e1` (a descendant of
`8ba08ebf9`), c=8, beyond-1024 cases, both arms, into a per-run `OUT` so the
artifacts are attributed to this run alone.

| arm | rows | refusal events | spanned AR tokens | by_reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `off` | 8/8 completed | 0 | 0 | `{}` |
| `on` | 8/8 completed | 256 | **248 of 256** | `{"no_provider": 248}` |

248 of 256 is the **same number** the pre-fix tree `d805bfae4` produced, so
`8ba08ebf9` did not change this path. Every row reports
`effective_route=speculative_mtp`, `mtp_used=false`, `mtp_output_tokens=0`, and
`activation_reason=target_context_k0`, with `prompt_tokens` of 1826, 1074, 2251,
1708, 1121, 1362, 3482, 1483 -- **every one beyond the documented 1,023-token
qualified window**. The rows decline to K0 before any provider work, so their
autoregressive cycles are attributed `no_provider` because the row deliberately
has no provider. The refusal label is a consequence of the context bound, not an
independent defect.

**What was wrong.** I generalized a c=1 observation to a c=8 case. The c=1
evidence in the corrected entry was real on its own harness (`/tmp/t21-cap.sh`,
two requests, 128 autoregressive tokens, prompts inside the qualified window):
126 of 128 tokens `no_provider` and no captures on `942bb6de5`, against 2 of 2
tokens and working captures on `8ba08ebf9`. That is a genuine c=1 capture-path
observation. It says nothing about c=8 beyond-1024 rows, whose refusals come from
a context bound that `8ba08ebf9` does not touch. Proven tree ordering plus a
commit subject that mentioned the hidden-rows prerequisite was not evidence that
the commit caused the c=8 number, and I presented it as though it were.

**What is unchanged.** The c=8 case is still not an MTP-default blocker, for the
better-evidenced reason: it is the documented qualified-window bound, visible as
`target_context_k0` on every row, and all eight requests complete in both arms
with no failures or tracebacks. That is the same conclusion
`benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md` already records for task #17's closure.

The `on` arm's lower median ITL (152 ms against 452 ms) is not an MTP effect:
`mtp_requests=0` and `mtp_output_tokens=0` in both arms, so no row used MTP. The
per-request decode rates are 5.76 against 6.05 tok/s, i.e. equivalent. No rate is
claimed.
