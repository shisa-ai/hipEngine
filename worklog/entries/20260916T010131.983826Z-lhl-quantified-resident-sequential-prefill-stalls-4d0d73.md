# Quantified: resident sequential prefill stalls 13/14 on gfx1100, all arms

## Summary

Per-subprocess quantification (`scripts/tp2_resident_race_quantify2.py`,
one fresh process per attempt, stalled children killed at the 45 s budget
which reclaims the device): **none 3/3, passthrough 3/3, events 3/3
STALL** — lifetime 13/14 with the four earlier unwrapped repros and the one
0.3 s instrumented completion (entry 2690c0). The stall is effectively
deterministic on this lane for the resident session's sequential prefill;
inter-layer event records do not reliably mask it (the single completion
was luck, not instrumentation). The "flaky race" framing narrows to a ~100%
failure rate with a rare escape — timing-sensitive to the device-side
command-stream layout at the margin, but broken regardless.

Standing contrast unchanged: the tp2/tp1 harness prefill (same runner,
own `_forward_token` dispatch) is clean on this host across every gate, and
the D1 bulk route runs (NaN defect aside). The defect is specific to the
ResidentSession sequential-prefill driver on gfx1100.
