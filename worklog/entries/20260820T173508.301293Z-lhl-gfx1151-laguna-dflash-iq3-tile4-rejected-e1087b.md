# Reject gfx1151 Laguna DFlash IQ3 tile4

## Summary

Rejected the attempted transfer of the exact Laguna IQ3 selected-down tile4
kernel to the gfx1151 explicit-public-DFlash target verifier. The native kernel
itself remains bit-exact and traced, but the complete current public-model route
has a pre-existing binding correctness failure under both tile1 and tile4, and
tile4 does not improve complete E2E wall. Restored gfx1151/public DFlash to
tile1 and left the previously qualified gfx1100 explicit-DFlash scope intact.

  **4.168-26.890 us** (mean **15.248 us**), VGPR40/SGPR128/LDS512/scratch0.

  both repetitions; all logits are finite, states align, repeats are
