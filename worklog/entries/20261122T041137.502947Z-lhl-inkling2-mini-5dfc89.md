# Screen integer-dot NVFP4 with Q8 activations

## Summary

A scratch NVFP4 integer-dot kernel was faster at the two packed Inkling expert shapes on both GPUs, including activation quantization. It changes projection arithmetic and is not enabled in hipEngine.

## Changes

- No product changes. The scratch path quantizes activations in groups of 32 and rounds their scales to FP16. Each wave reads the original GGUF NVFP4 rows, uses signed four-byte integer dot instructions, and accumulates the independently scaled partials in FP32. Original outer scales still apply after the dot. No weight repack is needed.

## Validation

- On Radeon Pro W7900 (`gfx1100`) and Radeon 8060S (`gfx1151`), `python -u ./hipengine_inkling_nvfp4_q8_probe.py "$backend"` verified activation codes and widened scales exactly and verified the projections against the float64 quantized oracle built with the independent GGUF decoder. At all three shapes, all 32 route projections agreed with their quantized oracle's top output index. Compared separately to the current unquantized kernel, agreements were 32/32 at `(input, output)=(64, 19)` and `(2304, 2304)`, and 31/32 at `(1152, 2304)`. Maximum synthetic projection KL versus FP32 was below `3.4e-7`. These are operator checks, not full-model logits or a production-profile gate.
- With eight routes and 32 resident synthetic experts, Strix median device-event cost over six samples of 120 launches moved from 0.243 to 0.149 ms at `(2304, 2304)` and from 0.109 to 0.077 ms at `(1152, 2304)`. W7900 moved from 0.085 to 0.059 ms and from 0.043 to 0.039 ms. These include activation quantization and projection, not model dispatch or transfers. The small `(64, 19)` shape was slower on both GPUs. Logical packed weight bytes per launch are unchanged. Scratch sources: `/tmp/hipengine_inkling_nvfp4_q8_probe.{hip,py}`. Logs: `/tmp/kendex-pi-bg/bg-58-1795320667139.log` and `/tmp/kendex-pi-bg/bg-59-1795320667153.log`.
