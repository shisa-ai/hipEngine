# Pin selector source revision and acquisition bound

## Summary

Closed two acquisition-review gaps: the recorded Wikimedia revision is now also
passed to the dataset loader, and the default stream bound is large enough for
the campaign's 18 disjoint 128K-token streams per language.

  recording it after an unpinned load.
