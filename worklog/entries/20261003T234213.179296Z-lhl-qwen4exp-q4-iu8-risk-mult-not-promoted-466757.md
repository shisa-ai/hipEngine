# The exact-iu8 risk multiplier passes the numerical gate and is still not promoted: the wall benefit does not transfer, and the repair pass is mostly fixed cost

## Summary

This supersedes the "Next" of `20261003T225747`, which measured the multiplier as a
2.11% prefill dial and left the gate running. **The gate passed, and the change is
still not promoted, because the wall win does not survive the canonical protocol.**
The two readings reconcile into a better finding than either alone.

**What is established and worth keeping:**

- Tightening `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q4_IU8_RISK_MULT` from 4.0 to 1.0 cuts the
  repaired share of rows **0.7119% -> 0.2092%, 3.40x fewer**, over 1504 calls and
  9,856,614,400 outputs, with `over_capacity_calls: 0` at every point.
- It **passes the production numerical gate with 3.3-6.4x aggregate margin**:
  mean KL 3.039e-4 against 1e-3, p95 1.785e-3, p99 4.271e-3 against 2e-2, max
  7.771e-3 against 5e-2, top-1 0.99556, per-scope top-1 min 0.99,
  `scope_failures: null`, determinism and isolation clean, over 18 prompts and 450
  rows against a production teacher.
- It is **274x better than the rejected `repair_free_gate_up` candidate on the very
  scope that rejected it**: `prefill_last` / `prefill_to_c1`, 18 rows, mean KL
  **7.891e-6 against 2.159e-3**, p99 1.047e-4 against a 2e-2 limit. That candidate
  repaired **nothing**; this one repairs **29% of what 4.0 flags**. **So the
  earlier rejection does not close this route**, and reading it as if it did would
  have discarded a gate-passing candidate on a scope its evidence never covered.

**Why it is not promoted.** The canonical twelve-case harness is the protocol that
produces the public row, and it reads flat. Interleaved A/B/A over the four 4K
cases, one session, same host, 1 warmup and 3 repetitions per arm:

| arm | mean tok/s | note |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| A, multiplier 1.0 | 264.11 | per-category 260.6-267.9, spread ~0.4% |
| B, multiplier 4.0 | 259.46 | **third repetitions degrade 6% progressively**: 245.8 / 249.7 / 252.5 / 247.0 |
| C, multiplier 1.0 | 266.30 (5 of 12) | per-category 262.6-269.4 |

**Arm B is contaminated, so the run is inconclusive rather than a win or a
regression** - but the contamination is not what decides it. B's *clean*
repetitions read about **0.6% above** the 1.0 arms (262.6 / 267.8 / 269.2 / 263.6
against A's 261.1 / 266.4 / 267.2 / 261.9), and the same-arm A-against-C drift is
**0.82%**, which is larger than any between-arm effect this run can defend. An
earlier non-interleaved canonical pair read -0.42% at 4K, but its baseline arm had a
GTT-contending job start 20 s into it, so it is not usable either way and is not
relied on.

**The reconciliation is the finding.** The change's own measurement says the
incidence is linear in the multiplier and the wall is not: 3.40x fewer flagged rows
buys 2.11% where a proportional model over the recorded 1370 ms repair share predicts
5.7%. **A pass that is mostly fixed cost cannot yield 3.4x of anything**, so -2.11%
is specific to the isolating harness (whose chunking gives the repair a larger
proportional share) and the canonical flat read is the expected one. **The lever is
the repair pass's launch geometry, not its threshold:** it launches over
`risk_capacity = compact * 2 * ffn` regardless of `risk_count`.

This is the case `AGENTS.md` describes when it says a candidate which is not
bit-exact must be re-reviewed under the production gates before any rejection - and
the mirror of it: passing the gates is necessary and not sufficient, because the
canonical protocol is what the public claim is made against.

## Limits

One host, one GPU architecture (gfx1151, not the gfx1100 default hardware), one
model file, one quant, production profile, BF16 KV. The A/B covers the four 4K cases
only. Arm B's contamination is real and unexplained - most likely a run-order or
thermal event - and the run should be repeated on a quiet machine with
per-repetition outlier rejection before the multiplier is called settled in either
direction. The 1370 ms repair share is from a 2026-09-16 capture.

## Next

  be pursued without waiting on anything here.
- Then, and only then, re-measure the multiplier on the canonical protocol in the

## Addendum: the first thing the launch-geometry unit should measure

Reading the launcher after writing the entry above narrows the fixed cost to one
concrete hypothesis, recorded here so the next unit does not start from zero.

`gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_sparse_exact_repair_bf16` takes `grid_blocks` with a
default of **1024** (`gguf_q4_k_selected_prefill.py:600,1161`) and **the runner
never passes it**, so every call launches 1024 blocks of 128 threads whatever the
risk count is. The kernel's loop is `for (slot = blockIdx.x; slot < bounded; slot
+= gridDim.x)` with `bounded = min(risk_count, max_risks)`, so the *work* is
already proportional: 70,167,220 risk elements over 1504 calls is 46.6k slots per
call at 4.0 and 13.7k at 1.0, i.e. ~46 against ~13 iterations per block. A 3.4x
reduction in inner work that shows up as 2.11% of wall is the anomaly to explain.

The candidate explanations, cheapest to distinguish first:

1. **Empty-block cost.** At 1.0, blocks 14-1023 have `blockIdx.x >= bounded` and
   exit immediately. On a GPU that should be nearly free, so this is the least
   likely explanation - but it is a one-line experiment (`grid_blocks =
   min(1024, bounded)`) and it is worth ruling out before building anything.
2. **The 1370 ms share is inflated.** That figure is from a 2026-09-16 capture
   whose own README says its per-kernel times are diagnostic. If the true repair
   share is nearer 500 ms, then 359 ms saved is most of it and there is no anomaly
   at all - **measure the repair's actual share in the current prefill before
   optimising it**, by profiling the repair kernel with `--kernel-trace` at both
   multipliers rather than inferring from the old capture.
3. **The risk rows are not uniformly spread across calls.** If the 4.0-only
   extra 3.4x is concentrated in a few calls, the total wall reduction is smaller
   than the aggregate ratio implies. The diagnostic is per-call `risk_count`
   against per-call repair time.

**Order: (2), then (3), then (1).** Explanation 2 is the one that would make the
whole lever smaller than the 1370 ms figure promises, so measuring the current
share is the prerequisite for deciding whether this unit is worth building at all.
