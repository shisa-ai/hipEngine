**1. Half to three fifths of a block is the host expert path.** 64-token blocks on the resident
traversal, third of three repeats:

| Prompt tokens | Time | Expert path (`feed_forward`) | Host routing inside it | Rest per visit | Expert per visit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 575 | 2.31 s | 1.37 s (59%) | 0.14 s | 2.18 ms | 3.17 ms |
| 2,057 | 9.84 s | 5.03 s (51%) | 0.49 s | 3.04 ms | 3.17 ms |

The expert path is the download of the normalized rows, the router projection and its download,
the host top-k, the tiled expert projection, and the upload of the branch rows. Its cost per
visit is the same at both depths; the rest grows with depth because attention does.

**2. Wider blocks buy up to 1.3 times, with the same kernels.** Time to the prompt end:

| Block width | 575 tokens | 1,031 | 2,057 | 4,095 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 64 (today) | 2.450 s, 235 tok/s | 4.585 s, 225 | 9.897 s, 208 | 20.93 s, 196 |
| 128 | 2.013 s, 286 | 3.890 s, 265 | 8.434 s, 244 | 17.95 s, 228 |
| 256 | 1.873 s, 307 | 3.600 s, 286 | 7.843 s, 262 | 16.73 s, 245 |
| 512 | 1.831 s, 314 | 3.509 s, 294 | 7.601 s, 271 | 16.21 s, 253 |

- Most of the gain is the first doubling; beyond 256 it is 1 to 3%.
- Against strict at the prompt end the KL is at most 7e-13 at width 64 and stays small at every
  width: 2.9e-7 at 1,031 tokens, 1.4e-9 at 2,057, and 6.9e-8 at 4,095 with width 512, where the
  largest logit change is 0.47. Strict's top-1 holds at every point.
- The public width is 64 with a scheduler budget of 64 tokens per round. A wider block is a longer
  step for the other streams of a multi-request server, so the width is a scheduling decision as
  well (plan task 18), and it is left at 64 here.

**3. The expert projection runs at about 1 TMAC/s.** A 64-row block is 512 routes per visit and
4.08 GMAC; the expert path takes 3.17 ms per visit. At 512 rows about 39 of the 42 ms per visit
are expert work for 32.6 GMAC. For comparison the same 64-row visit reads about 0.62 GB of expert
weights, which is 1.08 ms at the 576 GB/s of the roofline model. The tile4 kernel gives one wave
four weight rows and four routes: per lane and iteration that is four 16-value block decodes and
256 FMAs, and each weight row is read again for every tile that uses it.
