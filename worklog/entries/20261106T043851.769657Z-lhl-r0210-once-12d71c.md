# Avoid deficit-only chunks for confined workspace growth

## Summary

Confined workspace placement no longer grows an aggregate deficit-only chunk
before requesting a full compatible chunk. The old ordering could consume the
remaining budget on an unusably small chunk and refuse a feasible workspace.

## Changes

- Confined leases run pressure reclamation, attempt placement, then use their
  existing bounded compatible growth. Ordinary workspace leases keep aggregate
  free-page behavior unchanged.
