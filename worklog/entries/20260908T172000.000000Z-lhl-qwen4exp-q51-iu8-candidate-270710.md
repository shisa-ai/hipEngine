# R4b: build the exact iu8-WMMA Q5_1 down candidate (default-off)

## Summary

Built the weight-exact iu8-WMMA Q5_1 grouped down chain as a default-off
route (`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q51_IU8_EXACT=1`, multiplier default 4.0) at
`270710155`, mirroring the promoted Q4 gate/up structure: in-kernel
three-plane fp32 activation staging (chunked 256-column staging with the
640 = 2.5x256 tail handled), raw 5-bit Q5_1 codes as the integer WMMA
operand, the m-offset reconstructed per 32-block from staged plane sums
(ds-trick; PLUS sign for Q5_1 versus the Q4_K minus), Kahan risk criterion
with the R4a at-risk guards (nonfinite values; raw subblock amax below
2^-80), and a sparse exact repair reproducing the production pair2
row-publish arithmetic exactly (128 logical lanes, even/odd column streams,
128-entry halving tree).

Screen path taken: the retained R2 family-capture raw kernel traces
re-ranked the Q5_1 down exact parent at 3.15-3.22s across the three p4096
cases (the largest single MoE kernel owner); the existing MMQ ds4 chain was
rejected (0.75-0.78x with the correct three-plane pack, 18% flip fraction,
and a latent single-plane-pack/three-plane-consumer wiring inconsistency in
the default-off runner route); the new WMMA-class kernel was then written
from the Q4 iu8 template.

Key finding during bring-up: **launch bounds dominate** — the unbounded
build ran at 0.46x of the parent; `__launch_bounds__(128, 2)` (matching the
Q4 iu8) recovered to 1.62-1.70x. Higher occupancy targets (3, 4) gave no
further gain (LDS-bound).
