# Q3_K becomes reachable under W4A16, and is blocked on a 6% mean overage

## Summary

Switching the default to W4A16 made Q3_K, IQ2_S and IQ2_XS routable for the
first time: the integer-MMQ contract could never express them, W4A16 expands
per element and has no such constraint. Routing all three measures **176.5
tok/s** - faster than the integer-MMQ route ever was - but moves the
strict-referenced mean from 0.000827 to 0.001061, **6.1% over the calibrated
1e-3 limit**. The shipped default stays at the four quants that provably pass.

## Changes

No default change. `hip_gfx1151/__init__.py` records the measured candidate and
how to enable it beside the policy; artifact
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-09-zbook-ud-q3k-w4a16-candidate.json`.

## Measurements

Leaf, 512 rows, against the strict dense GEMV:

| quant | GEMV | W4A16 | speedup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q3_K | 86.74 ms | 14.75 | 5.88x |
| IQ2_S | 82.26 | 14.93 | 5.51x |
| IQ2_XS | 80.79 | 13.31 | 6.07x |

End to end and against hipEngine strict:

| policy | prefill | mean | p95 | p99 | max | >5e-2 | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| integer MMQ, 4 quants | 171.9 | 0.002110 | 0.006797 | 0.024044 | 0.170390 | 1 | FAIL |
| **W4A16, 4 quants (shipped)** | 150.3 | **0.000827** | 0.004547 | 0.012475 | 0.023513 | 0 | **PASS** |
| W4A16, all 7 quants | **176.5** | 0.001061 | 0.003980 | 0.016612 | 0.032730 | 0 | FAIL (mean only) |

**The failure is qualitatively unlike the integer route's.** That one breached
the 5e-2 absolute maximum-row ceiling by 3.4x. This one passes every tail with
margin, breaches no ceiling, and misses only the mean, by 6%.

K_M carries no IQ2_S or IQ2_XS tensors, so the entire mean delta is **Q3_K
alone**: +0.000234 mean for +26 tok/s.

## Why it is not shipped

Section 6.1 binds all limits together, and the human lead's standing choice on
this campaign has been the admissible route over the faster one. Shipping a
marginal miss would undo that on the first opportunity.

## Unblock paths

1. **Re-calibrate the envelope for this scope.** The 1e-3 mean limit was frozen
   by a 2026-08-16 Qwen3.5 cluster8 calibration; no UD-scope floor has ever been
   established, and hipEngine strict itself sits at mean 0.001271 against the
   llama.cpp teacher, which suggests the achievable floor here differs.
2. **A task gate** showing the overage does not affect output quality.
3. **Reduce the per-quant mean cost at source.** Each routed quant adds mean
   roughly independently of operand format - an int8 path and a 25x more
   accurate fp16 path landed within 0.00003 of each other - which points at
   accumulation order rather than precision. Untested, and the last open lead.

## Validation

Route, W4A16, dense 84-case exact parity and gfx1151-backend suites green with
the policy back at four quants. The candidate's numbers come from a full
sweep plus a strict-referenced capture over the same 18 prompts x 9 forced
tokens as every other arm in this campaign.
