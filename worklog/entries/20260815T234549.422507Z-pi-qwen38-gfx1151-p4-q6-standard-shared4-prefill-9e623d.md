Retain the exact four-wave shared-weight standard-Q6 prefill owner for the 24
rows>=512 K5120/N10240 recurrent-QKV tensors on gfx1151. Both immutable QKV
weights improve **2.961-3.548x** at 512/1K/4K with **90/90 paired wins** and

The cumulative development path now beats clean llama HIP and Vulkan at all
three campaign shapes.

- Added `q6_k_t16_wmma_prefill_shared4_bf16_kernel` and raw-pointer C/Python
  wrappers. One 128-thread block cooperatively decodes a 48 x 256 standard-T16
  Q6 slab into 24 KiB LDS; four waves preserve independent copies of the exact
  48-column x 64-row K16 WMMA/BF16 sequence.
- Added a gfx1151 backend override for
  `linear/gguf_q6_k_t16_v1/t16_wmma_prefill_bf16_bf16_out`. It admits only
  `(K,N)=(5,120,10,240)` at rows>=512; short rows, shape misses, and peer
  backends retain the registered 16x16 primitive.
- This differs from the rejected single-wave standard-Q6 48x64 tile: that path
  reduced total workgroups and lost 4.10-10.76%, while this body preserves the
  total wave count and removes three of four repeated decodes/global fetches.
- No resident layout, payload, workspace, decode/MTP owner, runtime ABI, or peer
  backend changes.
