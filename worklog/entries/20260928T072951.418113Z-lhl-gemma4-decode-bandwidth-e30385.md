## What that costs

The four ablation arms covering these launches -- norm/rope 4.05 ms, moe elementwise
3.13 ms, router 2.35 ms, attention 0.84 ms -- total **10.37 ms, 29.3% of the step**.

```
10.37 ms / 448 launches = 23.1 us per elementwise launch
```

**23.1 us is high for an elementwise kernel.** If pure launch overhead on this host were
about 5 us, that would be 2.24 ms of overhead and **8.13 ms of real small-kernel work**.
Those two are not distinguished yet, and the distinction decides the fix: overhead calls
for fusion and fewer launches, real work calls for making the kernels themselves faster.

## The GEMV path amortizes far better

```
dense:   1866.7 MB / 218.67 launches =  8.5 MB per launch
experts: 1324.8 MB /  61.86 launches = 21.4 MB per launch
```

The expert MMQ path moves **2.5x more per launch** than the dense GEMV path and runs at
91.6% of DRAM peak against the dense path's 87.0%. Same conclusion as the efficiency
measurements, from a different direction.

## One thing worth auditing

**`gemma4_rmsnorm_f32w_bf16` runs 5.0 times per layer.** A transformer layer of this shape
needs on the order of two to three norms. Five is high enough that redundant norms are
worth checking before optimizing the kernel, because deleting one is cheaper than making
five faster.
