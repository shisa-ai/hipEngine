# MX-1 build handoff: the F16 routed-expert candidate

## Summary

MX-1's numerical prerequisite is complete: the F16-operand routed-expert route
is admissible by quant, on real activations, for both halves of the family pair
(`20260929T090239`, `20260929T091151`). What remains is the kernel. This entry
fixes the design, the interface, the acceptance criteria and the known risks so
the next unit does not re-derive them.

**MX-1 is the keystone for 7454.3 ms of the 12973 ms gap (57.5%)**: it carries
`expert_gate_up` (2640.6 ms), `expert_down` (1969.5 ms), and it unblocks `MR-4`
(`moe_reduce`, 654.5 ms) and `hyper_connection` (2189.7 ms, the worst ratio at
7.52x) because the competitors' combine only exists once the routed output is
already F16.

## Why this and not something else

The alternatives are measured shut, not assumed shut:

- `ED-1` LDS staging for the existing iu8 down kernel loses 48/48 real
  populations and moves DRAM only 2.92% (`20260929T090239` ledger row). `EG-1`
  inherits the conclusion.
- Row-batch retiles at a constant accumulator budget are dead, and the 32-row M
  tile spills 492 B/thread (§7B).
- `DP-1`'s scope extension is closed: the wide route is a retiling of the f16
  WMMA arithmetic, not a reassociation, so 12-47 and 8-47 inherit its drift
  (`20260917T053701`). The matrix cores take only f16/bf16 operands, so the wide
  tile's operand precision cannot be improved in place.
- `qsa_attention`'s K/V-reuse and LDS-staging levers are measured dead
  (`20260927T090500`); its next unit is a WMMA `Q*K^T`/`P*V` candidate, a
  separate track.

## What to build

The competitor design, which is also the ledger's MX-1 description:

- Quantized expert weights decoded to **F16** in LDS, staged once per block and
  reused across the block's rows.
- Activation cast to F16 once.
- **F16 WMMA with F32 accumulation.**
- gate/up paired with SwiGLU; down writes F16.
- **No iu8 risk collection and no repair pass.** This removes 1239.8 ms
  outright, with no comparator equivalent.
- Expert tiles of 48/64/128 rows chosen from host-read bucket counts.

Activation packing needs no work: our `quantize_pack` is already 0.0 ms.

Reuse the promoted `dense_wide256` tile geometry as the starting point
(`BM=128, BN=256, BK=64`, 128x64 dequantized f16 LDS tile, 256-thread block,
`gguf_q8_0_dense_wide.hip`). It is the same operand class and it already passes
at layers 16-47.

Gate/up, `moe_linear` / `gguf_q4_k`, parent
`gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill_bf16_bf16_out`
(`hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q4_k_selected_prefill.py:414`):

max_risks, risk_multiplier, compact_rows, in_features, out_features_a,
out_features_b, num_experts, wmma_total_rows)`. A candidate drops the three
risk arguments.

Down, `moe_linear` / `gguf_q5_1`, parent
`selected_grouped_prefill_pair2_row_publish_bf16_bf16_out`:
`(input_ptr, expert_start_ptr, weights_ptr, output_ptr, compact_rows,
num_experts, in_features, out_features)`.

Real shapes, measured from the model file: gate/up are **Q4_K** in 94 of the 96
tensors with `K=2560, N=640`; down is **Q5_1** in 43 layers, **Q8_0** in 5
(layers 2, 4, 30, 46, 47) and **Q5_K** in 2, with `K=640, N=2560`. All with 512
experts. A first candidate can scope to the 43 Q5_1 + 94 Q4_K layers and leave
the 7 Q5_K/Q8_0 layers on the existing route; that is a scope choice, not an
inherited admission.

## Acceptance

1. Operand budget: already satisfied (Part A/B/C of the packet).
2. Correctness: KL ≤ 0.05 and top-1 ≥ 90% against `kernels/cpu_reference/` on
   fixture inputs is the outer smoke floor, not the bar.
3. The bar is the production envelope: mean KL ≤ 1e-3, p95 ≤ 5e-3, p99 ≤ 2e-2,
   max ≤ 5e-2, top-1 ≥ 99% overall and ≥ 97% in every declared category, plus
   determinism, isolation and the task gates. `MR-4` also needs the fused
   combine to be re-measured once the output is F16.
4. A registered strict fallback stays in place; the route is default-off until
   the whole gate passes.
5. `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` showing the new kernel under its expected name
   with a plausible duration.

## Risks

- **The operand budget is not a prediction.** The GR family's F16 wide tile was
  4.61x faster on its projection and still failed the 780-row gate at mean KL
  1.554e-3 (`2026-09-27-gr-up-wide-tile-screen`). Budget a full gate run, and
  localize by layer/role before concluding anything from a partial scope.
- Routing is diffuse: ~335 of 512 experts are active per layer-chunk and top-16
  cover 25.3%, so expert tiles cannot assume dense buckets. That is why the
  competitor reads bucket counts on the host.
- The 780-row gate is the one that has bound every previous routed-MoE attempt
  in §7B; run it before the longer suite.
