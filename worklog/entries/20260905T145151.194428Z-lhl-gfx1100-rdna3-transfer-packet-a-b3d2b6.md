# Retain gfx1100 singleton-indexed GDN decode (audit packet A)

## Summary

Promoted the singleton-indexed GDN packed-AR decode route on `hip_gfx1100`
after independent W7900 qualification, executing packet A of
`docs/20260905-gfx1100-audit.md`. The gfx1151-proven one-token-per-active-row
indexed recurrence replaces the segmented packed decode for supported rows;
the arbitrary-length segmented recurrence stays registered as the strict
fallback. This continues the campaign recorded in
`worklog/entries/20260905T133536.962565Z-lhl-gfx1100-rdna3-transfer-4e7ee4.md`
(baseline capture, RED contract, and the profiler-census repair).

## Decisions

- Retained: `GGUF_GDN_INDEXED_SINGLETON_DECODE = True` on `hip_gfx1100`.
  Direct graph decode (Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_M, BF16 KV, p512/d128, 1 warmup +
  3 measured, physical W7900 `epyc`, same-host same-protocol):
  c2 49.992 -> 53.690 (+7.40%), c4 86.955 -> 92.227 (+6.06%), native C8
  124.595 -> 131.953 tok/s (+5.91%); every affected width's sample range
  fully separated. c1 is route-unchanged (+0.57%, ordinary variance).
- The standalone packed-vs-strict-c1 state oracle fails identically with and
  without the flip (verified by a stash-reverted control run: same hashes,
  same 422-element prefill-layer-0 divergence). This is the known
  qualified-production-arithmetic property recorded by the gfx1151 campaign;
  the binding gate is the candidate-vs-rollback differential, which is exact.
