# EVIE perf triage: device-bound, batching is the lever

## Summary

Went through the perf-review optimization list with trustworthy
measurement (HIP event pairs around every launch, summed after sync):

- **Profile correction (retraction):** a document page is device-bound —
  GPU-busy 196.2 of 215.9 ms wall (91%) across 1782 timed spans. The
  earlier "212 of 231 ms launch/dispatch overhead" claim came from async
  wrapper timing and is wrong, as the perf review suspected. HIP graphs
  are bounded by the 9% host gap.
- **Batching quantified:** torch bf16 docs batch-1 2165.3 ms (270.7
  ms/page) vs batch-8 1086.3 ms (135.8 ms/page) — a 1.99x batching gain.
  Our sequential fp16 path (~204 ms/page) is already 1.33x faster than
  torch's sequential. torch's entire remaining advantage IS batching.
- **Review items 1 (transfers) and 5 (head 128)** were already fixed in
  f995c89e1 (-2.3% total). Item 4 (casts/fusion): casts measured ~5 ms
  (2%) earlier; elementwise fusion is secondary to batching given the
  device-bound profile.
