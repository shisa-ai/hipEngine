# Close R0191 and R0194 MoE physical C2 plan ownership

## Summary

Pair-reuse (R0191) and exact-linear (R0194) selection for the MoE physical C2
target now come from the immutable profile plan: the gfx1151 MoE binder publishes
`PhysicalTargetArithmeticPlan(True, True, True)`. Public validation confirms the
plan engages on `LLM.generate`. The `moe_physical_c2_*_session` contexts remain
supported diagnostic scopes used by the production-gate and teacher-run scripts,
so they are not retired. Both entries close.

## Changes

- Removed R0191 and R0194 from `docs/REFACTOR.md`. No code change.

## Validation

All runs: gfx1151, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q4_K_M, `max_active_requests=2`,
`speculative_mtp_serving="opt_in"`, two prompts, greedy, 48 tokens, one `LLM` reused
for every call.

- Engagement per explicit-MTP call. Strict: 680 pair-reuse dual-GEMV launches and
  510 `VerifyMoeC2LinearLayer` bindings. Production: 750 exact-linear bindings and
  0 pair-reuse launches; the production expert route does not meet the pair-reuse
  geometry gate.
- Two MTP repeats per profile are token-identical. Zero tracked allocations after
  `close()`.
- AR c1 equals AR c2, and width-1 MTP equals AR. At width 2 the second prompt's MTP
  output departs from AR at token 40 under strict, and at token 2 under production.
  Bisecting the plan fields under production: (F,F,F), (T,F,F) and (F,T,F) all
  depart at 40; exact_linear alone departs at 2. The token-40 split is a
  batch-composition difference of the packed C2 verifier, which neither strict nor
  production promises to avoid (docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md profile table;
  `batch_invariant` owns that guarantee). Exact-linear substitutes strict row-exact
  linear layers inside a production verifier, so its early split is an expected
  arithmetic difference rather than a fault. No quality claim is made.
- `tests/test_unit_moe_target_arithmetic_scopes.py`,
  `tests/test_unit_qwen35_gguf_compact_moe_gemv_routing.py`,
  `tests/test_unit_qwen35_gguf_verify_layer_stack.py`: exit 0.
- Not exercised: peer cancellation during a C2 cycle.
