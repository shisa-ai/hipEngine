# qwen38 gfx1151 c8 single Q4/Q5 projections rowtile8 (unit 2)

## Summary

Unit 2 of the c=8 GEMV decode campaign. After unit 1 (gate/up rowtile8 +
native-batch fix), the c8 packed-AR step was 312.4 ms with the remaining cost
being single Q4/Q5 projections (attn_qkv, attn_q/k/v/o, attn_gate, ffn_down,
ssm_out) dispatching **WMMA prefill** at rows 5-8 (212x Q4 = 183.7 ms + 60x
Q5 = 42.8 ms) because their rowtile owners were gated to rows 2-4.

## Changes

- `kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_t16_selected_gemv.hip`:
  - `launch_q4_dense_rowtile` (8-col) ROW_TILE 2-8.
  - `launch_q4_dense_rowtile_col4` ROW_TILE 1-8.
  - `launch_q5_dense_rowtile_col4` ROW_TILE 2-8.
  Each keeps per-row independent accumulation, so rows 5-8 stay BF16-bit
  exact to the serial c1 owner.
- `kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_t16_selected_gemv.py`: `_check_dense_q4_t16_
  rowtile_shape` and `_check_dense_q5_t16_shape(rowtile=True)` now accept
  rows 2..8.
- `runtime/gguf_linear.py`:
  - `_q4_t16_sidecar_decode_variants`: rows 2..8 (was 2..4).
  - `_q4_t16_dense_native_dispatch`: rows 2..8 (was 2..4), so single Q4
    projections (attn_q/k/v/o, attn_qkv/attn_gate singletons, ffn_down) route
    to `dense_rowtile`/`dense_rowtile_col4` at rows 5-8.
- `kernels/hip_gfx1151/__init__.py`: `GGUF_T16_NATIVE_ROWTILE_MAX_ROWS_BY_QUANT`
  for `gguf_q5_k_t16_v1` becomes a per-shape cap dict: the 27B
  ssm_out/ffn_down/qkv/v shapes rowtile to c8 while the narrow 0.8B
  (2048,1024) shape keeps cap 4 so its measured direct leaf still wins at
  c5-c8 (`_native_batch_decode_dispatch` now reads the dict form).
- `tests/test_gguf_t16_selected_gemv_decode.py`: Q4 rowtile RED extended to
  rows 2..8 (rows 5-8 verified bit-exact against the serial c1 owner because
  the pack8 production control caps at rows 4); Q5 rowtile RED extended to
  rows 1..8.

## Validation

- RED: Q4 rowtile rows 2-8 + Q5 rowtile rows 1-8, 15/15 passed (BF16-bit
  exact vs c1 / pack8 rows 2-4).
- `rocprofv3` c8 profile: c8 step **312.4 -> 139.8 ms**; Q4/Q5 WMMA prefill
  **eliminated** (was 226.8 ms). New breakdown: gate/up rowtile8 64x 64.2 ms,
  Q4 single rowtile 212x 35.1 ms, Q5 single rowtile 60x 15.3 ms, Q6 lm_head
  rowtile col8 2x 9.6 ms, gdn 8.1 ms, full-attn 3.5 ms. No WMMA-preload in the
  decode step.
- c4 no regression (c4 aggregate stays ~42.5 tok/s with single projections now
  on the extended rowtile). Full c1/c2/c4/native_c8 bench numbers recorded in
  the artifact.
