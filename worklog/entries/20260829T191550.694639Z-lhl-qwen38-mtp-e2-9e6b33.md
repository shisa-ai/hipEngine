# MTP E2 Q4 true-R12 candidates rejected

## Summary

Two exact one-sweep Q4 R12 morphologies lose to the current R8+R4 parent on
every actual Qwen3.8 target shape:

1. **Register-bounded col4:** +9.11% to +44.74% slower, 0/15 paired wins per
   shape.
2. **Shared-weight multi-wave:** +72.93% to +169.68% slower, 0/15 paired wins
   per shape.

The candidates are removed. The current R8+R4 Q4 target owner remains. E2
continues to Q6, whose kernels and register geometry differ.

## Changes

- Added a RED-first temporary actual-weight R12 fixture for one single
  K5120/N6144 and one dual K5120/N17408+SiLU shape against R8+R4.
- Screened an exact four-column R12 owner (48 accumulators/wave).
- After that lost, screened the required shared-weight design: four 3-row waves
  for a single matrix and eight waves for gate/up, staging compact Q4 tiles in
  LDS once per K block.
- Removed both candidate kernels, wrappers, registry variants, and temporary
  test after rejection.
- Added the rejected artifact and updated both campaign plans.

## Validation

- Both temporary candidates match every representative BF16 output and preserve
  guard words versus the exact R8+R4 parent.
- Five single actual shapes and the dual FFN gate/up shape use 5 warmups and 15
  counterbalanced HIP-event pairs per candidate.
- Col4 medians (parent->candidate ms): K5120/N6144 0.1351->0.1553;
  K5120/N10240 0.2042->0.2715; K5120/N12288 0.2590->0.3616;
  K6144/N5120 0.1261->0.1825; K17408/N5120 0.5078->0.6612;
  dual K5120/N17408 0.9633->1.0511.
- Shared medians: 0.1389->0.2770; 0.2066->0.4350; 0.2582->0.6182;
  0.1271->0.3055; 0.4584->1.2362; dual 0.9527->1.6475 ms.
- No runtime admission or complete-wall screen was run because every operation
  leaf failed its all-shape falsifier.
- Final tracked runtime/kernel source is unchanged from `47542dc45`.

## Decision

Reducing sweep count is not sufficient here. Four-column splitting doubles
output workgroups, while multi-wave sharing adds larger blocks, LDS copies, and
barriers; both cost more than the second cached weight sweep at R12 on gfx1151.
Do not reopen Q4 rowtile thresholds without a new operation-complete dataflow
premise.
