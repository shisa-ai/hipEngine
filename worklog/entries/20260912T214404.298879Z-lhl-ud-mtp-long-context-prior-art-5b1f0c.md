# Correction: the 1024 verifier flip is retained prior art, and its long route measured neutral

## Summary

The measured boundary in
`worklog/entries/20260912T212716.391678Z-lhl-ud-mtp-long-context-verifier-126dac.md`
is correct and is the useful part of that entry. Two claims around it were
wrong, and this entry corrects them.

**The flip is retained prior art, not an oversight.** `strict_long_rows` was
introduced deliberately in `7f1734158` ("fix: make dense long split-K MTP rows
byte-exact via c1 dispatch") and is documented in `docs/REFACTOR.md` under
"Dense long split-K multi-row eager verifier row-wise strict dispatch". The
batched multi-row staging above the split threshold is **not byte-exact**: a
real B3 full accept ending at 1024 crossed a BF16 rounding boundary in the
staged multi-row linear-attention/FFN output versus scalar AR (layer 46 row 3,
max abs 0.015625). The per-row route is the RF1 strict eager/oracle fallback
that preserves exactness. So the zero-KL 4096 result is the *intended* behaviour
of an exact fallback, not a missing multi-row verifier.

**The obvious optimization has already been measured and is neutral.** The
previous entry's "Next" said admitting the batched chain in the split-decode
regime is the concrete long-context MTP optimization. The REFACTOR ledger and
the RF2 entry record the opposite outcome: the retained per-row route is 44.7 s
per 8 generated tokens, and the long-context graph alternative measured a
4079-token/eight-output wall of 97.206 s graph versus 97.101 s eager, **0.9989x**
— no benefit — so no long graph bucket is admitted to automatic routing. Proposing
the batched chain as a win without a new measurement would have been a
speculative claim contradicting recorded evidence.

## What stands

- The exactness gate cannot qualify the multi-row verifier above
  `start_position + rows >= 1024`, and its zero-KL output there is uninformative
  rather than reassuring. That is worth knowing before the Phase 5 4096 point is
  cited as a long-context pass.
- The honest declared MTP `context_max` for the UD envelope is 1023.
- The measured boundary itself: `max_abs_diff` 0.073-0.280 at 1021/1022/1023,
  exactly `0.0` at 1024 / 1536 / 2048 / 4096.
