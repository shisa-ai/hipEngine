# Make packed AR c1-c8 measurement and correctness complete

## Summary

Audit of the arbitrary-width measurements found the benchmark harness had not
been completed when c3/c5/c6/c7 configurations were added in `76bc14e95`:
its default packet still ran only c1/c2/c4/c8 plus controls, its complete-packet
set had silently changed, two host tests were red, and the cross-configuration
correctness gate did not check c3/c5/c6/c7 at all. A partial c5/c6/c7 sample
could therefore report `passed=True` from route/length/repeat checks without
proving each row matched the independent repeating c4/c1 fixture. The worklog
claim that all widths were prefix-exact was not mechanically bound by the
artifact schema.

The rocprof parent also artificially restricted physical widths to 2/4/8 even
though the packed model owner accepts direct rows 2-8, preventing the c5/c7
trace needed to distinguish the Q5 cap-4 and Q6 cap-6 fallback thresholds.

## Changes

- `scripts/gguf_packed_ar_bench.py`
  - The default/complete packet is now direct c1/c2/c3/c4/c5/c6/c7/c8 plus
    chunked-c8 and serial-c4 controls, in canonical load order.
  - Cross-configuration correctness checks every direct width against the
    repeating four-row fixture (`c4 + prefix`) and exposes per-width verdicts.
  - Scaling output now carries the complete direct c1-c8 aggregate curve and
    each width's ratio to c1.
  - Build-profile provenance names the direct c1-c8 packet.
- `scripts/gguf_packed_ar_rocprof.py`
  - Allows direct physical c2-c8 in parent and child modes; no trace semantics
    changed.
- Updated the two focused host test files to pin canonical order, direct group
  shapes, every-width trajectory equality, complete scaling rows, and c5/c7
  profiler admission.
