# Gemma 4 MTP status on W7900: 1.40x at budget 4, verify block is not row-invariant

## Summary

Status check of the Gemma 4 assistant-head MTP route on the W7900, prompted by
the reference document still saying nothing was implemented.

It is implemented and reachable (`speculative_provider="gemma4_mtp"`,
`draft_model=<head>`, `LLM.generate_speculative_mtp_detailed`), greedy only.
Measured with `scripts/gemma4_mtp_probe.py`, production profile, target
`UD-Q4_K_XL`, head `Q8_0`, 18 prompts from the two suites under
`benchmarks/prompts/`, two samples, 128 tokens, whole-call rate including the
short prompt's prefill: plain 74.3 against MTP 94.2 tok/s at budget 2 (1.27x),
74.0 against 103.3 at 4 (1.40x), 74.2 against 102.2 at 6 (1.38x), 73.7 against
72.8 at 8 (0.99x). Code prompts gain most (1.51x at budget 4), Japanese least
(1.28x).

The probe exits nonzero at every budget: MTP output is token-identical to plain
greedy on 11 of 18 prompts and diverges on the same 7 each time. Under the
strict profile at budget 4 it is identical on 14 of 18. The provider's
docstring and its `verify_equivalence: bit_identical_to_target_ar_greedy`
telemetry say otherwise.

The cause is that a multi-row verify block is not the arithmetic of one-token
decode. After the same prefill, K tokens forwarded one at a time against one
K-row forward with `logits_rows=K, verification=True` give no bitwise-equal row
in either profile (prompts of 120, 600, 1100 tokens; K of 3 and 7; production
row KL 5.8e-8 to 3.7e-2; no argmax flip in those 30 rows). The first row of the
block already differs from the one-token step after layer 0 in both profiles.
