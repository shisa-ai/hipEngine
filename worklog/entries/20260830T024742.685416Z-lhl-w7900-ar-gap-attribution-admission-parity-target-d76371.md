# W7900 Qwen3.8: every AR loss is owned by admission in ms; admission parity wins all eight rows

## Summary

Splitting the AR wall gap against llama.cpp current into milliseconds per submodule
settles the objective's question. **Decode is faster than the peer at all eight
widths** (negative delta everywhere) and **admission is slower at all eight**
(+92 ms at C1 growing to +1594 ms at C8). Every AR loss is owned by admission, and
**admission parity with the peer - not superiority - wins all eight rows.**

## Changes

- Analysis and target definition only; no code or flag changed.

## Validation

Computed from the committed submodule decomposition (`scripts/
gguf_engine_submodule_decomposition.py` on the retained packets, identity
`long_wall = admission + (tokens-1) x decode_step` verified to <0.5 ms at every
width), against the same peer packet the README rows come from:

| C | our wall ms | their wall ms | gap | delta admission | delta decode |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1097 | 1105 | -8 | +92 | -100 |
| 2 | 1576 | 1354 | **+222** | +235 | -14 |
| 3 | 2021 | 2339 | -318 | +390 | -707 |
| 4 | 2447 | 3458 | -1011 | +583 | -1594 |
| 5 | 2918 | 3298 | -380 | +831 | -1211 |
| 6 | 3389 | 3163 | **+226** | +1093 | -867 |
| 7 | 3856 | 3236 | **+620** | +1295 | -675 |
| 8 | 4330 | 3268 | **+1062** | +1594 | -532 |

At C6 admission costs 1093 ms while decode earns back 867 ms; the row is lost by
226 ms. C3-C5 are won only because decode is far ahead, and C1 is won by 8 ms.

Counterfactual with admission set equal to the peer's admission and decode left
exactly as measured:

| C | now | at admission parity | best peer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 21.87 | 23.88 | 21.72 |
| 2 | 30.45 | **35.80** | 35.44 |
| 3 | 35.62 | 44.13 | 30.79 |
| 4 | 39.23 | 51.51 | 27.76 |

| 6 | 42.49 | 62.71 | 45.83 |
| 7 | 43.57 | 65.61 | 52.54 |
| 8 | 44.34 | **70.16** | 59.35 |

Caveat held explicitly: the C2 margin at parity is **1.0%** (35.80 vs 35.44), which
is inside the documented ~0.8-1.0% same-protocol packet spread, so parity alone does
not *secure* C2 even though it secures C3-C8 by 19-136%. C2 needs grouping beyond
parity, or it stays a coin flip.

Provenance: these are the pre-promotion packet numbers carried in the committed
artifact. Today's promotions already cut admission 4.3-6.1%
(`...w7900-prefill-row-route-counters-single-request-e96fc0.md`), so the real parity
gap is now narrower than the table above; the numbers refresh when the clean one-token
packet lands. The achievable grouped rate is measured (877.0 tok/s at 512 rows), so
parity is not aspirational - it is what a 360-row forward already costs.
