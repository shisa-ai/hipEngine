# VibeVoice ASR torch-free E2E transcription on real speech

## Summary

First torch-free end-to-end VibeVoice-ASR transcription: raw PCM ->
hipEngine front-end -> hipEngine incremental Qwen2 greedy -> parsed JSON
transcript, all on gfx1151 without torch anywhere on the path.

## Changes

- `scripts/vibevoice_asr_e2e.py`: E2E harness. Feature-extractor contract
  (RMS normalize to -25 dB FS, clip, mandatory pad to 3200 multiple),
  hand-rolled chat template matching the processor's transcription
  request (verified token-identical against the LM fixture ids), numpy
  RNG acoustic sampling, `tokenizers`-lib decoding at the boundary,
  tolerant JSON parsing.
- Real-speech smoke input: 11 s JFK inaugural excerpt (openai/whisper
  test fixture, ffmpeg-resampled to 24 kHz mono; scratch file, not
  committed).

## Validation

- gfx1151, real speech (JFK excerpt): transcript
  "And so, my fellow Americans, ask not what your country can do for
  you, ask what you can do for your country." with Start 0.0 / End 11.0
  / Speaker 0 - exact, well-formed JSON. Synthetic-noise audio yields
  sensible non-speech labels ([Humming] [Music]) stable across noise
  seeds.
- All prior gates remain green (front-end 7/7 gpu + 5/5 live; Qwen2
  runner 3/3 gpu).

## Next

- Speed phase: measure hipEngine E2E wall time vs torch GPU (same
  audio, same host) end-to-end; then optimize the dominant costs -
  incremental per-token prefill (batched flash-attention prefill,
  dense GEMM projections) and front-end conv kernels - with rocprof
  evidence.
