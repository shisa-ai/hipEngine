wired PF-4 lever 2 T0 fused combine (commits 455e176ab, 546018f9c, fbb64edfa)

## Measurement (whole-model A/B, gfx1151 zbook, one session)

Protocol: synchronized_direct_runner, canonical fixture
(4 categories × p512/p1024/p4096, 3 samples/case, 36 samples/arm), ABBA-style
counterbalance (incumbent_a, candidate_a, candidate_b, incumbent_b).
Incumbent arm = unfused chain (`UNFUSED_COMBINE=1` in the pre-flip gating);
candidate arm = fused default before the flip. All arms
`all_cases_deterministic: true`.

Overall: pp Σ 1959.1 → 1925.7 tok/s (−1.71% by case sum; −1.69% mean of
per-case deltas), tg Σ 330.6 → 329.9 (−0.17%; mean −0.19%).

Notable: pre-win arm-phase drift exists (incumbent_a pp Σ 995.8 vs
incumbent_b 963.3, −3.3%), which is exactly why the counterbalanced deltas,
not raw arm totals, are the verdict; the 12/12-negative pp sign pattern is
not explainable by session drift.
