# One graph launch costs 0.0004 ms, so the decode remainder is not launch overhead

## Summary

**The launch-consolidation hypothesis is refuted, by ~125x.** Worklog
`20261004T104131` handed off the argument that the decode step's non-kernel
remainder (~10.1 ms/step at p512 after the `DE-11` correction) might be per-launch
device-side transition cost: the decode loop calls `_run_decode_layer_graphed`
once per layer, so a step issues **48 graph launches**, and 48 launches at ~0.2 ms
would reproduce the remainder exactly.

**Measured: one graph launch costs 0.0004 ms.** 48 of them are **0.018 ms/step**,
and the graph wrapper's own cost over the equivalent eager launch is **0.0016 ms**,
or **0.079 ms/step** at 48 launches. Both are two to three orders of magnitude
below the ~10 ms/step the hypothesis needed.

**So the remainder is not graph-launch overhead and `DE-6`'s PM4 / retained
command-list row is not the explanation either**, at least not through the
per-launch path.

## Changes

- `scripts/qwen4exp_graph_launch_cost_probe.py` - the probe. Three arms, each
  launched 2000 times back-to-back with one sync at the end, 5 runs, median:
  an empty graph, a one-memset graph, and the same memset launched eagerly.

## Validation

**Measured** (`uv run python scripts/qwen4exp_graph_launch_cost_probe.py`, host
gfx1151 Strix Halo / Radeon 8060S, `HipRuntime.load()`, nonblocking stream,
4096-byte buffer, N=2000, 5 runs, one `stream_synchronize` per run):

| arm | median ms/launch | runs |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| A. empty graph launch | **0.0004** | 0.0004 x5 |
| B. one-memset graph launch | 0.0037 | 0.0041 / 0.0037 / 0.0037 / 0.0037 / 0.0039 |
| C. one-memset eager | 0.0021 | 0.0023 / 0.0020 / 0.0020 / 0.0021 / 0.0021 |

Per-launch overhead (A) **0.0004 ms**; graph wrapper over eager (B - C)
**0.0016 ms**. Run-to-run spread is 0.0000 ms on arm A and 0.0002 ms on arms B
and C, so the medians are stable well past the resolution this question needs.

**Engagement is structural, not inferred:** `_run_decode_layer_graphed` is called
inside the per-layer loop in `qwen35_gguf_runner.py` (the `moe_graph is not None`
branch of the decode layer loop), and `MoeGraphCache.run` calls
`runtime.graph_launch` exactly once on the replay path. The count of 48 is the
model's layer count.

**Limits.** The probe measures a *small* graph; a real per-layer MoE graph holds
~68 kernels, so this bounds the **launch** cost and not the intra-graph
scheduling cost. It measures an empty and a one-op graph, not the engine's own
captures. One host, one architecture. It does not measure the engine's actual
graph contents, so it cannot exclude intra-graph gaps as the remainder's source -
only the per-launch transition.

## Next

**The graph-launch path is closed; the remaining question is whether the decode
wall tracks kernel time at all.** An interleaved A/B at `code-p512`, chunk 1024,
with `HIPENGINE_GGUF_PACK8_GEMV_WIDE` on (default) against off, was started at the
end of this session. That lever is worth a **measured -1.416 ms/step of kernel
time** (`DE-3`), i.e. ~3% of the 47.7 ms/step p512 wall - resolvable, because an
interleaved comparison on this host repeats to 0.05-0.48%. **If the wall does not
move, the wall is decoupled from kernel time and the remainder is not a kernel-side
quantity at all.** Its result is not in this entry.
