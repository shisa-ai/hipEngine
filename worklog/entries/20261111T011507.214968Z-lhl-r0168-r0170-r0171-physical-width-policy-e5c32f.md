# Physical-MTP R24/R32 trace: gfx1100 physical target policy is widths 1/2/8; C5/C6 R24 not a physical group

## Summary

Investigating the R0168/R0170/R0171 physical-MTP trace cluster on the W7900.
Key finding: the **gfx1100 production physical-target width/depth policy is
widths 1, 2, 8 only** —
`GGUF_SPECDEC2_MTP2_PHYSICAL_WIDTH_DEPTHS["production"] = ((1,2),(1,3),(2,2),(2,3),(8,3))`
(`hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/__init__.py`). **C5/C6 are not physical target
widths**, so R0168's "grouped-R8 at R24 for C5/C6 / five and six active target
slots" cannot be met as written; the policy was deliberately narrowed to the
dense Qwen3.8 C8-K3 serving key. C8 (R32) is a physical width, so R0170/R0171
remain validatable. A 500 ms generation batch window forces decode-cohort
cohesion (the earlier "R24 intermittent" blocker).
