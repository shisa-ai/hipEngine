Supersedes the comparison-basis blocker in
[`20260903T023407.749350Z-lhl-qwen4exp-pf1c-closure-basis-161547`](20260903T023407.749350Z-lhl-qwen4exp-pf1c-closure-basis-161547.md).

[`20260903T033202.234656Z-lhl-qwen4exp-pf1-basis-predeclaration`](20260903T033202.234656Z-lhl-qwen4exp-pf1-basis-predeclaration-6f6810.md).

## Summary

The superseded entry asked the profile owner to choose between a "whole-model
trajectory basis" and a "single-transition admission basis". **Both premises
were wrong, and the real defect is a third thing.**

1. **There is no single-transition basis.** The 2026-08-16 calibration harness
   (`scripts/execution_profile_gdn_calibration.py` ->
   `scripts/gguf_gdn_semantic_gate.py:118`) and the Qwen4Exp production gate
   (`scripts/qwen4exp_layer2_profile_gate.py`, docstring: "18 prompts x
   prefill-last plus 24 c1 rows") both run `reset() -> prefill() -> 24 forced
   steps` with the candidate carrying its own KV/recurrent state. That is the
   basis the section 6.1 thresholds were calibrated on and the basis the PF-1
   gate uses. They are the same basis.
2. **The August harness is durably in-tree**, not a `/tmp` one-off.
   `qwen4exp_layer2_profile_gate.py` is committed and produced the 2026-08-31
   layer-2 rejection. The `/tmp` command strings in the 2026-08-29 artifacts
   are copies of that in-tree pattern, not a lost alternative methodology.
3. **The actual defect is route coverage, not basis.**
   `QWEN4EXP_Q8_MMQ_PREFILL_POLICY` admits MMQ only at `rows >= 64`
   (`hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_q8_0_mmq_prefill.py:104`), and
   the 18-prompt admission suite is 39-71 tokens per prompt. Decode steps are
   single-row. **Only 2 of 18 prompts, 50 of 450 rows (11.1%), can dispatch
   the route the packet admitted.**

## Verdict

The 2026-08-29 Q8 MMQ component gate
(`2026-08-29-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-q8-mmq-prefill-production.json`,
`kl_mean 1.01e-4`, 450/450 top-1) is classified **`route_vacuous_for_scope`**
under predeclared Rule B: 11.1% route-engagement coverage against a 50% floor.
Its whole-model numbers are retired as admission evidence *for this route*.

Three things this is **not**:

- **Not a defect claim against the shipped chain.** Its unit-tier evidence is
  sound and was measured where the route runs — 512/128-row shapes in
  [`20260829T051123`](20260829T051123.358476Z-lhl-qwen4exp-q8-mmq-prefill-wiring-753e6a.md)
  and `tests/test_gguf_q8_0_mmq_prefill.py`. Nothing here says the route is
  wrong; it says the whole-model packet did not test it.
- **Not general to the August manifest.** Q5_1/Q4_K selected MMQ and GDN peer
  gate at `rows >= 2` (`qwen4_exp_runner.py:2898,2926,3107`) and dp4a decode
  runs at `rows == 1`, so those components were genuinely exercised by the
  same packet. The vacuity is specific to the dense-Q8 route.
- **Not a re-judgement of PF-1c/PF-1d.** Per predeclared Rule D, d4x2 and the
  P3 shape row are not re-adjudicated here. The canonical fixture is
  `route_covering` (100%), and on it both failed binding limits. Those
  rejections stand.

## Open item found while measuring

On the 18-prompt fixture the 2-plane candidate is **bit-identical to the
3-plane incumbent on all 18 prompts** (trajectory SHA-256 equal per prompt),
including the two that do engage the route. The leading explanation is that
`policy.planes` is consulted only for the `gemv_f32_f32_out` /
`prefill_f32_f32_out` variants (`hipengine/runtime/gguf_linear.py:5883`),
while `prefill_bf16_bf16_out` is hard-wired to `d4x3` at line 5871 — so at
64-71 rows the engaged shapes take a plane-insensitive path. **This is
inferred from the dispatch source, not traced**, and it means the 18-prompt
fixture cannot exercise the plane knob at all. It reinforces Rule B rather
than qualifying it, and it does not touch the incumbent-versus-strict result
above, which is what this entry turns on.
