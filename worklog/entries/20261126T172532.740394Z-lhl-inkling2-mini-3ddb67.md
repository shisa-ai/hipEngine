# Read the dense Q8_0 projections of Inkling prompt blocks through the wide F16 tile

## Summary

After the experts, the dense Q8_0 projections of a prompt block were the largest cost of the
prefill: on the W7900 at 2,057 prompt tokens and 1,024-row blocks they were 817 of 2,328 ms of
wall time (35%), six projections per visit through the exact coltile kernel at 7 to 8 TFLOP/s.

`fast` and `production` now read them through `dense_wide64x128_f32_f32_out`, a 64 by 128 F16 tile
of the Q8_0 dense-wide family that was registered for the Qwen4Exp dense projections. From 64 rows
for 1,024 or more outputs and from 384 rows for fewer; narrower blocks, the 32-output convolution
projections of a file that keeps them in Q8_0, and `strict` stay on the exact kernel. The tile is
changed arithmetic: both operands are F16, and against the dequantized reference in FP64 its error
is 2.9e-4 of the RMS, where the exact tile's is 1.1e-7.

## Changes

- `Inkling2GGUFDeviceProjections` takes `wide_dense`; the generator passes it for `fast` and
  `production`. It resolves the tile through the registry, keeps one loaded library per build, and
  chooses the build by the rows, as the family's own wrapper does. Importing the module registers
  the family on `hip_gfx1100`; `hip_gfx1151` aliases it.
- Two scopes join the plan, `prompt_block_ge_64_rows_q8_0_dense_n1024_n2304_n3072` and
  `prompt_block_ge_384_rows_q8_0_dense_n384`: `strict` selects the exact kernel, `fast` and
  `production` the tile, with the exact kernel as the fallback each names.
- The 64 by 128 tile rather than 128 by 128: within 25% of it on the large shapes of both GPUs,
  and faster for 1,024 and 384 outputs.

## Validation

- `tests/test_gpu_inkling2_wide_dense.py`: the launch each shape takes, with every launch mocked,
  on both file layouts; and on the model's weights, five projections against the exact kernel,
  within 2e-3 of the largest output and bitwise equal below the rows the tile starts at.
- The Inkling GPU and unit test files pass with the model present: 645 in 4 minutes 22 seconds.
- The default-route test records the tile for the omitted and the explicit `production` profile,
  and its absence under `strict`.
