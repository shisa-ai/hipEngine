# Qwen4Exp exact Q4 logical128 physical64 decode

## Summary

Promoted an exact selected-Q4_K dual+SiLU decode owner that maps logical lanes
`tid` and `tid+64` onto each of 64 physical threads while publishing the same
four strict wave sums. Counterbalanced graph decode improves 12.003 to 13.167
tok/s (+8.84%) on zbook/gfx1151 with exact full logits and generated IDs.

## Changes

- Added and registered `selected_dual_silu_logical128_t64_gemv_bf16_bf16_out`.
- Preserved both projection BF16 boundaries, the four-wave reduction order, and
  the standalone SiLU/product publication bits.
- Made it the unconditional selected-Q4_K dual+SiLU default; retained the
  physical128 operation-complete owner and singleton chain as fallbacks.
