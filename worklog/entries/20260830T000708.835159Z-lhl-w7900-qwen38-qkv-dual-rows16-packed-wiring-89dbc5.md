# W7900 Qwen3.8: QKV dual owner from rows16 (+1.7-2.1% prefill) and the packed-entry session wiring that makes it visible to the shipping route

## Summary

Two findings, the second one more valuable than the first.

**(1) The unequal dual owner qualifies far below its old floor.** The QKV pair
shape (K5120 -> N10240 + N6144) had a 512-row perf-qualification floor for the
dual-WMMA prefill owner. Outputs are bit-identical to the two
`gguf_q4_k_t16_wmma_prefill_shared_b` singletons at that exact shape, so the
floor came down to 16 (rows<=8 keep their GEMV/rowtile decode owners). Bulk
prefill A/B, shipping route, 3 runs/cell, runtime-restored controls:

| rows | control (floor 512) | candidate (floor 16) | delta | generated id |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 0.2362 s (0.2365 second set) | 0.2321 s | +1.7% | identical |
| 24 | 0.2577 s | 0.2528 s | +1.9% | identical |
| 32 | 0.2599 s | 0.2552 s | +1.8% | identical |
| 45 | 0.2703 s | 0.2647 s | +2.1% | identical |
| 96 | 0.3056 s | 0.3001 s | +1.8% | identical |
| 192 | 0.3821 s | 0.3758 s | +1.7% | identical |

Rows 16/32 controls agree across the two independent probe sets (0.2362/0.2365
and 0.2599/0.2599), which is the cross-check that the cells are comparable. This
matches the earlier attribution that said ~2 of the 6.3 points available at 45
rows belonged to the unequal dual rather than to the SiLU dual.

**(2) The shipping route could not see this route at all.** With the floor at 16
committed, the topline was flat, not smaller: AR 22.16 -> 22.14 (C1) and 46.06 ->
46.00 (C8), MTP unchanged, against a **measured** noise floor of ±0.2% aggregate
and ±0.4% on same-cycle pairs (two independent control packets of the same
state). Instrumenting the harness (`scripts/gguf_pair_route_count_probe.py`)
showed why: the route opened `q4_t16_unequal_pair_prefill_session` **zero times**
while still issuing 5120->10240/6144 pair launches at rows
35/36/39/43/46/48/60/67 with the session off (plus 3168 row-1 decode launches,
which the floor excludes anyway). The prefill route sessions were opened only by
`Qwen35GGUFResidentSession.prefill`, but `hipengine/generation/qwen35_gguf.py`
binds `prefill_batch_native` for admission at five sites. `prefill_batch_native`
now opens the same capability-gated session.

**Outcome: both changes are kept.** By crossover measurement the route is worth
+1.8% packed prefill (10/10 configurations) with decode neutral, and the shipping
C8 wall gains +0.7% in a same-chain A/B where the candidate ran second. Details and
the harness-resolution finding are in Validation.

Verification stays on the exact singletons for two independent reasons:
`verify_target_blocks_batch` / `verify_rows` never call this entry (checked over
the whole body of both), and even if a future caller did, the captured verify
groups are 16/32 rows and the pair dispatch cache key already contains the
session flag (`test_q4_t16_unequal_pair_context_is_nested_and_cache_safe`).

  Prefill gains in 10/10 configurations and every measured trajectory hash is
  identical between the two floors, so the arithmetic is byte-identical end to end
  through the packed graph route. But decode is slower in 10/10 configurations
  (-0.1% to -1.0%), which quantitatively explains the flat server topline: at
  native_c8 a +0.8% prefill gain on ~58% of the wall cancels a -1.0% decode cost on
  ~42% of it. The decode cost has two candidate explanations: a real occupancy cost
  of the dual-weight kernel during the weight-streaming decode phase (L2 state
  carried from prefill), or a short-window artifact of the 8-step decode measurement.
- Reversed-order packed A/B (floor 16 measured first, then 512) to exclude run-order
  drift, and a longer-decode pair: **the decode cost was drift, and the prefill gain
  is larger than round 1 suggested.** With the order flipped, the same candidate is
  ahead on *both* metrics:

  | | prefill mean (configs positive) | decode mean (configs negative) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | round 1 (512 measured first) | +1.02% (10/10) | -0.41% (10/10) |
  | round 2 (16 measured first) | +2.61% (10/10) | +0.95% (0/10) |
  | crossover mean | **+1.81%** | **+0.27% (neutral)** |

  The first-measured process is ~1-2% ahead on every rate regardless of which floor
  it carries, so this harness family needs an A/B + B/A crossover; a single
  control-then-candidate pair here is biased by roughly one full point against the
  second arm, which is exactly the size of the effect being measured. The crossover
  mean (+1.8% prefill, decode neutral) agrees with the matched-control bulk-prefill
  measurement (+1.7-2.1%), and trajectory hashes are identical in 20/20 configurations
  across both orders.
- Server topline replication with both arms on the *same* binary (only the runtime
  row gate differs, so the wiring is present in both arms and the comparison
  isolates the route). Control floor 512 measured first, candidate floor 16 second:

  | | control (512) | candidate (16) | delta |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | C1 AR | 21.97 tok/s (1.0926 s) | 21.95 (1.0932 s) | -0.1% |
  | C1 MTP | 31.36 (0.7653 s) | 31.33 (0.7660 s) | -0.1% |
  | C8 AR | 45.62 tok/s (4.2089 s) | **45.94 (4.1797 s)** | **+0.7%** |
  | C8 MTP | 31.51 (6.0929 s) | 31.57 (6.0811 s) | +0.2% |

  The candidate ran second, which the crossover experiment says biases it *down* by
  roughly a point, so C8 +0.7% is the conservative reading of a mechanism-level
  +1.8% prefill gain over ~55% of the C8 wall. C1 is flat as expected: prefill is
  ~25% of that wall, so the predicted +0.45% is under this harness's resolution.
- Harness resolution, measured rather than assumed: four packets of the same
  shipping state (floor 33 SiLU, floor 512 QKV, no wiring) spread C1 21.97-22.16
  and C8 45.62-46.06, i.e. up to **~1% packet-to-packet**, while two packets run
  back-to-back in one chain agreed to 0.24%. Single-rep server comparisons on this
  suite are therefore only trustworthy inside the same chain, and sub-1% claims need
  mechanism-level confirmation (packed rates, kernel counters) to be defensible.
- Decision: **keep** the floor and the wiring. Arithmetic is byte-identical
  (identical trajectory hashes in 20/20 configurations across both orders and
  identical generated ids in every prefill cell), prefill is +1.8% by crossover and
  +1.7-2.1% by matched bulk controls, decode is neutral once drift is removed, and
  the C8 wall improves 0.7%.
