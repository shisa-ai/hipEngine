# the t16 variant of the MMQ32 design is 13 percent faster, but both modes are prototypes

## Summary

`scripts/gguf_q4_k_t16_selected_prefill_microbench.py` times several Q4_K selected-dual
prefill **designs** on one synthetic fixture. Five of them, at 128 experts, 32 rows per
expert, hidden 2816:

| mode | ms | TFLOPS |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `q8-1-ds4-mmq32` | 22.334 | 5.69 |
| **`q8-1-ds4-t16-mmq32`** | **19.422** | **6.54** |
| `q8-1-ds4-wmma32` | 33.397 | 3.80 |
| `q8-1-ds4-wmma64` | 33.173 | 3.83 |
| `q8-1-ds4-wmma32-lds` | 63.069 | 2.01 |

**The t16 variant of the MMQ32 design is 13 percent faster than the plain one**, and the
WMMA designs are 1.5x to 2.8x slower. That ordering is consistent with the instruction
mixes taken in `20260928T154000`, where the dp4a families sat at a bit-manipulation ratio
of 0.5 to 1.2 while the WMMA kernels have no dp4a at all and 250-plus bit-field
instructions.

## What this does and does not price

**It prices a design direction, not a route switch.** The harness's own modes are
prototypes -- its docstring calls `q8-1-ds4-mmq32` "the source-faithful Vulkan-style
32x32 packed-dot tile" and `q8-1-ds4-wmma32` "a first wave32 integer-WMMA prototype" --
and the artifact records `"status": "diagnostic_retained"` with the note "use to compare
kernel designs, not as full-model routing evidence." **The production gate/up kernel is
not among the modes.** So the 13 percent says the dp4a MMQ32 family has 13 percent more
available in its t16 shape; it does not say the shipped route can be switched to capture
it, because the shipped kernel is a different implementation.

**The fixture is also not the production geometry.** Its `out_features_total` is 5504
against the gate/up projection's 1408, and `--out-features-b 0` is rejected
(`out_features_b must be positive`), so the harness cannot be reduced to the production
shape. The comparison between modes is valid because every mode sees the same fixture;
the absolute milliseconds are not production milliseconds.

**And the registered t16 route is a real symbol.** The registry lists
`hipengine_gguf_q4_k_t16_selected_dual_q8_1_ds4_mmq32_prefill_compact32_bf16_bf16_out`
(and a `fused` variant of it), with Python wrappers at lines 1912 and 1956 of
`gguf_q4_k_q8_1_selected_prefill.py`, while the production gate/up path imports only
`..._ds4_mmq32_fused_prefill_compact32_...` at `gemma4_experts.py:1061`. So a route
switch is possible in principle -- but the t16 kernel is a different arithmetic path, and
its correctness against the strict owner has not been checked this unit.

## Next

- **The promotion question is a bounded unit and it is the only live candidate with a
  number attached.** It needs: the t16 route wired for the production shape, the
  arithmetic checked against the strict owner (the MMQ routes' error is bounded by the
  activation quantization step, not by reassociation, so the gate is the route-parity
  test rather than a bit-exactness test), and an end-to-end prefill measurement. Expected
  size if the 13 percent transfers: 0.13 x 156.5 ms = **20 ms of a 687.7 ms prefill, 3.0
  percent**, which is a metric movement rather than a rounding change.
- **Do not read the 13 percent as an end-to-end number.** The fixture differs and the
  modes are prototypes; the number that matters is the campaign bench's prefill seconds
  with the route switched.
- **The WMMA direction is closed for this shape.** Three WMMA modes are 1.5x to 2.8x
  slower than the MMQ32 prototype on the same fixture, which is a large enough margin to
  not need a second measurement.
