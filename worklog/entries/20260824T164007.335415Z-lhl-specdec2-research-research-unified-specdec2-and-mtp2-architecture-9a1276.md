# Research unified SPECDEC2 and MTP2 architecture

## Summary

Completed a code-level review of hipEngine's current C2/speculative hot paths and
seven external implementations. The current provider-neutral records and native
single-request work are valuable migration assets, but dense GGUF speculative
submission still computes complete outputs synchronously under the model-loop
submission lock; `ResidentEngineLoop` never executes `VERIFY_CHAIN` work.

The proposed direction is SPECDEC2: EngineService-owned, bounded
proposal/target-frontier/accept-commit work with AR represented as K=0 and MTP2
as the first staged provider. This is research/proposal only; `docs/PLAN.md` is
unchanged pending explicit architecture approval.
