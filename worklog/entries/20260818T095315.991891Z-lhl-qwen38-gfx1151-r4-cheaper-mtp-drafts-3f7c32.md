# R4: cheaper MTP drafts (deferred on Q4_K_S topline)

## Summary

Assessed whether "cheaper MTP drafts" (the external stack's two levers: int8
drafter module + truncated 40k-token draft-vocab head, ~1ms vs ~3ms per draft)
transfer to the Qwen3.8 Q4_K_S gfx1151 topline. They do **not** as a c=1
headline: on the retained exact B3 topline the entire draft/proposal stage is
only **2.08% of decode wall** (0.206 s of 9.920 s; proposal per cycle 2.37 ms),
while target verify is **97.41%** (9.663 s). So even a fully-free draft buys
~2% B3, and a truncated draft-vocab head — which only touches the draft lm_head,
~57% of a ~2% draft stage — would recover **well under 1%** of decode wall while
carrying acceptance-rate risk that could inflate the dominant 97.4% verify term.
The truncated-head mechanism **already exists** (`mtp_draft_vocab_cap` /
`vocab_cap` in `Qwen35GGUFResidentMTPDraftRunner`; `draft_vocab_cap` in
`mtp_nextn.py` shared-head logits) and is retained at cap=65536 for
Qwen3.6-PARO — but the Qwen3.8 topline draft path is the dense `NextN` executor
(`Qwen35GGUFNextNExecutor`), which exposes **no** vocab cap and scores the full
248320 vocab. Decision: **defer**; revisit only if a multi-request/concurrency
target is adopted where the draft share grows (same boundary as the R2
fp16-state finding).

  23.853/1.7845x README) runs through `scripts/qwen36_dense_gguf_suite.py` →
  `Qwen35GGUFNextNDraftProvider` → `Qwen35GGUFNextNExecutor`, which uses
  `proposal_top1_exact_bf16` over full vocab 248320 and exposes no vocab cap.

  `vocab_cap` built in (default 32768; `Qwen3.6-PARO` retained cap=65536), but
  `_new_mtp_draft_runner` in `hipengine/generation/qwen35_gguf.py` passes the
  full `output.weight` vocab (248320) for Qwen3.8.
- Confirmed `mtp_draft_vocab_cap` / `draft_vocab_cap` wiring exists in
  `scripts/gguf_mtp_bench.py` (experiment flag, not retained) and in the
  `mtp_nextn.py` shared-head logits kernel/numpy wrapper.
- Measured the draft stage share from the retained exact B3 artifact
  (`benchmarks/results/2026-08-17-gfx1151-qwen38-27b-q4ks-exact-native-b3.json`):
  proposal 0.2060 s = 2.08% of decode wall; target_verify 9.6632 s = 97.41%.

  showing draft lm_head Q6 top1 = 56.9% of draft-kernel time (3.70 ms/step) —
  the kernel a truncated draft-vocab head would shrink, but it is ~57% of a
  ~2% stage.
