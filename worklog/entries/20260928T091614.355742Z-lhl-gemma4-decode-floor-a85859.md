# the mmq route beats the fp32 grouped route at one row and the decode floor says kernel tuning cannot reach 2x

## Summary

Two measured results and one piece of arithmetic. The arithmetic is the
important part: **it says the 2X decode objective cannot be reached by making
the GEMV kernels faster, and that MTP is the mechanism that reaches it.**

## The route measurement

`HIPENGINE_GEMMA4_MOE_GATE_UP_MMQ=0` restores the fp32 grouped route, so the
two routes can be compared directly on the canonical instrument:

| route | `decode_tps` at 512p/128o |
| --- | ---: |
| MMQ (default) | **28.01** |
| fp32 grouped (`..._GATE_UP_MMQ=0`) | 24.62 |

**The MMQ is 13.8 percent faster at one-row decode, so the route the engine
already takes is the better one and switching routes is not a lever.** This
also retires the open question `20260928T091229` left: there is no faster route
sitting behind the gate. (A first attempt to measure this with an ad-hoc
harness gave 5.7 tok/s for the fp32 route, which was wrong -- that run hit EOS
after one token, so 179 ms was model load rather than decode. The campaign
bench counts forwards rather than output text and is not fooled by that.)

## The arithmetic that matters

Decode is 35.6 ms per token (`20260928T084824`), and the four weight-bearing
paths account for 21.62 ms of it. **The remaining 13.98 ms -- 39 percent -- is
unattributed.**

```
total weight bytes per token = 3.174 GB
at the part's 256 GB/s       = 12.4 ms  ->  80.6 tok/s absolute floor
2X objective                 = 17.8 ms  ->  56.2 tok/s
```

**So 2X sits at 17.8 ms against a 12.4 ms hard floor, and the 12.4 ms is the
weight traffic at *perfect* bandwidth.** Even with the MoE at the dense path's
199 GB/s and the dense and lm_head at lm_head's 227, the four paths come to
14.38 ms; adding the untouched 13.98 ms of rest gives **28.36 ms = 35.3 tok/s**.
To reach 17.8 ms every path would have to run at peak *and* the unattributed
14 ms would have to shrink by 2.6x.

**Both of those are large asks, and neither is what the last several units have
been chasing.** The MoE gate/up going from 75 to 199 GB/s -- the target
`20260928T091229` set -- is worth about 6 ms, or **+21 percent, not 2X.**

## Why this points at MTP

MTP breaks the floor rather than approaching it. The 12.4 ms is weight traffic
*per token*, and speculative decoding produces more than one token per forward,
so it amortizes that traffic across the accepted tokens. **That is the only
mechanism on the table that moves the 12.4 ms floor itself**, as opposed to
moving the efficiency of paths that are already at 78 and 89 percent of peak.

This is objective item 6, which is the one item currently marked a capability
gap, and `20260926T191119` records the drafter as done and measured competitive
-- 0.796 acceptance, beating llama.cpp on three of four points -- with a
registered provider as the missing piece. **Items 5 and 6 are therefore the
same piece of work, and it is the piece with the largest measured headroom.**
