# Promote temporary chat capture harness

## Summary

Audited the seven recent user-owned top-level Python and shell scripts under
`/tmp` and checked shallow temporary directories outside known clones and tool
state. The reusable gap was repeated capture and exact comparison of
OpenAI-compatible assistant messages. Promoted that behavior into a tested,
engine-neutral repository tool rather than retaining fork-specific wrappers.

## Changes

- Added `scripts/openai_chat_capture.py` with `capture` and `compare`
  subcommands. It preserves reasoning/content, timings, usage, per-response
  hashes, within-mode repeatability, and cross-mode exactness for JSONL prompt
  suites.
- Added `tests/test_openai_chat_capture.py` for response normalization,
  repeated capture, cross-mode comparison, prompt identity checks, and input
  validation.
- Classified `/tmp/qwen4exp_survey_collect_messages.py` as superseded by the
  new tool. Classified `run_apepojken_mtp_survey.sh` and
  `run_nathan_mtp_survey.sh` as hard-coded wrappers whose reusable behavior is
  now covered by `openai_chat_capture.py` plus tracked `mtp-bench.py`; their
  exact historical commands remain in the survey artifact.
- Did not promote `build_qwen4exp_strix_survey_artifact.py` or
  `build_qwen4exp_p1_reopened_artifact.py`: both are one-shot assembly scripts
  for already committed compact artifacts and embed dated raw paths and prose.
- Did not promote `run_qwen4exp_survey_logits.sh` or
  `run_qwen4exp_survey_teacher_logits.sh` as-is: they depend on untracked
  temporary comparator binaries, fixed local model paths, and hundreds of
  megabytes of raw output. Their reusable logit comparison is already tracked
  in `scripts/qwen4_exp_compare_logits.py`; the compact survey artifact retains
  raw hashes and the exact historical protocol.
