# Prefill is kernel-bound: 78% matmuls, attention 15% at 512

## Summary

A kernel trace of one 512-token prefill says where the 3.89 ms per token goes,
and corrects the redirection the length curve made one unit ago.

| ms | % | calls | kernel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 714.91 | 35.5% | 29 | `q5_1_selected_grouped_prefill_staged` |
| 566.68 | 28.1% | 205 | `gguf_q8_0_exact_prefill_tiled_out` |
| 301.09 | 15.0% | 30 | `gemma4_attention_prefill_kernel` |
| 189.24 | 9.4% | 29 | `q4_k_selected_dual_q8_1_ds4_mmq32_fused` |
| 110.89 | 5.5% | 1 | `gguf_q5_k_selected_grouped_row4` |

2.013 s of kernel time against 1.991 s of prefill wall is 101%, so the prefill is
entirely kernel-bound and there is no host-side overhead to recover. That is also
the explanation for the flat per-token cost the length curve found: it is the sum
of a fixed per-layer kernel bill, and matmuls are about 78% of it.

Two things the profile settles that estimates had only guessed at. Layer 29
appears as a single launch, `gguf_q5_k_selected_grouped_row4_bf16_kernel` at
110.89 ms against 29 calls of the q5_1 kernel at 24.65 ms each, so that layer is
about 4.5x a normal one on its gate_up alone - the campaign's second item, seen
directly. And the MoE scheduler the campaign lists third is about 42 ms or 2.1%
combined here, well under the 25 ms figure the campaign carries.

## Validation

- `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` on a single 512-token prefill with one decode step,
  after a non-profiled warmup so the profiled process never spawns `hipcc`, per
  `docs/OPTIMIZATION.md` section 7. 1558 launches over 31 distinct kernels.
- Timestamps were used rather than `DurationNs`; the total matching wall to 1%
  is the check that no streams are double-counted.
- Launch counts corroborate the shape: 29 calls of the q5_1 and MMQ32 kernels for
  29 layers, 205 of the q8_0 kernel at seven per layer, 30 attention launches.
