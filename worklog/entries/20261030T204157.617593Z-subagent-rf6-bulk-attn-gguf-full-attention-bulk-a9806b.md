# Extract bulk prefill attention half

## Summary

Moved the attention half of `Qwen35GGUFFullStackRunner._run_full_attention_prefill_layer_aotriton` into `hipengine/runtime/qwen35_gguf_full_attention_bulk.py`. The runner method remains the same adapter signature and still owns the FFN call; the new owner receives explicit geometry, views, selected operations, policy predicates and caller-owned lifetimes. Decode-batch attention, the FFN owner, graph/storage owners, the GDN verifier handoff and PARO were not changed. The parent updated the architecture reference, PLAN and remaining RF-6 scope for the implemented boundary.

- New `hipengine/runtime/qwen35_gguf_full_attention_bulk.py`: bulk prefill attention norm, Q/K/V projection, q/gate split, head norm and partial RoPE, KV write, direct-INT8/AOTriton/native dispatch, gate multiply and attention output projection.

- `hipengine/runtime/qwen35_gguf_runner.py`: added the bulk view/selector/policy adapters and replaced the old method body with the owner call plus the unchanged FFN call.

- New worklog entry only. No flag, default, gate or backend conditional was added.
