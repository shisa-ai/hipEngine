# Qwen4Exp K320 full-row GR-up DPP qualified and promoted

## Summary

The K320/H2560/four-branch complete-rowbatch4 GR-up DPP sibling
(`coltile2_branch4_rowbatch4_wave_scale_dpp_k320_full_rows_f32_exact`) passed
the full dense-Q8-style qualification pipeline and is promoted to the
production default for the qualified lane (production profile,
`gguf_ud_q4_k_xl`). It is an exact-class narrowing of the promoted GR-up DPP
owner — same ordered FMA/DPP arithmetic, bit-identical outputs — that removes
compile-time-provable generic row-validity and variable-K control for the
K=320 production shape. The generic DPP owner remains the registered strict
fallback and the owner of every other shape;
`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_GR_K320_FULL_ROWS=0` is the bisection opt-out.

Complete-engine A/B (one residency, 12 canonical cases, 72 balanced samples):
prefill 339.258 -> 343.010 tok/s (1.0111x), client wall 484.934 -> 483.709 s
(0.9975x), decode 0.9967x (arm noise; both arms launch the bit-identical
parent kernel at decode), cross-mode outputs exact on 12/12 cases, 4,608
candidate calls, zero teardown allocations.
