# Reject grouped Q4 dual SiLU rows6

## Summary

Reject and remove grouped Q4 dual+SiLU R6 compositions. The retained WG64
candidate was exact and positive in isolated actual-weight leaves, but the clean
same-build target marker regressed both operation-complete Q4+SiLU device wall
and target host wall. The retained grouped projection pair plus standalone SiLU
remains optimal.

## Changes

- Remove the HIP body, C/Python wrapper, registry key, backend policy, runtime
  route, tests, environment flag, and refactor entry introduced by `c52a0fbe9`.
- Preserve the committed screen checkpoint as immutable history and add the
  rejection to `docs/KERNELS.md`.
- Emit compact rejection evidence:
  `benchmarks/results/2026-09-01-w7900-q4km-k3-q4-grouped-dual-silu-rejected.json`.

## Validation

- Actual Qwen3.8 Q4_K_M `blk.{0,32,64}.ffn_{gate,up}` at R24/R30: the WG64
  candidate is **6/6 BF16-bit exact** and isolated event medians improve
  **1.013-1.035x** (minimum 21/30 wins). Its rejected WG128 precursor lost
  **1.1-4.9%** in every cell.
- Clean tracked-source `c52a0fbe9` cached C8 candidate→rollback
  `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace --marker-trace`, exact IDs and acceptance:
  candidate ownership appears 64 times and reduces target launches
  **1,560→1,427**, but Q4+SiLU device sum worsens
  **75.826→77.222 ms (+1.84%)** and target marker worsens
  **173.633→174.305 ms (+0.39%)**. Candidate-first complete wall moves
  **1.3674→1.3599 s**, but this uncounterbalanced direction is nonbinding in

- Exact command, hardware/model/profile identity, raw paths, and hashes are in
  the compact artifact. Two earlier profiler attempts were terminated and
  discarded after a width correction and lingering-child overlap; only the

- The complete C5-C8 gate was intentionally not run after marker rejection.
