# Scope Q4 shared-activation pair selection to its runner

## Summary

R0416 is complete. The profile publishes `q4_pair_prefill` on the generator and
runner: production True, strict False. The retired environment selector is
ignored. Kernel arithmetic, supported shapes, registry checks, expert-grid
admission and pair2 precedence over bundle are unchanged. This is a control
migration, not a new arithmetic promotion or performance claim.

## Changes

- Carry the decision through late-clone inheritance, scalar layer/MoE calls,
  the recursive captured closure, batch layer helpers and mixed target calls.
  Include it in the batch graph-policy key so a changed choice cannot reuse a
  capture made under the other choice. Scalar MoE captures require rows=1,
  below this selector's threshold.
- Add exception-safe `q4_pair_prefill(runner, enabled)` diagnostic scope.
  Campaign and state-gate `q4-pair` arms bind the owning runner and report the
  publication. Both arms force the same exact grouped chain and bundled
  fallback, close routed-F16, production-MoE and exact-iu8 preemptors, and set
  expert-grid64. The record names all forced controls; the scope restores
  both runner and environment state, including on failure. This prevents a
  named pair comparison from measuring a preempting route at zero pair calls.
- Move the Q4 out4 ablation's pair preemptor to its runner scopes. Preserve
  journey localization's strict matrix arms and split prefill/decode choices
  explicitly after removing the environment publication.
- Mark the ENVS row retired, remove the completed ledger entry and map all
  original requirements in the sweep manifest. The original accepted primitive,
  actual-weight, state/KV/logit and trajectory evidence is not reinterpreted or
  promoted here. Preserve its rejection of independent-team and wider
  serial-output sweeps: the admitted implementation shares activation loads.
  Smaller-row and bundled/serial strict fallbacks remain registered.
