# Reject Qwen4Exp prepared strict GDN

## Summary

Rejected and removed a two-kernel strict early-GDN route that precomputes
normalized Q/K, value, beta, and decay before recurrence. It preserves output
and recurrent state exactly but regresses the real rows508 geometry.

## Changes

- Prototyped strict prepare and prepared-recurrence raw-pointer kernels/wrappers.
- Extended the existing strict-vs-serial fixture during screening.
- Reversed all candidate code/tests after the binding microbenchmark loss.
- Added compact rejection evidence and updated P4 campaign/changelog.

## Validation

- Reduced rows5 fixture: prepared output/state array-exact to strict prefill and
  serial decode after correcting the prepared planar Q/K/V pointer layout.
- Production geometry rows508, 16 K/V heads, 128 dimensions:
  - strict fused owner `12.716 ms`;
  - prepare+prepared recurrence `12.757 ms`;
  - ratio `0.996738`.
- Production output and recurrent state are F32-bit exact after one pass.
- Candidate patch is hash-pinned and reversed cleanly; no model dispatch or
  whole-model timing was added.

## Next

Do not add prepared-QKV/scalar traffic to early GDN. A retainable route needs
direct column ownership or persistent state-layout reuse. Proceed to the QSA
attention decomposition or implement a direct recurrence-layout candidate.
