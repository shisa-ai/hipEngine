# Qwen4Exp halo-box campaign review and remeasurement plan

## Summary

Independent review of the completed halo-box section-6 pass found that the
new kernels are covered by focused bit-exact tests, but the retained
whole-model promotion evidence does not satisfy its stated protocol. Each
A/B arm invoked `scripts/qwen4exp_candidate_ar.py` in a separate Python
process and constructed a new model residency; the artifacts and worklogs
instead call the four process-separated arms "same-session". The harness
itself labels its output diagnostic-only and "never a retained claim". This
invalidates the old whole-model deltas as promotion evidence, especially the
sub-2% PF-3 and sub-1% PF-1 effects, without invalidating the kernel-level
measurements or exactness tests.

## Changes

- Added `scripts/qwen4exp_halo_box_campaign_ab.py`, a committed one-residency
  campaign A/B harness that alternates the two exact route compositions per case, retains raw
  samples and exact command/source/profile/model identity, and requires
  within-arm determinism plus cross-arm token equality before reporting a
  speed ratio.
- The before composition is the two registered owners used at campaign start
  (`0436e138b`): pre-M1 Q5_1 expertgrid64 down plus strict per-lane Q8_0
  selected down. The after composition is the current M1 Q5_1 owner plus PF-1
  grouped Q8_0 down. All other PF candidates are default-off or were removed,
  so this isolates the complete retained campaign delta in one current binary.
- Repaired the strict/production manifests to name both reviewed T0 routes.
  Because the prior promotion evidence is invalid, both profiles temporarily
  select the pre-campaign Q5_1 expertgrid64 and per-lane Q8_0 owners. Profile
  binding owns both selectors explicitly; only the review harness opts into
  Q5_1 M1 and grouped Q8_0 until a valid packet supports re-promotion.
- Routed the Q5_1 and Q8_0 calls through their four-axis registry keys instead
  of directly calling imported wrappers.
- Added `tests/test_qwen4exp_halo_box_campaign_ab.py` for arm balance,
  complete-wall arithmetic, sample-count enforcement, determinism, and
  fail-closed cross-arm token equality. Extended
  `tests/test_qwen4_exp_profiles.py` to pin the new manifest rows and binders.
- Corrected the campaign, benchmark rollup, CHANGELOG, and REFACTOR ledger:
  PF-1/PF-3 are review-pending, PF-4's whole-model loss is provisional, PF-5
  is open because tile-16 was not attempted, and D1 remains an owner decision.
  Removed the stale completed PF-4 A/B flag entry.
- Marked the affected PF-1/PF-3/PF-4 artifacts withdrawn or provisional and
  corrected PF-2 to a correctness rejection with non-binding process-separated
  timing. Stored numbers remain available as historical diagnostics.
