# Staged-f32 tile beats the dense-projection leaf by 23.6% on attn_qkv

## Summary

The LDS-staged f32 prototype built for the GR screen (`20260929T092430`) is a
general tiled GEMM over Q8_0 weights, so it was measured on the shape the census
says matters most: `dense_projection` is 4553.007 ms of a 15741.963 ms prefill
kernel union, **28.9%**.

It beats the promoted dense-projection leaf on **every layer** and is the best
variant in the sweep at all five:

| layer | promoted leaf ms | staged_f32 ms | speedup |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 16.1363 | 13.1740 | 1.2248 (+22.5%) |
| 9 | 16.1803 | 13.1611 | 1.2294 (+22.9%) |
| 22 | 16.1757 | 13.0907 | 1.2357 (+23.6%) |
| 34 | 16.1929 | 13.0773 | 1.2383 (+23.8%) |
| 46 | 16.1915 | 13.0574 | 1.2400 (+24.0%) |

Median **+23.6%**, 3315-3327 -> 4075-4112 GFLOP/s.

The same arithmetic question now has an answer on two shapes: the weight-traffic
win is available at f32 precision, worth 2.39x on the GR projection (K=320) and
1.24x here (K=2560). The GR shape gains more because it is the more
traffic-starved of the two.

  being wrong. The parent is retained as `PARENT_VARIANT`.

## Next

**The numerical character is inferred, not measured.** 6.9e-06 to 1.05e-05 is
consistent with f32 summation-order drift for K=2560 (sqrt(K)*eps*|S| ~ 3e-6*|S|,
so |S| ~ 3) and is two to three orders of magnitude too small to be f16 operand
rounding, which is what the sibling `dense_wide256` route produces (relative L2
4.7e-05, max abs 7.3e-05 on the GR shape). But no relative-error figure was
computed. Before this goes to a gate, compute one.

**The 6.8%-of-prefill figure is an upper bound and must not be quoted as a
result.** This run measured one tensor suffix at one row count. `dense_projection`
also contains `attn_output` and the dense-layer MLP projections, whose shapes
differ and whose staged-f32 performance is unmeasured.

Next units, in priority order:

1. **Measure the rest of `dense_projection`.** Re-run this harness on
   `attn_output.weight` and the dense MLP suffixes. That converts a one-tensor
   result into a family number, and it is the same command with a different
   suffix.
2. **Compute a relative error figure** against the production leaf and, if it
   lands where the magnitude suggests, take the candidate to the
   `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` production gate with a registered strict
   fallback. It is **not** a strict change: the output is not bit-identical.
3. **MX-1 build** - still the campaign keystone at 7454.3 ms of the 12973 ms gap.
