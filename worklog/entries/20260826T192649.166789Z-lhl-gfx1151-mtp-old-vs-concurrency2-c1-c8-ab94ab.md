# Measure gfx1151 MTP OLD versus CONCURRENCY2 c1-c8

## Summary

Measured the requested same-host gfx1151 MTP systems comparison at every
logical width c1-c8. The primary metric is median aggregate generated
completion tok/s over p512/d128 blocking completions HTTP with one warmup and
three measured runs per width. CONCURRENCY2 is **71.9% to 83.3% faster** than
OLD across the curve.

| Route | c1 | c2 | c3 | c4 | c5 | c6 | c7 | c8 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| OLD MTP | 7.907 | 7.127 | 7.068 | 7.068 | 7.222 | 7.088 | 7.056 | 7.013 |
| CONCURRENCY2 MTP | 13.596 | 12.935 | 12.956 | 12.948 | 13.091 | 12.957 | 12.665 | 12.552 |
| Difference | +71.9% | +81.5% | +83.3% | +83.2% | +81.3% | +82.8% | +79.5% | +79.0% |

This is an end-to-end systems comparison, not an isolated concurrency-only
ablation: OLD uses its legacy hard-coded B2 serial-exact MTP route, while
CONCURRENCY2 uses its default B3 native-verifier MTP route. It is also a fixed
synthetic-prompt diagnostic, not the full category+heldout anti-gaming suite,
so it is not a retained speculative performance claim.
