## Comparison

- **Vs the 2026-09-19 campaign end** (4b5a17f1f/f4b087690, same host, same
  protocol): off 345.4 -> 350.2 s (+1.4%), default 281.8 -> 287.8 s,
  retained 16 275.8 -> 285.8 s. Every arm including the no-cache baseline
  ran ~1.4-3.6% slower wall on the fresh day, so the vs-baseline deltas
  moved -18.4/-20.1 -> -17.8/-18.4 while the hit/reuse pattern stayed
  exactly identical. The ~2-point residual beyond the baseline drift is
  within the same-session AR variance band previously recorded on this host
  but is not decomposed here; a same-day bisection of e2dccb3a0 against the
  merge parent would be needed to attribute it to the private-KV budgeting
  fixup rather than day-to-day variance. No evidence of a regression: no
  declined hits, no `gapped_suffix_exceeds_paged_budget`, and the retained-16
  worst single-turn prefill is 15.8 s against the 18.0 s of the default
  retention and the 114 s of the original regression.
- **Vs where the campaign started** (2026-09-18 first served A/B, same host
  and protocol): prefix caching measured **+6.4% wall then (424.5 s vs
  399.0 s off; 14.02 vs 14.92 tok/s) — it made serving slower**. On the
  merged main it measures **-18.4% wall (285.8 s vs 350.2 s; 20.82 vs
  17.00 tok/s)**. The radix arm's absolute wall dropped 424.5 -> 285.8 s
  (-32.6%) while the off baseline itself improved 399.0 -> 350.2 s (-12.2%)
  from collateral fixes during the campaign, so the like-for-like swing is
  the +6.4% -> -18.4% delta change of ~25 points.
