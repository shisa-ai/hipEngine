# UD gfx1151 optimize2 E4a: route five Q5_T16 decode shapes to tile8 owner; parity metric pending

## Summary

Iteration 8 executed E4a against census item 1 (the `qk_t16_selected_direct_gemv<ushort,5,...>`
direct GEMV at 242.9 µs/launch, 20.47 ms/tok, no plain counterpart). Investigation refuted the
"MoE selected-experts" reading: both arms resolve zero `selected*` variants through
`hipengine.LLM.generate()` — the symbol name is historical selected-rows naming on the ordinary
`linear` layer. The real lever was a policy-table miss: gfx1151's
`GGUF_T16_C1_VARIANTS_BY_QUANT_SHAPE` carried exactly one Q5_T16 shape, `ssm_out` (6144,5120),
which is plain's entire Q5 residency (48 tensors). UD's other six decode shapes — ffn_up,
ffn_down, attn_gate, attn_qkv, attn_v, attn_q (119 tensors) — fell through
`_t16_c1_variant_dispatch` to the slower direct owner.

Decision: **route lever (b), not leaf tuning (a)** — tile8 already exists, is already registered
for `gguf_q5_k_t16_v1`, and needed only policy rows. A rows=1 screen (allocate-once timing, warm
20 + 200 measured launches per shape) established per-shape truth: tile8 is bit-exact versus the
direct owner and the `gguf_quant_gemv` CPU reference on all seven production shapes, and wins
1.16–1.39× on five of the six direct-fired shapes. `attn_v` (5120,1024) ties at 0.99× and was
deliberately left on direct — no row, no risk, no expected gain.
