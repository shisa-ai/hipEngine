# Gemma 4 MTP wide verify block lm-head split

## Summary

A Gemma 4 MTP draft budget of 8 gave no speedup because its nine-row verify
block's lm head left the Q8_0 rowtile kernel (2-8 rows) for the generic
per-(column, row) kernel: 21.89 ms of a 56.7 ms block in a W7900 kernel trace.
`Gemma4Runner` now launches a wider head in balanced chunks of at most eight
rows. Nine-row verify 56.7 -> 39.4 ms (exact 66.4 -> 49.1), budget 8 0.99x ->
1.24x; budgets 4 and 6 unchanged.

The same traces split the remaining per-row cost of a five-row production
verify block (27.6 ms against a 13.9 ms one-row block): dense Q8_0
projections 9.8 ms on the T16 WMMA prefill kernel (3.2 ms at one row on the T16
GEMV), experts 7.6 ms (2.1), attention 2.8 ms on the class kernel (1.0 on
flash), lm head 2.4 ms (1.2). llama.cpp `45451c3` on the same card runs a
five-row block in 18.6 ms against 14.6 ms for one row (`llama-bench -p 5 -d
600`), and its MTP with the same head reaches 2.01x on the same 18 prompts.
