# Separate shared PLE projections from indexed history updates

## Summary

Expose the existing PLE stage's stateless preparation and finish around its
request-indexed convolution update. This is a prerequisite for sharing
projections across mixed target rows without treating a candidate chain as
independent requests. No arithmetic, flag or default selection changes.

## Changes

- Preserve `run_qwen4_exp_batch_ple_stage` as the composition wrapper; add a
  validated plan plus preparation, convolution and finish functions.
- Existing owner admission, scratch bounds and registry resolution remain the
  preflight authority.
