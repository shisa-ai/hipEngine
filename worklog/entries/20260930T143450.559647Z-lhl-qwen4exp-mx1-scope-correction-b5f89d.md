# MX-1's premise is not established: the repair is a bit-parity mechanism and the F16 WMMA machinery already exists

## Summary

MX-1 is the ledger's largest remaining lever — the repair is **1,219.978 ms = 7.75%** of
the 15,741.963 ms prefill union — and the previous unit promoted it out of the census's
"closed" list. The next action was its build. **Reconnaissance before writing any kernel
changes what that build is, and shows a cheaper route to the same prize.**

The row's premise is: *remove the repair, and the way to do that is to change the
arithmetic to F16 operands.* Reading the tree says the second half does not follow from
the first.

**The repair is a bit-parity mechanism, by its own documentation.**

- The risk kernel's docstring: "Identical published arithmetic to the plain iu8 kernel;
  outputs whose F32 value lies within `risk_multiplier * (abs(kahan_err) + FLT_EPSILON *
  sum(abs(term)))` of a **BF16 rounding boundary** are queued by linear output index for
  the sparse exact repair pass."
- The risk multiplier's docstring: "Under the production-correctness contract a rare
  escape is bounded drift, **not a contract violation**; 16.0 is chosen so the route
  stays bit-identical to the strict parent in practice, **keeping every existing gate
  green**."
- `qwen4_exp_profiles.py` states the purpose of each flag directly: gate/up is
  "**bit-identical to the pair2 owner** (state gate + 72 exact trajectories)", Q5_1 down
  is "**bit-identical to the pair2 row-publish parent** in practice at multiplier 16",
  Q5_K is "**bit-identical to the strict row4 parent**". Each is marked
  "production-only binding".
- `EG-3` records that the repair "reproduces the pair2 parent", and that the iu8 route's
  promotion was 1.50-1.82x operation-complete.

**So the tree's structure is: pair2 = exact and slow; iu8+repair = 1.50-1.82x faster
AND bit-identical to pair2. The repair is the price of bit-identity, not of accuracy.**
`AGENTS.md` states the binding contract is production correctness and that strict is "a
debugging oracle, not the promotion bar" — yet the production profile sets all three
`*_IU8_EXACT` flags to `"1"`. **The premise "removing the repair requires changing the
arithmetic" is therefore not established.**

## What the reconnaissance found, item by item

**1. The F16/BF16-WMMA routed-expert arithmetic already exists in this tree.**
`gguf_q4_k_t16_selected_expert_major_wmma_comp_bf16_bf16_out` is **registered for
`moe_linear`** under `gguf_q4_k_t16_v1` and `gguf_q6_k_t16_v1`
(`hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_k_t16_selected_prefill.py`). It decodes the
**bit-lossless T16 repack** to F16 in registers, runs
`__builtin_amdgcn_wmma_f32_16x16x16_f16_w32`, accumulates in F32 with a
`compensated_wmma_accumulate` correction, and has **no int8 plane, no ds-trick and no
repair by construction**. The plain grouped fallback
`gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_wmma_prefill_compact_bf16_bf16_out` is likewise a
BF16-operand WMMA route already on the path. MX-1's WMMA, quant-to-F16 decode and
compensated accumulate are **existing machinery, not this row's build**.

**2. The gate/up leg's repair-free configuration is reachable today.**
The dispatch checks `exact_grouped_q4_gate` **before** the `q4_iu8` branch
(`qwen4_exp_runner.py`, the `elif` chain), so
`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_EXACT_GROUPED_Q4=0` + `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q4_IU8_PREFILL=1`
selects `gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_wmma_iu8_prefill_bf16_bf16_out` — the **plain iu8
kernel, which takes no risk buffers and never repairs**, and by the docstring quoted
above publishes *identical arithmetic* to the risk kernel. That is 573.211 ms of the
repair as a **configuration, needing only the production gate, with no kernel written**.

**3. The down leg is the real gap, and it is smaller than the row assumed.** There is
**no plain (non-risk) iu8 Q5_1 down prefill** — `qwen4_exp_q5_1.hip` offers only
`q5_1_selected_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill_kernel` — and its non-repair alternative inside
`exact_grouped_down` is the bit-exact logical256 owner, the slower route iu8 was
promoted over. Dropping the down leg's 661.210 ms needs a **plain sibling of that same
kernel** (the risk body minus the risk criterion and queue), not a new arithmetic.

**4. What a full F16 port would still need, exactly:** a **`gguf_q5_1_t16_v1` layout and
kernel, which does not exist anywhere in the tree** (T16 layouts exist only for q4_k,
q5_k, q6_k and q8_0 — verified by enumerating the registry keys), and the expert-major
kernel `static_assert`s `qtype == 4 or qtype == 6`, so **q8_0 and q5_k are uncovered on
`moe_linear`**; plus a dual-plus-SiLU moe pairing (the existing dual-plus-SiLU
registration is `linear_pair_silu`, the dense layer). So the F16 port is a real build,
but on existing machinery rather than from scratch.
