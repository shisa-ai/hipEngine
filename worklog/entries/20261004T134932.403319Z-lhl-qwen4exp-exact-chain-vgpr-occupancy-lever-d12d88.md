# The exact coltile chain's leaf is 12 VGPR from the 32-wave ceiling, and its 8 row pointers are wave-uniform

## Summary

**The exact coltile chain's production leaf sits at 76 VGPR / 25 waves per CU
(78.1%). 64 VGPR would reach the 32-wave architectural ceiling, so 12 VGPR stand
between it and +28% occupancy. Eight of its live values are wave-uniform pointers
that do not need VGPRs.**

**This is the only prefill lever left that cannot break the numerical gate**,
because a register/scheduling change does not alter the arithmetic. Every other
prefill lever identified in this campaign - the dense-wide scope extension and the
MoE repair removal - is blocked by per-scope KL envelopes.

## Measured census

`scripts/qwen4exp_repair_register_census.py --source
hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/quant/gguf_k_gemv.hip` (193 kernels compiled,
`--save-temps`):

| kernel | VGPR | SGPR | static ins | waves/CU | occupancy |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| non-pipelined `coltile_rowbatch` (q8, 8x4, wave-scale) | 72 | 58 | 205 | 28 | 87.5% |
| **production leaf `pipelined_dpp` (q8, 8x4, wave-scale, DPP)** | **76** | **48** | **309** | **25** | **78.1%** |
| production leaf with DPP off (same tile) | 76 | 48 | 378 | 25 | 78.1% |
| sibling `pipelined` (q8, 4x8) | 88 | 46 | 434 | 23 | 71.9% |

**Two facts fall out that the ledger did not have in one place.** The DPP
reduction cuts **378 to 309 static instructions at identical 76 VGPR and identical
occupancy**, which is exactly the shape its own promotion recorded. And the
**pipelined** leaf is at *worse* occupancy than the non-pipelined one it replaced
(25 against 28 waves), so the 6.84% the pipeline bought was won against an
occupancy regression rather than with one.

## The register budget, and where the 12 can come from

The leaf's live state decomposes as `acc[ROW_BATCH][COL_TILE]` = 4 x 8 = **32**,
the two pipelined operand stages `wa[8] + xa[4] + wb[8] + xb[4]` = **24**, and
`weight_rows[COL_TILE]` = 8 pointers at 2 VGPR each = **16**, giving 72 against an
observed 76.

**The 16 VGPR of `weight_rows` hold wave-uniform values.** Every input to the
pointer is uniform across the wave:

```
weight_rows[col] = qweight + (out_col_base + col) * row_bytes
out_col_base     = blockIdx.x * COL_TILE        // uniform
row_bytes        = (in_features / Q8_0_BLOCK) * Q8_0_BLOCK_BYTES   // uniform
qweight                                             // kernel argument
```

**No term depends on `threadIdx.x`**, so these belong in scalar registers, and
only **48 of roughly 106 SGPRs** are in use. Moving them out is the single
identified route to 64 VGPR and 32 waves.

**The honest uncertainty, stated because the arithmetic above does not close:**
72 predicted against 76 observed means about 4 VGPR are already spent somewhere
the decomposition does not name, and the non-pipelined sibling observes 72 for a
budget that decomposes to 60. So the compiler is already promoting *some* uniform
state and the split is not fully accounted for. **Whether the pointer array is the
recoverable 12 VGPR is a hypothesis to measure, not an established fact** - the
measurement is a build with the array replaced by a scalar base plus uniform
offsets, re-read through the same census.

## Why this is the right row to open

The ledger's `DP-8` recorded the opposite conclusion for this leaf and then
corrected it: *"Tile-shape family exhausted, no spills, occupancy at the
register-only maximum"* is **"WRONG FOR THE PROMOTED LEAF... it is not at the
register-only maximum; the unscaled pipelined sibling is at 83 VGPR / 23 waves.
The tile conclusion stands unchanged... but 'occupancy at the register-only
maximum' should not be quoted as a property of this leaf, because it is what makes
an occupancy-shaped retile look impossible when it is merely unmeasured."*

**This entry supplies the number that correction lacked**: the ceiling is 64 VGPR,
the leaf is at 76, the gap is 12, and the uniform pointer array is 16 of them.

**Scope and value, bounded.** The exact chain serves the dense layers the wide
route does not cover, which at 13-47 is layers 0-12. `DP-8` records the chain at
**2650 GFLOP/s, 16.1% of its own instruction-mix ceiling** (a probe with the same
40 FMA + 105 integer mix sustains 16467 GFLOP/s), so the gap is real but the
attainable fraction is unproven. **No speedup is claimed here.** A +28% occupancy
gain applied to roughly 17% of prefill would be worth a few percent, not a
headline.

  `gguf_k_gemv.hip` with `--save-temps` and read the `.amdhsa_next_free_vgpr` /

  the template arguments: `IffLi8ELi8ELi4ELb1ELb0ELb1` is
  `scalar_t=float, out_t=float, qtype=8, COL_TILE=8, ROW_BATCH=4, WAVE_SCALE=true,
  ROW_FIRST=false, DPP_REDUCTION=true` - the production leaf named in the ledger.
- The pointer-uniformity argument is read from the kernel source
  (`gguf_k_gemv.hip`, the pipelined kernel body and `gguf_k_coltile_load_stage`),
  not inferred.
