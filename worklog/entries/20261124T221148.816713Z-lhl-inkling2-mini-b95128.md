# Run F32-activation experts on the Inkling device-route path

## Summary

Campaign task 8, after [the gate's first rows](20261124T221148.767997Z-lhl-inkling2-mini-9fb31d.md)
failed on the code category. The Q8-dot expert chain carries the whole divergence of the opt-in
path from strict; nothing else in the `fast` plan does. The device-route path now runs the
selected F32 kernels for gate/up and down, and the same gate passes it.

Where the divergence comes from, measured on the gate's 672 teacher-forced rows with the `fast`
profile and one kernel family exchanged at a time (W7900, mainline `UD-Q4_K_M`):

| `fast` with | Median KL against strict | p90 KL | Code-category p90 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| fast experts and controllers (the shipped plan) | 5.74e-5 | 3.03e-3 | 2.98e-2 |
| fast experts, strict controllers | 6.14e-5 | 4.00e-3 | 2.83e-2 |
| strict experts, fast controllers | 8.38e-14 | 1.97e-7 | 1.37e-12 |
| strict experts and controllers | 1.10e-13 | 4.78e-7 | 3.91e-12 |

The last row keeps the fast attention variants, so those are at rounding scale too. With fast
experts the largest logit change on a row has a median of 0.58 to 0.62 nat; with strict experts
it is 1.2e-5.

The gate on the device-route path, before and after the change (W7900, gfx1100, HIP 7.2.53211,
trees that held the uncommitted change):

| Arm | Median KL | p90 KL | Rows over 5e-2 | Top-1 | Gates |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| resident decode, Q8-dot experts | 5.34e-5 | 3.68e-3 | 2.08% | 98.81% | fails `code` |
| resident decode, F32 experts | 1.14e-13 | 1.27e-10 | 0 | 100% | all pass |
| decode graph, F32 experts | 1.14e-13 | 1.27e-10 | 0 | 100% | all pass |

- **The dense envelope passes as well:** mean KL 1.82e-6, p95 1.8e-7, p99 1.06e-5, maximum 3.0e-4.
- **The graph is the resident path bit for bit.** The two arms ran in separate processes; all 672
  candidate rows are equal, largest absolute difference 0.
- **Controls, repeats, isolation, public route and tasks hold as before:** no control mismatch over
  744 calls, bit-stable state, 25 of 25 bit-identical in the reversed-order replay, 25 of 25 on
  the public route, 9 of 12 known-answer cases for strict and for each arm with the same IDs.
- **The F32 chain costs 3.5 ms per step.** Session step, median of 64 steps after a warm request:
  26.7 against 30.2 ms after 47 prompt tokens (37.4 against 33.1 tok/s) and 33.4 against 36.7 ms
  after 1,717 (29.9 against 27.2 tok/s). The expert kernels were 3.4 ms of a 20.7 ms step in the
  trace; the selected F32 kernels replicate the activation row eight times and read it per route.

## Changes

- `hipengine/loading/inkling2_gguf_device.py`: `project_experts_device_routes` drops its NVFP4
  Q8-dot branch and runs the selected F32 kernels for every bank, one replicated activation row
  per route, each launch taking the route's outer-scale pointer. `device_routes_supported`
  requires the selected kernels for every bank. The Q8-dot kernels stay on the eager single-token
  visit, where `_run_selected` and `project_experts_mixed_rows` select them.
- `docs/KERNELS.md`, `docs/ENVS.md`, `docs/REFACTOR.md` (R0526 to R0528): the route the
  device-routed experts take and the gate rows.
- Tests: `tests/test_unit_inkling2_device_routes.py` records what the route chain asks of its
  kernels and requires the gate and down scale pointers.
