# Shared random operands: the torch and HIP lanes now time one request

## Summary

The two VibeVoice-TTS lanes were being compared across different random draws. The
fork's stochastic inputs are all recorded in the committed fixture, so the torch
lane now serves them instead of drawing its own, and the comparison is
like-for-like:

| lane | pooled RTF | warm synthesis | load |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| hipEngine (median of 4) | 1.337 | 4.457 s | 4.275 s |
| torch (median of 3) | 1.336 | 4.454 s | 1.907 s |

Both reach EOS at 27 tokens under the request's declared 35-token cap. The
remaining difference is weight-load time, not generation.

## Why the operands are the ones to share

The fork draws randomness in exactly three places on this path, and the fixture
records all three:

| site | draw | recorded as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `modular_vibevoice_tokenizer.py` gaussian sampling | `randn(batch_size)` | `encode_draw0` (1,) |
| same | `randn_like(self.mean)` | `encode_draw1` (1, 70, 64) |
| `modeling_vibevoice_inference.py:sample_speech_tokens` | `randn(2, acoustic_vae_dim)` per call | `callN_initial_noise` (2, 64), 25 calls |

`install_recorded_operands` patches `torch.randn` and `torch.randn_like` and answers
**by shape**, not by call order, so any draw it does not recognise reaches the real
RNG. It returns a ledger of what was consumed.

## Verification, not assertion

The shim is checked against the fixture's recorded step-0 `eps`, captured by
wrapping the diffusion head. This is the part that matters: a shim that is subtly
wrong — serving the wrong frame, or the wrong operand for a shape — still produces
plausible audio and a plausible RTF. It does not reproduce the recorded tensor.

```
max_abs_diff            0.0
mean_abs_diff           0.0
reference_abs_max       1.164062
max_abs_diff_relative   0.0
```

Bit-identical, on three runs. The cold run consumes exactly the recorded set
(`encode_draw0` 1, `encode_draw1` 1, `frame_noise` 25, `unmatched_frames` 0) with 25
frames available.

The torch lane's own `generated_tokens` is 27 and `diffusion_frames` 25, which
matches the HIP lane's `oracle_generated_tokens` 27 — the two lanes took the same
trajectory, which is what serving identical noise should produce.

## Changes

- `scripts/vibevoice_tts_torch_bench.py`: `install_recorded_operands` (shape-matched
  `randn`/`randn_like` shim plus consumption ledger) and `install_eps_capture` (the

  longer claims the lanes draw their own tensors.
