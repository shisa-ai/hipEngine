# DSpine Q8_0 drafter quantization: forward 2.57x, matched decode +13%, AR-exact

## Summary

Task #34(a): Q8_0-quantize the DSpine drafter's bandwidth-bound projections. The forward is bandwidth-bound (rocprof kernel-sum 43.2 ms vs wall 45.6 ms = 5% dispatch gap; `dflash_dense_bf16_to_bf16_wmma` was 86% of the forward at ~57-184 GB/s). Quantizing gate/up/down/o to Q8_0 T16 tiles and running the row-tiled Q8 WMMA prefill (`gguf_q8_0_t16_wmma_prefill_auto_2wave_bf16_bf16_out`, bf16 activations, weight reuse across the 16 rows) cuts the forward **44.8 -> 17.4 ms (2.57x)** and the matched full-mtpbench decode **12.43 -> 14.05 tok/s (+13%)**, AR-exact, acceptance preserved. Ships default-on; `HIPENGINE_DSPINE_DRAFTER_Q8=0` is a precision rollback lever (R0523).
