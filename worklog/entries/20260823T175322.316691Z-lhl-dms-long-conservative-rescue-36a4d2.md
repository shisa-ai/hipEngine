# Add conservative long DMS rescue and fresh holdout exclusion

## Summary

Prepared one predeclared conservative bias-only rescue after the train-only CR2
bias repair transferred at 8K but missed the 32K Japanese max/tail gate. The
rescue reduces eligible eviction from one-half to one-third (effective CR1.5)
on calibration rows only. Also made the corpus builder exclude arbitrary prior
source manifests so the already-used development heldout cannot become final
evidence.

## Changes

- Long bias calibration now accepts finite fractional target CR values greater
  than one and records the effective target/eviction fraction separately from
  the package's historical CR2 objective.
- Long corpus construction accepts repeatable additional source-manifest
  exclusions and records every exclusion hash.
- Added tests for CR1.5's one-third eviction fraction and combined short/long
  source exclusion.

## Validation

- The train-only CR2 bias candidate (64 changed biases, 327,680 unchanged
  weights) passes all four untouched 8K categories: aggregate max/mean KL
  0.01620/0.002192, 100% top-1, heldout CR 1.707-2.091x.
- At untouched 32K, code/English/mixed pass; Japanese rejects at max KL 0.17280
  and 87.5% top-1. Aggregate max/mean is 0.17280/0.009218 and 96.875% top-1.
  No-evict remains green at max KL 0.000475/100%, and every route drains to zero.
- Eight focused calibration/manifest tests pass. Ruff, py_compile, and diff
  checks pass.

## Next

  32K final heldout excluding both v1 and v2 sources; do not promote from reused
  development rows.
- Escalate to full learned-weight retraining if the conservative rescue fails.
