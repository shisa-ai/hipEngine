# my own gap summary was incomplete: we win 7 of 8 AR cells and lose MTP by 45 percent at C8

## Summary

For four consecutive reports I stated the goal gap as "prefill 149 vs 201 tok/s, AR
loses C2, K3 trails at most widths". Every part of that is literally true and the
summary is still misleading, because it quotes single cells instead of the matched
C1-C8 matrix in `benchmarks/README.md:122-133` (W7900, Qwen3.8 `Q4_K_M`, complete
wall, content-exact). Read cell by cell, the objective is in a different shape than
I reported, and the priorities come out reordered.

## Changes

- None to code. This corrects my own reporting, not the engine.

What that says, against what I had been reporting:

- **Decode is not the weak axis.** AR wins 7 of 8 cells, by 34-95%. I described the
  row as "loses C2" as though that were the headline. It is one cell.
- **The real defect is MTP under concurrency.** K3 peaks at C4 (55.780) then *falls*
  to 47.960 / 49.020 / 55.225 while llama.cpp scales 59.644 -> 101.072. At C8 we are
  41% behind, the largest single gap in the matrix. "Trails at most widths" hid a
  scaling failure, which is a different bug and a bigger one.
- **Prefill is bimodal, not uniformly behind.** We win C4 (+13.0%) and C7 (+14.0%).
  The deficit lives at C1-C3 (-18% to -27%), i.e. the low-concurrency path. Paired
  with the single-wave entry's observation that the published metric's mean wave wall
  is 0.641 s against a 0.233 s engine prefill wall, the C1-C3 deficit plausibly sits
  in non-kernel wall time, not in kernels.
