# Campaign path forward: PF-0 fixture prerequisite and T0-first lever ordering

Follows [`pf1-basis-correction`](20260903T034205.467427Z-lhl-qwen4exp-pf1-basis-correction-6336a4.md).

## Summary

The campaign goal is unchanged — close the section 5.2 prefill gap against the
external engines. Two things changed about *how*:

1. **The dense-Q8 arithmetic levers are spent.** Three were measured and
   rejected (tile retiles bit-exact but slower; single-plane `d4` fails unit
   numerics; two-plane `d4x2` and the P3 shape row fail the section 6.1
   envelope). The 23-26x dense-projection deficit they targeted is a schedule
   problem, not a precision problem, so further precision dials are
   unproductive as well as expensive.
2. **The production-numerics gate has a fixture problem, not a standard
   problem.** No threshold moved. But neither committed fixture is both
   route-covering and natural text:

   | Fixture | Natural text | Q8 MMQ route coverage |
   | --- | --- | ---: |
   | `qwen4exp_admission_suite_18prompt.json` (39-71 tokens) | yes | 11.1% |
   | `qwen4exp_canonical_ar_p512_p1024_p4096.json` | **no** — `code-p512` is 104 unique tokens in 512, built by repeating four short prompts | 100% |

   Every remaining arithmetic-changing candidate is therefore unadjudicable
   until a fixture exists that is both.

## Decisions recorded in the campaign doc

- **PF-0 added as the next executable unit**: build a natural-text
  route-covering fixture (>=512-token natural prompts, four canonical
  categories, per-case token-ID hashes, coverage and unique-token ratio
  recorded). It is the prerequisite for PF-1c lever 2, PF-3, PF-4, and any
  PF-5 reassociation.
- **Section 6.3 arithmetic-class routing**: `T0` (bit-exact restructuring)
  costs exact-parity RED + trace + A/B and **no** numerics packet; `T1/T2`
  costs PF-0 plus a coverage-complete packet per candidate; `T3` is not
  admissible through the drift gate at all. Standing instruction: exhaust `T0`
  on a family before proposing `T1/T2` for it.
- **Ordered queue**: PF-0, then PF-2 (QSA, 19.5x at p4096, no external
  mechanism to port so it is pure headroom), PF-5 (GDN M6, external evidence
  2,139.4 -> 648.0 ms), PF-3, PF-4, PF-1 remainder. `T0` levers may start
  immediately and in parallel with PF-0.
- **PF-1's remaining fork stated explicitly**: extending the MMQ route to
  coltile-served shapes is inherently `T1/T2` (that is what PF-1c lever 1
  measured and what failed). The unexplored and cheaper path is a *bit-exact*
  faster dense kernel matching the coltile reduction order.
- **Section 6.4 open owner decisions**, not for a coder to resolve: D1 whether
  the shipped Q8 MMQ route re-qualifies once PF-0 exists (canonical-fixture
  incumbent measures mean 2.73e-3 / p95 1.14e-2 / top-1 96.67% vs strict,
  single-layer isolation); D2 the sub-25-row scope power problem, which belongs
  to the 2026-08-31 recalibration campaign.
- **Section 6.5 per-unit definition of done** consolidates the gates so
  PF-2..PF-5 no longer inherit them by reference to a PF-1 row that changed.
- **Scope of reopening** is bounded: PF-0 may legitimately re-judge `d4x2` and
  the P3 shape row under a rule predeclared before the run; it does **not**
  reopen the `T0` tile retiles (bit-exact, lost on speed) or single-plane `d4`
  (a `T3` representation question).
- **Standing risk added**: no KL or top-1 number may be quoted without naming
  which fixture produced it.
