    --model /models/gguf/Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M.gguf --backend hip_gfx1100 \

- Reachability: the shape is genuinely multi-round and genuinely mixed, which
  is exactly the coverage F1 asks for. `row_capacity` 768, `total_rows` 3200,
  `route` `slot_fair_bounded_rounds`, **`chunk_count` 5**, `chunk_rows`
  `[768, 768, 768, 768, 128]`, `full_prompt_lengths`
  `[512, 384, 384, 384, 384, 384, 384, 384]`.
  `chunk_final_output_slot_indices` is `[[], [], [], [1..7], [0]]`: the first
  three rounds contain **no** final slot at all, and round four mixes seven
  final slots with one intermediate.
- Available reduction: **`intermediate_tail_samples` = 25**. With the mask on,
  `output_norm_rows` and `lm_head_sample_rows` would fall from **33 to 8** for
  this shape. `final_output_mask_enabled` is `False` in this arm, confirming
  the control is the unmasked route.
- Control-arm oracle verdict: `tokens_exact` true and `lifecycle_state_exact`
  true, but `initial_state_exact`/`final_state_exact` false with
  `first_divergence` at prefill layer 0 `linear`/`conv`. This is the documented
  pre-existing packed-versus-strict-c1 divergence recorded for packet A
  (`20260905T145151.194428Z-lhl-gfx1100-rdna3-transfer-packet-a-b3d2b6.md`),
  not a new fault: it is present with the mask off, so the binding gate for the
  flip is the candidate-versus-rollback differential through
  scripts/diff_differential_arms.py, not the absolute oracle pass.
