## Changes


- Validate the cross-campaign comparison without treating it as a hardware-normalized benchmark. Both Laguna artifacts share the **15.6647424-GFLOP/token** active-linear ledger: current W7900 Q2 XL rowbatch8 at **79.585 tok/s** is **1.247 TFLOP/s**, while completed gfx1151 Q4_K_M at **654.249 tok/s** is **10.249 TFLOP/s** and **8.583x** its own **76.226-tok/s** matrix512/attention128 starting row. This is strong directional evidence of software headroom. It does not establish that IQ explains only 15% of the gap. The proposed **157.177 GB / 6.433 s = 24.4 GB/s** is the conservative one-scan-per-M128-chunk source-byte model divided by wall, not physical DRAM traffic. The frozen Q4 artifact has **75.169 GB** of tensor bytes and cannot replace the 39.681-GB Q2 XL artifact on a 48-GB W7900.
- Source audit confirms the affine/codebook distinction but corrects the scale premise. IQ2/IQ3 must expand grid indices plus sign parity before a valid dot; their post-expansion magnitudes fit signed bytes. Current exact grouped prefill stores eight FP32 magnitudes and performs eight BF16-to-FP32 FMAs per segment. IQ2's scale byte contains two nibbles shared over **16 weights each**, not one scale over 32; exact pair16/shared-scale prefill was already rejected after regressions up to **5.25%**, though decode retains pair16. IQ3's high-nibble scale is shared over 32. Both constant grids are 1 KiB, so LDS staging remains a measured exact hypothesis rather than an assumed win.
- Do not rebuild the proposed integer path: retained explicit P6 already expands raw IQ2 to signed-byte fragments, stages a 32-column x K256 tile in **10,240 B LDS**, and runs D4-Q8_1 integer WMMA. Its prior quantizer-inclusive synthetic screen improved exact auto **22.49-28.76% at 256 tokens** and **45.03-49.86% at 512**, with a conservative 2,560-compact-row crossover; M128 provides 1,280 rows and M512 5,120, making WPF-C1 directly relevant. It remains changed arithmetic with routing-padding, runtime scratch, all-layer quality, and exact-repair gates open. Standalone P2 `dp4a` was already rejected/removed after inclusive regressions and must not be repeated.
- Update `docs/LAGUNA-prefill.md` to keep the execution order unchanged while making WPF-2 exact-first: expert-major IQ2/IQ3 reuse and measured optional 1-KiB grid staging precede any repriced P6 comparator; a future changed-math route requires complete quality and bounded fail-closed exact repair. Correct the stale current-efficiency sentence from the 41.720-tok/s control's **0.652 TFLOP/s** to retained rowbatch8's **1.247 TFLOP/s / 1.470%** of the 84.8-TFLOP/s BF16 reference. No runtime, kernel, benchmark rollup, or performance claim changes; no GPU or 4K run is needed for this plan-only unit.
