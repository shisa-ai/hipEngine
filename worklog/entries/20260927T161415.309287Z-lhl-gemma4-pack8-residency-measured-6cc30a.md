# Keeping both expert layouts would cost 36 GB, so the fallback must go pack8-aware

## Summary

Measured the residency cost, and it **forces** the design decision the previous
entry left open.

**The plan.** `plan_gemma4_gguf_resident_specs` plus `resident_bytes` over the real
artifact: **16995.2 MB (17.0 GB) across 657 tensors**, of which the **60 rank-3
expert tensors are 14416.5 MB (14.4 GB) - 84.8% of all residency**.

**The expansion is 1.33x, not 2x.** One fused expert tensor goes from 285.5 MB raw
to 380.6 MB packed: 253.8 MB int32 `qweight` plus 126.9 MB fp32 `scales` and
`mins`.

**So the choice is not close:**

| option | total | delta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| keep both layouts | 36.2 GB | +19.2 GB (+113%) |
| drop raw after promotion | 21.8 GB | +4.8 GB |

Keeping both is almost certainly beyond what is affordable, which means the
fallback **cannot** simply keep reading the raw layout. Either
`gemma4_project_experts_rows`' `per_expert_offset` route must be made
pack8-aware so the raw copy can be released, or the raw copy must be retained only
for the tensors the fallback actually needs.

This converts the previous entry's open question into a forced decision, and it is
the kind of thing that would otherwise have surfaced as an out-of-memory at
promotion time - after the correctness gate had passed, which is the worst moment
to discover it.

## Validation

- Measured with the loader's own planning path (`plan_gemma4_gguf_resident_specs`,
  `resident_bytes`), which computes the total without allocating, so this is the
  real artifact's plan rather than an estimate.
- The expansion factor is from the earlier synthetic repack's actual output shapes
  and dtypes, applied to the real tensor's declared shape.
- **Not verified:** the box's actual device-visible memory ceiling. The 36.2 GB
  figure is the plan's requirement; whether it fits is a separate measurement, and
  the 21.8 GB option is the one to compare against that ceiling.
