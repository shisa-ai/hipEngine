# Decode is MoE-and-packing bound, not attention bound

## Summary

First kernel-level profile of the decode path, and it says where the 2X target
has to come from.

At 128-token context, 64 decode steps, 21.0 tok/s, so 47.6 ms per token:

| ms | % | calls | per step | kernel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 860.49 | 28.0% | 1827 | 28.5 | `gguf_q4_k_selected_prefill_out_kernel` |
| 576.92 | 18.8% | 12915 | 201.8 | `gguf_k_pack8_prefill_out_kernel` |
| 530.48 | 17.3% | 1827 | 28.5 | `q5_1_selected_gemv_bf16_bf16_kernel` |
| 222.23 | 7.2% | 64 | 1.0 | `gguf_k_pack8_prefill_out_kernel` |
| 50.04 | 1.6% | 1575 | 24.6 | `gemma4_attention_decode_class_kernel` |

**Decode is not attention-bound.** Attention is 1.6%. The expert matmuls and the
activation packing together are about 71%, and that is where a 2X has to come
from - which is a different answer from the prefill side, where attention was
worth 15% before the warp512 change.

**The packing is the surprise.** `gguf_k_pack8_prefill_out_kernel` appears twice
and together accounts for 26.0% of decode, and the 12915-call instance is **202
launches per decode step** at 44.7 us each. That is a quarter of decode time spent
in a very large number of very small launches, so reducing the launch count is a
lever in its own right, separate from making any individual kernel faster.

**One check worth recording because the names mislead.** 54% of decode time sits
in kernels whose names contain "prefill", which reads as a mis-dispatch. It is
not: `gemma4_moe_prefill_route_enabled` requires
`lanes >= _PREFILL_MIN_LANES_PER_EXPERT * num_experts`, and decode has
`lanes = 1 * top_k = 8` against 128 experts, so the row-reusing prefill route is
correctly off and the selected/GEMV family runs. The names are historical. I
verified this before writing it up, and it is the kind of thing that would
otherwise have started a chase in the wrong direction.

## Validation

- `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` over a 128-token prefill followed by a real 64-step
  decode loop, run after a non-profiled warmup so the profiled process never
  spawns `hipcc`, per `docs/OPTIMIZATION.md` section 7. 71868 launches over 38
  distinct kernels, 3067.9 ms of kernel time.
- The per-step counts corroborate the shape: 1827 is 28.5 per step for 29 MoE
  layers, and 12915 is 201.8 per step for the seven per-layer dense projections.
- The route-selection claim is read from `gemma4_moe_prefill_route_enabled`'s
  condition and the decode `lanes` value, not inferred from kernel names.

## Next

- The 202-per-step packing launch count is the most concrete lever: find out why
  the dense path issues seven separate pack launches per layer per token and
  whether they can be batched or fused into the matmul.
- The Q4_K gate/up at 28% is the largest single line; the earlier route work on
  the prefill side does not apply here, so it needs its own look at the decode
  shape.
- Attention at 1.6% means the query-tiling work proposed for prefill is not a
  decode lever.
- This profile is at 128-token context; decode cost rises with context, so
  re-measure at a longer context before treating these shares as fixed.
