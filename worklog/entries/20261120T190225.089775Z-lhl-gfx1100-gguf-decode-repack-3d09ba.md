## Validation

- Use separate processes per arm and model, with `HIPENGINE_GGUF_DECODE_REPACK` unset for default and set to `0` for raw. For each of `Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_S.gguf` and `Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M.gguf`, call `LLM(..., backend='hip_gfx1100', max_sequence_length=256, max_active_requests=1, prefix_cache='off').generate_detailed()` with `[9707] * 9` and `[9707] * 73`, greedy AR, EOS ignored, and four new tokens. Every matched arm returned the same generated IDs; this checks short output, not model quality.
- Inspect each loaded `Qwen35GGUFResidentWeights.weights` through the runner's materializer and record row-one keys at `gguf_linear._record_gguf_linear_dispatch`. Default selected Q4/Q5 T16 and planar Q6 T16; raw selected Q4 pack8 and raw Q5/Q6 dispatch. No resident weight had both raw and T16 allocation keys. Tracked materializer allocation grew by 15,510,112,256 (default) vs 19,244,025,856 bytes (raw) for Q4_K_S and 16,406,317,056 vs 19,590,711,296 bytes for Q4_K_M. Tracked allocations and bytes returned to the zero baseline after each `close()` and device synchronization.
- Decode-repack dispatch, semantics, and fastpath safety unit files pass.
