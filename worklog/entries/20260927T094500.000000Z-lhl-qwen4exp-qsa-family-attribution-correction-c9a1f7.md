# Correction: the QSA family listing in 20260927T090500 was 2x and incomplete

## Summary

`benchmarks/results/2026-09-27-qsa-prefill-grid-resolution/` (worklog
20260927T090500, commit 9c7384e5a) reported the QSA family as 1237.3 ms with
`qsa_sparse_attention_h256_wave_rows_f32_kernel` at 910.8 ms and 73.6% of it.
**Both figures are wrong**, for two independent reasons, and the artifact is
corrected in place with this entry recording the superseded path.

**Cause 1 - unhalved trace.** The `rocprofv3` CSV in
`2026-09-17-qwen4exp-per-role-cost/role-trace` is **two repetitions**: its raw
kernel total is 44173.3 ms against the role-analysis `window_ms` of 22698.6, and
raw/2 = 22086.6 matches. The role-analysis is already at 1x. Sprint Q read the raw
CSV directly, so every figure it reported is 2x. (Sprint M and Sprint R used the
role-analysis or halved explicitly, so those are unaffected.)

**Cause 2 - name filter.** The listing filtered kernel names on `qsa`. The
family's largest member is
`qwen35_paged_full_attn_decode_context_tensor_batch_kernel`, whose name contains
no `qsa`, so it was dropped entirely.

## What changes and what does not

- **Unchanged: the ranking conclusion.** K/V amortization saturates at HEADS=2, so
  K/V reuse and LDS staging are dead and tensor cores are the only lever on the
  sparse kernel. That is a kernel-level microbenchmark at fixed geometry and does
  not depend on the family total.
- **Changed: the prize.** HEADS=12 extrapolates to about 8% of 455.4 ms = **36 ms**,
  not 73 ms.
- **New: the ranking was scoped to 32.1% of the family.** The **801.2 ms
  full-attention decode-context kernel is 56.4%** and was not examined at all. No
  statement in the Sprint Q artifact applies to it, and it is the largest single
  piece of the worst-ratio family in the taxonomy.
