# HC-6 closes: three GR-up variants spanning 400x in projection precision all fail the gate in one narrow band

## Summary

`HC-6` was "the competitor HC route" - down as int8 WMMA, up as Q8 to **F16** WMMA, combine
fused with the MoE epilogue and the next norm - with the status `OPEN — changed/scoped mechanism
required` and the action "use layer/role localization plus the f32-operand staging path before
another full gate".

**The f32-operand staging path has now been run (`HC-1`) and it fails.** That removes the
prerequisite the row was waiting on, and it does so in a way that closes the row on the
mechanism rather than on one measurement.

## Changes

**Ledger `HC-6` closed**, and the finding recorded below because it is broader than the row.

### The finding: on the GR-up route, operand precision does not buy back the gate

Three independent GR-up arithmetic variants now have a production gate result. Their projection
drift spans **400x**; their gate mean KL spans **0.15e-3**; all three fail.

| route | projection drift (rel L2) | gate mean KL | top-1 | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| GR-up iu8 (rejected) | 1.12e-07 - 3.69e-07 | 1.402e-03 | 772/780 | FAIL |
| **GR-up staged f32 (`HC-1`, new)** | **1.72e-06** | **1.499e-03** | 0.99818 | FAIL |
| GR-up wide f16 | 4.709e-05 | 1.554e-03 | 768/780 | FAIL |

The iu8 and wide rows are from
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-27-gr-up-wide-tile-screen/README.md`; the staged-f32 row is
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-29-qwen4exp-hc1-gr-staged-gate/`. The staged-f32 drift figure is read
from `benchmarks/results/2026-09-29-gr-staged-f32-screen/screen.json` (`relative_l2`
`1.720768573065372e-06`), not from prose.

**Monotone in drift, but almost flat.** Going from iu8's ~2e-07 to wide's 4.7e-05 - a 400x
loosening - moves the gate from 1.402e-03 to 1.554e-03. Tightening back to 1.7e-06 recovers only
to 1.499e-03. **The route's gate failure is not bought back by precision.** A variant an order of
magnitude tighter than the f16 tile still fails by the same margin.

This is the quantified form of what `MX-1`'s packet already called cliff-shaped: "The GR family in
particular is cliff-shaped: its F16 wide tile was 4.61x faster on the projection and still failed
the 780-row gate at mean KL 1.554e-3."

### Why this closes HC-6 specifically

`HC-6`'s own mechanism cell equates its arithmetic with the wide tile's: *"This is the arithmetic
the GR wide tile tried and failed at the 780-row gate (mean KL 1.554e-3)."* Its error is entirely
the **weight** operand - the screen measured `wide_f32in` and `wide_f16in` at **identical** error
(4.709e-05 to every printed digit) because both stage the dequantized weight tile as f16 halves.

So HC-6's arithmetic is already measured failing at 1.554e-03, and the route it proposed to try
instead - the f32-operand staging path - is now measured failing at 1.499e-03 while being ~27x
tighter on the projection. **There is no precision headroom left to spend.** The row's
"changed/scoped mechanism required" cannot be satisfied by scoping or by tightening operands,
which is exactly what it proposed.

**What would still be open, stated so this is not overread:** `HC-6` is a *larger fusion* than the
GR-up projection - it also fuses the combine, the MoE epilogue and the next norm. Nothing here
measures those parts. What is closed is the up-projection arithmetic that the row identifies as
its own mechanism, and the precision-based plan for rescuing it.

## Limits

The three-point comparison is **across packets, not one arms pair**: the iu8 and wide rows come
from a 780-row gate at `code-p4096`, the staged-f32 row from a 550-row gate over 22 prompts with
the long-form suite. So the absolute mean-KL values are not a controlled comparison. **The
monotonicity in drift and the narrowness of the band survive that caveat** - three routes, three
drift decades, one failure band - but the ordering of the three numbers should not be read as a
measurement of precision sensitivity. One model, one quant, one host. The staged-f32 result is
`mixed_ja_en`-driven (1.499e-3 against a 1e-3 limit) while the wide tile's is a whole-suite mean,
so the two figures are not the same statistic.
