# Tile exact Qwen4Exp Q8 prefill

## Summary

Promoted exact raw-Q8_0 F32 coltile4/rowbatch8 on the Qwen4Exp prompt path.
Natural p508 reaches 34.517 tok/s (1.785x) and p1012 34.164 tok/s (1.909x),
with bit-exact primitive and full logits.

## Changes

- Exported/registered the previously missing Q8/F32 rowbatch and coltile
  instantiations from the generic raw-K kernel.
- Corrected generic admission to use Q8's 32-value block rather than Q5/Q6's
  256-value block.
- Added an independent gfx1151 Q8/F32 capability without enabling the rejected/
  unqualified W7900 Q5/Q6 policies on this backend.
- Qwen4Exp defaults to rowbatch selector 32 + coltile, resolving to coltile4/
  rowbatch8; env `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_RAW_ROWBATCH=0` restores scalar.
- Artifact:
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-28-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-exact-q8-coltile-prefill.json`.

## Validation

- Primitive rows37/H256/out32: rowbatch32 and coltile4/rowbatch8 FP32 bits
  exactly match scalar Q8 prefill.
- Paired p508 scalar→candidate: 26.264→14.718 s (-43.96%, 1.785x), full logits
  exact; 34.517 prompt tok/s.
- Paired p1012: 56.550→29.622 s (-47.62%, 1.909x), full logits exact; 34.164
  prompt tok/s.
- Geometry screen: rowbatch4/8/16/32 = 20.365/19.775/19.641/20.592 s;
  coltile4 beats retained rowbatch16 20.170→14.737 s; coltile2 is slower at
  16.037 s and was not retained.
- Cached p512 trace: total kernel 25.008→14.230 s; Q8 13.972→3.121 s,
  3,952 launches, mean 789.7 us, zero scratch.
