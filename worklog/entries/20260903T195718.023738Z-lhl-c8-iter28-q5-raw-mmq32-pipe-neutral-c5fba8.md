# C8 iter28: pipe variant neutral on correct layout; kmajor artifact corrected

## Summary

Implemented and measured the software-pipelined raw MMQ32 (prefetch next K32
tile into registers, double-buffered LDS, one barrier per iteration). The
variant is bit-exact versus the serial owner, but on the correctly fed
(non-kmajor d4s4) raw owner it is neutral: 9.123 vs 9.109 ms per 48-weight
R24 pass (1.0015x, 22/48 wins). The initial 1.118x reading was a harness
layout artifact. The serial mb2 owner stays the registered default; the
pipe kernel remains a registered exact diagnostic.

## Findings

- Both iter27/iter28 leaves initially fed all arms from the kmajor
  source-layout producer. Under that feeding the serial mb2 arm ran slow
  (10.170 ms) and pipe appeared 1.118x faster (9.096 ms).
- The outer-floor reference check in the new pipe contract test exposed the
  wrong feeding (KL ~46, top1 0.25 vs the CPU reference): production feeds
  raw MMQ32 owners from `gguf_q8_1_d4s4_f32_quantize_bf16` (non-kmajor);
  kmajor is rows==32 source-layout only (`gguf_linear.py` routing).
- With the corrected producer the leaf reruns: mb2 9.109 ms, pipe 9.123 ms
  (1.0015x, 22/48), mb4 10.170 ms (1.1164x SLOWER). The iter27 mb4-neutral
  conclusion is corrected by a follow-up decision entry.
- Pipe == serial bit-exactness holds under both layouts (identical address
  math and per-(row, output) accumulation order; verified 48/48 in both
  runs and by the pytest contract).
- The layout gotcha is recorded in docs/KERNELS.md ("MTP verify profiling
  traps" item 0) so future raw-owner leaves use the non-kmajor producer.
