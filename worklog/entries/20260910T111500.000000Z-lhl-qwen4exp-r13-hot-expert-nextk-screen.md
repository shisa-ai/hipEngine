# #35 hot-expert weight reuse / next-K pipelining: bounded SCREEN (both levers REFUTED)

Review guidance #3 asked to test the two levers left open by the amended R13
closure. Recorded as a screen first; no gated candidate was promoted because
neither lever clears.

## Method

New capture driver `scripts/qwen4exp_hot_expert_reuse_screen.py`: wraps the
three per-layer Python entry points (QSA decode, GDN, QSA prefill) and
snapshots each layer's `scratch.moe.selected` between layer calls — outside
any graph capture, so the sync is safe (the earlier router_select-level hook
poisoned capture with error 901). 12 canonical cases: prefill chunks plus 48
decode steps each; 28,800 layer-call captures (1,152 prefill + 27,648
decode). Prefill totals reproduce the R13 capture exactly (10,813,440 pairs,
335.08 nonzero experts/capture, 386,017 instances).

## (a) Hot-expert weight reuse (prefill): REFUTED as a weight-set lever

- **No small hot set**: top-16 of 512 experts cover 25.3% of pairs; top-128
  (a quarter of the pool) covers 73.6%.
- **Routing is diffuse**: ~335/512 experts (~65%) selected per (layer,
  chunk); ~1.05 GB of expert weights streamed per capture - the layer nearly
  fully streams its expert pool every chunk regardless of scheduling.
- **Capacity infeasible**: top-8-per-layer across 48 layers = 1.20 GB vs
  MB-class caches (top-128 = 19.3 GB).
- **No universal hot set**: cross-case top-32 Jaccard 0.316.
- Consecutive-chunk expert-set Jaccard is high (0.797) but that is stability
  of a large diffuse set, not a reuse lever.

**Residual (recorded, not promoted)**: within-expert weight-tile reuse - the
R13 ladder item 3 mechanism. The current dual_wmma kernels grid one block per
(col_tile, 16-row fragment) and re-read the expert's weight rows per
fragment; perfect within-expert reuse would cut expert weight-read traffic
up to **2.32x** (897,210 fragments vs 386,017 distinct selections, fixed
16-row tiles). Kernel structure confirmed in
`gguf_q4_k_selected_prefill.hip`. Promotion condition: rocprof memory
counters must first show fragment re-reads are material AND not already
L2-absorbed; and the R13 decomposition's dominant factor (3-plane iu8
compute tax) must not dwarf it. Arithmetic-preserving (scheduling only) if
attempted.

## (b) Next-K pipelining: REFUTED as a TG lever

- Same-set prediction covers only **37.7%** of the next step's selections
  (exact repeat rate 0.16%, 48 steps x 48 layers x 12 cases).
- Decode expert weight traffic is ~1.50 GB/step = ~29 GB/s at the 52.3
  ms/token TG median - far below the platform memory peak, so expert weight
  fetch is not the decode bottleneck; overlap/prefetch cannot move TG
  materially.
- Cross-chunk prefill routing pipelining is blocked by the sequential
  dependency (chunk t+1's router input depends on chunk t's full forward
  pass); speculating on hidden states would change arithmetic and violate the
  no-exactness-relaxation constraint.

## Consequence for the R13 closure

Both levers the amendment left open are now measured and closed for this
envelope regime: the amended closure's open set shrinks to the within-expert
tile-reuse residual (bounded 2.32x, condition-gated) and the 6.1 policy fork.
The 6.01s-vs-1.00s routed-expert attribution remains provisional (producer/
consumer costs unaligned); the screen adds that the gap's reusable-weight
fraction is small by construction.
