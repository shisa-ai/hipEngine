# Add exact Git provenance ledger to Qwen3.8 scaling campaign

## Summary

Added a historical audit ledger to the closed Qwen3.8 gfx1151 scaling
campaign. The ledger records exact full Git hashes and ISO-8601 committer
timestamps for the campaign definition, X1, M0-M5, P1-P3, R1, closeout, and
post-closeout correction. Multi-commit items are split by implementation,
measurement/default decision, and later correction instead of assigning one
misleading completion commit.

The audit also makes two evidence limitations explicit. M2b-M2g were local,
reverted diagnostic passes whose observations are summarized only by the M2h
entry. M2i's HIP API/copy/kernel trace remains raw-only under `/tmp`; its
interpretation first became durable in M2j. Neither should be treated as a
self-contained retained artifact in a future performance claim.
