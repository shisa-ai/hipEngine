## Summary

P8's device-chain oracle localizes the C2 acceptance/state defect beyond GPU
accept: all 173 GPU accept results equal the CPU oracle; duplicated same-root
rows have equal proposals/target paths; initial prompt-root acceptance matches
C1 10/10. Later provider histories diverge. Twelve physical proposal cycles emit
out-of-vocabulary `INT_MAX` because one request's entire 5,120-element NextN
output hidden becomes NaN immediately after a prior full-accept cycle, while the

the final candidate. This is not exact for the affected full-attention provider

accept: restore the pre-proposal checkpoint, consume the root, then consume every
accepted candidate in order. The K-1 and reject-root snapshot optimizations stay
unchanged.
