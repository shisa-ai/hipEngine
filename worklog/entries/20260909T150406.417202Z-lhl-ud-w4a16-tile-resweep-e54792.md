# W4A16 tile re-sweep on gfx1100: row-conditional 16x64/32x64 retained (+6% prefill E2E)

## Summary

Route-plan item A steps 2-3 (impact-list task 3), run on GPU1 (7900 XTX)
after moving the lane off GPU0. The retained 16x128 tile was swept on
gfx1151 only; the gfx1100 re-sweep on the real 512-row IQ4_XS shapes
retains **32x64** - 18-20% under 16x128 on BOTH gfx1100 cards (XTX 9.24 vs
11.25 ms total, W7900 10.38 vs 12.93), winning on every shape, the opposite
ordering from gfx1151 where 32x64 measured 50% worse. The small-row region
flips the other way: 16x64 wins through 64 rows (2.4x 16x128 at 8 rows -
the TILE_N activation padding dominates), with the tile crossover between
64 and 128 rows.

The retained config is therefore row-conditional and arch-conditional:
gfx1100 builds use 16x64 through 64 rows and 32x64 above; gfx1151 keeps
16x128 everywhere. `launch()` selects the library by rows; the build cache
keys each tile set; explicit `HIPENGINE_IQ_WMMA_TILE_M/N` env overrides
still win (that is the sweep hook).

## Measurements

- End-to-end A/B on the XTX (published protocol, median of 3): prefill
  452.6 -> **479.1 tok/s (+5.9%)**, decode unchanged (19.99 -> 20.09). The
  W4A16 IQ4_XS share is ~26% of prefill and the leaf is 18% faster, so the
  E2E delta matches the estimate.
- The 27B UD model fits in 16.5 GiB on the XTX - full-model runs work on
  GPU1.
- W4A16 crossover re-measured with the new tiles (task 6's answer):
  crossover 8 rows on the wide shapes (1.10-1.15x at 8, was 0.68x with the
  128 tile) and 12 on ffn_down/qkv. `min_rows=8` is now approximately
  correct on gfx1100; keep the shared floor - rows below 8 still lose 2-3x,
  and a per-shape floor for the two narrow shapes at rows 8-11 is not worth
  the second variable.

  gfx1151 hardware here to re-measure, and none was needed - the code path
  keeps the zbook-swept values.
