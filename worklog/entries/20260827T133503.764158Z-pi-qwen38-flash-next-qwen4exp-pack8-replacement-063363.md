# Reject Qwen4Exp pack8 replacement profile

## Summary

Built, measured, rejected, and removed a one-layout Q4 expert gate/up pack8
replacement profile. Its real-tensor microkernel is excellent, but full-model
production numerics fail and uncached repacking is impractical.

## Changes

- Prototyped one-layout planning/materialization for 94 Q4 gate/up pack8
  replacements and composed existing single pack8 selected kernels.
- Removed all experimental planner/materializer/runtime code after rejection.
- Retained compact evidence at
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-27-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-pack8-replacement-rejected.json`.

## Validation

- Actual layer-0 expert gate micro: 471.9→629.1 MB, pack 1.616 s, raw
  0.368 ms vs pack8 0.082 ms (4.47x), sampled BF16 output bits exact.
- Full 94-tensor profile: load/repack 978.87 s, warm p512 18.51 tok/s, warm
  decode 6.27 tok/s, 98.157 GB tracked peak, zero teardown bytes.
- Binding sequential single-pack8 paired decode: 5.497→6.437 tok/s (+17.1%),
  top-1 100%, but mean/p95/p99/max KL
  0.002089/0.006529/0.008669/0.009503. Mean and p95 fail production.

## Next

- Do not retain a replacement profile until exact whole-model arithmetic and a
  persistent cache are both solved. The microkernel indicates layout potential,
  but sampled tensor equality is insufficient for profile promotion.
