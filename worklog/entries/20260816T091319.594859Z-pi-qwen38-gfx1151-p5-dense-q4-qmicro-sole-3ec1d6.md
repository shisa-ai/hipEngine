# Retain sole qmicro Q4 dense FFN ownership

## Summary

Retained sole qmicro-Q4 ownership for the 128 Qwen3.8-27B dense
H5120/N17408 gate/up weights on gfx1151. A materially new direct-metadata
native consumer and a bounded 4K transient expansion repair the operation-
completeness blockers from the prior rejected qmicro attempts. Resident and
tracked peak fall by 178,257,920 bytes (170 MiB) with no sidecar, new workspace,
or teardown ownership. Complete repeated-shape prefill/AR, natural AR, and
native B1 are all positive and exact.

## Changes

- Added the `gguf_q4_k_qmicro_t16_v1` dense singleton, dual-SiLU, rows2-4
  rowtile, direct-WMMA, and expanded-metadata WMMA registry families with
  explicit primitive fallbacks.
- Added gfx1151 role/shape capability for only `layers.*.ffn_gate` and
  `layers.*.ffn_up` at N17408/K5120; gfx1100 and every other role retain their
  prior layout.

  rows2-4 use direct-metadata rowtiles; 512/1K use direct WMMA; 4K expands only
  coefficients into the already-dead FFN gate/up scratch plane before WMMA.
- Added planning, dispatch, materialization, synthetic exactness, and runtime
  route tests. Updated the kernel catalog, campaign plan, benchmark rollup, and
  compact retained-development artifact.
