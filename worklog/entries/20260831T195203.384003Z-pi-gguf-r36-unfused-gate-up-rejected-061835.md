# Reject exact-unfused R36 verifier gate-up

## Summary

Rejected the exact unfused Q4 gate/up+SiLU chain as a replacement for the
current fused R36 target-verifier owner. On actual Qwen3.8 layer-0 weights, the
current fused owner is BF16-bit exact to the strict chain and 1.175x faster by
HIP-event median. No dispatch or runtime change is retained.

## Changes

- Added a strict selector RED in `tests/test_gguf_q4_k_t16_dense.py` before any
  implementation. It failed at collection because no verifier-specific fused
  selector exists, as expected.
- Screened actual `blk.0.ffn_gate.weight` and `blk.0.ffn_up.weight` at
  R36/K5120/N17408 with five warmup pairs and 20 counterbalanced HIP-event
  samples per arm, three operation-complete launches per sample.
- The strict chain is two shared-B singleton Q4T16 WMMAs plus standalone BF16
  SiLU. The incumbent is the fused dual-WMMA+SiLU owner identified by the parent
  C6-C8 profile. Outputs match bit-for-bit with zero mismatches.
- A first complete measurement reported 1.5197 vs 1.2993 ms but exited nonzero
  after measurement because the ad-hoc harness called `weights.close()` instead
  of `weights.free()`. That teardown typo was repaired and the packet rerun.
- The clean rerun reports **1.524657 ms unfused vs 1.297709 ms fused**. Unfused
  is **17.49% slower** (`0.8511x`), and fused wins all 20 sample pairs.
- Removed the unimplemented selector RED after the measured candidate failed;
  the tracked source tree is unchanged. Recorded the rejection in
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-31-w7900-q4km-r36-unfused-gate-up-rejected.json`.
