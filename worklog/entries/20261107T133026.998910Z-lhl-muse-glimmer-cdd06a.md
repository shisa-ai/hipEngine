# Muse Glimmer decode per-shape GEMV bandwidth

## Summary

Measured rows=1 GEMV time per projection shape and computed effective bandwidth
from resident bytes. The large projections are near the read roofline; the
narrow K/V is underfilled but tiny; the T16 layout expands bytes +2.78% vs raw
GGUF. Per-shape bandwidth tuning alone cannot close the +10% decode gap
(Amdahl ~1.06x), confirming the reviewers' calculation.
