## Decision

Native correctness and ownership gates pass, but the candidate is not
non-regressive on the complete request boundary. No production profile or
model-throughput claim follows. Strict, Q4_K_M, other backends and unqualified
shapes retain the parent. The earlier operation-level screen remains useful
mechanism evidence but is not a model-wall certificate.
