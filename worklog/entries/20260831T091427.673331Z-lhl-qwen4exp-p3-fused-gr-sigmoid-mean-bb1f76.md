# Add Qwen4Exp fused GR sigmoid mean candidate

## Summary

Added a strict raw-pointer GR epilogue that performs sigmoid materialization
and branch gated-mean in one launch while preserving both public F32 outputs.
The candidate is bounded to rows <=256: direct gfx1151 HIP-event screening
shows a large c1/small-row win but a 508-row loss, so operation-complete p508
stays on the wider two-kernel chain.

## Changes

- Added and registered `gr_gated_mean_sigmoid` with the separate exact sigmoid
  plus gated-mean chain retained as fallback.
- Added `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_GR_SIGMOID_MEAN_FUSED=1`, fail-closed profile
  binding, <=256-row policy, direct primitive exactness, composition coverage,
  registry coverage, and profile-gate candidate metadata.
- Added the candidate cleanup trigger to `docs/REFACTOR.md` and cataloged the
  primitive in `docs/KERNELS.md`.

## Validation

- RED: the new direct exactness test failed import before the kernel/wrapper
  existed.
- GREEN: 16 focused GR/profile/gate tests pass on gfx1151.
- Direct exactness: fused materialized gate and mixed output are F32 array-exact
  to the two-kernel chain for 2x4x2560 fixture inputs.
- Alternating HIP-event microbench, 100 launches/sample and 10 samples:
  - c1 chain 8.403 us versus fused 2.825 us, 2.974x;
  - rows508 chain 274.749 us versus fused 309.093 us, 0.889x.
- A broader 1/2/4/8/16/32/64/128/256/508 row screen shows the fused owner wins
  every measured bucket through 256 and loses at 508, motivating the explicit
  <=256 guard rather than benchmark-specific prompt logic.
- An all-row diagnostic p508 A/B is exact but aggregate-neutral (1.0013x, 95%
  CI 0.9970–1.0056); it is not promotion evidence and the final policy no
  longer selects the fused owner at that shape.
- Counterbalanced same-process p508 + 128-step AR, five pairs: bound
  9.119 s / 14.037 tok/s versus candidate 8.486 s / 15.083 tok/s, ratio
  1.07464 (95% CI 1.05477–1.09451). All five pairs win and every 129-token
  sequence is exact across routes and repeats.
- The complete T0 exact/state/trace gates remain in progress; the candidate
  stays default-off until they finish.
