# Establish the teacher-forced AR-versus-verification numerics gate

## Summary

Phase 3 of `docs/UD-GFX1151-OPTIMIZE.md`, and the remainder of U6 item 5. The
new gate compares the **production single-row AR route** against the
**production multi-row target verification route** at identical contexts, with
full-vocabulary logits, so verification-specific drift is separated from
quant-route drift and from control/batching bugs.

Both UD presets pass the complete section-6.1 screen with two to three orders of
magnitude of margin, at both a short root (captured native graph) and a
512-token root (eager multi-row verifier):

| Arm | rows | root | verifier path | mean KL | p95 | p99 | max | top-1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| UD-Q4_K_M | 162 | 64 | native graph | 3.45e-05 | 2.03e-04 | 2.56e-04 | 3.48e-04 | 100% |
| UD-Q4_K_M | 162 | 512 | eager multi-row | 3.51e-05 | 2.46e-04 | 4.39e-04 | 7.00e-04 | 100% |
| UD-Q4_K_S | 162 | 64 | native graph | 3.06e-05 | 1.31e-04 | 2.60e-04 | 6.98e-04 | 100% |
| UD-Q4_K_S | 162 | 512 | eager multi-row | 1.03e-05 | 6.65e-05 | 8.27e-05 | 1.07e-04 | 100% |

Binding limits are mean `1e-3`, p95 `5e-3`, p99 `2e-2`, max `5e-2`, top-1
`99%` overall and `97%` per scope. Top-1 is 100% in every category
(`code`, `general_en`, `general_ja`, `mixed_ja_en`) in all four arms. Both
repeats of every arm are bit-identical.

Three findings matter for the rest of the campaign.

**The UD-Q4_K_S generated-ID near-ties are not an arithmetic failure.** At
identical contexts the K_S multi-row verifier reproduces the AR logits to a
maximum row KL of `1.07e-04` with 100% top-1. The two `general_ja_plan`
divergences in the paired production artifact are free-running near-tie drift
under the section-4.1 production profile, not verification arithmetic.

**gfx1100 caps the captured native target graph at position 95.**
`GGUF_SPECDEC2_NATIVE_TARGET_GRAPH_MAX_CONTEXT = 95`, and the context-bucket
predicate rejects any cycle with `root_position + rows > 95`. At a 512-token
root the production caller therefore takes the **eager multi-row verifier**
(`fallback_reason = "target_graph_context_bucket_miss"`), not a serial route.
The 512-token arms measure that path directly; it passes too.

**The residual is the decode-owner difference, and Phase 4 targets it.** The AR
route uses the rows==1 local32 IQ decode owner; the verifier uses the strict
per-row GEMV. The largest logit difference between them is 0.15-0.17 absolute
on a wide-margin distribution, which is what produces the residual KL.
Replacing the verifier owner with the same local32 family (Phase 4 candidate A)
should drive this residual toward zero.

## Changes

- `scripts/ud_mtp_ar_verify_numerics_gate.py` (+ test): the gate. Tokenizes
  each fixture with the model's own chat template, extends the window with the
  model's own continuation, walks the production AR route for
  `max_budget + 1` steps with full logits, then runs one production native
  target block per candidate budget from a fresh prefill and compares each
  verifier row against the AR logits for the same position.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-12-ud-gfx1100-phase3-ar-verify-numerics.json`:
  the four-arm gate artifact with the pooled screen, per-scope and per-budget
  top-1, per-budget KL, the rows above p99, and the coverage gaps.

## Validation

- **Device identity.** Every run set `HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES=1` with
  `ROCR_VISIBLE_DEVICES` unset on physical GPU1 (RX 7900 XTX, gfx1100).
- **Alignment.** The verifier row `j` consumes `ar_tokens[j]` at the root
  position and is compared against `ar_logits[j+1]`, the AR logits for the
  position that consumed the same token. `target_top1` equals the AR greedy
  trajectory in every case.
- **Path coverage.** The 64-token arms report `native_graph = true` with no
  fallback reason; the 512-token arms report `native_graph = false` with
  `target_graph_context_bucket_miss`. Both paths are therefore measured, which
  also satisfies the plan's eager-versus-graph coverage item.
- **Determinism.** Two repeats per arm produce identical per-row KL and top-1
  vectors.
- `python3 -m pytest tests/test_scripts_ud_mtp_ar_verify_numerics_gate.py`:
  7 pass.
- `python3 scripts/worklog.py check`: valid.
