# Trace Gemma attention drift through hidden states and expert ownership

## Summary

On the saved 1,031-token mixed-summary prompt, the residual attention prototype
already changes expert top-k sets for one of the first 512 rows after layer zero.
By layer 29 it changes sets on 239/512 rows and the block's hidden-state relative
L2 difference versus strict reaches 0.1351. The next block ends at 0.1191, and the
seven-row final prefill block at 0.0960. Local attention precision alone therefore
does not prevent model-layer amplification on this chain.

An external causal control forces both selected expert IDs and routing weights
to the strict captures at the corresponding layer/block/teacher step, after the
ordinary router executes. The same residual arm's block-end hidden differences
fall to 0.0320, 0.0334 and 0.0219. Drift still grows with fixed routes, so expert
selection/weight changes are a contributor, not the sole cause. This control is
not a deployable policy or a candidate: using the teacher's expert decisions in
production would violate the anti-gaming contract.

The first three teacher-forced logit rows have mean/max KL 0.000461/0.000944 for
residual attention, versus 0.000188/0.000518 with strict expert ownership. These
three rows cannot resolve category/top-1 gates or replace the earlier full-chain
failure. Independent residual replays across the two diagnostic drivers have
bitwise-identical logits. The strict replay matches the stored strict baseline
bitwise on these three rows.

## Static review

Read-only review of the attention kernels, wrappers and evaluator found no
ordinary finite normalized-input mask/window/GQA/row-offset/selection defect.
Two proposed edge-case reproducers remain unverified: a masked BF16 V exceeding
FP16 range can contaminate sliding WMMA, and the older unselected scalar tiled
experiment may mishandle an initially empty tile. Neither is established as the
cause here, and neither observation clears a production numerical gate. The
review result does not certify the final workspace; no reviewed kernel changed.
