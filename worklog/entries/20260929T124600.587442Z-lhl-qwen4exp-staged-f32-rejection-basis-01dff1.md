# Staged-f32 has no scope that both passes and has value

## Summary

The exact-chain region - the only region where the staged tile can beat anything -
fails at every scope, and **narrowing makes it worse**:

| Scope | Layers | Mean KL | p95 KL | Max KL | Top-1 | Verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0-47 | 48 | 1.269e-3 | 6.663e-3 | 2.925e-2 | 0.98385 | fail |
| 0-15 | 16 | 1.177e-3 | 6.121e-3 | 4.905e-2 | 0.98643 | fail |
| 0-11 | 12 | 1.274e-3 | 6.585e-3 | 4.686e-2 | 0.98643 | fail |
| 0-7 | 8 | **1.354e-3** | 6.613e-3 | 6.988e-2 | 0.98902 | fail |

Engagement confirmed on every scope.

## The finding: the drift is not cumulative over layers

**0-7's mean KL (1.354e-3) exceeds 0-47's (1.269e-3).** Eight layers produce more
whole-model divergence than forty-eight. The drift is therefore concentrated in
the earliest layers rather than accumulated across all of them - and those are
exactly the layers with marginal value, because the wide route starts at 16.

The same non-monotonicity appears in the upper ladder: 8-47's `max_kl`
(6.441e-2) exceeds 0-47's (2.925e-2) while its mean is lower. Two independent
directions of non-monotonic behaviour, which is the strongest single argument
against the summed-KL budgeting the ledger already warns about, and against
extrapolating any scope from a neighbouring scope's numbers.

**I am recording this as an observation, not a mechanism.** I do not have a
verified explanation for why fewer staged layers produce more divergence, and I
am not going to invent one.

## Conclusion so far

**The route has no scope that both passes and has value.** 16-47 passes but is
5-10x slower than the already-promoted wide route on the same shape, so a pass
there buys nothing; every scope where the staged tile actually beats the exact
chain (layers 0-15 and narrower) fails the envelope on mean, p95 and top-1.

One gap remains before that is final: 13-47, 14-47 and 15-47 are untested, and
they would add layers beyond the wide route's 16 boundary. Their best case is
small - three layers, ~0.66% of prefill at the measured per-layer-step saving -
and 12-47 already fails on top-1 (0.98837 against a 0.99 limit), so the numerical
margin at any passing scope in that band is near zero. The boundary ladder is
running to close the gap rather than reject over an untested interval.
