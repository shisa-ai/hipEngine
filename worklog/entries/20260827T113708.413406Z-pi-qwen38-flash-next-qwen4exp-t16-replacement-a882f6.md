# Reject Qwen4Exp T16 replacement profile

## Summary

Built, measured, rejected, and removed a one-layout Q4 expert gate/up T16
replacement profile for Qwen4Exp. It is neutral for optimized prefill, slower
for decode, and fails production mean/p95 KL.

## Changes

- Prototyped opt-in planning/materialization for 94 lossless Q4T16 replacement
  tensors with no duplicate raw owner; composed existing T16 compact WMMA/GEMV.
- Removed all experimental planner/materializer/runtime code after rejection.
- Retained only compact negative evidence at
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-27-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-t16-replacement-rejected.json`.

## Validation

- Corrected capacity audit: raw Q4 gate/up payload is 44.353 GB and T16 is
  45.587 GB, so dual ownership would not fit the measured 262K headroom.
  One-layout replacement would be viable at 83.756 GB device weights.
- Plain T16 profile: 153.68 s load/repack, 26.59 tok/s warm p512, 3.66 tok/s
  warm decode, clean teardown.
- With grouped Q5/Q8 + peer-GDN: 213.52 tok/s p512, neutral versus raw grouped
  211.76; warm diagnostic decode 6.56 tok/s.
- Binding sequential paired decode: raw strict 5.925 tok/s versus T16 3.615
  tok/s; mean/p95/p99/max KL 0.003010/0.008338/0.009863/0.010529, top-1 100%.
  It fails production mean/p95 and regresses paired performance 39%.

## Next

- Do not revisit T16 without a different consumer or measured kernel-level
  mechanism. Focus raw-Q4 metadata/dequant efficiency and exact decode launch
  reduction instead.
