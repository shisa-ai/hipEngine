# Prefill chunk size is the largest queued lever: 1.7-1.9 s with no kernel change

## Summary

The `code-p4096` protocol runs four 1024-token chunks. Both families that
dominate the prefill pay for that chunking, and neither has to:

- **Dense wide Q8_0 route: 1382.2 -> 1057.2 ms (-23.5%, 325 ms), measured.**
  One 4096-row launch of every role costs less than four 1024-row launches for
  identical arithmetic. 2048-row chunks already capture 22.6% of it, so the
  second halving is not worth its scratch.
  [Packet](../../benchmarks/results/2026-09-22-dense-chunk-rows/README.md)
- **Routed-MoE main kernels: 57.7 -> 36.1 ms gate/up and 34.5 -> 21.6 ms down
  per layer, i.e. 1.0 s + 0.6 s of a 16.67 s prefill.** The MoE has a structural
  reason the dense route does not: weight traffic is
  `sum over experts of ceil(rows_in_expert/16) x bytes_per_tile` at a constant
  104-126 GB/s, so four chunks pay 4480 tiles per layer (`grid_y = 1120` each in
  the engine's own capture) where one chunk pays 2801. This side is arithmetic
  on measured per-launch times and the measured traffic model, not an engine
  measurement at chunk 4096.
  [Packet](../../benchmarks/results/2026-09-22-moe-main-kernel-cost/README.md)

Total **1.7-1.9 s of 16.673 s (10-11%)**, which is larger than any kernel
rewrite currently queued and needs no kernel change at all.

## Decision

Ranked ahead of the 32-row M-tile kernel (task #8) and of any MMB rewrite. The
M-tile is still the right second step and composes with this one: at a
4096-token chunk the MoE sees ~80 rows per expert, so a 16-row tile pays 5 tiles
per expert and a 32-row tile 3.

Implementation must be **lane-scoped, not a global `PrefillConfig` change**.
The 1024-token value is shared with other lanes, and the dense H5120
`MOSTLY_Q4_K_M` geometry has a recorded preference for 1024-row chunks over
4096 at capacities above 8K (see the comment on
`_gguf_dense_prefill_scratch_row_cap`'s capacity policy). The knob is
`PrefillConfig.linear_chunk_size` / `moe_chunk_size`; the `qwen4exp_profile_gap.py`
`--prefill-chunk-size` flag mirrors the default rather than overriding it, so
the retained 16.67 s baseline is the shipped default's configuration and not a
harness artifact.

## Validation

- Dense side measured: `scripts/qwen4exp_dense_wide_f16_activation.py --rows
  1024,2048,4096 --repetitions 16 --weight-buffers 8` on gfx1151 (Radeon 8060S,
  Strix Halo, 125 GB). The packet's per-launch times were validated against the
  engine's own role-marked capture to 1.4% in the 2026-09-17 unit, and the
  totals here are its own per-role launch counts re-weighted by chunk count.
- MoE side: per-launch bandwidth from the engine's capture (143 GB/s gate/up at
  `grid_y = 1120`, 160 GB/s down) applied to the tile counts the row sweep
  measured (2801 tiles at 40960 rows, 4480 across four 10240-row launches).
- Not run, and required before promotion: an engine A/B at chunk 4096 with
  digests, a Tier-1 capacity probe for the larger activation scratch, and a
  lane-scoped plan entry.
