# P3: Q5_K grouped rowbatch gate_up owner

## Summary

Punchlist P3's gate_up half is a missing *owner*, not a missing algorithm. Layer 29
carries the artifact's only Q5_K gate_up (`scripts/gemma4_family_census.py:53`), and
while every other layer's gate_up runs the grouped rowbatch leaf —
`gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_grouped_rowbatch8_out4_expertgrid64_bf16_bf16_out`, 6
launches / 66.501 ms in the recorded 1024-token trace — that owner was registered on
the `gguf_q4_k` quant axis only. Resolving Gemma 4's dispatch keys through its own
`_ensure_linear_kernel_registered` path gives YES for `gguf_q4_k` and NO for
`gguf_q5_k` on every variant `gemma4_experts.py` requests, so Q5_K gate_up fell
through to the per-row GEMV.

The cost model was wrong in both directions and was corrected during this unit. The
earlier "49 touch points in a 7408-line `.hip`" figure described porting the MMQ32
leaf — a different and much larger job; Q5_K is not devoid of MMQ (it already
registers `mmq32_q8_1_{d4s4,d8r8s8,d8s8}` and `mmq_i128_j128_k256_q8_1_ds4`), and
`selected_dual_q8_1_ds4_mmq32_prefill_compact32_bf16_bf16_out` appears in neither
the registry inventory nor the punchlist's named target. The real work is one symbol
in one translation unit, because the shared kernel already encodes Q5_K behind its
`Q5K` template parameter.

Two earlier framings were withdrawn earlier in this campaign and are recorded here so
they are not revived: option (i) — registering Q5_K's working `iu8_risk` owner under
the `compact` variant name — is refuted, since `compact` and `iu8_risk` are separate
kernels (10 distinct Python functions, 7 distinct exported HIP symbols in
`gguf_q4_k_selected_prefill.py`); and the claim that gate_up was a mere routing miss
cited `qwen4_exp_profiles.py`, which belongs to Qwen4-Exp on gfx1151 and is never
consumed by Gemma 4.
