# Fold missed RDNA3 tuning lessons into the guide

## Summary

Reviewed `docs/RDNA3-TUNING-GUIDE.md` against both `LESSONS-LEARNED.md` copies,
the hipEngine design and campaign documents, and the commit and worklog history,
then folded the material it did not yet carry into the guide. The first draft
was accurate on kernel and layout method but omitted three whole topics
(speculative-decode economics, the dispatch-floor model, persistent-kernel
evaluation) and stated many rules without the measurement that makes them
actionable. One claim was stale.

The guide is now the entry point for accumulated tuning lessons from both trees;
section 14 points at every document holding the underlying derivation.

## Changes

- Corrected the `gfx1151` queue claim. `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=1` is documented risk
  reduction for the open long-prefill stall (`ROCm/ROCm#6437`), not a fix; the
  stall reproduces with one queue, with SDMA disabled, and under HIP 7.13 and
  7.15. Added the hazard to the `gfx1151` cautions.
- Added `6.7` speculative decode and verification economics: the `eta`
  definition and bands, break-even inequality, the `C_B` cycle formulation, why
  high-cardinality MoE with sequential state loses while dense targets win, the
  three ledgers, and the short-cell-versus-full-suite diagnostic.
- Added `7.10` dispatch floor: the measured per-launch model (~5.6 us plus a
  grid term, argument-count independent), HIP graph replay near-neutrality
  against CUDA's 1-2 us per node, why dispatch is absent from `--kernel-trace`,
  the launch-budget calculation, and per-path launch census.
- Added `7.11` persistent kernels: `grid.sync()` at ~1 us, the sub-cache versus
  external-memory decision table, the fresh-slice correction to the
  cache-resident microbenchmark, the corrected reading of low bandwidth
  utilization, and the absence of HIP dynamic parallelism.
- Added `4.7` work-volume invariants (non-finite logits, route diversity, unique
  tokens, fallback share) and the top-1-does-not-certify-calibration case.
- Added `4.8` evaluator separation, discovery/qualification/confirmation sets,
  and the rule against repairing scorer and candidate in one iteration.
- Added `5.13` vendor libraries as banded candidates, with the rocBLAS versus
  hipBLASLt and AOTriton crossover evidence.
- Added step 8 stop and pivot rules; extended step 1 with maximum-prize framing
  and the Amdahl check; extended step 3 with block-parallelism, loop-length, and
  synchronization-density questions.
- Sharpened existing rules with their measurements: VGPR/waves occupancy ladder
  and machine-fill point, vec8 unroll heuristic, `gfx1100` dot-instruction
  availability, INT8 not being a 2x FP16 compute path, no native FP8 on `gfx11`,
  VOPD encoding limits, dead-lane workgroup sizing, arithmetic-intensity
  thresholds and the `2M/bpw` rowtile formula, ROCr scratch reserve, ACE overlap
  ceiling, byte-ledger bandwidth substitute for unusable `MemUnitBusy`, power
  envelopes as separate lanes, launch-bounds knob validation, fusion pricing
  against the dispatch floor, and the online-softmax fixture requirement.
- Recorded that grid dimensions do not control block scheduling order on RDNA3,
  which is why expert-sequential reshaping is structurally unavailable.
- Added rejected-shortcut rows for pre-filled microbenchmarks, `blockIdx`
  reordering for locality, and unverified fast rows; separated the one Vulkan
  comparison (serialized tiny-dispatch command-buffer replay) that did transfer.
- Expanded the implementation map and further reading to cover
  `SPECULATIVE-DECODE.md`, `MTP.md`, `DFLASH.md`, `MEGAKERNEL.md`,
  `PROCESS-IMPROVEMENT.md`, `PROCESS-EXPLORATION.md`, `CONCURRENCY2.md`,
  `CONCURRENCY.md`, `GFX1151-TUNING-LANDSCAPE.md`, `TUNING-gfx1151.md`,
  `TUNING-gguf.md`, `GGUF_DECODE_REPACK.md`, `LLAMACPP-HIP-PARITY.md`,
  `HIP-vs-VULKAN.md`, `DEBUG-GFX1151-STALL.md`, `QUANTS.md`, `KVCACHE.md`,
  `OOM.md`, and `ENVS.md`.
