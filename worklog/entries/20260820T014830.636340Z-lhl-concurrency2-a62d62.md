# Correct the width-adaptive GEMM section: FFN is not the c>4 bottleneck

## Summary

Review of the `CONCURRENCY2.md` "GEMM roofline and width-based dispatch model"
section added in 741f2d577 found the premise and both load-bearing conclusions
wrong. Section rewritten and renamed to **"Width-adaptive GEMM selection:
measured model and dispatch policy"** (the pointer in worklog entry
20260820T014009 refers to the old title). No code changed; this is a decision +
docs unit that re-orders the c>8 plan.

The headline correction: **the FFN GEMM is not the dominant packed-decode cost
above c4.** Marginal step cost is 3.3 ms/row over c1->c4 but 17.1 ms/row over
c4->c8, while the measured marginal FFN GEMM cost is ~1.8 ms/row throughout
(rowtile slope 0.0103 ms/row/tile x 3 tiles x 64 layers). Subtracting the
measured per-call FFN kernel times from the measured step times gives a non-FFN
cost that is **flat at ~20-23 ms for c1-c4 and then jumps to ~53-57 ms at c5-c6
and ~84 ms at c7-c8** — a cliff, not a roofline slope. ~90% of the c4->c8
growth is outside the FFN GEMM. This restores the ranking that entry
20260819T181510 had already reached ("GDN/attention is the next bottleneck")
and which the doc section had inverted.

Second correction: the section's central strategy table priced an 8-row rowtile
group at the M=2 efficiency (64%) when the measured 8-row group runs at ~41%.
Redone against measured per-call times, rowtile multi-group beats the wmma
prefill by 1.56x at M=16 and **loses by 1.15x at M=32** — the claimed "beats it
at every M>8" is false, and the real crossover is M~24-32.

Third: "the wmma prefill is latency/occupancy-bound at 11% of peak BW" names
the wrong mechanism. `ROW_TILES_PER_BLOCK = 4` with `grid.y = ceil(rows/64)`
(`gguf_k_t16_selected_prefill.hip:533-537,1686-1687`) and a `valid_row` guard
that suppresses only the load means the kernel **executes 64 rows of MAC work
at every M in 1..64**. At M=16 its executed rate is 27.3 TFLOP/s ~= 32% of the
measured matrix roof. It is row-padding bound first and parallelism bound
second (363 blocks of one wave for gate/up; **107 blocks for the down shape**,
~1 wave per CU, ~1.0 ms/call). The fixes are a template parameter and a grid
change, not a new kernel.
