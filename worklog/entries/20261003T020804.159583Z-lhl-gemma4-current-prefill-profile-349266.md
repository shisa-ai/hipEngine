# Current Gemma prefill profile still reaches 256 VGPRs without spills

## Summary

Current compiled full-attention prefill still uses 256 architectural VGPRs, but
its private segment is now zero. The decode shape uses 240 VGPRs and zero
private bytes. The combine kernel uses 192 VGPRs and 60 private bytes. These are
fresh `rocpd_info_kernel_symbol` measurements of the shipped compiled objects,
not an assumption based on the halved source accumulator.

The Radeon 8060S agent reports 40 CUs and 80 SIMDs, not the 96 CUs used in
some earlier worklog reasoning. That error invalidates the old normalized
issue-capacity estimates; it does not change measured kernel wall times.

On `scripts/gemma4_prefill_kernel_bw.py`, one full layer, 65,536 keys, all-ones
mask, unprofiled medians of three: decode 1.364 ms and 256-row prefill 59.362 ms.
The latter launches 128 blocks of 256 threads, with 58,112 dynamic LDS bytes per
block. `Grid_Size=32768` in the profiler is total threads, not blocks.

Fresh prefill fetch counters report 708.18/708.22 MB in separate dispatches,
about 10.68/10.79 GB/s at their profiled durations, while the separate L2 hit
counter reports 74.57/74.63%. The decode fetch is about 268.61 MB, agreeing with
its known unique K/V band. Low prefill DRAM rate is not a 25x speedup guarantee;
cache/shared-memory reuse changes the applicable roofline regime.

Instruction-counter shares for prefill are VALU 56.70%, LDS 21.94%, SALU
15.76%, branch 4.55%, FLAT 1.05%. These are counts, not execution-time shares.
Do not compare their raw totals with older profiler groups without calibration.

## Counter reliability

The occupancy group reports prefill MeanOccupancyPerCU 6.59/6.49 and
OccupancyPercent 20.61/20.29. Those are raw diagnostic readings, not validated
efficiency. Its decode reading gives 24.43 waves/CU on a 40-CU part when the
whole dispatch only launches 128 waves: that is impossible. ALUStalledByLDS
also reaches 115.51% despite the metric's documented 0-100 range. The derived
occupancy/stall normalization needs independent calibration before it can
support a bottleneck claim. The prefill LDSBankConflict reading is 10.31 in
both dispatches, but causal time savings remain unmeasured.

FETCH_SIZE, WRITE_SIZE and L2CacheHit could not be collected together: ROCm
aborted with hardware counter-configuration error 38. FETCH_SIZE and L2CacheHit
were then collected successfully in separate runs. No runtime/kernel failure
was observed in the repaired profiler runs.

## Numerical localization

Replaying four saved 64-row strict-teacher chains with full attention built
from `595f67e26^` (`ed73a7935190acad71b8e33807cc67a08efbf208`) confirms that
failures predate partial-score sharing. The parent full kernel reaches max KL
0.310697 on `heldout_mixed_summary`; strict full plus WMMA sliding reaches
0.318726; strict sliding plus WMMA full reaches 0.375813. Neither attention half
alone is sufficient to explain or repair the failure. The parent versus current
full-kernel arm also has substantial model-logit differences, so the latest
change is not assumed harmless on sensitive inputs. This four-case localization
is diagnostic, not a replacement for the full 18-case gate.
