### Graph-era ladder closure

- The final past-1 graph profile has 103 kernels, **0.791-ms** aggregate, **1.089-ms** first-to-last span, and one host graph launch. Treating the full 0.297-ms span difference as removable gives a deliberately generous **2.915-us/node** bound; real unprofiled event remains 0.868 ms.
- The first MLP rung is 16 -> 8 calls over **0.235 ms** of kernels and can remove at most **23.3 us / 2.8%** before one mandatory cooperative grid barrier. Its 1,664 local32 output waves then 416 local64 blocks stream **4.161 MB/layer**; a fused resident grid must preserve mixed exact reductions and inactive participants under the hardware's 40 CUs / wave32 / 32-wave-per-CU ceiling. This does not clear admission against the open tied-LM W8A16 bandwidth lane.
- Self and cross composites have generous gap ceilings **70.0 us / 8.8%** and **46.6 us / 5.7%**, but each needs at least two globally visible transitions between incompatible wide projection grids and eight-head attention grids while retaining all FP16 weight traffic. They are not admitted. Layer and persistent-token kernels depend on those rejected rungs; the graph path is already one host launch and the approximately 80.6-MB active weight stream cannot stay on chip.
- No megakernel prototype is coded. This follows the approved pre-code admission and Amdahl redirect rules rather than producing an expected losing kernel. Retain the exact residual/LayerNorm, MLP, RoPE/cache composites, graph route, and primitive/eager fallbacks.
