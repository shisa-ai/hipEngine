# Open Qwen3.8 gfx1151 scaling campaign

## Summary

Open `docs/QWEN38-GFX1151-SCALING-CAMPAIGN.md` as the successor to the closed
external-parity campaign. It targets the two subsystems the final six-engine
matrix left open: MTP concurrency scaling and the two prefill cells that hold
60% of the prefill deficit. AR is explicitly out of scope; it leads C3-C8.

## Decision

Scope the campaign to MTP scaling first, prefill second, and only two prefill
cells. Rationale from the frozen 2026-08-30 matrix:

- MTP saturates at ~29.5 tok/s from C3 upward (29.468/29.385/18.657/28.195/
  29.305/28.577) while AR climbs 23.659 -> 45.936. Normalized to C1, AR reaches
  4.17x and MTP stops at 3.66x-3.77x from C3 on. Every external engine's MTP
  keeps rising with width.
- Prefill C2 and C8 hold 32.4% and 27.5% of the absolute deficit; the other six
  widths are within 4.3-9.5% and do not justify a kernel campaign.

## Attribution (new, from current source + retained telemetry)

- **A1 per-row verify cost:** a C8 subgroup cycle is 688.1 ms for R16 (~43 ms
  per target row) versus ~21.8 ms per row for the matched AR cycle. A wide
  verify should cost *less* per row than AR decode, not ~2x more. This is the
  multi-row amortization wall the parity campaign named as the shared
  MTP/DFlash2 suspect, now with a number.
- **A2 width-4 partition:** `engine_loop.py:2336`
  `_maybe_run_partitioned_speculative_decode` runs one complete
  proposal+verify cycle per subgroup, and the width bound is a literal `4` in
  `qwen35_gguf_mtp2.py:871/894/934` plus `partition_max_requests` at `:905`,
  with `GGUF_SPECDEC2_MTP2_C4` capping the capability. Telemetry confirms
  subgroup shapes `4`, `4+1`, `4+2`, `4+3`, `4+4`. C5 is the matrix's worst
  cell (0.5249x AR) because `4+1` is the most unbalanced split.
- **B C1 coverage:** production C1 is 0.7086x AR while our own strict C1/K3 is
  1.6445x. C1 is absent from both policy tables that produced the C3 wins:
  `GGUF_SPECDEC2_PHYSICAL_PROMPT_STREAMING_POLICIES` admits `(2, 3)` only
  (E1a was +27.06% at C3) and `GGUF_SPECDEC2_PROPOSAL_LM_HEAD_ROWTILE_POLICIES`
  admits rows 2/3/4 only (E1b was +7.47% at C3). This is coverage, not
  capability.
- **C Q4 verify ceiling:** `GGUF_T16_TARGET_VERIFIER_PRODUCTION_Q4_ROWTILE_ROWS`
  is `{6, 8, 9, 12}`; R16 exists only for Q5/Q6. Q4 is 29.97% of C4 target
  kernel time and 740 ms at C2. Widening the group without a Q4 owner above R12
  moves the wall rather than removing it.
- **D prefill C2:** hipEngine prefill *falls* C1 -> C2 (146.758 -> 142.806)
  while all five external engines rise 25-42%. That is a grouping/dispatch
  signature at width 2, distinct from the C8 high-row Q4 blocker, and it was
  never independently attributed.
- **E stale trace:** the recorded 360.56 ms Q4 prefill family figure predates
  P2.3's low-VGPR work, which roughly doubled server prefill. No post-P2.3
  prefill kernel trace exists; prefill kernel work must re-trace first.
- **F MMQ continuation:** `GGUF-PREFILL-OPTIMIZATION.md` item 19 recorded an
  explicit continuation condition for integer-MMQ prefill that was never
  attempted, and `kernels/hip_gfx1151/` holds no `.hip` files. Sized down as
  conditional: on gfx1151 INT8 WMMA is 59.4 TOP/s, the same rate as BF16, so
  the payoff is dequant-ALU removal, not a matmul-rate win.
