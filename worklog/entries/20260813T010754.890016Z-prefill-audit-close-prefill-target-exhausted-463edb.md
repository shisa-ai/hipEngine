# Close Qwen3.6 prefill target as exhausted

## Summary

Closed the requested Qwen3.6-27B 512/1K/4K prefill optimization objective as
**not met under the no-regression, single-layout, byte-neutral constraints**.
The retained Q6 and Q4 FFN-down absolute matrix improves the pre-ladder package
10.08%/9.44%/8.73%; the later narrow K/V and attention-output routes add small
paired wins but do not supply a new independent topline. Even compounding those
paired ratios leaves hipEngine 16.36%/11.97%/10.37% below clean llama.cpp HIP.

## Changes

- Added a compact exhaustion artifact that reconciles the cumulative engine
  result, final category quality, neutral decode and residency, current M512
  profile, all retained/rejected candidate lanes, and explicit reopen rules.
- Updated the benchmark rollup/changelog and XTX campaign scorecard. The public
  absolute matrix stays at 801.326/859.484/847.283 tok/s because independent
  load/thermal drift exceeds the later sub-percent A/B increments; their
  806.751/863.593/848.582 tok/s compounded estimate is labeled non-topline.
- Recorded that source-shaped Q4/Q5 integer MMQ lose, FFN gate/up and wide Q/QKV
  are non-uniform, wider FFN-down tiles add 61-346 MiB, GDN lacks a matched
  comparator deficit, and residual families lack enough isolated ceiling.

- Conservative decode changed only +0.042%/-0.067%/-0.014% at 512/1K/4K;
  tracked peaks remain exactly 15.605/15.720/16.368 GiB and MTP dispatch is
  unchanged.
