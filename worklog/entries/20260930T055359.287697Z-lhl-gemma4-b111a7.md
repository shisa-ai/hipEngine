# x7 build: bucket-keyed decode capture module with bitwise replay proof

## Summary

Build phase of X7 per the design entry (20260930T052009.020087Z,
`1c3626`): the Gemma 4 decode forward body split into three
stream-parameterized phases so a capture stream can drive it, the ctypes
surface for captured memcpy nodes in `core/hip.py`, and the new
`runtime/gemma4_decode_graph.py` implementing bucket-keyed single-step
capture + replay with the qwen-module keying discipline. The design's RED
— replayed output bitwise-equals the launched path across consecutive
positions — is green, including the singleton tiled bucket at 127 and the
128 bucket-grid boundary.

## Changes

- `hipengine/runtime/gemma4.py`: `_forward_block_inner` split into
  `_stage_block_content` / `_launch_block` / `_collect_block`, all
  taking an explicit `stream`; `_stage_upload(..., stream=...)` for the
  caller-owned staging path. Non-graph behavior is unchanged (live
  equivalence suite green).
- `hipengine/core/hip.py`: `HipPos` / `HipPitchedPtr` / `HipExtent` /
  `HipMemcpy3DParms` layouts plus `graph_memcpy_node_params` and
  `graph_exec_memcpy_node_set_1d`, the memcpy-node surface the captured
  tail zeroing needs.
- `hipengine/runtime/gemma4_decode_graph.py` (new): `capture_gemma4_decode_step`
  + replay with full state-identity keying, bucket keying with
  `KEY_TILE = 128` singleton/tiled-bucket rules, and the padded keep-mask
  as the semantic boundary (masked columns past the live position carry
  zeros into masked softmax lanes).
- `tests/test_gpu_gemma4_decode_graph_capture.py` (new): the RED —
  capture one decode step, replay it, assert bitwise equality to the
  launched path across ≥2 consecutive positions, then again through the
  127 singleton and the 128 boundary.
- `tests/test_unit_gemma4_decode_graph_bucket.py` (new): bucket-math
  unit pinning the route-cut contract (no bucket range may straddle a
  128 multiple except as a singleton).
