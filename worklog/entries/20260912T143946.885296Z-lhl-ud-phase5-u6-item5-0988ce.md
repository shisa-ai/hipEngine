# UD Phase 5: qualify U6 item 5 on the post-Phase-4 6.1 gate

## Summary

U6 item 5 (`exact_ar_mtp_control_behavior`) is now qualified on both UD
records, so the U6 unit is down to one open item: the declared
backend/profile/context/width envelope.

The item was blocked because the only control evidence on file was
free-running generation, and `docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md` 6 makes
free-running generated-ID equality recorded-but-not-the-denominator. The
teacher-forced 6.1 calibrated gate that supplies the missing evidence already
existed as `2026-09-12-ud-gfx1100-phase4-ar-verify-numerics.json`, but the
certification table was never updated to point at it. Re-running the gate on
the post-Phase-4 tree is what unblocks the item; no source changed.

**The re-run is a reproduction, not a new measurement.** All sixteen numbers
(mean/p95/p99/max for all four arms) are identical to the Phase 4 artifact's
`after` values to the last digit, so what this run establishes is that the
gate is deterministic across fresh processes. Reporting it as new evidence
would be wrong.

Physical GPU1 (RX 7900 XTX, gfx1100), c1, budgets B1-B3, ten prompts across
`code`/`general_en`/`general_ja`/`mixed_ja_en` plus the GDN category heldouts,
two fresh-process repeats, 162 pooled rows per repeat, at the short and
512-token prompt points:

| Arm | Verifier route | mean KL | p95 | p99 | max | top-1 |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `ud_q4_k_m` short | native graph | 3.499e-05 | 1.696e-04 | 2.281e-04 | 2.999e-04 | 1.0000 |
| `ud_q4_k_s` short | native graph | 4.231e-05 | 2.637e-04 | 3.764e-04 | 3.764e-04 | 1.0000 |
| `ud_q4_k_m` 512 | eager multi-row | 1.824e-05 | 8.366e-05 | 2.674e-04 | 4.341e-04 | 1.0000 |
| `ud_q4_k_s` 512 | eager multi-row | 1.502e-05 | 9.615e-05 | 1.955e-04 | 2.935e-04 | 1.0000 |

Every arm is top-1 1.0000 in every category and at every budget, and repeats
are deterministic. The worst row-mean KL is 4.231e-05 against the 1e-03
envelope, a 23.6x margin; the worst row-max is 4.341e-04 against 5e-02.

Two of the four arms are *better* than their Phase 3 counterparts on the
post-Phase-4 tree (K_M 512 mean 3.310e-05 -> 1.824e-05, p95 2.451e-04 ->
8.366e-05). The residual drift did not collapse, which matches the Phase 4
post-rows attribution: what is left is the t16 accumulation order, not the
rows 2-4 IQ owner.

  `qualified=not control_blocker` instead of a hardcoded `False`, so the
  per-artifact `control_blocker` argument is the single switch. Both records

  and a qualified item must not keep one.

  `benchmarks/results/*.json` path in any item's evidence.
  `test_u6_certificate_is_incomplete_while_the_scope_item_is_open` pins the
  remaining state.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-12-ud-gfx1100-phase5-ar-verify-numerics.json`:
  the four-arm gate reproduction with the host identity, the exact command, the
  envelope verdict, and the provenance note that HEAD's kernel/quant/runtime
  sources are byte-identical to the retained Phase 4 kernel commit.
