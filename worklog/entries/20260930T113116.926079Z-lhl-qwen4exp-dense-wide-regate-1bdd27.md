## The new finding: the false-pass detector is blind to this route

The gate reports `candidate_route_engaged: False` and `teacher_route_engaged:
False` with no selector differing between arms. **That is a coverage artifact,
not evidence about the route.**

`wmma_prefill_dispatch_recorder` (`hipengine/runtime/gguf_linear.py`) records
exactly two things: the `wmma_prefill` selector via
`_record_wmma_prefill_dispatch`, and the collateral selectors `use_wmma` moves
(`q4k_rowtile`, `native_batch_decode_session`) via `_record_selector`. **The
`dense_wide` route has no recorder**, so its selector never appears in
`selector_states` and the engagement check cannot fire for it.

So the mechanism added in `056e6fbd8` - the one that caught the WMMA false pass -
**covers the retired route and is blind to the live production default at 16-47.**
The gate has a row floor for `dense_wide` (`ROUTE_ROW_FLOORS["dense_wide"]`) but
no dispatch recorder, so the floor is never fed.

Recorded in `docs/REFACTOR.md` with the removal condition (add the recorder,
re-run, confirm the selector appears engaged).

## What this does and does not establish

- **Does establish:** the live default's certificate is stale in both hashes; the
  full envelope at HEAD passes every hard gate with >2x headroom; the two routes
  remain byte-identical in candidate logits at HEAD.
- **Does not establish:** that the `dense_wide` route ran in this gate. The
  tracker cannot answer it. The drift is real and the envelope passes, but
  **attribution to `dense_wide` rests on the documented byte-identity with the
  `wmma` route** (which does have a recorder), not on direct evidence.
- **Not measured:** a `dense_wide` run with a working recorder.
