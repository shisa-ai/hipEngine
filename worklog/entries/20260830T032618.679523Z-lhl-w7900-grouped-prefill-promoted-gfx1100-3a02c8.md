# W7900 Qwen3.8: grouped prefill promoted on gfx1100 after same-suite content-exact win

## Summary

`GGUF_C2_PACKED_PREFILL_MAX_ROWS = 8` is declared for gfx1100, so the resident AR loop
groups a wave into one native prefill call by default. On the canonical suite this raised
C8 AR **45.68 -> 78.67 tok/s (+72.2%)**, flattened draft acceptance from 0.467-0.614 at
C2-C8 to **0.789 at every width**, and kept every generated id identical. hipEngine now
leads llama.cpp current at **7 of 8 AR widths** (was 4 of 8); C2 is the remaining loss.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/__init__.py`: declare the capability (8) with provenance
  and export it, mirroring gfx1151. Registry-native: the value is read by
  `backend_package_capability`, so no backend/quant branch was added anywhere.
- The `..._capability_is_undeclared_by_default` tripwire is inverted to
  `..._capability_is_declared`, keeping its protective half (lookup default stays 1, so an
  unqualified package remains lane-by-lane). Same unit as the earlier partial-grouping fix
  and the `native_full_prefill_groups` counter.
- `docs/REFACTOR.md`: the "what still stands" paragraph becomes "promoted", with the
  remove-only-if condition and the known limit (`chunk == row.prompt_ids`, so prompts
  longer than the chunk cap still prefill serially and gain nothing).

Width 1 is the control on itself: nothing to group, and both arms move by less than the
session band with acceptance identical. Grouping is directly observed, not inferred from
the wall: `native_full_prefill_groups` = 10 at C4 and C8, 0 at C1, with the capability
echo showing `GGUF_C2_PACKED_PREFILL_MAX_ROWS previous=None applied=8`.

The pre-registered prediction in `20260830T030803...-7fe388` is confirmed in direction on
all eight widths and **conservative in magnitude**: predicted 22.1/32.3/41.4/53.7/56.9/
62.1/68.2/68.9 against measured 22.0/31.9/45.3/54.2/61.9/71.2/74.9/78.7, i.e. -0.5% to
+14.7%, with the C6/C7/C8 flips and the surviving C2 loss exactly as stated.

Two of my own hypotheses are falsified by measurement, not by re-reading:
- `serial_exact` (verify path bypassed, exact serial verification, no grouping) changes
  acceptance by **exactly +0.000 at C1/C2/C4/C8**. The packed verify path is innocent.
- The eager-verify/graph hypothesis in `20260830T030945...-50b888` explained nothing; the
  width-dependent acceptance loss was the ungrouped prefill schedule. Mechanism for why
  grouping restores acceptance is **inferred, not measured**: grouping aligns lane
  context lengths within a wave, so cycles stop running over skewed/padded per-lane state.
  The measured facts are the flattened acceptance and the near-vanishing MTP c1-route
  steps (13-28 before, 1-2 after); the padding story is not itself gated.

Framing correction: "C8 admission wall 23.207 -> 9.030 s" is a **sum over the 10 cells** of
the one-token packet (per-cell 2.321 -> 1.156 s). The ratio 2.57x is what transfers; the
D24 C8 whole-cell wall is only ~2.4 s, so the two protocols measure different quantities.
Also unconfirmed: the earlier "ceiling" packet (per-row verify cost ~160 ms/row inherent)
is gone from disk, and its `--candidate-budget 8` would have been clamped by
`_gguf_mtp_server_candidate_budget` to the default 3, so that conclusion is withdrawn
pending a sweep over valid budgets 1..4.
