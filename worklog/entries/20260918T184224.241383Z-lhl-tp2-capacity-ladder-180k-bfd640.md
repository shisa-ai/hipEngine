# TP2 capacity ladder: 180K context fits, the 24 GiB XTX rank is the bound

## Summary

With the rank MLP residency fix in place, the TP2 route was laddered with the
Tier-1 allocation probe (`scripts/tp2_capacity_probe.py`, short prompt, finite
logits, real session build) at the retained 0.44/0.56 split. The route fits
**180224 context and OOMs at 196608**, and the binding rank is the 24 GiB RX 7900
XTX, not the 45 GiB W7900.

| declared context | rank 0 (W7900) | rank 1 (XTX, 24 GiB) | free on XTX | status |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 8192 | 11.994 GiB | 13.182 GiB | 10.82 GiB | pass |
| 32768 | 13.510 GiB | 14.697 GiB | 9.31 GiB | pass |
| 65536 | 15.527 GiB | 16.715 GiB | 7.29 GiB | pass |
| 131072 | 19.564 GiB | 20.752 GiB | 3.25 GiB | pass |
| 147456 | 20.574 GiB | 21.762 GiB | 2.24 GiB | pass |
| 163840 | 21.584 GiB | 22.771 GiB | 1.23 GiB | pass |
| **180224** | **22.594 GiB** | **23.781 GiB** | **0.22 GiB** | **pass** |
| 196608 | — | — | — | **OOM** |
| 262144 (model max) | — | — | — | **OOM** |

The per-rank scaling is ~61.6 KiB per context token on the XTX rank
((23.781 − 13.182) GiB / (180224 − 8192)), which is the KV pool plus its scales
and metadata tables for the rank's share of the heads. The same slope is visible
across the whole ladder: 13.182 → 14.697 → 16.715 → 20.752 → 23.781 GiB at
8192 → 32768 → 65536 → 131072 → 180224.

**This is a 6.4x capacity gain from the residency fix.** Before it, each rank held
22.402 GiB at a 2048 context and the XTX rank had 1.58 GiB free; at the measured
~61.6 KiB/token that puts the pre-fix bound near **28K context**. The same cards
now reach **180K**, which is the practical ceiling set by the XTX rank's 24 GiB
rather than by the model (262144 declared) or the W7900.
The even split is not laddered here, but it uses the same bytes per rank as
0.44/0.56 within a few hundred MiB (12.402 GiB at 2048 for both ranks against
11.650/12.840), so its bound is within a ladder step of this one.

**Capacity, before and after the residency fix.** The pre-fix bound is derived,
not measured: 22.402 GiB per rank at a 2048 context with 1.58 GiB free on the
XTX, at the measured ~61.6 KiB/token, gives roughly **28K context**. The same two
cards now reach **180K**, a 6.4x gain, and the ceiling is the XTX rank's 24 GiB
rather than the model (262144 declared) or the 45 GiB W7900.

**Split-direction note.** The 0.44/0.56 split deliberately gives the *larger*
shard to the XTX rank because that rank streams faster per byte; the consequence
is that the XTX is also the rank that runs out of memory first. Reversing the
split would trade decode throughput for context headroom, which is a deliberate
product choice rather than an oversight, and it is recorded here so the trade is
visible.

## Changes

- `scripts/tp2_capacity_probe.py` — the probe reported
  `memory_stats()["current_allocated_bytes"]` as `resident_gib` per rank, but that
  counter is process-wide (both ranks reported the same value at every point), so
  the field was mislabeled and could not answer a per-rank fitting question. It
  now reports `vram_used_gib` from `hipMemGetInfo` minus that rank's pre-build
  baseline (the device truth, validated device-scoped earlier this session) and
  keeps the tracked counter under an explicit
  `tracked_allocated_gib_process_wide` name.
- Artifacts: `2026-09-18-w7900-tp2-capacity-{8192,32768,65536,131072,147456,163840,180224,196608,262144}-044.json`.

## Validation

- Protocol: `benchmarks/HARNESSES.md` Tier 1 — session at the target declared
  context, one short prompt, a few decode transitions, finite-logit check, no
  full-prompt run. The allocation decision is fully determined at session build,
  so no Tier-2 point was spent to answer a fitting question.
- Every passing point reports `finite_logits: true` and a nonzero decode row
  count; the two failing points fail at build with `HIP error 2: out of memory`
  and record `status: oom` rather than a partial pass.
- `python3 -m pytest tests/test_unit_scripts_tp2_capacity_probe.py -q` → 8 passed
  after the memory-reporting change.
- Per-rank numbers are device deltas, not process-wide counters, and the
  XTX rank's free space is computed against its 23.984 GiB total.

## Next

- The XTX rank is now the pacing constraint for context, not the W7900. If more
  context is wanted, the levers are a reversed split (throughput for headroom),
  a smaller per-rank KV dtype for the bulk pool, or accepting the 180K ceiling.
- TP2 prefill remains the dominant throughput gap (39-43 tok/s against the
  single-card bulk route's ~876); the rank-local bulk prefill plan is the next
  substantial implementation unit.
- Decide whether 0.44/0.56 becomes the default TP2 split.
