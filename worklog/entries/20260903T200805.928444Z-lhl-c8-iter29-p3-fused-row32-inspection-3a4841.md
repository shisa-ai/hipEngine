# C8 iter29: P3 fused row32 inspection — VGPR pressure binds at 2 active waves

## Summary

Completed the open C8-P3 inspection item ("whether LDS capacity, VGPR
pressure, barriers, weight decode, or WMMA issue is binding; do not assume
occupancy from resource counts alone") for the fused Q4 gate/up+SiLU row32
owner via offline ELF/ISA audit plus the already-measured profile artifact —
the established pattern, since rocprofv3 PMC counters remain broken on this
stack (iteration 9).

## Findings

- ELF audit (`hipcc -S`, gfx1100): the row32 instantiation
  `<false,false,1,2>` of `gguf_q4_t16_dense_dual_wmma_prefill_silu_bf16_kernel`
  allocates **next_free_vgpr = 241** (the campaign table's 248 is the
  rocprof-aligned figure), SGPR 28, fixed LDS 32,768 B.
- Occupancy: 241 VGPRs/wave × 64 lanes = 15,424 of 16,384 per SIMD →
  1 wave/SIMD; the 128-thread block (4 waves) spans 4 SIMDs → **1 block/CU,
  2 active waves of 32 slots = 6.25% occupancy**. LDS would allow 4 blocks/CU
  (128 KB / 32 KiB) — VGPR pressure binds, not LDS.
- Instruction mix (1,583 disassembled instructions): 3 barriers, 8 WMMA,
  66 LDS, 87 VMEM, 1,100 vector ALU — Q4 T16 decode dominates; barriers and
  WMMA issue are not binding.
- Achieved bandwidth: 104.50 MB/call / 0.5499 ms/call = **190 GB/s**
  (weights-only 187) vs the 864 GB/s theoretical peak — not
  bandwidth-saturated.
- Cross-check against the measured sibling record: row48/row64 owners at
  3–4 active waves measured 1.36–1.61x faster per row on actual weights
  (KERNELS.md), independently confirming occupancy/latency-hiding as the
  limiter.
- Live screen axis (pre-authorized in the item): a lower-VGPR sibling via
  the instruction-layout axis. The accumulator floor is 32 VGPRs
  (acc_a[2][1] + acc_b[2][1] float8); the ~209 remaining VGPRs are Q4 decode
  temporaries and pointers, so the candidate must narrow decode state —
  not reschedule loads (the iteration-15 finding for the grouped kernels
  applies here too).
