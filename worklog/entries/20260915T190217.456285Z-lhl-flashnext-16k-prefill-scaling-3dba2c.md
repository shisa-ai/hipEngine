# Admitted 16K prefill is linear; QSA is the only superlinear family

## Summary

The admitted 16K fixture case now has a production-default capture, and it closes
two review items at once: the long-context profile the gap attribution needed,
and a measured answer to how the prefill scales with depth.

**The prefill is linear in token count.** 4096 tokens take 23225 ms and 16384
tokens take 92697 ms — a ratio of **3.99** for a 4x token increase. Every family
scales at 3.96-3.99 except one.

| family | 4K ms | 16K ms | ratio | 4K share | 16K share |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| linear | 10726.8 | 42532.6 | 3.97 | 47.5% | 47.2% |
| moe | 6744.2 | 26679.4 | 3.96 | 29.9% | 29.6% |
| gr_read | 2654.8 | 10555.5 | 3.98 | 11.8% | 11.7% |
| **qsa_prefill** | 1429.2 | 6330.7 | **4.43** | 6.3% | **7.0%** |
| gdn | 890.9 | 3531.9 | 3.96 | 3.9% | 3.9% |
| prefill_boundary | 99.1 | 393.7 | 3.97 | 0.4% | 0.4% |
| ple | 16.0 | 66.0 | 4.11 | 0.1% | 0.1% |
| **TOTAL** | 22561.1 | 90089.7 | **3.99** | | |

`qsa_prefill` is the only family that grows faster than the token count. Linear
scaling would put it at 5716.8 ms; it measures 6330.7 ms, so **+613.9 ms of
depth-dependent cost** (0.68% of the 16K total) that is absent at 4K. It is also
the only family whose share rises with depth, 6.3% -> 7.0%.

The other ratios sit within 0.03 of each other, which is inside the family-level
noise floor established in the previous entry (~1-2%). Only the QSA result is
large enough to read as a finding; the rest are consistent with pure linearity.

## Comparator role mapping (task #12)

`scripts/qwen4exp_comparator_role_map.py` maps both comparator dialects onto the
shared family taxonomy, leaving **0.1%** of halo-box and **0.6%** of pwilkin time
unattributed.

The rules are derived, not guessed:

- **ggml quant type identifies the expert families.** `GGML_TYPE_Q4_K` = 12,
  `Q5_1` = 7, `Q8_0` = 8, read from `ggml/include/ggml.h` in the comparator
  source. The model's `ffn_gate_exps`/`ffn_up_exps` are its only Q4_K tensors and
  `ffn_down_exps` is Q5_1 in 43 of 48 layers.
- **Grid shape separates the four Q8_0 expert-down layers**, which would
  otherwise hide among dense Q8_0 dispatches. Their grid Y equals the routed
  token count (1704/1708) rather than a weight-derived dimension.

## The comparator traces are not delimited (blocks the component gap report)

This is the finding that matters for task #16, and it is why the mapped family
totals **cannot** be used yet.

| comparator | measured prompt | kernel sum | ratio |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| halo-box 69946438a (BF16) | 5531.0 ms | 10856.3 ms | **1.96x** |
| pwilkin 40a9f4d01 (F16) | 3951.2 ms | 7945.6 ms | **2.01x** |

The halo-box capture spans **79.2 s** while its measured prefill is 5.53 s, at a
13.7% kernel duty cycle. The dispatch timeline shows three evenly spaced prefill
bursts about 5.75 s apart (spikes at ~67.5 s, ~73.75 s, ~79.25 s) — warmup plus
measured repetitions inside one undelimited window. Neither comparator capture
has a marker trace, so the measured region cannot be recovered from the files.

A per-component gap built on these numbers would compare hipEngine's single
delimited prefill against roughly two comparator prefills. The script now reports
the ratio and refuses to call such a trace delimited, so the problem cannot be
reintroduced silently.
