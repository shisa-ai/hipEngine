## Summary

The user approved completing a quality-focused ZBook campaign after handing
SPECDEC2 performance to its stable-hardware owner. AGENTIC-QUALITY2 is a
quality and runtime-semantics campaign, not a performance campaign: every live
artifact sets `performance_claim=false`, thermal/power values are provenance
only, and no ZBook rate forms a denominator for SPECDEC2 or another host.

`878d07a9...`, Qwen3.6-35B `UD-Q4_K_M` completed 10/48 automatic-tool turns,
with valid call/correct tool 18/48, external-oracle pass 16/48, safe patch 0/6,
and Japanese complete success 0/8. Current source has materially changed
server/tool/constraint paths. AQ2 therefore reruns the existing v2 suite on
current clean ZBook source before an expanded suite or implementation.

oracles before candidate code, classifies every failure at its earliest
runtime/model boundary, and admits at most one model-general mechanism. The
candidate may be a parser/template correctness repair, full declared-schema
argument constraint, one bounded invalid-tool repair attempt, bounded patch
constraint, or no implementation. Prompt/token/task/category/heldout/model-name
conditioning is forbidden.
