# Coalesce exact Inkling router transfers across requests

## Summary

Resident Inkling decode groups the router activation exchange within each logical layer visit. Each row still uses the original scalar matrix–vector projection, independent expert selection and expert kernels, and its own attention and convolution state. Single-row decode, prefill and provisional verification remain on the non-staged traversal.

## Changes

- Separate preparation and completion of a visit at its expert boundary. Same-tick decode rows reach that boundary independently, then the existing projection owner submits their separate scalar router projections in one exchange. Rejoin each row to its own expert work and continuation. Keep the packed head grouping unchanged.
