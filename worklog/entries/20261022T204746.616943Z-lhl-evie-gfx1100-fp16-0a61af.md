# EVIE fp16 numerics on gfx1100 and the per-architecture envelope

## Summary

On gfx1100, a GPU-visible suite failed five EVIE tests, and they also fail at
`v0.8.2` and at `56da7b9c5` (the last EVIE change). The fp16 envelope was
recorded on gfx1151 (zbook) and had never been measured on gfx1100. Three
batched tests demanded bit-equality across batch shapes, which their own
module contract does not require.

Decision (2026-10-23, lead): fp16 is acceptable on gfx1100 for MaxSim; record
the gfx1100 measurements as that architecture's envelope and keep the gfx1151
one. Bit-equality across batch shapes is not a production correctness
requirement.

## Changes

- `scripts/evie_numerics_report.py`: fp32/fp16 vs the oracle fixture
  (query, document and visual-feature cosine and error, MaxSim error and
  per-token selection with oracle margins, batching deltas).
- Artifact `benchmarks/results/2026-10-23-xtx-evie-4p5b-gfx1100-numerics.json`;
  the comparison table goes into `docs/model-cards/MODEL-EVIE.md`.
- `tests/test_live_evie_gpu_runtime_fp16.py`: `_FP16_BOUNDS` per
  architecture, resolved with `selected_device_arches()`; an unrecorded
  architecture fails naming the report script.
- `tests/test_live_evie_gpu_runtime_batched.py` (`884878923`): cross-shape
  checks use a 1e-4 bound; same-shape and repeatability stay bit-exact.

## Validation

- RX 7900 XTX: fp32 MaxSim error 1.9e-6, 17/17 token selections; fp16 query
  cosine 0.9999986/0.9999924 (equal to the gfx1151 record), document
  0.99987/0.9948, visual-feature min 0.99955, MaxSim error 7.1e-3 (0.12%),
  16/17 selections; the changed one is query token 1, whose oracle top-2
  margin 0.00108 is the smallest of the 17. W7900 measured the same fp16
  document mean and MaxSim through the test.
- Batching: at most 2.4e-5 cross-shape in fp32 (fp32 vs oracle 3.3e-5),
  0 in fp16.
- `test_live_evie_gpu_runtime_batched.py` 6 passed and
  `test_live_evie_gpu_runtime_fp16.py` 4 passed on the XTX.
