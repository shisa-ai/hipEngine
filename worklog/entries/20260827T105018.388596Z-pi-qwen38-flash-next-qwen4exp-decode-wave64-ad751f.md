# Reject Qwen4Exp Q5_1 wave64 decode

## Summary

Added and rejected a default-off 64-thread Q5_1 selected decode kernel. It
improves warm decode by 6-8% but fails binding production mean/p95 KL.

## Changes

- Added `selected_gemv_wave64_bf16_bf16_out` for raw Q5_1 and a Qwen4Exp
  diagnostic selector `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q5_1_WAVE64`.
- Retained the 256-thread strict reduction as default/fallback.
- Preserved compact evidence at
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-27-gfx1151-qwen38-flash-next-decode-wave64-candidate.json`.

## Validation

- Focused Q5_1 direct/wave64/grouped/WMMA tests pass against CPU dequant.
- Independent warm 32-step run: 5.739→6.220 tok/s (+8.4%).
- Paired one-residency 32-step teacher-forced run: 5.754→6.103 tok/s (+6.1%),
  top-1 32/32.
- Strict→wave64 mean/p95/p99/max KL:
  `0.002565/0.007202/0.010349/0.011210`. Mean and p95 exceed the binding
  `0.001/0.005` production limits, so promotion is rejected.

## Next

- Use a layout-preserving selected-expert repack or exact reduction kernel;
  merely changing workgroup geometry consumes too much numerical budget.
- Decode also needs launch reduction/graph or retained submission after exact
  selected kernels are available.
