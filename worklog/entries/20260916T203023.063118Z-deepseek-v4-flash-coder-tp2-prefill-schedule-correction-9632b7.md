# Correction: mixed prefill schedules are legitimate; no hard equality gate and no causality claim

## Summary

Parent review of `949f64603` rejected three parts of that unit's interpretation
and one behavioral choice. This entry supersedes those specific claims; the
saved measurements are unchanged.

**Withdrawn:**

1. **Hard schedule-equality gate.** `report_sustained` must not require a
   matched prefill schedule before scoring a cross-product numerical
   comparison. Different native prefill algorithms are legitimate product paths;
   they are not required to be identical, only to satisfy the unchanged
   production numerical envelope.
2. **"Misattributed numerical failure."** The TP2 KL 0.108406 at
   `mixed_ja_en_translate` position 146 remains a **genuine end-to-end quality
   failure**. Recording the prefill schedule does not convert it into a
   non-failure.
3. **"Verified no control defect / T2 drift / causality."** The smooth,
   monotone per-layer RMS growth and the small prefill-end logit difference
   (KL 6.08e-05) are consistent with arithmetic amplification. They do not rule
   out subtle state/layout errors and do not prove causality. The bisect is
   diagnostic, not exculpatory.

**Kept:** explicit per-arm `prefill_schedule` provenance, the saved
bulk-vs-token-serial boundary observations, and the requirement that every arm
share input/position/profile and meet the unchanged envelopes.

- `scripts/tp2_teacher_coverage_broad.py` `report_sustained`: removed the
