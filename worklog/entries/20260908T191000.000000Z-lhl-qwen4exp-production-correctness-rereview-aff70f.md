# Production-correctness re-review of strict/exactness-based discards

## Summary

User directive (recorded in AGENTS.md): there is no exactness contract; the
binding contract is production correctness (docs/EXECUTION-PROFILES.md).
Re-reviewed every optimization previously discarded for strict/exactness
reasons against the production gates.

Findings (details in the campaign doc's re-review section): the Q8 early
wave publication and expanded F32 Q8 cache rejections were
performance-based and stand; the Q5_K bundle stays rejected by the binding
production envelope (mean KL 0.001179 > 0.001) but is the highest-value
near-miss; the Q8 MMQ attention-gate rejection was for nondeterminism and
is REOPENED as a fix-then-requalify target; the GDN gate 3-plane MMQ
family is production-eligible in modified form (the exact-scheme
non-transfer conclusion only bounded the exact route), with the specific
(2560,6144) candidate still rejected narrowly by the envelope; the 2-plane
MMQ and Q5_1 MMQ ds4 rejections stand.

Applied the corrected framing to the in-flight R4b Q5_1 iu8 down route:
captured five real down-input snapshots (four cases, multiple layers) with
a new capture probe; measured the flip-escape floor across multipliers
(worst case: code-p4096 layer needs 12.0; 4.0 escapes up to 7 per
chunk-layer); verified in situ that the route's down output matches the
parent to 5-of-13.1M outputs at multiplier 4 and that teacher-forced
prefill logits are bit-identical at every size with only free-running
decode divergence at p4096 (diagnostic under the contract). Raised the
default multiplier to 16 (zero flips on all captures, ~1.3x margin over
the floor) so the route stays bit-identical to the strict parent in
practice; a control-flow bug that ran the parent after the iu8 chain
(doubling the down cost and masking route output) was found via in-situ
rocprof and fixed — the route now replaces the parent exclusively
(in-situ: 950ms pair2 -> 593ms iu8+repair per p512 chunk at multiplier 4).
