# R4b first screen: Q5_1 down owner ranking and MMQ chain rejection

## Summary

Re-ranked the MoE kernel owners from the retained R2 family-capture raw
kernel traces (post both promotions, iu8 exact active): the Q5_1 down
exact parent (pair2 expertgrid64 m1 row-publish template) is 3.22s at
code-p4096, 3.13s general_en, 3.19s mixed — the largest single MoE kernel
owner of the 18.7s device total, double the stale ~1.6s targeting clue.

Built and ran the Q5_1 MMQ flip probe (actual blk.0 down weights, uniform
routing, 512/1024 rows, ten pairs, alternating order) comparing the exact
pair2 row-publish parent against the default-off weight-exact MMQ ds4
chain. Findings:

1. The runner's default-off Q5_1 MMQ route pairs the **single-plane** ds4
   pack (`hipengine_gguf_q8_1_mmq_ds4_pack_bf16`, residual_passes=1) with
   the **three-plane** consumer — a latent inconsistency in a default-off,
   approximate-class route (median relative error ~1.3%, 86% flip
   fraction). Not a production defect; documented for whenever that route
   is next touched.
2. With the correct three-plane residual pack (`..._d4x3`), the chain is
   still **0.75-0.78x of pair2** (14.3ms vs 11.2ms at 5120 compact rows;
   23.1ms vs 17.3ms at 10240) with an **18% BF16 flip fraction** (fp16
   -rounded ds4 scales contribute). No speed headroom, high repair cost:
   the existing DP4A ds4 kernel is rejected as the promotion vehicle.

The viable R4b candidate is a new weight-exact iu8-WMMA-class Q5_1 kernel
(in-kernel three-plane fp32 staging, raw 5-bit codes as the integer
operand, m-offset from staged plane sums, Kahan risk criterion with the
R4a nonfinite/scale guards, sparse exact repair reproducing the pair2
row-publish arithmetic) — mirroring the promoted Q4 gate/up structure.
