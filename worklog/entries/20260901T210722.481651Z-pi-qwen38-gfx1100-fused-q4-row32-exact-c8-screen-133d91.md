# Screen fused Q4 row32 for exact C8 targets

## Summary

Added a default-off exact-C8 R32 screen together with the missing two-active-wave
fused Q4 gate/up+SiLU owner. The previous R32 attempt reduced target/device time
but regressed complete wall because rows32 fell below the fused-FFN floor and
expanded gate/up into singleton rowtiles. The new sibling preserves the row48
template arithmetic/BF16 boundary while using exactly two 16-row waves. It is
exact and decisively positive at the leaf and marker gates.

## Changes

- Added and registered
  `dense_dual_wmma_prefill_row32_bf16_bf16_out`, instantiated as
  `row_tiles_per_wave=1, active_waves_per_block=2`.
- Added default-off `GGUF_SPECDEC2_EXACT_C8_TARGET_ROWS_POLICY` under
  `HIPENGINE_GGUF_SPECDEC2_EXACT_C8_TARGET_ROWS=1`; extended exact Q4/Q5/Q6 R32
  composition and selected the fused row32 owner only in physical exact scope.
- Added RED→GREEN production-shape BF16 parity, selector, adapter rollback, and
  Q4/Q5/Q6 ownership tests plus a compact screen artifact.

## Validation

- W7900 actual Qwen3.8 layer 0/32/64 gate/up weights, R32, 40 alternating
  HIP-event pairs per layer, three launches/sample: row32 is BF16-bit exact to
  padded row48, wins 40/40 pairs at every layer, and improves
  **1.3603–1.3641x** (0.652–0.663→0.478–0.486 ms median).
- Dirty current-source cached-only C8 marker, one train prompt, K3/D12, control
  then candidate: generated IDs and `[3,3,2]` acceptance match; physical rows
  change `[36,36,24]→[32,32,24]`. Three-cycle target wall improves
  **590.042→519.014 ms (-12.04%)**, device union **491.262→441.460 ms
  (-10.14%)**, fused gate/up **95.905→70.991 ms (-25.98%)**, Q4/Q5/Q6 sums
  **-13.31%/-1.55%/-6.61%**, and profiled complete throughput improves
  **11.27%**.
- Focused adapter, selector, production-shape parity, and Q4/Q5/Q6 ownership
  nodes pass. Evidence:
  `benchmarks/results/2026-09-02-w7900-q4km-k3-c8-fused-row32-screen.json`.
