# W7900 Qwen3.8: prefill carries ~250 ms fixed cost that dwarfs a 45-token prompt

## Summary

Opening analysis for the small-row prefill unit (task #12). Splitting shipping-route prefill
wall into a fixed and a marginal term, on two independent sessions that agree to 5%, the
C1-C3 deficit is not GEMM throughput: hipEngine pays **252-265 ms of fixed cost per prefill**,
and llama.cpp current completes the *entire* 45-token admission - prompt processing plus first
step - in **223.4 ms**. Our fixed term alone is larger than their whole admission. Length
hides it, which is why the internal rate looks healthy at 512 tokens and why grouping (task
#8) fixed C4-C8 but cannot touch C1-C3.

## Changes

Analysis and ledger only. No engine change, no benchmark row changed.

The 45-token wall predicted from each fit (0.2938 s, 0.3030 s) brackets the measured
0.2818-0.303 s, and today's 0.303 s at 45 tokens implies 148.5 tok/s, which is the published
C1 prefill row (148.891) - so the same quantity is being measured on both days by three
routes. Comparison target, from the same retained packet as the parity entry: llama.cpp current
admission 223.4 ms at 44.9 tokens with a 38.3 ms first step, i.e. roughly 185 ms of prompt
processing. To reach parity at C1 we must remove on the order of **70 ms** of fixed cost, or
find it is being counted twice.

Instrument gap that must close before any attribution work: `scripts/qwen35_gguf_bench.py`
reports `gpu_stage_timings: True` while `runs[0].gpu_stage_timings_ms` contains only
bookkeeping keys (`method: None`, `enabled`, `decode_tokens`) and no stage rows, for both the
45- and 512-row shipping runs. The flag says timings exist; the payload says otherwise. Per
`docs/KERNELS.md` the correct next measurement is a prebuilt `.so` with `require_cached` under
`rocprofv3 --kernel-trace`, not this field.
