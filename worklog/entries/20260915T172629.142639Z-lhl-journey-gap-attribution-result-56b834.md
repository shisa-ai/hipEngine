# Same-file Flash-Next prefill comparison: the gap is kernel quality, not conservative arithmetic

## Summary

The external comparison is now measured against engines that can open the same
file, and the 4K gap is attributed per kernel family. hipEngine's production
default runs 183.6 tok/s at 4K against 1061.9 for pwilkin `strix-halo`
`40a9f4d01` (5.78x), 653.2 for halo-box `69946438a` (3.56x) and 452.9 for
upstream llama.cpp `6011c34ce` (2.47x), all on the same host, same
`UD-Q4_K_XL` file and same twelve canonical cases.

The gap splits into **1.65x conservative arithmetic** and **3.38x kernel
quality at equal arithmetic**, measured in the same unit so they multiply to
the total rather than being two estimates of it. The conservative-arithmetic
factor is a lower bound: the census at `53512b509` had six of the fifteen
recovery selectors on. The largest single item is not a correctness tax at all
- the generic K-quant dense prefill kernel costs 10095.6 ms of the 22206.4 ms
prefill against about 2770 ms for pwilkin's dense and fused-split variants,
while the sparse exact repair passes that conservative arithmetic requires cost
1396.6 ms, 6.3% of the prefill and 7.7% of the gap.

This supersedes the framing that the conservative arithmetic recovery is the
main remaining problem. Restoring every disabled selector leaves hipEngine
about 3.4x behind pwilkin on the default dense path.

## Validation

- Attribution totals cross-checked against an independent measurement path.
  The name-based bucket total for hipEngine (22206.4 ms) agrees with its
  marker-delimited owner total (22201.9 ms) to 0.02%. The same trace's raw
  total is 2x the per-prefill figure because the profiled window contains a
  warmup prefill; the divisor of two is recorded in the script and confirmed by
  that agreement rather than assumed.
- Bucket-vs-owner rows are reconciled in the artifact, not hidden: MoE
  -1820.7 ms, GR -1208.8 ms, dense +2119.2 ms, because
  `gguf_k_prefill_out` serves the linear, MoE-down and GR-down owners and
  name-based bucketing cannot split it. Totals agree; individual rows are
  documented as not interchangeable.
- The census figure 287.07 tok/s was re-derived from its own artifact
  (`results/2026-09-14-journey-progress/artifact.json`,
  `owner-refresh-code4k.json`, `profiled_window_ms` 14268.35) rather than
  carried from prose. It is recorded with `performance_claim: false` and used
  only as a same-case device-time divisor.
- KV-dtype confound bounded by re-running two engines in both dtypes: halo-box
  660.6 f16 vs 653.2 bf16, upstream 459.0 f16 vs 452.9 bf16, about 1%.
- Excluded engines are recorded with their reason, not dropped: pwilkin
  `master` (`e8e6c7af2`) has no `qwen4exp` architecture and cannot load the
  file; halogen refuses `Q4_K`/`Q5_K`/`Q5_1`/`Q4_1` by name at startup and its
  published figures are on `UD-IQ4_XS`.
- `scripts/sync_benchmark_readme.py --check` passes.
- No numerics gate applies and none is claimed: no engine's output was
  compared against another's.

## Limits

- Prefill only, up to 4096 tokens, single text AR. Not an MTP, long-context or
  decode result. pwilkin's lead grows with context (1.83x/4.51x/5.78x at
  512/1K/4K), consistent with its sparse-QSA and indexer work, but this
  measurement does not isolate that mechanism.
- pwilkin's arm runs F16 KV because `40a9f4d01` asserts at model load with
  BF16 (`src/models/qwen4exp.cpp:1360` requires k/v F16 on the sparse-QSA
  direct-indices path). hipEngine's QSA kernels are `bf16_kv` only, so no
  matched arm exists; the effect is bounded at about 1%, not eliminated.
- The census is a profiling capture, not a harness rate. It bounds the
  conservative-arithmetic factor from below and nothing more.
- The 1.65x/3.38x split rests on one census configuration with six of fifteen
  selectors restored. An all-restored same-case capture would tighten both
  bounds and is the cheapest way to firm up this result.
