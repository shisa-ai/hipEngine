# rocprofv3 device truth: GEMV is 89.6% of the diffusion stage; 3 of my inferred figures were 7-10x wrong; the real gap is 78 blocking copies

## Summary

I ran the measurement I named as decisive in `20260915T204937.687834Z`:
`rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` on the diffusion head, with the DSOs prebuilt outside the
profiler and `require_cached`. It overturns three figures I had inferred by
microbenchmark subtraction, and it locates the `forward()` gap that two prior entries
failed to explain.

**Device truth for one solver step (42 kernels), 20 steps:**

| kernel | /step | device mean | my earlier inferred figure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `dense_gemv_out_kernel` | 21.9 | **64.34 us** | ~40 us (assumed) |
| `vv_diff_rmsnorm_modulate_kernel` | 5.0 | **27.83 us** | 17.93 us |
| `vv_diff_silu_kernel` | 6.0 | **1.44 us** | 10.75 us |
| `vv_diff_gated_residual_kernel` | 4.0 | **1.72 us** | 8.92 us |
| `silu_mul_separate_out_kernel` | 4.0 | **1.63 us** | 10.67 us |
| `vv_diff_add_kernel` | 1.0 | **1.51 us** | 10.12 us |
| **total** | 41.9 | **1572 us/step** | ~1138 us (wrong) |

The elementwise kernels are **7-10x cheaper than my isolated microbenchmarks said**.
My isolation method was measuring something other than the kernel. GEMV is **28.18 of
31.44 ms = 89.6% of device time**.

## Changes

No production code change. One immutable worklog entry. Prebuilt the five cached
DSOs and produced a profiler trace under `/tmp` (not committed, per the
never-commit-raw-rocprof-dumps rule).

## Validation

### Command

Prebuild outside the profiler, then trace (both with the compiler-version file so no
`hipcc` can be spawned from the profiled process):

```
HIPENGINE_COMPILER_VERSION_FILE=/tmp/vibevoice-compiler-version.txt \
rocprofv3 --kernel-trace -f csv -d /tmp/vvprof -o vvdiff -- \
  uv run python scripts/vibevoice_tts_decoder_rocprof_smoke.py \
  --compiler-version-file /tmp/vibevoice-compiler-version.txt
```

1485 dispatches captured; the diffusion region is the trailing 916 (838 compute + 78
copies), identified by the first `vv_diff_*` dispatch. Counts confirm 20 solver steps
(6+5+4+4+1 non-GEMV kernels per step, plus 21.9 GEMVs).

### The `forward()` gap, located

`forward()` wall is 2069 us/step; device time is 1572 us/step. The 497 us/step
residue is **not** dispatch overhead, and **not** uniform. Splitting the 915
inter-dispatch gaps in the diffusion region:

```
median gap                  3.53 us   (normal enqueue + launch latency)
gaps < 20 us       820 of 915        total 3.69 ms
gaps > 100 us         24             total 6.78 ms
```

I separately refuted the dispatch hypothesis by timing 42 real GEMV launches at a tiny
shape: **3.37 us/call wall for ~2.5 us of device work, i.e. ~0.87 us of host overhead
per launch**. 42 launches/step therefore cost ~141 us, not 497 us.

### The actual cause: blocking host/device copies

```
diffusion region: 916 dispatches = 78 copyBuffer + 838 compute
copyBuffer device time : 0.181 ms total, 2.32 us mean   (3.9 copies per step)
compute device time    : 31.44 ms

gaps AFTER a copyBuffer : n=77  total 6.50 ms  mean 84.4 us
gaps AFTER a compute    : n=838 total 5.85 ms  mean  7.0 us
```

**78 copies cost 0.181 ms of device time but 6.50 ms of GPU idle -- a 36x
amplification.** A gap after a compute kernel is 7.0 us; after a copy it is 84.4 us.
That is the signature of a blocking readback: the host issues the D2H, blocks until the
queue drains, and only then enqueues the next step's work, leaving the GPU idle.

Per frame that is 6.50 ms of the 43.95 ms diffusion span; across 25 frames per request
it is **~162 ms, ~4.1% of the 4.005 s warm synthesis**. This is the same magnitude
that `20260915T203624.733692Z` estimated for a device-side CFG combine + DPM step, now
confirmed by direct device evidence rather than inference.

### What this corrects

Three of my own earlier conclusions were wrong and are superseded:

- The "~930 us/call unexplained gap" in `20260915T204937.687834Z` -- wrong; the real
  residue is 497 us/step, and it is 78 blocking copies, not a mystery.
- The elementwise per-kernel costs (8.92-17.93 us) in `20260915T204837.504087Z` --
  wrong by 7-10x. Real device times are 1.44-1.72 us, except
  `vv_diff_rmsnorm_modulate_kernel` at 27.83 us.
- The claim that the diffusion stage's GEMV runs at ~204 GB/s and is therefore at
  peak -- the device figure is 246.6 MB of weights per step in 1.409 ms = **175 GB/s**,
  against the ~169.5 GB/s that `threads=64` achieves in the LM GEMV microbenchmark. It
  is at the achievable rate for this kernel shape, so there is no easy bandwidth win.

Note the diffusion head **already** uses `_GEMV_THREADS = 64`; `vibevoice_tts_diffusion.py`
line 36 documents it as "2.08 ms against 3.33 ms at 256". The cliff documented in
`20260915T204837.504087Z` is in the LM, not here.

## Next

**Eliminate the per-step blocking copies** -- the only well-evidenced opportunity left
with >1% of warm attached. Two routes, in order of preference:

1. **Move the DPM solver step to the device.** Then `eps` never leaves the GPU, the 20
   per-frame D2H readbacks disappear, and with them the ~6.5 ms/frame of copy-induced
   idle. This is the larger and cleaner fix.
2. **Async copies with one sync per frame** (or per request). Cheaper to implement and
   captures most of the idle, but leaves the host-side numpy solver in the loop.

Expected: **~162 ms, ~4.1% of warm** from route 1, before counting the host numpy time
it also removes. Bounded, and it does not approach 2x.

## Caveat

The diffusion head is GEMV-bound (89.6% of device time) at ~175 GB/s, which is the
achievable rate for these shapes. **The diffusion stage therefore has no >1.5x
opportunity left**; route 1 above is worth ~4%, and the arithmetic conclusion of
`20260915T200702.494130Z` (2-10x unreachable) stands.
