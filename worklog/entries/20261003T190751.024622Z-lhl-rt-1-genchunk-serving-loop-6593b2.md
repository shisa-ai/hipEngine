# RT-1's win IS reachable through the serving loop: the generator chunk, not the cap

## Summary

Supersedes the "Next" section of
`worklog/entries/20261003T185516.925434Z-lhl-rt-1-prefill-peer-fairness-3e9475.md`,
which recorded the generator-chunk measurement as blocked. It is not blocked - the
failure was a defect in my own override.

**The generator-level chunk 4096 reproduces RT-1's prefill win through the engine
loop: -5.74% prefill wall, with the decoding peer's worst single gap halving.**

| arm | gen chunk | loop cap | workspace | `prefill_token_ids` | prefill wall | peer TTFT | peer ITL median | peer max gap |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `g1024_l1024` | 1024 | 1024 | `[1024]` | 8,192 B | 15.992 s | 16.854 s | 49.19 ms | 147.9 ms |
| `g4096_l1024` | 4096 | 1024 | `[4096]` | 32,768 B | **15.075 s** | 15.770 s | 49.09 ms | 75.1 ms |
| `g4096_l4096` | 4096 | 4096 | `[4096]` | 32,768 B | **15.001 s** | 15.717 s | 49.17 ms | 70.5 ms |
| **delta** | | | | | **-5.74% / -6.20%** | -6.43% / -6.75% | -0.20% | **-49% / -52%** |

RT-1 measured -4.94% by calling `runner.prefill(ids)` directly, bypassing the loop.
The serving loop now measures -5.74% at the same prompt and host - same direction,
comparable magnitude.

**The peer is not harmed, it benefits.** Its TTFT falls 6.43% because it queues
behind a prefill that finishes sooner, its ITL median is unchanged, and its worst
single gap halves.

Combined with the earlier packet, the two knobs are now separated by measurement:

- **The engine-loop cap** changes the tick decomposition (5 -> 2) and **not** the
  workspace. It costs the peer nothing, and it is **not** a route to the win.
- **The generator chunk** changes the workspace (`[1024]` -> `[4096]`,
  `prefill_token_ids` 8,192 -> 32,768 B) and **does** deliver the win.

## The binding point

Patching `Qwen4ExpGGUFTextGenerator.__init__` alone does not hold the chunk. `LLM`
reaches the generator through the registry factory, which sets
`_implicit_profile_chunk_size = prefill_chunk_size is None` - True whenever no chunk
is passed, which is always on this path - and
`qwen4_exp_profiles._bind_default_chunk` then runs **after** construction and resets
`generator.runner.prefill_chunk_size` to 1024. The override was silently reverted
while the already-allocated workspace stayed at 1024 rows, which is the
`copy size 32768 exceeds device buffer size 8192` failure I recorded as a limit.

Neutralising `_bind_default_chunk` makes the chunk survive to the workspace
allocation. **That constant is the real binding point and is where a production
promotion has to move** - it is not a cosmetic detail.

This also means the earlier packet's claim that "cap and workspace are different
knobs" was right, but its conclusion that the win therefore has no serving-loop
route was wrong.

Engagement is observed rather than inferred, from three independent readbacks:
`_prefill_workspaces[*].prefill_chunk_size` `[1024]` -> `[4096]`,
`prefill_token_ids.nbytes` 8,192 -> 32,768, and the traced session prefill reporting
`prefill_chunk_size` 1024 -> 4096 with `workspace_chunks` `[1024]` -> `[4096]`.

The prefill deltas (-5.74%, -6.20%) are far outside the <=0.11% band this harness
showed on the cap comparison, so they are real at one repetition per arm. The peer
ITL medians are not resolved beyond "unchanged".
