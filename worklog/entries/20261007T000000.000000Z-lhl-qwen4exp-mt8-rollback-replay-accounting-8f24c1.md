# Qwen4Exp MT-8 rollback/replay accounting screen

## Summary

The bounded MT-8 diagnostic is complete for the public Qwen4Exp MTP provider.
A CPU-only instrumented screen exercises a four-token proposal at every
rejection depth, including all-reject and full-accept. It reports target and
draft forwards, cursor trim, snapshot/restore calls, restored bytes and
allocation ownership without changing production behavior.

The current serial provider has no duplicate target replay: verification stops at
the first mismatching target row. The draft executes the complete proposal, then
`trim(target.position)` restores its append-only cursor. The screen therefore
finds zero restore bytes, zero restore allocations and zero per-cycle
allocations. Shared resident weights are attached once and are not charged as
per-case rollback work.

## Decision

MT-8 remains diagnostic and is not a production optimization. No redundant
replay removal or dirty-destination restore change is justified by this screen:
the suspected duplicate target forwards and full-state rollback are absent from
this provider's current ownership model. A future transaction redesign must
reopen the screen with real state/KV/recurrent ownership before changing the
provider.

`performance_claim: false`.
