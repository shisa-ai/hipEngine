review. Three committed entries recorded `base_commit` values that look like
commit hashes and are not commits in this repository; the worklog validator only
checked the format, so nothing caught them. The three are corrected here with
their real values, and `check` now resolves every recorded hash against the

## Guard: `check` now resolves base commits

- `scripts/worklog.py`: `check` resolves each recorded `base_commit` with
  `git cat-file -e <sha>^{commit}` and reports the count in its summary line;
  `check --provenance` lists the entries.
- This is reported, not enforced. A committed entry is immutable, so a hard
  failure would leave no in-tree remedy and would permanently redden the gate.
- `worklog/README.md` documents the new flag and the reason it is a report.
- `tests/test_integration_worklog.py`: an entry with an invented tail is counted
  and named while `check` still exits 0, and a real hash is not reported.
  RED before the change: 1 failed, 2 passed. GREEN: 3 passed.

## ACS classification: raw controls, not a blanket verdict

`parse_acs_entries()` treated `SrcValid`, `TransBlk`, and `DirectTrans` as
peer-DMA blockers alongside the redirect bits. Only `ReqRedir`, `CmpltRedir`, and
`UpstreamFwd` redirect a peer TLP to the root complex; `SrcValid` and `TransBlk`
influence routing in specific cases, and `DirectTrans` is a capability for
translated requests. The parser now reports `redirect_bits_enabled` (which
decides `blocks_peer_dma`), `other_bits_enabled`, `direct_translated_p2p_enabled`,
and a `routing_note`, and its docstring states that these are register controls
rather than proof that a pair of endpoints cannot reach each other.

Raw values on this host, unchanged by the reclassification: `ACSCtl` on
`00:03.1`, `00:03.2`, `0c:00.0`, `0f:00.0` reads `SrcValid+ TransBlk- ReqRedir+
CmpltRedir+ UpstreamFwd+ EgressCtrl- DirectTrans-`.

## Display-load visibility

`collect_display_load()` skipped a process whose fd table could not be read
without recording that fact, and stopped after the first DRM node per process.
An empty result therefore read as "no opener" when it could mean "not
observable", and a compositor holding both cards appeared to hold only one. It
now records `unreadable_fd_tables` and `visibility_complete`, and records every
DRM node a process holds.

## Per-device architecture identity

`collect_devices()` assigned `arches[rank]` from a deduplicated architecture list,
so a mixed-architecture host or a reordered device selection was mislabelled by
position. New `detect_hip_target_arches_per_device()` keeps one entry per visible
device in HIP device order, `arch_for_device()` selects by HIP device index, and a
device the probe did not cover reports `None` plus a recorded error instead of
borrowing another device's architecture. Each entry also records `arch_probe`
with the raw per-device list.
