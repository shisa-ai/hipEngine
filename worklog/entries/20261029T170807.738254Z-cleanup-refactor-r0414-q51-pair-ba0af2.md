# Scope Q5_1 output-pair reuse to its runner

## Summary

R0414 is fixed. The profile publishes `q51_pair_prefill` on the generator and
its runner instead of setting `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q51_PAIR_PREFILL`.
Production stays on, strict stays off. Rows>=64, input width<=4096 and registry
capability still guard selection. Kernel arithmetic, fallback ordering and
accepted profile choices are unchanged; this is not a performance claim.

## Changes

- Forward the choice through scalar and batch layer calls, the captured MoE
  closure, the mixed verifier and late runner clones. Include it in the batch
  graph key so a changed choice cannot reuse the previous captured segment.
- Add exception-safe `q51_pair_prefill(runner, enabled)` diagnostic binding.
  Campaign/state-gate arms use it rather than process mutation; fold arms
  require and record the owning runner's pair2 publication. PF13 and Q5_1 out8
  ablations close pair2 on their runner and restore it on exit.
- Retire the environment entry, remove the completed REFACTOR section and map
  its requirements in the sweep manifest. The deleted section's historical
  performance evidence remains in Git; this control migration does not rerun
  or reinterpret those measurements.
- Record three audit false positives on unchanged DP4A layer guards. The
  scanner rekeyed them after call-site forwarding changed; their documented
  failure/removal conditions under `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q4_DP4A64{,_LAYERS}`
  are unchanged. No budget increase or unrelated guard change.
