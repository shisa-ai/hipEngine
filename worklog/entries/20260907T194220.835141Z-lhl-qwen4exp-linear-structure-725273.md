# Non-GR linear family structural study (completes the FFN+linear study)

## Summary

Completed the linear-family half of the FFN/linear structural study (the
FFN half produced the promoted exact iu8-risk+repair gate/up route).
Measured on the retained post-QSA p4096 trace: the non-GR linear family
(~4.45 s) is ~1.9 s exact wave-scale coltile (dominated by the GDN gate
projection (2560,6144), plus small alpha/beta tails), ~2.7 s of
3-residual-plane MMQ on policy-admitted shapes, and ~0.5 s of
quantize/small roles. The structural gap to Vulkan (1.02 s) decomposes
into (a) the exact coltile fallback on the GDN-gate shape, which is ~1.9x
slower than the MMQ chain but blocked by the T2 production-numerics bars
(p95 5.26e-3 vs 5e-3, top-1 98.67% vs 99%), and (b) the MMQ chain itself
carrying 3 activation planes - roughly 3x Vulkan's single-plane MMQ
arithmetic - which is the deliberate price of the numerics bars
(2-plane failed badly: KL mean 2.8e-3 vs 1e-3).

New discriminator probe on actual blk.0.attn_gate weights (Q8_0,
K2560->N6144, rows 512/1024, F32 in/out):
- Speed: exact coltile 5.47/10.99 ms vs quantize+guarded 3-plane MMQ
  2.83/5.91 ms = 1.94x/1.86x operation-complete.
- Exactness: 91.2% of F32 outputs differ between the chains -> the
  exact risk+repair scheme does NOT transfer to this owner (F32
  consumer, no rounding boundary to repair at; repair would recompute
  ~everything).
- Error decomposition vs float64 truth: the parent coltile itself
  deviates from truth by median 5.7e-8 / p95 1.9e-7 (~1 ulp), the
  3-plane MMQ by median 1.2e-7 / p95 5.2e-7 (~2 ulp) - only 2.1-2.8x
  the parent's own f32-order error. The 3-plane activation residual is
  already below this floor, so a 4th plane cannot move the T2 bars: the
  chain is at the f32 accumulation-order noise floor of ANY reassoc-
  iated arithmetic on this shape.

## Next

- The GDN-gate exact coltile owner (~1.4-1.6 s of p4096) is at a hard
  numerics boundary: its ~1.9x MMQ speedup is only reachable through a
  T2 production-numerics acceptance, and no kernel-side change (more
  planes, better repair) can improve the bars because the error is
  order-dominated. Any future action there is a numerics-policy decision
  (scope recalibration), not a kernel task.
- The remaining structural linear lever is the 3-plane MMQ overhead on
  admitted shapes (~2.7 s): also numerics-bound (2-plane failed), so it
  is closed under the current policy.
- Recommend recording this as the linear-family floor under the current
  numerics regime and redirecting structural effort to the remaining
  FFN owners (Q5_1 exact down 1.6 s, where the iu8-risk+repair scheme
  does transfer) and GR (2.44 s composite, BF16-boundary repair may
  transfer since its consumers round to BF16).
