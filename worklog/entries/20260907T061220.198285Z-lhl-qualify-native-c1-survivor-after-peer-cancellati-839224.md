# Qualify native C1 survivor after peer cancellation

## Summary

The real controller-side peer-cancellation diagnostic passes all 10 category-suite
prompts at both N8 and N2 on epyc W7900 GPU0. Each capacity executes 19 paired
cycles and 51 native C1 survivor cycles. Cancelled collector histories are exact
nonempty proper AR prefixes; surviving children match full independent AR output.
This is a bounded engine diagnostic, not public cancellation or full numerical
qualification.

## Changes

- Add controller-side `collect_cancelled_pair` helper. It waits for a successful
  paired-target event, cancels only the first real handle, requires service
  acknowledgement and cancelled finish details, and rejects cancellation of the
  survivor. Never call blocking handle cancellation from the service/GPU worker.
- Planned integration uses two D24 children, signals after successful paired
  target execution, compares the cancelled output with the exact independent
  AR prefix, and compares the survivor with full AR output. Preserve request-ID
  association, native packed survivor traces, and clean teardown.

- Tests cover wait/cancel/result ordering, missing pair, failed acknowledgement,
  absent cancellation finish details, and cancellation leaking into the peer.

## First GPU result

- `bg-92` N8 stops after acknowledged cancellation: the first handle's `result()`
  raises `GenerationCancelled`. N2 does not run. This is the documented service
  outcome in `_cancel_state`/`EngineServiceHandle.result`, not a normal returned
  `GenerationOutput` with cancellation finish details as the diagnostic assumed.
- `_CollectorBase` preserves committed IDs in its terminal `CollectedOutput`,
  validates terminal history against published tokens, and exposes locked reads.
  Next recognize only `GenerationCancelled`, inspect that terminal collector
  record (including error/request identity), and label the prefix source rather
  than fabricating a public output. Unexpected runtime/reclaim errors still fail.
- No GPU cancellation qualification yet; the failed helper stopped before
  collecting and validating the survivor.
