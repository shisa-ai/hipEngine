# Paired re-ladder on the promoted build to 131072

## Summary

The paired hipEngine-vs-llama.cpp depth ladder ran at 512-131072 on the
promoted WMMA-prefill build, both engines serialized on zbook/Radeon 8060S
with identical prompt IDs, BF16 KV, greedy 128-output requests and
per-protocol sample counts. hipEngine leads prefill at six of nine depths and
decode at eight of nine; at 131072 it leads both phases.

| depth | prefill hip/llama | decode hip/llama |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 512 | **1.45x** | 0.93x |
| 1024 | **1.17x** | **1.04x** |
| 2048 | **1.11x** | **1.14x** |
| 4096 | **1.09x** | **1.14x** |

| 131072 | **1.21x** | **1.05x** |

At 131072 hipEngine measures 470.7 prefill / 12.04 decode tok/s against
llama.cpp's 388.7 / 11.45. The campaign started this phase at 0.31x prefill
and 0.79x decode at this depth, so the promoted build closes the deep-prefill
gap and keeps the decode lead.

llama.cpp's own repeatability gate rejected its 8192 and 32768 rows in this
run (non-repeatable greedy continuations; the isolated five-sample re-measure
reproduced the 8192 divergence and cleared 16384). Those comparator numbers
are retained as best-available evidence with the rejection recorded. The
remaining honest gaps: the mid-depth prefill band 8192-32768 trails at
0.88-0.97x, and 512-token decode trails at 0.93x. Prompt hashes match across
engines at every depth; hipEngine repeats, public-path parity and finite
logits pass on every row.
