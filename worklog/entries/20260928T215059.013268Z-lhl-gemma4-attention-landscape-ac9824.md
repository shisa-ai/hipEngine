# Attention's open surface, and the one bet that is not tracked as a row

## Summary

Attention is 36.3% of the 4096 prefill and is the family the loop's metric
(`attn.sliding@4096`) actually measures. This entry records where every
attention candidate stands after reading each row rather than its summary,
and flags one item that is called "the live bet" for the biggest gap without
ever being a row of its own.

### P1's blocker is a properly formed refusal

P1 is the one row here that looks like it might be an agent-sized build, and
it is not — but its `blocked` status is exactly the shape AGENTS.md asks for,
so it is worth confirming rather than chipping at:

- **Named capability miss**: "the vendored AOTriton 0.11.2b has no
  bottom-right-aligned windowed launch."
- **Clearing command**: install a runtime whose windowed images take
  absolute or bottom-right bounds, re-run the same comparison to confirm the
  alignment flipped, then restore the plumbing.

The measurement behind it is decisive: at the discriminating shape
`rows=512 keys=1536 window=128`, `top_left_window` matches at maxabs 0.0078
while `bottom_right_window` misses at 2.7266, `plain causal` at 2.6406 and
`block_local_causal` at 2.5859 — so it is genuinely a window and genuinely
top-left. Sweeping `window_left` across five integer values produces output
identical to plain causal. The two frames cannot be mixed, and integer bounds
cannot be bent into bottom-right by offsetting `window_left`, because the
right bound sits at block-local `i` rather than `start + i`.

**This is a dependency upgrade, not a kernel task.** It belongs to the human
lead.

### P2 is the one open kernel row with a measured comparator

Tiled flash attention at head_dim 512 for the 5 global layers. Option (a) —
port llama.cpp's `flash_attn_tile<512,512,4,8>` — measured **6.7 ms at 1024
against hipEngine's 39.2 ms**, and the row estimates ~32 ms / ~525 ms.
Option (b), an AOTriton head_dim-512 image, is **closed as vendored**: the
shipped set is 12 files, all `*bf16@16_256_*`, and
`_AOTRITON_PREFILL_HEAD_DIMS` is `(256,)`.

### X4 sits in front of both

"Re-evaluate the vendored AOTriton release" is an investigation, not a
build, and it gates two rows at once: a newer release may ship the
head_dim-512 image P2 option (b) wants, or windowed bounds P1 is blocked on.
It is the cheapest thing on the attention surface and nobody has run it.

## The untracked bet

P1's row ends by saying the record contains two bets on the same gap —
window plumbing (P1) and **a tiled kernel proposed by `a4c7cdfc8`** — and
that the tiled kernel *"remains the live bet for the gap."*

That alternative is **not a punchlist row**. It appears nowhere in the
candidates section; it exists only inside P1's own cell, from which P1's
`blocked` status could read as closing the gap entirely. A reader scanning
row statuses for open work would miss the item the row itself calls live.

Recorded here rather than promoted into a row in the same commit: deciding
whether it deserves one, and what its estimate is, needs the `a4c7cdfc8`
worklog entry read in full, which this entry did not do.
