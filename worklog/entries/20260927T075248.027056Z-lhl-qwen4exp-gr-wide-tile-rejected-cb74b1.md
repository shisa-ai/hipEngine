# GR up wide tile rejected by the 780-row gate; prefill-KL screen is not a predictor

## Summary

Sprint H found the GR-up limiter and the fastest known projection for the shape,
and the production gate rejects it. The wide tile is 4.38x faster on the
projection (1.4925 against 6.5379 ms at rows=1024, K=320, N=10240) but fails the
780-row canonical gate on the numerical envelope: mean KL 1.554e-3, p95
7.081e-3, max 7.349e-2, top-1 768/780. Determinism, state repeatability, and
lifecycle pass, so the failure is purely numerical. The route stays default-off.

Two findings outlive the rejection:

1. **The prefill-boundary KL screen does not predict the gate outcome.** This
   route screened at 5.17e-11, 285x better than the rejected iu8 route's 1.48e-8,
   while carrying 300x more projection drift (4.709e-05 against 1.12e-07 -
   3.69e-07 relative L2) - and failed at the same plateau. The publication row's
   ~22-logit argmax gap makes the softmax effectively one-hot, so KL is blind to
   logits that genuinely moved (median absolute drift 0.095, max 1.20). The
   localization's "pass condition is prefill-boundary KL below ~5e-9" is not
   sufficient across route types.
2. **The GR family is closed to arithmetic change.** Six measured routes now
   saturate at 1.3e-3 - 1.6e-3, including corrections that were more accurate
   than the incumbent. Only a bit-exact change can land, and the two
   equal-accumulator retiles were already measured (0.80x, 1.007x).

A third, process-level finding: the layer-2 gate's default prompt suite is 39-71
tokens, below these routes' `rows > 256` admission, so a candidate run against it
never executes and scores a perfect zero that reads as a pass. Only the
harness's independent dispatch count exposes it.

## Next

Do not retry GR arithmetic. Reopen only with (a) a confirmed router-flip
mechanism plus a way to make the downstream discrete selection robust, or (b) a
bit-exact route that beats the fused kernel without changing operand or
accumulation order. Separately, stop using the prefill-boundary KL probe as an
admission screen for GR candidates: go straight to
`qwen4exp_q8_repair_depth_gate.py`, which runs the canonical fixture the routes
actually admit. Any future GR or dense candidate must assert a non-zero dispatch
count before its numerical result is read.
