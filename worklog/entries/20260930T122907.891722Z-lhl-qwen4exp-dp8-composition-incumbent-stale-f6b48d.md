# DP-8's composition certificate transfers, and its incumbent reference is stale

## Summary

DP-8's end-to-end composition gate passes (staged 13-15 + the wide route at
16-47, mean KL 5.443e-4 against an exact-chain teacher), and the ledger now also
records that its **value** is still only an estimate. Before pricing the value I
re-read what the certificate actually binds, and found two things.

**One is a correction: the certificate's own justification is false.** Both
composition routes in `scripts/execution_profile_q8_wmma_prefill_layers_gate.py`
bind the f16 **WMMA** route at 16-47 and clear `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_DENSE_WIDE`
in both arms. `staged_f32_composed_vs_exact` states the reason in its comment -
"with WMMA still bound at 16-47, exactly as promotion would leave it" - and that
premise is wrong. WMMA was **retired as the production default at this scope on
2026-09-17**; `PRODUCTION_Q8_WMMA_PREFILL_LAYERS` carries the retirement note,
and the production plan binds `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_WMMA_LAYERS=""` with
`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q8_DENSE_WIDE=1` over `PRODUCTION_Q8_DENSE_WIDE_PREFILL_LAYERS`
= 16-47. Promotion leaves `dense_wide` bound, not WMMA. The same DP-8 cell
sentence - "16-47 ... is the scope the promoted f16 WMMA route already covers" -
is stale for the same reason.

**The other is the finding that keeps the certificate valid: the two routes are
byte-identical at 16-47, so which one is bound cannot move any KL.** Measured,
not argued - `strict_logits_sha256` `294e6c08d5b2c315…` and
`candidate_logits_sha256` `10fd0ae50b6eb9ce…` are equal across all four
artifacts:

| artifact | route | strict | candidate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `artifact-dense-wide16-47-chunk1024-at-head.json` | `dense_wide` | `294e6c08…` | `10fd0ae5…` |
| `artifact-wmma16-47-chunk1024-at-head.json` | `wmma` | `294e6c08…` | `10fd0ae5…` |
| `artifact-dense-wide16-47-chunk1024-engaged.json` | `dense_wide` | `294e6c08…` | `10fd0ae5…` |
| `artifact-wmma16-47-chunk4096.json` | `wmma` | `294e6c08…` | `10fd0ae5…` |

**So no re-gate is required, and the certificate describes the production
composition's arithmetic even though it names the retired route.** The
byte-identity is a property of the current pair rather than a standing
guarantee, so a change to either route at 16-47 reopens this - which is why it
is recorded as a finding with its evidence rather than used to silence the
comment.
