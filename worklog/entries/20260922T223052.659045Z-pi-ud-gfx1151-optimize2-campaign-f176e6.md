# Open UD-GFX1151-OPTIMIZE2: verify the UD/plain gap and freeze hypotheses

## Summary

The lead asked whether "UD Q4 is about 30% slower than regular Q4" is true and
for a review of the UD optimization work. Verified first, then opened
`docs/campaigns/UD-GFX1151-OPTIMIZE2.md` as the execution plan for the two
recorded root-cause fixes.

**The claim holds.** Both arms re-ran at HEAD `91f56a7c8` (clean tree), back
to back in one thermal window on zbook/gfx1151, same harness and protocol as
the 2026-09-21 pair: decode **-32.48 / -31.85 / -32.53%** and prefill
**-36.65 / -40.71 / -40.46%** at 512/1024/4096, reproducing the 2026-09-21
deltas (-32.15..-33.15% decode, -37.59..-43.46% prefill) within spread. Every
CV is at or below 0.62% (decode at or below 0.13%), both arms pass the 18/18
graph/eager id, logits and state gate, and absolute rates sit 4-5% below the
2026-09-21 pair (day-to-day spread; the plain arm lands at its recorded 294.8
tok/s zbook floor) with ratios unchanged. "About 30%" is right for decode and
understates prefill.

**Both root causes are unchanged at HEAD.** No commit between `d3a5ccf02` and
`91f56a7c8` touched `qwen35_gguf_materialize.py`, `qwen35_gguf_policy.py`,
`gguf_linear.py`, or `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1151/`:

- C1 (decode): `hip_gfx1151` still declares no `GGUF_IQ_DENSE_DECODE_POLICY`
  (and neither strict-slot pin table); the strict IQ GEMV was measured at
  53.42% of UD pure decode on 2026-09-21.
- C2 (prefill/layout): the production route audit at HEAD still plans the UD
  file `repack=OFF` (103 of 104 Q4_K optimized; Q5_K x131, IQ4_XS x117,
  Q8_0 x104, Q6_K x24, Q3_K x7, IQ4_NL x7, IQ3_S x4 all raw) against plain's
  `repack=on` (288 Q4_K + 48 Q5_K + 65 Q6_K on tuned layouts).

**The C1 fix exists only as stashed local work.** `stash@{0}
(pre-origin-main-merge-preserve-local-work-20260922)` holds the gfx1151
declaration (+56 lines: decode policy plus both strict-slot pins plus
`GGUF_IQ_DENSE_VERIFY_POLICY`), a `gguf_linear.py` change, and a dispatch-cache
test. It was stashed before the 2026-09-22 origin merge and never re-applied;
stale `__pycache__` was the only residue in the tree. Not touched: recovering
it is campaign item E2a, reviewed against the post-merge tree rather than
blind-popped (it carries unrelated audit inventory churn).

Campaign review outcome: U0-U7 bring-up, the route campaign (prefill 13.3x ->
1.72x), the gfx1100/XTX campaign (UD MTP/AR 1.5258x, admission shipped) and
the 236-commit integration are done and honest for their own scope; gfx1151
parity was never achieved on this lane, and its two fixes were recorded
decisions rather than landed code. Carried out of scope: gfx1100 lanes, UD MTP
on gfx1151 (unmeasured), K_S speed-claim eligibility, route-plan item 15.
