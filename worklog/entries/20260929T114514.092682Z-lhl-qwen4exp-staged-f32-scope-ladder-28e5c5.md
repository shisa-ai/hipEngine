# Staged-f32 scope ladder: 16-47 passes but is 5-10x slower than the promoted route

## Summary

Full scope ladder, canonical 12-case D128 protocol, 1548 rows each:

| Scope | Mean KL | p95 KL | p99 KL | Max KL | Top-1 | Verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0-47 | 1.269e-3 | 6.663e-3 | 1.620e-2 | 2.925e-2 | 0.98385 | fail |
| 8-47 | 8.818e-4 | 3.843e-3 | 1.302e-2 | 6.441e-2 | 0.98837 | fail |
| 12-47 | 6.593e-4 | 3.469e-3 | 9.765e-3 | 3.482e-2 | 0.98837 | fail |
| 16-47 | 3.236e-4 | 1.629e-3 | 4.843e-3 | 1.610e-2 | 0.99483 | **pass** |

Engagement confirmed on every scope (candidate/teacher logits hashes differ).

## 16-47 passing is not a win, and I am not promoting it

16-47 is the scope the f16 WMMA route **already covers and already runs**. The
question that matters is not "does it pass" but "does it beat what is already
there". On the identical shape (`attn_qkv`, 2560 -> 10240, 1024 rows):

| route | TFLOP/s |
| --- | ---: |
| `dense_wide256`, f16 input | 40.54 |
| `dense_wide256`, f32 input | 20.32 |
| **staged f32 tile** | **4.10** |
| exact coltile leaf | 3.32 |

The staged tile is ~5x slower than the wide route with f32 input and ~10x slower
with f16 input, on the same shape. Promoting it at 16-47 would replace a faster
already-promoted route. A numerical pass there buys nothing.

The staged tile's only advantage is over the **exact FMA chain** (4.10 against
3.32 TFLOP/s). The exact chain is what runs where the wide route does not, which
is the 0-15 region. So the live question is a scope inside 0-15, and that ladder
is running.

I flagged this before the ladder finished, and it is the reason a passing scope
is not being reported as a retained result. The candidate is a faster *slow*
kernel, not a faster kernel.

## A non-monotonicity worth keeping

**8-47's `max_kl` (6.441e-2) exceeds 0-47's (2.925e-2) while its mean is lower.**
Adding layers made the worst case *better*. That is direct evidence against the
summed-KL budget the ledger already warns about, and against extrapolating a
scope's numbers from a neighbouring scope's. It also means the 8-47 failure is a
different failure than the 0-47 one: 8-47 fails on a tail and top-1, 0-47 fails
broadly.

## Failure shape

0-47 fails on mean, p95 and top-1 while p99 and max pass - broad-based drift, not
a rare-outlier problem. That rules out a per-layer guard on offending layers as
the fix, because no small set of layers carries it.
