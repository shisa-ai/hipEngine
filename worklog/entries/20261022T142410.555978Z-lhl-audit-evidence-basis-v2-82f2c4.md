# Audit evidence basis covers facts, not counts

## Summary

The audit had 352 stale decisions, up from 334 a day earlier after eleven
unrelated Flash-Next commits. Most were count changes, not new evidence:

- a kernel named by one more test file;
- a flag read by one more script;
- "read at 9 runtime sites" becoming "read at 10".

Under the old hash, the stale backlog grew with ordinary work, so
`check --review` could never pass and a stale decision said nothing.

The hash now covers facts: the title, defaults, ledger and docs membership,
missing paths, quoted text and every signal. Counts and reader lists are left
out, because the signals already state the part a decision rests on: whether a
runtime caller or test exists, whether branching is spread out, whether anyone
continued the work.

Stale went from 352 to 75. Each decision now keeps a compact copy of the
evidence it was made against, so `open --stale` prints what changed.

## Changes

- `audit/hipaudit/core.py`:
  - `VOLATILE_EVIDENCE` adds `sites_by_root`, `runtime_sites`,
    `runtime_referrers`, `test_referrers` and `continuation_entries`.
  - `COUNTED_SIGNAL` blanks the numbers in the audit's own count templates.
    Other numbers in signals, such as a quoted dependency, still count.
  - `Triage.basis`, `compact_basis()` and `basis_changes()` are new.
- `audit/audit.py`:
  - `triage` records the basis.
  - `open --stale` prints the decision and the change.
  - New `confirm <id>... --note`: re-affirms a decision against the current
    row, keeping its verdict, appending a dated note, and requiring a new
    `--expires` when the review date has passed.
- Corpus, doc and tree scans iterate in sorted order. The truncated reader
  lists on each row no longer depend on filesystem order: two consecutive
  scans are byte-identical except for `meta.json`.
- Migration, recorded in `audit/migrations/evidence-basis-v2.json`:
  - 2,099 decisions matched their row and were rebased.
  - 277 stale decisions went stale only through counts and were rebased.
  - 58 stay stale. Each keeps its decision-time basis, which was recovered
    from committed inventory snapshots, so it shows a diff.
  - 24 hashes could not be recovered; they are unchanged and need review.
  - Notes, verdicts and review dates are unchanged.
- The triage store grows from 2.3 MB to 3.0 MB to carry the bases.
- `audit/README.md` and `audit/agents/triage.md` describe the basis and
  `confirm`.

## Validation

- `python3 -m unittest discover -s audit/tests`: 128 OK. New tests cover:
  - counts and reader lists not aging a decision;
  - presence, quoted text and facts still aging one;
  - the diff output;
  - `confirm` rebasing a decision, and refusing a past review date.
- `python3 audit/audit.py refresh`, then `check`: `audit: ok`, with 75 stale
  and 7 expired.
- `python3 scripts/docs/check_all.py`: all 4 docs gates pass.
