# Add full-logit GDN calibration adapter

## Summary

Added a calibration-only adapter that evaluates complete full-vocabulary GDN
teacher-forced trajectories with the execution-profile numerical comparator.
The adapter keeps historical accepted/rejected labels separate from fresh
metrics, requires three identical-schedule candidate runs, and explicitly
refuses to make a performance or profile-qualification claim.

This closes an evidence-tooling gap discovered during P4: retained Laguna and
W7900 GDN summaries preserve maxima/top-1, but their raw full-logit files and
the local Laguna model are unavailable, so they cannot honestly establish row
p95/p99 by themselves.

## Changes

- Added `scripts/execution_profile_gdn_calibration.py` to capture one explicit
  strict GDN trajectory and three same-context runs of each labelled control
  over the complete prompt plus heldout suite. It runs
  `compare_profile_logits`, records full-matrix and per-prompt trajectory
  hashes, validates repeat logits/IDs, and binds each label to a hashed
  historical artifact without allowing that label to affect the verdict.
- Calibration artifacts state the missing runtime-manifest, exact-control
  telemetry, and fresh task evidence blockers. They cannot certify a route or
  publish performance.
- Added synthetic RED/GREEN coverage for full-logit evaluator integration,
  three-run determinism localization, and exact one-source-per-control binding.
