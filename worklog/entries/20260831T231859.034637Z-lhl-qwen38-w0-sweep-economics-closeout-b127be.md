# Close W0 on measured Q5/Q6 row-sweep amplification

## Summary

Closed extension W0 with durable same-host cycle, kernel-time, and actual
video-memory-fetch evidence. The all-ten C6/C8 K1 refresh passes 20/20 exact,
engaged, and candidate-budget cells. It measures **2.1069 / 2.1423 matched-AR
steps per speculative cycle**, while 1.15x requires **1.7013** at the observed
95.65% acceptance.

The separate claim-ineligible one-prompt profiler diagnostic exercised
R8/R12/R16/R20/R24/R32 in one physical group. R32/R8 family time is
**2.27x Q4 / 12.59x Q5 / 15.69x Q6**, failing W1's <=1.25x flatness gate.
The gfx1151 `FETCH_SIZE` hardware counter localizes the mechanism: R32 fetch is
**1.07x / 34.62x / 26.69x** each family's resident target bytes for Q4/Q5/Q6.
Q5 and Q6 re-sweep almost once per row; Q4 bytes are already near one sweep,
but Q4 still takes 2.27x R8 time and therefore needs an issue/row-loop fix.

Applying the written 1.25x ceiling to the measured family times gives a sized
71-cycle full-suite upper opportunity of **33.9 s at C6/R24** and **44.8 s at
C8/R32**. These bounds comfortably exceed the remaining 7.46/8.88 s K1 wall
gaps and open W1. They are mechanism-sizing bounds, not promised end-to-end
speedups.
