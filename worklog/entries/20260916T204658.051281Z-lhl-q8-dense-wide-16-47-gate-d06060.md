## The inheritance, verified rather than argued

The route A/B showed bit-identity on three cases and two processes, which made
this the expected outcome; the gate shows it on the full suite. Against
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-16-q8-wmma-dense-prefill-layers-gate/artifact-layers16-47-4cat.json`:

- `strict_logits_sha256` `550bb9b832bc7ba2…` and `candidate_logits_sha256`
  `65370710c0b9c40f…` are equal.
- Flattening both quality summaries, **one** field differs: `top1_mismatch_rows`,
  and only in the route name embedded in each row's `scenario_id`. Every KL, token
  id, margin, NLL and logit delta in those ten rows matches exactly.

So the wide kernel is a retiling of certified f16 arithmetic, and the certified
16-47 figures are inherited. Ten top-1 misses, all inside the 154-row
flip-eligible set, none outside.
