# gemma4 D11 expert decode chain wired: tiles then pack8 then incumbent

## Summary

D11's screen won 4.35x on layer 29's expert gate_up and 3.05x on its down
projection; this unit puts those winners on the production decode path. The
selected-expert dispatch (`gemma4_project_experts_selected`) now tries three
candidates in order, gated on registration and geometry only: the t16 tiles
sibling under `spec.tiles_quant_key` when the weight ships the `tiles`
allocation, the pack8 selected leaf when `out_features % 8 == 0`, then the
registered `selected_gemv` incumbent. A candidate whose key is not registered
falls through, so every quant that ships neither sibling resolves exactly the
kernel it resolved before, and D3's q5_1 logical-t64 owner — registered *as*
`selected_gemv` — is never reordered. The weight pointer follows the
candidate: the tiles sibling reads the tiles allocation, every raw candidate
reads the primary, so prefill owners stay byte-for-byte on raw.

The planner side: Q5_K expert gate_up stacks that pass `Q5_K_T16_SHAPE` now
plan raw plus a Q5T16 tiles side copy (not byte-neutral: 16 raw rows of
176-byte Q5_K blocks repack to 2880-byte tile blocks, so the copy is ~2.3%
larger than the raw rows — capacity sums both). `tiles_quant_key` names
`gguf_q5_k_t16_v1`, the registry key the decode rewrite resolves under. The
Dense Q8_0 side allocation from D9 (010904eff) is untouched.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/gemma4/gemma4_experts.py`:
  `gemma4_project_experts_selected` becomes the three-candidate chain
  (tiles -> pack8 -> incumbent) with exact-`is_registered` gating, a
  2-attempt registry-restore retry, and the allocation hint carried to
  `weight.allocation(...)`. Docstring records the screen numbers and why
  the incumbent keeps exact-registered semantics.
- `hipengine/loading/gemma4_gguf_device.py`: the Q5_K gate_up expert slot
  plans `raw + tiles` when `Q5_K_T16_SHAPE.validate` accepts the stack
  (fail-open: a rejected stack keeps raw residency alone), and
  `_planned_nbytes` counts the Q5T16 side copy at its own size.
- `tests/test_unit_gemma4_gguf_device.py`: three new tests — the chain
  preference order (tiles over pack8 over incumbent, allocation hint
  followed), the chain falling through when no sibling is registered
  (incumbent keeps every unqualified quant), and the Q5_K stack planning
  raw plus tiles with capacity counting both. 22/22 green.
- `scratch/d11_expert_chain_differential.py`: the arm-off/arm-on greedy
  differential harness with per-candidate resolution counters.
