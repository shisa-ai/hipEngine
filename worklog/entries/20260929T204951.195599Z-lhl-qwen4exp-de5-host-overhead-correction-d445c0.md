# DE-5 corrected: the 65% host/launch figure is a cross-run artifact and the sync copies are 10.2 KiB

## Summary

`DE-5`'s appended `20260929T` note concludes that decode is **not** kernel-bound - "the unprofiled
wall for the same transition is median 153.0 ms, so the kernels are only ~35% of a decode step and
**~65% is host/launch overhead**". **Both halves of that inference are unsound, and the same-host
history contradicts it.** The note has been corrected in place.

## Changes

**Ledger `DE-5` corrected; CHANGELOG entry added.** No code, kernel, dispatch or default changed.
**No new measurement was taken** - every figure is quoted from artifacts already in-tree.

## Defect 1 - the two numbers come from different runs

| figure | value | source | instrumentation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| step wall | **153.04 ms** | `decode-warmup.json` | `profile: false`, `role_markers: false` |
| kernel sum | **54.218 ms** | `decode-profiled.json` | `profile: true`, `role_markers: true` |
| *that run's own wall* | *225.79 ms* | `decode-profiled.json` | same |

The kernel sum is taken from the **profiled** run; the wall from the **unprofiled** run. The
profiled run's own wall median was **225.79 ms** (min 82.0, max 228.1) - not 153.0 ms. Subtracting a
kernel sum measured under one instrumentation state from a wall measured under another and calling
the remainder "host/launch overhead" is not a decomposition.

## Defect 2 - the same host, model and case decode ~3x faster

`benchmarks/results/2026-09-13-framework-qwen4exp-strix-journey-baselines.json`, on the **same
machine** (`host.machine_id` `55ea6c509d0b49eea8de7094a1023668`, `hostname` `gfx1151`) and the
**same model** (`unsloth-Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-UD-Q4_K_XL`, fingerprint `fb1f2fbf73d5…a88ec53`):

| arm | metric | value |
|---|---|---:|
| `he-ar` | `cases.code-p4096.decode_tok_s.median` | **19.134 tok/s** |
| `he-ar` | `shapes.4096.decode_tok_s_weighted` | **19.166 tok/s** |

**19.134 tok/s = 52.26 ms/token**, decode-only, full serving request, production defaults.

| quantity | value | ratio |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2026-09-13 hipEngine AR decode (measured) | **52.26 ms/token** | 1.00x |
| x4 profiled kernel sum | 54.218 ms/step | 1.04x |
| **x4 unprofiled step wall** | **153.04 ms/step** | **2.93x** |

**The kernel sum agrees with the independently measured step time; the wall does not.** That is the
opposite of the note's reading - the wall is the outlier, not the kernel sum. A 2.93x regression
between 09-13 and 09-29 would be a headline event, and the rollup shows only improvements in that
window (09-26 F16 staging +3.4%, 09-27 full-attention decode-context +2.11%, 09-28 four-token QK
unroll +2.65%) with **no decode regression of any size**.

## Defect 3 - the row's stated target is negligible

`DE-5`'s action is *"target the remaining synchronous copies and sampling path"*. The harness's
runtime census, **identical across all three repetitions**:

| operation | calls | bytes |
|---|---:|---:|
| `memcpy` (synchronous) | 3 | **10,440** |
| `memcpy_async` | 12 | 6,144 |
| `memset` | 1 | 16 |
| `graph_launch` | 48 | - |

**The entire synchronous-copy budget is 10.2 KiB per step.** At the documented ~221 GB/s practical
read roof that is ~47 ns; even charging ~5 us of launch cost per call, all three synchronous copies
together are ~**15 us** - **~0.03%** of a 52.26 ms step. There is no win in the row's stated target.

The sampling half is not exercised at all (`capture_logits=False`, `token_id_resident=True`).

## Defect 4 - the row already said this

`DE-5`'s **mechanism** cell, written before the appended note:

> Serving-wrapper host time, ~3.6 ms/token above the runner loop … **Layer graphs are measured
> negative: the launch host time is already overlapped**

That is the same conclusion this correction reaches, from a direct measurement, and it contradicts
the appended 65% claim. The appended note is self-inconsistent with its own row.

## Verdict

- **The "~65% is host/launch overhead" figure does not hold** and should not drive work.
- **The row's stated target is measured at 10.2 KiB / ~15 us per step (~0.03%)** - nothing to win.
- **The row's original scope stands**: the wrapper's ~3.6 ms/token (logits D2H, sampling,
  bookkeeping) = **~6.9%** of a 52.26 ms step. Real but modest, and *not* what 65% describes.
- **The harness, not the engine, needs the follow-up.** Most likely mechanism:
  `snapshot_restore_between_repeats: true` interacting with the QSA indexed-sparse path's rewindable
  index state (`Qwen4ExpQSAIndexDeviceState.append` short-circuits on `if complete <= first:
  return`), so a restore plausibly forces work the steady-state serving path does not repeat.

## Limits

**No new measurement.** All figures are quoted from pre-existing in-tree artifacts. The 09-13 decode
figure uses a different protocol from the x4 harness (full serving request vs one snapshot-restored
step; `capture_logits` on vs off; different dates, possibly different defaults in between), so the
2.93x gap is an **inconsistency requiring explanation**, not by itself proof of which number is
wrong. The supporting argument is that the x4 run's *own* kernel sum matches the independently
measured step while its wall does not, which locates the fault in the wall. The snapshot-restore
mechanism is stated as a **hypothesis and is untested**.
