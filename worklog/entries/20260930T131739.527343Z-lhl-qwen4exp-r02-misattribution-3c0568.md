# A recorded rejection the artifact contradicts: R02 passed the gates the ledger says it failed

## Summary

The ledger's section-7 row recorded the multi-column GDN variant (`R02`) as failing
"Japanese complete-EOS", citing `R01`'s evidence entry and lumping two separate candidates
together. **The `R02` artifact contradicts it outright.** `R02` passes the full 594-row
short gate (593/594 top-1) and the canonical depth gate at **every** declared limit, and
the artifact states: *"The candidate does not reproduce the single-column Japanese
speculative-decoding error."* Its actual finding is a **different prompt**
(`heldout_general_en_quant`) that the artifact itself scopes as *"not proof of worse
expected quality or a GDN kernel defect"*. **`R01` failed that task; `R02` did not.**

## Changes

- `docs/QWEN4EXP-OPTIMIZATION-LEDGER.md` section 7 - the row's evidence cell corrected,
  with both candidates' entries now cited separately.
- `docs/REFACTOR.md` - the multi-column removal premise annotated: the removal stays
  pending a gate that actually ran against `R02`.

## How the error survived

Three things had to line up, and they did:

1. **Two candidates on one row.** The row name itself - "Journey tiled/DPP (`R01`) /
   multi-column (`R02`) restorations" - invites reading them as one item.
2. **One evidence citation for both.** The cell cites `20260914T201148`, which is `R01`'s
   entry. Nothing points a reader at `2026-09-15-journey-gdn-multi-restoration`, where
   `R02`'s own result lives.
3. **A shared-looking failure phrase.** "Japanese complete-EOS" is `R01`'s failure. `R02`
   mentions Japan too - but to say it does **not** reproduce the error, and its two
   Japanese-plan responses merely hit the 2048-token cap.

So a reader following the row's own citation never reaches the artifact that contradicts
it. **The row was checkable and had not been checked.**

## Why it matters beyond bookkeeping

`docs/REFACTOR.md` carried a removal instruction whose stated trigger was "the numerical
or operation-complete performance gate rejects it". Read through this row, that trigger
looked already-fired. Read through the artifact, **it has not fired**: `R02` passed every
declared numerical limit, and its one finding is explicitly scoped by its own author as
not evidence of worse quality or a kernel defect. Removing the path on that basis would
have deleted a numerically passing candidate while citing a rejection that did not happen.

It also bears on GD-4, which faces the same gate. `R02`'s artifact states the admission
principle GD-4 needs - *"exact generated IDs are not the admission criterion"* - which is
the envelope-based contract GD-4 must be admitted under.

## Still no retained win

A corrected rejection is not a retained improvement. But it is the first row this session
whose **recorded result was wrong** rather than merely stale or unpriced, and the
correction restores a candidate to "unevaluated" instead of "rejected".
