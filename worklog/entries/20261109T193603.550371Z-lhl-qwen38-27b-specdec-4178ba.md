# Rowtile cliff root-cause correction: code_lru_cache divergence already closed; caps are deliberate ownership

## Summary

Task #33 framed the `_PACK8_ROWTILE_MAX_ROWS=4` cliff as an un-root-caused AR divergence on `code_lru_cache`. Investigation corrects that premise: the divergence was **already root-caused and closed** on 2026-08-30 (`worklog/entries/20260830T024414.189176Z-lhl-qwen38-dflash-row8-739887.md`). The root-cause class was *over-broad dispatch admission without complete layout-qualified verifier ownership* — not rowtile arithmetic or a state-commit failure. The current 4-row caps are deliberate fail-closed ownership boundaries, not bugs to lift blindly.
