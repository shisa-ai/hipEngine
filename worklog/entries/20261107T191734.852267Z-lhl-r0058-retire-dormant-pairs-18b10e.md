# Retire dormant ordered-pair Q3/IQ3/Q6 fused owners (R0058)

## Summary

Lead decision (2026-11-07): retire failed candidates rather than keep them as
repair campaigns. R0058's four ordered-pair `linear_pair_silu` owners were
dormant: dispatch declines them at every row count, because their production-shape
screens ran 0.97-0.99x (IQ3_S/IQ4) and 0.60-0.61x (Q5/Q6) against the
pre-registered >=1.00x gate. The Q3_K/IQ4_XS and IQ4_XS/Q3_K owners had no strict
Q3/Q3 side-A owner to complete them. All four are removed.

Removed keys: `gguf_q3_k+gguf_iq4_xs`, `gguf_iq4_xs+gguf_q3_k`,
`gguf_iq3_s+gguf_iq4_xs`, `gguf_q5_k_t16_v1+gguf_q6_k_t16_qmicro_planar_v1`.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/fused/gguf_iq4_q4_pair.hip`: removed the four

  emulation, B_KIND 3 strict IQ3_S emulation, A_KIND 3 Q6 planar chain), the IQ3_S

  pairs, plus every strict single and unfused chain.
