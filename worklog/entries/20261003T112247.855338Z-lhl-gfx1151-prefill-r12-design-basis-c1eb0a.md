# R12 prefill port design basis: the MMQ premise is refuted, the expert legs are the gap

## Summary

Task #12 was scoped as "extend the R12 dense-Q8 MMQ tiling/occupancy work to the
H2048-MoE prefill shapes". Measurement refutes the premise: **the shipping route
does not use MMQ at all**, and the MMQ family is **~3x slower** than the t16 WMMA
family we do ship, so porting R12's MMQ tiling work here would be a regression,
not a win. R12's *diagnosis* (tiling/occupancy/staging, not the arithmetic
family) survives and is confirmed — it just applies to the **selected-expert
legs**, which run at 8.51 TFLOP/s against 22.22 TFLOP/s for the dense t16 legs
and are 36% of prefill kernel time.

## What was measured

### The shipping owner is t16 WMMA, not MMQ

`q8_mmq_prefill_policy` is `None` for `gguf_ud_q4_k_m` (only `gguf_ud_q3_k_m`
and `gguf_ud_q4_k_xl` register one). All four dense shapes dispatch to
`KernelKey(hip_gfx1100, linear, gguf_q8_0_t16_v1, t16_wmma_prefill_bf16_bf16_out)`
with the gfx1151 override `gguf_q8_0_t16_wmma_prefill_auto_4wave_bf16_bf16_out`.
No MMQ kernel appears anywhere in the shipping prefill profile.

### Isolated rates at the real geometry (M=1024, one chunk)

| geometry | K, N | t16 WMMA TF/s | MMQ TF/s | ratio |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `attn_qkv` | 2048, 8192 | **23.82** | 7.59 | 3.14x |
| `attn_gate` | 2048, 4096 | **21.13** | 7.07 | 2.99x |
| `ssm_out` | 4096, 2048 | **20.20** | 6.40 | 3.16x |
| `lm_head` | 2048, 248320 | 12.34 | 6.90 | 1.79x |

### The wide-N penalty is refuted at our shapes

R12's sharpest clue was wide-N `attn_qkv` running 1.5x slower than `attn_q` at
the same K. That does not reproduce here: at K=2048, **N=8192 is 12.7% faster per
FLOP than N=4096** in the t16 family (23.82 vs 21.13) and 7.2% faster in MMQ
(7.59 vs 7.07). The wide-N penalty is therefore not a general property of these
shapes and cannot be the mechanism behind the R12 observation.

There *is* a cliff, but far outside the shipping envelope: N=16384 21.76 TF/s,
N=32768 12.56, N=65536 12.19. It is unreachable in the real route — **no lm_head
GEMM exists in the prefill profile**, because prefill needs logits only for the
final position. The largest GEMM `grid.x` observed is 8192 (`attn_qkv`), so the
cliff is a synthetic-microbench finding, not an addressable target.

### R12's occupancy diagnosis is confirmed — for MMQ

| kernel | threads | VGPR | LDS | blocks/CU | wave occupancy |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `q8_0_raw_mmq_tile_q8_1_d4_kernel<128,128,false,3>` | 256 | 184 | **57856** | **1** | **25%** |
| `gguf_q8_0_t16_prefill_wmma_nwave_kernel<32,4>` | 128 | 80 | 1024 | 8 | **100%** |

MMQ is limited to one block per CU by 56.5 KB of LDS. The t16 `nwave` kernel has
100% wave occupancy and is 3x faster. R12's conclusion was right; the family it
was drawn from is simply not ours.

## The actual gap: the selected-expert legs

Shipping-route profile, 4096-token prefill at chunk 1024, kernel sum 2755.6 ms
against a 2802.6 ms wall (**98.3% kernel time**), 5432 dispatches:

| kernel | dispatches | ms | % | block | LDS | TF/s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `gguf_q4_k_t16_selected_dual_wmma_prefill_compact32` | 160 | 594.0 | 21.6% | 32 | **0** | 9.49 |
| `gguf_q8_0_t16_prefill_wmma_nwave` | 440 | 476.3 | 17.3% | 128 | 1024 | **22.22** |
| `qwen35_gdn_prefill_recurrent_decode_order_exact_segm` | 120 | 456.9 | 16.6% | 32 | — | n/a |
| `gguf_k_t16_selected_wmma_prefill_compact` | 148+12 | 399.4 | 14.5% | 32 | **0** | 7.05 |
| `gguf_q8_0_t16_prefill_wmma` | 560 | 171.0 | 6.2% | 32 | 0 | — |
| `attn_fwd` (AOTriton) | 40 | 142.7 | 5.2% | 128 | — | n/a |
| `qwen35_router_logits_token_tile` | 320 | 123.2 | 4.5% | 256 | — | n/a |

FLOP accounting for the dense t16 legs at 4 chunks x 1024 rows
(`attn_qkv` 41 layers N=8192 K=2048, `attn_gate` 30 layers N=4096 K=2048,
`ssm_out` 42 layers N=2048 K=4096):

- dense Q8: **10582.8 GFLOP / 476.25 ms = 22.22 TFLOP/s** — reproduces the
  isolated microbench rate, so the microbench is representative.
- expert gate+up (41 layers, 1024x8 assignments, N=512 K=2048, gate+up):
  5635.0 GFLOP / 594.02 ms = **9.49 TFLOP/s**.
- expert down (41 layers, N=2048 K=512): 2817.5 GFLOP / 399.38 ms =
  **7.05 TFLOP/s**.
- experts combined: **8452.5 GFLOP / 993.40 ms = 8.51 TFLOP/s**.

**The expert legs are 2.61x less efficient than the dense legs and are 36.0% of
prefill kernel time.** Structurally they are one-wave workgroups
(`block=[32,1,1]`, exactly 1 wave) with **LDS = 0**, i.e. no shared-memory
staging of the activation plane, launched as `grid=[1024, ~512-586]` — the same
one-wave/no-reuse shape that the `nwave` design already fixed on the dense side.

## Pre-declared target (campaign rule: no ladder before target rates)

Same protocol as the baseline: `scripts/qwen35_gguf_bench.py`, 4096-token
prefill, `--public-ar-profile`, chunk 1024, gfx1151, 3 measured runs.

- **Baseline (confirmed this session):** 1458.4 tok/s median (2808.6 ms),
  -0.15% against the committed 1460.5.
- **Primary target: >= 1600 tok/s (+10.0%)**, which requires the combined expert
  legs to reach **>= 11.3 TFLOP/s from 8.51** (a 1.32x gain), i.e. to cut
  242.6 ms from the 993.4 ms expert total.
- **Stretch: >= 1870 tok/s (+28%)**, if the expert legs reach the dense legs'
  22.22 TFLOP/s (380.7 ms, saving 612.7 ms).
- **Retainable only if** the change is exact or passes the production-profile
  gate, keeps a registered strict fallback, does not regress any non-expert leg,
  and the complete-prefill throughput improves on the identical protocol.
- **Falsified if** the expert legs cannot be moved off the one-wave/LDS-0 shape
  without a numerical change that fails the gate, in which case this entry's
  measurement stands and the ladder closes as a documented negative.

## Changes

- No code change. Design basis and pre-declared target only.

## Validation

- Profile: `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` over a 4096-token prefill at chunk 1024
  with `--require-cached-build` and a precomputed compiler-version file, so the
  profiled process spawned no `hipcc`. Kernel sum 2755.6 ms / wall 2802.6 ms =
  98.3%.
- Throughput: `scripts/qwen35_gguf_bench.py --prompt-length 4096
  --decode-tokens 0 --public-ar-profile --prefill-linear-chunk-size 1024
  --prefill-moe-chunk-size 1024`, 3 measured runs, median 1458.4 tok/s.
- The dispatch owner was read from the dispatch entry point, not guessed, and
  the t16 rates reproduce between the isolated microbench and the profile FLOP
  accounting (22.20 vs 22.22 TFLOP/s), which is the cross-check that the
  microbench geometry is representative.
- Limits: one host (gfx1151), one model, one quant, one prompt length, M=1024
  chunks. Microbench weights are synthetic raw Q8_0, so absolute microbench
  rates can differ slightly from the real tensors; the profile FLOP accounting
  above is the real-tensor number and agrees. The N>=32768 cliff is a
  microbench finding with no shipping counterpart.
