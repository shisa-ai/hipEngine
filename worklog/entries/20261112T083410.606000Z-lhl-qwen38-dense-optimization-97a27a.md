Optimize the corrected Qwen3.8 dense path from `ff1c5ed41`, starting with
mixed IQ4_XS/Q4_K gate/up SiLU row-loop removal. Use the available gfx1151
Radeon 8060S host and Qwen3.8-27B-UD-Q4_K_M artifact. Keep the scalar
arithmetic contract and do not change the scalar reference.
