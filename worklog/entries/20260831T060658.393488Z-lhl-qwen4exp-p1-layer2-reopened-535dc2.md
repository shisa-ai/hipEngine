# Reopen Qwen4Exp P1 layer-2 grouped route

## Summary

Reopened qualification of the existing layer-2 grouped Q5_K WMMA route. The
previous five-pair ~1% regression had no retained raw artifact/order log and is
not reproducible under the durable same-process counterbalanced harness. A
fresh role trace shows that the intended kernel runs and removes the measured
Q5_K miss; p508 and every canonical p512 category gain about 5% with low
variance. The candidate remains default-off because it changes logits and has
not passed the complete current production-profile gate.
