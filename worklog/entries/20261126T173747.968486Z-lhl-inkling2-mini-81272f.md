# Take the wide F16 dense tile off the production plan

## Summary

The clean-tree `production` gate at `d86dc9358`, where `production` read the dense Q8_0
projections of prompt blocks through `dense_wide64x128_f32_f32_out`, failed on one category.
The long-context prompts missed the median: a KL of 0.00138 against a limit of 0.001 over 147
rows. Their 90th percentile was 0.0133 against 0.020, their top-1 agreement 0.966 against 0.96 and
their flip damage 0.0068 against 0.03. Every other category passed with a median of 0. The global
figures passed: median 7.6e-13, 90th percentile 0.0028, top-1 agreement 0.990, flip damage 0.0044.
Repeat determinism, request isolation (25 of 25 bit-identical) and task quality (9 of 9 correct
under both, 12 of 12 generated sequences identical) passed. Before the tile, at `4211bf4f8`, the
same category's 90th percentile was 0.0045 with a top-1 agreement of 0.993.

So the tile's arithmetic (both operands F16, 2.9e-4 of the RMS on a projection) costs about three
times the divergence on long prompts, and `production` selects the exact coltile kernel again on
both scopes. `fast` keeps the tile.

The same clean tree's decode bench, with the tile on the default path, gave a first token at 0.423 s
for 575 prompt tokens and 1.590 s for 2,057 (the exact kernel: 0.645 and 2.383 s at
`4211bf4f8`), and the 128-token greedy output after 575 prompt tokens differed from the exact
kernel's. The `fast` arm of that run fails `code` as it did before, and `long_context` at a median
of 0.00161.

## Changes

- `Inkling2GGUFGenerator` passes `wide_dense` for `fast` only. It names the tile from now on.
- The two `linear` scopes select the exact kernel under `production`; `fast` keeps the tile with the
  exact kernel as its fallback.

## Validation

- The Inkling unit tests over the plan: 239 pass.
- The default-route GPU test records the tile absent under the omitted and the explicit
  `production` profile and present under `fast`: 9 GPU tests pass with the model present.

  the exact kernel's at about three times the tile's time, which stays well under the exact
  kernel's; it takes the gate again.
