# Screen gfx1151 residual Q8_1x2 dp4a decode

## Summary

Retain primary-plus-residual Q8_1 activation packing and same-resident Q4T16
dp4a gate/up+SiLU for Qwen3.8-27B rows1 on gfx1151. The actual-weight family
improves 1.03427x and projects 1.2495% of the complete eager 512/128 wall.
Production graph AR improves at every required shape, and the complete natural
AR/B1-B3 suite is exact against both retained controls. No persistent weight
sidecar or tracked-peak increase is introduced; exact local32 projections plus
primitive SiLU remain the policy-miss fallback.

This is retained development evidence, not a clean publication topline. P5
remains open because current AR still trails clean llama.cpp Vulkan.

## Changes

  approximation; plane 1 quantizes the residual left by the exact FP16 scale
  reconstruction consumed by dp4a.
- Add selected and dense same-T16 dual dp4a+SiLU consumers which replay both
  activation planes while each Q4 payload is live.
- Register one model/backend/quant/shape-qualified rows1 owner only for the
  H5120 -> FFN17408 dense gate/up pair. Rows2+, other roles/geometries, and peer
  backends retain exact owners.
- Reuse bounded decode scratch for the 11,520-byte rows1 two-plane workspace;
  add fail-closed workspace, dispatch, backend-policy, and quality coverage.

  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-15-gfx1151-qwen38-27b-q4-q8x2-dp4a.json`.
