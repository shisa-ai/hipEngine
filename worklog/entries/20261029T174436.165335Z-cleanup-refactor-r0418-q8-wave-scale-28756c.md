# Scope Q8 wave-scale prefill to its runner

## Summary

R0418 is complete. The profile publishes `q8_wave_scale_prefill` on its
generator and runner, production True and strict False. Late clones inherit
it. The retired environment selector is ignored. Kernel arithmetic, registry
fallback, other raw geometries, MMQ/WMMA precedence and decode are unchanged.
This is a control migration, not an arithmetic promotion or performance claim.

## Changes

- The chunked-prefill owner uses its field when selecting the
  `coltile8_wave_scale` raw geometry. Its per-chunk context still restores the
  caller's geometry before hidden-seed consumers or the final single-row head.
  No new batch-decode, mixed-verifier or scalar-decode binding was added.
- Add exception-safe `q8_wave_scale_prefill(runner, enabled)` diagnostic scope.
  Campaign and state-gate arms bind their runner and record the field rather
  than publishing the process key. Both hold raw coltile8/rowbatch32, close
  MMQ/WMMA and disable the downstream DPP successor. The campaign previously
  counted the grandparent even though dispatch prefers the pipelined leaf;
  both diagnostics now name/count that existing pipelined leaf. Forced
  controls are recorded and restored. Historical campaign results are not
  reinterpreted by this correction.
- Preserve journey-localize's former strict Q8 environment-family overrides
  explicitly, including split prefill/decode arms. The historical dense
  family prefix list does not include Q8 wave scale; do not silently broaden
  that experiment while migrating its controls.
- The original registered coltile parent and wave-scale implementation remain.
  The old ledger recorded admission under symmetric device-fill conditioning,
  and adverse unconditioned attention-gate timing reversals by order. Those
  caveats still apply to that historical evidence. No new timing result is
  claimed, and no SGPR instruction-count claim is made without generated-code
  inspection. Existing registered successors and strict fallback are intact.
- Retire the ENVS entry and remove the completed ledger unit; each original
  requirement is mapped in the sweep manifest. Unrelated dirty audit decisions
  remain untouched and unstaged.
