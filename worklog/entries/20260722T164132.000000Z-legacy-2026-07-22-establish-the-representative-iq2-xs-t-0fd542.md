Fused decode is BF16-bit exact to selected-single+SiLU over 10,240 elements; rowbatch4 is exact to grouped scalar over 327,680 elements. The unconditional K3072 policy is therefore not generally optimal; adaptive work remains Task #25 rather than being folded into this baseline unit.
