# Share the Inkling decode head across resident rows

## Summary

Multiple resident decode rows now share the packed Q8_0 vocabulary projection. Attention, convolution, expert projections, output capping, and sampling stay independent per request. The shared projection uses only the head's existing packed padding and never exposes padded token IDs.

## Changes

- Delay the vocabulary projection only for simultaneously stepping rows. Leave prefill, the first draw from prefill logits, single-row decode, and verification on their existing routes. Reuse the scalar head's uploaded backing, including the small physical tail required by the registered four-row tile. Use serial projection when the tensor cannot supply the aligned packed width.
