# Interleave Inkling expert wave-row partial sums

## Summary

Interleave the four independent partial sums in the wave-row expert kernel. Preserve the order of each fused multiply-add chain and the original reduction tree.

## Changes

- Process full tiles together and guard the tail by column. Keep the singleton projection as the bitwise oracle for NVFP4 and MXFP8 grouped launches.
