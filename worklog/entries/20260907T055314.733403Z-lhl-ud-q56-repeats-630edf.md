# Record UD Q5 Q6 fresh-process repeat gate

## Summary

Both published UD K_M and K_S Q5/Q6 compact candidates produce byte-identical
full logits across three fresh-process captures on zbook / gfx1151. This closes
same-schedule short-context repeatability only. Defaults are unchanged.

  `benchmarks/results/2026-09-07-zbook-ud-c1-residency-logits-diagnostic.json`
