# Retain record-owned Q6 F16 producer

## Summary

Replaced the admitted planar-Q6 source-F16 composite's scalar column-owned
producer with an exact record-owned tile producer. One 256-thread workgroup owns
each K256xN16 tile block; each thread reads one contiguous 12-byte qmicro record
once and emits its K4xN4 values. D/scales use 288 bytes of LDS. The previous
producer remains registered as the exact reference/fallback primitive.

## Changes

- Added the raw-pointer record-owned HIP leaf and registry wrapper.
- Promoted it only inside the existing Q6 T16 source-F16 composite.
- Added RED-first exact source-F16 and production-composite ownership tests.
- Added compact evidence in
  `benchmarks/results/2026-08-13-qwen36-27b-q6-direct-f16-producer-retained.json`.
- Added no persistent or scratch bytes and changed no model/tile policy,
  rocBLAS solution, arithmetic, decode/MTP owner, peer backend, or fallback.

## Validation

- Actual FFN-down, linear-QKV, and full-attention-V weights on XTX and W7900:
  **66/66 leaf pairs win**, byte exact, at **2.21-2.67x** producer speedup.
- Producer + production rocBLAS + BF16 output, all three roles at
  M512/M1024/M4096 on both boards: **18/18 median wins**, exact final BF16,
  **139/142 paired wins**, and **1.012-1.255x** complete-chain speedups.
- The initially noisy M4096 attn-V boundary was repeated burst-10: **1.032x
  (14/15)** on XTX and **1.034x (15/15)** on W7900.
- Changed kernel file: **15 passed**. Dispatch, scratch-liveness, and runner
  safeguards: **103 passed, 9 skipped**. Combined: **118 passed, 9 skipped**.
