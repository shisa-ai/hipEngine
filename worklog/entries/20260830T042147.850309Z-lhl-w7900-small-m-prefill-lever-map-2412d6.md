# W7900 Qwen3.8: no runtime lever exists for small-M prefill GEMM

## Summary

Task #12 cannot be finished by tuning. I swept the one control that looked reachable -
`HIPENGINE_GGUF_Q6_K_DENSE_WMMA_TILE` over all six allowed tiles at the fixed-cost shape - and
the sweep turned out to be **vacuous**: that env belongs to the `gguf_q6_k_wmma_prefill_*`
wrappers in `gguf_q4_k_prefill.py`, while the kernel the 45-row prefill actually runs is
`q6_k_t16_qmicro_planar_wmma_prefill_bf16`, launched from `gguf_q6_k_t16_gemv.py`, whose
rowtile/col8/qmicro variants take **no tile parameter at all**. Six arms agreeing to 1.6% is
not a null result about tiles; it is a null result about an unused knob. Combined with the two
other routes, there is no runtime control over small-M dense prefill on this host today.

## Changes

- `scripts/gguf_first_token_probe.py`: `--length-scan` now measures each length
  `--requests` times and reports the **median with its spread**, because the single-shot walls
  repeat at +-15% at short lengths - enough to move a fitted fixed term by tens of
  milliseconds. The existing six tests still pass unchanged.

## Validation

Tile sweep at the fixed-cost shape (fit printed per arm, small-row regime):

| `Q6_K_DENSE_WMMA_TILE` | fitted fixed | ms/token |
| --- | --- | --- |
| default (64x16) | 314.7 | 0.30 |
| 16x16 | 312.3 | 0.31 |
| 16x32 | 314.4 | 0.30 |
| 32x16 | 314.2 | 0.30 |
| 32x32 | 317.2 | 0.28 |
| 64x32 | 314.5 | 0.31 |

Two independent reasons to distrust this as evidence about tiles: (1) each arm was a single
request per length - `--requests` did not apply to the scan until this commit, so the 1.6%
spread is single-sample noise, and I quoted it too confidently in conversation; (2) the inertness
check at shapes where a tile *must* matter shows the override is not even on the live path -
285 rows: 0.7466 s default vs 0.7139 s at 16x16, inside the spread of a shape the older single-shot
scan measured at 0.7124 s; 665 rows: 1.1838 vs 1.1917 s, 0.7% apart. A 16x tile-count change at
665 rows moving nothing by 0.7% means the knob never reached the kernel.

What the levers actually are, from the code:

- Hot kernels `q6_k_t16_qmicro_planar_wmma_prefill_bf16` and the `q4/q5_t16_dense_*_wmma_prefill`
  families are fixed-shape rowtile launches: no `tile_m`/`tile_n` parameter, no env. Their
  `_default_tiles(rows, out_features)` heuristic already yields 32x32 at rows >= 32.
- The alternative source-F16 rocBLAS route is gated by
  `_gguf_t16_f16_rocblas_prefill_policy` (`qwen35_gguf_runner.py:11224`), which refuses MoE
  models outright and otherwise needs an entry in the backend package's
  `GGUF_DENSE_T16_F16_ROCBLAS_PREFILL_POLICIES`. Its per-shape row buckets start at 512 and
  `GGUF_T16_F16_ROCBLAS_MAX_ROWS_BY_QUANT_SHAPE` records explicit losing-shape ceilings, so at
  M=45 this route is out of play **by design**, not by omission.
- The all-GEMV arm is unreachable: `qwen35_gguf.py:1107` and `:1180` pass
  `use_wmma_prefill=True` literally into `_acquire_shared_session`, and the env var is opt-in
  only. Tracked in `docs/REFACTOR.md`.

Consequence, stated plainly: closing a 6x-15x roofline gap at M=45 needs **new kernel work** (a
small-M variant of the planar/rowtile family, or measured extension of the F16-rocBLAS admission
below 512 rows), not a parameter sweep. The admission-extension experiment is still the cheapest
way to learn whether a different engine at 45 rows is even competitive, but it changes arithmetic
(F16 dequant path), so it is a diagnostic measurement and must be labelled as such, never shipped
on the strength of a wall.
