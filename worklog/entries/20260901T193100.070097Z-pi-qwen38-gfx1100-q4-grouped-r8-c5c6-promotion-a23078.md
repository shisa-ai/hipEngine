# Promote grouped Q4 R8 for physical C5-C6

## Summary

Promoted the BF16-bit-exact grouped-Q4 R8 primitive only for physical R24
verifier packets with five or six live target slots.  This is the bounded
requalification of the all-width R8 policy rejected in
`benchmarks/results/2026-09-02-w7900-q4km-k3-q4-grouped-rows8-rejected.json`:
C7/C8 and all scope/row/shape misses remain on grouped R6.  On W7900, the
current-source marker gate proves candidate ownership and positive target/device
sub-windows at C5/C6; the counterbalanced ten-prompt category+heldout gate then
wins every cell and slice with exact IDs and acceptance.

## Changes

- Added `GGUF_Q4_T16_GROUPED_ROWS8_C5C6_POLICY` to gfx1100 capability metadata
  and made it the production default only for active slots `{5, 6}` at R24.
- Kept `HIPENGINE_GGUF_Q4_T16_GROUPED_ROWS8_C5C6=0` as explicit same-build
  grouped-R6 rollback; C7/C8, strict, inactive scope, and misses are unchanged.
- Updated the focused default/rollback selector test, kernel catalog, refactor
  ledger, retained benchmark artifact/rollup, and benchmark changelog.
