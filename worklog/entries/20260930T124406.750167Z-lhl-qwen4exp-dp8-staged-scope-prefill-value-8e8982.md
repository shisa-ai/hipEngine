# DP-8's staged 13-15 scope is worth 0.6% of prefill, measured

## Summary

DP-8's composition (staged f32 on layers 13-15, the wide route on 16-47) passes
every hard gate, and its **value** was the last thing in the row still estimated:
`3 layers x 8.985 ms per layer-step x 4 chunks of 1024 rows = ~0.66% of prefill`.
That is the derivation the source artifact's own `extrapolation_warning`
forbids, so this unit measured the share instead.

**Result: ~90 ms, 0.6% of the 15742 ms X-3 prefill anchor.** Two independent runs
of one protocol (4096-token prompt, chunk 1024, production profile, 2 warmups +
9 repetitions per arm, alternating arm order, both arms on the shipping
configuration and differing by exactly one selector):

| estimator | samples | value | share of anchor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| both-fast regime, mean | 7 | **-96.1 ms** (SE 13.5) | **0.61%** |
| both-fast regime, median | 7 | -92.7 ms | 0.59% |
| pooled median | 18 | -89.5 ms (95% CI [-171.1, -56.0]) | 0.57% |
| run 1 median / median-of-arms | 9 | -114.0 / -145.5 ms | 0.72% / 0.92% |
| run 2 median / median-of-arms | 9 | -72.5 / -62.8 ms | 0.46% / 0.40% |

**17 of 18 paired differences are negative and all 7 same-regime fast samples
are negative.** The retained figure is ~90 ms / 0.6%, estimator spread 63-146 ms.

**The estimate was right, and that is the least interesting thing about it.** The
ledger's 107.8 ms sits inside the measured range, so the forbidden extrapolation
was accurate to within the measurement's own spread - but it rested on the same
4-chunk multiplicity the warning declares unestablished, so what this unit
supplies is a direct value that replaces the derivation rather than confirming
it. The warning's own inconsistency is untouched: 38.906 ms per layer-step x 48
layers x 4 passes = 7469.9 ms against the 4553.007 ms family anchor, a 1.64x
mismatch this measurement does not localize.

**Engagement is exact, and it is what makes the timing interpretable.** Both runs
report the identical census: the staged variant launched **900x in the candidate
arm and 0x in the baseline**, the exact-chain leaf fell **4824 -> 3924** with the
delta **exactly** the staged count, the staged launches are on layers **13, 14
and 15 only** (288 / 288 / 324), and the two variants the selector cannot claim
are identical across arms (`f32_hidden_f32_out` 8640 / 8640,
`pack8_gemv_f32_f32_out` 27 / 27). That is a one-for-one replacement of one leaf
with nothing else re-dispatched, which is a stronger statement than "the route
engaged".

## The instrument that had to be fixed first

The first engagement counter wrapped `resolve_gguf_linear_dispatch`, which is
*inside* the memoization it was meant to observe. `launch_gguf_linear` caches the
whole selector chain - including the staged-f32 rewrite - in
`_DISPATCH_RESOLVE_CACHE`, the warmups fill that cache for both arms, and the
measured repetitions therefore never call the resolver. It read `{}` for both
arms and the harness refused with "the staged variant was not resolved", which
reads as "the route did not run". It had run.

The counter now wraps the cached kernel callable, which `launch_gguf_linear`
invokes once per launch, so the dispatch chain and the timed region are
untouched - which matters because the quantity measured is 0.15% of prefill. A
dispatch-cache entry created inside a timed arm refuses as an incomplete census
rather than reading as a clean zero, and a malformed entry raises. Fixed and
pinned in the preceding unit
(`20260930T123020` / `tests/test_unit_qwen4exp_dp8_staged_scope_launch_counter.py`,
10 tests).

## A confound both runs share, reported rather than smoothed

**The host stepped its level mid-run in both runs**: baseline repetitions 0-3
median 15.13 s, repetitions 4-8 median 17.83 s, **+18%**. It is not an arm
effect - both arms move together - but it is large enough to dominate any
unpaired statistic, which is why the arm medians disagree (145.5 vs 62.8 ms)
while the same-regime paired statistic does not (104.7 vs 84.7 ms). The two
samples that straddle the step are reported rather than dropped: run 1
repetition 6 (-1106.8 ms, a transient inside the slow regime) and run 2
repetition 3 (-2317.9 ms, where the step landed *between* the arms - candidate
fast at 14.89 s, baseline slow at 17.20 s). They are the reason a median is
quoted instead of a mean. No cause was identified for the step; it reproduced in
both runs and no competing GPU process was visible.

## What this does not establish

- **Not a promotion.** No default, flag or dispatch changed. The composition
  still carries `eligible_for_automatic_admission: false` (2 rows over the 0.02
  KL review boundary) and five open `qualification_blockers`.
- **Not a correctness claim.** This harness measures wall time. The staged
  tile's arithmetic is qualified by the composition gate, and the identical
  `token_id` in both arms is weak evidence because the prompt is one repeated
  token.
- **Not a decode or serving claim**, and not a claim about any other prompt,
  shape, model, quant or profile. One host: `gfx1151`, machine-id
  `55ea6c509d0b49eea8de7094a1023668`.
- **Not the route's whole value.** 13-15 is the deepest passing scope. 16-47
  also passes numerically but is 5-10x slower than the wide route already
  promoted there, so it has no marginal value; the 0-15 region, where the exact
  chain still runs and the value would be large, fails the envelope.

## Validation

- Engagement asserted before any timing was read, and asserted per arm and per
  layer rather than in aggregate: 900 / 0 launches, the exact-chain delta equal
  to the staged count, the staged launches confined to the three selected layers,
  and both unaffected variants identical across arms.
- `uncounted_cache_entries_by_arm` is 0 in both arms and both runs, so no launch
  escaped the counter.
- Both runs' artifacts read directly for every number quoted, rather than from
  the console summary: the console prints the median-of-arms figure, which is the
  estimator the level step most distorts.
- The launch census was cross-checked against the source microbenchmark
  (`benchmarks/results/2026-09-29-dp8-staged-f32-dense-family/family.json`),
  whose `total.leaf_mean_ms` is the per-layer-step total over the six tensors -
  each tensor's `leaf_mean_ms` is already a mean over five layers, not a sum.
  Reading it as a five-layer total would have made the estimate 5x wrong in the
  other direction and is worth recording as a trap.
- `benchmarks/README.md` deliberately not updated: this is the measured value of
  a default-off candidate, not a change to a shipped result, so there is no
  topline row to move.
