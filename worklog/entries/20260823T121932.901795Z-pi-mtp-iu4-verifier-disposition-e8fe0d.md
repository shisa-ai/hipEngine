# Clarify IU4 MTP verifier impact and next steps

## Summary

Closed the documentation ambiguity around whether IU4 currently accelerates the
exact Qwen3.8 B3 MTP verifier. No retained product does: the Kairic PFS admits
M96–M2048, while B3 uses M2–M4 and therefore falls back exactly. The rebuilt
research U4/S4 DOT8 leaf does make M4 gate/up 1.230x faster, but that is not IU4
WMMA and projects only about 1.062x complete B3 before natural-suite tails,
resident bytes, and T3 quality are charged.

The current re-Q4 tiny-M sidecar and immutable Kairic PFS remain non-promotable.
The former has an M2 loss and no model-level quality packet; the latter fails
both Q4_K_S and matched-authority distribution gates.
