# Sustained D128 numerical failure blocks the native-product baseline

## Summary

The approved measurement controller stopped on the first TP2 sustained
correctness failure, **before launching any native-product timing worker**.
All capacity-200 paths passed; both resident TP1 controls passed full D128
repeatability/lifecycle checks. TP2 then exceeded the unchanged absolute KL
ceiling on `mixed_ja_en_translate`, decode index **82**, absolute input position
**146**: **KL 0.10840648254320791 > 0.05**. All logits were finite and top-1
agreed (`248046`). This is a distribution failure, not a token-ID-equality
requirement or evidence of a corrupted GPU context.

One bounded localization used a fresh resident session and the identical
prompt/forced prefix/graph horizon. Normal bulk prefill reproduced the saved
teacher row **bit-exactly**. Switching **only prefill** to token-serial produced
KL **0.2992785432102255** against the bulk result and **0.02837899409137805**
against TP2. This establishes strong sensitivity to the prefill schedule at the
failing continuation, but does not identify a single faulty kernel or qualify
serial prefill as a replacement product denominator.

**No per-GPU product rates, TP2 rates/spreads, or speed ratios exist for this
unit.** None are inferred from untimed quality runs, warmup durations, older
unqualified timing denominators, or the short 903-position probe. No default
promotion or ranked performance optimization is justified before this blocker
is resolved.
