## Changes


- Added prompt-file support to `scripts/qwen35_gguf_int8_kv_correctness.py` so hipEngine can run BF16-vs-INT8 KV checks on the same generated corpus text used by the llama.cpp `llama-perplexity` comparison, and fixed `scripts/llamacpp_q8_kv_quality_sweep.py` to parse one-chunk KL tables that do not print a mean footer.
- Matched sweep context: W7900/GPU0, `/models/gguf/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q4_K_M.gguf`, generated corpus `/tmp/hipengine-final-matched-int8kv-20260624/generated-corpus.txt` (hipEngine tokenizer saw 47,345 tokens), cached ROCm 7.13 compiler-version file, `HIPENGINE_GGUF_DECODE_REPACK=1`; hipEngine pure run used `HIPENGINE_GGUF_INT8_KV_ALLOW_UNVERIFIED_LONG=1`, `--max-sequence-length 131202`, `--kv-scale-dtype fp32`, and `--require-no-bf16-mirror`.
- llama.cpp ROCm `q8_0` K+V (`scripts/llamacpp_q8_kv_quality_sweep.py --contexts 128,512,4K --reference-cache-type-k/v f16 --candidate-cache-type-k/v q8_0 --no-warmup`) fails all hipEngine-like rows: `128` KL `0.09076`, same-top `0.98413`; `512` KL `0.909704`, same-top `0.83922`; `4K` KL `1.424879`, same-top `0.84563`.
- hipEngine pure per-token/head FP32 INT8 on the same corpus is mixed at short contexts but strong at 4K: `128/1` fails KL (`0.226528`, top-1 `1.0`); `512/1` fails top-1 (`KL=0.025637`, top-1 `0.5`); `4K/1` passes (`KL=0.001260`, top-1 `1.0`); extra `4K/16` passes (`KL=0.007812`, top-1 `0.941176`). All hipEngine candidate rows had zero BF16 mirrors and all 10 full-attention layers INT8.
- Decision: do not expose a general pure GGUF INT8 KV option yet. Do not give up: the matched 4K/16 result is good enough to justify a broader long-context relaxed-mode prompt/decode suite, but short-context behavior and metric differences (llama.cpp corpus KL/same-top-p vs hipEngine prefill/decode logits) keep this diagnostic-only for now. Added artifact `benchmarks/results/2026-06-24-w7900-gguf-q4km-matched-int8kv-quality-sweep.json` and updated `docs/GGUF.md`, `docs/TUNING-gguf.md`, `benchmarks/README.md`, and `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`.
