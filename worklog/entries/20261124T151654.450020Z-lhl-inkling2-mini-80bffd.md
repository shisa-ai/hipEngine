# Transport timing replicated on gfx1100

## Summary

Replicated the `--transports aql pm4` comparison three times (1,300-node chain,
50 replays each, same epyc/W7900 host):

| rep | eager | hipgraph | aql | pm4 |
|-----|-------|----------|-----|-----|
| 1   | 4.45  | 3.00     | 3.46| 1.29|
| 2   | 3.88  | 3.70     | 4.46| 1.54|
| 3   | 5.60  | 5.60     | 6.13| 1.51|

(all us per launch)

pm4 is the fastest submission path in every rep (~1.3-1.5 us/launch,
~2.2-3.6x faster than hipgraph replay and ~3-4x faster than eager). aql is
never faster than hipgraph. The earlier single-run pm4 result was not a
flake, but the eager-vs-hipgraph relationship is bimodal across runs
(parity vs ~1.5x), likely clock/power-state related on an idle card; do not
quote eager-vs-graph ratios without replication.
