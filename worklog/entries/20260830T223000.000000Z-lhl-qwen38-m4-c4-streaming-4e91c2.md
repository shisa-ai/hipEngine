# M4 C4 prompt-streaming: contract decision and retention

## Summary

Contract decision + C4 streaming retention (see Decision/Evidence).

## Decision

The binding frozen correctness contract is the per-cell
`mtp_self_exact` generated-ID equality plus engaged-route and
budget-conformance checks. The acceptance/opportunity trajectory (the
frozen matrix's 628/796) is a campaign comparability diagnostic, **not**
a soundness gate: under self-exactness any accept/reject trajectory
still emits ID-exact output by verifier construction. Where a streaming
variant is considered for retention it must additionally clear the
campaign economics (aggregate 1.15x own AR gate and every category at
or above own AR); the acceptance trajectory is reported, not required
to be bit-identical.

## Evidence

Re-screened at the current head (post M2j/M3, `.worklog/
tmp_m4_c4_stream_screen.py`): width-4 streaming is +4.2% with **every
cell's generated IDs equal to replay**, acceptance 92/121 vs 93/120
(the historical 628/796 -> 624/800 drift does not reproduce), stream
flag true. The full frozen protocol with the promoted `(1, 2, 3, 4)`
production key: **C4 = 35.618 tok/s >= 34.596 gate (1.030x), 1.178x own
AR**, categories 1.1835/1.2352/1.1496/1.2026 (every category >= AR);
48/48 cells exact/engaged/budget. C1-C3 unchanged within 0.08%; C5-C8
subgroups of <= 4 requests also gain (+2.62/+2.69/+2.32/+5.65%) and
remain below their 1.15x gates - those cells stay M2/M5's.

The historical rejection therefore resolves on **economics** (it was
0.68-0.74x AR per category then), not on contract semantics; today it
passes both. C4 prompt-streaming is promoted to default; replay remains
for strict profile, other keys, and peers.
