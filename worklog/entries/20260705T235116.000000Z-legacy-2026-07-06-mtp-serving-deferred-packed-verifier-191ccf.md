## Changes


- Added and promoted default-on
  `HIPENGINE_GGUF_MTP_SERVER_DEFER_VERIFY_SCATTER` for GGUF MTP serving packed
  verifier batches. The verifier now keeps owner-side packed state live through
  acceptance and commits only the accepted hidden seed rows, full-attention KV
  prefix, and final linear-state row. The env remains as an opt-out for
  bisection.
- During promotion, a fresh no-env first request reproduced the earlier
  `mixed_ja_en_translate` economy shift (`draft=20`, `accepted=12`) even though
  warm reruns were clean. Root cause: startup verifier warmup marked packed
  full-attention KV as written, then `reset()` zeroed sessions without clearing
  `_packed_verify_max_written_positions`; the first real request could skip
  prompt-KV sync and verify against stale warmup packed KV. `reset()` now
  invalidates packed verifier/decode session/write-position metadata without
  freeing the workspaces.
