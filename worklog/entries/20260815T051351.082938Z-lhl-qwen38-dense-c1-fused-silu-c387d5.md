## Summary

The human-approved gfx1151 0.8B transfer audit found one directly applicable operation: dense gate/up+SiLU fusion. The exact 0.8B pack8 t128 kernel is shape-specific and was not copied, but gfx1100 already registers a Q4T16 dense dual local32+SiLU sibling matching Qwen3.8's sole layout and c1 `[K5120,N17408]` shape.
