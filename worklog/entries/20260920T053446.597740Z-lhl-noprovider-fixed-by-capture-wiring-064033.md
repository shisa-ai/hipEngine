# The pervasive no_provider is one bug fixed by the capture-wiring commit, proven by tree ordering

## Summary

The pervasive `no_provider` is one bug, it is not in the current tree, and the
commit that fixes it is the same one that makes prefix-checkpoint capture work.

Three live samples on three trees, all c=1 with cache off, MTP on, trace on, two
requests, same script:

| tree | subject | captures | `no_provider` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `942bb6de5` | feat: capture the provider prefix checkpoint at the walk's aligned boundary | **none** | **126 of 128 AR tokens**, 128 events |
| `d805bfae4` | docs: record the hidden-rows prerequisite for the prefix checkpoint restore | (c=8 run, not this probe) | **248 of 256 AR tokens** |
| `8ba08ebf9` | feat: wire prefix-checkpoint capture into the runner and the batch walk | prefix=256 blocks=1 held=1; prefix=512 blocks=2 held=2 | **2 of 2 AR tokens**, 2 events |

The ordering is proven, not assumed: `git merge-base --is-ancestor 8ba08ebf9
d805bfae4` is false and `git log 8ba08ebf9..d805bfae4` is empty, so
`d805bfae4` is an ancestor of `8ba08ebf9`; and `git merge-base 8ba08ebf9
942bb6de5` is `942bb6de5`, so that one is an ancestor too. Oldest to newest is
`942bb6de5` -> `d805bfae4` -> `8ba08ebf9`.

So the two pervasive samples (126/128 at c=1, 248/256 at c=8) both predate
`8ba08ebf9`, and the sample on `8ba08ebf9` collapses to one bounded event per
request. The mechanism is the one already quoted from
`hipengine/generation/qwen35_gguf.py:10345-10358`: the provider needs the
prompt's hidden rows before it can admit a row. The intermediate tree's own
subject is "record the hidden-rows prerequisite for the prefix checkpoint
restore", and `8ba08ebf9` is the commit that wires the capture into the runner
and the batch walk, i.e. supplies those rows. A row that never receives them
reports `no_provider` on every cycle, which is exactly the pervasive shape.

**Consequence: the c=8 `no_provider` finding recorded in
`worklog/entries/20260920T052944.836518Z-lhl-t17-closure-ar-fallback-aa8730.md`
was measured on a tree that predates the fix, so it is superseded rather than
open.** It should not be treated as a live defect or as a blocker for the MTP
default. The bounded one-event-per-request activation wait remains by design and
is separately recorded in
`worklog/entries/20260920T053236.663861Z-lhl-no-provider-activation-wait-bound-baf7fa.md`.

Attribution strength: the tree ordering and the two sample values are measured.
That `8ba08ebf9` is the cause is a strongly supported inference from the
intervening-commit set plus that commit's subject, not a bisect. A c=8 re-run on
current HEAD is the direct confirmation and has not been done.
