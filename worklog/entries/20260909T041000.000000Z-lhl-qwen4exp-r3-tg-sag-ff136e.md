# R3-followup: canonical third-rep TG sag narrowing probe

## Summary

The R3 followup targeted the canonical third-rep TG sag at p4096 (the R2b
refresh reproduced 18.0/18.0/12.8-16.2 samples; the earlier controlled
probes had already ruled out hardware state - clocks, thermal, CPU
frequency, logits capture - and the R2b refresh showed it independent of
the QSA v2 arm).

Findings on the current tree (six promotions through the dense iu8
route, e9c3de827):

1. The v4 combined-default baselines (five promotions, clean
   3fcceff86) show only a mild residual: p4096 TG rep-2 dips to 16.8
   (mixed) and 17.9 (ja) versus 18.4-18.6 flat elsewhere; the code and
   general_en cases are flat.
2. A dedicated six-repetition probe on the exact sagging case
   (mixed_ja_en-p4096, canonical structure: 1 warmup + 6 repetitions,
   production default, chunk 1024) is **flat at 18.7/18.7/18.7/18.8/18.7/
   18.7** with PP flat at 258-259 - no third-rep sag, no decay, no
   periodicity.
3. The deep 12.8 sag from the three-promotion refresh does not
   reproduce at all on the current tree, and the mild v4 wiggle does
   not reproduce under the dedicated probe minutes later on the same
   host.

Conclusion: the canonical third-rep sag is not reproducible on the
current tree even on the case that showed the strongest recent instance;
the residual p4096 TG variation is bounded (~5%), case-dependent (ja/mixed
only), and consistent with transient system-state noise rather than a
structural host or launch cost. Per the task's stop rule, no unmatched
residual is labeled as host cost, and no further narrowing is warranted
unless a future canonical packet reproduces a >=10% dip under a
dedicated probe.
