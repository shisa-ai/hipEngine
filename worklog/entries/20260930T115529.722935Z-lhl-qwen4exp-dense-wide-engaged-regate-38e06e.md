## Open, not closed: the claim rate against the expected window share

The claim rate is **0.3372** canonical and **0.2640** on the reduced screen. A
different case and shape mix is a sufficient explanation, and the window is 32 of
48 layers so a fraction well under 1 is expected. But **the exact expected
fraction has not been derived** - that needs a per-layer, per-shape dispatch
census this run does not record.

A rate that did not match the window share would mean the route claims more or
less than its scope. Nothing suggests it does and the layer scope is unit-tested,
but "plausible" is not "derived", so this is recorded as open rather than
asserted as confirmed.
