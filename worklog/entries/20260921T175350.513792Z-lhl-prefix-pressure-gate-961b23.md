# Prefix eviction-pressure gate with MTP on and off

## Summary

Readiness-checklist item: "Prefix hit/miss and eviction under pressure with MTP
on and off". The mechanism existed and the retained eviction reasons were
counted, but nothing drove the cache past its retained budget while requests
were live, and nothing compared a reused request against the same workload
re-prefilled from scratch.

Three layers now cover it, all of them declared before measuring:

- **Runner-level pressure gate**
  (`tests/test_unit_gguf_prefix_cache_runner.py`): seventeen 900-token
  conversations against the wide 16-entry retained budget, so the trim runs
  under pressure. Asserts the oldest entry is evicted with `trim_retained`
  recorded, every survivor still matches its captured boundary, the evicted
  conversation reports a clean `miss` and re-prefills from token 0, the
  survivor prefills only its suffix, and `evict_prefix_cache_for_pressure`
  releases the oldest entries first with the page count it actually freed.
- **Cancellation gate** (same file): a request that captures a boundary and is
  then cancelled leaves no snapshot, no trie entry, and no pinned page, and a
  later identical request misses cleanly instead of resuming from a released
  boundary, while another conversation's retained entry is untouched.
- **Served-surface pressure gate** (`scripts/prefix_pressure_gate.py`): six
  900-token conversations through `POST /v1/chat/completions`, twice, once with
  `speculative_mtp: true` and once with `false`, against a live server whose
  retained budget is four entries. The four newest conversations must reuse
  their captured 768-token boundary, the two oldest must miss cleanly, and every
  re-request must reproduce its cold run's text token for token. The `/ready`
  hit and miss deltas must equal exactly what the requests reported.

The text-parity assertion is the "no corrupted provider position" half: the
evicted conversations re-prefill the whole prompt, so the same workload runs
with and without reuse inside one pass, and a reused boundary that produced a
different continuation would fail. The `/ready` accounting assertion is the
reporting half: a served surface that stopped counting reuse fails here even
when the reuse itself still works.

The gate's decision logic is pure and unit-tested
(`tests/test_unit_prefix_pressure_gate.py`), so the checks cannot be weakened
without a failing test: reuse depth, clean miss, output parity, non-empty
output, `/ready` accounting, a quiescent counter sample, and a route with no
conversations recorded.

## Changes

- `scripts/prefix_pressure_gate.py` (new): the served-surface gate. Drives the
  live chat route with a cold pass, a reuse pass over the newest conversations,
  and a re-prefill pass over the oldest, then evaluates the record. Exits
  non-zero on any failed expectation and writes a compact JSON artifact.
- `tests/test_unit_prefix_pressure_gate.py` (new): eleven cases over the gate's
  pure functions — `captured_boundary`, `reuse_expectation`,
  `conversation_prompt`, and `evaluate` on an accepted record plus one failing
  record per acceptance clause.
- `tests/test_unit_gguf_prefix_cache_runner.py`:
  `test_retained_working_set_under_pressure_evicts_oldest_and_keeps_survivors`
  and `test_cancelled_request_drops_its_prefix_state_and_nothing_else` (new).
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-21-gfx1151-gguf-prefix-pressure-gate.json` (new):
  the live run's rows, both routes' `/ready` samples, and the protocol.
- `benchmarks/HARNESSES.md`: catalog row for the gate.

## Validation

- Unit: `pytest tests/test_unit_prefix_pressure_gate.py
  tests/test_unit_gguf_prefix_cache_runner.py` — 36 passed (25 + 11).
- Live, `gfx1151`, `Qwen3.8-27B-UD-Q4_K_M.gguf`, retained budget 4, one
  `hipengine serve` process, gate run twice against it (the artifact is the
  second run):

  | route | conversation | prompt tokens | expected reuse | observed | cold TTFT | warm TTFT | text parity |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | MTP on | 0 (evicted) | 921 | miss | 0 | 4.50 s | 4.76 s | ✓ |
  | MTP on | 1 (evicted) | 921 | miss | 0 | 4.75 s | 4.79 s | ✓ |
  | MTP on | 2-5 (retained) | 921 | 768 | 768 | 4.76-4.78 s | 1.50-1.61 s | ✓ |
  | MTP off | 0 (evicted) | 921 | miss | 0 | 4.21 s | 4.19 s | ✓ |
  | MTP off | 1 (evicted) | 921 | miss | 0 | 4.42 s | 4.20 s | ✓ |
  | MTP off | 2-5 (retained) | 921 | 768 | 768 | 4.20-4.38 s | 1.21-1.23 s | ✓ |

  `/ready` per route: `usable_hits` +4 and `misses` +8 (six cold plus two
  re-prefilled conversations), `retained_snapshot_entries` 4 of 4,
  `unusable_hits` 0, and `retained_snapshot_evictions_by_reason.trim_retained`
  advancing as the cache was driven past its budget. `text_parity` is the cold
  run's text against the re-request's, compared token for token.
- Gate verdict: `prefix pressure gate: PASS` on both routes. The artifact sets
  `performance_claim: false`: the TTFT columns are context for the reuse depths,
  not a throughput claim.
- `python3 scripts/docs/check_all.py` — all four gates passed;
  `python3 scripts/sync_benchmark_readme.py --check` — synchronized.
