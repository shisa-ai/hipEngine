# YuE2 AR paired-branch lm head: 6.2% off the decode step, bit-identical

## Summary

The AR decode runs two CFG branches over one set of weights, and a `rocprofv3` trace of a
40-step run puts **79% of the step in the projection GEMV families**. The largest single
caller is the lm head: 756 MB of BF16 weight per call, once per branch, 3.42 ms each and
6.84 ms per step. `dense_gemv_bf16_f32_out_rowtile2` puts two rows in one block so the
weight is issued once for both branches. Each row keeps the single-row kernel's K
traversal and local-256 reduction order, so a row is bit-identical to
`dense_gemv_bf16_f32_out`. `Yue2ArRuntime.logits` now runs the head once for every branch
and caches the rows until a branch's hidden row moves, which takes the matched
fixed-token decode of `mandarin-off-s1234` from **58.30 ms to 54.88 ms per step** (1.06x)
with the trajectory digest unchanged.

## Changes

- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/linear/dense_gemv.hip`: `dense_gemv_bf16_f32_out_rowtile2_kernel`
  plus its `extern "C"` launcher. Two rows per block, one weight read per K vector, two
  accumulators, a per-row local-256 tree, and a tail loop that reads the weight once for
  both rows. Odd row counts run the last block with one row.
- `hipengine/kernels/hip_gfx1100/linear/dense_gemv.py`: the wrapper and symbol, exported
  through `linear/__init__.py` and added to `PRIMITIVES` / `DECODER_PRIMITIVES` in
  `hipengine/kernels/vibevoice.py` so `resolve_vibevoice_kernels` carries it.
- `hipengine/runtime/yue2_ar.py`: `logits(branch)` runs the final norm for every branch
  into a contiguous `_normed_pair`, one `dense_gemv_bf16_f32_out_rowtile2` call for all
  branches into `_logits_pair_f32`, and caches the host rows until a hidden row moves.
  The cache is cleared by `forward_layers`, `push_token`, `reset`, and both prefill paths.
- `scripts/yue2_ar_rowtile_bench.py` (new): serial two-call against one two-row call at
  the AR's production shapes, with effective weight bandwidth.
- `scripts/yue2_ar_paired_head_check.py` (new): loads the AR, drives a two-branch prefill,
  and compares the paired head's rows against the serial kernel's on the same normalized
  rows.
- `tests/test_unit_yue2_ar_gemv_rowtile2.py` (new, 6 tests):
  `tests/test_unit_yue2_ar_paired_head.py` (new, 4 tests).
- `docs/KERNELS.md` (catalog row), `benchmarks/README.md` (a paired-branch section and the
  corrected AR row in the matched stage table), `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Measurements

Host `zbook`, Radeon 8060S (gfx1151, LPDDR5X), BF16, matched fixed-token protocol
(`scripts/yue2_ar_matched_timing.py`, case `mandarin-off-s1234`, 1 297 decode steps, 98-token
positive and 12-token negative prefix, two CFG branches):

| Quantity | Before | After |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Decode step | 58.302 ms | **54.884 ms** (1.062x) |
| Trajectory digest | `6a308700b3b949ac` | `6a308700b3b949ac` (unchanged) |
| Reference advantage on this protocol | 1.889x | 1.778x |

Kernel A/B at the production shapes (`scripts/yue2_ar_rowtile_bench.py`, per-call latency,
synchronize per call, best of 30 after 5 warmups):

| Shape (in x out) | Serial, 2 calls | Two-row, 1 call | Speedup | Serial GB/s | Two-row GB/s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| lm_head 2048 x 184704 | 6.723 ms | 3.798 ms | 1.77x | 112.5 | 199.2 |
| q 2048 x 2048 | 0.060 ms | 0.033 ms | 1.78x | 140.7 | 250.6 |
| o 2048 x 2048 | 0.054 ms | 0.031 ms | 1.77x | 154.3 | 272.7 |
| v 2048 x 1024 | 0.041 ms | 0.028 ms | 1.46x | 102.7 | 150.3 |
| k 2048 x 1024 | 0.028 ms | 0.022 ms | 1.30x | 149.0 | 193.1 |

The two-row head reaches 199 GB/s, 78% of this device's roughly 256 GB/s peak, against
112 GB/s serial. The step gain (6.2%) is close to the head's share of the step alone
(5.0% from 3.42 ms of 58.30 ms), and the rest of the difference is run-to-run spread on
the same case.

Profile that directed the work (`rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` on a 40-step run): the
projection families are 79% of the traced dispatch time (`dense_gemv_out_kernel` for
`down` 527.9 ms, `dense_dual_gemv_out_kernel` for `gate`/`up` 498.6 ms, the f32-output
GEMVs 236.7 ms of which the head is 266.9 ms over 78 calls at 3.42 ms each), attention
6.1%, norms 5.3%, and hipBLASLt 8.6%. Profiler per-dispatch overhead is comparable to the
kernels it measures, so those are shares, not absolute step time.

## Validation

- `python3 -m pytest tests/test_unit_yue2_ar_gemv_rowtile2.py` -> 6 passed. Five shapes
  (q/o, the head, k/v, an odd row count, a single row) are bit-identical per row against
  the single-row kernel, plus an FP64 NumPy outer check.
- `python3 -m pytest tests/test_unit_yue2_ar_paired_head.py tests/test_unit_yue2_ar_runtime.py
  tests/test_unit_yue2_ar_gate.py tests/test_unit_yue2_operators.py` -> 48 passed. The
  paired-head tests drive `logits` with a stub kernel namespace and assert that two
  branches cost one head call, that each branch gets its own row, and that the three
  hidden-row writers clear the cache.
- `python3 scripts/yue2_ar_paired_head_check.py` -> **max abs difference 0.0** on both
  branches, with the two branches' rows confirmed different (`argmax` 168417 against
  173518). This is the wiring proof: the paired path is exactly the serial path.
- `python3 scripts/yue2_ar_replay.py --json /tmp/yue2_ar_replay_paired.json` -> status
  `pass`, pooled mean KL 0.001158 over 576 rows and 18 cases, top-8 recall 0.9588. The
  committed 2026-09-16 artifact reports 0.001433 for the same fixture matrix, but it
  predates the M7 attention and projection-selection commits, so the two are not a
  like-for-like pair; the equivalence evidence for this change is the exact-match check
  and the unchanged trajectory digest above.
- `python3 scripts/sync_benchmark_readme.py --check` -> blocks synchronized.
