# PN4 quant correction: QKV/gate is Q8_0 t16 dual GEMV (not Q4_K); P3-LA2 withdrawn, P3-LAQ1-B declared

## Summary

Material correction to the PN3/P3-LAQ1/P3-LA2 analysis: the Qwen3.6-35B
UD-Q4_K_M c1 linear-attention `attn_qkv` (2048->8192) and `attn_gate`
(2048->4096) weights are **GGUF_Q8_0_T16, not Q4_K T16**. Verified from
materialized weight specs (layout `gguf_q8_0_t16_v1`) and the pair-dispatch
resolution cache during a real 6-step decode (`q8_t16_dual_split`: 210 fused /
0 unfused `launch_gguf_linear_pair` calls). Q4_K T16 is used only by the MoE
expert tensors (`ffn_gate_exps`/`ffn_down_exps`).

Consequences:
- The P3-LAQ1 Q4_K local32 leaf (0.045 ms/layer pair, projected 1.52 ms/token)
  is **not in the qkv_gate path**. The rejection outcome (Q4 leaf cannot help
  the stage) is still correct, but for the wrong reason.
- The actual leaf is the **Q8_0 t16 dual GEMV, already fused** via
  `q8_t16_dual_split`: burst50 0.0677 ms/layer = 2.03 ms/token (30 layers),
  vs the 2.29 ms/token stage wall (incl. 6.5 us/layer norm) => the stage is
  ~90% **kernel-bound**, not dispatch-bound.
- P3-LA2 (launch-count reduction) is **withdrawn**: the qkv+gate is already
  fused, the norm is 6.5 us/layer, and the stage wall (72 us/layer) is
  at/below the kernel sum (6.5+67.7 us) -> no dispatch headroom. A prototype
  Q4 local32 fused pair kernel (bit-exact, registered, but not routed because
  the model uses Q8 for qkv/gate) was implemented, RED-tested, and reverted.
- Corrected target: **P3-LAQ1-B** = optimize the Q8_0 t16 dual GEMV kernel
  (the real kernel-bound family). A 20-30% leaf reduction is worth ~0.4-0.6
  ms/token (~0.9-1.3% complete-request), above the 1% in-tree exact threshold.
