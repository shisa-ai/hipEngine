Screen one exact block-parallel grid for the remaining 24 recurrent
Q4T16-QKV/Q4T16-gate pairs after the retained heterogeneous Q6/Q4 route. The
candidate concatenates the two local32 block ranges without changing either
primitive arithmetic tree.

## Changes

- Added a RED wrapper/route/correctness fixture and a transient registered
  `linear_pair/gguf_q4_k_t16_v1/dense_unequal_local32_bf16_bf16_out` candidate;
  removed all candidate code/tests after the leaf rejected it.

  prefill, peers, and every miss retain the singleton projections.
- Audited but did not implement Q5 `ssm_out` -> add-RMSNorm store fusion: exact
  normalization consumes the unrounded FP32 sum while the residual publishes a
  rounded BF16 sum, so a conventional producer store cannot replace the
  primitive without extra F32 traffic or a persistent global synchronization
  premise.
