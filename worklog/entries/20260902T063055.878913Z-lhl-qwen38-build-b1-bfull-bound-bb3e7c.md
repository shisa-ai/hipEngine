# Qwen3.8 B-full bound rowtile widening infeasible

## Summary

Decision on the remaining B1 item-2 sub-unit (one-sweep verifier lm-head at
R>8): widening the exact rowtile template is structurally infeasible; a
one-sweep body is a new kernel design. Recorded as a measured/named bound
and deprioritized behind B2.

## Analysis

q6_k_t16_qmicro_planar_gemv_rowtile_col8_kernel keeps
`float acc[ROW_TILE][kTileCols=8]` plus `xr[ROW_TILE]` live per thread.
ROW_TILE=16 needs 128 accumulator VGPRs, ROW_TILE=32 needs 256 — the RDNA3
per-thread VGPR budget — before working registers, so a wide instantiation
would spill or collapse occupancy. A one-sweep R20-R32 lm-head must be a new
schedule (WMMA sweep or split-row accumulation with its own exactness RED).

Measured context (B1 owner map + smoke): the lm-head chunked rowtile costs
23.9-33.4 ms per wide verify pass after the A transfer; at ~7.1 cycles per
one-group cell that is an estimated (derived) 2.4-3.2% of complete wall.
B2's P1 prefill bound is 19.8/20.1% by comparison.

## Decision

Keep B1 item 2 as [~] with this named prerequisite recorded in the campaign
doc; proceed to B2. The B1 measured retention (default-on production
transfer) stands independently.
