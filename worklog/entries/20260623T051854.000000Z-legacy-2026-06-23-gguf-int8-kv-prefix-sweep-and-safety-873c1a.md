## Changes


- Ran a W7900/GPU0 prefix-depth sweep with the hermetic TheRock ROCm 7.13 env and compiler-version env guards. The prefill-only sweep reused one BF16 reference per context and tested `HIPENGINE_GGUF_INT8_KV_BF16_PREFIX_FULL_LAYERS=3..10` at `32K`, `64K`, and `128K` with `max_sequence_length=131202`, no BF16 mirror. Result: every memory-saving prefix `3..8` failed at every context; prefixes `9` and `10` passed prefill-only. Best failing prefix was still over the KL gate (`32K` prefix 8: `KL=0.0864`; `64K` prefix 8: `KL=0.3536`; `128K` prefix 8: `KL=1.7411`). Prefix 9 uses 9 BF16 full-attention layers and 1 INT8 layer, with tracked peak `25.111878 GiB` on W7900.
- Confirmed prefix 9 on the full `128K/128` BF16-vs-hybrid gate: `HIPENGINE_GGUF_INT8_KV_BF16_PREFIX_FULL_LAYERS=9 python3 scripts/qwen35_gguf_int8_kv_correctness.py --prompt-lengths 128K --decode-steps 128 --max-sequence-length 131202 ...` -> accepted, `KL mean=0.000259`, `KL max=0.002344`, top-1 agreement `0.968992`, no BF16 mirror, candidate peak `25.111878 GiB` vs BF16 `24.984677 GiB`.
- Interpretation: per-token/head GGUF INT8 KV only passes long-context quality when it saves no useful memory; the GPU1 prefix-3 `128K/128` throughput diagnostic remains unpromoted. Added artifact `benchmarks/results/2026-06-23-w7900-gguf-q4km-int8kv-prefix-sweep.json`. Updated runtime safety so long explicit GGUF INT8 KV defaults to prefix 9 and lower prefixes/pure INT8 require `HIPENGINE_GGUF_INT8_KV_ALLOW_UNVERIFIED_LONG=1` for diagnostics.
