# Correction: the down route's production owner is the fastest of the three - it is env-selected, not unselected

## Summary

Supersedes `20260929T200650.489738Z-lhl-qwen4exp-down-owner-ab-c7fbc6.md` and the ledger row
`ED-4` as I first wrote them.

That entry said `selected_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill_bf16_bf16_out` is "a registered owner that no
manifest entry selects", and framed the measurement as finding an unpromoted 50% win. **That
framing was wrong.** The variant is named by no `_selection()` entry, but **production does
run it**, because:

- `hipengine/generation/qwen4_exp_profiles.py:712` binds
  `"HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q51_IU8_EXACT": "1" if production else "0"`;
- `hipengine/runtime/qwen4_exp_runner.py:4574` then evaluates
  `q51_iu8_exact = _qwen4_exp_q51_iu8_exact_enabled() and rows >= 2 and ffn % 128 == 0` and,
  when true, calls `q51_iu8_risk_down(...)` - **bypassing the variant-selection path
  entirely**.

So the correct reading of the measurement is a **validation**: the owner production runs is
the measured-fastest of the route's three owners. **Nothing to promote.**

## Why I got it wrong, and why it is worth recording

I established "no manifest entry selects it" by scanning every `_selection(...)` block. That
scan is sound for variant-selected routes and **blind to env-gated ones**. This tree wires
default-off and production-only routes through the profile env map as well as through
`_selection()` entries, and at least two routes do it this way
(`HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q4_IU8_EXACT` on line 708 and `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_Q51_IU8_EXACT` on
712, both `"1" if production else "0"`).

I caught it by following the docstring on `_qwen4_exp_q51_iu8_risk_multiplier`, which
documents a screened route with a "~1.3x operation-complete speedup" - a route that could
not exist if nothing selected it. Following that to the runner found the branch.

**Rule for future manifest reads in this tree:** a route can reach production through the
env map (`qwen4_exp_profiles.py`, `"1" if production else "0"`) without any `_selection()`
entry naming its variant. Check both before concluding a registered owner is unselected.

## What the measurements actually establish (unchanged)

The measurements themselves were not affected; only the interpretation was.

Three registered owners of `moe_linear` /
`prefill_rows_ge512_k640_exact_grouped_q5_1_down` in one arms pair for the first time, main
kernel, 12 of 12 cases, 15 HIP-event reps, same physical host as the ED-1 artifacts:

- `selected_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill_bf16_bf16_out` (production) is **-21.0% to -79.2% faster
  than `selected_grouped_prefill_pair2_row_publish` (median -50.4%)**;
- `selected_grouped_prefill_pair2_row_publish` is **+82.5% to +562.0% faster than
  `selected_grouped_prefill_compact_rowbatch8_out8_expertgrid64`**.

Both production choices on this route are therefore validated by direct measurement.

**The main-kernel gap explains the recorded operation-complete gain.** The 2026-09-08
promotion `a57a3e418` recorded +1.9-3.0% prefill per case over 12 cases, 72/72 trajectories
exact, and a 12/12 bit-identical state gate. The difference between -50.4% main-kernel and
+1.9-3.0% operation-complete is the **repair**, which this packet excludes (`max_risks=0`)
and production pays.
