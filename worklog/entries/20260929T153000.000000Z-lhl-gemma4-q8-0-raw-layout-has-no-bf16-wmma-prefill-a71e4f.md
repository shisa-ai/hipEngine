# for bf16 output the raw Q8_0 layout has no WMMA prefill, and the T16 layout has two

## Summary

`20260929T143000` established that gemma4's Q8_0 dense leaves cannot reach the two-wave/four-wave
Q8T16 prefill schedules because gemma4's loader has no repack step, and that the gate would fire
if they could. This asks the question that decides whether the loader change is worth its risk to
the decode path: **what is the raw route actually doing?**

**Answer: for bf16 output, no WMMA prefill exists on the raw key at all.** The T16 key has the
whole `wmma_prefill_*` family, both wave schedules included.

## The raw key's prefill surface

`gguf_q8_0` has 104 registered `linear` variants on gfx1151. The prefill-capable ones:

| variant | kind |
| --- | --- |
| `exact_prefill_tile16x4_bf16_bf16_out` | exact tile kernel |
| `exact_prefill_tile4x16_bf16_bf16_out` | exact tile kernel |
| `exact_prefill_tile8x2_bf16_bf16_out` | exact tile kernel |
| `exact_prefill_tile8x4_bf16_bf16_out` | exact tile kernel |
| `mmq128_prefill_q8_1_d4x2_guarded_f32_f32_out` | MMQ |
| `mmq128_prefill_q8_1_d4x3_guarded_bf16_bf16_out` | MMQ |
| `mmq128_prefill_q8_1_d4x3_guarded_f32_f32_out` | MMQ |
| **`iu8_wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out`** | **the only WMMA prefill, f32 -> f32** |
| `prefill_{bf16,f32,fp16}_{bf16,f32,fp16}_out` | the older prefill family |

**The one WMMA prefill is `f32 -> f32`.** These are hidden projections with bf16 output, so it is
not a candidate. For bf16 output the raw key's prefill options are the exact tile kernels and
MMQ128.

The T16 key by contrast carries `wmma_prefill_bf16_bf16_out`, `wmma_prefill_2wave_bf16_bf16_out`
and `wmma_prefill_4wave_bf16_bf16_out` -- the exact variants the admitted schedules name.

## Why this matters for the decision

The dense term is the largest single prefill term (189.3 ms of 659 ms) and is pinned at **127 GB/s
of weight traffic against an achievable 223** (`20260929T100000`). That is 57 percent of what the
lm head demonstrates the hardware does.

So the question "should gemma4 repack its Q8_0 dense leaves to T16" is not a wash between two
WMMA schedules. It is **a non-WMMA prefill route against a WMMA route that has an admitted
two-wave and four-wave schedule**, on the biggest term, at 57 percent of demonstrated bandwidth.
That makes the loader change worth its risk, and it explains the 127 GB/s without needing a new
hypothesis.

**This is still not a performance claim.** The raw route's actual per-kernel selection has not
been observed -- `exact_prefill_*` and `mmq128_prefill_*` are both plausible and they are different
kernels -- and T16-vs-raw at rows=512 has not been measured. The claim here is a capability fact
read off the registry: **the bf16 WMMA prefill family is registered only under the T16 key.**

## Refinement of the earlier entry

`20260929T143000` said "the raw key has no wave wiring at all", which is true, and implied the raw
key had no WMMA prefill. It does have one -- `iu8_wmma_prefill_f32_f32_out` -- but only for f32
output, which these projections do not use. The conclusion is unchanged and the reason is
sharper.
