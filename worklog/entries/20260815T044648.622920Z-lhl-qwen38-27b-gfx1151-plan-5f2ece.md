# Plan Qwen3.8-27B gfx1151 optimization campaign

## Summary

Created the dedicated Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_M gfx1151 campaign plan around the
user-selected 512/128, 1K/128, and 4K/128 working set. The plan targets matched
llama.cpp HIP/Vulkan prefill and true-AR parity, correctness-valid exact B3 MTP,
and lower matched process memory, with a separate quality-first INT8 K/V lane.

The main review finding is structural: gfx1151 explicitly disables the modern
dense-H5120 sole-Q4T16, planar-Q6, Q5T16 recurrent-output, and compact-peer GDN
package used on gfx1100. Current Qwen3.8 therefore opens at 147.984 tok/s pp512,
7.1358 tok/s natural AR, 19.4404 tok/s exact B3, and 31.659 GiB tracked-owned,
while current llama.cpp records 353.620 pp512, 12.7723 best AR, and 19.6552
correctness-valid HIP B3. The plan qualifies those existing shared gfx11 bodies
one owner at a time before any new microkernel ladder.
