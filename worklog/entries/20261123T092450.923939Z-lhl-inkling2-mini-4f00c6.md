# Pair wide Inkling Q5_K prefill outputs

## Summary

Use a two-output schedule for wide, even Q5_K expert tiles. Keep the one-output schedule for smaller and odd widths. The ABI, packed weights, and per-output reduction order are unchanged.

## Changes

- Share the one- and two-output kernels through a compile-time tile width. Each routed activation is loaded once for both outputs. Q8_0 continues to use the one-output schedule.
