# The 45.66 C8 row predates grouped prefill: I compared a pre-change number against a post-change claim

## Summary

Corrects `worklog/entries/20260830T134942.798537Z-lhl-w7900-submodule-parity-gate-651f3d.md`, which
stated: "the ledger line C8 AR 45.68 -> 78.67 is the AR-only control protocol while the published
server-bench row is 45.66 here - the grouped-prefill promotion did not move the published row". That is
**false**, and the mistake was trusting a filename instead of metadata:

- `benchmarks/results/2026-08-30-w7900-q4km-c1c8-hipengine-refresh-post-promotions.json` was added at
  **11:39** (`bce85cbe8`).
- Grouped prefill shipped at **12:26** (`72a29e9ad` "perf: ship grouped prefill on gfx1100 (C8 AR
  45.68 -> 78.67 tok/s, 7 of 8 widths lead)").

So 45.66 is a **pre**-grouping measurement of the same server-bench protocol, despite the file being
called "post-promotions" (it meant the dual-SiLU rows12/rows33 promotions). The two numbers are a valid
consecutive pair, and grouped prefill moved C8 AR by roughly 1.75x. The correct provenance check is
`git log --diff-filter=A -- <artifact>` against the promotion commit time, never the artifact's name.
