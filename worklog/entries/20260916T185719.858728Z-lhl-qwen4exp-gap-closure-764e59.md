# Add gap-closure columns to the family table and correct the default-on scopes

## Summary

Two changes to `docs/QWEN4EXP-STATUS.md`, one additive and one a correction.

**Additive: §2.4 says how much of the gap is recoverable and whether it is
admissible.** §2.2 says where the 18323.2 ms gap is; it did not say how much of
it we have evidence to take back.

| | Share of the 18323.2 ms gap |
| --- | ---: |
| `dense_projection` + `hyper_connection`, the only families with measured recovery | 62.1% |
| ...admissible today (certified dense 16-47, a byte-share prediction) | **24.1%** |
| ...measured but not shippable | 42.6% total |
| No candidate at all (`expert_gate_up`, `expert_down`, `qsa_attention`, `moe_reduce`) | 35.7% |
| `gdn`, candidates inside the run-to-run spread | 3.0% |
| `elementwise_norm`, where we are ahead | −0.8% |

**The headline: 24.1 points of the gap are shippable, another 18.5 points are
measured but blocked on numerics, and 35.7 points have no candidate at all.**
The certified dense route takes the total ratio from **5.87x to 4.70x**, and the
measured top-four combination (+7.808 s, 1.493x) would take it to about 3.80x.

The largest single blocked item is the hyper-connection read: `GR_IU8` and
`GR_IU8_DOWN` are **+2.494 s measured**, which exceeds that family's entire gap
(2188 ms) and is larger than the whole unbuilt routed-MoE-down family gap
(1976 ms). Both are rejected at mean KL `1.4e-3` against a `1e-3` limit. That is
the one place in the campaign where a numerics result buys seconds directly.

## Correction: the "default-on at a certified scope" claims were wrong

§3 claimed four routes were default-on at certified scopes. They were read off
the profile's flag map, and **that is the wrong source for this quant**.

For `gguf_ud_q4_k_xl` the production binder sets the base profile env, then
**zeroes fifteen `PRODUCTION_ARITHMETIC_RECOVERY_FLAGS`**, then restores six
`PRODUCTION_Q8_QSA_RESTORED_FLAGS`. So this quant's arithmetic comes from the
production **selection table**, which is keyed on rows and quant, not from those
flags. Reading the flags gives the wrong answer in both directions.

The layer scopes are now read from the kernel trace instead
(`role-analysis.json`), which is direct evidence:

| Claim as written | Actual, from the trace |
| --- | --- |
| "WMMA-MoE layers 27-47" | `gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill` ran on layers **0-1 and 3-47**; the `selected_dual_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill_bf16_bf16_out` selection is a manifest entry keyed on `prefill_rows_ge64_exact_grouped_q4_gate_up`. Not the `PRODUCTION_MOE_PREFILL` route, whose flag is `0`, and not a 27-47 scope — which is what the profile's own comment claims |
| "iu8 gate/up 35-47" | `Q4_IU8_PREFILL` is `0` for this quant. The iu8 gate/up comes from the selection table |
| "Column-warp GDN layers 27-47 default-on" | `GDN_COLWARPS_PREFILL` is `0`; the base `qwen4_exp_gdn_prefill_f32` ran on the 36 GDN layers. Enabling column warps is worth +0.228 s measured, inside the run-to-run spread |
| "QSA flash layers 35-47 default-on" | `QSA_FLASH_PREFILL` is `0`. What is restored is `QSA_H256_WAVE_PREFILL=page256` and `QSA_HEAD_PAIR=quad`; `qsa_sparse_attention_h256_wave_rows_f32` ran on the 12 attention layers |
| "DP4A decode layers excluding 1,3,4,7,12 default-on" | `Q4_DP4A64` is `0` for this quant |

The profile's own comment saying the WMMA route "covers MoE layers 27-47 via the
backend capability constant" is **stale** — the trace shows every layer. That is
recorded rather than silently fixed, because a comment like that is how the wrong
scope propagates into documents.

Two consequences beyond the table: `gdn` and `qsa_attention` are running their
*base* implementations, so their headroom is the sweep's measured numbers rather
than a certified scope, and `expert_gate_up`'s good ratio (4.48x) is a real
result of the selection table rather than of the flag this document credited.

  route) are **different selectors** on the same tensors, not one route at two
  scopes. The family-wide +6614 ms therefore has **no numerical verdict recorded
  at that scope**; the rejection we hold is for the f16 `Q8_WMMA_LAYERS` path at

  is 16-47.

  hyper-connection numerics first and the unbuilt kernels second.
