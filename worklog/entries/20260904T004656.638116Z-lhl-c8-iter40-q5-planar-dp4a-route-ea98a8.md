# C8 iter40: session-gated planar-Q5 dp4a R24 dispatch route (default-off)

## Summary

Wired the iter39 kernel (grouped integer-dp4a planar-Q5 sibling, leaf wins
9/9, strongest at R24 0.77-0.84x) into the production dispatch path as a
default-off, session-gated R24 route. This is the direct-load LDS-free
dp4a schedule iter37 identified as the one unexplored axis against the
C8-P2 residual (7.280 ms/cycle of the 11.392 peer target still open).

Scope decisions:
- **R24-only.** At R32 the leaf candidate (0.2597-0.2623 ms op-complete)
  loses to the retained source-layout FP32-metadata owner (0.2297 ms), so
  the R32 route is untouched.
- **Resident sidecar, not runtime conversion.** The kernel consumes
  qmicro-planar tiles; a new `qmicro_planar` weight allocation
  (25,559,040 B per [5120,6144] Q5_K weight, ~+190 MB for 48 weights)
  materializes from raw at load only when the env opt-in is set. The
  retained raw allocation stays resident underneath (strict fallback).
- **Zero-valued env rolls back at both layers** (loader sidecar + dispatch
  route), the campaign convention.
