# iu8 exact repair: unroll the inner loop, and the measured dead ends

## Summary

The promoted `q4_iu8_exact` MoE route spends 1420.9 ms of a 16733.4 ms
`code-p4096` prefill in sparse exact-repair passes that correct 0.712% of output
elements. That is the largest ratio of cost to work anywhere in the engine, and
the refreshed attribution ranked the MoE pair first, so this unit asked what sets
that cost.

**Answer: memory-level parallelism, not bytes and not grid size.** The runtime
trip count of each repair kernel's inner loop serializes its loads behind one
dependent round trip; `#pragma unroll 4` on the three repair kernels is
arithmetic-preserving and takes the repair total **1420.9 -> 1239.8 ms (-12.7%)**
in the engine, with the main MoE kernels unchanged to 0.1%.

Two redesigns that the traffic arithmetic recommended are measured dead ends and
are recorded as such: cutting per-element traffic 3.3x with a row-bucketed repair
is 22% *slower*, and making the re-read activation rows L2-resident gains 13.8%.

| Measurement | Result |
| --- | ---: |

| Same, grid 65536 / 262144 | 3.0500 / 3.1778 ms (no effect) |
| Down repair, 186,685 risks | 1.6404 -> 1.2922 ms (-21.2%) |

The two captures agree with the packet: gate/up -5.2% against -5.8%, down -18.8%
against -21.2%. `gguf_q4_k_selected_dual_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill` (2708.8 ->
2710.3 ms) and `q5_1_selected_wmma_iu8_risk_prefill` (1483.8 -> 1484.5 ms) are
unchanged in the same captures, so the change is confined to the repair passes
and the captures are comparable.

## Validation

- 58 GPU tests pass across the three exactness suites
  (`test_gpu_qwen4exp_q4_iu8_exact.py`, `test_gpu_qwen4exp_q51_iu8_exact.py`,
  `test_gpu_qwen4exp_q5_k_iu8_exact.py`) and the new row-bitmap suite. The
  exactness suites are the oracle for the pragma: they compare the full
  iu8-risk+repair chain against the pair2 parent bit for bit at the production
  multiplier, on skewed/empty experts, tail tiles, cancellation-heavy and
  adversarial inputs, nonfinite activations, and queue overflow.
- The row-bitmap suite compares both repair kernels element by element against a
  poisoned sentinel, so it pins both value identity and ownership, and it pins
  the artifact's traffic claim.
- No RED-first step for the pragma itself: a one-line compiler hint has no
  behavioural RED state, so the existing bit-identity suite is the gate. The
  row-bitmap pair did have a RED state (the kernels did not exist).

## What this does not establish

- **No prefill-level win is claimed.** The whole-prefill kernel total moved
  16733.4 -> 16673.2 ms (-0.36%), inside the captures' own drift: the non-repair
  kernels summed 1.1% higher in the second capture. A compile-time change cannot
  be A/B'd interleaved in one process, so the retained evidence is a sub-window
  reduction measured twice.
- **The repair is still expensive.** 1239.8 ms to correct 0.7% of elements is
  7.4% of the prefill. Reducing the *incidence* is a numerical-contract question
  (the criterion's multiplier is calibrated to bit-parity with the bf16 parent,
  with a screened floor between 0.5 and 1.0), not a kernel question, and it is
  untouched here. That is the only remaining lever this unit identified.
- **The down route's incidence is inferred, not measured.** 186,685 risks is the
  0.712% rate applied to the down shape; it reproduces the engine's pre-unroll
  per-launch time to 0.3%. The risk-diagnostics instrumentation covers the
  gate/up route only.
- **The row-bitmap pair is unused.** It is bit-identical and slower; it stays
  registered as measurement surface with a removal trigger in
  `docs/REFACTOR.md`, not as a route.

## Next
