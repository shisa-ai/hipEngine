# R0519 compensated Gemma WMMA promotion premise is stale on gfx1151

## Summary

The lead decided to promote the compensated Gemma 4 WMMA MoE prefill (`HIPENGINE_GEMMA4_MOE_PREFILL=wmma`)
to the production default. A same-process public A/B on gfx1151 shows that is now a 4x regression:
the production default already runs fused T16 WMMA owners, and the `wmma` pin falls back to the
selected GEMV on the T16-converted Q4_K layout. Promotion was not applied, and R0519 is
returned to the lead.

## Changes

- `docs/REFACTOR.md` R0519: added the measurement and the stale-premise note.

## Validation

- Host: gfx1151 (Radeon 8060S). Artifact: `gemma-4-26B-A4B-it-UD-Q4_K_XL.gguf`. Profile:
  production. Measured with `LLM.generate_detailed`, `max_tokens=1`, greedy, random-token
  prompts, alternating `auto`/`wmma` per rep, best of 3, same process.
- `auto`: 256 tokens 0.156 s (1640 tok/s), 1024 tokens 0.486 s (2106 tok/s), 2048 tokens
  1.030 s (1989 tok/s). Routes: `gate_up_fused_t16`, `down_t16`, plus one `gate_up_iu8` (Q5_K layer).
- `wmma`: 0.533 s (480 tok/s), 2.033 s (504 tok/s), 4.211 s (486 tok/s). Routes: `selected_gemv`,
  plus one `grouped_prefill`. No compensated WMMA owner engaged.
- Tracked allocation after close: 0 bytes.

## Next

- Lead: retire the compensated candidate wiring, or repair it to serve the T16 layout. Promoting
  it as it stands would regress prefill.
