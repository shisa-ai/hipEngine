# Promote exact Qwen4Exp device argmax

## Summary

Bind registered `argmax/f32/top1_i64` in Qwen4Exp strict and production normal
greedy generation. Direct runner, MTP, numerical, and explicit debug/API calls
retain full-logit capture. Keep `HIPENGINE_QWEN4_EXP_DEVICE_ARGMAX=0` as a
short-lived rollback until one clean default confirmation.

## Changes

- Added the exact argmax selection to strict and production manifests.
- Bound compact normal-greedy output on and retained explicit full-logit APIs.
- Added physical-c2 route coverage and fail-closed profile-test cleanup.
- Published the compact benchmark artifact and campaign/scoreboard rollup.

## Validation

- Five p508+128-step counterbalanced pairs are wall-neutral: full-logit host
  argmax 14.7555 tok/s versus device argmax 14.8051 tok/s, ratio 1.00343x with
  95% CI 0.98776-1.01909. Generated outputs are exact.
- Python-visible D2H is 993,280 bytes/token for full logits and 8 bytes/token
  for device argmax, a 124,160x byte reduction. H2D counts and bytes are equal.
- The complete 18-prompt/category-heldout packet has 450/450 full-logit rows at
  KL=0 and top-1=1.0, 18/18 exact generated task sequences, three repeat-exact
  candidate trajectories, exact normal state, and zero tracked bytes after
  teardown.
- The first broad run had one strict/compact state mismatch on
  `code_markdown_table`. The tightened focused repair repeated strict and
  compact generation three times; all six state hashes agree. The other 17
  broad prompts already had strict equality and three exact compact repeats.
- Physical c2 with two distinct prompts and two runs per route is repeat-exact
  and cross-route exact; D2H is 31,784,960 versus 256 bytes over 32 outputs and
  tracked memory returns to zero.
- Cached `rocprofv3` traces `argmax_stage1_kernel` at 8,176 ns and
  `argmax_stage2_kernel` at 2,484 ns with plausible geometry.
