# Parity P2.3: low-VGPR 16-column Q4T16 owners retained (rows 17-48)

## Summary

Fourth retained kernel unit of the external-parity campaign, direct follow-up
to the counter attribution (iteration 8: latency-bound at ~2 waves/SIMD,
248 VGPRs/thread). The dense Q4T16 prefill template gained an
OUT_TILES_PER_BLOCK parameter (default 3 keeps existing instantiations
byte-identical) and two low-VGPR instantiations: one 16-column out tile per
32-thread block with two (16-float accumulator) or three (24-float) 16-row
tiles.

Microbench (synthetic Q4T16, warmup 4 + 20 iters, all owners bit-exact vs
shared-B with max_abs 0.0 on every shape/row):

- rows 17-32: the 32-row-block variant wins on all six shapes, e.g.
  5120->17408 0.881->0.496 ms (-43.7%, 104 GB/s effective) and
  6144->5120 0.573->0.271 ms (-52.7%).
- rows 33-48: the 48-row-block variant wins on (5120,17408) 0.923->0.681 ms
  (-26.2%), (5120,10240) -39.1%, (5120,12288) -29.4%; the 32-row variant
  wins on (5120,6144) -36.7%, (17408,5120) -43.4%, (6144,5120) -46.2%.
- rows 49-64: the 48-column single-wave owner retains the band; >=65 keeps
  shared-B fail-closed.

gfx1151 selector bands updated accordingly (17-32 lowvgpr; 33-48 per-shape
lowvgpr48/lowvgpr; 49-64 single-wave; unadmitted shapes unchanged).

Real-model (physical gfx1151, standard Q4_K_M parity model):
