# c8: q5 residual bounded by measured compliance and composition blockers

## Summary

Closes the C8-P2 bullet "close the remaining 7.280 ms/cycle gap to the
peer-derived 11.392 ms/cycle Q5 target, or record the measured
resource/roofline reason it cannot close." The requested mechanism (Laurent's

## The three measured reasons

1. **Precision-compliance cost (measured, iteration retained).** The
   source-faithful FP16-metadata build — the fastest variant, 12.806→9.514
   ms/cycle (-25.71%), every actual weight a winner — fails the campaign
   slice rule: task gate changed one acceptance decision (1,256→1,248) and
   regressed `general_en` 1.38%/1.90% across orders. The compliant FP32
   metadata build is retained at 10.940 ms/cycle; the compliance premium is
   1.426 ms/cycle of the residual, and no intermediate metadata precision can
   pass (GGUF Q5_K scales are fp16; any narrowing reintroduces the same
   acceptance divergence the gate rejects).
2. **Composition gap (measured, iterations 42-44).** Per-weight leaf wins do
   not compose 1:1 into e2e: the planar-Q5 dp4a route passed the full L4 gate
   and won its k2/R24 retention e2e (+0.44%) while its target-stage delta
   (+0.56%) sat inside the ±1.2% control spread, at 1.23 GB persistent
   sidecar cost. The retained rowtile/route mechanisms' e2e realizations
   (+0.44% to +5.71%) are consistently fractions of their leaf ratios.
3. **Scheduling/occupancy recovery family-wide rejected (measured, iters
   45-47).** Unroll reshaping, launch-bounds occupancy forcing, and
   co-residency grid reordering all measured neutral-to-negative on the Q6
   head (790 GB/s = 96% of peak already), the Q4 grouped owner (477 GB/s
   effective), and the fused row32 owner (241 VGPR structural floor). No
   scheduling-only mechanism recovers additional bandwidth on these shapes.
