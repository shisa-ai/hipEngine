# Reject Qwen4Exp GR down scaled SiLU

## Summary

Rejected and removed exact Q8 GR down+scaled-SiLU publication. It improves a
two-row diagnostic but regresses the binding rows508 operation-complete window,
so whole-model timing was not warranted.

## Changes

- Prototyped raw-Q8 coltile8/rowbatch4 down with scaled-SiLU in the original
  publication threads.
- Added direct exactness during screening, then reversed all candidate kernel,
  wrapper, registry, and test changes after the measured loss.
- Added compact rejection evidence and updated the P3 campaign/changelog.

## Validation

- rows37 K10240/N320 F32 output is bit-exact to the retained Q8 coltile
  projection followed by in-place `qwen4_exp_scaled_silu_f32`.
- The candidate preserves K ownership, FMA order, wave shuffle, and serial
  four-wave publication.
- Alternating HIP-event screen:
  - rows2 `40.700→36.401 us` (`1.118x`);
  - rows508 `1.655→1.667 ms` (`0.9926x`).
- Candidate patch is hash-pinned and reversed cleanly. Whole-model p508 was
  short-circuited after the binding primitive failure.

## Next

Do not fuse transcendental scaled-SiLU into this register-heavy down kernel.
Return to the larger 36-layer qkv+gate exact input-ownership design or evaluate
GR down+inject where the extra four F32 reductions can share one output tile
without perturbing Q8 down reductions.
