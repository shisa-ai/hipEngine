# The decode MoE gate/up runs at 15% of bandwidth and a pack8 GEMV sits unwired

## Summary

The decode MoE gate/up is the largest single line at 28.0% of decode, and it is
now sized: it runs at **38.5 GB/s, about 15% of the 8060S's roughly 250 GB/s**.

Arithmetic. The q4_k `ffn_gate_up_exps` is 128 experts x 2816 x 1408 at 4.5 bpw,
285 MB total. Top-8 routing reads 17.8 MB per token, and across 29 layers that is
517.4 MB in the 13.45 ms the profile attributes to this kernel. So the kernel is
not compute-limited and there is roughly 4-6x of bandwidth headroom on this line
alone - more than the entire 2X decode target.

**A pre-packed variant exists and is not wired.** `hipengine_gguf_q4_k_selected_
pack8_gemv_bf16_bf16_out` is defined at `gguf_q4_k_gemv.py:47` with a wrapper at
:1503, and a repository-wide grep finds **no caller outside its own module**. The
q5_k and q6_k equivalents in `gguf_k_selected_pack8_gemv.py` do have wiring.

I am not treating that as the answer yet. pack8 needs a weight repack at load
time, so it is not free, and an unwired function is equally consistent with
"never finished" and "retired for a reason". The next unit is to establish which
before changing the route - an unwired fast path is a hypothesis, not a finding.

**A hypothesis I checked and dropped, recorded because it was plausible.** The
profile shows `gguf_q4_k_selected_prefill_out_kernel` running 1827 times during
decode while the expert down projection runs a kernel named `selected_gemv`, which
looks like a mis-dispatch. It is not: `gguf_q4_k_selected_prefill_out_kernel` is
defined in `gguf_q4_k_gemv.hip`, so it is the decode GEMV, and
`gemma4_project_experts_rows` reports its route as `selected_gemv` at decode. The
`prefill` in the name is historical - the third instance of that pattern this
campaign, after the MoE and q8_0 kernels. The check cost one grep; the wrong
conclusion would have cost a unit.

## Validation

- The bandwidth figure is arithmetic over the artifact's own measured kernel time
  and the tensor's declared dimensions and bit width, not a new measurement. It
  sizes the next unit rather than claiming a speedup.
- The unwired claim is a repository-wide grep for the symbol across `hipengine/`,
  `scripts/` and `tests/`, which returns nothing outside the defining module.
- The route claim is read from `gemma4_project_experts_rows`'s own return value
  and the kernel's source file, not inferred from names.
