# Measured GEMV/MMQ crossover: min_rows 32 -> 8

## Summary

Corrects the one unmeasured number in the dense IQ MMQ route. `min_rows = 32`
was reasoning from the GEMV's 8-row slab, not a measurement. Swept properly,
the crossover is **8**, so the previous floor was leaving 1.6x-7x on the table
for every prefill between 8 and 31 rows. Short-prompt prefill gains **+67% at
16 tokens and +97% at 24**; 48 and 128 tokens are flat, which is the control.

## Changes

- `scripts/gguf_iq_dense_mmq_crossover.py` (new): interleaved GEMV-vs-MMQ sweep
  over every distinct rank-2 IQ4_XS shape in the published UD file that meets
  the kernel's K256/N128 alignment, 16 row counts each, min-of-5. Every cell is
  also compared for agreement, so it doubles as a correctness screen across the
  128-row padding boundary.
- `hip_gfx1151/__init__.py`: `min_rows` 32 -> 8, with the worst-case-over-shapes
  sweep row recorded in the policy comment so the number is not re-guessed.
- `tests/test_gguf_iq_dense_mmq_route.py`: explicit decline cases at rows 1/2/4,
  admission cases at 8/16/32/512, and a test pinning the policy floor to the
  swept value.

## Measurements

Worst case over the six measured shapes:

| rows | 2 | 4 | 8 | 16 | 32 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| min speedup | 0.59x | 0.68x | **1.58x** | 2.62x | 4.34x |

8 is the first row count where the MMQ wins on **every** shape; 4 still loses
on `attn_gate` (6144x5120) at 0.68x. Per-shape crossovers: `ffn_down` 1,
`ffn_gate`/`attn_q`/`attn_qkv`/`ssm_out` 4, `attn_gate` 8.

End to end (resident session, bulk + WMMA prefill, best of 6, both arms in the
same tree by swapping only the policy value):

| prompt | min_rows=32 | min_rows=8 | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 tokens | 28.3 tok/s | 47.2 | +67% |
| 24 tokens | 33.6 | 66.1 | +97% |
| 48 tokens | 88.0 | 88.4 | +0.5% (control) |
| 128 tokens | 110.5 | 110.9 | +0.4% (control) |

48 and 128 are above both thresholds and take the MMQ either way. Their
flatness is what shows the change is scoped exactly to the 8-31 band - and it
is also why the published 512/128 sweep does not move, so no topline row
changes.

## Validation

- 96 (shape, rows) cells agree with the strict GEMV at max relative error
  0.0025-0.0099, correlation 0.999983, **uniform across row counts** including
  1 and 2 where the 128-row padding dominates.
- `tests/test_gguf_iq_dense_mmq_route.py` 25 cases,
  `test_gguf_iq_source_mmq_prefill.py`, `test_gfx1151_backend.py`,
  `test_gguf_e2e_acceptance.py` green.
- Artifact: `benchmarks/results/2026-09-08-zbook-ud-iq-mmq-crossover.json`.

## Limitations

- The model-level teacher gate for this route was run at 512-row prefill, which
  does not exercise the 8-31 band. The evidence that small rows are equally
  accurate is **kernel-level** (the uniform agreement above), not a
  short-prompt model gate.
- Swept on IQ4_XS only; IQ3_XXS shares the policy and kernel but its own
  crossover is unmeasured.
- gfx1100 does not declare this policy and is unaffected.
