# Fail close gfx1151 bulk WMMA shape policies

## Summary

D08-X and D08-X2-K5 had retained full-model p512 evidence but their backend
capability booleans admitted every rows>=16 compatible shape. That generalized
a measured campaign result beyond its row/shape matrix. The gfx1151 defaults
now fail closed while preserving every Qwen3.5-0.8B shape exercised by the
retained p512 A/B.

## Changes

- Added explicit gfx1151 `(rows, K, N)` policy sets for resident-pack8 Q4 WMMA
  and expanded dense-BF16 WMMA.
- Q4 admits the five pack8 projection shapes present in the 0.8B Q4_K_M plan at
  rows 512; dense BF16 admits the Q6 attention-V and dense-down shapes at rows
  512.
- Generic dispatch checks backend policy metadata without adding backend or
  quant conditionals. The explicit `use_q4_pack8_wmma=True` plugin path remains
  independent; only the D08 backend-default route is constrained.
- Pair fallback routing applies the same Q4 policy as singleton dispatch.
- Updated rollback/refactor notes to require matched evidence before expanding
  either policy.

## Validation

- RED: p256 Q4, p256 dense BF16, and an unmeasured p512 dense shape all selected
  WMMA; the K-tail case already failed closed after the preceding unit.
- GREEN policy nodes: 9 passed, covering qualified and unqualified singleton
  shapes, backend policy contents, and registered pair ownership.
- `uv run pytest -q tests/test_gguf_linear_dispatch.py` — 103 passed.
- Read-only materialization-plan inventory for
  `/models/gguf/Qwen3.5-0.8B-Q4_K_M.gguf` on gfx1151 found exactly the five
  pack8 and two dense-BF16 `(K,N)` shapes listed in the policies (92 pack8 and
  16 dense-BF16 weights). No model allocation or benchmark was run.

## Next
