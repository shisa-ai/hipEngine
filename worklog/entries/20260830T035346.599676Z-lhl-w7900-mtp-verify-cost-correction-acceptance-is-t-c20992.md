# W7900 Qwen3.8: verify rows are cheaper than AR rows; the 3.21x claim was a cross-artifact error

## Summary

`20260830T030216...-37f4a1` ("MTP cost is per-row verify, not packing") and the task-#9
reframe are wrong about the cost half, and I have been quoting their 3.21x all day. Measured
inside one packet, with admission subtracted from an independently measured d1 packet, MTP
verify rows are **cheaper per row** than AR decode rows (0.49x-0.93x, row-weighted **0.79x**).
The 3.21x came from dividing an MTP slope taken from the C1-C8 server packet by an AR slope
taken from the submodule-decomposition artifact - two different context shapes, pp256/ctx200
versus ~43-in/24-out. That is the same-host / same-suite error my own evidence rule names, and
the correction changes which lever matters.

## Changes

Analysis and record only. Supersedes the cost conclusion of `20260830T030216...-37f4a1` and the
framing of `20260830T030945...-50b888`; the packing half of both survives.

## Validation

Same K3 packet as those entries (`/tmp/he-c1c8-k3`, 24 tokens, protocol intact), admission
taken as the min 1-token wall per width from `/tmp/he-d1v3`. Per row per lane per cycle,
`(wall - admission) / cycles / (width x rows_per_cycle)` against AR's
`(wall - admission) / (tokens - 1) / width`:

| C | AR ms/row | MTP ms/row | MTP/AR | rows/cycle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 33.14 | 16.15 | 0.49 | 3.80 |
| 2 | 20.22 | 15.65 | 0.77 | 3.83 |
| 4 | 18.60 | 17.36 | 0.93 | 3.85 |
| 5 | 18.57 | 15.47 | 0.83 | 3.84 |
| 6 | 18.44 | 14.66 | 0.79 | 3.84 |
| 8 | 18.49 | 14.19 | 0.77 | 3.85 |

Verify is therefore *efficient*: a cycle verifies 3.85 rows per lane for about 3.4x the wall of
an AR step at the same width, which is less than the 3.85x of rows it does. MTP loses at width
only because each cycle pays back 1 + 3 x 0.468 = **2.40** tokens instead of the 3.37 it would
pay back at the C1 acceptance rate. Acceptance is not the secondary lever; it is the only one.

Consequence, and it is the decision-relevant one: restoring acceptance to the C1 rate takes C8
from 31.45 to about **44 tok/s**, which roughly matches *ungrouped* AR (45.66) but is still far
below the **78.67** that grouped AR ships at today, because MTP waves are excluded from grouped
prefill (`physical_group_not_qualified`, 24 rows > cap 8). So MTP profitability at width now
needs acceptance recovery *and* admission parity; neither alone closes it.

Caveats recorded: this route assumes MTP and AR share admission cost (MTP adds a draft forward,
so its true admission is somewhat higher, which would make MTP per-row look slightly worse, not
better), uses the packet's own rows/cycle rather than an assumed 3.82, and is a wall-based
estimate - the block-row trace sweep is still the right way to put a hard number on it.
