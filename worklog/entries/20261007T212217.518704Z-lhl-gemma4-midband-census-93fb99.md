# Census the mid-band prefill: MoE expert GEMMs own the gap

## Summary

Where does the 8192-32768 prefill deficit against llama.cpp live on the
promoted build (0.88x at 8192, 0.91x at 16384, 0.97x at 32768 -- while
512-4096 lead 1.09-1.45x and 65536-131072 lead 1.06-1.21x)? A kernel census
of full prefills answers it: **the MoE expert GEMM kernels, not attention.**

Per-prefill kernel time, aggregated by family from rocprofv3 kernel traces of
`scripts/gemma4_prefill_profile.py` (warmup + one repeat, per-prefill =
total/2, require-cached build):

| family | 8192 share | 32768 share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| expert/dense GEMM | **61.2%** | **48.9%** |
| attention (wmma) | 19.1% | 35.4% |
| router logits | 10.4% | 8.4% |
| elementwise (norms/rope/gelu) | 4.1% | 3.4% |
| MoE glue (gather/accumulate) | 3.6% | 2.9% |

The three expert-quant kernels alone own 46-57% of every mid-band step:
`gguf_q8_0_t16_prefill_wmma_nwave` (the Q8_0 gate/up projection, 19-24%),
`gguf_q4_k_t16_selected_dual_wmma_prefill_compact32` (the Q4_K selected
dual, 15-18%) and `q5_1_selected_grouped_wmma_prefill_bf16_bf16` (12-15%).
The `qwen35_router_logits_token_tile` kernel adds another 8-10%.

The interpretation is direct: attention tuning cannot close a 12% total gap
at 8192 -- attention is only 19% of the step there, and the WMMA promotion
already did its work. Closing the mid-band means making the expert GEMMs
~20% faster at the 1024-row-block shapes, or the router cheaper. These are
shared qwen35-lineage kernels whose tile variants (nwave, compact32,
grouped, token-tile) were tuned on gfx1100/W7900; the 8060S (gfx1151, 40
CUs, wave32) was never the tuning target. The in-tree probes for the next
unit exist: `gemma4_t16_leaf_ab.py`, `gemma4_q8_t16_4wave_microbench.py`,
`gemma4_mmq_k_width_leaf_bench.py`, `gemma4_router_tile_ab.sh`,
`gemma4_router_variant_flip_probe.py`.

Session variance note, recorded for honesty: the same build measured 1128.4
tok/s at 8192 in the published ladder and 1244.1 tok/s in this census run --
the mid-band moves ~10% between sessions on the shared-memory iGPU, so
mid-band tuning must A/B arms within one process, as the existing probes do.
