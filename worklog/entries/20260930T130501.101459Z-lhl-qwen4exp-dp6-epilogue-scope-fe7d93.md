# DP-6 is priced: 85.791 ms, 3.72x below the figure it was credited with, and it ranks below DP-5

## Summary

DP-6's stated gap - the SSM causal conv and the KV store were unpriced, so the row
was not rankable against DP-5's 131.001 ms - is closed from the committed X-3
capture. **DP-6's scope is 85.791 ms = 0.545% of the 15,741.963 ms kernel union,
against DP-5's 131.001 ms = 0.832%, so DP-5 outranks DP-6 and the row's own
caution was right.** The 319.494 ms figure the row was resting on overstated it by
**3.724x**.

## The finding that matters most

The row was carrying 319.494 ms as its credit while explicitly warning that the
number covered more passes than it would fuse. **That warning is now quantified:
the true scope is 3.724x smaller.** The reason is that the `elementwise_norm`
bucket also holds `grouped_rmsnorm_bf16_f32_kernel` at 115.351 ms, of which
57.012 ms sits under `gr_read:layers.*.hc_attn_down` and 56.862 under
`gr_read:layers.*.hc_ffn_down` - hyper-connection reads, not norms DP-6 would
fuse.

A reader who built the `attn_qkv` epilogue on the strength of 319.494 ms would
have been optimizing a 0.545% owner while believing it was 2.03%.

## Ceiling, not net

85.791 ms is an owner cost, i.e. a ceiling. The conv still has to happen; what
DP-6 eliminates is the pass over the wide activations. Same ceiling-vs-net
distinction recorded for DE-2 and MX-1, and no speedup is claimed.
