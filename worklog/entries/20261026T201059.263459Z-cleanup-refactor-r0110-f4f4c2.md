# Scope Q5_1 row publication to the owning prefill

## Summary

Retire the Q5_1 row-publication environment hop in favor of explicit owning
prefill/profile selection, preserving the upstream register-cache dependency.

## Changes

- Preserve rows>=512, K640, parent-variant and registration guards; do not
  alter other Q5_1 dispatch or device arithmetic.
- Carry profile selection to primary and lazy cloned runners, and migrate
  diagnostics to explicit restored controls with real route engagement.
