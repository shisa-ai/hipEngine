# Promote WMMA prefill under the section 2.11 envelope

## Summary

The WMMA prefill candidates are the production default. The lead's 2026-10-07
decision (EXECUTION-PROFILES section 2.11) scoped the production envelope for
sparse-router models to what the arithmetic can answer: robust statistics over
all teacher-forced rows (median <= 1e-3, p90 <= 2e-2) plus a bounded
flip-damage budget (rows over the 5e-2 line: <= 3% global, <= 8% per
category) and top-1 floors (>= 96% / >= 94%), with task non-inferiority,
three-run repeatability and isolation binding unchanged.

The promotion packet -- 18 cases, 1,152 teacher-forced rows, the strict chain
forced into both arms -- passes every binding metric with real margin:

| metric | measured | requirement |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| median row KL | 3.824e-06 | <= 1e-3 |
| p90 row KL | 5.960e-03 | <= 2e-2 |
| flip-damage rate | 1.32% global / worst 2.78% | <= 3% / <= 8% |
| top-1 agreement | 98.41% / worst 96.83% | >= 96% / >= 94% |

Tasks are non-inferior on 18/18 cases, repeats and isolation are green on
18/18, and the routing diagnostic measures the mechanism the envelope
anticipates: 91% of rows flip a boundary expert at some of the 30 layers, but
only 1.3% of rows carry damage above the 5e-2 line -- flips are ubiquitous and
benign, which is why their rate is budgeted and their magnitude is not.

Two same-day promotion attempts are recorded as invalidated and are not
evidence: their harness replayed the candidate's own greedy chain instead of
the strict teacher chain, so they measured free-running chain divergence
(global medians KL 17-24) rather than arithmetic drift. The defect, both runs
and the correction are disclosed in EXECUTION-PROFILES section 2.11 and the
campaign doc; the binding bars were frozen from the earlier saved 1152-row
packet before the corrected run.

The shipped default is a split: multi-row prefill routes to
`gemma4_wmma_flash` (sliding head_dim 256) and `gemma4_wmma_flash_full`
(head_dim 512) -- 2.073x-5.121x over the staged path where the gap was largest
-- while single-token decode keeps the strict-parity pipelined staged
singleton (the measured decode lead over llama.cpp) via an appended request
entry, and strict plain remains the registered fallback. The selector skips
the WMMA entries for one-token blocks when staged is requested; an explicit
WMMA-only request still takes the WMMA decode shape unchanged. The public
route was verified end to end after the flip: sliding prefill -> wmma_flash,
full prefill -> wmma_flash_full, decode -> staged, public_path_parity True at
512 tokens with prefill 1183 tok/s.
