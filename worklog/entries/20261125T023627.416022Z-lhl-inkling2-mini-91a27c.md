# Read Inkling fast prompt blocks 256 rows wide and keep strict at 64

## Summary

`fast` now reads prompts in blocks of 256 rows, with the scheduler's chunk and round budget set to
256 so a block is collected and computed in one round. `strict`, which is the profile selected when
none is requested, keeps 64 rows.

Decode bench through the in-tree harness, 128 outputs, five repetitions after a warmup, W7900,
mainline `UD-Q4_K_M`. The rows were taken at `f54f3f0ec` with the width set for the run; clean-tree
rows at this commit follow in a later entry.

| Profile | Block width | 47 tokens: first token, decode | 575 tokens | 2,057 tokens |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `fast` | 64 | 0.209 s, 18.06 tok/s | 2.340 s, 17.92 | 9.723 s, 17.48 |
| `fast` | 256 | 0.209 s, 17.99 tok/s | 1.867 s, 17.78 | 7.691 s, 17.45 |
| `strict` | 64 | 0.795 s, 14.82 tok/s | 9.915 s, 12.85 | 37.359 s, 11.29 |
| `strict` | 256 | 0.813 s, 14.80 tok/s | 10.683 s, 12.86 | 39.462 s, 11.24 |

- **`fast` at 256:** the first token comes 25% sooner after 575 prompt tokens and 27% sooner after
  2,057. Decode is within noise.
- **`strict` at 256 is 6 to 8% slower** to the first token at 575 and 2,057 tokens, so it stays at 64.
- **Output IDs:** all 128 are the same at both widths for each profile, the same across the five
  repetitions, and the same for the two profiles after 575 and 2,057 tokens.
- **Logits:** the [session-only width sweep](20261125T012524.445129Z-lhl-inkling2-mini-b7405e.md)
  found a prompt-end KL against strict of at most 6.9e-8 from 64 to 512 rows, with strict's top-1
  at every point.

Mixed load through `hipengine serve` (`fast`): stream A decodes 150 greedy outputs from a short
prompt; one second later request B arrives with a 2,114-token prompt and four outputs. Two servers
per width in both orders, three repeats each, W7900:

| Width | B's first token | A's total time | A's gaps between text chunks: median, p90, longest | Gaps over 250 ms, over 600 ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 64 | 12.4 to 12.5 s | 18.6 to 18.7 s | 56 ms, 376 to 381 ms, 389 to 395 ms | 33, 0 |
| 128 | 10.0 to 10.1 s | 17.1 to 17.3 s | 56 ms, 470 to 481 ms, 627 to 641 ms | 17, 7 |
| 256 | 9.1 to 9.2 s | 16.65 to 16.8 s | 56 ms, 58 ms, 1,127 to 1,141 ms | 9, 8 |

A wider block is a longer step for the other streams: at 256 the prefilling request gets its first
token 26% sooner and the decoding stream finishes 1.9 s sooner with a third of the stalled tokens,
but its longest gap grows from 0.39 s to 1.14 s. Width 128 is the middle: 10.0 s and 0.63 s.

What wider blocks leave, from a profiler trace of a 2,048-token prompt in `fast` on the W7900:

| Width | Expert projection (tile4) | Block attention | Short convolution | Dense GEMMs | Kernel time as share of wall |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 64 | 3.90 s | 2.05 s | 0.41 s | 1.11 s | 79% |
| 256 | 3.54 s | 1.88 s | 0.42 s | 0.26 s | 82% |
| 512 | 3.52 s | 1.80 s | 0.42 s | 0.13 s | 80% |

The expert projection does not shrink with width and is the largest kernel cost at every width.

## Changes

- `hipengine/generation/inkling2_gguf.py`: the width and loop budgets are per profile
  (`prefill_block_width`, `fast_prefill_block_width`, the two `engine_loop_config_defaults`); the
  generator takes its pair in `__init__`.
- `scripts/inkling2_prefill_width_probe.py`: a width override replaces both pairs.
- `tests/test_unit_inkling2_gguf_resident.py`: the width equals both budgets in each profile, fast
  reads wider blocks than strict, and a block runs only after the scheduler has handed over its whole
  width.
- `docs/campaigns/INKLING2-MINI-GGUF-EXECUTION.md`: status under step 3 and under step 1.
