# On the probe corpus the MMQ route tracks the anchor and the fp32 arm does not

## Summary

Per-step logits over a shared context show the MMQ route and llama.cpp agreeing
token for token through the first four continuation steps, while the fp32 arm
parts from llama.cpp at step 0 with a margin of +7.14. The route then degenerates
at step 4 at a genuine near-tie of +0.37.

This corrects the attribution in
`worklog/entries/20260927T064800.227391Z-lhl-gemma4-mmq-continuation-degeneration-9d2c41.md`.
That entry's observation was right - the route does collapse into a repeated
token while the anchor keeps producing varied output - but the conclusion drawn
from it was not. It implied the route was the deviant arm. Measured step by step,
the route is the arm that agrees with the outside implementation, and the arm
that disagrees is our own fp32 path.

The measurement only becomes interpretable once the chains part, because after
that the arms see different contexts and their logits are not comparable. Steps
0 to 3 are the comparable window, and in that window llama.cpp and MMQ emit
236800, 236800, 236743, 236832 while fp32 emits 236778, 236825, 236743, 236828.

## Changes

- `scripts/gemma4_mmq_arm_attribution.py`: per-step confidence for all three
  arms, a `_p_top1` that is comparable with llama.cpp's post-softmax
  probabilities, a `_repeat_check` that measures one prompt twice in one process,
  and raw-logit capture at the prefill so the softcap can be ruled in or out.
- `scripts/gemma4_llamacpp_reference_bench.py`: optional `n_probs` on the request
  body, off by default so recorded benchmark rows keep their request shape.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-27-gemma4-mmq-gate-up-three-arm-attribution.json`:
  the per-step rows, the raw-logit comparison and the determinism check.

## Validation

- Probe corpus, 96 ids, 16-token continuation, all three arms:
  - Steps 0-3, contexts identical: llama and MMQ agree on every token; fp32
    parts at step 0 (236778 against 236800) with a margin of +7.14, which is a
    confident disagreement and not a near-tie.
  - Step 4, contexts still identical for llama and MMQ: llama emits 236810,
    MMQ emits 236832 at a margin of +0.37. The degeneration starts here, at a
    near-tie, and the margins on 236832 then grow to +32 as the repeated context
    makes the next repeat easier to predict.
  - Raw final-row logits at 96 ids: median difference -1.552, max absolute
    difference 9.76, top-5 span 11.12 for fp32 against 6.00 for the route. The
    difference is structured, not quantisation noise.
  - `softcap_preserves_argmax` is true for both arms, which is the check that the
    cap is the monotonic elementwise transform it claims to be and therefore
    cannot move an argmax. An earlier run of mine reported the opposite and was
    wrong: it forwarded twice without resetting, so the second call prefilled at
    an advanced position. Fixed, and the fix is why the check now exists.
  - `_repeat_check` reports bit-identical logits across two prefills of the same
    prompt in one process, so the runner is deterministic and `reset()` is
    sufficient. The recorded divergences are not sweep-order artifacts.
- llama.cpp's `completion_probabilities` did not come back on this request, so
  the anchor's own per-step confidence is `n/a` in the rows. The token sequences
  are unaffected; the comparison is against llama.cpp's tokens, which are exact.

## Next

- The fp32 expert gate/up path needs the same scrutiny the route has had. It is
  the campaign's reference and on this corpus it is the arm that departs from the
  outside implementation, at a step where it is confident.
- Neither arm can be declared right on the strength of llama.cpp agreeing with
  one of them, because llama.cpp quantises its expert weights too. Breaking the
  tie needs exact arithmetic: the streaming CPU reference in
  `hipengine/kernels/cpu_reference/gemma4_streaming.py`.
- The first-token promotion gate is insufficient in a way that cuts both ways. At
  96 ids it passes the route, which matches the anchor, and fails fp32. Any gate
  that scores one token cannot distinguish agreement-then-degeneration from
  disagreement, and it cannot see a confident wrong first token either.
- Fix the `n_probs` request so the anchor's confidence is recorded; it is the
  only way to tell whether llama.cpp was itself near a tie at step 4.
