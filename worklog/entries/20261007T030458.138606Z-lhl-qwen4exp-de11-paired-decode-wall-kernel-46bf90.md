# qwen4exp DE-11 same-session paired decode wall and kernel sum

## Summary

**The `DE-11` decode non-kernel remainder is re-derived from one interleaved
session, and its recorded `7x` growth is a cross-session artifact: same-session
the remainder grows 3.15x and the p512 step is 92.8% kernel, not 96.9%.**

The ledger's remainder is a wall captured in one session minus a kernel sum
captured in another, and a sequential `code-p4096` comparison on this host drifts
up to 8%. This unit runs both producers for both depths in **one session**,
counterbalanced `A/B/A/B -> B/A/B/A`, one process per arm, cooldown to <=50 C,
production profile, chunk 1024, HEAD `7d97832d3`, gfx1151 Strix Halo.

| case | wall ms/step | kernel sum ms/step | remainder ms/step | kernel share |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `code-p512` | **47.700** (47.744 / 47.655) | **44.242** (44.228 / 44.256) | **3.457** | 92.8% |
| `code-p4096` | **55.946** (55.844 / 56.048) | **45.050** (45.048 / 45.053) | **10.896** | 80.5% |

Within-arm spread 0.01-0.36%. Wall **+8.246 ms/step (+17.3%)**, kernel sum
**+0.808 (+1.8%)**, remainder **+7.439 (3.15x)**. The p4096 remainder reproduces
the ledger's `10.56` within 3%; the p512 remainder is **3.46, not 1.47**.

**Why the p512 kernel sum moved, and why the recorded remainder is a mismatched
subtraction.** The p512 wall is unchanged from the ledger's (47.700 vs 47.656,
0.09%), but this session's p512 kernel sum is 1.95 ms/step lower (44.242 vs
46.193). `gguf_k_pack8_prefill_out_kernel` is **2851.6 ms** over 128 steps at the
default against the ledger's **3015.8 ms**. One extra profiled p512 capture with
`DE-3`'s `HIPENGINE_GGUF_PACK8_GEMV_WIDE=0` gives **45.758 ms/step** and
**3039.9 ms** for that family - reproducing the ledger's kernel time to 0.8%
while the default is 5.8% below it. The ledger's **wall is the lever-on wall**
(47.656; the measured lever-off wall is 49.201). So the recorded `1.47 ms/step`
p512 remainder is a **lever-on wall minus a lever-off kernel sum** - the same
class of apples-to-oranges subtraction the retracted third correction suspected,
driven by the `DE-3` block-size lever rather than the `DE-1` promotion. Caveat:
the ledger's walls are head `8c6f2bc94` with manifest `a55324f9...` while this
session is head `7d97832d3` with manifest `be32e719...`, so the production
dispatch changed too; the lever-off reproduction is the best available
explanation, not proof the ledger capture had the lever off.
