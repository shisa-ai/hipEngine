# Reject grid-exploded multirow QSA prefill

## Summary

Rejected the first exact multirow p4096 QSA prefill decomposition. It preserved
bytes but generated an untenable selected-token CTA grid.

## Changes

- Implemented a chunk-bounded T0 score/coefficient/weighted-value prototype with
  `strict_rows_spans` fallback, then removed it after the screen failed.
- Preserved only the compact rejection artifact and campaign decision.

## Validation

- RED passed byte equality at rows=3, Hq=24, Hkv=2, D=256, and selected counts
  2,049/2,050/2,051. Existing QSA runner tests also passed.
- At p4096 the score pass generated about 25.2 million CTAs per 512-row chunk.
  The auto-clock whole-model screen remained before its first warmup sample after
  more than 168 seconds at 99% GPU use and was terminated. No timing sample was
  fabricated and no route was promoted.

## Next

- Re-rank QSA toward a row-tiled or persistent selected-token decomposition that
  bounds both scratch and CTA count. Do not retry one CTA per selected token.
