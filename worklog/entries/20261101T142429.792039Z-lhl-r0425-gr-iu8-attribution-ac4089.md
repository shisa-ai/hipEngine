# Read GR owner attribution in recovery drivers

## Summary

Recovery-driver attribution follows the effective GR owner pair reported by the
profile-gap child; stale process values cannot confirm a requested GR choice.

## Changes

- Ranking records the GR pair, removes owner axes from process-route fields and
  reports missing owner choices as missing keys.
- Family sweep preserves the child's GR pair through summaries and final rows,
  and checks requested GR axes independently of any mixed process overrides.
- Keep the existing override CLI: profile-gap already translates these arguments
  into restoring owner bindings without writing the process inputs.

## Validation

- Nine new attribution cases fail RED before implementation; all 35 focused
  cases pass after the change and update of the old process-report expectation
  (`/tmp/r0425-gr-iu8-attribution-focused.xml`). The command replay also enters
  the real profile-gap binding and checks its effective pair and restoration.
- This change only consumes child reports; GPU dispatch is unchanged. The
  profile-gap public interface was already exercised by its migration.
- Compile, all five documentation gates, audit, test-tier and diff checks pass.
