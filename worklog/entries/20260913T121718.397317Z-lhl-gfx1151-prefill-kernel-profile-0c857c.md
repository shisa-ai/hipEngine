# gfx1151 Q4_K_M prefill kernel profile

## Summary

Qwen3.8-27B `Q4_K_M` prefill on the physical `gfx1151` host is 55.0-57.8%
dense K-quant WMMA work at 512, 1024 and 4096 prompt tokens, and two owners
carry it: the fused gate+up kernel `dense_dual_wmma_prefill_bf16_bf16_out`
(33.0-36.5%) and the 48-column shared-weight kernel
`t16_wmma_prefill_shared_b_bf16_bf16_out` (21.1-22.0%). Q6_K follows at
17.5-18.7%, Gated DeltaNet and its preparation kernels at 12.8-14.5%, the
rocBLAS F16 route at 3.1-4.1%, and flash attention at 1.0-3.0%. Traced device
time is 98.1-99.4% of the measured prefill wall time, so the prefill cost is
kernel execution rather than launch gaps.

Two properties of the mix are actionable. Every dense owner runs at 2 waves
per SIMD because its 248-256 VGPR footprint fits only twice into the 512-VGPR
budget, so a drop below 171 VGPR would admit a third wave. And on the identical
wide-down `(17408, 5120)` shape the Q4_K shared-weight owner reaches
27.50 TFLOP/s while the Q6_K planar owners reach 21.28 and 20.34 TFLOP/s, a
25-29% gap that quant size does not explain: both move weights at 8.5-11.0 GB/s
against a 256 GB/s memory system, so neither launch is bandwidth-bound. That
shape is 10.9% of 4096-token prefill kernel time.

## Changes

- `scripts/qwen38_gfx1151_prefill_kernel_profile.py` (new): a three-boundary
  profiler. An unprofiled build child populates a scoped JIT cache, an
  unprofiled warm child proves the same workload starts with
  `HIPENGINE_REQUIRE_CACHED_BUILD=1`, an empty compiler guard and an unchanged
  cache-tree hash, and `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace --selected-regions` then wraps
  one child per prompt length so the trace holds the measured prefill and not
  model load, the discarded warmup or session teardown. `--summarize-only`
  re-derives the family and kernel tables from an existing trace without
  touching the GPU. Trace summarization converts rocprofv3's work-item
  `Grid_Size_*` columns to workgroup counts.
- `benchmarks/results/2026-09-13-qwen38-gfx1151-prefill-kernel-profile/`
  (new): `artifact.json`, its generator `assemble.py`, and `README.md`. No
  number in the artifact is hand-transcribed. Family and owner tables come from
  the trace CSVs, wall times and first-token checks come from the driver JSON,
  and the shape-to-owner attribution is derived from the model's own GGUF
  tensor table: each row's launch count divided by its chunk count gives the
  tensor slots it must cover, and that matches all 12 tensor groups at all
  three prompt lengths. The only unattributed tensors are the MTP `nextn`
  layer's copies, which prefill does not run. Tensor byte sizes come from the
  gaps between GGUF tensor offsets, so the weight-traffic figures are measured
  rather than assumed from nominal bits per weight.

No engine code changed, so no published benchmark number moves. The recorded
prefill rates (431.2, 412.7 and 399.8 tok/s at 512, 1024 and 4096 tokens) are
context for the resident-sweep row already rolled up on 2026-09-13; this unit
is attribution, not a performance claim, and the artifact says so.

## Validation

- Command: `PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python scripts/qwen38_gfx1151_prefill_kernel_profile.py
  --model /models/gguf/Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M.gguf --backend hip_gfx1151 --quant
  gguf_q4_k_m --prompt-lengths 512 1024 4096 --compiler-version-file
  /tmp/hip1151-t6/hipcc-version-gfx1151.txt --cache-root
  /tmp/qwen38-prefill-profile/cache --run-root /tmp/qwen38-prefill-profile/run
  --run-tag qwen38-gfx1151-prefill-kernel-profile --out
  /tmp/qwen38-prefill-profile/profile.json` (full argv with the ROCm `--python`,
  `--rocprofv3` and `--roctx-sdk` values is in `artifact.json.provenance.command`).
- Correctness: all three profiled children returned first token 9707 and finite
  logits under the production execution profile with the Q4 rowtile and GDN
  capture verifiers enabled; the warm child started compiler-free with an empty
  compiler guard and an unchanged cache-tree hash.
- Attribution self-check: 9/9, 9/9 and 12/12 owner rows and 12/12 tensor groups
  explained at 512, 1024 and 4096 tokens respectively, with every residual 0 or
  1 (`nextn`).
- Cross-check: `assemble.py` is idempotent, and the dense weight traffic it
  derives (12.35 GB at 512 and 1024 tokens, 47.82 GB at 4096) is consistent
  with 48 linear-attention layers being re-read once per 1024-row chunk.
- `.venv/bin/python scripts/sync_benchmark_readme.py --check` passes.

## Next

- The Q6_K planar wide-down path is the clearest single target: 1110.7 ms
  (10.9%) at 4096 tokens at 20.3-21.3 TFLOP/s while the Q4_K owner does the
  same shape at 27.50 TFLOP/s without being bandwidth-bound. Porting the
  shared-weight 48-column structure to Q6_K, or finding why the planar decode
  gives up 25-29% at the same 2 waves per SIMD, is the follow-up.
- VGPR reduction below 171 on the fused gate+up kernel would admit a third wave
  per SIMD on 33-36% of prefill time; the kernel currently uses 248 VGPR.
- The 1024-column owners (attention K and V) run at 8.75-19.12 TFLOP/s against
  30-33 TFLOP/s for the same families on wide outputs; the profile does not
  establish the mechanism and grid occupancy does not explain it alone.
