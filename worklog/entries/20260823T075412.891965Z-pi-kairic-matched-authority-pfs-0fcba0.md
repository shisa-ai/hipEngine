# Kairic matched-authority PFS gate

## Summary

The complete same-resident Kairic authority versus PFS IU4 packet rejects the
PFS on binding correctness. Matching the public coherent GGUF does not close the
quality gap; it makes the arbitrary-context teacher result worse than the prior
Q4_K_S comparison. The product is mechanically fast and its fallback/lifecycle
contracts pass, but speed cannot override the frozen distribution envelope.

The explicit `iu4_ffn_pfs_path` research configuration remains available with
strict M<96/c1 fallback. It is not promoted to `production`, omitted/default, or
decode behavior. The artifact deliberately records `performance_claim=false`.
