## Changes


- Added `docs/QUANTS.md` from header scans of all 23 recipes and every shard in `unsloth/Laguna-S-2.1-GGUF@99d7f9a`: 814 tensors and 117,561,977,600 logical parameters per recipe. The document separates native compressed support from parser/CPU-dequant coverage, records the exact mixed tensor inventory and effective bpw of each recipe, and identifies `IQ2_XS` as the highest-return missing format for a single-W7900 `UD-Q2_K_XL` target.
- Preserved Unsloth's published Qwen3.5-35B-A3B PPL, 99.9%-KLD, and mean-KLD rows as cross-model quality priors rather than Laguna claims. The main cliffs are 2-bit routed gate/up and under-protected expert down; Q3 M-to-XL was small, while Q4 M-to-XL improved tail/mean KLD at low size cost. Planning targets are Laguna `UD-Q4_K_XL`/`UD-Q5_K_XL`/`UD-Q6_K_XL` for nominal 120 GiB, `UD-Q2_K_XL` for one 48-GiB W7900, and hypothetically layer-sharded `UD-Q3_K_XL` for W7900+7900-XTX. Current multi-GPU support remains explicitly unimplemented and TP K/V replication is not presented as aggregate pooled memory.
- Derived Laguna BF16 K/V from the pinned 48-layer, 12-global/36-SWA512, 8-KV-head, head-dim-128 configuration: 4,096 bytes per layer-token; with physical SWA eviction, `KV(C)=C*192 KiB` through 512 and `KV(C)=C*48 KiB+72 MiB` thereafter. One 1M sequence needs 48.070 GiB; tables cover context-to-bytes, bytes-to-context, concurrency, and 4/8-GiB runtime-reserve scenarios. The document requires physical sliding-cache reclamation evidence because masking without reclamation would make 1M consume 192 GiB.
- Added the document to `docs/README.md`. Documentation review, independent K/V arithmetic assertions, local-link checks, and `git diff --check` pass; no GPU run was needed and no performance/quality claim was made.
