# DE-1 scope suite: the 30-layer scope is the only arm that passes every binding bar, and it dominates the current production default on all five axes

## Summary

The DE-1 decision test ran: the full 18-case admission suite, **both arms
against the strict teacher**, at four dp4a scopes. All figures below are
production-versus-strict (§6.1), 1494 rows over 18 cases at 82 decode steps,
measured in one harness.

| arm | dp4a | warp | mean | p95 | p99 | max | top1 | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| production default | 43L | off | 0.001054 | 0.003732 | 0.024299 | **0.078490** | 0.994645 | **FAILS max** |
| production + warp | 43L | on | 0.001164 | 0.003536 | 0.019565 | **0.261188** | 0.993307 | FAILS mean+max |
| **candidate** | **30L** | **on** | **0.000428** | **0.001611** | **0.009040** | **0.027240** | **0.995314** | **PASSES ALL** |
| 30L control | 30L | off | 0.000547 | 0.001467 | 0.012965 | 0.043120 | 0.995984 | passes |
| 17L + warp | 17L | on | 0.000320 | 0.000444 | 0.002440 | **0.115957** | 0.998661 | FAILS max |
| 48L + warp | 48L | on | 0.001062 | 0.003327 | 0.015839 | 0.128120 | 0.992637 | FAILS mean+max |
| 48L control | 48L | off | 0.001231 | 0.004273 | 0.018549 | 0.193628 | 0.993307 | FAILS mean+max |

Bars: mean 1e-3, p95 5e-3, p99 2e-2, max 5e-2, top-1 0.99 overall and 0.97 per
category.

**The 30-layer scope passes every bar with 1.84x-3.10x margin** (mean 2.33x, p95
3.10x, p99 2.21x, max 1.84x, top-1 0.99531) and all four categories clear their
0.97 floor (code 0.99799, general_en 0.99398, general_ja 0.99398, mixed_ja_en
0.99398). Its 7 top-1 flips are all low-KL near-ties; the largest flip KL is
**0.00161**, 31x below the max bar and 12x below the p99 bar. The tightest flip
margin is `code_topological_sort` step 3 at **0.00177**.

**It dominates the current production default on every axis**, not merely
passes: mean 2.46x better, p95 2.32x, p99 2.69x, max 2.88x, top-1 0.99531 vs
0.994645. So this is not a speed-for-quality trade at all on this fixture - the
candidate is faster *and* more accurate.

**The current production default itself fails the max bar on this fixture**
(0.078490 > 0.05). That is consistent with the earlier finding that this suite
fails both arms at the production scope and is a stress suite rather than an
admission basis; it is recorded here because it bounds how much the suite's bars
can be used to rank arms.

**Two mechanisms are now separated, which is what the bisection predicted.**
Reducing the scope 43 -> 30 alone accounts for most of the numerical
improvement: 30L/off is mean 0.000547 / max 0.043120 against 43L/off at
0.001054 / 0.078490, i.e. **1.93x better on mean and 1.82x on max**. The warp
then improves it *further* at this scope (30L/on 0.000428 / 0.027240 against
30L/off 0.000547 / 0.043120, **1.28x on mean and 1.58x on max**) - the exact
opposite of its behaviour at the production scope, where it degraded max 3.33x.
The bisection's conclusion explains this: the warp's damage was confined to the
`{0,2,5,6,8,9,10,11} ∪ [13,18)` group, and removing those layers from the dp4a
route removes the damage while leaving the warp's own arithmetic.

**`[31,48)` confirms that single-case cleanliness is not admissibility.** It was
flip-clean on `heldout_mixed_review` in the bisection and still **fails max**
(0.115957) on the full suite. The caveat recorded with the bisection was correct
and is now demonstrated rather than asserted.

**A speed caveat that must not be projected away.** The 2.792 ms/step warp gain
was measured at the 43-layer scope. Reducing the scope moves 13 layers from the
dp4a route back to the exact route, and that costs time. From the post-promotion
figures (dp4a owner 5.404 ms/step over 43 layers = 0.1257 ms/layer; exact
residual 1.480 ms/step over 5 layers = 0.2960 ms/layer):

```
13 layers dp4a -> exact : +13 * (0.2960 - 0.1257) = +2.214 ms/step
warp gain at 30 layers  : -2.792 * (30/43)       = -1.948 ms/step
projected net                                      -0.266 ms/step
```

**That is a projected small LOSS, and it is a projection of exactly the kind
this ledger has had to retract before.** It is stated so the next measurement
can falsify it. No promotion decision is taken in this entry.

## Validation

- One harness for every row: `--packet dual --teacher`, 18 cases, 82 decode
  steps, `comparison = "strict teacher (production-versus-strict, 6.1)"`, 1494
  rows. The production-scope row is from the earlier run in the same harness and
  its numbers reproduce.
- `t0`-style control is present at 30L and 48L: the no-warp arm at each scope.
- Layer lists read back from each artifact's own `dp4a_layers_override`.
- Three launches failed before a valid one and the causes are recorded because
  two are easy to hit again: (1) `set -u` with `local tag="$1" ... f="$OUT/$tag.json"`
  in one statement evaluates `$tag` before assignment; (2) the base miniforge
  prefix has no ROCm, so `docs/ENVS.md`'s TheRock wrapper is required; (3)
  **`HIPENGINE_HIP_ARCH` and `HIP_DEVICE_LIB_PATH` are build-cache-key inputs**,
  so adding them to the wrapper makes `HIPENGINE_REQUIRE_CACHED_BUILD=1` miss.
  The working wrapper is `therock10-staging-20260828` (ROCm 7.15) with **neither**
  variable set.
- Limits: one model, one quant, one host (gfx1151, not the gfx1100 default
  hardware), one profile, one fixture family. The incumbent arm's
  `top1_by_category` is not recorded by the probe (`None`), so the per-category
  comparison is candidate-only. **No speed claim is made**; the net cost of the
  scope reduction is projected, not measured, and the projection is negative.
